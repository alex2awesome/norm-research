LEGAL-OUTCOME-PREDICTION verifier boundaries (precision-first)

The bank evaluates the quality of legal writing, reasoning, advocacy, and analysis;
it is not a taxonomy of substantive legal rules or case outcomes. A court's bare
holding, elements test, burden statement, or conclusion that conduct is lawful,
unlawful, proved, or unproved is normally NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION when the passage does
not evaluate the quality of reasoning or advocacy. Do not convert every legal
proposition into a bank gap or MATCH.

- a30 is broad legal-analysis quality: rule synthesis, law-to-fact application,
  explicit reasoning, counterarguments, persuasion, and defensible conclusions. It
  can cover a direct evaluation that an analysis is legally erroneous or would fail
  to persuade. a60 is narrower candor/accuracy/evidentiary grounding: misstated facts
  or authorities, unsupported record assertions, omitted adverse authority, or
  inaccurate characterization. Do not choose a60 merely because a legal conclusion
  uses an evidentiary standard.
- a63 is the use and development of legal authority or precedent. a79 requires an
  actual analogy/distinction based on materially similar or different facts. a85 is
  complete spotting, preservation, or identification of issues/sub-issues. A cited
  appellate decision offered against an assertion is a63 unless the passage performs
  an explicit fact-pattern analogy.
- a17 concerns compliance with document-type/forum conventions and the assigned
  legal product's scope or register. a44 concerns tailoring to the particular
  jurisdiction, court, judge, or docket. A request for jurisdiction/location that is
  needed to give forum-specific advice may instantiate a44.
- a41 is terminology/definition clarity and consistent use of legal terms; it beats
  a30 when the explicit defect is a category or term being used to mean the wrong
  thing. a59 covers candidly acknowledging uncertainty or knowledge limits and
  restrained, fair advocacy.
- A generic claim that a draft is an improvement, a position is unconvincing, or a
  decision should be accepted does not identify an exact quality leaf unless the
  evidence passage supplies the missing reason. Use GENERIC_VERDICT or CONTEXT_NEEDED
  rather than projecting an analysis metric.

For a wrong proposal, use BETTER_CANDIDATE only if a visible alternative decisively
wins; otherwise abstain. CONFIRM_MATCH requires the proposed exact leaf to beat all
visible siblings. When a30/a60/a63/a79/a85 or a17/a44 cannot be cleanly separated,
return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH.
