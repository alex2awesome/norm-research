Press-releases GEPA calibration (derived from independent train errors; exact frozen-bank leaves)

- A reported communication failure can reveal a norm even when written as a factual sentence. “Declined/did not respond to a request for comment,” “has not announced/provided details,” “did not disclose/list,” “denied,” “scrubbed/withheld,” and “did not air/correct” evaluate the communication act itself. Do not choose NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION merely because the grammar is journalistic or declarative.
- Match the object actually criticized, not a surrounding genre, strategy, audience, or consequence. Do not upgrade a literal omission, contradiction, or wording problem into a broad crisis/brand/investor strategy leaf unless that strategic condition is explicit.
- Leaf boundary: `a12` Openness and media access/responsiveness covers willingness to answer reporters, requests for comment, press questioning, refusals, silence, and responsiveness. `a40` Media access and follow-up requires concrete access/package/contact logistics such as reachable contacts, links, resources, paywalls, or follow-up mechanics. `a9` Crisis response speed and visibility requires an explicit crisis-response timing/visibility condition, not merely “did not immediately respond to comment.”
- Leaf boundary: `a216` Specificity and concreteness covers absent concrete plans, methods, amounts, or operational detail. `a30` Truthfulness/correction/non-suppression covers denial, correction, withholding, or omission of a material fact. `a7` Candor about uncertainty/limitations/tradeoffs requires candid treatment of limits or uncertainty. `a11` Message consistency/non-contradiction covers an explicit contradiction or incompatible messages; do not substitute a broad crisis-strategy leaf without a crisis strategy being evaluated.
- Leaf boundary: `a31` Claim restraint/non-sensational language covers hype, bravado, bold overclaiming, and sensational wording. Prefer a more literal overstatement/alarmism leaf only when the evidence explicitly compares the magnitude of a claim with evidence, not merely because “hyperbole” is mentioned.
- A proposal may be confirmed when it directly captures the explicit operational criterion and a supplied alternative adds an unexpressed condition. Mark AMBIGUOUS_MATCH when two leaves are genuinely co-entailed; do not confirm a broad proposal just because it is related.
