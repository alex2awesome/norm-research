Read the full EVIDENCE PASSAGE before classifying the short extracted statement. The

Humor has many overlapping siblings. MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY is correct only when the words
and context genuinely fail to express a leaf's unique condition. Do not use it merely
because several cards share words such as timing, clarity, originality, delivery, or
punchline. Conversely, a bare reaction to a punchline, style, reference, or relatability
can remain family-level when no unique structural condition is expressed.

Exact leaf boundaries

- a141 Core joke structure: setup→punch→tags requires the architecture: expectations or
  setup, a decisive switch/punch, and/or disciplined tags. Do not reduce an explicit
  construction critique to GENERIC_VERDICT or family-only. a196 Button/punchline landing
  is specifically the final scene/piece button, tag, stinger, or ending payoff. Bare
  "weak punchline," "best retort," "ending was flat," or "the punchline hit" without
  enough evidence to distinguish general punch construction from a final button is
  MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY, not automatically a196.
- a111 Purposeful pauses/audience space is silence, laughter beats, negative space, and
  allowing reactions. a171 is overall timing/rhythm/momentum. a235 is live performance
  flow, transitions, energy, mic/room read, and delivery control. a239 is editing,
  continuity, transitions, and pacing of a produced piece. Use the unique medium/object;
  do not collapse all four into family-only.
- a184 Structural economy/editorial rigor is whole-piece architecture, progression,
  cutting even good jokes that break flow, and structural revision. a118 Economy,
  brevity, and clarity is plain, tight language/lines with no fat. a202 is concise,
  precise phrasing at word/sentence level. a71 is editing for setup economy and joke
  density. a50 is specifically laughs-per-minute/page and dead-air return.
- a114 Frame-shift/resolution/causal intelligibility requires an initial reading, a
  coherent alternate frame, and a satisfying causal reframe. a222 Internal realism and
  logic coherence concerns stable character behavior/world rules and contrivance. If a
  complaint merely says "makes no sense" or "I don't follow the logic" without
  distinguishing reframe causality from world logic, use MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.
- a280 Misdirection/reveal design requires fair cues that prime one interpretation,
  conceal another, then justify the reveal. Do not use family-only when that expectation

  Accumulation/format subversion uses lists, repetition, overcommitment, tedium, or a

Typed abstention calibration

- NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION for descriptive facts about a comic or work that do not praise,
  criticize, prescribe, compare, or reveal a quality condition. A creator saying they
  will work on tags is not itself an evaluation of tag quality.
- GENERIC_VERDICT for undifferentiated funny/unfunny, amazing/weak, or broad praise/blame
  without a discriminating dimension. "Didn't laugh because it isn't funny" is generic;
  do not force a2 unless laugh frequency/strength/yield is actually evaluated.
- NO_CANDIDATE_FITS only for a clear specific criterion absent from all cards, not for a
  bare fact or generic verdict.

reason short and contrast the closest sibling.
