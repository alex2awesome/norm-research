NOTICE-AND-COMMENT verifier boundaries (precision-first)

Verify the proposed leaf against the stated operational object and causal reason.
Shared words such as "burden," "access," "clarity," "compliance," or "coordination"
are not sufficient. If the evidence does not decisively resolve the closest boundary,
return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH rather than confirming.

- a47 is direct unnecessary administrative, reporting, procurement, respondent-effort,
  or industry cost/burden. a12 is operational workability, staffing/resources,
  transition phasing, or implementation timing. a15 is tailoring or proportionality,
  especially a minor violation or subgroup/project condition producing a severe or
  disproportionate consequence. a23 is an overlooked indirect/hidden impact or cost
  driver. a18 is assessment of a broader or unintended consequence, including a
  perverse incentive caused by the rule. Do not let a generic burden phrase erase
  these different objects.
- a8 is a quantified estimate of impact or cost. A numeric payment/reimbursement
  effect is not merely a12 feasibility.
- a13 applies when the criterion concerns the definition or consequences of a
  performance objective, target, threshold, or outcome metric. A threshold that
  blocks entry or continuation is not automatically a12 merely because it is a
  practical barrier.
- a1 is a concrete, implementable ask, condition, remedy, or ready-to-adopt drafting
  choice. Prefer it when the human criterion is the requested operational solution
  itself. a25 requires coordination or harmonization itself to be the criterion; a10
  requires equity/distributional fairness itself, not merely that an implementable
  remedy names a protected group.
- a0 is explicit comparison against an existing, voluntary, non-regulatory, or other
  baseline/alternative and its trade-offs, including a request to let that alternative
  operate before adding restrictions.
- a43 is whether regulated parties can identify precisely what duties, coordinates,
  deadlines, or compliance steps are required. a55 is stable, uniform application or
  enforcement and reduced uncertainty across cases. Asking how one is expected to
  comply is a43; inconsistent application/enforcement is a55.
- a51 is whether an issue is genuinely open to comment or whether comments could
  change the outcome. It beats a40 when the agency lacks discretion and therefore
  notice/comment cannot affect the result. a40 is reserved for legally salient
  authority, court-precedent, notice/logical-outgrowth, or record-preservation issues.
- a16 requires competition or antitrust effects/safeguards. A failure to apply or
  enforce an existing rule consistently is a55 unless the expressed criterion is the
  competitive effect itself.

For a proposed leaf that loses one of these contrasts, use BETTER_CANDIDATE only when
one supplied alternative clearly wins. Otherwise use AMBIGUOUS_MATCH. CONFIRM_MATCH
requires the proposal to survive the strongest contrast, not merely to be related.
