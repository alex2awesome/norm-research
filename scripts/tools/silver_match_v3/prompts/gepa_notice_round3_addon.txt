Task-specific routing for NOTICE-AND-COMMENT

The bank mixes norms about the quality of a public comment, the quality of an agency's proposed/final rule, and the participation process. First identify which object the human evaluates. Then apply these contrastive boundaries; these are semantic rules, not keyword shortcuts.

- a1 versus a2: a concrete implementable ask, text change, exception, date, threshold, or mitigation is a1. Require a2 only when the comment also justifies an alternative's reasonableness and analyzes benefits, costs, impacts, or trade-offs.
- a39/a40/a20: use a40 for legally salient arguments about statutory authority, inconsistency with law, notice/logical-outgrowth defects, preserving an issue, or triggering response duties. Use a39 for substantive engagement with the agency's rationale, mandate, questions, or significant issues without that legal posture. Use a20 when evaluating whether the agency itself supplied a reasoned, record-supported explanation for its choice.
- a18 versus a23: a18 concerns whether the analysis covers the full set of economic/social/environmental/safety/distributional effects. a23 concerns a concrete impact or cost driver the agency omitted or failed to count. Do not collapse all adverse consequences into a23.
- a12/a15/a47: a12 is operational feasibility, transition timing, resources, or whether compliance can actually be implemented. a15 is whether regulatory design is proportionate and least burdensome while achieving its objective. a47 is avoidable administrative, reporting, paperwork, procurement, or respondent effort and process efficiency.
- a9 versus a10: a9 identifies who bears costs or receives benefits. a10 additionally evaluates equity/fairness across subgroups or calls for representative analysis and mitigation.
- a19 versus a6: a19 evaluates whether the agency's analysis/decision uses best available science, transparent models/data, and appropriate review. a6 evaluates whether the commenter's own argument is credibly sourced and methodologically supported.
- a13 is about outcome-focused objectives and performance indicators, not merely an omitted impact. a14 is specifically a preference for flexible, performance-based rather than prescriptive regulatory design.
- a43 concerns fair notice and precise legal duties or guidance. a44 concerns whether the notice adequately describes the action/issues/effects so the public can comment. a60 concerns whether participation itself is timely, meaningful, authentic, transparent, and adequately resourced.
- A quotation or factual claim can still reveal a norm when the surrounding human passage uses it as a legal challenge, reason for a recommendation, identified omission, or evaluative comparison. Use NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION only when no such normative force is present.

Apply these boundaries only when the relevant metric cards are supplied. Continue to use NO_CANDIDATE_FITS when the explicit construct is absent from the slate.
