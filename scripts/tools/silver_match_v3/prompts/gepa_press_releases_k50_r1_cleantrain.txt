Press-releases calibration, derived only from clean training-panel errors

Treat the extracted statement as naturally occurring journalistic evidence about a
communication act. A reported breach can reveal the communication norm even when it
is written as a declarative fact and contains no word such as "should," "bad," or
"unprofessional." In particular, refusals or failures to comment, prolonged silence,
missing announcements or details, denials contradicted by the passage, omitted or
unaired material, and discrepancies between statements can be explicit norm evidence.
Do not choose NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION merely because the grammar is reportorial.

This does not make every negative fact a communication norm. Business performance,
product performance, policy merit, compensation generosity, competitive position,
missed operational deadlines, or a person's general political embarrassment remain
NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION when the human words do not evaluate how something was
communicated. Match the criticized communication act, not the substantive outcome.

Use these frozen-bank leaf boundaries contrastively:

- a12 Openness and media access/responsiveness: a reporter's request for comment,
  refusal or failure to answer, an unavailable source or spokesperson, unwillingness
  to permit an interview, cagey/tight-lipped treatment of questions, or general
  responsiveness to independent reporting. In this calibration, even refusal to make
  a CFO or treasurer available for an interview is a12. Do not move such cases to a38
  merely because a spokesperson or executive is named.
- a38 Authoritative, newsworthy quotes and available spokespeople: whether the release
  itself supplies substantive attributable quotes from appropriate leaders or experts
  and designates useful spokespeople. It is not the default leaf for a reported failure
  to respond to a journalist; that is a12.
- a40 Media access and follow-up: concrete access-package mechanics—paywalls, reachable
  contact details, resource/background links, boilerplate, or follow-up logistics. A
  bare refusal or nonresponse is a12, not a40.
- a7 Candor about uncertainty, limitations, and tradeoffs: candidly acknowledging
  limits, challenges, reluctance, uncertainty, or tradeoffs. a6 is narrower: explicitly
  separating what is known from unknown or preliminary and communicating that status.
  a164 is narrower still: an explicit study/claim limitation or caveat needed for
  interpretation. Generic reporter access remains a12.
- a8 Timely, frequent, transparent updates: delayed disclosure, a first statement only
  long after an event, promised future disclosure, going quiet, or update cadence. a9
  requires an explicit crisis/backlash setting plus response speed or public visibility;
  "not immediately available for comment" alone is a12.
- a11 Message consistency and non-contradiction: an explicit changed position,
  incompatible account, discrepancy between speakers/documents, or repeated versus
  conflicting forecast. Do not treat a disagreement between accounts as merely
  descriptive when the passage uses it to expose inconsistency.
- a25 Truthfulness, substantiation, and non-deception: broad false, misleading,
  unsubstantiated, hyped, manipulative, or spin-laden claims. a30 is the narrower leaf
  for denial, correction, withholding, suppression, or omission of a material fact.
  a28 is accuracy/evidence/independent verifiability when credible support, sources,
  citations, checking, or independent verification is the expressed criterion.
- a31 Claim restraint and non-sensational language: bold, hyped, grandiose,
  over-the-top, or sensational wording. a70 requires the more specific comparison that
  claimed importance or alarm exceeds what the evidence supports. a87 concerns whether
  a spokesperson quote sounds human, respectful, humble, and not canned; it is not the
  general hype leaf.
- a216 Specificity and concreteness: a missing concrete plan, amount, office, method, or
  operational detail. a97 requires a more complete/checkable factual package such as
  the 5Ws/H, methodology, metrics, pricing/availability, or verification-ready claims.
  a65 instead asks for background or rationale needed to interpret significance.
- a51 Consumer-facing clarity and disclosures: precise customer/product naming,
  packaging language, price, coverage, or out-of-pocket disclosure. a123 is narrower:
  what changes for customers, what stays the same, timing, support, and transition
  arrangements. a29 is uncertainty/risk disclosure rather than ordinary customer facts.
- a15 Authenticity and values/actions alignment requires an expressed mismatch between
  professed values or messaging and genuine commitments/actions. a57 requires a
  message-versus-reality or cross-channel/over-time alignment problem. Do not infer
  either solely from a disappointing substantive outcome.
- a0 is sponsorship, funding, relationship, or conflict disclosure. Do not replace it
  with a30 merely because nondisclosure is an omission.

Apply the most literal discriminating leaf supplied in the candidate cards. A concrete
reported omission or contradiction is not NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION. If the passage truly
supports overlapping sibling leaves without distinguishing them, return
MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY; otherwise choose exactly one MATCH. Keep typed abstention for bare
facts, generic verdicts, missing context, absent-bank criteria, and noise.
