Math recurring-boundary addendum (GEPA round 2)

Apply these rules only when the quoted act and rationale are explicit. They
cover confusions recurring across two train-only rounds; otherwise retain the
round-1 abstention policy.

- Detecting/debugging one's computational or logical mistake is a114. Mere
  public disclosure of a typographical erratum is a34.
- An exhaustive split into named cases is a136 even when offered as a hint.
- Ambiguity that makes exposition hard to read is a18; ambiguity in formal
  definitions, hypotheses, or theorem wording is precision/rigor a12.
- A WLOG normalization, limiting model, or modified easier problem used to
  solve the original is a125; an example used mainly to illustrate is a80.
- Stating that a method still times out, diverges, or cannot apply under the
  relevant constraints is a109. Choosing a named tool because it is suitable
  is a73; supplying an explicit construction without a tool-choice comparison
  is a95; a high-level plan/subgoal is a112.
- A missing justification for one claimed proof step or bijection is a26,
  regardless of whether the surrounding method is standard or elegant.
- Identifier naming and maintainability inside formalized code is a17;
  mathematical notation/symbol convention is a2.
- Deliberately changing examples, level, or tone for readers is audience
  adaptation a3; general clarity without audience adaptation is a18.
- Using a counterexample to reject/refine a proposed characterization is a128,
  not broad correctness a56.
- Contest/problem placement, unintended difficulty, and scope calibration are
  benchmark design a104, not topical fit a33.
- A short direct verification praised for its simple explanatory power is a52;
  do not reduce every "works" statement to broad correctness.
- A LaTeX/formatting error discussed together with language/clarity/proofreading
  polish is publication readiness a14. Numbering, labels, and cross-references
  specifically are a102.
- A wrong or unfinished named branch of a case analysis is coverage a98; broad
  correctness a56 applies only when no specific case-coverage criterion is
  expressed.

If a boundary above is not textually decisive, return MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.
