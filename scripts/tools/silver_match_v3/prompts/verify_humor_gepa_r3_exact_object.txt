Humor verifier GEPA round 3: exact evaluation object and causal role

Apply this three-part contrast before any high-confidence CONFIRM_MATCH:

1. Name the property the human directly praises, requests, or criticizes.
2. Decide whether each mentioned cue is the evaluation target, the mechanism that
   changes it, or merely evidence/context from which the target is inferred.
3. Contrast the proposal's unique condition with the strongest alternative's
   unique condition.

Confirm only when the proposal's evaluation object and distinctive mechanism are
an explicit statement or an unavoidable paraphrase of the human criterion. Do not
confirm a related leaf merely because its definition contains one of the cues.
In particular, never turn diagnostic evidence into the target of evaluation.

Do not become indiscriminately conservative. A broader or synonymous sibling does
not by itself make an exact proposal ambiguous. If the proposal is the leaf that
most specifically owns the explicit object and mechanism, while an alternative
matches only an umbrella theme or surface word, CONFIRM_MATCH may be high. Use
AMBIGUOUS_MATCH only when the statement genuinely leaves two distinct mechanisms
unresolved, not whenever two cards overlap.

Two operational boundaries are mandatory:

- Complaints about audibility, volume balance, recording, or mixing target
  technical production execution. They do not entail audiovisual craft *for
  comedy* unless the human explicitly evaluates sound/camera/editing as a device
  for tone, misdirection, rhythm, or punchlines.
- Nervous relief, reduced threat, emotional detachment, or an audience becoming
  able to laugh targets affective-distance conditions. It does not entail a
  play/safety/consent frame unless the performer explicitly establishes trust,
  permission, shared rules, or stakes management as the framing mechanism.

If a visible alternative owns the explicit object and mechanism, return
BETTER_CANDIDATE. If only the family is supported, return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH. If the
target is absent from the bank, return NO_CANDIDATE_FITS. A high CONFIRM_MATCH
reason must identify the object, mechanism, and decisive sibling contrast.
