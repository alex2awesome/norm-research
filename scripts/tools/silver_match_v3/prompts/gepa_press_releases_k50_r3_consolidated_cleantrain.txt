Press-releases round-3 consolidated calibration (clean training panels only)

Judge the selected norm fragment together with its evidence context. The fragment can be
a shorthand attribution or clause whose communicative property is made explicit by the
surrounding sentence. Positive compliance also counts: candidly stating uncertainty,
giving a timed update, making a material investor disclosure, announcing a partnership
with its purpose, reporting follow-through on a pledge, or detailing corrective action
can reveal a bank norm without criticism or the word "should." Conversely, do not map a
fact merely because a company, official, or press release stated it.

Decision gate

1. Identify the actual evaluated/revealed object: message, disclosure, evidence,
   access, timing, tone, framing, commitment/reporting, explanation, media package, or
   corrective communication. A substantive business/product/policy/medical/political
   outcome is NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION unless the fragment plus context makes one of those
   communicative properties explicit.
2. A true extraction fragment with no usable semantic content after reading context is
   NOISE. A meaningful bare fact is NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION. Broad importance or quality
   such as "substantive" without an operational dimension is GENERIC_VERDICT.
3. Apply the task-specific object priorities below before generic wording overlap.
4. MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY requires a real definition-level tie after applying those
   priorities; shared words such as evidence, clarity, truth, timing, or consistency do
   not create a tie.

Access, candor, timing

- a12: reporter access itself—declined/no response to comment, unavailable interviewee,
  refusal to answer, or hostile handling of questions—with no more specific object.
- a7: "tight-lipped," hesitant, terrified to speak, tiptoeing, cautious, or otherwise
  candid/not candid about a named sensitive issue, limitation, negative implication,
  uncertainty, or tradeoff. This content-specific candor beats generic a12.
- A domain-specific disclosed/withheld object beats a12: a13 for safety/risk facts;
  a20 for material investor/deal/IPO facts; a51 for consumer-facing product facts;
  a198 for an amount or monetary figure.
- a6: explicitly separates known from unknown or preliminary status. a29: claim/study
  scope, limitations, external validity, disclaimers, realistic timelines. a164: an
  explicit study/claim caveat needed for interpretation.
- a8: delayed/first disclosure, update cadence, promised future release, or silence.
  a118: whether information is genuinely timely/new/recent, including an expressly
  long-awaited item or superseded old data. a9: public response speed/visibility in an
  explicit crisis or backlash—not the speed of substantive government action.

Truth, evidence, consistency, alignment

- a25 is the default for the truth/substantiation of a claim: dishonest or misleading
  claims, unbelievable promises, hype, spin, manipulative promotion, taking credit
  without support, or a promoted claim exposed as false by evidence/reality.
- a30 is the narrower communication act of denial, correction/walk-back, suppression,
  withholding/omitting a material fact, or failure to correct a disputed interpretation.
- a28 is whether a claim has credible evidence, checking, citations, or independent
  verification. a1 is attribution/transparency/reliability of the source or speaker.
  a4 is disclosure of sources, data, methods, assumptions, or validation so others can
  assess/replicate. Do not return family-only for a plain demand for claim evidence when
  a28 is supplied.
- a11 is a changed or incompatible message across statements, speakers, documents, or
  time: a defense despite prior distancing, apology paired with incompatible denial,
  going off the announced message, or repeating/changing a forecast. An operational
  business reversal alone is not a11.
- a15 requires authenticity between an expressed value/mission/cause/commitment and
  actions. a57 is message/tone versus reality/actions or cross-channel alignment without
  the values condition. a25 wins when deception/hype of the claim is the locus; a30
  wins for correction/suppression; a11 wins for message-to-message contradiction.
- a0 wins whenever the object is sponsorship, funding/use of funds, relationships, or
  conflict disclosure—even in an investment pitch or message-versus-reality contrast.

Specificity, context, audiences

- a47: clarity, explicitness, plain articulation, or ability to articulate/explain.
  "Most explicit statement" and struggling to articulate reasons are a47.
- a216: a named missing concrete detail. a97: a complete/checkable fact package such as
  5Ws/H, methods, metrics, partners, pricing or availability. A very brief announcement
  that leaves core product facts/features unknown is a97.
- a115: meaningful numeric KPI plus performance context. a198: concrete quantity/money.
  a199: benchmark, baseline, peer, or historical comparison/comparability.
- a65: background or rationale needed to interpret importance, implications, or an
  otherwise incomplete story. a64: the narrower public/audience benefit or "why it
  matters." a216 is merely the missing concrete detail; a30 is deceptive material
  omission.
- a51: consumer-facing naming, packaging, price/cost/coverage, feature activation or
  offer timing, and disclosures preventing customer confusion. a123 requires an actual
  customer change/transition plus what changes/stays, timing, support, or arrangements.

Tone, framing, stakeholders, action

- a31 wins for boast, bravado, bold claim, grandiose, over-the-top, hyperbole, hype, or
  sensational wording. a70 additionally requires that significance/alarm exceeds the
  supporting evidence. a87 is only the natural, respectful, humble, non-canned voice of
  a spokesperson quote. a125 is tone/wording that trivializes gravity or misreads the
  moment; a scoff at safety wording or optimism amid deaths is about tone, absent proof
  of a values/actions mismatch.
- a20: material, compliant investor disclosures, deal terms, strategy/performance/risk,
  market expectations, analyst guidance. a22: investor confidence-building/value story,
  convincing or calming investors, operational drivers, or anticipated Q&A.
- a16: a commitment plus actual communicative follow-through/progress versus the pledge,
  including fulfillment or action after a vow. a71: requiring the pledge itself to be
  specific, measurable, time-bound and reported; a bare internal target is neither.
- a17: listening/responding to affected stakeholders or changing course after their
  concerns. a52: selecting the proper public-affairs instrument (guidance versus
  punishment/release/advisory/PSA) and clarifying PA/PIO roles. a59: partnership
  announcement purpose, parties, contributions, benefits, or credibility.
- a41: completeness/quality/accessibility of press assets or visuals. a44: accessible
  risk/public-health guidance and feasible protective actions. a72: concrete remedial
  or preventive actions after harm, including safety-driven removal/adjustment. a36:
  choosing and evaluating a deny/diminish/rebuild or respond-versus-ignore crisis
  strategy, rather than the truth of the misinformation itself.
- a3: transparency about content origin, including undisclosed AI or covert content
  creation; delayed discovery of who generated content is an origin-transparency issue.

Keep the JSON reason contrastive and at most 24 words so the object cannot be truncated.
