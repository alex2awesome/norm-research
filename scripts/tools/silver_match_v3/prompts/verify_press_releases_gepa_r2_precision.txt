Press-releases strict verifier round 2 (clean training errors only)

Precision dominates yield. CONFIRM_MATCH is warranted only when the proposal's unique
operational object is explicit and every supplied near-sibling is worse. If a plausible
alternative captures a more specific object, return BETTER_CANDIDATE; if the evidence
does not resolve the boundary, return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH. Relatedness is insufficient.

First separate communication evidence from surrounding subject matter. A positive or
negative communication act can reveal a norm, but a fact is not a criterion merely
because a reporter asked about it, a spokesperson stated it, or it appeared in a press
release. A refusal to perform or condemn a substantive act is not automatically a media
access failure. A representative's ordinary answer is not a failure of responsiveness.
Neutral market commentary such as an expected outcome or "not huge news" should not be
upgraded to a precise leaf when the normative construct is absent or family-level.

Use these proposal stress tests; they supersede broader heuristics when they conflict:

- Proposed a12 Openness/access: confirm only for reporter access itself—declined/no
  response to comment, unavailable interviewee, refusal to answer, or hostile/cagey
  questioning—with no more specific content object. "Tight-lipped," terrified or
  hesitant to speak, or tiptoeing around a named sensitive issue is a7 candor. Refusal
  to disclose safety/health facts is a13; material investor/deal facts can be a20;
  customer-facing facts can be a51; a requested amount can be a198. A substantive
  request to condemn behavior, or an ordinary limited answer from a representative,
  may be NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION rather than a12.
- Proposed a30 Truthfulness/correction/non-suppression: require denial, correction or
  walk-back, suppression, withholding, or an explicitly material omission. An
  affirmative dishonest, unsupported, hyped, manipulative, taking-credit, or misleading
  claim is a25. A generally incomplete story lacking interpretive background is a65
  unless deception/material suppression is explicit.
- Proposed a25 Truthfulness/non-deception: prefer a57 when the exact object is a message
  or claim versus real actions/cross-channel reality. Prefer a14 when the expressed
  standard is public interest, stakeholder fairness, or presenting harm as benefit.
- Proposed a11 Message consistency: confirm for incompatible messages, positions, or
  accounts across speakers/documents/time. Prefer a57 for message or promise versus
  actual action/reality. Prefer a199 for a numeric comparison/benchmark and a115 for a
  KPI whose comparability or performance context is the problem. An apology paired with
  an incompatible denial can reveal a11 even though an apology card is supplied.
- Proposed a15 Authenticity/values-actions: require a stated value, mission, cause, or
  commitment plus evidence about genuine actions. A safety phrase that merely inspires
  a scoff, optimism amid deaths, or wording that misreads gravity is a125 tone-deafness
  absent an explicit actions/value mismatch.
- Proposed a57 Message-reality alignment: confirm for message/tone versus reality or
  cross-channel action without the values condition. Prefer a25 when deception or hype
  of the claim is the locus, and a11 for message-to-message contradiction.
- Proposed a216 Specificity: reject if the unique object is clearer elsewhere. Difficulty
  articulating or an explicitly worded statement is a47 clarity/plain language. A vague
  explanation inside a broader crisis-response posture can be a27. Missing calculation
  of a performance number is a115. A brief announcement missing a complete/checkable
  product fact package is a97. A raw amount is a198.
- Proposed a65 Context/significance: confirm for absent background or rationale needed
  to interpret importance. Prefer a20 for the reason/details in an investor filing,
  sale, IPO, deal, or material financial disclosure. Prefer a97 when a short launch or
  announcement omits core complete/checkable facts and features.
- Proposed a20 Investor clarity/compliance: confirm when deal terms, IPO details,
  material performance/risk, market expectations, analyst guidance, or a filing is the
  expressed object. Do not replace it with generic a12/a216 merely because the company
  declined or omitted details.
- Proposed a51 Consumer clarity/disclosure: confirm for consumer policy, product
  naming/packaging, price/cost/coverage, amount owed/erased, feature or offer timing, or
  disclosures preventing customer confusion. a123 requires an actual transition/change
  plus what changes/stays, timing, support, or arrangements; a216 is only generic detail.
- Proposed a123 Customer transition: reject in favor of a51 when the passage merely asks
  for a customer-facing feature, product, duration, availability, or policy fact and no
  transition/support arrangement is expressed.
- Proposed a31 Claim restraint: generic boasting, bravado, boldness, over-the-top,
  hyperbole, hype, or sensational wording is a31. a87 requires a quoted spokesperson's
  human/humble/non-canned voice, not merely any public figure boasting. a70 additionally
  requires that significance or alarm exceeds the supporting evidence. Neutral language
  that "looks not too dramatic" need not reveal a criterion.
- Proposed a87 Humble spokesperson tone: reject for generic boasting/hype outside the
  distinctive spokesperson-quote voice; a31 is then more precise.
- Proposed a97 Complete/checkable facts: confirm when the release lacks a complete
  verification-ready package (5Ws/H, method, metrics, partners, pricing/availability),
  not merely one unspecified detail. Proposed a115 requires meaningful numeric KPIs and
  performance context. Proposed a199 requires an actual benchmark/peer/baseline contrast.
- Proposed a119 Original/substantive information: do not confirm bare "not huge news"
  when it expresses only broad newsworthiness or cannot distinguish novelty siblings;
  use AMBIGUOUS_MATCH or the appropriate abstention.

Exact-ID confirmation is the goal. Do not confirm a broad proposal merely because it
contains the same surface words as the passage. Keep the reason under 24 words. Use high
confidence only when the proposal wins every contrast above; otherwise abstain or name
the better supplied alternative.
