Press-releases round-2 refinements from clean round-1 training errors

These refinements supersede broader earlier heuristics when they conflict. First locate
the object and property expressed by the selected norm fragment, using its context only
to resolve the referent and contrast. A report about a communication can reveal a norm;
a report merely attributed to a press release does not automatically do so.

Calibrate abstention before selecting a leaf:

- A source tag ("it said in a press release"), connective fragment ("but even so"),
  or other extraction debris with no meaningful criterion is NOISE, not
  NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION.
- A substantive business, product, policy, political, operational, medical, or market
  outcome is NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION even if it misses a target, changes over time, is
  embarrassing, slow, surprising, inconsistent with expectations, or is described by
  a company spokesperson. Do not convert an operational about-face, a missed internal
  deadline, financial underperformance, a policy dispute, or a company's substantive
  action into a communication metric unless the selected words evaluate the message,
  disclosure, evidence, framing, or communicative follow-through.
- A denial or statement of position is not by itself a truthfulness violation. A planned
  absence of an announcement is not by itself media unresponsiveness. A reporter asking
  a subject to condemn a substantive act is not automatically requesting ordinary PR
  access. Require the context to make withholding, contradiction, evidence failure, or
  another communication property the relevant locus.
- Comparative adjectives such as "substantive" can still be only a GENERIC_VERDICT
  when they convey broad importance/quality without a discriminating operational
  property. Do not equate "substantive" mechanically with specific details.

Exact-leaf contrasts:

- a12 versus a7: use a12 when the locus is reporter access—the request for comment,
  nonresponse, unavailable interviewee, refusal to answer, or hostile/cagey handling of
  questioning—with no more specific content property. Use a7 when "tight-lipped,"
  hesitant, cautious, or afraid to speak describes candor about a named sensitive
  issue, limitation, uncertainty, negative implication, or tradeoff. The substantive
  topic of the withheld comment can therefore make a7 more precise than generic a12.
- a20 overrides generic a12/a216 when the missing or withheld material is an investor
  disclosure: deal terms, IPO details, material risks, performance, strategy, market
  expectations, or analyst guidance. Use a22 only for the broader investor value story,
  confidence-building engagement, operational drivers, or anticipated investor Q&A.
- a47 is clarity/plain articulation. A person struggling to articulate or explain a
  reason is a47 unless a particular missing concrete fact is named. a216 is a missing
  tangible plan, method, amount, office, reason, or other specific detail. a97 is the
  complete and checkable factual package (5Ws/H, methods, metrics, partners,
  pricing/availability). a115 is specifically meaningful numeric KPIs with performance
  context; a198 is concrete quantities or money; a199 is a benchmark or comparison.
- a51 covers consumer-facing product naming, packaging, price/cost/coverage, feature or
  availability timing, and disclosures needed to prevent customer confusion. a123
  requires a change or transition and explains what changes, what stays the same,
  timing, support, or transition arrangements. Ordinary missing customer-facing
  timing/details should not be upgraded to a123 merely because customers are affected.
- a25 is the broad claim-level norm: truth, substantiation, nondeception, hype,
  manipulation, spin, misleading promotion, or a promise that evidence/reality makes
  unbelievable. a30 is the narrower act of denying, correcting/walking back, suppressing,
  withholding, or omitting a material fact, including failure to correct a disputed
  interpretation. a28 is evidence, credible sourcing, checking, or independent
  verification; a1 is transparency and reliability of the source/speaker itself.
- a11 is an incompatible message, changed position, or discrepancy across statements,
  speakers, documents, or time. Prefer a30 for an actual correction/walk-back or
  suppression/omission; prefer a25 when the locus is a misleading or unsubstantiated
  claim rather than consistency as such.
- a15 requires authenticity between an expressed value, mission, cause, promise, or
  commitment and real actions. a57 is message-versus-reality, tone-versus-action, or
  cross-channel/over-time alignment without a values/mission condition. Prefer a25 when
  the contrast is evidence that an advertised/promoted claim is deceptive or hyped.
  Prefer a0 when the object is sponsorship, funding, use of funds, relationships, or
  conflict disclosure, even if the passage also describes message/reality mismatch.
- a31 is general boastful, bold, grandiose, hyped, hyperbolic, over-the-top, or
  sensational wording. a70 requires the explicit additional condition that the claimed
  importance/alarm exceeds supporting data. a87 is specifically a quoted spokesperson's
  human, respectful, humble, non-canned voice, not boasting in general.
- a65 supplies background or rationale needed to interpret importance or implications.
  a64 is the narrower audience-facing "why it matters" or public benefit. a216 is a
  missing concrete detail, while a30 is a materially misleading omission.
- a8 requires timeliness, cadence, delayed/first disclosure, promised future updates,
  or going silent. a9 additionally requires an explicit crisis/backlash context and
  evaluates public response speed/visibility. Merely saying an event occurred "just
  before" another event, or criticizing the speed of substantive government action,
  does not establish either communication norm.
- a16 is a concrete commitment plus communicative evidence of progress versus targets.
  a71 is a specific measurable/time-bound pledge plus reporting follow-through. A bare
  operational target, promised technical capability, or action after a vow is not enough
  unless communicative accountability/follow-through is the expressed property.

Do not choose MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY merely because multiple cards mention evidence,
clarity, truth, timing, or consistency. Use the unique object and condition above to
select the exact leaf. Reserve family-only for a genuine tie after those distinctions.
