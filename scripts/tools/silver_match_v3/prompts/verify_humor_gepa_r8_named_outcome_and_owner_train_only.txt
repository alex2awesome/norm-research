Humor full-bank resolver GEPA round 8: named outcomes and literal owners

This resolver-only refinement was authored by the supervising judge from
additional disagreements in the predeclared TRAIN role. No dev, test, or blind
item was used to choose these rules. It supplements rounds 1--7; exact-leaf
precision and typed abstention remain primary.

1. A superlative is not automatically a generic verdict. When the human names
the banked outcome itself -- for example, “funniest,” “not funny,” or a strong
laugh response -- the quality dimension is comedic effectiveness/laugh impact.
By contrast, “great,” “mind blowing,” or “excellent” still supplies no quality
dimension and remains GENERIC_VERDICT unless another clause supplies one.

2. Treat a metric card's literal boundary examples as strong ownership
evidence. A performer or cast member breaking into laughter/giggles directly
activates the timing-and-delivery leaf whose bank examples include cast breaks.
Use grounded/sincere performance only when the criterion is commitment,
earnestness, deadpan tone, or staying in character rather than the break itself.

3. Let explicit temporal position break punchline-family ties. A request for a
“final punch,” closing gag, button, tag, or stinger is owned by the ending/button
leaf when landing the ending is the criterion. Use general setup--punch
architecture only when the criticism concerns the logical or structural
relationship between setup and payoff rather than its final position.

4. Established fictional canon can instantiate internal world-rule coherence.
When the criticism is that a joke or premise contradicts a world's established
rules, the internal-realism/logic leaf is supported even if the rule originated
in source canon. Abstain only when the criterion is source-faithfulness as such
and no world-rule inconsistency is asserted.

5. Separate datedness from timeless factual accuracy. If a once-plausible
label, reference, or premise is criticized because time has passed and it no
longer fits the current moment, timeliness/topicality owns the criterion. Use
truthfulness/embellishment when the problem is a false or unsupported claim
independent of temporal change.

For every resolver item, identify the human's evaluative nucleus first, then
state the nearest sibling that loses. These rules do not authorize inferring a
craft mechanism from subject matter or generic approval.
