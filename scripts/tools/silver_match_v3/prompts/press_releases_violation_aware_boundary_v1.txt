Press-releases exact-leaf boundary guidance (violation-aware)

The general independent-labeling guide controls. In particular, a concrete
breach, refusal, omission, correction, contradiction, or disclosure can reveal
the human norm even when the sentence is written as a factual report. Do not
require an explicit adjective such as "bad," "misleading," or "unresponsive."

- A reported failure or refusal to answer press requests directly activates
  the media-access/responsiveness leaf. "Did not respond," "declined to
  comment," and an unanswered request are not automatically
  NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION when they function as accountability evidence.
- An explicit limitation or trade-off disclosure can match candor about
  limitations/trade-offs even when the underlying limitation concerns product
  access, compatibility, or operations.
- A precise clarification denying an unauthorized association can directly
  instantiate the disclaimer/correction leaf; it need not criticize itself.
- A stated omission of an operational safety policy can reveal the applicable
  public-safety/risk-communication norm when the omitted information would let
  affected people understand or act on the risk.

Still reject pure topical co-occurrence. A discrepancy is normative only when
the statement actually reports a claim, omission, refusal, contradiction, or
other accountability-relevant relation; two unrelated facts placed nearby do
not manufacture one.

Sibling contrasts:

- Use factual accuracy / non-misleading-claims leaves for a concrete false,
  exaggerated, or quantitatively contradicted claim (for example, advertised
  20% versus actual 40%).
- Use general message-versus-reality alignment for a broader narrative or
  operational mismatch that is not already captured by a more specific
  factual-accuracy leaf.
- Use values/authenticity alignment only when an actual value, mission, cause,
  or identity commitment is part of the criterion.
- Use promise/commitment follow-through only when a concrete promise, pledge,
  assurance, or commitment is the nucleus.
- Use evidence-grounded truthfulness only when the bank definition uniquely
  wins over the narrower accuracy/misleading/exaggeration siblings. If the
  supplied words establish the family but not that exact leaf, return
  MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.

Apply these rules symmetrically to praise and violations. Never force a leaf
when the exact sibling contrast remains unresolved.
