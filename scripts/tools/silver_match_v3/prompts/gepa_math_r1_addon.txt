Math exact-leaf addendum (GEPA round 1)

Derived only from the predeclared Math prompt-train errors. Identify the
human's operational act (checking, choosing a tool, repairing a gap, adapting
for readers, etc.), not merely the mathematical topic or words such as
"wrong", "simple", or "clear".

- Self-detection, sanity checking, debugging a computation, or explicitly
  finding/correcting one's error is metacognitive monitoring a114. Broad rigor
  or correctness leaves do not override that act.
- Selecting or sequencing a named theorem, algorithm, data structure, or
  computational device because it fits the problem is tool choice a73.
  Economy a72 concerns avoiding an overlong/overpowered proof technique;
  strategy a112 concerns the high-level plan; constructive content a95 merely
  supplies an explicit algorithm. Use the stated reason to distinguish them.
- Do not default to broad logical correctness a56 when a specific failure is
  named: omitted/unused premise a68; uncovered or unfinished case a98;
  counterexample used to refine a claim a128; disclosed correction/erratum
  a34; missing local justification a26.
- A request to extract or reuse a lemma to break up a proof is modularization
  a64. A request to justify one unexplained inference or bijection is a26. A
  hint explicitly based on an exhaustive partition is proof by cases a136.
- A normalization, limiting model, WLOG reduction, or nearby easier problem
  used to make progress is special-case/reformulation a125. A concrete example
  used mainly to illustrate an abstraction is a80.
- Calibrating contest/problem difficulty, placement, scope, or unintended
  difficulty is benchmark design a104, not general question topicality or
  correctness.
- Formatting plus language/proofreading polish or a reported LaTeX/misprint
  issue in a submission is publication readiness a14. Math layout alone is
  a10; LaTeX numbering/labels/cross-references are a102.
- General readable exposition is a18; adapting objects, level, or tone for a
  readership is a3; revealing why an idea works for human understanding is
  a25. If the concern is a particular missing proof step, use a26 instead.
- In formalized code, identifier/API maintainability is code presentation a17;
  mathematical symbol/terminology convention is a2.
- Explaining where a method cannot work under realistic constraints is a109,
  even when an alternative strategy is also discussed.

When two visible leaves remain equally supported after these contrasts, return
MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY. Exact MATCH requires the quoted action and rationale to
distinguish the chosen frozen-bank leaf.
