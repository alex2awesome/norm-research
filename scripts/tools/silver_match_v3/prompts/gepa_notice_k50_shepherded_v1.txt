Task-specific exact-leaf boundaries for NOTICE-AND-COMMENT at K=50

First identify the evaluated object and the specific reason for praise, objection,
or request. A public comment is often actionable, but do not choose the generic
actionable-alternative leaf merely because it says “should,” “recommend,” or proposes
a change. When the stated reason directly instantiates a construct-specific card
(equity, competition, legal clarity, burden, privacy, etc.), prefer that specific card.

- Burden leaves: use a47 for unnecessary administrative, reporting, procurement, or
  respondent effort/cost imposed on industry or regulated parties. Use a12 for whether
  implementation is operationally workable, resourced, phased, or timely—not for a
  bare claim of cost/burden. Use a15 when requirements should be tailored, waived, or
  reconsidered as unnecessary, duplicative, or disproportionate to the objective. Use
  a10 when the central objection is unfair or disproportionate harm to a named group
  (victims, rural/underserved people, disabled people); use a24 for small entities.
- A concrete ask does not automatically become a1. Use a1 only when the quality of the
  proposed implementable alternative/draft itself is the criterion. If the ask invokes
  distributional fairness, competition, proportionality, or another supplied specific
  construct, route to that construct. Use a0 when the comment asks the agency to compare
  a regulatory option with an existing/voluntary baseline and its trade-offs.
- Use a43 for fair notice and precise legal duties or compliance terms—including exact
  coordinates, deadlines, and what regulated parties are expected to do. Use a42 for
  general plain-language organization, formatting, and communication usability.
- Use a59 for access to participation, services, benefits, or adoption by affected or
  most-impacted groups, including disability/underserved access. Use a61 only for
  environmental-justice program design and environmental burdens.
- Use a40 for legally salient arguments about statutory authority, inconsistency with
  judicial opinions, notice/logical-outgrowth defects, or preserving issues for review.
  Existing court or statutory requirements can reveal the legal-effectiveness norm.
- Use a78 for confidentiality, anonymization, PII/proprietary information, and conditions
  for public posting. Use a52 for broader algorithmic/data-system governance such as
  human review, explainability, contestability, purpose limits, and due process.
- Explicit anticompetitive effects or stifled competition select a16 even when the rule
  is also burdensome. Use a25 when coordination harmonizes a concrete regulation,
  product label, or requirement; use a46 for formal cross-jurisdiction legal interaction
  and institutionalized interagency consistency.
- Use a51 when the issue is what is genuinely open for comment or whether comment could
  change an outcome. Use a60 for the quality, timing, authenticity, transparency, and
  capacity of the participation process itself.

Do not turn declarative legal or regulatory language into NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION merely
because it is phrased as a fact. A required compliance step, evidence failure, stated
legal duty, or endorsed/violated safeguard can reveal a norm when the supplied passage
makes that evaluative role explicit. Continue to abstain when it is genuinely only a
fact, topic, or undifferentiated policy preference.
