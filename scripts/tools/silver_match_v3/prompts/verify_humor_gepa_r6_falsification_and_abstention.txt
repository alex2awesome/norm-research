Humor verifier GEPA round 6: proposal falsification and calibrated abstention

This optimize-only round adds an independent burden-of-proof check to the
criterion-nucleus procedure.  Treat the proposal and its rationale as an
untrusted hypothesis, never as evidence.

Before reading a metric card as an answer, write down internally one short
quotation from the human evidence that carries the evaluative commitment.  The
context may identify the speaker's referent or make a speech act intelligible;
it may not supply a missing leaf-level property.  Then apply these tests:

1. Direct-paraphrase test.  The proposed card must directly generalize the
   quoted criterion.  A topical cue, downstream consequence, possible remedy,
   or word shared with the card is insufficient.

2. Unsupported-condition test.  Name the proposal's distinctive condition.
   If that condition comes only from the card, extraction hint, proposal
   rationale, or world knowledge rather than the human evidence, do not
   confirm.  Use AMBIGUOUS_MATCH when the evidence identifies only a bank
   family, NO_CANDIDATE_FITS when it states a specific out-of-bank property,
   or the appropriate evidence-gate abstention.

3. Alternative-owner test.  For the strongest supplied alternative, name its
   distinctive condition too.  Return BETTER_CANDIDATE only when the human
   words support that condition more directly than the proposal's.  A merely
   imaginable sibling, broad thematic overlap, or an alternative that requires
   an unstated condition must not cause a false rejection.

4. Abstraction test.  Short umbrella phrases such as “structure,” “energy,”
   “recovery,” “another layer,” “make sense,” or “finish the joke” do not by
   themselves select a leaf when several mechanisms could realize them.
   Conversely, a concise directive or attribute can be exact when its object
   and requested change uniquely reproduce a card's operative condition.

5. Typed-abstention test.  Generic praise or dislike without a named quality is
   GENERIC_VERDICT.  A deictic edit with unrecoverable purpose is
   CONTEXT_NEEDED.  Neutral explanation is NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION.  A clear
   criterion absent from all cards is NO_CANDIDATE_FITS.  Do not convert any of
   these into a nearby thematic match merely to retain the proposal.

CONFIRM_MATCH only if the quoted human criterion survives all five tests and no
supported alternative owns it better.  In the reason, report compactly:
“evidence = …; proposal delta = …; alternative delta = …”.  For an abstention,
state which required delta is missing.  The goal is calibrated precision, not
maximum confirmation or maximum rejection.
