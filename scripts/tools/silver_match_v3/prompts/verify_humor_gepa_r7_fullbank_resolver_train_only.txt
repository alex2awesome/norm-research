Humor full-bank resolver GEPA round 7: train-only boundary corrections

This resolver-only refinement was authored by the supervising judge from early
disagreements in the predeclared TRAIN role. No dev or blind disagreement was
used to choose these rules. It supplements the criterion-nucleus and
falsification rules; precision remains primary.

1. Prefer the specific task-local owner over an umbrella sibling when the
human names its distinctive object. An unintended visual focal point or
instant readability problem is visual composition/clarity, not general set or
production quality. General praise or criticism explicitly of delivery can be
owned by the bank's general expressive-delivery leaf; do not demand a narrower
timing, voice, or showmanship mechanism when the general leaf is itself the
specific owner.

2. Do not let a secondary unbanked appraisal erase a separate explicit banked
criterion in the same extracted statement. Re-derive every evaluative clause.
If the human directly says the comedy is funny and also says it is underrated,
the laugh-impact criterion remains explicit even if recognition is not banked.
This rule does not license choosing an incidental clause when the extraction's
actual nucleus is clearly the unbanked one.

3. Naming a praised artifact or technique is not enough to identify a quality
dimension. “Mind blowing,” “great,” or similar undifferentiated approval of an
improv, rap, bit, or premise is GENERIC_VERDICT unless the evidence states the
property that made it good. Do not promote the object's category into a craft
criterion.

4. Distinguish MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY from CONTEXT_NEEDED by what is missing.
MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY applies when the evaluative property and a bank family are
recoverable but the leaf delta is absent (for example, “word the punchline
better” without specifying economy, clarity, rhythm, or verbal wit).
CONTEXT_NEEDED applies when missing referent, purpose, or surrounding evidence
prevents recovery of even the criterion family. Missing leaf detail alone is
not CONTEXT_NEEDED.

5. A concrete suggested rewrite can reveal the craft mechanism through its
form and the explicit request around it. If the context asks to mock a target's
pretension and the rewrite faithfully imitates that target's conventional
form, parody/satire form fidelity is supported. Do not require an additional
adjective that merely restates the demonstrated mechanism.

For each resolver decision, first quote the evaluative clause internally, then
state the exact owner or the precise missing delta. Never infer a family anchor
from a free-text rationale when the structured decision is
MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.
