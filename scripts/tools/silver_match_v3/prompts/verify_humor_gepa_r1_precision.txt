Humor verifier GEPA round 1: exact-leaf uniqueness and sibling boundaries

This is a precision filter, not a semantic-relatedness check. CONFIRM_MATCH is a
uniqueness judgment: the proposed leaf must be more specifically entailed than every
visible sibling. In the reason, identify the strongest losing sibling and the exact
object or condition that rules it out. If the proposal merely applies but the evidence
does not distinguish it from a sibling, return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH. If a visible sibling is
better, return BETTER_CANDIDATE. Precision dominates yield.

- a218 versus a20: a218 is the audience's familiar, shared “I've felt that” recognition
  and sympathy. a20 is a recognizable social/emotional truth or acute observation used
  as the humor engine. Bare “relatable” language favors a218; do not confirm a20 unless
  the truth/observation mechanism is explicit. If only broad recognizability is given,
  mark ambiguity rather than treating both as interchangeable.
- a280 versus a142: both are misdirection/reinterpretation leaves. a280 requires a
  fairly cued initial reading, concealed alternate interpretation, and justified reveal.
  a142 emphasizes the operational two-story mechanics—compatible setup, calibrated
  ambiguity, avoiding telegraphing, and the clean aha reinterpretation. Confirm only
  when the passage names the proposal's distinguishing mechanism; otherwise ambiguity.
- a184 versus a68: a184 is whole-piece architecture, progression, coherence, cutting,
  and editorial resolution. a68 is visual draftsmanship/composition/economy that makes
  an image-caption or visual gag read instantly. A layout, panel, image, drawing, or
  visual-readability complaint is not a184 merely because “structure” sounds related.
- a255/a8 versus a1: a255 is personal ownership, self-implicating candor, vulnerability,
  and emotional presence. a8 is calibrated taboo/dark/shock content, earned offense,
  audience tolerance, and blowback risk. a1 is the narrower comedy-first comparison:
  laughs and joke craft must beat shock, sermon, applause-line, or agenda. Do not confirm
  a1 for any vulnerability or taboo passage unless that explicit laughs-over-shock-or-
  agenda comparison is present; choose a255/a8 when their named object is visible.

General fail-closed rules:

- A concrete manifestation can support an abstract metric, but shared vocabulary or a
  plausible paraphrase is insufficient for exact-leaf uniqueness.
- When the human names a production object, audience condition, temporal condition,
  ethical object, or narrative function that a sibling uniquely owns, that object wins.
- Do not confirm a broad leaf because the more specific gold-like alternative is absent;
  use NO_CANDIDATE_FITS or AMBIGUOUS_MATCH as appropriate.
- Sarcasm and quoted descriptors still require evaluative force in context. A quoted
  word such as “relatable” is not automatically an endorsement or an exact criterion.
- Keep the reason under 28 words and make it contrastive.
