PEER-REVIEW independent full-bank labeling boundaries (precision-first)

Apply these together with INDEPENDENT_LABELING_GUIDE.md. Select MATCH only when
the quoted reviewer language resolves one exact leaf against its strongest sibling.
When the construct is explicit but overlapping leaves remain genuinely
indistinguishable, use MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY. The aspect hint and manuscript topic are
never sufficient evidence.

- a0 is missing or inadequate methodological description, rationale, data/code,
  documentation, or information needed to verify/reproduce the work. a1 is an
  actually inappropriate, incorrect, uncontrolled, underpowered, or poorly executed
  design/analysis. A request to explain an ad-hoc choice or clarify what data/method
  was used is normally a0; do not select a1 from the word "method" alone.
- a43 is whether claims/contributions are supported by evidence, including direct
  theoretical support. a66 is coherence and integration of the theoretical framework
  itself. Do not substitute a66 merely because the support is theoretical.
- a72 covers whether the paper establishes motivation, background, need, and scope.
  Directly judging the work well or poorly motivated is not a bank gap.
- a10 covers positioning against related work and comparison with strong, relevant,
  or current baselines/models. It requires an evaluative or prescriptive comparison
  criterion; a bare result that one estimator beats another is not itself a10.
- a16 covers adequacy or representativeness of sampled populations or data sources,
  including evaluation limited to one dataset. a6/a47 cover explicitly stated
  applicability or transportability beyond studied settings. Because a6 and a47
  overlap, use MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY unless the exact words resolve their definitions.
- a29 is direct technical correctness, logical soundness, or argumentative coherence.
  A bare judgment that a paper is technically sound can instantiate a29. Missing
  exposition alone is a0/a11, not a29.
- Novelty leaves overlap. a74 requires novelty positioned against prior work; a73 is
  substantive originality or conceptual advance. A bare "novel" or "incremental"
  fragment that does not resolve this contrast is MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.
- Direct praise or criticism that a research question/problem is interesting,
  challenging, important, or worth addressing is a48, not GENERIC_VERDICT.
- Direct judgments that conclusions are clear or unclear select a44 unless the words
  explicitly identify an evidentiary-support problem.
- A request to add related references is citation coverage a33.
- Direct praise that detailed experiments were conducted can instantiate a1's
  execution-quality criterion; it is not automatically generic.
- Be conservative with a0 when a request for more text/discussion could instead be
  claim support (a43), experimental rigor (a1), or general exposition.
- Undifferentiated performance, results, importance, or praise/blame without a
  bank-defined quality dimension must not be forced. Use GENERIC_VERDICT,
  CONTEXT_NEEDED, or NO_CANDIDATE_FITS as appropriate.

For every MATCH, state why its exact definition beats the nearest sibling. A typed
abstention is a valid positive outcome; precision dominates yield.
