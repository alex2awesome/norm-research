- Direct praise/criticism that a research question or problem is interesting,
  challenging, important, or worth addressing is a48 (question importance and

- Direct judgments that conclusions are clear or unclear select the conclusion-

- A request to add related references is citation coverage a33. Do not reject it
  solely because another broad citation card overlaps.
- Direct praise that detailed experiments were conducted can instantiate a1's
  execution-quality criterion; it is not automatically generic.
- Be substantially more conservative with a0. If the reviewer asks for more text or
  discussion but the underlying issue could instead be support for a contribution
  (a43), experimental rigor (a1), or general exposition, do not confirm a0 unless the
  missing reproducibility-relevant method/data/rationale is explicit.
- Be conservative with a10. It requires an evaluative or prescriptive criterion about
  positioning or adequacy of comparison to prior work/baselines. A bare descriptive
  result that one estimator has lower variance than another does not itself evaluate
