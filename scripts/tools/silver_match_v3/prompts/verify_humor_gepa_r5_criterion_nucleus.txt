Humor verifier GEPA round 5: criterion nucleus, role, and sibling delta

This round corrects both false confirmation of thematic neighbors and false
rejection caused by broad sibling overlap. Apply the following procedure in
order; do not inherit the proposal's explanation as evidence.

1. Re-derive a criterion nucleus from the human's own words: the smallest
   property, action, consequence, or constraint that is praised, criticized,
   prescribed, or contrastively attributed. A prescription, comparison,
   operational quality attribute, or claimed comic consequence can reveal a
   criterion without an explicit word such as “good.” Mere explanation of what
   a joke refers to, how its content works, or what topic it has does not.

2. Assign the nucleus a role: evaluated outcome, craft mechanism/remedy,
   performer behavior, production behavior, or contextual evidence. Confirm
   only a card whose distinctive condition owns the evaluated role. Do not map
   a means to an outcome card, an outcome to a means card, or a contextual cue
   to a card merely because the cue appears in its definition.

3. State the proposal's leaf delta over the strongest sibling. The delta must
   be supported by the human statement, not supplied by the metric card or the
   proposal rationale. Diagnostic phrases such as “dark,” “too soon,” “clean,”
   “cliche,” “graphics,” “pause,” “processing,” “inside joke,” and “punching
   up” are clues, not automatic IDs; resolve what is actually being evaluated.

4. Use a symmetric sibling rule. CONFIRM_MATCH when the proposal directly owns
   the nucleus and an alternative overlaps only by umbrella theme, synonym, or
   downstream consequence. BETTER_CANDIDATE only when the alternative owns the
   nucleus more directly. AMBIGUOUS_MATCH when the human evidence supports the
   family but does not supply the unique delta needed to choose one leaf. Do not
   invent a delta to avoid abstention, and do not abstain merely because several
   cards mention the same broad concept.

5. Preserve the typed evidence gate. Undifferentiated liking or disliking is
   GENERIC_VERDICT. A deictic edit whose purpose cannot be recovered is
   CONTEXT_NEEDED. A specific criterion with no exact bank owner is
   NO_CANDIDATE_FITS. A neutral content explanation is NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION.

For high-confidence CONFIRM_MATCH, the reason must compactly identify:
“nucleus = …; role = …; sibling delta = …”. If the sibling delta cannot be
grounded in the human evidence, do not return high-confidence confirmation.
