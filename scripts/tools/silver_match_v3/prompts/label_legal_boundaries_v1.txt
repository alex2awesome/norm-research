LEGAL-OUTCOME-PREDICTION independent full-bank labeling boundaries (precision-first)

Apply these together with INDEPENDENT_LABELING_GUIDE.md. The bank evaluates legal
writing, reasoning, advocacy, and analysis; it is not a taxonomy of substantive
rules or case outcomes. A bare holding, elements test, burden statement, or outcome
is normally NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION when it does not evaluate reasoning or advocacy.
Select MATCH only when one exact leaf beats its strongest sibling. If the quality
construct is explicit but neighboring leaves cannot be separated, use
MATCH_FAMILY_ONLY.

- a30 is broad legal-analysis quality: rule synthesis, law-to-fact application,
  explicit reasoning, counterarguments, persuasion, and defensible conclusions.
  a60 is narrower candor/accuracy/evidentiary grounding: misstated facts or
  authorities, unsupported record assertions, omitted adverse authority, or
  inaccurate characterization. General claims that something is untrue or
  unpersuasive do not establish a60 without explicit record/evidence/fact/statute/
  authority/support grounding.
- a63 is use and development of authority or precedent. a79 requires an actual
  analogy/distinction based on materially similar or different facts. a85 is complete
  spotting, preservation, or identification of issues/sub-issues. A cited decision
  offered against an assertion is normally a63 unless an explicit fact-pattern
  analogy is performed.
- a17 concerns document-type/forum conventions and the assigned legal product's
  scope or register. a44 concerns tailoring to a particular jurisdiction, court,
  judge, or docket; a request for jurisdiction needed to give forum-specific advice
  can instantiate a44.
- a41 is terminology/definition clarity and consistent legal-term use; it beats a30
  when the explicit defect is what a named category or term means. a59 is candid
  acknowledgment of uncertainty or knowledge limits and restrained, fair advocacy.
- Declarative court/lawyer language can reveal a quality norm when it explicitly
  identifies failure to decide a material issue (a85), lack of record/evidentiary
  support (a60), need for material record development (a95), or incorrect legal
  application (a30). Do not reject it merely because it is phrased as a holding.
- For a60 versus a63, when a cited precedent is the operative answer, a63 remains a
  live sibling; do not choose a60 merely because the authority disproves an assertion.
- For a79 versus a85, a79 requires a precedent/fact-pattern analogy or distinction.
  A question surfacing an overlooked claimant, standing theory, issue, or sub-issue
  is a85 even if it says "similar theory."
- a95 includes an express need for additional factual or record development. a64
  includes evaluating how a concurrence/test works across future cases; a28 concerns
  articulation of a narrow, citable rule for uptake.
- A generic claim that a draft improved, a position is unconvincing, or a decision
  should be accepted does not identify an exact leaf unless context supplies the
  missing quality reason. Use GENERIC_VERDICT or CONTEXT_NEEDED rather than projecting
  an analysis metric.

For every MATCH, state why its exact definition beats the nearest sibling. A typed
abstention is a valid positive outcome; precision dominates yield.
