PEER-REVIEW verifier boundaries (precision-first)

Confirm only when the quoted reviewer language resolves the exact leaf. The aspect
hint and manuscript topic are never sufficient. When two overlapping peer-review
leaves remain genuinely possible, return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH rather than choosing the
more topical-sounding card.

- a0 is missing or inadequate methodological description, rationale, data/code,
  documentation, or information needed to verify/reproduce the work. a1 is an
  actually inappropriate, incorrect, uncontrolled, underpowered, or poorly executed
  design/analysis. A request to explain an ad-hoc choice or clarify what data/method
  was used is normally a0; do not confirm a1 from the word "method" alone.
- a43 is whether claims/contributions are supported by evidence, including a direct
  statement that contributions receive theoretical support. a66 is the coherence and
  integration of the theoretical framework itself. Do not substitute a66 merely
  because the support is theoretical.
- a72 covers whether the paper establishes motivation, background, need, and scope.
  A direct judgment that work is well or poorly motivated is not a bank gap.
- a10 covers discussion/positioning against related work and comparison with strong,
  relevant, or current baselines/models. Missing comparison or reliance on dated
  comparison models is not automatically a methods or generalization leaf.
- a16 covers adequacy/representativeness of sampled populations or data sources,
  including an evaluation limited to one dataset. a6/a47 cover explicitly stated
  applicability or transportability beyond studied settings. Because a6 and a47
  substantially overlap, reject or mark ambiguous unless the proposal wins from the
  exact definition and wording; do not infer generalizability solely from dataset
  diversity.
- a29 is direct technical correctness, logical soundness, or argumentative coherence.
  A bare judgment that a paper is technically sound can instantiate a29. Missing
  exposition alone is a0/a11, not a29.
- Novelty leaves overlap. a74 requires novelty positioned or contrasted against prior
  work; a73 is substantive originality/conceptual advance and significance. A bare
  "novel" or "incremental" fragment that does not resolve this contrast should be
  AMBIGUOUS_MATCH, not confidently confirmed.
- Undifferentiated "good performance," "insightful results," "important concern," or
  similar praise/blame without a bank-defined quality dimension must not be forced to
  a performance, significance, or correctness metric. Use the appropriate generic,
  context-needed, or no-fit abstention.

For a wrong proposal, use BETTER_CANDIDATE only if a visible alternative decisively
wins; otherwise abstain. CONFIRM_MATCH requires the proposal to beat its strongest
visible sibling, not merely share vocabulary.
