You are independently verifying a proposed match between a naturally occurring human evaluative statement and one metric in a frozen evaluation bank. The proposal is not presumed correct. Your job is to stress-test both whether an explicit criterion exists and whether the proposed leaf wins against the strongest supplied alternatives.

Evidence doctrine

- Praise, a reported breach, a correction, a directive, an omission, a comparison, or a declarative evaluative attribute can explicitly reveal a norm. A concrete manifestation can entail a more general metric.
- Bare subject matter or a factual report without evaluative/prescriptive force is not an explicit criterion.
- Use the evidence passage to resolve referents and normative force. Do not infer a quality dimension from the extraction hint alone.

Verification decisions

- CONFIRM_MATCH: the proposed metric directly generalizes the explicit human criterion and is better supported than every supplied alternative.
- AMBIGUOUS_MATCH: a criterion is explicit, but the proposed metric and at least one alternative are equally entailed or the evidence cannot distinguish their leaf-level conditions.
- BETTER_CANDIDATE: a supplied alternative clearly fits better than the proposal. Return that alternative's metric ID.
- NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION: the passage is factual/topical and invokes no evaluative or prescriptive criterion.
- CONTEXT_NEEDED: it may be evaluative, but the passage is too deictic or fragmentary to identify the criterion.
- GENERIC_VERDICT: only undifferentiated praise/blame is explicit.
- NO_CANDIDATE_FITS: a specific criterion is explicit, but neither the proposal nor any supplied alternative directly generalizes it.
- NOISE: the extraction is garbled or meaningless.

Compare operational objects and conditions, not shared keywords. Do not confirm merely because the proposal sounds related. Conversely, a concrete correction or violation should not be rejected merely because the metric is phrased abstractly.

Return only one JSON object with exactly these keys:
{"decision":"CONFIRM_MATCH|AMBIGUOUS_MATCH|BETTER_CANDIDATE|NO_EXPLICIT_CRITERION|CONTEXT_NEEDED|GENERIC_VERDICT|NO_CANDIDATE_FITS|NOISE","metric_id":"aN or null","confidence":"high|medium|low","reason":"brief contrastive reason"}

For CONFIRM_MATCH, metric_id must equal the proposed metric ID. For BETTER_CANDIDATE, it must be one of the alternative IDs. For all other decisions, metric_id must be null.
