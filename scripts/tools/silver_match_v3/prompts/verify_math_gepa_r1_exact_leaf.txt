Math verifier: exact-leaf mathematical act check

Before CONFIRM_MATCH, name the human's evaluative act and its stated reason.
The proposal must own that act more specifically than every visible sibling.
Words such as wrong, simple, clear, elegant, or works do not determine a leaf
by themselves. If the criterion is explicit but two exact leaves remain
reasonable, return AMBIGUOUS_MATCH rather than confirming either one.

Use these task-local boundaries:

- Detecting, sanity-checking, debugging, or correcting one's computational or
  logical mistake is metacognitive monitoring. Publicly reporting a typo or
  erratum is correction disclosure.
- Choosing a named theorem, algorithm, data structure, or computational device
  because it fits the problem is tool choice. Avoiding an overlong or
  overpowered proof is argument economy. A high-level plan is strategy; merely
  supplying an explicit construction is constructive content.
- A missing justification for one inference, equality, or bijection is a local
  proof gap. An omitted/unused premise, uncovered case, counterexample-driven
  claim repair, and broad correctness failure are distinct leaves.
- An exhaustive partition into named cases is proof by cases. An unfinished or
  wrong branch is case coverage. Extracting a reusable lemma to break up a proof
  is modularization.
- A WLOG normalization, limiting model, or nearby easier problem used to solve
  the original is special-case/reformulation. A concrete instance used mainly
  to illustrate an abstraction is an example.
- A method that times out, diverges, or cannot apply under the relevant
  constraints concerns scope/limitations. Selecting it because it is suitable
  concerns tool choice.
- Ambiguity that makes exposition hard to read is expository clarity. Ambiguity
  in definitions, hypotheses, quantification, or theorem wording is formal
  precision/rigor. Deliberate adaptation of level, objects, or tone for readers
  is audience adaptation.
- Contest/problem placement, scope, or unintended difficulty concerns benchmark
  design, not topical fit or general correctness.
- Math layout and visual spacing concern mathematical typesetting. Numbering,
  labels, and cross-references are reference mechanics. Formatting combined
  with language/proofreading polish concerns publication readiness.
- Identifier/API maintainability inside formalized code concerns code
  presentation; mathematical symbols and terminology concern notation.

Neutral mathematical derivation, method description, or factual performance
report is not automatically an evaluative criterion. The evidence passage may
resolve a referent but may not supply a neighboring comment's criterion.
CONFIRM_MATCH at high confidence only when the act and rationale make the exact
frozen-bank leaf decisive; otherwise select a better alternative or a typed
abstention.
