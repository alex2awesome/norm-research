# Medium: The Pivot Announcement Press Release

## Why Pivot Announcements Are Tricky
- Confessing prior approach didn't work
- Risk of investor confidence
- Customer migration challenges
- Employee morale considerations
- Competitor positioning opportunity

## When to Announce a Pivot Publicly
- Existing customers need transition information
- Funding rounds tied to new direction
- New target market needs awareness
- Building credibility through honesty
- Hiring needs new positioning

## When NOT to Announce Publicly
- Quiet pivot mid-stealth
- Internal restructuring without external impact
- Customer base too small to matter
- Investor-only communication needed

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Company] Pivots from [Old Focus] to [New Focus]"
Or
"[Company] Sharpens Focus on [New Direction]"

### Lede
"[Company] today announced a strategic pivot from [old focus] to [new focus], driven by [specific market insight]. The company will [specific actions] over the next [timeframe]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: What's Changing**
- Specific old focus / specific new focus
- Timeline of transition
- Geographic or segment changes

**Para 2: Why Now**
- Market insight that drove the decision
- Customer feedback patterns
- Competitive landscape shifts
- Internal data findings

**Para 3: What This Means For Customers**
- Existing customer transition plan
- Support for migration
- Pricing implications
- Timeline for changes

**Para 4: What This Means For Employees**
- Roles affected
- New hiring areas
- Cultural continuity
- Leadership team

**Para 5: Quote**
- CEO honest about the journey
- Specific market insight
- Acknowledgment of customers' patience
- Vision for new direction

### Boilerplate
- Updated company description
- New mission articulated
- Maintained context (founded date, scale)

## Honesty as Strategy
- Acknowledge what didn't work
- Specific learnings cited
- Avoid blame on others
- Frame as informed decision
- Invite scrutiny on new direction

## What Works in Pivot Announcements
- Concrete data driving decision
- Customer voice acknowledging change
- Investor support quote
- Roadmap clarity
- Honest acknowledgment of challenges

## Quality Rubric for Pivots
- Honest framing of change
- Specific market insight cited
- Customer impact addressed
- Employee impact addressed
- Substantive CEO quote
- Roadmap clarity
- Investor support visible
