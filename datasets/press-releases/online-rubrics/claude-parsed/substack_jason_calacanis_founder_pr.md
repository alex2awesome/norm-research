# Jason Calacanis: The Founder PR Playbook

## Why Founders Should Care About PR
- Builds asymmetric trust before scale
- Creates inbound pipeline (talent, customers, investors)
- Differentiates in crowded market
- Provides social proof for cold outreach

## When NOT to Do PR
- You haven't validated product-market fit
- You don't want competitor attention
- You're in stealth for legitimate reasons
- Your product doesn't work yet
- Your story isn't different from competitors

## My Founder PR Rules
1. Be your own PR person at first — no agency until $5M ARR
2. Build relationships with 10-20 reporters BEFORE you need coverage
3. Always offer exclusives, never mass-blast
4. Tell the truth — embellishment kills you in 6 months
5. Reporters remember founders who DIDN'T overhype

## The Founder Email Template
- Subject: News + your name + company
- Para 1: The news in one sentence
- Para 2: Why this matters
- Para 3: What I can offer (call, exclusive, demo)
- Sign off with phone number
- Total: under 150 words

## Story Angles That Work for Startups
- Counter-intuitive market insight you discovered
- Big-name customer/partner reveal
- Pivot story (with honest learnings)
- Hiring announcement (only if person is famous)
- Funding (Series A+, mention investors specifically)
- Data report from your unique data set

## Mistakes Founders Make
- Hiring expensive PR agency too early
- Asking for "coverage" generally, not pitching specific story
- Following up too aggressively
- Burning embargo
- Lying or exaggerating numbers
- Pitching the same reporter the same story repeatedly

## Quality Rubric for Founder PR
- Real news worth telling
- Personalized pitch, not blast
- Founder voice, not PR-speak
- Honest numbers
- Exclusive offered first
- Phone number provided
