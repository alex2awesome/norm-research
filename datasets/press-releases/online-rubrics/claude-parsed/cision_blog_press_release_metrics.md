# Cision Insights: Press Release Metrics That Matter

## What to Measure
### Distribution Metrics
- Total impressions
- Geographic reach
- Industry vertical coverage
- Device type (mobile vs desktop)

### Engagement Metrics
- Click-through rate to landing page
- Time on page
- Scroll depth
- Multimedia view rate
- Social shares

### Pickup Metrics
- Direct republications (verbatim)
- Stories citing the release
- Quotes used in coverage
- Headlines that match release language
- Backlinks generated

### Business Metrics
- Inbound leads attributable to release
- Demo requests in 7-day window
- Sales pipeline impact
- Recruitment inbound

## Benchmarks Across Industries
- Tech average: 12-15 pickups per release
- Healthcare: 8-10 (regulated language)
- Consumer brands: 18-22
- B2B SaaS: 6-8 (niche audience)
- Nonprofit: 14-16 (mission-driven coverage)

## What Drives Higher Pickup Rates
- Multimedia attached (1.5x lift)
- Industry-trending keyword in headline (2x lift)
- Quote from C-suite (1.3x lift)
- Distribution timed Tue-Thu morning (1.4x lift)
- Embargo offered to top outlets (1.8x lift)

## Common Measurement Mistakes
- Focusing only on impressions (vanity metric)
- Not tracking pipeline impact
- Comparing across industries inappropriately
- Missing brand mention without link
- Not tracking long-tail SEO benefits

## Reporting Framework
- Distribution + reach (top of funnel)
- Engagement (middle)
- Pickup + sentiment (earned media)
- Business outcomes (bottom of funnel)

## Quality Rubric for Measurable Releases
- Tagged with UTM parameters
- Multimedia tracked separately
- Sentiment analysis enabled
- Pipeline tracking integrated
- Long-tail search visibility monitored
- Quarter-over-quarter benchmarking
