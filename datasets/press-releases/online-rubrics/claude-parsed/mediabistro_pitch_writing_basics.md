# MediaBistro: Pitch Writing Basics

## The Anatomy of a Successful Pitch

### Subject Line Rules
- 6-10 words ideal
- Front-load the news
- Avoid promotional language
- Test against "would I open this?"
- No misleading "exclusive" claims

### Salutation Rules
- First name only ("Hi Sarah")
- Never "Dear Reporter" or "[FIRST NAME]"
- Make sure name is spelled correctly
- Verify the byline is current

### Opening Sentence Rules
- Reference specific recent work
- Demonstrate you've read them
- Avoid "Hope you're doing well"
- Avoid "I came across your article"
- Be specific: "Loved your piece on X published Tuesday"

### Body Rules
- One news item per pitch
- Story angle clear within 2 sentences
- Concrete numbers where possible
- Source credentials in 1 sentence
- Visual assets linked, not attached

### Closing Rules
- Single clear ask (interview? data? exclusive?)
- Time-bound offer (today, this week, by Friday)
- Phone number + calendar link
- Backup contact if you'll be unavailable

## Word Count Limits
- Subject line: 6-10 words
- Body: 100-200 words total
- Total email: under 250 words

## Pitch Patterns That Work
**The Data Pitch**
"We surveyed 1,000 X about Y. The surprising finding: [specific stat]. Full report attached. Available for interview."

**The Trend Pitch**
"Following your piece on [trend], we have data showing [related insight]. CEO available for follow-up."

**The Source Pitch**
"For your ongoing coverage of [beat], [Name] at [Company] is uniquely positioned to comment on [topic]. 30 years of expertise in [area]."

**The Exclusive Pitch**
"Offering you first look at [news] before broader release. Embargo until [date/time]. Phone interview available now."

## Quality Rubric (MediaBistro)
- Subject line under 10 words, news-first
- Personalized opening with specific reference
- One news item only
- Story angle clear in first 2 sentences
- Single time-bound ask
- Visual assets linked
- Phone number + backup contact
