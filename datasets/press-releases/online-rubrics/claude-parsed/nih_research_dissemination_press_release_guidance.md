# NIH Press Release Coordination and Research Dissemination Guidance

## NIH Acknowledgment Requirement
Each publication, press release, or other document about research supported by an NIH award must include an acknowledgment of NIH award support.

## NIH Coordination Process

### Before Issuing
- Notify NIH communications offices
- May highlight release on NIH websites
- Coordinate with funding institute/center
- Allow advance review

### Required Elements
- NIH institute/center name
- Grant number
- Specific disclaimer language
- "The content is solely the responsibility of the authors and does not necessarily represent the official views of the National Institutes of Health."

### Strategic Recommendations
- Mention NIH early in press release text
- Consider including quote from NIH expert
- Provides outside perspective on significance
- Adds credibility

## Empirical Findings on Public Health Dissemination
- 33% of public health researchers use press releases as dissemination method
- Same rate use media interviews
- Press releases are core dissemination tool

## Innovative Dissemination Methods (NIH-supported research)
Beyond traditional press releases:
- Nonspecialist summaries
- Blog posts
- Visual abstracts
- Video abstracts
- Infographics
- Podcasts
- Social media campaigns
- Plain language summaries

## Implicit Quality Rubric for NIH-Funded Research Press Releases

A press release for NIH-funded research:
1. Acknowledges NIH support (institute + grant number)
2. Includes required disclaimer
3. Coordinated with NIH PIO before release
4. Cites peer-reviewed publication (DOI)
5. Plain language for general audience
6. Health literacy considered (Grade 8 reading)
7. Distinguishes findings from speculation
8. Includes researcher contact
9. NIH expert quote (where appropriate)
10. Multimedia (researcher photos, lab images)
11. Visual/video abstracts available
12. Honest about clinical applicability
13. Notes study design (RCT, observational, animal)
14. Confidence intervals included
15. Effect sizes in absolute terms
16. Available for follow-up (researcher + PIO)

## Special Considerations
- NCI for cancer research
- NIMH for mental health

- Coordinate with appropriate IC
