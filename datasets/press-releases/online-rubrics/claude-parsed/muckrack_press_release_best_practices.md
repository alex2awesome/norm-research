# Muck Rack - Press Release Best Practices

## Core Principles

### Crafting Press Releases
- Always lead with a hook
- Use quotes to add insight (not fluff)
- Optimize headlines for search engines and readers
- Distribute via targeted pitches AND wire services

### What Makes an Effective Release
- Highlights newsworthy announcement clearly
- Explains why it matters
- Shows relevance to target audience
- Easy for journalists to use

## Make Journalists' Jobs Easier

### Required Elements
- Contact information (visible, complete)
- Boilerplate company description at end
- Multimedia in shared folders (videos, headshots, logos)

### Reduce Friction
- Minimize need for follow-up questions
- Anticipate journalist needs
- Provide pre-vetted facts
- Offer expert availability

## Distribution Strategy

### Two-Pronged Approach
- **Online distribution**: Helps with SEO and AI-generated answers
- **Personalized outreach**: Sparks coverage and follow-up

### Relevance Over Reach
- Target relevant journalists
- Quality over quantity
- Match release to beat
- Customize for outlet

## Avoid Pitfalls

### Common Mistakes
- Overhyping (undermines trust)
- Generic mass distribution
- Missing contact info
- No multimedia
- No clear news value

## Measurement

### Basic Metrics
- Opens
- Engagement
- Coverage pickups

### Advanced Metrics
- Which journalists responded
- Follow-up questions asked
- Click-throughs
- Audience reach

## Implicit Quality Rubric (Muck Rack)
1. Strong hook in first 30 words
2. Quote adds insight
3. Headline SEO-optimized
4. Newsworthy (passes "so what" test)
5. Relevance to specific audience clear
6. Contact info complete and accurate
7. Boilerplate present
8. Multimedia provided in folder
9. Targeted distribution plan
10. Personalized outreach component
11. Measurement plan in place
12. Embargo discipline
13. Avoids overhype
14. Pre-answered likely questions
15. Expert available for follow-up
