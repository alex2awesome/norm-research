# Ryan Holiday: Trust Me I'm Lying — PR Tactics Exposed

## The Media Manipulation Cycle (Critical View)
1. Trade media (small, specialized) covers a story
2. Mid-tier media picks it up to amplify
3. Top-tier media validates it as "news"
4. Story shapes public discourse regardless of accuracy

## What Holiday Reveals About PR Tactics
- "News" is often planted by PR through small outlets
- Embargo-and-exclusive is manipulation tool
- Anonymous sources often = strategic leaks
- "Trending" can be manufactured
- "Confidential" memos are sometimes deliberate leaks

## Ethical Reflections
- The line between "PR" and "manipulation" is thin
- Founders have responsibility to journalists' time
- Honest framing builds long-term reputation
- Manipulative tactics work short-term, fail long-term
- Reputation is the most valuable asset

## What Honest PR Looks Like
- Real news, not manufactured stories
- Verifiable claims with methodology
- Named sources with clear stakes disclosed
- Acknowledgment of limitations
- Long-term relationships over short-term wins

## Tactics to Avoid (From Holiday's Critique)
- Planting fake stories in small outlets to bait larger ones
- Using anonymous sources to attack competitors
- Manufactured controversies for attention
- Embargo offered without actual exclusivity
- "News" that's actually advertorial content

## What Reporters Wish PR Would Stop Doing
- Pitching stories that aren't real news
- Using emotional manipulation tactics
- Burning embargoes when convenient
- Hiding spokesperson conflicts of interest
- Pitching the same "exclusive" to multiple outlets

## The Honest PR Manifesto
- Tell the truth, even when uncomfortable
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Make sources genuinely available
- Honor embargoes religiously
- Build relationships, not transactions
- Long-term reputation over short-term coverage

## Quality Rubric (Ethical PR)
- Real news, not manufactured
- Honest framing throughout
- Conflicts disclosed
- Named sources with credentials
- Embargoes honored
- Long-term reputation focus
