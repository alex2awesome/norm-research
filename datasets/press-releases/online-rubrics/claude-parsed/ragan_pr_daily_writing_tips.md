# Ragan PR Daily: Press Release Writing Best Practices

## The Modern Press Release Mindset
- Write for journalists, not search engines
- Optimize for excerpting, not republishing
- Assume mobile reading
- Plan for AI summarization (your release will be scraped)
- Write in newsroom voice, not marketing voice

## Headline Best Practices
- Lead with the news, not the brand
- 80 characters or less
- Avoid jargon and buzzwords
- Use active voice
- Test with the "would I tweet this?" filter
- No clickbait — wires reject

## Lede Paragraph Standards
- Compress 5 W's into 50 words
- Concrete numbers when possible
- Direct subject-verb-object structure
- Avoid throat-clearing ("Today, we are pleased...")
- Front-load most newsworthy element

## Quote Best Practices
- 25-35 words ideal
- Spokesperson should sound human
- Add information, don't repeat headline
- Avoid superlatives without proof
- Attribute to named C-level executive
- Real quote from real conversation when possible

## Body Paragraph Structure
- One idea per paragraph
- Inverted pyramid: most important first
- Subheaders for scannability
- Active voice throughout
- 3-4 paragraphs max in body

## Boilerplate Best Practices
- 50-75 words maximum
- Founded date, headquarters
- Mission in one sentence
- Key metric (customers, revenue, scope)
- Website URL
- Avoid jargon-heavy descriptions

## Contact Information Standards
- Named individual, not "media@"
- Direct phone number
- Direct email
- Available time zone if applicable
- Backup contact if primary unavailable

## Final Format Requirements
- "###" end marker
- Single space after periods
- AP style throughout
- No emojis or special characters
- PDF version + plain text version

## Implicit Quality Rubric (Ragan)
- News-first headline
- 50-word lede with 5 W's
- 25-35 word substantive quote
- Inverted pyramid body
- 75-word boilerplate
- Named contact with phone
- AP style
