# Press Release Structure, Dateline and Format Checklist

## Standard Structure

### Required Components in Order
1. Letterhead/logo
2. Contact information (right margin)
3. Release timing notice
4. Headline (centered, bold)
5. Subheadline (deck)
6. Dateline + lead paragraph
7. Body paragraphs (3-4 typical)
8. Quotes (1-2)
9. Boilerplate
10. End marker (### or -30-)

### Release Timing Notice
- "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" - all caps, top left
- OR "EMBARGOED UNTIL [Date and Time]"
- Aligned to the left

### Dateline AP Style Format
- City name in ALL CAPS
- State abbreviation (when required)
- Date with month spelled out
- Day as number (no st/nd/th)
- Year
- Em dash before story
- Example: "WASHINGTON, March 15, 2026 — Acme Corp today announced..."

### State Abbreviations (AP Style)
- Use AP-specific abbreviations (Calif., not CA)
- Major cities stand alone (no state needed): NEW YORK, LOS ANGELES, CHICAGO, BOSTON, etc.
- International cities: include country if not well-known

### Lead Paragraph
- 25-35 words
- Answers 5Ws + H
- Most newsworthy first
- Active voice
- One sentence ideal, max two

### Body Paragraphs
- 3-4 paragraphs typical
- Each 1-3 sentences
- One idea per paragraph
- Inverted pyramid order
- Includes quotes
- Total 400-500 words

### Boilerplate
- Final paragraph
- "About [Company]" header
- 50-100 words
- Third person
- Mission, scope, contact URL

### Contact Information
- Top right of release
- Name, title
- Phone (with area code)
- Email
- Time zone
- Available hours

### End Markers
- ### centered
- OR -30- centered
- Indicates release is over

## Implicit Format Checklist (Pass/Fail)

- [ ] Letterhead/logo present
- [ ] Contact info top right
- [ ] "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or embargo notice
- [ ] Headline centered, bold, ≤100 chars
- [ ] Subheadline present
- [ ] Dateline in correct AP format
- [ ] Lead answers 5Ws in 25-35 words
- [ ] At least one quote with attribution
- [ ] AP style throughout
- [ ] Inverted pyramid order
- [ ] No spelling/grammar errors
- [ ] Length 300-500 words
- [ ] Boilerplate at end
- [ ] ### or -30- marker
- [ ] Page numbers (if multi-page)
- [ ] Standard fonts (Times/Arial/Calibri)
- [ ] Body 10-12pt, headline 14-16pt
