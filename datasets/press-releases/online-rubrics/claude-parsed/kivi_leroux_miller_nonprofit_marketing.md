# Kivi Leroux Miller: Nonprofit Press Release Best Practices

## Modern Nonprofit Communications
- Mobile-first audience
- Social media as primary discovery channel
- Email newsletter as relationship builder
- Press release one tool among many
- Cross-channel coordination essential

## Press Release Tests for Nonprofits

**The Stakeholder Test**
- Does this matter to donors?
- Does this matter to beneficiaries?
- Does this matter to volunteers?
- Does this matter to the broader community?
- Does this matter to peer nonprofits?

**The Action Test**
- What do you want readers to do?
- Donate? Volunteer? Attend? Share?
- Single clear call-to-action

## Story Types
- Impact reporting (with specific metrics)
- Campaign launches (with goals)
- Beneficiary stories (with permission)
- Partner announcements
- Crisis response
- Annual reports
- Major gift recognition

## Writing Best Practices
- Headline: impact-first, not mission-first
- Lede: who, what, when, where, why, how
- Body: methodology, quotes, proof points
- Quote: from beneficiary AND from leadership
- Boilerplate: mission, scale, impact metrics
- Contact: communications staff member

## Visual Storytelling
- Photos of beneficiaries (with consent)
- Infographics showing impact
- Video testimonials when possible
- Before/after images
- Impact maps for geographic work

## Distribution Strategy
- Local press first
- Sector publications second
- Wire syndication for SEO
- Social media amplification
- Email newsletter inclusion
- Partner organization sharing

## Common Nonprofit PR Mistakes
- Too much mission language, not enough news
- Beneficiary stories without permission
- Vague impact claims ("transformed lives")
- No multimedia
- Generic timing without news cycle awareness

## Quality Rubric (Kivi Leroux Miller)
- Stakeholder relevance demonstrated
- Single clear call-to-action
- Specific impact metrics
- Multimedia (photos, infographics)
- Beneficiary voice with consent
- Cross-channel coordination
- News-cycle awareness
