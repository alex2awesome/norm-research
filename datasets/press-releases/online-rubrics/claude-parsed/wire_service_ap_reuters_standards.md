# Wire Service Standards (AP, Reuters) - Implications for Press Releases

## Editorial Standards That Press Releases Should Meet

### Sourcing Standards
- Multi-source verification required
- Named sources preferred
- Disclose when information from single party
- Independent verification of claims

### Writing Standards (Reuters Manual)
"Simple, direct language which can be assimilated quickly, which goes straight to the heart of the matter, and in which, as a general rule, facts are marshalled in logical sequence according to their relative importance."

### Style Standards
- AP Stylebook is American journalism standard
- AP libel guidelines
- AP state abbreviations
- AP standardized writing conventions

### Correction Policies (Reuters)
- Transparent about errors
- Rectify promptly and clearly
- Don't disguise or bury corrections in subsequent stories

### Neutrality and Objectivity
- Single objective news feed to all subscribers
- Avoid overt partiality
- Demonstrably correct information

## Implications for Press Releases

### A press release likely to be picked up by wire services must:

#### Verifiability
- All facts checkable
- Multiple sources for major claims
- Named sources where possible
- Documents linked

#### Clarity
- Simple, direct language
- Logical sequence
- Most important first
- No marketing-speak

#### Accuracy
- All names spelled correctly
- All titles current
- All dates accurate
- All numbers verified

#### Style Compliance
- AP style throughout
- Standard formatting
- Professional appearance
- No errors

#### Neutrality
- No partisan framing
- No promotional language
- Objective tone
- Balanced presentation

## Wire Service Distribution Considerations

### What Wire Services Look For
- Newsworthiness (genuine news value)
- Clarity (no ambiguity)
- Accuracy (verifiable)
- Brevity (tight)
- Relevance (target audience)
- Visuals (often required)

### How to Get on the Wire
- Distribute through Reg FD-compliant channels
- Use major distribution services (BusinessWire, PR Newswire)
- Direct relationships with wire bureau chiefs
- Emerging: AI-mediated distribution

## Implicit Wire Service Quality Rubric

A press release passes wire service standards if:
1. AP style compliant
2. All facts verifiable
3. Multiple sources cited
4. Plain language throughout
5. No errors of fact, grammar, or spelling
6. Logical sequencing
7. Brief but complete
8. Visuals provided
9. Named sources
10. Genuinely newsworthy
11. Neutral tone
12. Multiple distribution channels
13. Local angles for regional bureaus
14. Embargoed appropriately
15. Provided with documents/data files
