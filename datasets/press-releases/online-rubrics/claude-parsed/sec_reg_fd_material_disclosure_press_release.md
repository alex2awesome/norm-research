# SEC Regulation FD - Material Press Release Standards

## Overview
The SEC adopted Regulation FD (Fair Disclosure) in August 2000 to address selective disclosure of material nonpublic information to securities analysts and institutional investors before disclosing to the public.

## Core Requirements
- No person acting on behalf of a company may make an intentional disclosure of material, nonpublic information to market professionals or shareholders unless public disclosure is made simultaneously
- For unintentional disclosure: provide disclosure as soon as reasonably possible (within 24 hours or start of next day's trading, whichever later)

## Press Release as a Disclosure Method
Public disclosure can be made by issuing a press release through:
- Dow Jones
- Bloomberg
- Business Wire
- PR Newswire
- Reuters

Must be a "widely circulated news or wire service."

## Three-Step Process for Planned Material Disclosure
1. First issue a press release distributed through regular channels
2. Provide adequate notice of a scheduled conference call
3. Hold the conference call in an open manner permitting investors to listen in

## What Counts as "Material"
Material information includes (non-exhaustive):
- Earnings information
- Mergers, acquisitions, tender offers, joint ventures, change in assets
- New products or discoveries, new contracts
- Changes in control or management
- Change in auditors
- Events regarding securities (defaults, redemptions, splits, dividends)
- Bankruptcies or receivership

## Implicit Rubric for Material Press Releases
- Distribute through SEC-approved channels (Reg FD-compliant wire services)
- Time release before any selective disclosure
- Be specific about earnings figures (per Reg G - non-GAAP reconciliation)
- File 8-K within four business days of material event
- Avoid forward-looking statements without safe harbor language

## Standard Sections in IR Press Releases
- Headline
- Summary bullets
- Lead paragraph with material news
- Body with details
- Quote from CEO
- Forward-looking statements safe harbor
- Reconciliation of non-GAAP measures (if used)
- About the company
- Investor relations contact
- Media contact
