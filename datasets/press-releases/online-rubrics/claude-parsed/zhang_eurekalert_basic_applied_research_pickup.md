# Determinants of Science Press Release Pickup

## Primary Findings (synthesized from multiple sources)

### The biggest single factor in press coverage
Publishing a press release. Press releases or press conferences were the initial source of ideas for 40-50% of news stories, and 45% of health news stories did not go beyond a news release.

### Subject Matter Effects
Media coverage is often determined more by the subject matter than by scientific importance:
- Personal health and climate change consistently get more coverage
- Cell biology and applied mathematics get less
- Public is interested in research with direct practical applications and societal benefits
- Applied research outperforms basic research in pickup

### Journal Prestige
- 6 of the top 10 journals in press coverage are health-focused
- Nature, Science, Cell, NEJM, Lancet, JAMA - high prestige drives coverage
- Lower-tier journals get much less pickup

### Press Release Quality
A good press release substantially increases pickup chances. Implicit factors:
- Accessible language
- Strong narrative angle
- Clear "so what?" framing
- Visual assets

### Newsworthy Elements
Press releases are more likely to be covered when they involve:
- "Records" - first, largest, oldest, most distant
- "Sexy topics" - black holes, exoplanets, cancer cures, AI breakthroughs
- Counter-intuitive findings
- Personal/relatable stories
- Health or environment relevance

### Institutional Factors
- Top universities (Harvard, MIT, Stanford) get disproportionate pickup
- PIO experience matters
- Press office reputation matters
- Embargo timing alignment with journal publication

## Implicit Pickup Rubric
A press release is more likely to be picked up if:
1. Topic is health- or environment-related
2. Published in a high-prestige journal
3. Has practical/societal application
4. Contains a "first" or "record"
5. Has compelling visual assets
6. Has accessible language
7. Comes from a prestigious institution
8. Has a clear human-interest angle
9. Is timely with current news cycle
10. Has video or interview availability
