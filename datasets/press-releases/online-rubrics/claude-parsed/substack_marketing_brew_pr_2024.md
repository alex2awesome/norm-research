# Marketing Brew: The State of the Press Release in 2024

## Who Still Reads Press Releases
- Wire scrapers (AI summarization tools)
- Trade press in regulated industries
- Local TV news producers (still!)
- Industry analysts compiling reports
- SEO tools and aggregators

## Who Doesn't Read Press Releases
- General business reporters at major outlets
- Podcast hosts
- YouTube creators / influencers
- Social-native journalists
- Most consumer beat reporters

## What Has Changed
- Wire services bought by financial data companies (Cision, Intrado)
- AI summarization makes "leading provider" boilerplate worthless
- LinkedIn newsletter takes role of traditional press release for B2B
- TikTok-first product launches skip press entirely
- "Founder mode" announcements come from CEO Twitter, not PR

## What Hasn't Changed
- Need for newsworthy substance
- Embargo etiquette
- Spokesperson availability requirement
- Visual assets needed
- Verification of facts

## Specific 2024 Best Practices
- Distribute to LinkedIn FIRST (newsletter and posts)
- Co-launch with podcast appearance same day
- Make founder do TikTok/Reel on launch day
- Use first-person "I" voice in CEO blog post
- Save the wire for institutional credibility, not reach

## What's Replaced the Press Release
- Founder X/Twitter thread
- Company Substack newsletter
- LinkedIn carousel post
- Podcast appearance
- Investor letter posted publicly

## Quality Rubric for Modern Releases
- Substantive news, not vanity announcement
- Distributed across modern channels
- CEO/founder visible voice
- Multimedia for social-native consumption
- LinkedIn-first or podcast-paired
- Wire for SEO credibility, not reach
