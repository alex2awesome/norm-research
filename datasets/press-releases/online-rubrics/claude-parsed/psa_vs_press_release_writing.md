# PSA vs Press Release Writing Guidelines

## Purpose Distinction

### Press Release
- Designed to attract journalists, writers, media outlets
- Promotes company, product, or service
- Announces newsworthy events
- Distributed to gatekeepers (reporters, editors)

### Public Service Announcement (PSA)
- Benefits the general public
- Raises awareness about issues
- Not promoting a product/service
- Informs or inspires action on:
  - Health
  - Safety
  - Community events
  - Public welfare

## Format and Length Differences

### Press Release
- Longer and more detailed (300-500 words typical)
- Comprehensive information
- Background and context

### PSA
- Concise and to the point
- Often limited to 30 seconds for broadcast
- 35-70 words for print
- 15-second radio = ~38 words
- 30-second radio = ~75 words
- Sentences 12-15 words each

## Writing Style

### Press Release
- Inverted pyramid structure
- Most important info first
- 5Ws + H in lead
- Reads like a news story

### PSA
- Clear single message
- Call to action focus
- Less storytelling, more informing
- Motivates public action
- Repeats key info (URL, phone, date)

## Constraint for PSAs
- Can reference your company as sponsor/underwriter
- Should NEVER blatantly promote organization
- If too promotional, producers/editors send to advertising
- Must serve public interest first

## When to Use Each

### Press Release
- Launching a product
- Major company news
- Research findings
- Awards
- Events to be covered

### PSA
- Health awareness campaigns
- Safety messages
- Donation drives
- Community events
- Crisis information

## Implicit Rubric for PSAs
- Public benefit clear
- Single, simple message
- Call to action specific
- Contact info repeated
- No promotion of sponsor
- Time-appropriate (15/30/60 seconds)
- Spoken aloud well (broadcast)
- Memorable phrasing
- Inclusive language
- Audio quality appropriate
