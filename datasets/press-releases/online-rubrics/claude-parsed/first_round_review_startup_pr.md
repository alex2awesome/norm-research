# First Round Review: The Startup PR Playbook

## Why Most Startup PR Fails
- Founders treat PR as validation, not strategy
- Hiring PR firms before product-market fit
- Pitching the wrong outlets with the wrong stories
- Being inconsistent — sporadic instead of sustained
- Misunderstanding what reporters actually want

## The First Round PR Framework

### Phase 1: Foundation (Pre-Launch)
- Build relationships with 10 reporters in your space
- Become a useful source on others' stories
- Develop your founder voice publicly
- Establish company website and newsroom

### Phase 2: Launch Moment
- Plan 3-6 months in advance
- Brief 2-3 trusted reporters under embargo
- Coordinate same-day distribution
- Prepare founder for media days

### Phase 3: Sustained PR
- Newsworthy moments every 4-6 weeks
- Data reports quarterly
- Customer wins as pitch material
- Industry commentary opportunities
- Founder thought leadership

## Story Types That Work for Startups
- Origin story (rich detail, founder voice)
- Customer transformation (with metrics)
- Industry data report (proprietary insight)
- Pivot story (with honest learnings)
- Hiring announcement (only if person is famous)
- Counter-intuitive market analysis

## How to Write a Startup Press Release
- Headline: News + specific number
- Lede: One sentence with the news; one sentence with stakes
- Body: Context, methodology, customer proof
- Quote: Founder voice, specific, no clichés
- Boilerplate: 50 words, what you do + scale
- Contact: Founder direct OR head of communications

## Distribution Strategy
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade outlet
- Coordinated release to 5-10 secondary outlets
- Wire for SEO (after initial coverage)
- Founder LinkedIn post same day
- Newsletter inclusion if relevant

## Common Mistakes
- Funding announcements with no use-of-funds clarity
- Quotes from CEO that say "we are excited"
- Customer logos without quotes or context
- Vague claims of being "first" or "largest"
- Wire-only distribution

## Quality Rubric (First Round)
- Sustained PR cadence, not one-shot
- Founder voice in quotes
- Specific story types matching startup stage
- Embargoed exclusives offered
- Founder available for interviews
- Honest, verifiable claims
