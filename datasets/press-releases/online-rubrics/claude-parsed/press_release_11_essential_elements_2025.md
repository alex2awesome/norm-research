# 11 Essential Elements of a Modern Press Release

## The Complete Component List

1. **Headline** - Concise statement of news (under 100 chars)
2. **Subhead** - Secondary supporting line, 1-2 sentences
3. **Summary** - Key highlights in 1-3 bullet points
4. **Opening Paragraph** - Answers what happened, when, where, why it matters (5Ws in 30 words)
5. **Details Section** - Expanded info and supporting data
6. **Quotes** - From executives or stakeholders
7. **Multimedia** - Images, video links, graphics, infographics
8. **Call to Action** - Clear next step
9. **Tags/Keywords** - Relevant categories for distribution
10. **Boilerplate** - Standard company description
11. **Contact Information** - Media contact details

## Modern (2025) Considerations
A modern press release template is structured for two audiences:
- Human journalists
- AI-driven discovery systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview)

## AI Search Optimization Elements
- Schema markup
- Q&A format sections
- Direct answer formatting
- Entity-rich content
- Citation-worthy data

## Required Format Elements
- Release date and originating city (dateline)
- "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or embargo notice
- Contact: name, title, phone, email, social handles
- Quote attribution: full name, title, organization
- Media link / press kit URL
- "###" or "-30-" at end (traditional end markers)

## Implicit Quality Rubric
A press release is high quality if:
- All 11 elements present
- Each element has substance (not filler)
- Headline + subhead + summary tell the story alone
- Body adds depth without redundancy
- Quote adds insight, not platitudes
- CTA is clear and actionable
- Boilerplate is current
- Contact is responsive
