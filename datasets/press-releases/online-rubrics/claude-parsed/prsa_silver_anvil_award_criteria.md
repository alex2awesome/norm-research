# PRSA Silver Anvil Award Criteria

## Overview
The Silver Anvil Awards celebrate the best strategic public relations campaigns of the year, as well as outstanding organizational excellence. Anvil Awards are PRSA's flagship industry recognition.

## Award Categories Most Relevant to Press Releases

### Media Relations Category
Tactics, programs and events driven entirely by media relations, including:
- Press releases
- Media advisories
- Pitch letters
- Requests for coverage
- One-page summary required with measurable objectives and results
- Media impact required

### Paid Promotions
- Sponsorships
- Paid placements
- Marketing communications complementing PR strategy
- Generate exceptional ROI
- Targeted and strategic
- Distinct purpose aligned with overall campaign

### Integrated Communications
- Creative and effective integrated campaigns
- Paid, earned, shared and owned efforts
- Meaningful PR/communications components
- Integration with other disciplines

## What Makes Winning Campaigns Stand Out

### Per 2002 Silver Anvil Study
Outstanding PR campaigns share these characteristics:
1. **Thorough research and benchmarking**
2. **Clear objectives**
3. **Research-based strategies**
4. **Documented results**

### 2025 Best of Silver Anvil Example
PRR + Washington State Department of Ecology - "Use Food Well":
- 126 partnership organizations
- 11+ million media impressions
- 31% behavior change in respondents
- 180 in-store sign locations
- Creative cooking demos by Chef Joel Gamoran

## RPIE Framework (PRSA-aligned)
1. **Research** - Benchmarks, audience insights
2. **Planning** - Strategy, objectives
3. **Implementation** - Tactics including press releases
4. **Evaluation** - Measurable outcomes

## Implicit Press Release Quality Rubric (Silver Anvil Standards)

A press release that contributes to award-winning campaigns:
1. Tied to research-based strategy
2. Has measurable objective
3. Targeted to specific audience
4. Aligned with broader campaign
5. Generates measurable media impact
6. Multiple impressions documented
7. Behavior change tracked
8. Partnership amplification
9. Multimedia integrated
10. Cross-channel coordination
11. Newsworthy hook
12. Story angle clear
13. Quotable spokesperson
14. Visual assets prepared
15. Documented outcomes

## Application to Press Release Pickup
Award-winning releases consistently demonstrate:
- Strategic targeting (not mass blast)
- Clear measurable goal
- Integration with broader campaign
- Quality content (not just volume)
- Documented impact
- Audience-specific framing
- Innovative tactics
- Measurable behavior change

## Sample Submission Requirements
- One-page summary with objectives and results
- Demonstrated alignment with strategy
- Clear measurable outcomes
- Examples of materials produced
- Media impact documentation
- Behavior change evidence
