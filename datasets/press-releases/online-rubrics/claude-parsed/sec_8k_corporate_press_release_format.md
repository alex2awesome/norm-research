# Corporate Press Release vs. Form 8-K

## Form 8-K Overview
Form 8-K provides investors with current information for informed decisions. Public companies must file Form 8-K within four business days of a material event.

## Material Events Requiring 8-K
- Acquisitions or asset sales
- Bankruptcy
- Executive changes (CEO, CFO, directors)
- Auditor changes
- Delisting notices
- Material agreements (entry/termination)
- Direct financial obligations
- Off-balance-sheet arrangements
- Costs of restructuring
- Material impairments
- Earnings releases
- Regulation FD disclosures
- Code of ethics changes
- Amendments to bylaws

## Press Release vs. 8-K Distinction

### Form 8-K
- Regulatory filing with SEC
- Specific format requirements
- Standardized item numbers
- Filed within 4 business days
- Available on EDGAR

### Press Release
- Company's own narrative
- Marketing-aware framing
- Distributed via wire services
- Reaches financial press
- Often included as 8-K exhibit

## Common Practice
Many companies announce quarterly/annual results simultaneously in:
- Press release distributed via wire (PR Newswire, Business Wire)
- 8-K filing including press release as exhibit
- Often with conference call announcement

## Required Elements of Earnings Press Release

### Core Disclosures
- GAAP financial highlights
- Reconciliation of non-GAAP measures (Reg G)
- Comparative periods (yoy, sequential)
- Segment disclosure
- Forward guidance (if provided)
- Forward-looking statements safe harbor

### Standard Format
1. Headline (top-line financial summary)
2. Subhead (key highlights)
3. Bulleted summary of metrics
4. Lead paragraph
5. CEO commentary (quote)
6. Detailed financial discussion
7. Segment results
8. Cash flow / balance sheet highlights
9. Outlook / guidance
10. Conference call details
11. Safe harbor / forward-looking statements
12. Non-GAAP reconciliation tables
13. About the company
14. IR contact

## Implicit Rubric for Material Corporate Press Releases
- Material info disclosed clearly
- All required SEC disclosures present
- Reg G compliant (non-GAAP reconciliation)
- Safe harbor language for forward-looking
- Consistent format quarter-to-quarter
- Conference call details included
- Investor and media contacts separate
- Filed simultaneously with 8-K
- Distributed via Reg FD-compliant channels
