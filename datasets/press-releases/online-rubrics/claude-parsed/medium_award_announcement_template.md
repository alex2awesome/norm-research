# Medium: Award Announcement Press Release Template

## When Awards Warrant Press
- Industry-recognized credible body
- Competitive selection process
- Substantive evaluation criteria
- Industry/regional/national prominence
- Multiple-year recognition pattern

## When Awards Don't Warrant Press
- Pay-to-play "awards"
- Vanity recognition
- Minor industry niche awards
- Awards with thousands of "winners"
- Self-nominated lists

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Company/Person] Named [Award Name] for [Year/Category]"

### Lede
"[Company] has been named [Award] by [Awarding Body], recognizing [specific basis for the award]. The award honors [criteria], and [Company] was selected from [number] nominees."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: About the Award**
- Awarding organization credibility
- Selection process
- Criteria
- Number of nominees / selectivity

**Para 2: Why [Company] Won**
- Specific accomplishments cited
- Years of qualifying work
- Customer/community impact
- Industry recognition

**Para 3: Quote**
- From CEO or senior executive
- Honors team and customers
- Acknowledges the awarding body
- Honest about ongoing work

**Para 4: Other Recognition**
- Brief mention of other recent recognition
- Pattern of credibility

### Boilerplate
- Standard company description
- Mission and scale
- Geographic reach

### Multimedia
- Award trophy/certificate image
- Award ceremony photos
- CEO accepting if applicable
- Customer/team work photos

## Distribution
- Local press for regional awards
- Industry publications for sector awards
- National press only for major recognition
- Wire syndication for SEO
- LinkedIn announcement from CEO and team

## Quality Rubric for Awards
- Award is from credible body
- Selection process described
- Specific accomplishments cited
- Substantive CEO quote
- Multimedia included
- Distribution matches award scope
- Honest about competitive context
