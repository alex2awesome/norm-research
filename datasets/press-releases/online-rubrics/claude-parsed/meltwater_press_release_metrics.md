# Meltwater - Press Release Effectiveness Metrics

## What to Measure

### Distribution Metrics
- Wire service reach
- Direct distribution opens
- Click-through rates
- Geographic distribution

### Coverage Metrics
- Number of pickups
- Outlet quality (tier 1/2/3)
- Earned media value (EMV)
- Share of voice (SoV)

### Engagement Metrics
- Social shares
- Likes, comments
- Backlinks generated
- Time on page (newsroom)

### Sentiment Metrics
- Positive / neutral / negative
- Topic sentiment
- Brand sentiment shift

### Business Outcome Metrics
- Website traffic uplift
- Lead generation
- Sales conversions
- Brand awareness lift

## Press Release Quality Indicators (from Metrics)

A press release is high-quality if it generates:
1. **High open rates** (subject line + sender working)
2. **High click-through** (compelling headline + summary)
3. **High pickup rate** (newsworthy and well-formatted)
4. **Tier 1 outlet pickup** (high quality)
5. **Positive sentiment** (well-framed)
6. **High social sharing** (resonant content)
7. **Quality backlinks** (authority sites)
8. **Long page dwell time** (engaging content)
9. **Follow-up coverage** (story has legs)
10. **Business impact** (ties to KPIs)

## KPIs Across PR Goals

### Awareness Goals
- Reach (impressions)
- SoV
- Brand mentions
- Search volume lift

### Consideration Goals
- Website traffic
- Time on site
- Newsletter signups
- Demo requests

### Conversion Goals
- Sales attribution
- Pipeline impact
- Customer acquisition cost reduction
- Revenue attribution

### Reputation Goals
- Sentiment scores
- Crisis recovery time
- Spokesperson quotes
- Industry rankings

## Implicit Measurement Rubric for Press Releases

A press release is empirically successful if:
- Pickup rate > 5% (above industry baseline)
- Tier 1 outlets cover (any)
- Positive sentiment > 70%
- Social shares > 50 (across platforms)
- Backlinks generated > 5 (authority)
- Website traffic measurable lift
- Coverage continues > 3 days
- Business KPIs measurably affected

## Diagnostic Use of Metrics

### If pickup low but opens high
→ Subject line working, body not converting

### If pickup low and opens low
→ Subject line / personalization issue

### If pickup high but business impact low
→ Story not connected to brand benefit

### If sentiment mixed
→ Framing or controversial content
