# Garrett Moon (CoSchedule): PR for Content Marketers

## CoSchedule's Approach to PR
- Content-first PR strategy
- Owned media as the foundation
- Press releases serve content amplification
- Long-tail SEO through earned coverage
- Repurposing content across channels

## The Content-PR Flywheel
1. Create substantive content (research, data, guides)
2. Pitch the content angle to journalists
3. Earn coverage with backlinks
4. SEO compounds over time
5. Inbound leads grow

## Content Types That Drive PR
- Original research with surprising findings
- Industry benchmark reports
- Year-over-year trend analyses
- Comprehensive how-to guides
- Tools and calculators with proprietary data

## Press Release for Content
- Headline: data finding, not company news
- Lede: 5 W's about the data
- Body: methodology + key findings + implications
- Quote: from research lead
- Multimedia: charts, infographics
- Distribution: trade press + bloggers + LinkedIn

## Distribution for Content-Driven PR
- Pitch to journalists who cover the topic
- Bloggers in the space (long-tail SEO)
- Industry newsletters
- Wire for SEO floor
- LinkedIn carousels
- Podcast pitch (research as discussion topic)

## Measurement Standards
- Earned backlinks (SEO value)
- Social shares
- Brand mentions (with and without links)
- Pipeline impact attributable
- Long-tail search rankings (90 days)

## What Doesn't Work
- Promotional press releases without data
- Generic "leading provider" framing
- Content with no methodology
- Wire-only distribution
- Mass-blast outreach

## Quality Rubric (CoSchedule)
- Content-first strategy
- Original data in release
- Methodology documented
- Charts/infographics included
- Distribution to bloggers + journalists
- Long-tail SEO measurement
