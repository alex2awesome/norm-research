# Funding Announcement Press Release - Startup VC Guide

## Purpose of Funding Announcement
- Notify audiences about new VC, angel, or grant funding
- Make stakeholders aware
- Raise media interest
- Build credibility with future investors and customers
- Recruit talent
- Attract customers

## Key Elements

### Headline
- Short
- Sum up who and what
- Lead with startup name
- Include amount of funding
- Active verbs (raises, secures, closes)
- Examples: "Acme Raises $50M Series B Led by Sequoia"

### Lede / Opening Paragraph
Answer three questions:
- WHO (company)
- WHAT (funding round and amount)
- WHY (purpose/use of funds)

Optionally include:
- WHEN
- WHERE
- Brief company description

### Body Content

#### Use of Funds
- Product roadmap
- Market expansion
- Talent acquisition
- Other initiatives
- Specific milestones to be funded

#### Company Traction
- User adoption metrics
- Revenue figures
- Customer growth
- Notable customers
- Geographic reach
- Other meaningful milestones

#### Investor Information
- Lead investor (named first)
- All participating VCs
- Angel investors
- Strategic investors
- Existing investors that participated

#### Investor Quote
- Why they invested
- Market opportunity
- Founder/team strengths

### Founder/CEO Quote
- Vision for use of funds
- Mission statement
- Team appreciation

## Distribution
- PR Newswire, Business Wire, Prowly
- Direct to tech press (TechCrunch, etc.)
- Coordinate with VC firm PR
- Embargo if multi-VC announcement

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Funding Announcements
1. Funding amount stated upfront
2. Round (Seed, Series A/B/C) specified
3. Lead investor named
4. All participating investors named
5. Use of funds specified concretely
6. Company traction metrics included
7. Founder + investor quotes
8. Strategic context (why this round, why now)
9. Total funding to date noted
10. Valuation (if disclosed)
11. Customer/product highlights
12. Hiring plans
13. Press contact at company AND VC
14. Logo and headshots
15. Demo/product URL
