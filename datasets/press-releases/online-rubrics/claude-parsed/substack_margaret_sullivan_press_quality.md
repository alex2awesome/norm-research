# Margaret Sullivan: On Press Release Credibility

## The Trust Crisis in PR
- Wire-distributed press releases increasingly seen as PR spam
- Journalists more skeptical of "research" releases without methodology
- Embargo violations have eroded source-reporter trust
- Sponsored content blurs editorial lines
- AI-generated releases flooding inboxes

## Markers of Credible Releases
- Named spokesperson with specific expertise
- Methodology disclosed for any data claim
- Funding sources / conflicts of interest noted
- Verifiable third-party confirmations
- Direct phone number for follow-up
- Public record information consistent

## Markers of Suspicion
- Vague "leading research" claims with no link to actual study
- Quotes from spokespeople you can't find on LinkedIn
- "According to our internal data" without methodology
- Embargo with no clear reason for delay
- Surveys with no margin of error reported
- "Independent" sources with hidden funding

## On Crisis Press Releases
- Apology must come before explanation
- Specifics matter more than statements of values
- Timeline of what was known and when
- Specific corrective actions, not abstract commitments
- Avoid "regret that some may have felt"
- Name the harm; don't deflect

## What Journalists Verify
- Spokesperson credentials
- Numerical claims against public records
- Quote authenticity (call the person directly)
- Customer/partner confirmations
- Financial claims against SEC filings
- Survey methodology and sample size

## Quality Rubric for Trust
- Verifiable spokesperson credentials
- Methodology disclosed for data
- Conflicts of interest noted
- Specific corrective actions in crisis releases
- Phone number for verification
- Consistent with public records
