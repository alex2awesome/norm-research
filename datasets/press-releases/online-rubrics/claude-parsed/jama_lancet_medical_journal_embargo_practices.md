# Medical Journal Embargo Practices

## Embargo System Overview
Medical journals (JAMA, Lancet, BMJ, NEJM) grant prepublication access to selected media members who agree not to publish, broadcast, post online, or otherwise disclose embargoed content until publication time.

## Standard Embargo Times

### The Lancet
- 6:30 PM US ET (23:30 UK time)
- Corresponds to article online publication
- May vary occasionally

### JAMA Network
- Specific embargo times set per article
- Generally aligned with publication day

### BMJ
- Set per article
- Coordinated with media offices

### NEJM
- Wednesday 5:00 PM ET typical
- Same time as journal publishes

## Purpose of Embargoes
- Creates "level playing field"
- Minimizes pressure on journalists to publish before competitors
- Allows time for careful preparation
- Allows fact-checking and expert consultation
- Minimizes economic chaos for material findings
- Enables coordinated coverage

## Access During Embargo
Reporters granted access may:
- Read embargoed articles
- Discuss findings with authors
- Consult with other experts
- Draft reports
- Prepare news stories
- Coordinate with photographers/illustrators

## Penalties for Breaking Embargo
- Loss of access to embargoed materials
- Loss of media credentials
- Suspension of media outlet
- Damaged reputation
- Difficulty getting future access

## Press Release Implications

### Press Release Should Include
- Embargo notice prominently displayed
- Specific date and time (with time zone)
- "EMBARGOED UNTIL [DATE/TIME]" at top
- DOI of the journal article
- Link to embargoed materials (where appropriate)
- Author availability for interviews
- Conflicts of interest disclosure

### Coordination Standards
- Journal sets embargo (PIO doesn't override)
- Distribute through approved channels (EurekAlert, Newswise)
- Don't tweet or post until embargo lifts
- Coordinate with co-authors at other institutions
- Inform institutional comms early

## Implicit Embargo Compliance Rubric
A press release that respects medical embargo norms:
1. States embargo clearly at top
2. Specifies time zone
3. Coordinates with journal embargo time
4. Limits pre-embargo distribution to vetted media
5. Prepares all materials ready for embargo lift
6. Provides DOI/citation
7. Author available at embargo time
8. Multimedia ready for embargo lift
9. Social media drafts prepared for embargo time
10. No spoilers in social media before embargo
