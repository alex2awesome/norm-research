# Sam Parr (The Hustle): Press Strategy for Bootstrapped Startups

## Why Press Matters Less Than You Think
- Most coverage doesn't move customer needle
- Pickup measured in inbound is often disappointing
- Vanity metrics ≠ business outcomes
- Better ROI in direct customer outreach
- Better ROI in audience building (newsletter, podcast, social)

## When Press IS Worth It
- Major capital raise
- Hiring/recruiting acceleration needed
- Acquisition/exit positioning
- Crisis you must respond to
- Long-term brand building (over years)

## The Hustle's PR Approach
- Built audience organically through newsletter
- Press came because of audience, not other way around
- Founder voice was the brand
- Didn't hire PR firm until $5M+ ARR
- Most coverage was earned through being interesting

## What Got The Hustle Coverage
- Counter-intuitive market analysis
- Funny, share-worthy newsletter content
- Acquisition by HubSpot — made the press easy
- Sam himself being a quotable, memorable founder
- Building "The Hustle" as a media property, not just a startup

## Pitching Reporters: Sam's Rules
- One reporter at a time
- Read 5 of their articles first
- Subject line must be specific
- Body under 150 words
- Phone number always included
- Follow up once, then drop it

## Founder Voice Development
- Tweet substantively in your space
- Build a newsletter or blog of your own
- Be quotable in industry conversations
- Have opinions, not just announcements
- Cultivate relationships before you need them

## Common Mistakes Sam Sees
- Spending too much on PR firm before product-market fit
- Wire-only distribution thinking it'll work
- "Look at me" announcements with no substance
- Burning embargo (career-ending mistake)
- Lying or exaggerating numbers (catches up)

## Quality Rubric (Sam Parr)
- Audience built before press
- Founder voice authentic
- Substantive news worth telling
- Personalized pitches
- Long-term relationship building
- Honesty as competitive advantage
