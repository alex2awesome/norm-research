# Medium: Partnership Announcement Press Release Guide

## When Partnerships Warrant Press
- Material to revenue or product
- Customer impact significant
- Strategic positioning shift
- Named, recognizable partner
- Exclusive arrangement

## When Partnerships Don't Warrant Press
- Standard customer relationship
- Reseller arrangement
- Affiliate program
- Generic "integration"
- Non-exclusive arrangement with minor party

## Common Mistakes
- "Strategic partnership" with no strategy described
- "Combining forces" buzzword
- Quote from partner that's just promotional
- No specific outcomes for customers
- No timeline for joint work

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Company A] and [Company B] Partner to [Specific Joint Outcome]"

### Lede
"[Company A] and [Company B] today announced a partnership to [specific outcome]. The collaboration will [specific customer benefit] starting [specific date]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: What the Partnership Does**
- Specific joint capability
- What customers can do that they couldn't before
- Timeline of availability
- Geographic scope if relevant

**Para 2: Why This Partnership Now**
- Market context driving the partnership
- Each company's contribution
- Why these two specifically

**Para 3: Customer Impact**
- Named customer if possible
- Specific use case
- Measurable benefit
- Migration path if existing customers

**Para 4: Quotes**
- CEO from each company
- Substantive on strategic value
- Specific on what's enabled

### Boilerplate
- Both companies' descriptions
- Combined scale or reach
- Headquarters
- Founded dates

## Distribution Coordination
- Same day from both companies
- Coordinated with sales teams
- Customer notification first
- Wire from both wires (PRN + BW)
- Co-promotional social
- Joint webinar or event

## Quality Rubric for Partnerships
- Material relationship, not boilerplate
- Specific joint capability described
- Customer benefit measurable
- Timeline of availability clear
- Substantive quotes from both
- Coordinated cross-company release
- Customer reference if possible
