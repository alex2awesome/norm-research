# Press Release Pickup Rate - Data-Driven Analysis

## Baseline Pickup Rates
- Only **2-3% of press releases result in media pickup** across all industries
- Reflects intense competition for journalists' attention
- Most never become news stories

## Strategies That Improve Pickup

### Targeted Distribution
**67% higher pickup rates** for releases focused on specific industry sectors vs. broad distribution.

### Executive Quotes
**40% higher pickup rates** for releases with at least one executive quote.

### Optimal Timing
- **Tuesday and Wednesday mornings** (8:00-9:00 AM local time): 30% more media attention
- Avoid Mondays (catching up from weekends)
- Avoid Fridays (winding down)
- Avoid weekends entirely

### Email Engagement Window
- 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM = ~45% click-through rate
- Best window for direct journalist outreach

## Visual/Multimedia Impact
- **94% increase** in views with multimedia
- **650% increase** in engagement with visuals
- **6x higher engagement** with multiple visual assets vs text-only
- **3x more interaction** for releases with video
- **48% increase** in social sharing with immersive multimedia
- **75% more likely to be shared** with engaging visual content

## Social Media Amplification
- 94% higher engagement rate on social with visuals
- Facebook posts with images: 53% more likes, 104% more comments
- Tweets with images: nearly 2x more retweets
- Visual content: 40x more likely to be shared

## Cognitive Reasons
- Visual content processed 60,000x faster than text
- Content with relevant images: 94% more views
- Images load faster than video, easier to scan

## Implicit Pickup-Rate Rubric

### Press release is more likely to be picked up if it:
1. Distributed Tuesday/Wednesday morning
2. Targeted to specific industry sectors
3. Includes at least one executive quote
4. Has high-quality images
5. Has video where relevant
6. Has ≥3 multimedia assets
7. Has shareable visual elements
8. Has clear "tweetable" facts
9. Has direct journalist outreach via email
10. Sent 10 AM - 2 PM local time

### Diagnostic Questions
- Is the news genuinely newsworthy?
- Does the headline tell a story?
- Is there a strong news hook?
- Are facts and statistics compelling?
- Is multimedia included?
- Is distribution targeted?
- Is timing optimized?
- Are journalist contacts personalized?
