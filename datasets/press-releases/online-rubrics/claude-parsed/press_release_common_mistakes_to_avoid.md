# Common Press Release Mistakes (Inverse Rubric)

## Major Mistakes to Avoid

### Lack of Newsworthy Angle
Without a newsworthy angle, journalists are unlikely to cover your story. Test: Would a journalist write about this without your release?

### Poor Timing
Press releases distributed too late lose relevance. Send before, not after, the news event happens.

### Bad Writing Quality
- Grammatical errors
- Stiff or unnatural writing
- Wrong style usage
- Excessively long drafts
- Even one typo damages credibility

### Weak Headlines
Generic headlines like "Company X Announces New Product" fail. Headlines should be:
- Specific
- Clear
- Tied to real benefits or impact

### Overly Promotional Tone
Avoid:
- "Leading provider of innovative solutions"
- "We're thrilled to announce"
- Marketing-speak
- Superlatives without proof

### Industry Jargon
Every technical term builds barriers between story and audience.

### Poor Formatting
- Long paragraphs
- Missing subheads
- Dense blocks of text
- No white space

### Missing Visuals
Text-only releases limit engagement. Always include images.

### Wrong Distribution Strategy
- Don't blast every contact
- Don't send to irrelevant beats
- Don't send during breaking news, weekends, off-hours

### Unclear Call to Action
Don't end with "Learn more soon" or no contact info.

### Missing Quote
Quotes humanize and provide credibility. Always include 1-2.

### Burying the Lead
Don't make journalists hunt for the news.

### Using Wrong Person
Quote should be from someone with authority + relevance.

### No Embargo Discipline
Misusing embargoes burns bridges with journalists.

### Wrong Audience
Tailor to the specific reader/outlet.

### Too Many Topics
One press release = one news event.

### Forgotten Boilerplate
Always include "About [Company]"

### Missing Contact
Make it easy to follow up - name, email, phone, time zone

### No Multimedia Assets
Photos, videos, infographics increase pickup probability.

### Word Count Issues
- Too short (no substance)
- Too long (over 800 words rare)
- Sweet spot: 300-500 for news, 500-800 for features

### Sending Without Follow-up
But also: not too many follow-ups (1-2 max)
