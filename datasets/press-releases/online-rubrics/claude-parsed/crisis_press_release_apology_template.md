# Crisis Press Release Standards

## Key Components

### Headline
- Directly addresses the situation
- Not defensive or evasive
- Names the issue clearly
- Avoids spin

### Opening Paragraph
- Acknowledge what happened
- Express appropriate concern for those affected
- Don't bury the news
- Plain statement of facts

### Body
- Detailed information about the incident
- Organization's actions taken
- Statement from high-ranking official
- Concern + commitment to resolution
- Resources for affected parties
- Timeline of events
- Update plan

## Apology Strategy

### When to Apologize
- Organization is clearly at fault
- Actions/negligence have harmed people
- Authentic apology essential for rebuilding trust

### How to Apologize
- Humble but competent tone
- Sincere, no-excuses
- People need to know you're sorry AND able to enact solutions
- Apology FIRST in any crisis statement
- Show customers/affected are foremost in thoughts
- Demonstrate understanding of severity and impact

## Tone and Approach

### Do
- Show cooperation and accountability
- Express empathy
- Be transparent about facts known
- Acknowledge what isn't yet known
- Outline corrective actions
- Provide regular updates

### Don't
- Make excuses
- Deflect blame
- Minimize impact
- Use jargon or legalese
- Speculate
- Promise what you can't deliver
- Use passive voice ("mistakes were made")

## Standard Crisis Release Structure

1. **Acknowledgment** - What happened, when
2. **Empathy** - Concern for affected parties
3. **Apology** (if at fault) - Sincere, specific
4. **Facts** - What we know
5. **Action** - What we're doing
6. **Support** - Resources for affected
7. **Accountability** - Who is responsible
8. **Update plan** - When more info coming
9. **Contact** - For more information

## Crisis Types and Response

### High Responsibility (Coombs - Preventable)
- Apology + corrective action
- Compensation where appropriate
- Mortification

### Medium Responsibility (Accidental)
- Acknowledgment + explanation
- Corrective action
- Empathy

### Low Responsibility (Victim)
- Information about response
- Empathy
- Resources

## Implicit Crisis Press Release Rubric
1. Acknowledged within 24 hours
2. Acknowledges incident clearly
3. Expresses empathy
4. Apologizes if at fault (specific apology)
5. States facts known
6. Notes facts unknown
7. Outlines actions taken
8. Provides resources for affected
9. Names accountable party
10. Provides update timeline
11. Direct contact for media
12. Single spokesperson designated
13. Coordinated across channels
14. Plain language (no spin)
15. No defensive posture
