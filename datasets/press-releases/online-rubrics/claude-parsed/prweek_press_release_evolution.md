# PR Week: The Evolution of the Press Release

## The Press Release Is Not Dead
Despite predictions, the press release still serves:
- SEO and search visibility
- Wire syndication for institutional credibility
- Newsroom archive of company news
- Scraping fodder for AI summarization tools
- Source material for analyst reports

## How It Has Evolved
### From Print Era
- Single format (text)
- Distribution via fax and mail
- 1-2 page maximum length
- AP style strict adherence
- Single spokesperson quoted

### To Digital Era
- Multi-format (text + multimedia)
- Distribution via wire + email + social
- Length flexible (300-1000 words)
- AP style preferred but not strict
- Multiple voices (executive + customer + partner)

## Modern Format Conventions
- Multimedia hub with downloadable assets
- Social media kit with pre-formatted posts
- Tweet-length quote pulled out
- Translation available for global reach
- Mobile-optimized HTML version

## What Hasn't Changed
- Need for newsworthy substance
- 5 W's in the lede
- AP style preference
- Spokesperson contact
- Boilerplate at end
- "###" end marker

## Hybrid Distribution Model
- Wire services for institutional credibility + SEO
- Direct journalist outreach for actual coverage
- Social media for amplification
- Newsroom posting for owned media
- Newsletter inclusion for engaged audience

## Measurement Evolution
- Old: Impressions
- Now: Pickup, sentiment, business outcomes
- Future: Attribution to revenue / conversion

## What's Still Required
- Substantive news
- Named spokesperson
- Verifiable claims
- Multimedia
- Clear contact info
- Distribution thoughtfully

## Implicit Quality Rubric (PR Week)
- Hybrid distribution strategy
- Multimedia hub with assets
- Multiple voices in quotes
- Mobile-optimized format
- Translation if global
- Measurement beyond impressions
