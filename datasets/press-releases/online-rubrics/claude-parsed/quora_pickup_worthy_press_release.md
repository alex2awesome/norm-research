# Quora: What Makes a Press Release Pickup-Worthy

## Top-Voted Answer Themes

### Element 1: Genuine Newsworthiness
The release must address one of these tests:
- Affects more than your immediate customers
- Provides new data or insight
- Reflects a meaningful change in the industry
- Has human-interest angle
- Tied to current news cycle

### Element 2: Reporter-Centric Framing
- Story angle, not announcement
- "Why this matters to your readers"
- Pre-thought hooks for headline writers
- Provides quotes journalists can use directly
- Includes data they can verify

### Element 3: Easy to Use
- Multimedia attached, not behind a form
- Spokesperson available
- Background materials linked
- Pre-formatted facts and figures
- All names, titles spelled correctly

### Element 4: Right Distribution
- Sent to journalists who cover your beat
- Sent at right time of day/week
- Personalized email, not blast
- Embargo if exclusivity warranted

## Common Misconceptions Debunked

**"Wire distribution = pickup"**
False. Wire is SEO and institutional. Pickup comes from personal pitches.

**"PR firms have magic relationships"**
False. They have lists. Quality of pitch still matters more than firm name.

**"Bigger announcement = bigger pickup"**
False. Smaller, well-angled stories often get more pickup than vague big-deal claims.

**"Embargoes guarantee coverage"**
False. Embargoes only matter if the story is genuinely worth competing for.

## Implicit Quality Rubric (Quora Aggregate)
- Genuinely newsworthy
- Story angle pre-thought
- Multimedia and facts pre-attached
- Spokesperson available
- Personalized distribution
- Tied to broader trend or moment
