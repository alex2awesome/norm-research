# Bospar: "I'm a Press Release" Critique Series

## The Series Premise
Each post analyzes a recent press release and critiques what worked and what didn't, from a journalist's perspective.

## Common "What Didn't Work" Themes

### Headlines
- "Acme Corp Announces Strategic Partnership With XYZ" — no news
- "Revolutionary New Solution Launched" — no specifics
- "Industry-First Platform Now Available" — no proof of "first"
- "Game-Changing Innovation Disrupts Market" — buzzword soup

### Ledes
- "Today, Acme Corp is excited to announce..." — throat-clearing
- "In a major development for the industry..." — telling not showing
- "Building on our mission to..." — about company, not news
- "We are proud to share..." — promotional, not informational

### Quotes
- "We are excited to bring this to market" — generic
- "This represents a major milestone" — meaningless
- "Our customers will benefit from..." — vague
- Quote attributed to executive who clearly didn't write it

### Boilerplate
- 200+ word company description — too long
- "Leading provider of solutions" — clichéd
- "Industry leader in innovation" — unverifiable
- Marketing language instead of factual description

## Common "What Worked" Themes

### Headlines
- Specific number + concrete outcome
- Named entity (recognizable customer/partner)
- Timeframe ("by end of year", "in next 90 days")
- Genuine "first" claims with verification

### Ledes
- Front-loaded news in 50 words or less
- Concrete proof point
- Stakes for reader clear
- 5 W's compressed but complete

### Quotes
- Specific reasoning behind decision
- Counter-intuitive insight
- Honest acknowledgment of tradeoffs
- Customer testimonial with metrics

## Repeated Recommendations
- Cut every adjective and adverb after first draft
- Read aloud to test for flow
- Have outside reader (not in marketing) review
- Test headline with "would I tweet this?" filter
- Verify all numerical claims

## Quality Rubric (Bospar)
- Headlines specific with verifiable claims
- Ledes news-first, throat-clearing-removed
- Quotes substantive and human
- Boilerplate concise and factual
- All claims verifiable
- Outside reader reviewed
