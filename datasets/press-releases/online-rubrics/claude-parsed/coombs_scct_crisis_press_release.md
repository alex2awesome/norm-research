# Crisis Communication Theories for Press Releases

## Image Restoration Theory (Benoit)
Introduced by William L. Benoit. IRT outlines five strategies to restore an image when reputation has been damaged:

1. **Denial** - Simple denial or shifting blame
2. **Evasion of responsibility** - Provocation, defeasibility, accident, good intentions
3. **Reduction of offensiveness** - Bolstering, minimization, differentiation, transcendence, attacking accuser, compensation
4. **Corrective action** - Promising to fix and prevent recurrence
5. **Mortification** - Apology and seeking forgiveness

## Situational Crisis Communication Theory (SCCT) - Coombs (2007)
SCCT suggests crisis managers should match strategic crisis responses to the level of crisis responsibility and reputational threat. Three response groups:

1. **Deny** - victim, scapegoat, attack the accuser
2. **Diminish** - excuse, justification
3. **Rebuild** - compensation, apology
4. **Bolstering** (supplementary) - reminder of past good works, ingratiation

## Crisis Type Categories (SCCT)
- **Victim cluster** (low responsibility): natural disaster, rumor, workplace violence, product tampering
- **Accidental cluster** (medium): challenges, technical-error accidents, technical-error product harm
- **Preventable cluster** (high): human-error accidents, human-error product harm, organizational misdeeds

## Implicit Crisis Press Release Rubric
- Match response strategy to crisis type
- For preventable crises: rebuild strategies (apology, compensation) work best
- Acknowledge stakeholders' concerns
- Provide instructional information (how to stay safe)
- Provide adjusting information (psychological support)
- Be timely - first hours are critical
- Be consistent across channels
- Designate single spokesperson
- Provide regular updates even when no new info
- Use press briefings when crisis goes viral

## Standard Crisis Press Release Components
- Acknowledgment of incident
- Expression of concern for affected
- Facts known so far
- Actions being taken
- Support resources
- Promise of updates
- Contact information for more info
