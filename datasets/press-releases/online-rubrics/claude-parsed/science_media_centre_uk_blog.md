# Science Media Centre UK: Working with Us

## Mission
"Provide accurate and evidence-based information to the media on science and engineering issues — particularly when stories are controversial or hit the headlines."

## What Makes a Good Science Press Release (SMC)
- Accurate representation of the underlying study
- Clear methodology summary
- Limitations explicitly stated
- Effect sizes given in both relative and absolute terms
- Multiple expert voices included
- External commentary from non-author scientists

## What Makes a Bad Science Press Release
- Hype that exceeds the study's findings
- Causal language for correlational findings
- Missing limitations
- Single-author voice without external context
- Rate ratios without baseline rates
- "First", "breakthrough", "revolutionary" without verification

## SMC's Press Release Critique Format
For each release, SMC analysts evaluate:
- Does the headline match the study?
- Is the lede accurate?
- Are the conclusions appropriate?
- Are limitations stated?
- Is methodology clear?
- Are statistical effects given fully?
- Are external experts cited?

## Common Issues Tracked by SMC
- Animal studies framed as human-relevant
- Preclinical work hyped as therapeutic
- Subgroup analyses framed as primary findings
- Correlations described as causes
- Effect sizes obscured by relative-only framing

## SMC's Recommendations for Press Officers
- Have authors review the release before issue
- Run drafts past statistical reviewer
- Avoid "first" claims unless verified
- Include external expert quotes
- Disclose all funding sources
- Specify embargo time clearly

## Recommendations for Journalists
- Read the underlying paper before writing
- Quote external experts
- Note limitations in the article
- Avoid headline hype that wasn't in the release
- Verify causal claims with statistician

## Quality Rubric (SMC)
- Headline matches study findings
- Methodology summary provided
- Limitations explicitly stated
- Effect sizes given fully
- External expert quotes included
- Funding sources disclosed
- Embargo time clear
