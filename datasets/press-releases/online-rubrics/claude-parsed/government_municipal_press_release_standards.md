# Government Press Release Standards (Municipal/State/Federal)

## Definition and Purpose
A government press release is an official written statement issued by a public agency, elected official, or government body to communicate news, announcements, or information to media and public.

Unlike corporate releases driven by brand or commercial goals, government press releases serve a public accountability function — formal mechanism for announcing:
- New policies
- Legislation
- Program outcomes
- Appointments
- Emergency information
- Use of public funds

## Writing Standards

### Tone
- Informational, not promotional
- Third person
- Plain language
- Avoid political spin
- Neutral framing

### Headline
- States news in plain language
- Avoids vague or promotional phrasing
- Reader knows exactly what happened from title

### Opening Paragraph
- Answer who, what, when, where, why in 2-3 sentences
- Everything essential up front
- Include impact on citizens

### Body
- Background and context
- Specific data and statistics
- Officials' quotes (mayor, agency head, etc.)
- Historical context if relevant
- Comparison to baselines

### Boilerplate
- Brief description of issuing department/agency
- Mission, size, location, achievements

## Standard Government Press Release Components
- "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE"
- Agency letterhead/seal
- Date
- Headline
- Subheadline
- Dateline
- Lead paragraph (5Ws)
- Body with quotes from officials
- Statistical/factual support
- Action items for citizens (if applicable)
- Department description
- Contact info for PIO

## Who Writes Them
Communications staff or Public Information Officers (PIOs) within the relevant agency or official's office.

## 3CMA Standards
City-County Communications & Marketing Association sets professional standards for local government communications.

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Government Releases
- Clear public benefit/impact
- Accurate facts and statistics
- Quotes from authorized officials
- Plain language (Grade 8 reading level)
- No partisan framing
- All affected populations identified
- Action steps for citizens (if needed)
- Agency contact for follow-up
- Accessible to all (translated if needed)
- Compliant with FOIA/public records standards
- Fact-checked for accuracy
- Approved through proper channels

## Special Categories
- **Emergency releases**: Brief, action-focused, frequent updates
- **Policy releases**: Background, rationale, implementation
- **Award/recognition**: Standard congratulatory format
- **Crisis response**: Acknowledgment, action, accountability
