# Medium: Product Launch Press Release Writing Guide

## What Makes a Releasable Product Launch
- New capability that wasn't possible before
- Significant improvement over prior version
- Tied to industry trend or market shift
- Customer-validated through beta
- Demoable to journalists immediately

## What Doesn't Warrant a Release
- Minor feature updates
- UI redesigns without functional change
- Bug fixes (no matter how big)
- Internal-only improvements
- "We added support for X" (unless X is significant)

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Company] Launches [Product] to [New Capability]"
Examples:
- "Acme Launches Workflow Builder for No-Code Automation"
- "BetaCorp Releases AI Code Reviewer Cutting PR Review Time 60%"

### Lede
"[Company] today launched [Product], [brief description] that enables [users] to [new capability]. The product is available [today/beta/waitlist] for [price/free]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: The Problem**
- What pain point this solves
- Who experiences this pain
- Why existing solutions fall short

**Para 2: The Solution**
- How the product works (specific mechanism)
- Key features that enable the capability
- What makes it different

**Para 3: Customer Validation**
- Named beta customer
- Specific use case
- Measurable outcome
- Quote from customer

**Para 4: Quote from Founder/CTO**
- Substantive on technical approach
- Specific on customer impact
- Honest about what's still unsolved

**Para 5: Availability**
- Pricing tiers
- Access method (signup, waitlist, sales)
- Geographic availability
- Roadmap for upcoming features

### Boilerplate
- Company description
- Stage/scale
- Total customers/users
- Mission

## Multimedia Requirements
- Product screenshots
- Demo video (under 90 seconds)
- Founder/team photos
- Customer use case visualizations
- Comparison charts where relevant

## Distribution Strategy
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade outlet
- Coordinated release across tech press
- Wire syndication for SEO
- Founder LinkedIn launch
- Twitter/X founder thread
- Hacker News submission (if relevant)
- Demo day at industry conference

## Quality Rubric for Product Launches
- Genuinely new capability
- Customer-validated
- Demoable immediately
- Specific use case included
- Multimedia comprehensive
- Founder available for demos
- Pricing/availability clear
