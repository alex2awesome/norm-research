# ESA/Hubble Press Release Guidelines for Scientists

## Core Purpose
Press releases serve as primary vehicles for communicating scientific advances to the press, decision-makers, mediators, scientists, and the public. They require close collaboration between the Public Information Officer and the Principal Investigator.

## Three Main Goals
1. Increase awareness of science and the scientific work process
2. Increase awareness of the organization
3. Increase awareness of specific scientific projects, instruments, or missions

## Release Types
- **News releases**: Focus on one or more scientific discoveries
- **Photo releases**: Feature compelling imagery without major discoveries
- **Video releases**: Broadcast-ready video news releases (VNRs) for television and websites

## 14 News Criteria for Evaluation
Stories should demonstrate newsworthiness through: timing, relevance, proximity, implications, conflict resolution, human interest angles, mystery elements, major scientific breakthroughs, fresh perspectives on existing findings, record-breaking achievements, "sexy topics" (black holes, exoplanets), aesthetic appeal, publication in distinguished journals, or cross-linking opportunities.

## Scientific Writing Standards
- Simplify complex concepts for general audiences
- Match explanations to target group comprehension levels
- Define all abbreviations and technical terminology
- Structure arguments from foundational to complex
- Edit rigorously for clarity and accuracy
- Conduct thorough research before drafting
- Maintain consistency in style and grammar

## Required Visual Support
High-quality accompanying images, illustrations, or videos are practically mandatory for success.
