# 3 Pro Tips for Writing Press Releases that Get Noticed

**1. Less is More (Headlines)**
"Keep the character length to 88 characters or less, and include the most important keywords in the first 55 to 70 characters." Craft concise, relevant headlines that search engines and readers will immediately recognize.

**2. Start with Why (Content Length)**
"The longer the press release, the less likely the reader is to read it in its entirety." Aim for approximately 600 words and prioritize leading with compelling reasons your audience should care about your announcement.

**3. Timing is Everything (Distribution)**
Rather than sending at peak times when "52% of news is sent on the hour and half hour," particularly "Tuesdays at 8:00 AM ET," choose unconventional send times like 8:47 AM to distinguish your release from competitors.
