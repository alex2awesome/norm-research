# Cutlip & Center "Effective Public Relations" - Press Release Principles

## Status
Often called "the bible of the public relations field" - the single most authoritative reference for public relations professionals.

## Press Release Writing Principles

### Core Skills Required
PR practitioners must:
1. Know what information will attract media attention
2. Identify a newsworthy angle and lead
3. Write and package the information appropriately for each medium

### Differential Packaging by Medium

**Print Media:**
- News release with feature story
- Photographs
- Media kit with detailed background information

**Broadcast Media and Blogs:**
- Broadcast-style news script
- Recorded interview or "sound bites"
- Video news release (VNR)
- Media kit with material suitable for broadcast or Internet posting

### News Values Cutlip Identifies
- Impact (significance, consequence)
- Timeliness (currency)
- Prominence (notable people, organizations)
- Proximity (local relevance)
- Bizarreness (unusual)
- Conflict
- Currency (current affairs)
- Human interest

### The PR Process Framework (RACE)
1. **Research** - Define problem and audience
2. **Action planning** - Strategy and tactics
3. **Communication** - Implementation including press releases
4. **Evaluation** - Measure results

### Standards for Press Release Quality
- Newsworthy (passes news values test)
- Accurate (verifiable facts)
- Timely (releases at right news cycle moment)
- Targeted (right outlets and journalists)
- Ethical (transparent about source and intent)
- Professional (AP style, proper format)

### Two-Way Symmetric Communication Model
Excellence theory (Grunig) - PR should be:
- Two-way (listening and responding)
- Symmetric (mutual understanding, not propaganda)
- Strategic (tied to org goals)
- Diverse (representing all stakeholders)

Press releases should serve mutual understanding rather than one-way persuasion.
