# TechCrunch: How to Pitch Us

## What We Cover
- Startups raising funding (Seed through Series C+)
- Product launches with new technical capability
- Founder stories with substance
- Industry analysis and trends
- Acquisition and exit news
- Crisis and controversy in tech

## What We Don't Cover
- Stealth-stage companies with no product
- Generic "we use AI" claims
- B2B SaaS announcements without major customers
- Routine partnerships or integrations
- Hire announcements unless executive is well-known
- Vanity awards
- Most enterprise software news

## Pitching Format
- Email to relevant reporter (not info@)
- Subject line: news in 8 words
- Body: 100-200 words
- Personalized to recent coverage
- Includes spokesperson availability
- Phone number provided

## Embargo Policy
- We accept embargoes from trusted PR contacts
- 24-48 hours is standard
- Embargo break = no future coverage
- Exclusive offered only to one reporter
- Co-exclusives not accepted

## What Speeds Up Coverage
- Pre-attached multimedia (screenshots, video)
- Customer references with logos and quotes
- Investor references for funding announcements
- Founder available within 24 hours
- Concrete metrics (revenue, users, growth rate)
- Story angle that fits our voice

## What Slows Coverage
- Spokesperson availability uncertain
- Multimedia behind a request form
- Vague metrics or unverifiable claims
- Press kit with PDFs only
- Insufficient context for the news
- Pitching during major news cycles

## Common Reasons for No Response
- Story doesn't fit our beat
- Already covered similar story recently
- Numbers don't justify coverage
- Spokesperson not credible
- Pitched too many reporters at once
- Not enough novelty in the news

## Quality Rubric (TechCrunch)
- Story fits our specific beats
- Subject line news-first
- Personalized to reporter
- Multimedia pre-attached
- Concrete metrics provided
- Spokesperson available
- Embargo respected if offered
