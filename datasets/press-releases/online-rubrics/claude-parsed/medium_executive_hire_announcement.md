# Medium: Executive Hire Press Release Best Practices

## When Worth Doing
- C-level hire (CEO, CTO, CFO, COO)
- Famous/recognized executive joining
- Significant board addition
- Hire signals strategic shift
- Hire validates market positioning

## When NOT Worth Doing
- Junior or mid-level hires
- Lateral moves without significance
- Internal promotions for non-named roles
- Vanity titles
- Single-day announcements

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Executive Name] Joins [Company] as [Title]"
Or
"[Company] Hires [Executive Name] to Lead [Specific Function/Strategy]"

### Lede
"[Company] today announced that [Executive Name] has joined as [Title], where [he/she/they] will [specific responsibility]. [Name] joins from [previous company], where [he/she/they] [specific accomplishment]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: Why This Hire**
- Company stage and context
- What this role enables
- Strategic priorities this addresses

**Para 2: Executive Background**
- 3-5 most relevant prior roles
- Specific accomplishments
- Education
- Industry recognition

**Para 3: What They'll Do**
- Specific priorities for first 90 days
- Long-term strategic objectives
- Reporting relationship

**Para 4: Quotes**
- CEO quote on why this hire
- New executive quote on why this opportunity
- Substantive, not generic

### Boilerplate
- Standard company description
- Mention any leadership team context
- Stage and scale

## What Quotes Should Say

### CEO Quote (Bad)
"We are thrilled to welcome [Name] to the team. Their experience will be invaluable as we scale."

### CEO Quote (Good)
"For the past year, we've been searching for a leader who could take our [specific area] from [current state] to [future state]. [Name]'s track record at [Company] doing exactly this is why I'm confident we have the right person."

### Executive Quote (Bad)
"I'm excited to join such a dynamic team."

### Executive Quote (Good)
"Three things drew me here: [specific reason 1], [specific reason 2], and [specific reason 3]. I'm joining at a moment when [specific market/product condition] makes this work uniquely consequential."

## Multimedia Requirements
- Professional headshot
- Optional: video of new executive on company vision
- Optional: photo with CEO

## Quality Rubric for Executive Hires
- Hire is genuinely material
- Background substantively detailed
- Specific responsibilities outlined
- Substantive quotes from both sides
- Professional headshot included
- Strategic rationale clear
