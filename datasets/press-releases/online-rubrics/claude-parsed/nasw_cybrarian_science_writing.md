# NASW Cybrarian: Science Writing and PR Best Practices

## National Association of Science Writers
- Membership organization for science journalists and PIOs
- Cybrarian column tracks practical resources
- Annual conferences on science communication
- Code of ethics for science writing

## Standards for Science Press Releases

### Accuracy Standards
- Match study conclusions exactly
- Methodology summary clear
- Effect sizes given fully (relative + absolute)
- Limitations explicitly stated
- Funding sources disclosed
- Conflicts of interest noted

### Source Standards
- Principal investigator interviewed
- External expert quote when appropriate
- Patient/affected community voice when relevant
- Multiple perspectives on controversial topics
- No single-source claims on contested findings

### Framing Standards
- Distinguish preclinical from clinical
- Distinguish animal from human studies
- Distinguish association from causation
- Acknowledge replication needs
- Note generalizability limits

### Multimedia Standards
- Researcher photos with consent
- Methodology visualizations
- Data charts properly labeled
- Captions with proper credits
- Alt text for accessibility

## What Distinguishes Good Science PR
- Authors review before release
- External experts consulted
- Statistical reviewer engaged
- Clear separation of facts from speculation
- Future research clearly distinguished from current findings

## Common Anti-Patterns
- Press release more enthusiastic than paper
- "First", "breakthrough" without verification
- Subgroup findings hyped as primary
- Causal language for correlations
- Single-arm studies framed as definitive
- Surrogate endpoints framed as clinical outcomes

## Communication Practices
- Embargo respected universally
- Materials sent 48-72 hours before lift
- Author availability confirmed
- External expert contacts offered
- Methodology document available
- Full paper PDF shared

## Quality Rubric (NASW)
- Authors review before release
- Conclusions match paper
- Methodology summary clear
- Effect sizes given fully
- Limitations stated
- External experts cited
- Embargo respected
- Multimedia with alt text
