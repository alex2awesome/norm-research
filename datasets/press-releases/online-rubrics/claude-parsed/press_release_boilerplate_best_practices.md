# Press Release Boilerplate Best Practices

## Purpose and Definition
A boilerplate briefly describes the firm in a professional tone, highlighting key information so a reader can quickly grasp what the firm is, does, and where to get more details. It's the company's elevator pitch in written form.

## Length and Format
- Ideal length: 50-100 words
- Single paragraph
- Roughly 2-3 sentences

## Tone and Point of View
- Lean formal
- Use third person
- Factual, not promotional ("the best," "the greatest," "the only")

## Key Content to Include
- Clear, concise statement of what the company does
- One impressive statistic, award, or unique positioning for credibility
- Where you operate or market position (if relevant)
- Always end with website URL

## What to Avoid
- Buzzwords: "innovative," "world-class," "cutting-edge"
- Repeating information from other sections of the release
- Marketing-speak

## Placement
- At the end of the press release, before contact info
- Headed "About [Company]"

## Maintenance
- Keep boilerplate up-to-date
- Update with new awards, product lines, milestones
- Review at least annually
