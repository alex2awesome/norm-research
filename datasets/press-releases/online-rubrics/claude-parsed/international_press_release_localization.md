# International Press Release Localization Standards

## Translation vs Localization

### Translation
- Literal language conversion
- Closely matches source language
- Doesn't address cultural nuances

### Localization
- Adjusts for cultural relevance
- Adapts language, tone, references
- Audience expectations
- Local data, market insights
- Regulatory considerations

## Best Practices for Global Press Releases

### Strategic Approach
- Clear structure first
- Then localized translations
- Consider context, cultural norms, local regulations
- Prioritize intent and impact over literal translation

### Global Consistency + Local Flexibility
- Key messaging must be evident and same in all markets
- Allow flexibility for tailored content
- Approach adjusted to what works locally

## Cultural Adaptation Examples

### Holidays and Calendar
- Black Friday doesn't translate to China
- "Singles Day" (11/11) is Chinese equivalent
- Avoid scheduling around local religious festivals
- Account for time zones

### Cultural Sensitivities
- Color symbolism
- Number meanings
- Imagery (food, hand gestures)
- Names and naming conventions
- Hierarchy in quotes

## Distribution and Timing
- Schedule per local news cycles
- Account for cultural calendars
- Avoid bank holidays / religious festivals
- Time zone coordination
- Different deadlines

## Required Elements for International Releases

### Basic Localization
- Translated headline
- Translated body
- Local currency conversion
- Local units (metric/imperial)
- Local date format (DD/MM vs MM/DD)
- Phone number formats

### Cultural Adaptation
- Quotes from local executives
- Local case studies
- Local statistics
- References to local context
- Culturally appropriate imagery

### Regulatory Compliance
- GDPR (EU)
- Privacy laws
- Industry-specific regulations
- Disclosure requirements
- Language laws (e.g., French in Quebec)

## Implicit Quality Rubric for International Releases
1. Localized (not just translated)
2. Native speaker review
3. Cultural references appropriate
4. Local quote/spokesperson
5. Local case study
6. Currency, units, date format localized
7. Time zone-appropriate distribution
8. Regulatory compliance per market
9. Local PR contact
10. Translation back to source language reviewed
11. Brand voice maintained
12. Avoid culturally insensitive imagery
13. Local market-specific data
14. Local competitive context
15. Coordinated across markets
