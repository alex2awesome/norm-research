# O'Dwyer's PR News: Press Release Industry Trends

## State of the Industry
- Traditional wire services consolidating
- AI-generated releases flooding the market
- Journalists increasingly skeptical of unsourced claims
- Multimedia required, not optional
- Personalization becoming the differentiator

## Distribution Channel Effectiveness
- Direct journalist outreach: highest pickup
- Wire services: lowest pickup but high SEO
- Social media (founder-led): rising influence
- LinkedIn newsletters: emerging B2B channel
- Niche industry newsletters: high engagement

## Quality Standards Industry-Wide
- Multimedia inclusion now expected, not bonus
- Methodology disclosure for any data
- Spokesperson availability within 24 hours
- Embargo lift times in PT/ET specified
- Translation availability for international news

## Crisis Communication Standards
- Acknowledgment within 2 hours of news breaking
- Specific facts, not statements of values
- Named spokesperson with appropriate authority
- Action steps, not just apology
- Update cadence specified for ongoing situation

## ESG and Sustainability Communications
- Specific metrics, not vague commitments
- Third-party verification when claimed
- Timeline with milestones
- Acknowledgment of unsolved challenges
- Avoid greenwashing language

## DEI Communications Standards
- Demographic data with methodology
- Specific actions, not aspirational language
- Acknowledgment of where progress is needed
- Named accountability owner
- Avoid performative language

## What's Declining in Effectiveness
- Generic "leading provider" boilerplate
- Quote-stuffed releases
- PDF-only attachments
- Friday afternoon distribution
- Embargo without exclusivity

## What's Rising
- Original data reports
- Founder-voice authenticity
- Multimedia-first releases
- Cross-channel coordination
- Long-form context pieces alongside releases

## Quality Rubric (Industry Trends)
- Multimedia required
- Methodology disclosed
- Spokesperson available within 24h
- Specific over aspirational
- Cross-channel coordination
- Named accountability
