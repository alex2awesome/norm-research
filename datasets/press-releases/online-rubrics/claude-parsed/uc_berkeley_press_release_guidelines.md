# UC Berkeley Media Relations Press Release Guidelines

## Press Release Structure and Style
UC Berkeley press releases are usually a few pages in length, written as a news story, include quotes from the source(s) and give context to the news being reported.

## Communication Tools Available
- **Press release**: Most frequent communication tool, produced in consultation with requesters, written as a news story with quotes from involved parties and context.
- **Media advisory**: Quickly lists the "who, what, where and when" of information

## Timeline Requirements
UC Berkeley Media Relations needs about **two weeks' notice** to issue a press release. This allows time to:
- Gather information
- Do interviews
- Write the release
- Get approvals
- Decide which reporters to alert

## Press Release Boilerplate
A standardized brief paragraph describing UC Berkeley, providing journalists essential context about the university, used at the end of every press release.

## What Berkeley Considers Press Release-Worthy
- Significant research breakthroughs
- Academic achievements
- Campus programs of broad interest
- Major awards
- Newsworthy events

## Writing Approach
- Written as news story (not promotional)
- Includes quotes from sources
- Provides context
- Multiple pages for substantive stories
- Tone: journalistic

## Distribution
- Strategic targeting of relevant reporters
- Coordinated with researchers/department
- Often paired with embargo if tied to journal publication
