# Bushee, Matsumoto & Miller (2004) — Reg FD and the broad-disclosure rule for press releases

Reg FD (Selective Disclosure rule, 2000) requires firms to make material disclosures broadly available rather than selectively to favored analysts. Bushee et al. document the pre/post Reg FD shift in conference-call practice, with direct implications for press-release timing.

## Headline empirical findings
1. **Reg FD shifted firms toward broader access** for material disclosures (open conference calls, simultaneous press release distribution).
2. **Some firms reduced disclosure entirely** post-Reg FD to avoid the broad-disclosure obligation.
3. **Trading volume during open conference calls** suggests retail investors gained information access; some price discovery shifted to call timing.

## Reg FD–derived press release rubric criteria
1. **Broad simultaneous distribution.** Material non-public information must be released through a broadly disseminated press release *before* (or simultaneously with) any selective disclosure (analyst call, investor meeting, briefing).
2. **8-K filing alignment.** Material developments triggering Item 2.02 (results) or Item 8.01 (other events) Form 8-K must be filed with the SEC concurrent with the press release.
3. **Pre-call release window.** When a press release accompanies an earnings call, the release should be issued at least 30 minutes before call start so all participants have equal access.
4. **Wire-distribution audit.** Use a recognized broad-distribution wire (Business Wire, PR Newswire, Globe Newswire) — direct-to-analyst email is *not* broadly available.
5. **Recorded-call posting.** If selective remarks are made on a call, post the recording / transcript publicly within the same day.
6. **Forward-looking disclosure parity.** Forward-looking statements made on calls should appear in the underlying press release in the same form (no "extra" guidance available only to call attendees).
7. **Materiality assessment trail.** Document why the issuer concluded an item was or was not material; the press release strategy should match the materiality call.
8. **Anti-leakage protocol.** Embargoes for media briefings under Reg FD must end at the same instant as the press release goes broad — no "exclusive head-start" for favored journalists.

## Pickup-worthiness implication
Reg FD reshaped which press releases reach the market and when. A pickup-worthy modern earnings release is structured around the broad-distribution-first rule: the release is the canonical disclosure event; the call is supplementary. Firms attempting to shift information to selective channels face SEC enforcement, market sanctions, and class-action exposure — all of which downgrade pickup of subsequent releases.
