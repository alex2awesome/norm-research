# Press Release Headline Formula

## The Formula for Effective Press Release Headlines
- Be clear and state the main point directly
- Use active verbs like "launches" or "reveals" to add energy
- Include numbers for credibility
- Answer "why it matters" by focusing on benefits or outcomes

## Key Elements to Include
- Lead with company name or primary subject
- Use strong action words: "Opens," "Releases," "Partners"
- Include specifics: numbers, dates, dollar amounts
- Include keywords for SEO

## Strong Action Verbs (data-driven recommendations)
- "Allow," "Reveal," and "Show" are more attractive
- "Unveil" and "reveal" get more engagement than "launch"
- Other high-impact verbs: revolutionize, accelerate, empower, transform, simplify, enhance
- Replace weak verbs (announced, launched) with stronger alternatives (unveils, reveals, debuts)

## Length and Performance
- Headlines between 51-75 characters perform best for engagement
- Headlines under 100 characters generally
- For SEO: most important keywords in first 55-70 characters
- Headlines with strong active verbs drive 25% more clicks than passive ones

## Verb Tense
- Present tense is standard ("launches," "announces")
- Avoid past tense

## Headline Approaches
1. **Problem/solution**: identify a widespread problem ("Remote Team Communication Challenges") and present the solution ("New App Solves...")
2. **Milestone**: large specific numbers + active verbs like "Surpasses" that signal momentum
3. **Announcement**: company name + strong action verb + specific noun
4. **Benefit-led**: focus on outcome for audience

## Don'ts
- Don't include hyperlinks in headlines (hurts SEO)
- Don't use clickbait
- Don't use jargon
- Don't use unnecessary adjectives
- Don't exceed 100 characters
