# Wilcox & Cameron - Public Relations: Strategies and Tactics

## Status
One of the most widely used PR textbooks in academia. Companion volume "Public Relations Writing and Media Techniques" is the most comprehensive PR writing text.

## Press Release Writing Standards (Wilcox & Cameron framework)

### Six Standard Components Required
1. Letterhead/identifying information
2. Contact information at top
3. Release date / "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE"
4. Headline (centered, bold)
5. Dateline + lead paragraph
6. Body in inverted pyramid

### Key Quality Criteria
- **Newsworthiness** - Real news, not promotion
- **Accuracy** - Verifiable facts
- **Brevity** - 1-2 pages preferred
- **Clarity** - Simple, direct language
- **Style** - AP style throughout
- **Format** - Standard formatting expected by media

### Types of Press Releases (Wilcox)
1. **Announcement** - Personnel, mergers, acquisitions
2. **Spot news** - Breaking events
3. **Reaction** - Response to external events
4. **Bad news** - Crisis, scandal
5. **Local news** - Community-relevant
6. **Feature** - Soft news, human interest

### Pitching Strategy (Wilcox)
- Customize to outlet/journalist
- Send only to relevant beats
- Follow up appropriately (not aggressively)
- Build relationships

### Audience-Centered Approach
- Identify primary audience
- Tailor message to publics
- Multiple versions for different segments
- Localize where possible

## Common Pitfalls Wilcox Identifies
- Burying the lead
- Using marketing-speak
- Generic quotes
- Missing contact info
- Wrong format
- Wrong outlet targeting
- Sending too early or too late
- Following up too aggressively
- Not having a clear angle
- Including untested claims
