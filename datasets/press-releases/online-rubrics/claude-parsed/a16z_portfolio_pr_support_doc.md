# a16z Portfolio Support: PR and Comms Guide

## a16z's PR Philosophy
- Founders own the narrative — not the firm
- Quality matters more than quantity
- Long-term relationships beat short-term coverage
- Be different, not loud
- Let your work speak first

## When to Engage Press

**Always**
- Series A+ funding rounds
- Major product launches that change capability
- Significant customer wins (Fortune 500 logos)
- Industry-shifting data reports
- Crisis communication

**Sometimes**
- Notable hires (well-known executives)
- Strategic partnerships with major players
- Awards from credible bodies
- Conference keynote opportunities

**Almost Never**
- Minor product updates
- Internal milestones
- Junior hires
- Pay-to-play awards
- Generic thought leadership

## The a16z Pitch Template

### Subject Line
"[News in 6-8 words] — [Why specific reporter]"

### Body
- Para 1: The news (specific, concrete)
- Para 2: Why it matters in your beat
- Para 3: What I can offer (interview, demo, exclusive, data)
- Sign-off: Founder name, phone, calendar link

## Pitching Different Tiers

**Tier 1 — Major Outlets (NYT, WSJ, Bloomberg)**
- Embargoed exclusive only
- Founder available immediately
- 2 days advance notice minimum
- Customer references ready
- Investor reference ready

**Tier 2 — Trade Press (TechCrunch, Information, Axios)**
- Coordinated release
- Multiple sources offered
- Demo video pre-recorded
- Press kit comprehensive

**Tier 3 — Newsletters and Podcasts**
- Founder availability flexible
- Conversational format
- Specific story angle
- Audience-fit demonstrated

## Crisis Communication Protocol
- Notify investors within hours
- Brief response team
- Single spokesperson designated
- Specific facts only — no speculation
- Update cadence specified
- Long-form context piece if needed

## Quality Rubric (a16z)
- Worth-doing test passed
- Founder owns narrative
- Tier-appropriate distribution
- Personalized pitches
- Customer + investor proof points
- Honest, specific claims
- Crisis protocol if needed
