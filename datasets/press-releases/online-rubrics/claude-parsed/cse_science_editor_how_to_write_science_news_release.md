# How and Why to Write a Science News Release (CSE Science Editor)

## Purpose and Importance

Science news releases serve to alert reporters and the public about research findings:

- **Boosting citations**: Media coverage of research correlates with increased article citations
- **Shaping coverage**: "Exaggerations in news releases about health-related research findings are reflected in subsequent news stories" about those findings
- **Direct audience reach**: Many outlets (ScienceDaily, Phys.org) republish releases directly to millions of readers monthly

## Getting Started

Before writing, clarify two things:
1. **Your goal** — Are you targeting a journal's core readers, funding agencies, or peer institutions?
2. **Your audience** — Who needs to understand this research?

### Essential Preparation
- Read the journal article thoroughly
- Interview the research team about their central question, key findings, surprises, and real-world context
- Understand how the work fits into existing knowledge and what questions remain

## Writing Structure

### The Headline
Must be "concise, catchy, and intellectually honest." Avoid hype while capturing reader interest.

### The Lede (Opening Paragraph)
"The lede must tell readers what's interesting about the story and why you're telling them about it now."
Avoid technical jargon in the opening; use accessible language while maintaining accuracy.

### Body Content
Include:
- Research question being addressed
- Concise description of findings
- Importance and context
- Methodology used
- Study limitations
- Future research directions
- Researcher names and affiliations
- Publication details with journal link
- Funding sources
- At least one researcher quote

### Length and Readability
"A news release should be as long as it needs to be—say what you need to say and then stop."

**Key principle**: Define technical terms when first introducing them, then use terminology consistently throughout.

## Review and Editing Process
1. **Researcher review** — Authors must verify technical accuracy and context
2. **Editorial editing** — Third-party editing ensures clarity and catches errors
3. **Balance** — Maintain accessibility while incorporating researcher feedback

## Distribution Strategy
- Publish on organizational newsroom websites
- Send to reporter mailing lists in relevant fields
- Contact reporters individually with story links
- Share with funding agencies for amplification
- Post to distribution sites (EurekAlert, AlphaGalileo, Newswise)
- Feed content to news aggregators

## Key Principle
"Yes, a news release must be honest and accurate about the research—that is nonnegotiable."
