# EurekAlert! Content Eligibility Guidelines Summary

## Core Timeliness Requirement
Content must describe information "first made public within 90 days of content submission." For publications with multiple dates, the most recent applies.

## Embargo Policy
Embargoes are permitted only when set by journal publishers or scientific meeting organizers announcing initial public release. Embargoes are prohibited if information is already publicly available or unrelated to publications, meetings, or press events.

## Eligible News Release Categories

**Peer-Reviewed Publications:** Studies in peer-reviewed journals, completed clinical trials, and peer-reviewed editorials/commentaries.

**Reports & Proceedings:** Scientific meeting presentations, government reports, clinical trial phase completions, validated survey data, and new research databases.

**Business Announcements:** Collaborations, mergers, regulatory approvals, clinical trial launches, forecasts, and policy statements.

**Meeting Announcements:** Upcoming or recently-held scientific meetings with media/public access.

**Grants & Awards:** Funding announcements and award nomination calls with societal impact.

**Book Announcements:** New science-related publications.

## Ineligible Content
- Unpublished research undergoing peer review
- Opinion-based editorials lacking peer-reviewed support
- Speeches and non-press-release formats
- Potentially biased findings with undisclosed conflicts of interest

## Multimedia Requirements
All images, videos, and audio must be cleared for third-party distribution. Stock image content is prohibited. AI-generated multimedia is ineligible except in documented AI/machine learning research cases.
