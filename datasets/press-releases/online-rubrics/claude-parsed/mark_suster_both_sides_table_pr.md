# Mark Suster (Both Sides of the Table): PR for Startups

## My Core Beliefs About Startup PR
- Most founders waste money on PR firms too early
- Best PR comes from substantive achievements, not announcements
- Build relationships before you need them
- Reporters remember founders who didn't bullshit them
- Long-term reputation > short-term coverage

## When to Hire a PR Firm
- $5M+ ARR
- Multiple ongoing news streams
- Founder no longer has time for media
- Strategic positioning around major moments
- IPO or acquisition prep

## Before Then: DIY PR
- Build your reporter list manually
- Use HARO to become a quoted source
- Write thoughtful blog posts (not press releases)
- Tweet substantively in your space
- Speak at conferences and panels

## The Funding Announcement Done Right
- Lead with what you're building, not the dollar amount
- Specific use of funds (engineers in X, expansion to Y)
- Substantive quote from lead investor (reasoning, not cheerleading)
- Customer logo or quote as proof point
- Spokesperson available for follow-up
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade publication

## The Funding Announcement Done Wrong
- Headline that's just "Company Raises $X"
- "We're excited" quote from CEO
- Investor quote that's promotional fluff
- No customer or use case validation
- Wire-blasted to 5,000 contacts
- Founder unavailable for interviews

## The Pitch That Always Works
"Hi [Reporter],

Saw your piece on [topic] last [day]. Wanted to share something relevant.

We just [specific news]. The interesting angle for your readers is [insight relevant to their beat].

Happy to set up a 15-minute call this week if useful. Have data and customer references ready.

[Phone number]
[Founder name]"

## Long-Term Relationship Building
- Be a useful source on stories you're NOT in
- Send relevant articles to reporters even when not pitching
- Remember things they care about
- Don't take "no" personally
- Recommend other sources when you're not the right fit

## Quality Rubric (Mark Suster)
- DIY before agency
- Substantive, not promotional
- Real news worth telling
- Personalized pitch
- Founder voice
- Long-term relationship building
- Honest framing
