# IABC - International Association of Business Communicators - Global Standards

## About IABC
The International Association of Business Communicators (IABC) is a not-for-profit professional association headquartered in San Francisco with more than 13,000 members in 100+ chapters across 62 countries.

## IABC Global Standard for Communication
IABC's Global Standard centers on six core principles for excellence in communication. Application to press release writing:

### 1. Strategic
Communication must be strategic - aligned with organizational goals. Press releases should serve a defined business or institutional purpose.

### 2. Engaging
Communication must engage stakeholders. Releases must capture attention and provide value to readers.

### 3. Listening
Communication is two-way. Press releases should anticipate audience reaction and address stakeholder concerns.

### 4. Ethical
Communication must adhere to high ethical standards. Truth, transparency, respect for diverse views.

### 5. Consistent
Messaging must be consistent across channels. Press releases align with web content, social, internal comms.

### 6. Analytical
Measure and improve. Track release performance and refine approach.

## IABC Code of Ethics for Professional Communicators (Press Release Application)

### Key Provisions
1. **Truthful**: Communicate accurately and avoid distortion
2. **Foster cultural values**: Respect human dignity, free speech, diverse cultures
3. **Sensitive to others' beliefs**: Respect differences
4. **Public interest**: Engage in only legal communications
5. **Refrain from taking advantage**: Don't exploit gained information for personal gain
6. **Disclose conflicts of interest**: Make clients/employers aware of conflicts
7. **Refuse undisclosed gifts**: Avoid conflicts of interest
8. **Independence**: Don't guarantee specific results outside their reach
9. **Honest in their work**: Be honest about credentials, services, capabilities

## Implicit Press Release Rubric (IABC framework)
- Tied to clear strategic goal
- Engages target audience emotionally and intellectually
- Anticipates and addresses stakeholder questions
- Truthful, no distortion
- Consistent with brand voice
- Includes metrics for evaluation
- Discloses conflicts where applicable
- Respects diverse audiences
- Honest about capabilities and outcomes
