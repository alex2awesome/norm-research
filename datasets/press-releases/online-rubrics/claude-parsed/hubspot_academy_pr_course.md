# HubSpot Academy: PR Foundations

## Course Overview
Free 2-hour course on PR fundamentals, distributed by HubSpot.

## Module 1: What PR Is and Isn't
- PR is reputation management, not advertising
- Earned coverage > paid placements
- Long-term relationships > one-time wins
- Substance > spin
- PR fits inside marketing, not above

## Module 2: The Press Release
**Standard Components**
- Headline (news-first, 80 chars)
- Subheadline (optional)
- Dateline (city, date)
- Lede (5 W's in 50 words)
- Body (3-4 paragraphs)
- Quote (25-35 words from C-level)
- Boilerplate (75 words about company)
- Contact info (named, with phone)
- "###" end marker

**Quality Checks**
- News passes "outside-the-org" test
- Headline tested with "would I tweet this?"
- Quote sounds human
- Multimedia attached
- Reviewed by outside reader
- AP style throughout

## Module 3: Pitching
- Personalized email > mass blast
- Subject line: news in 8 words
- Body: 100-200 words
- Single clear ask
- Phone number provided
- Available for follow-up

## Module 4: Distribution Strategy
- Wire services for SEO (not pickup)
- Direct outreach for actual coverage
- Social media for amplification
- Newsroom for archive
- Newsletter inclusion when relevant

## Module 5: Measurement
- Coverage tier and sentiment
- Pickup velocity (24-hour, 7-day)
- Brand mention tracking
- Pipeline impact (30-day)
- Long-tail SEO (90-day)

## Module 6: Crisis Communication
- Acknowledge within 2 hours
- Specific facts, not vague statements
- Named spokesperson with authority
- Concrete corrective actions
- Update cadence specified

## Quality Rubric (HubSpot Academy)
- Newsworthy substance
- Standard format components
- Personalized distribution
- Measurement beyond impressions
- Crisis protocol if needed
- Long-term reputation focus
