# Churnalism and Hype in Science Communication: South African Study

## Background
Studies have shown that news stories exaggerated associations 81% of the time if claims were also exaggerated in the press release. When press releases improperly extrapolated animal findings to humans, the news extrapolated too — 86% of the time.

## Definition of "Churnalism"
"Churnalism" refers to the practice of journalists rewriting press releases as news with little or no original reporting, fact-checking, or context.

## Common Hype Mechanisms in Press Releases
1. **Causal overreach** - Correlation framed as causation
2. **Generalization** - Animal/cell findings applied to humans
3. **Magnitude inflation** - Effect sizes exaggerated
4. **Implication leaps** - Speculative practical applications
5. **Novelty exaggeration** - "First" claims overstated
6. **Cherry-picking** - Highlighting subset of results
7. **Caveat omission** - Limitations dropped
8. **Quote inflation** - Researchers say "groundbreaking"
9. **Funding/COI omission**

## Anti-Hype Press Release Standards

### A non-hyped press release should:
- Match the strength of claims in source paper
- Include all major caveats
- Disclose study limitations
- Use causal language only when supported (RCT, mechanistic)
- Specify sample type (animal, in vitro, human)
- Provide effect sizes in absolute terms
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Avoid words like "breakthrough," "revolutionary," "cure" without basis

### Press release intervention study (Sumner & colleagues 2019)
Press officers sent press releases to original scientists who:
- Checked evidence strength
- Flagged causal-correlational mix-ups
- Identified missing limitations
- Returned corrected versions

This intervention reduced exaggeration without reducing news pickup.

## Implicit Rubric
A press release passes the anti-churnalism test if it:
- Could be reproduced verbatim without misleading readers
- Includes all material caveats
- Matches journal article language strength
- Specifies study design clearly
- Includes counterfactual framing where appropriate
- Discloses funding sources
- Provides original article DOI/link
