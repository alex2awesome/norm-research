# Joanna Penn: Indie Author PR Guide

## Why Most Author PR Fails
- Authors confuse PR with marketing
- Generic "buy my book" messaging
- Wrong outlets pitched (NYT for indie genre fiction)
- Press releases sent without hook
- No long-term audience building

## When PR Works for Authors
- Award nominations or wins
- Original data report (e.g., research for non-fiction)
- Hot topic angle (book ties to current events)
- Industry recognition
- Personal story with broader appeal

## The Indie Author Press Release
- Headline: hook tied to news, not "Author Releases Book"
- Lede: 5 W's about the book + author + relevance
- Body: relevant excerpt or insight
- Quote: from author or industry figure
- Boilerplate: author bio with credibility markers
- Contact: author or publicist info
- Multimedia: book cover, author photo, related visuals

## Pitching for Indie Authors
- Bookstagrammers and BookTok creators
- Niche genre podcasts
- Industry-specific media
- Local newspapers (regional angle)
- Bloggers who review your genre
- Newsletter writers in your niche

## What Doesn't Work
- Wire-only distribution
- Mass-blasting NYT, WSJ
- Generic "available now" pitches
- No multimedia
- Self-published without distinction
- "Bestseller" claims without verification

## Long-Term Author PR Strategy
- Build email newsletter (own audience)
- Podcast appearances regularly
- Collaboration with other authors
- Genre community engagement
- Consistent content output (blog, social)

## Quality Rubric (Indie Author)
- Hook tied to relevant news or trend
- Multimedia (cover, author photo)
- Niche pitching to relevant outlets
- Author voice authentic
- Long-term audience building alongside one-time PR
- Honest claims (no fake "bestseller")
