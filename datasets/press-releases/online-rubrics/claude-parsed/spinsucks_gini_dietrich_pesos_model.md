# Spin Sucks (Gini Dietrich): PESO Model and Press Releases

## The PESO Model
- **P**aid: Sponsored content, ads, paid placements
- **E**arned: Traditional media coverage from pitches
- **S**hared: Social media engagement and sharing
- **O**wned: Your blog, newsroom, newsletter

## Where Press Releases Fit
- Earned media tactic primarily
- Owned media if posted to your newsroom
- Can be amplified through Shared
- Should NOT be Paid (loses credibility)

## What Makes a Press Release Worth Writing
- News that crosses 2+ PESO categories
- Provides material for blog posts (Owned)
- Worth sharing on social (Shared)
- Has an angle media will pick up (Earned)

## Spin Sucks's "PR Manifesto" Principles
- Transparency over manipulation
- Relationships over transactions
- Substance over style
- Long-term reputation over short-term coverage
- Measurement over assumption

## Press Release Quality Tests (Spin Sucks)
1. Will it create conversation or just announcements?
2. Does it have a "so what?" answer for the reader?
3. Can it be excerpted by other media?
4. Will it still be relevant in 6 months?
5. Does it advance our brand narrative?

## Common Anti-Patterns
- "Leading provider of solutions"
- "We are excited to announce"
- Press releases that read like advertisements
- Quotes from executives who didn't review them
- Releases timed without consideration of news cycle

## Modern Best Practices
- Multimedia (photos, video, infographics) required
- Mobile-first formatting
- Social media kit accompanying release
- Internal stakeholders briefed before release
- Analytics tracking on landing page

## Quality Rubric (Spin Sucks)
- Worth-doing test passed
- Substance over hype
- Multimedia included
- Cross-PESO amplification planned
- Transparent and honest
- Conversation-starter, not just announcement
