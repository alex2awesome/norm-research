# Video News Release (VNR) Standards

## Definition
A VNR is a video segment made to look like a news report, created by PR firm, ad agency, marketing firm, corporation, government agency, or non-profit. Provided to TV newsrooms.

## Production Criteria

### Content and Format
- No dissolves or fancy effects
- Straightforward footage stations can edit
- Length: 5 to 7.5 minutes total package

### Technical Production
- Longer "handles" on shot ends (for transitions)
- Split audio tracks for editor flexibility
- Minimal color correction (broadcast legal)
- Standard NTSC/PAL specs
- Multiple aspect ratios (16:9, 4:3)
- High-quality audio (separate music/dialogue)

### Standard Components
- Scripted narration
- B-roll footage (for stations to use)
- Interviews with designated experts
- Suggested anchor lead-ins
- Lower-third graphics templates
- Closing tags

## Distribution
- Satellite feeds (C-band traditional)
- Now: digital downloads from PR distribution sites
- Continuous loop feeds for stations to capture
- Ready for air with minimal editing

## FCC and Labeling Standards
- Federal scrutiny since early 2000s
- Sponsorship identification requirements
- Public advocacy groups call for clearer labeling
- Stations should disclose VNR origin

## CPSC Recall VNR Guidelines
For product recall releases:
- Clear identification of product
- Specific safety hazard
- Action consumers should take
- Refund/repair process
- Contact info for affected consumers

## Implicit VNR Quality Rubric
1. Length 5-7.5 minutes
2. B-roll separate from package
3. Multiple shots/angles for editor choice
4. Clear audio (split tracks)
5. Sound bites in clean format
6. Suggested intro script
7. Lower-third graphics provided
8. High-resolution (broadcast quality)
9. Standard aspect ratios
10. Color-corrected for broadcast
11. Captioned/transcribed
12. Sponsorship clearly identifiable
13. Distributed through standard channels
14. Subject expert visible/quoted
15. Story arc (beginning, middle, end)

## Modern Digital VNR
- Web-ready short cuts (60 seconds)
- Social media format (vertical, square)
- Embeddable players
- Closed captions
- Multiple language tracks
- YouTube optimized
