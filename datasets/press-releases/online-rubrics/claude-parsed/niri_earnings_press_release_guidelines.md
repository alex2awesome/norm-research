# NIRI Standards of Practice for Investor Relations Press Releases

## About NIRI
NIRI (National Investor Relations Institute) is the largest professional investor relations association, representing over 1,500 publicly held companies and $12 trillion in stock market capitalization. NIRI began setting standards for the investor relations profession in 1996.

## Core Standard
NIRI believes the best practice, under any circumstances, is to first issue a broadly disseminated news release containing the new material information.

## Earnings Announcements

### Timing
- Earnings press releases should generally be issued as soon as practicable after quarter-end
- Plan release timing around market hours (typically before market open or after market close)
- Provide minimum 4 hours between release and conference call

### Content Requirements (FEI/NIRI Joint Guidelines)
- Headline summarizing financial highlights
- Summary section with key metrics (revenue, EPS, guidance)
- Comparison to prior periods (yoy, sequential)
- Discussion and analysis from management
- Reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP measures (Reg G)
- CEO/CFO quote with strategic context
- Forward-looking guidance (if provided)
- Conference call details
- Forward-looking statements safe harbor
- About the company (boilerplate)
- IR contact information

### Disclosure Standards
- Maintain consistent format quarter-to-quarter
- Same metrics each period (don't cherry-pick)
- Disclose changes in non-GAAP definitions
- Provide segment information
- Discuss business drivers, not just numbers

## Best Practices for IR Releases
- Adopt a written disclosure policy
- Designate authorized spokespersons
- Maintain quiet periods around earnings
- Address one-time items transparently
- Provide reconciliations and definitions
