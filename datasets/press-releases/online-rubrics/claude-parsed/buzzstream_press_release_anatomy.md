# BuzzStream's Complete Press Release Guide

## What is a Press Release?

A press release is "an official statement or proclamation announcing news that provides relevant information to the media." It's fundamentally informational rather than editorial—designed to announce newsworthy developments that journalists can build upon.

## Four Main Types

**1. Product or Brand Launches**
Announces new offerings to reach potential customers through industry publications. Only warrant press releases when sufficiently novel; minor updates suit email campaigns instead.

**2. Company News Announcements**
Covers executive hires, partnerships, mergers, awards, and milestones. The key test: Does this impact people outside the organization?

**3. Event Announcements**
Promotes conferences, webinars, or in-person gatherings. Primary goal is attendance; include the 5 W's throughout.

**4. Data-Driven Reports**
Releases research with proprietary insights. Journalists prize these because they establish thought leadership and remain relevant for years.

## Essential Anatomy Components

- **Headline**: Concise, engaging, benefit-focused
- **Dateline**: Publication date
- **Lead-in**: Branded opening statement
- **Key Facts**: 8-13 newsworthy takeaways
- **Quote(s)**: 2-4 sentences from executives or stakeholders
- **Call-to-Action**: Registration links, landing pages, or website URLs
- **Boilerplate**: Company background for unfamiliar readers
- **Contact Information**: PR team details as media gatekeeper
- **Media Assets**: Infographics, videos, or data visualizations when available

## Five Ready-to-Use Templates

The guide includes complete templates for general releases, product launches, company announcements, event promotions, and data reports.

## Pro Tips

- Target 600-800 words for standard announcements; data reports may exceed 1,000
- Include visuals for better engagement
- Proofread multiple times (consider tools like Grammarly)
- Distribute strategically using platforms like BuzzStream for tracking opens and clicks
