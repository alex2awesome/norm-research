# Lenny's Newsletter: PR for Product Managers

## When PR Is Actually Worth Doing
- Major product launch (not minor releases)
- Funding announcement (only if Series A+)
- New executive hire (only if name-brand)
- Industry-shifting data report
- Crisis or controversy response

## When NOT to Do PR
- Minor feature ships
- Hiring junior team members
- Vanity awards
- "Thought leadership" with no actual point
- Anything you'd be embarrassed by in 6 months

## The Product Launch Release Formula
- Hook: What's now possible that wasn't before
- Mechanism: How does the product actually work
- Proof: Beta customer logo or quote
- Stakes: Why this matters to the industry
- Action: Where to try it

## Quote Anti-Patterns
- "We're excited to announce..."
- "Today marks a milestone..."
- "Our mission has always been..."
- Quotes that contain marketing taglines
- Quotes attributed to executives who didn't say them

## Quote Patterns That Work
- Specific user complaint that drove the work
- Counter-intuitive design decision and why
- Hard tradeoff the team wrestled with
- Surprising number from internal data
- Honest admission of what's still unsolved

## Distribution Order
1. Embargoed exclusive to top trade publication
2. Hours later: pitch to 5-10 secondary outlets
3. Then wire release for SEO
4. Then post on company newsroom
5. Then social and community channels

## Metrics That Actually Matter
- Quality of coverage > quantity
- Inbound from VCs, partners, customers
- Hiring pipeline lift
- Sales lead lift in 30 days
- Long-tail SEO from earned coverage

## Implicit Quality Rubric
- Worth-doing test passed (real news)
- Hook describes new possibility
- Real proof point
- Spokesperson quote that sounds human
- Embargo-then-wire distribution sequence
- Tied to measurable business outcome
