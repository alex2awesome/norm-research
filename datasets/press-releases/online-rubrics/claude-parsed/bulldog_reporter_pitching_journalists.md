# Bulldog Reporter: Pitching Journalists Best Practices

## Building the Right Media List
- Start with 20-30 reporters who actively cover your space
- Use Cision/Muck Rack for contact info, not bulk lists
- Read 5+ recent articles per reporter before adding
- Categorize by: beat, frequency, openness to PR
- Update quarterly — beats shift constantly

## The First Email
- Subject line: news + specific angle (under 60 chars)
- Greeting: First name only
- Para 1: Why I'm pitching THIS reporter (refer to recent work)
- Para 2: The news (one specific paragraph)
- Para 3: What I can offer (interview, demo, exclusive, data)
- Sign-off: Direct phone, email, calendar link

## What Reporters Notice
- Whether you've actually read their work
- Whether your story fits their beat
- Whether you understand their deadline cycle
- Whether you can deliver on what you offer
- Whether you're a relationship or transaction

## Building Long-Term Relationships
- Be a useful source on stories you're NOT in
- Send relevant items even when not pitching your client
- Respect "no" gracefully
- Remember personal details (when appropriate)
- Connect them with sources outside your client roster

## Pitching Different Outlet Types
**Daily Newspapers**
- Pitch by 10am for next-day consideration
- Local angle essential
- Quick turnaround on requests
- Multiple sources expected

**Trade Publications**
- Industry expertise expected
- Technical depth welcome
- Longer lead times (1-2 weeks)
- Product reviews possible

**Broadcast TV**
- Visual story essential
- Talent available for live shot
- Timing tied to news cycle
- B-roll and sound bites useful

**Podcasts**
- Pitch the host, not network
- Listen to recent episodes first
- Suggest specific episode angle
- Spokesperson must be conversational
- Tee up to 3 talking points

## Quality Rubric (Bulldog Reporter)
- Hand-built, current media list
- Personalized opening referencing recent work
- Story angle, not generic announcement
- Right format for outlet type
- Relationship-first approach
- Follow-through on what you offer
