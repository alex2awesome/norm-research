# Academic Press Release Writing Grading Rubric

## Standard Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Structure and Format (20%)
Adherence to standard press release structure:
- Headline
- Dateline
- Introduction (lead)
- Body
- Boilerplate
- Contact information
- "###" or "-30-" end marker

### 2. Clarity (20%)
- Clear, concise communication
- Reader can understand without ambiguity
- Effective transmission of necessary information
- Free from confusing language

### 3. Content (25%)
- Relevant information
- Accurate facts
- Engaging to reader
- Newsworthy
- All 5Ws + H addressed
- Appropriate quotes

### 4. Grammar and Spelling (15%)
- No spelling errors
- Correct grammar
- Proper punctuation
- AP style adherence

### 5. Tone and Voice (10%)
- Third person
- Active voice
- Journalistic (not promotional)
- Audience-appropriate

### 6. Distribution Strategy (10%)
- Target audience identified
- Distribution plan articulated
- Timing rationale
- Outlet selection

## Five-Level Rating Scale

### 5 - Exemplary
- All criteria met excellently
- Publication-ready
- No edits needed
- Distinctive and compelling

### 4 - Proficient
- Most criteria met well
- Minor edits needed
- Clear and effective
- Well-organized

### 3 - Adequate
- Basic criteria met
- Some revision needed
- Functional but not distinctive
- Acceptable for purpose

### 2 - Developing
- Several criteria not met
- Significant revision needed
- Some clarity/accuracy issues
- Below professional standard

### 1 - Beginning
- Most criteria not met
- Major revision required
- Lacks essential elements
- Not publication-quality

## Implicit Press Release Quality Rubric (Academic Use)

Score 1-5 on each:
- [ ] Format adherence
- [ ] Clarity
- [ ] Content quality
- [ ] Grammar/spelling
- [ ] Tone appropriate
- [ ] News value
- [ ] Quote quality
- [ ] Audience targeting

Total: out of 40 points
