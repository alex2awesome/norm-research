# Press Kit / Media Kit Components

## Press Kit vs Media Kit Distinction

### Press Kit
- Tied to specific announcement
- 5Ws focused
- Multimedia for the story
- Time-sensitive
- Distributed with press release

### Media Kit
- Fuller picture of brand identity
- Always available
- Comprehensive
- Updated periodically
- Self-service for journalists

## Required Components

### Company Information
- Mission and values statement
- Company overview (one-pager)
- History and key milestones
- Awards and recognitions
- Office locations
- Employee count
- Industry positioning

### Press Releases
- 3-5 recent newsworthy releases
- Archive link to all releases
- Categorized by topic

### Media Assets
- High-resolution logos (multiple formats)
- Product images
- Headshots of leadership
- Office/facility photos
- Brand guidelines
- Color palettes
- Stock photo libraries

### Leadership / People
- Bios of executives
- Professional headshots
- Quotes/sound bites
- Speaker availability
- Areas of expertise

### Media Coverage / Earned Media
- Links to past press features
- Interview clips
- Magazine articles
- Podcast appearances
- Video clips

### Statistics / Fact Sheets
- Key metrics
- Industry stats
- Customer demographics
- Geographic reach
- Year-over-year growth

### Contact Information
- Direct press contacts
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Social media handles
- Website URLs
- Time zones

### Additional Elements
- Videos (corporate, product)
- Case studies
- Customer testimonials
- Awards (with images of trophies)
- White papers
- Research reports
- Brand guidelines
- Annual reports

## Format and Distribution

### Digital Press Kit
- Newsroom landing page
- Downloadable PDF
- Embedded multimedia
- Mobile-friendly
- Searchable

### Physical Press Kit (rare today)
- Folder with materials
- USB with multimedia
- Printed business card
- Used at conferences/events

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Press Kits

A press kit is high-quality if:
1. Easy to find on website
2. All assets downloadable
3. Logos in multiple formats (SVG, PNG, JPG)
4. High-resolution images
5. Updated within 6 months
6. Includes recent press releases
7. Has expert bios with photos
8. Provides quotable quotes
9. Has video assets
10. Has clear press contact
11. Has fact sheet
12. Has past media coverage
13. Mobile-accessible
14. Categorized for navigation
15. Brand guidelines included
