# Princeton University Editorial Style Guide

## Foundation
Princeton's Editorial Style Guide helps University communicators follow a consistent style for print and online materials. Uses The Associated Press Stylebook for published content, with Princeton-specific exceptions.

## Press Release Standards

### Purpose of Press Releases
News releases help make work accessible to everyday people:
- Inform media of University events and research
- Promote Princeton expertise
- Reach diverse audiences

### What Princeton Communications Helps With
- Drafting press releases
- Coordinating with academic departments
- Identifying experts
- Distributing strategically
- Following up with media

### Standard Format
- AP style throughout
- Princeton-specific name usage (e.g., "Princeton University," not "Princeton" on first reference)
- Faculty titles consistent
- Department names consistent
- Standard boilerplate

## Princeton-Specific Style Considerations
- "Princeton" preferred after first reference
- Faculty titles: full title on first reference, last name only after
- Department names: official name on first reference
- "alumni" (men/mixed), "alumnae" (women)
- "the University" capitalized when referring to Princeton

## Implicit Quality Rubric
- Follows AP style with Princeton exceptions
- Accessible to general audience
- Promotes expertise without overhype
- Strategic distribution
- Coordinated with academic stakeholders
- Standard boilerplate and contacts
