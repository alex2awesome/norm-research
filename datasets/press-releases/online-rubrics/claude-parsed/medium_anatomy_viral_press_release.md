# Anatomy of a Viral Press Release

## What Makes a Release "Viral" (Not Just Picked Up)
- Triggers emotional reaction (surprise, outrage, delight)
- Gets shared by reporters BEFORE it's published
- Has a "tweetable" moment — one stat or quote that fits in 280 characters
- Creates a hashtag-worthy phrase
- Spawns thinkpieces and reaction articles for days afterward

## The Three-Layer Release Structure
**Layer 1 — The Hook (first 50 words)**
Surprising stat + clear stakes + name a recognizable entity

**Layer 2 — The Substance (next 150 words)**
Methodology if data; timeline if news; before/after if change

**Layer 3 — The Quotable (final 100 words)**
One quote that a reporter could lift verbatim into the headline

## Viral Triggers
- David vs. Goliath framing
- Counter-intuitive finding
- Concrete villain
- Personal stakes for an identifiable group
- Money figures (especially round numbers)
- Generational framing ("Gen Z is...")

## Distribution Tactics for Virality
- Brief 2-3 trusted reporters under embargo first
- Time release for Tuesday-Thursday, 6-9am ET
- Avoid Friday afternoon (forgotten by Monday)
- Avoid major news days
- Have spokesperson on standby for first hour

## Common Mistakes That Kill Virality
- Burying the lede
- Quote attributed to no one important
- No real number in the headline
- Too much corporate hedging language
- Releasing on Friday or before holiday

## Implicit Rubric for Viral Potential
- Hook within first 50 words
- One tweetable quote or stat
- Recognizable name or entity invoked
- Concrete numerical anchor
- Counter-intuitive or emotional element
- Optimal time/day of release
