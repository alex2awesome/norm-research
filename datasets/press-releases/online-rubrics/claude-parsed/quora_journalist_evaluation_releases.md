# Quora: How Journalists Evaluate Press Releases

## The 4-Second Filter
Journalists give each release roughly 4 seconds before deciding to read or delete. The decision is made on:
- Sender name (known source vs. unknown)
- Subject line clarity
- Time of arrival (during deadline = delete)
- Whether the news affects their beat

## The 30-Second Filter
If they open the release, they spend 30 seconds scanning:
- Headline tells them the actual news
- Lede paragraph has the 5 W's
- Quote on first page (signals real source)
- Spokesperson contact in standard place
- Multimedia available

## The "Is This a Story?" Test
Reporters mentally check:
- Is there an angle here that's not just promotional?
- Can I verify the claims?
- Will my editor approve?
- Does this fit our publication's voice?
- Have we covered this exact topic recently?

## What Increases Coverage Probability
- Personal email vs. wire distribution (10x increase)
- Exclusive offered (3x increase)
- Embargo with notice (2x increase, if reporter trusted)
- Pre-built data visualizations (1.5x increase)
- Reporter has covered the company before (2x increase)

## What Decreases Coverage Probability
- Sent during major news event (anything global pushes you to the bottom)
- Sent on Friday afternoon
- Subject line uses "groundbreaking" or "revolutionary"
- Boilerplate longer than the news
- Quote attributed to PR firm
- Multiple bcc/cc'd recipients visible

## Editor's Filter (Second Stage)
Even if reporter wants to write, editor evaluates:
- Audience interest
- Newsworthiness vs. promotional balance
- Differentiation from existing coverage
- SEO opportunity
- Visual story potential

## Quality Rubric From Journalists' Side
- Subject line accurate and specific
- Newsworthy beyond promotional value
- Story angle visible immediately
- Source verifiable
- Multimedia ready
- Timing strategic
- Personalized to beat
