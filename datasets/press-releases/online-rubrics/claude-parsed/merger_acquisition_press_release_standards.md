# Merger & Acquisition Press Release Standards

## Standard Structure
- Headline
- Summary
- Lead
- Body
- Quotes
- Boilerplate
- Contact information

## Lead Paragraph Must Address
- WHO is involved (acquirer + target)
- WHAT the transaction is (merger, acquisition, joint venture)
- WHEN it occurs (or expected close date)
- WHY it matters to the market

## Required Content Elements

### Deal Specifics
- Deal type and structure (merger vs acquisition)
- Purchase price (if disclosed)
- Form of consideration (cash, stock, mix)
- Expected accretion/dilution
- Expected synergies (if any)
- Expected close date
- Conditions to close
- Approvals required (regulatory, shareholder)

### Strategic Rationale
- Strategic fit
- Combined company description
- Market position
- Customer impact
- Geographic expansion
- Product/technology synergies

### Financial Details
- Revenue/EBITDA impact
- Pro forma financials
- Multiple paid (if disclosed)
- Financing details
- Advisor fees (rare)

### Governance
- Board representation
- Management changes
- Headquarters
- Brand strategy

## SEC Requirements

### Form 8-K Filing
- Required if material to acquirer
- Filed within 4 business days
- Press release often filed as exhibit

### Press Release Should Include
- Forward-looking statements safe harbor
- Cautionary language
- Tender offer / proxy statement references (where applicable)
- "Additional information" notice

## Stakeholder Considerations
- Customer reassurance
- Employee impact (if known)
- Supplier impact
- Competitive landscape

## Implicit Quality Rubric for M&A Releases
1. Deal terms clear (or appropriately vague if undisclosed)
2. Strategic rationale articulated
3. CEO quotes from both companies
4. SEC compliance language
5. Forward-looking safe harbor
6. Approval/closing conditions stated
7. Combined company facts (revenue, employees, geography)
8. Customer/employee impact addressed
9. Conference call/investor materials referenced
10. Legal counsel + management review
11. Coordinated with both PR teams
12. Embargo discipline
13. Distribution via Reg FD wire
14. Ticker symbols included
15. Tender offer specifics (where applicable)
