# ESG / Sustainability Press Release Standards

## Major Reporting Standards Underlying ESG Releases
- **GRI** (Global Reporting Initiative)
- **SASB** (Sustainability Accounting Standards Board)
- **TCFD** (Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures)
- **CDP** (formerly Carbon Disclosure Project)
- **ESRS** (European Sustainability Reporting Standards)
- **CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive - EU)
- **IFRS S1/S2** (International Sustainability Standards Board)

## ESG Press Release Required Content

### Core Disclosures
- Specific metrics (not vague claims)
- Year-over-year comparison
- Baseline year reference
- Methodology / standard used (GRI, SASB, etc.)
- Third-party verification (where applicable)
- Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions breakdown
- Targets vs. progress

### Environmental Topics
- Carbon emissions
- Water use
- Waste reduction
- Renewable energy
- Biodiversity
- Land use

### Social Topics
- Diversity statistics
- Pay equity
- Worker safety
- Community investment
- Human rights
- Supply chain

### Governance Topics
- Board diversity
- Executive compensation
- Ethics policies
- Anti-corruption
- Cybersecurity

## Anti-Greenwashing Standards
- Avoid vague terms ("green," "sustainable" without metrics)
- Specific numbers, not promises
- Time-bound commitments
- Verification by third party
- Comparable methodology
- Baseline year
- Methodology disclosed

## Required Press Release Elements

### Honest Framing
- Distinguish targets from achievements
- Specify scope (org, product, supply chain)
- Note methodology limitations
- Acknowledge gaps
- Discuss trade-offs

### Defensible Claims
- Cite reporting standard
- Provide supporting documentation link
- Identify auditor/verifier
- Specify time period
- Note assumptions

## Implicit Quality Rubric for ESG Press Releases
1. Specific metrics (not vague claims)
2. Methodology cited (GRI, SASB, etc.)
3. Baseline year stated
4. Comparison to prior period
5. Third-party verification noted
6. Scope clearly defined
7. Target vs. achievement distinguished
8. Time-bound commitments
9. Limitations acknowledged
10. Full report linked
11. ESG team contact provided
12. Investor implications noted
13. Aligned with regulatory framework
14. Avoids superlatives without proof
15. Stakeholder impacts addressed
