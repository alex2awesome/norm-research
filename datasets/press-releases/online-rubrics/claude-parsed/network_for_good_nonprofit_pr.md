# Network for Good: Nonprofit PR Guide

## Nonprofit PR Differs From Corporate
- Mission-driven, not product-driven
- Donor relations critical
- Volunteer recognition opportunities
- Community impact stories
- Partnership amplification
- Crisis response = trust preservation

## Story Types That Work for Nonprofits
- Impact stories (specific beneficiaries with metrics)
- Annual report releases
- Campaign launches with specific goals
- Partnership announcements
- Award/recognition announcements
- Crisis response (donor accountability)
- Major gift announcements

## Nonprofit Press Release Structure
- Headline: Mission impact + specific outcome
- Lede: Who benefited, what changed, when
- Body: Methodology, partners, quotes
- Beneficiary quote (with permission)
- Executive quote
- Boilerplate: Mission, year founded, scale, impact
- Contact: Communications director or executive director
- Multimedia: photos of beneficiaries, infographics

## Donor-Centric Releases
- Specific impact per dollar donated
- Named donor recognition (with permission)
- Annual giving impact summary
- Major gift recognition
- Foundation grant announcements

## Crisis Communication for Nonprofits
- Trust is everything — be transparent
- Acknowledge issues quickly
- Specific corrective actions
- Independent oversight if appropriate
- Donor communications dedicated
- Public reporting of resolution

## Distribution for Nonprofits
- Local press (community impact angle)
- Sector publications (Chronicle of Philanthropy)
- Donor newsletter inclusion
- Volunteer communication channels
- Social media with shareable assets
- Wire syndication for SEO

## Quality Rubric (Nonprofit PR)
- Mission-aligned story
- Beneficiary impact specific
- Named donor recognition where appropriate
- Methodology clear
- Multimedia of impact
- Transparent reporting
- Crisis trust preservation
