# NASA Communications Toolkit: Press Release Standards

## NASA's Communication Mission
- Public engagement with space exploration
- Scientific accuracy non-negotiable
- Inspirational tone balanced with rigor
- Multimedia-rich to enable visual storytelling
- Accessible language for broad audiences

## NASA Press Release Format

### Header
- NASA logo
- "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or embargo
- Release number (NA-XX-XXX)
- City, date

### Headline
- Mission name + key finding/event
- Active voice
- 80 characters or less
- News-first framing

### Lede
- Mission, finding, significance in 50 words
- Specific scientists, instruments, dates
- Why it matters in space science context

### Body
- Background on mission
- Methodology of finding
- Quote from project scientist
- Quote from senior NASA official
- Future implications
- Acknowledgment of partner organizations

### Multimedia Standards
- High-res images mandatory
- Video b-roll if available
- Animation explainers when complex
- Infographics for data-heavy stories
- Caption credits proper

### Boilerplate
- NASA's mission in 50 words
- Year founded (1958)
- Budget context
- Major current programs

## Scientific Accuracy Standards
- Conclusions match underlying papers
- Effect sizes given fully
- Methodology summarized
- Limitations acknowledged
- Independent reviewer approval
- Author/scientist quote authentic

## Public Engagement Best Practices
- Plain language over jargon
- Concrete examples for abstract concepts
- Human interest angles
- Educational hooks for students
- Social media kit included

## Quality Rubric (NASA)
- Scientific accuracy verified
- Multimedia comprehensive
- Mission context clear
- Quote from project scientist
- Quote from NASA official
- Plain language for accessibility
- Educational hooks included
- Acknowledgment of partners
