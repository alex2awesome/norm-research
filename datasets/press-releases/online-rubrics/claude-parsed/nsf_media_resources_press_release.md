# NSF Press Release Standards

## NSF Office of Legislative and Public Affairs (OLPA)
Manages press releases, media advisories, and communications about NSF-funded science.

## Categories of NSF Press Releases
- Budget requests and announcements
- Scientific discoveries (NSF-funded research)
- Awards and recognitions
- Research funding announcements
- Major program launches
- Crisis/incident communications

## NSF Press Release Standards

### Format
- AP style
- Federal agency style requirements
- Standard NSF boilerplate
- Required NSF acknowledgment language for grant-funded research

### Required Acknowledgment for NSF-Funded Research
"This material is based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under Grant No. [Award Number]. Any opinions, findings, and conclusions or recommendations expressed in this material are those of the author(s) and do not necessarily reflect the views of the National Science Foundation."

### Coordination Requirements
For grantees announcing NSF-funded research:
- Coordinate with NSF program officer
- Acknowledge NSF support
- Use disclaimer language
- Time release with publication

## Press Release Content Quality Standards

### NSF-funded research releases should:
- Clearly identify NSF as funder
- Cite the journal article
- Explain societal benefits
- Use plain language
- Include researcher contact
- Provide multimedia
- Distinguish what's known vs. speculative
- Avoid sensationalism

## Implicit Rubric for NSF-Related Press Releases
- Acknowledges NSF funding properly
- Includes required disclaimer
- Cites peer-reviewed publication
- Uses plain language for general audience
- Provides accurate scientific framing
- Includes researcher availability
- Provides multimedia (NSF often co-promotes)
- Coordinated with NSF OLPA before release
