# Twitter Thread: What Makes Me Delete Your Pitch

## Thread Excerpt (Aggregated From Multiple Journalist Threads)

### "Things that make me delete in the first 3 seconds"
- Subject line that's all CAPS
- "URGENT" or "BREAKING" when it's not
- "Hi [FIRST NAME]" — template fail
- Subject line longer than the preview shows
- Sender name I don't recognize + no signature

### "Things that make me read but not write back"
- Pitch about something I covered last week
- Story angle I've already done
- Spokesperson with no credentials I can verify
- Generic "innovative new product" framing
- No clear story angle, just announcement

### "Things that make me write back"
- Subject that mentions a specific number or named entity
- First sentence references my recent work
- Clear story angle with verification path
- Spokesperson available within 24 hours
- One specific ask, time-bound

### "Things that get a story written"
- Trustworthy source I've worked with before
- Pitch that anticipates my editor's questions
- Pre-attached multimedia (no asking for it)
- Customer/partner reference ready
- Founder/CEO personally available
- Story that fits my publication's voice

## Recurring Frustrations Mentioned
- "Just following up" emails (62% of journalists hate this)
- PR firms cc'ing 10 people on pitch
- Embargo with no exclusivity
- "Exclusive" pitched to 50 journalists simultaneously
- Long bio paragraphs about the spokesperson
- Failure to send follow-up materials promised
- Pitching during major news events

## Thread Conclusion Themes
- "Build relationships, not lists"
- "Respect my time and I'll respect yours"
- "If you can't tell me why I should care in 2 sentences, I won't"
- "Honesty about what you can and can't offer"
- "Once you burn me, we're done"

## Quality Rubric (From Journalist Twitter)
- Subject line specific and accurate
- First sentence shows knowledge of my work
- Clear story angle with verification path
- Spokesperson available and credible
- Multimedia pre-attached
- One specific time-bound ask
- No "just following up" follow-ups
