# Exclusive and Tier 1 Media Strategy for Press Releases

## Tier Definitions

### Tier 1
- Massive business, consumer, or news outlets
- Forbes, WSJ, NYT, USA Today, CNN, Good Morning America
- National/global audiences (20M+ people)
- Highest credibility

### Tier 2
- Trade publications
- Strong industry-specific outlets
- Regional major papers
- High credibility for specific audiences

### Tier 3
- Smaller trade
- Local outlets
- Niche blogs
- Long-tail coverage

## Exclusive Strategy Definition
An exclusive is the offer of news to one reporter who will publish first, giving them exclusive access to cover the news before (or with) the press release crossing the wire.

## Effectiveness Data
- Exclusive stories get **42% more social media engagement** vs. non-exclusive
- 82% of journalists prefer working with PR contacts they know
- Tier 1 journalists receive 300+ pitches on average

## Exclusive Pitching Best Practices

### Subject Line Format
- Lead with "EXCLUSIVE:" or "EXCLUSIVE INSIDE:"
- Followed by compelling news hook
- Example: "EXCLUSIVE: First AI-Powered Ocean Cleanup System Launches"

### Timing
- Approach reporters under embargo
- At least 2 weeks in advance
- Allows time for interviews
- Allows asset gathering
- Allows fact-checking

### Strategic Targeting
- Select appropriate outlet for story
- Match outlet's coverage focus
- Know reporter's beat history
- Provide what they specifically need

## Multi-Tier Strategy

### Single Major News Story
1. Tier 1 exclusive (one outlet)
2. Embargoed wire distribution
3. Tier 2 follow-up
4. Tier 3 coverage

### Coordinated Release
- Multiple outlets simultaneously
- Wire distribution
- Embargoed for level playing field
- Press conference if major

## What Tier 1 Outlets Want

### From Press Releases
- Genuine exclusivity (not "exclusive to 10 outlets")
- Original data
- Access to leadership
- Multimedia
- Documents/evidence
- Story angle, not just announcement

### From PR Contacts
- Personal relationships
- Reliability
- Quick response
- Honest answers
- Source connections
- No hype

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Tier 1 Pitches

A pitch likely to land Tier 1 coverage:
1. Genuine exclusive offered
2. Reporter selected based on past work
3. Story angle (not just news)
4. Multimedia prepared
5. Leadership available
6. Two-week lead time
7. Embargo discipline
8. Documents/evidence offered
9. Independent verification possible
10. Subject line includes "EXCLUSIVE:"
11. Personalized to reporter
12. Provides reporter scoop
13. No PR hype
14. Authentic narrative
15. Ongoing relationship cultivated

## When NOT to Pursue Tier 1
- Local angle (use local Tier 2 instead)
- Trade-specific (use trade Tier 2)
- Limited time/resources
- News not strong enough
- Don't have exclusive to offer
