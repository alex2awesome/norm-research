# Janet Murray PR Podcast: Practical PR for Small Business

## Janet's Core Philosophy
- Most small businesses don't need traditional PR
- Owned media (newsletter, podcast) more valuable than coverage
- Build expert reputation in your niche
- Long-term audience > one-time press
- Authenticity over polish

## When Press Helps Small Business
- Local press for community-based business
- Niche industry publications
- Podcast appearances in your space
- Awards from credible bodies
- Media-friendly origin story

## When Press Wastes Time
- Pitching national press for local business
- Trying to "go viral" without strategy
- Generic press releases without hook
- Mass distribution without targeting
- Following up aggressively

## Janet's Pitch Framework
- Subject line: specific story idea + journalist's beat
- Para 1: Why this story matters to their readers
- Para 2: Your unique angle/expertise
- Para 3: What you can offer (interview, data, exclusive)
- Sign-off: phone number, calendar link

## Story Angles That Work for Small Biz
- Local community impact
- Personal story behind business
- Industry trend you're seeing first
- Counter-intuitive insight from experience
- Data from your customer base
- Award nominations or wins

## What Janet Recommends Avoiding
- Hiring expensive PR agency
- Wire-only distribution
- "We are excited to announce" pitches
- Mass-blasting national press
- Generic "look at me" stories

## Building Long-Term Press Relationships
- Be a quotable source for industry stories
- Comment thoughtfully on others' work
- Send relevant items even when not pitching
- Remember journalists' beats
- Don't burn bridges with bad behavior

## Quality Rubric (Janet Murray)
- Right-sized PR ambition
- Owned media as foundation
- Personalized to journalist
- Specific story angle
- Honest, authentic framing
- Long-term relationship building
