# Journalist Pitch Personalization (Press Release Outreach)

## Why Personalization Matters
- PR pitch personalization can DOUBLE the journalist response rate vs. generic outreach
- Mass emailing generic press releases is unlikely to yield results
- Can damage your reputation
- Best pitches are highly targeted and personalized

## Research and Targeting

### Investigate Each Journalist
- Recent stories
- Preferred topics
- Audience
- Bio
- Past work
- Social profiles
- Stated preferences

### Respect Stated Preferences
- If they say "no attachments" - don't attach
- If they prefer Twitter DMs - use Twitter DMs
- Time of day they say they read
- Topics they explicitly cover

## Pitch Structure

### Length
- Fewer than 100 words ideal
- Maximum 200 words
- Most journalists don't have time for long emails

### Components
- Compelling subject line
- Hook
- Headline
- Clear reason to care
- Press release link/attachment if requested
- Direct contact info

### Subject Line
- Specific (not "Press Release")
- Direct
- References journalist's beat
- Gives news angle
- Under 50 characters ideal

## Personalization Techniques

### In Subject Line
- Reference recent story
- Reference area of coverage
- Personalized greeting

### In Opening
- Mention specific past article
- Connect news to journalist's interests
- Demonstrate you've read their work

## Timing
- Mid-morning, mid-week ideal
- Tuesday-Thursday
- Avoid Mondays (catching up)
- Avoid Fridays (winding down)

## Follow-up
- Wait ~48 hours
- One polite follow-up max
- Keep it short
- Reiterate relevance
- Offer additional info
- Then leave alone

## Multimedia Support
- ~55% of journalists prefer pitches with multimedia
- Attach or offer images, infographics, videos
- Provide press kit URL

## Implicit Personalization Rubric

A pitch passes the personalization test if:
1. Subject line specific to journalist
2. Opening references journalist's past work
3. Pitch tied to journalist's beat
4. Format respects stated preferences
5. Length under 200 words
6. Multimedia offered (not pushed)
7. Press release link included
8. One clear ask
9. Direct contact info
10. Sent at appropriate time
