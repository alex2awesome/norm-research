# Product Launch Press Release Standards

## Headlines
- Under 100 characters
- Strong action verbs
- Avoid vague buzzwords ("game-changing")
- 10-20 words ideal length
- Include specific value proposition

### Headline Examples
- BAD: "Company X Launches New Software"
- GOOD: "Company X Unveils AI-Powered Software That Cuts Design Time in Half"

## Six-Paragraph Structure for Product Launch

### Paragraph 1: Concise Overview
- What the product is
- Who can benefit
- Main value proposition
- Market need addressed

### Paragraph 2: Specifics
- Key features
- How it works
- Differentiation from competitors

### Paragraph 3: CEO Quote
- Strategic context
- Why this product, why now
- Vision for impact

### Paragraph 4: Industry Context
- Market trends
- Why announcement is timely
- How it fits broader landscape

### Paragraph 5: Customer/User Quote (or second leadership quote)
- Customer perspective
- Early use cases
- Specific benefits realized

### Paragraph 6: Boilerplate + Contact
- Company history
- Current scope
- Press contact

## Length
- 300-500 words traditional
- 600-800 for major launches
- One page maximum

## Focus on Value, Not Features
- "Why now" angle critical
- Measurable impact
- Customer problem solved
- Specific use cases

## Distribution for Tech/Startup PR
- Journalists receive 100+ pitches/week
- Spend less than 10 seconds scanning
- Paste full content in email body (no attachments)
- Find reporters who cover your space
- Tailor each email to journalist's beat

## Startup-Specific Advice
- Emphasize innovation
- Differentiation
- Growth potential
- How product challenges status quo
- Founder story if compelling

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Product Launch
- Product clearly named and described
- Value proposition stated in first paragraph
- Specific features (not generic claims)
- Customer problem addressed
- Differentiation from competitors
- Market context provided
- Authoritative quotes (not promotional)
- Pricing/availability included
- Clear next steps for journalists
- High-resolution product images provided
- Beta access or demos offered to press
- Industry-relevant news hook
