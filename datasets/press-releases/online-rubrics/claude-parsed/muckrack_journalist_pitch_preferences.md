# Muck Rack: Annual State of Journalism — Pitch Preferences

## Survey Findings From 2,000+ Journalists

### How Journalists Want to Be Pitched
- Email: 96% prefer
- LinkedIn message: 30%
- Twitter/X DM: 22%
- Phone call: 5% (mostly broadcast)
- Snail mail: 1%

### Best Time to Pitch
- Morning (5-8am ET): 50% open rate
- Mid-morning (9-11am): 35%
- Afternoon: 20%
- Evening / weekend: <10%

### Optimal Pitch Length
- Under 200 words: 73% read
- 200-400 words: 52% read
- 400+ words: 23% read

### Subject Line Preferences
- "Story idea about [specific topic]" — 45% open
- "Exclusive: [news]" — 40% open (only if true)
- "[News] — quick interview opportunity" — 38%
- "Press release: [headline]" — 18%
- "Hi [Name]" — 8%

## What Journalists Wish PR Pros Would Stop Doing
- Following up more than once (62%)
- Mass-blasting the same pitch (58%)
- Pitching irrelevant beats (55%)
- Sending PDF-only attachments (43%)
- Using "exclusive" inaccurately (40%)
- Vague subject lines (38%)

## What Journalists Want More Of
- Data and original research (78%)
- Diverse expert sources (65%)
- Embargoed exclusives (52%)
- Visual assets ready to use (49%)
- Quick-turnaround sources (42%)

## What Makes a Source Re-Quotable
- Available within 24 hours
- Quotable in clear, jargon-free language
- Provides specific examples
- Honest about limitations of expertise
- No PR rep gatekeeping

## Quality Rubric From Muck Rack Survey
- Email format
- Sent during morning hours
- Under 200 words
- Specific subject line with story idea
- Personalized to beat
- Visual assets attached
- Source genuinely available
