# WikiHow: How to Write a Press Release

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Part 1: Preparing
1. Identify a newsworthy story
2. Know your target audience
3. Research relevant journalists
4. Gather all key facts and approvals
5. Prepare multimedia assets

### Part 2: Writing the Headline
1. Lead with the most important news
2. Use active voice
3. Keep under 80 characters
4. Avoid clichés ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
5. Test against "would I tweet this?"

### Part 3: Writing the Lede
1. Compress 5 W's into 50 words
2. Lead with most newsworthy element
3. Include concrete details (numbers, names, dates)
4. Avoid throat-clearing ("Today, we are pleased...")
5. Active voice throughout

### Part 4: Writing the Body
1. Use inverted pyramid (most important first)
2. One idea per paragraph
3. Include a quote on first page
4. Provide methodology if data-based
5. Keep body to 3-4 paragraphs

### Part 5: Writing Quotes
1. 25-35 words ideal
2. Spokesperson should sound human
3. Add information, don't repeat headline
4. Avoid superlatives without proof
5. Attribute to named C-level executive

### Part 6: Boilerplate and Contact
1. 50-75 word company description
2. Founded date, headquarters, mission
3. Named media contact
4. Direct phone number
5. Direct email
6. End with "###" marker

### Part 7: Review and Distribution
1. Have outside reader review
2. Verify all facts and numbers
3. Check spelling of names
4. Test all links
5. Distribute to relevant journalists
6. Wire syndication for SEO

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Burying the lede
- Using too many adjectives
- Quote that says nothing
- Boilerplate too long
- No multimedia
- Mass-blast distribution
- Sending Friday afternoon
- Following up too aggressively

## Quality Rubric (WikiHow)
- Newsworthy substance
- Active voice headline under 80 chars
- 50-word lede with 5 W's
- Inverted pyramid body
- 25-35 word substantive quote
- 75-word boilerplate
- Named contact with phone
- Outside reviewer used
