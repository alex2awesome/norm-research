# Brian Dean (Backlinko): PR for Link Building

## Why PR Matters for SEO
- Earned media = high-authority dofollow backlinks
- Wire releases = limited nofollow value
- Branded mentions count even without links
- Coverage clusters create topical authority signals

## The Skyscraper Approach to PR
1. Find a topic with existing media coverage
2. Create a substantially better resource (data, depth, design)
3. Pitch journalists who covered the inferior version
4. Offer them a "newer, better" version they can link to

## Data-Driven PR Releases
- Original research = highest pickup rate
- Survey data your industry hasn't seen
- Internal data anonymized and aggregated
- Year-over-year trends with proprietary numbers
- "State of the X" annual reports

## Pitch Email Template (Brian Dean Style)
- Subject: Numbered insight from data study
- Para 1: One specific finding that surprises
- Para 2: Methodology in one sentence
- Para 3: Offer of full report + interview
- Sign-off: Phone, email, calendar link

## The "Ego Bait" Strategy
- Quote the journalist or publication in your release
- Mention them in social posts about the release
- Offer them first look in exchange for coverage
- Tag them appropriately (not spammy)

## Distribution Hierarchy for Link Value
1. Tier 1 outlets (Forbes, NYT, etc.) — highest authority
2. Industry trade publications — topical authority
3. Niche blogs with engaged audience
4. Wire syndication for SEO floor

## What to Avoid
- Buying coverage (Google penalizes paid links)
- Reciprocal link arrangements
- Mass cold outreach without research
- "Guest post for link" schemes
- Generic "we have some data" pitches

## Quality Rubric for PR-as-SEO
- Original data or substantially better resource
- Specific numerical hook
- Personalized to journalist's prior work
- Multiple media outlets briefed
- Clean, dofollow earned coverage
- Topical authority cluster created
