# Harvard SEAS Press Release Tips

## Definition & Purpose
"Press releases, typically 600-800 word summaries written for a non-technical reader" distributed to media to highlight newsworthy activities, events, or institutional changes.

## Criteria for Determining Press Release Worthiness

1. **Scientific/Practical Impact**: Does the research have major scientific implications or practical applications?

2. **News Hook**: Is there something broad-based that journalists might find compelling?

3. **Quality Standards**: Is the research groundbreaking, original, and timely?

4. **Uniqueness**: Is the work unusual or unexpected in nature?

5. **Visual Assets**: Do you have engaging or beautiful accompanying photos, movies, etc.?

6. **Commercial Interest**: Is there a patent pending or collaboration with industry?

7. **Award Recognition**: Is the award considered "major" (Nobel, NAE, etc.)?

8. **Publication Considerations**: Verify the journal's embargo policy and whether it issues its own press releases or promotes authors.

9. **Collaborative Coordination**: If the work is collaborative, discuss the possibility with colleagues and coordinate with the SEAS Communications Office to align efforts with other institutions.
