# HubSpot Press Release Complete Guide

## Basic Elements to Include
Every press release should include:
- Contact information (top or bottom)
- "For immediate release" on top right
- Dateline (city and state in all caps + release date)
- Headline that quickly explains the announcement
- Business location in first sentence
- Boilerplate (company bio) at end
- "###" or signifier at end

## Writing Guidelines
- Write in newsy, straightforward way
- Imagine it as an article on a news site
- First paragraph: state announcement + business context
- Use AP style throughout
- Multiple paragraphs but each focused

## Templates Available (HubSpot)
1. Product release
2. Event announcement
3. Partnership/acquisition
4. Funding announcement
5. New hire/promotion
6. Award announcement
7. Survey/research findings
8. Crisis response

## Key Writing Tips
- Plan keywords (SEO)
- Write interesting headlines
- Vary content with multimedia and quotes
- Photos are #1 factor positively influencing views and engagement
- Tell a story
- Multimedia helps engagement

## Distribution Recommendations
- Wire service for major news
- Direct to relevant journalists
- Post on company newsroom
- Share on social media
- Pitch to industry publications

## Visual Elements Importance
- Photos #1 factor in engagement
- Infographics for data stories
- Videos for product launches
- B-roll for broadcast
- Headshots for executive quotes

## Implicit Quality Rubric (HubSpot)
- All required elements present
- Newsy tone (not promotional)
- Clear announcement in first paragraph
- Includes location/context
- Has boilerplate
- Has photos/multimedia
- Strategic keyword placement
- Imaginable as a news article
