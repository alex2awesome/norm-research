# Press Release Best Practices for 2024

## The Modern Press Release Is...
- Multi-format (text + assets)
- SEO-optimized for both Google and Google News
- Social-first in distribution timing
- Designed to be excerpted, not republished verbatim
- Optimized for AI summarization (LLMs scrape press releases)

## Length Guidelines for 2024
- 300-400 words is the new sweet spot
- Down from the 600-800 word standard of 2010s
- Reflects shorter journalist attention windows
- Mobile-first — most reads happen on phone

## SEO Considerations
- Primary keyword in headline (front-loaded)
- Secondary keywords in subhead
- 1-2 backlinks to landing pages, not homepage
- Anchor text should be descriptive, not "click here"
- Schema.org NewsArticle markup if posting on own site

## Multimedia Requirements
- One high-res photo MINIMUM (1200x630 for social)
- Optional: short b-roll video (under 60 seconds)
- Optional: shareable infographic with social-media dimensions
- All assets should be downloadable without registration

## Distribution Strategy 2024
- Wire services losing influence (Cision/PRN/BW now scraped by AI for summaries)
- Direct outreach via Muck Rack, Qwoted, or HARO
- Pitch via Twitter DMs to active journalists
- LinkedIn newsletters as alternative channel
- Substack-native journalists prefer email pitches

## What Doesn't Work in 2024
- Mass-blast distribution (gets filtered as spam)
- Wire-only strategies
- Press releases without visuals
- "About Us" boilerplate longer than 75 words
- Quotes longer than 35 words

## Quality Rubric
- Front-loaded keyword in headline
- 300-400 words target
- Mobile-friendly formatting
- Multimedia included
- Direct journalist outreach in addition to wire
- Schema markup if self-hosted
