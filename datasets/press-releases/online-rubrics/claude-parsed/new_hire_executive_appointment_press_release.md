# New Hire / Executive Appointment Press Release Format

## Standard Structure
- Logo and contact info
- Release date
- Headline
- Location/dateline
- Body
- Boilerplate

## Headline Requirements
- Full name of new hire
- Title
- Company name
- Strong verbs: "welcomes," "appoints," "announces"
- Under 15 words
- Clear and informative

### Examples
- "Acme Corp Appoints Jane Smith as Chief Marketing Officer"
- "TechFirm Welcomes Dr. John Doe as Head of AI Research"

## Body Content

### First Paragraph
- Name (full)
- Job title
- Relevant background
- How expertise aligns with company goals
- What they bring to the team

### Subsequent Paragraphs (50-200 words)
- Detailed expertise and accomplishments
- Career highlights
- Education
- Notable previous companies
- Awards/recognition
- How hire expands or strengthens business

## Quotes
Include quotes from:
- The new executive
- A senior leader (CEO, board chair) about strategic value

## Length
- 400-500 words ideal
- Up to 600 for major executive

## Multimedia
- Professional headshot (essential)
- Team photo (optional)
- Company logo
- Introductory video (for major hires)

## Boilerplate
30-100 word company description, standard format.

## What NOT to Include
- Personal life details
- Compensation
- Reasons for previous departure
- Speculation about strategic direction
- Promises about future actions

## Implicit Quality Rubric for New Hire Releases
1. Full name and exact title
2. Specific previous role and employer
3. Years of experience
4. Quantifiable achievements (revenue grown, team built, products launched)
5. Strategic context (why this hire fits company strategy)
6. Two quotes (new hire + senior leader)
7. Education credentials (if relevant)
8. Reporting structure (if changed)
9. Start date
10. Headshot included
11. Boilerplate current
12. AP style throughout
13. No promotional fluff
14. Distinctive (not generic announcement)
