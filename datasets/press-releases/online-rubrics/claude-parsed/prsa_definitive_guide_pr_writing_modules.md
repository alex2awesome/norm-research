# PRSA Definitive Guide to PR Writing - Curriculum

## Course Overview
Comprehensive e-learning course covering every major writing skill required of a PR communicator. Approximately 6 hours total.

## Four Modules

### Module A: Writing Like a Journalist
"The Definitive Guide to Writing Like a Journalist Without Having to Be One: Get More Placements, Page Views and Shares by Showing Up the Smart Way"

Topics include:
- Adopting journalistic voice
- News judgment
- Inverted pyramid
- Lead writing
- Why news matters to journalists

### Module B: Writing News Releases
"How to Write News Releases Management Will Love and No Editor Can Ignore"

Topics include:
- News release structure
- Headlines that work for both audiences
- Quotes that get used
- Editor-friendly format
- Internal-stakeholder management

### Module C: Polishing for PR Perfection
"Master Proofreading Principles and Grammar for Boosting Your Credibility and Results"

Topics include:
- AP Style mastery
- Common grammar errors
- Proofreading techniques
- Quality control processes

### Module D: PR Writing Mastery
"How to Set Your PR Writing Apart From Everyone Else and Maximize Your Impact"

Topics include:
- Creative angles
- Style for differentiation
- Brand voice
- Storytelling
- Multimedia integration

## Implicit Press Release Rubric (PRSA Definitive Guide)

A press release passes PRSA standards if it:
1. **Reads like news** (not marketing copy)
2. **Has clear news value** (passes journalist news judgment)
3. **Inverted pyramid structure**
4. **Strong, specific headline**
5. **5Ws in lead**
6. **Quotes that journalists will actually use**
7. **AP style throughout**
8. **No grammar/punctuation errors**
9. **Distinctive angle** (not generic)
10. **Brand voice consistent**
11. **Story-driven**
12. **Multimedia included**
13. **Editor-ready** (minimal editing required)
14. **Management-approved** (legal/exec sign-off if needed)
15. **Strategic alignment** with org goals
