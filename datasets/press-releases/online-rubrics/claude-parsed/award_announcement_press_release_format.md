# Award Announcement Press Release Format

## Seven Required Elements
1. Action-oriented headline
2. Five Ws first paragraph
3. Award credibility paragraph
4. Achievement details
5. Two meaningful quotes
6. Association boilerplate
7. Contact information

## Structure

### Headline
- Conveys significance immediately
- Includes specific award name
- Includes organization name
- 10-15 words maximum
- Action-oriented

### Subheadline
- Details that didn't fit in headline
- Consecutive streak, competitive pool size, rank, category count
- Example: "Recognition marks fifth consecutive year on the program's annual list"
- Gives journalist reason to keep reading

### Lead Paragraph
Two to three sentences answering:
- Who won
- Which award
- When
- Where
- Why selected (criteria)
- Third person throughout

### Body
- Background on awarding organization
- Selection criteria details
- What specifically earned the recognition
- Recipient's relevant background
- Industry context

### Quotes
- One from recipient
- One from senior executive (your org)
- Or one from awarding org leader
- Insightful, not braggy

## Length
- 300-500 words
- One page

## Words to Drop
- "Proud to announce"
- "Prestigious" (let award name speak for itself)
- Self-aggrandizement

## Distribution Timing
- Immediately after award announced (24-48 hours)
- Day of or after ceremony if attending
- Maximum relevance window

## Implicit Quality Rubric for Award Releases
- Award name and source clear
- Specific selection criteria mentioned
- Why winner deserved it
- Industry context (significance of award)
- Two perspectives (recipient + awarding body)
- No fluff or self-praise
- News value beyond "we won"
- Visual assets (awards photo, headshot)
