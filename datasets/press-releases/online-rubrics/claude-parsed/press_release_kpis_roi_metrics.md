# Press Release KPIs and ROI Metrics

## Common PR KPIs

### Direct Business Metrics
- Lead generation (form fills, sign-ups)
- Booked meetings
- Sales / conversions (with $ amounts)
- Pipeline impact
- Customer acquisition cost reduction
- Revenue attribution

### Earned Media Metrics
- Pickup count (number of placements)
- Outlet quality (tier 1/2/3)
- **Earned Media Value (EMV)**: monetary worth equivalent to paid advertising
- Reach (impressions)
- Share of Voice (SoV)
- Sentiment analysis

### Engagement Metrics
- Press release views
- Click-through rate
- Time on page
- Social shares
- Backlinks generated
- Domain authority of citing sites

### Awareness Metrics
- Brand mentions
- Search volume lift
- Branded keyword rankings
- Direct traffic to site

### Reputation Metrics
- Sentiment scores
- Crisis recovery time
- Industry rankings
- Spokesperson quote frequency

## Tracking Methods

### UTM Parameters
- Track conversions from PR-driven pages
- Attribute revenue to specific releases
- Measure source-specific traffic

### Distribution Platform Analytics
- Built-in dashboards (PR Newswire, Business Wire)
- Geographic and demographic data
- Audience profile

### Media Monitoring Tools
- Cision, Meltwater, Muck Rack
- Track mentions across web
- Sentiment analysis
- Competitor benchmarking

## Implicit Quality-via-Outcomes Rubric

A press release is empirically high-quality if:

### Distribution Stage
- High open rate (>30%)
- High click-through (>5%)
- Wide geographic distribution
- Quick pickup (within 24 hours)

### Coverage Stage
- 5+ pickups (above industry average)
- Tier 1 outlet pickup
- Coverage continues 3+ days
- Original quotes used (not just rewrites)

### Engagement Stage
- 50+ social shares
- 5+ quality backlinks
- Meaningful page dwell time
- Embed/downloaded multimedia

### Business Outcomes Stage
- Lead generation lift
- Sales attribution
- Brand awareness lift
- Reputation score change

## Why This Matters for Press Release Quality
A press release that scores well on these KPIs has demonstrated:
- News value (got picked up)
- Quality writing (used as-is)
- Multimedia value (engagement)
- Distribution choice (right outlets reached)
- Targeting (right audience reached)
- Format (mobile-friendly, scannable)
- Strategic alignment (drove business outcomes)

## Implicit Reverse-Engineered Quality Rubric
Top KPI-performing releases tend to have:
1. Genuine news value
2. Specific data/findings
3. Compelling quote
4. Good multimedia
5. Strong subject line for distribution
6. Targeted distribution
7. AP-compliant format
8. Mobile-friendly structure
9. SEO-optimized headlines
10. Executive availability for follow-up
11. Embargo discipline
12. Localization where applicable
13. Wire service distribution
14. Direct journalist outreach component
15. Pre-prepared social amplification
