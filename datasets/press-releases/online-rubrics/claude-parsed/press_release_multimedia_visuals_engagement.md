# Multimedia and Visuals in Press Releases

## Engagement Statistics
- **94% more views** with multimedia elements
- **650% increase** in engagement with visuals
- **6x higher engagement** with multiple visual assets
- **3x more interaction** with video content
- **48% increase** in social sharing with immersive multimedia
- **75% more likely to be shared** with engaging visual content

## Social Media Impact
- 94% higher engagement on social platforms with visuals
- Facebook posts with images: 53% more likes, 104% more comments
- Tweets with images: ~2x more retweets

## Why Visuals Work
- Visual content processed 60,000x faster than text in the brain
- 40x more likely to be shared on social media
- Content with relevant images: 94% more views
- Images load faster than video, easier to scan quickly

## Images vs. Video
- Images more effective for press releases
- Load faster, easier to view quickly
- Make immediate impression
- 88% of video marketers say video is crucial to their strategy

## Types of Press Release Multimedia

### Essential
- High-resolution product/event images
- Headshots of quoted executives
- Company logos

### Recommended
- Infographics for data stories
- Short videos (under 60 seconds)
- Diagrams for complex topics
- Charts and graphs

### Advanced
- B-roll for broadcast pickup
- Interactive content
- 3D visualizations
- Animated explainers
- Embedded social media

## Implicit Multimedia Rubric

A press release succeeds with multimedia if:
- At least one high-resolution image included
- Caption with each image
- Alt text for accessibility
- Multiple resolution options for different uses
- Cleared for third-party distribution
- Logos and branding clear
- B-roll or video for broadcast
- Schema markup for SEO
- Optimized file sizes
- Mobile-friendly formats

## Best Practices
- Include images upfront (not as afterthought)
- High resolution (300+ dpi for print)
- Standard aspect ratios
- Diverse representation in images
- Real photos over stock when possible
- Clear copyright/usage rights
- Describe images with alt text
- Compress for web while preserving quality
