# Columbia University Office of Public Affairs - Press Release Standards

## Office of Public Affairs Structure
Two departments:
- Communications (telling Columbia's story)
- Government and Community Affairs

## Communications Function
- Voice of Columbia globally
- Reaches diverse audiences (media, neighbors, government, alumni)
- Crafts and executes communications strategy
- Furthers president and trustee priorities

## Press Release and Media Services

### Statements and Announcements
Posted by Office of Public Affairs from across the University.

### Media Inquiries
- Press email for institutional inquiries
- Direct contact for non-institutional issues

### Experts for the Media
Columbia maintains a regularly-updated resource:
- Connects reporters with university experts
- Categorized by topic
- Helps with news-driven coverage

## Press Release Quality Considerations

### What Columbia Releases
- Major research findings
- University statements on public issues
- Awards and honors
- Major hires
- Significant programs
- Crisis responses

### Format Standards
- AP style
- University-approved boilerplate
- Standard contact info
- Faculty titles per Columbia style
- Embargo coordination with journals

## Implicit Quality Rubric
- Aligned with University priorities
- Identifies relevant experts available for interviews
- Provides context (research importance)
- Includes journal links for studies
- Standard format
- Strategic timing
- Coordinated across departments

## Special Considerations
- University statements require legal review
- Crisis communications follow specific protocols
- Faculty quotes require consent
- IP/grant disclosures included where relevant
