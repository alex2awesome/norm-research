# 5W Public Relations: Press Release Best Practices

## What 5WPR Tells Clients
- Press release is one tool, not the whole strategy
- Quality over quantity in distribution
- Multimedia is now table stakes
- Founder/executive voice differentiates
- Long-term media relationships matter most

## Pre-Release Checklist
- News passes the "would I share at dinner?" test
- Spokesperson briefed and available
- Multimedia assets ready (photos, video, infographics)
- Social media kit prepared
- Customer/partner approvals secured
- Crisis response plan if needed
- Internal stakeholders briefed

## Headline Construction
- News-first, brand-second
- Active voice
- Concrete numbers when possible
- 80 characters maximum
- Tested with focus group of 3-5 people

## Lede Paragraph
- 5 W's compressed into 50 words
- Most newsworthy element first
- Concrete details (numbers, dates, names)
- Active voice
- No throat-clearing language

## Body Structure
- Inverted pyramid
- One idea per paragraph
- Subheaders for scannability
- Quote on first page
- Customer/partner proof point
- Methodology if data-based claim

## Quote Best Practices
- 25-35 words
- Spokesperson sounds human
- Adds new information
- Avoids superlatives without proof
- Can be excerpted independently

## Distribution Strategy
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade publication
- Coordinated release to 5-10 secondary outlets
- Wire for SEO (after initial coverage runs)
- Social media kit deployed
- Newsletter inclusion if relevant
- Founder/CEO LinkedIn post

## Measurement Standards
- Coverage tier (Tier 1, 2, 3)
- Sentiment analysis
- Brand mention tracking
- Pickup velocity (first 24 hours)
- Long-tail SEO impact (90 days)

## Quality Rubric (5WPR)
- News substantive enough for outside-org test
- Multimedia ready
- Spokesperson available
- Tier-appropriate distribution
- Coordinated cross-channel
- Measurement beyond impressions
