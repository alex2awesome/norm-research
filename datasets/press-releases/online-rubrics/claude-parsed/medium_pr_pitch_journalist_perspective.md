# Why I Deleted Your Press Release: A Journalist's Confession

## My Inbox Triage Process
- Subject line decides 90% of the time
- If I open: first 2 lines decide
- If I read past 2 lines: maybe a 30-second skim
- Less than 5% reach a "save for later" folder
- Less than 0.5% become a story

## What Makes Me Delete Immediately
- "I'm reaching out because..." (cold spam template)
- "Hope you're doing well in these unprecedented times"
- Subject line in ALL CAPS
- Subject line with "EXCLUSIVE" when it's not
- More than 2 paragraphs in the email body
- Attachment without any context in body
- "Please find attached our press release"

## What Makes Me Keep Reading
- Subject line that names a real number or named entity
- First sentence that mentions my recent article
- A specific story angle, not just the announcement
- Spokesperson name + credentials in line 2
- "If interested, I can have her on a call within 30 minutes"

## Pitch Structure That Works
- Line 1: The hook (one sentence)
- Line 2: Why this matters / why now
- Line 3: What I can offer (interview, data, exclusive)
- Line 4: Quick credentials / why this source matters
- Sign-off with phone number

## On Embargoes
- Don't offer them unless I cover the beat seriously
- 24-48 hours is the sweet spot
- Anything longer feels like manipulation
- Breaking embargo is a career-ender for the source company

## On Follow-ups
- One follow-up max, after 3 business days
- Don't ask if I "saw your previous email"
- Send a NEW angle in the follow-up
- After 2 emails total: assume no, move on

## Quality Rubric From The Other Side
- Subject line specific and accurate
- First sentence is the hook, not throat-clearing
- Personalized to my beat
- Spokesperson concrete and available
- Story angle, not just announcement
- Short, scannable email body
