# The Verge: How to Pitch Us

## What We Cover
- Consumer technology and gadgets
- Tech-policy intersections
- Platform power and consolidation
- Cybersecurity stories with consumer impact
- Climate-tech with real product
- Cultural impact of technology

## What We Don't Cover (Mostly)
- Pure enterprise software
- B2B finance/legal tech
- Generic "we have an app" announcements
- Crypto pump pieces
- AI hype without verifiable demos

## What Makes a Good Pitch
- Story angle clear in 2 sentences
- Demonstrates you've read recent Verge coverage
- Verifiable demo or product available
- Source available on short notice
- Multimedia (photos, video, screenshots) ready

## What Makes a Bad Pitch
- Mass-blasted to multiple Verge reporters
- "Following up" emails
- Demo behind NDA only
- Crypto/blockchain claims without working product
- "Industry-first" claims without proof

## Format Preferences
- Email to specific reporter, not tips@
- 150-250 words ideal
- Subject line specific and accurate
- Multimedia linked, not attached
- Spokesperson info at bottom

## Embargo Practices
- We accept embargoes
- 24-72 hours standard
- One reporter at The Verge per embargo
- Embargo violations end the relationship
- Exclusives strongly preferred over embargoes

## Special Considerations
- Consumer impact angle helps
- Privacy and security implications relevant
- Diverse expert voices appreciated
- Marginalized communities' perspectives valued
- Climate angle increasingly relevant

## Quality Rubric (The Verge)
- Consumer tech focus
- Story angle clear in 2 sentences
- Demo verifiable
- Multimedia pre-attached
- Spokesperson available
- Embargo respected
- One Verge reporter per pitch
