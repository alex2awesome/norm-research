# State Department / USAID Press Release Standards

## USAID Press Office Structure
USAID's Press Office responsibilities:
- Respond to press
- Present agency mission and message
- Coordinate press goals and messages
- Monitor agency-related media
- Disseminate media coverage to staff
- Generate proactive agency press activities

## State Department Spokesperson Office
The Office of the Spokesperson releases:
- Statements
- Media notes
- Notices to the press
- Fact sheets

Released daily as posted to website.

## International Press Release Considerations

### Coordination Requirements
- Headquarters releases use press material templates
- Missions consult with State Department Public Affairs
- Follow embassy guidance
- Coordinate across time zones

### Multilingual Needs
- Translate for affected populations
- Original English maintained
- Culturally appropriate adaptation
- Local context considered

### Diplomatic Sensitivity
- Avoid characterizations of foreign govts
- Use neutral language
- Coordinate with country desks
- Avoid creating diplomatic incidents

## Standard International Release Components

### Required
- Date (with time zone)
- Distribution list (US + international)
- Embassy/mission attribution if applicable
- Translation availability noted
- Cultural advisory if applicable

### Common Topics
- Diplomatic statements
- Treaty announcements
- Aid commitments
- Visa/travel advisories
- Sanctions
- Recognitions
- Human rights statements
- Disaster response
- Election-related statements

## USAID-Specific Standards

### For Aid Announcements
- Specific funding amount
- Source (agency, program)
- Recipient organization/country
- Purpose/use
- Implementation partners
- Performance period
- Expected impact metrics
- Coordination with country teams

### For Crisis Response
- Acknowledgment
- Aid commitment
- Coordination with other agencies
- How to help (if appropriate)
- Where to get info

## Implicit Quality Rubric

A State/USAID press release passes standards if:
1. Uses approved templates
2. Coordinated with relevant offices
3. Approved through proper clearance chain
4. Diplomatic-sensitive language
5. Cites specific facts (not generalizations)
6. Embassy/mission coordination noted
7. Time zone disclosed
8. Translation availability mentioned
9. Cultural context appropriate
10. Specific funding/program details
11. Implementation specifics
12. Multilingual where needed
13. Section 508 compliant
14. Avoids creating diplomatic incidents
15. Properly attributed to spokesperson
