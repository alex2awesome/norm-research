# Edelman Trust: Crisis Communications

## Edelman Trust Barometer Findings
- Trust in institutions at historic lows
- CEO leadership expected on social issues
- Quick acknowledgment essential during crisis
- Authentic communication outperforms PR-speak
- Stakeholders demand specific actions, not values statements

## Crisis Press Release Standards

### Phase 1: Acknowledgment (Hours 0-2)
- Brief statement acknowledging the issue
- "We are aware and investigating"
- Single spokesperson designated
- Update cadence specified
- Avoid speculation

### Phase 2: Initial Response (Hours 2-24)
- Specific facts as known
- Timeline of events
- Immediate corrective actions
- Stakeholder communication plan
- Media contact dedicated

### Phase 3: Substantive Response (Days 1-7)
- Comprehensive timeline
- Root cause analysis (preliminary)
- Detailed corrective actions
- Independent oversight if relevant
- Long-term commitments

### Phase 4: Resolution (Weeks/Months)
- Outcome reporting
- Lessons learned
- Policy/practice changes
- Independent verification
- Stakeholder follow-through

## What Builds Trust in Crisis Releases
- Specific facts over vague statements
- Named accountability (not "the company")
- Concrete actions (not aspirational language)
- Independent verification when possible
- Update cadence honored
- CEO visible and accessible

## What Erodes Trust
- Generic "we take this seriously" language
- Apologizing without specifics
- Blame deflection to victims or others
- Lawyered language stripping empathy
- Slow response or silence
- Failing to update on progress

## Crisis Communication Rubric (Edelman)
- Acknowledgment within 2 hours
- Specific facts as known
- Named spokesperson with authority
- Concrete corrective actions
- Update cadence specified and honored
- CEO visible and accessible
- Independent verification when possible
- Long-term commitments measurable
