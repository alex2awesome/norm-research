# Medium: The Funding Announcement Press Release Template

## Why Funding Announcements Matter
- Signal milestone to market
- Attract talent
- Build customer confidence
- Demonstrate investor backing
- Establish category positioning

## What Doesn't Work
- Headline that's just "Company Raises $X"
- "We're excited" CEO quote
- Promotional investor cheerleading quote
- No use-of-funds clarity
- Wire-only distribution
- No customer or product validation

## The Funding Announcement Template

### Headline Formula
"[Company] Raises $[Amount] to [Specific Use of Funds]"

Examples:
- "Acme Raises $20M to Expand AI-Powered Inventory to Europe"
- "BetaCorp Raises $50M to Build Out 200-Person Engineering Team"
- "Gamma Raises $5M Seed to Launch Climate-Tech Marketplace"

### Lede Formula
"[Company], a [brief description], today announced $[amount] in [round type] funding led by [lead investor], with participation from [other investors]. The funds will be used to [specific use]."

### Use-of-Funds Specifics
- Hiring (specific numbers, specific roles)
- Product development (specific roadmap items)
- Geographic expansion (specific markets)
- Customer acquisition (specific channels)
- R&D in specific areas

### Investor Quote
- Lead investor only
- Substantive reasoning behind investment
- Specific market thesis
- Why this team
- Why now

### CEO Quote
- Acknowledge the milestone briefly
- Focus on what the funding enables
- Specific upcoming priorities
- Customer focus
- Honest about challenges remaining

### Customer/Partner Validation
- Named customer with quote
- Specific use case
- Measurable outcome
- Industry relevance

### Boilerplate
- Brief company description
- Founded date and headquarters
- Total raised to date
- Number of customers/users
- Mission in one sentence

## Distribution Best Practices
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade outlet
- Coordinated release to 5-10 secondary outlets
- Wire syndication for SEO (after initial coverage)
- Founder/CEO LinkedIn announcement
- Investor co-promotion

## Quality Rubric for Funding Announcements
- Headline includes specific use of funds
- Use of funds detailed in body
- Substantive investor quote with thesis
- Customer validation included
- Multimedia (team photos, product screenshots)
- Founder available for interviews
- Embargoed exclusive offered
