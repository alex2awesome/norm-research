# Embargo Watch: Press Release Ethics

## What This Blog Tracks
- Embargo violations across science journalism
- Press release inaccuracies in science coverage
- Patterns of selective fact disclosure
- Industry vs. academic press release quality

## Key Findings From Embargo Watch's Critique

### Common Embargo Issues
- Embargoes broken by overseas outlets
- Selective lifting (one outlet given early access unfairly)
- "Soft embargoes" where info shared but not formally embargoed
- Source-PR firm coordination breakdowns
- Embargo with no exclusivity offered

### Press Release Inaccuracies
- Conclusions stronger than the underlying study supports
- Causal claims when only correlation found
- Misleading effect sizes (relative without absolute)
- Cherry-picked subgroup analyses
- Missing methodology details

### Spin Patterns
- Implying clinical relevance from preclinical work
- Hyping early-stage research
- Omitting limitations
- Quoting authors without quoting external experts
- Press release more enthusiastic than the paper

## What Good Science PR Looks Like

### Embargo Practices
- Clear lift time in PT/ET
- Materials sent 48-72 hours before lift
- Fair access across outlets
- Honor revoked for violations
- Author availability confirmed

### Accuracy Practices
- Conclusions match the paper exactly
- Limitations explicitly noted
- Effect sizes given as both relative and absolute
- Methodology summary provided
- External expert contacts offered

## Quality Rubric (Embargo Watch)
- Conclusions match underlying paper
- Limitations explicitly noted
- Effect sizes given fully
- Methodology summary provided
- Embargo time clear and respected
- External expert contacts offered
- No selective subgroup hyping
