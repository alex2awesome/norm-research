# Tomasz Tunguz: Data-Driven B2B PR

## What B2B PR Should Optimize For
- Inbound demo requests
- Enterprise buyer awareness
- Analyst recognition (Gartner, Forrester quadrants)
- Talent recruitment
- Investor confidence

## What B2B PR Should NOT Optimize For
- Consumer-style impressions
- Vanity metrics
- General brand awareness in irrelevant audiences
- Social media virality

## The Three B2B Story Types That Work
**1. Customer Win Story**
Named enterprise customer + measurable outcome + technical detail

**2. Industry Data Report**
Proprietary data from your platform + segmented insights + benchmark comparisons

**3. Standards/Category Definition**
You name a new category, define its rules, position as leader

## Quote Standards in B2B
- CEO/CTO quote should reference specific technical or market reality
- Customer quote should include numbers (% improvement, $ saved)
- Avoid analyst quotes unless they're free of paid relationship
- Investor quotes should be reasoning, not cheerleading

## Distribution Mix for B2B
- Trade press first (Information Week, TechCrunch Enterprise, ZDNet)
- Industry analyst briefings before public release
- LinkedIn newsletter same day
- Wire for SEO (B2B buyers Google before buying)
- Webinar / podcast appearance week of

## Common B2B PR Failures
- Generic "AI-powered" claims
- Customer quote that says nothing specific
- Funding announcement with no use-of-funds clarity
- Survey data with N too small or methodology missing
- "Our platform helps companies..." without naming what kind

## Quality Rubric for B2B PR
- Audience is enterprise buyer, not consumer
- Story type matches B2B repertoire
- Quotes reference specifics, not generics
- Methodology disclosed for any data
- Trade press + analyst + LinkedIn distribution
- Inbound metrics tracked, not just impressions
