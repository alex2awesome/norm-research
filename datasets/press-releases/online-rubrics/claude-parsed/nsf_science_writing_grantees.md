# NSF: Science Writing Tips for Grantees

## NSF's Public Engagement Mission
- Grantees encouraged to communicate findings
- NSF acknowledgment required in releases
- Plain language preferred over technical
- Multimedia integration encouraged
- Educational hooks valued

## Press Release Standards for NSF Grantees

### NSF Acknowledgment
- "This research was supported by the National Science Foundation under grant XXXXX"
- Required in all NSF-funded research releases
- Should appear in body of release
- Logo usage subject to guidelines

### Scientific Accuracy
- Conclusions match underlying paper
- Methodology summarized
- Limitations stated
- Effect sizes given properly
- Funding disclosed
- Co-author institutions credited

### Plain Language Standards
- 8th-grade reading level
- Active voice
- Concrete examples for abstract concepts
- Define technical terms
- Use analogies appropriately
- Visual aids for complex concepts

## Story Angles That Work
- Real-world applications
- Connection to current events
- Educational opportunities
- Citizen science components
- International collaboration
- Diverse research teams

## Multimedia Recommendations
- Researcher photos (with consent)
- Lab/field site images
- Visualizations of findings
- Animations for mechanisms
- Video of researchers explaining
- Infographics for data

## Distribution Recommendations
- Coordinate with university PR office
- NSF Office of Legislative and Public Affairs (OLPA) review
- EurekAlert posting
- Wire syndication for SEO
- Social media amplification
- Local press for regional angles

## Quality Rubric (NSF)
- NSF acknowledgment included
- Scientific accuracy verified
- Plain language for public
- Multimedia rich
- Real-world relevance shown
- Methodology summarized
- Limitations stated
- Educational hooks included
