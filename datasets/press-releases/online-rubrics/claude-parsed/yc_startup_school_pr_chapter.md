# YC Startup School: PR and Press for Startups

## YC's Core Principles
- PR is not a substitute for product
- Founders should do PR themselves at first
- Truth is the best PR strategy
- Build relationships, not transactions
- Quality of coverage > quantity

## When You're Ready for PR
Check all boxes:
- Product is working in production
- 10+ paying customers (or 1,000+ active users)
- Founder can articulate the problem clearly
- You have data or proof points to share
- You can spare time for media days

## Story Hierarchy for Startups (YC)
**Tier 1 — Always Worth PR**
- Series A+ funding
- Major customer wins (named)
- Acquisition or significant partnership
- Industry-defining data report

**Tier 2 — Often Worth PR**
- Notable hire (if person is famous)
- Major product launch with new capability
- Crisis or controversy response
- Pivot announcement

**Tier 3 — Rarely Worth PR**
- Minor product updates
- Junior hires
- Vanity awards
- Generic "we hit milestone X"

## YC's Pitching Rules
- Always pitch to one reporter at a time
- Personalize based on their recent work
- Offer exclusive when possible
- Honor embargoes religiously
- Don't follow up more than once

## Founder Interview Prep
- 3 key talking points memorized
- 1 anecdote ready for "tell me a story"
- Specific numbers ready (revenue, users, growth)
- Honest about what you don't know
- Avoid hype language
- Practice with someone on the team first

## Crisis Communication for Startups
- Acknowledge within hours, not days
- Be specific about what happened
- Take responsibility, don't deflect
- Outline concrete corrective steps
- Update regularly during ongoing situation

## What to Skip
- Hiring expensive agency too early
- Wire-only distribution
- Vanity metrics in press releases
- Pitching the same story to many reporters
- Press tours before product is ready

## Quality Rubric (YC Startup School)
- Product genuinely ready
- Tier 1 or 2 story type
- Founder is spokesperson
- Personalized pitches
- Exclusive offered
- Honest, specific claims
- Founder prepared for interviews
