# Founder Institute: PR Workshop for Early-Stage Founders

## Workshop Overview
2-hour workshop on practical PR for founders pre-Series A.

## Module 1: Why PR Now?
- Builds inbound pipeline (talent, customers, investors)
- Creates social proof for cold outreach
- Differentiates in crowded market
- Long-term reputation asset

## Module 2: When PR Doesn't Help
- Pre-product
- Pre-PMF
- During stealth periods
- When competing on price (not differentiation)
- When team can't sustain media obligations

## Module 3: The Founder's PR Toolkit
- Email outreach template
- Press release template (use sparingly)
- HARO/Connectively for source-building
- Muck Rack for journalist research
- LinkedIn for direct outreach

## Module 4: Story Development
- What's the founding story?
- What problem are you solving?
- What's the surprising insight you have?
- What's the data you uniquely possess?
- What's the human angle?

## Module 5: Pitching Reporters
- Build list of 20 relevant reporters
- Read 5+ articles per reporter
- Personalize first email
- Subject line: news + entity in 8 words
- Body: 100-200 words
- Single clear ask
- Phone number provided

## Module 6: Press Release Anatomy
- Headline: news first, brand second
- Lede: 5 W's in 50 words
- Body: 3-4 paragraphs inverted pyramid
- Quote: 25-35 words from founder
- Boilerplate: 50 words
- Contact: founder direct
- "###" end marker

## Module 7: Distribution
- Direct outreach for actual coverage
- Wire for SEO
- LinkedIn for amplification
- Newsletter inclusion
- Podcast appearances

## Module 8: Crisis Basics
- Acknowledge within 2 hours
- Specific facts
- Named accountability
- Concrete actions
- Update cadence

## Quality Rubric (Founder Institute)
- Founder is spokesperson
- Story types match stage
- Personalized pitching
- Multimedia ready
- Press release as one tool
- Crisis protocol established
