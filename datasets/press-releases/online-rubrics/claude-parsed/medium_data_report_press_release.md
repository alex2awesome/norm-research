# Medium: Data Report Press Release Template

## Why Data Reports Drive PR
- Journalists love proprietary data
- Establishes thought leadership
- Generates long-tail SEO
- Reusable for follow-up coverage
- Becomes industry reference

## What Makes a Releasable Data Report
- Original data your competitors don't have
- Sample size justifies generalizations
- Methodology defensible
- Surprising or counter-intuitive findings
- Tied to broader industry trend

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Surprising Finding] in [Industry], According to New Report"
Examples:
- "67% of Remote Workers Cite Loneliness as Top Concern, New Survey Shows"
- "Cybersecurity Spending Up 40% in 2025, Outpacing IT Budgets"

### Lede
"A new report from [Company] finds that [surprising finding]. The [study type] of [N] [respondents] reveals [implication for industry]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: Top Finding**
- Most surprising or important finding
- Concrete numbers
- Comparison to expectation/baseline

**Para 2: Methodology**
- Sample size
- Demographics surveyed
- Time period covered
- How conducted (online, phone, observational)
- Margin of error

**Para 3: Additional Findings**
- 2-3 secondary insights
- Subgroup differences
- Year-over-year comparisons

**Para 4: Implications**
- What this means for the industry
- What it means for affected stakeholders
- Future trends suggested

**Para 5: Quote**
- From research lead or company executive
- Substantive interpretation
- Honest about limitations

### Boilerplate
- Standard company description
- Methodology link
- Full report download

## Multimedia Requirements
- Headline chart with key finding
- Infographic with multiple findings
- Methodology summary visual
- Customer/respondent quote graphics
- Social-media-ready images

## Distribution Strategy
- Embargoed exclusive to top trade outlet
- Coordinated release to industry press
- Wire syndication for SEO
- Webinar discussing findings
- LinkedIn carousel breakdown
- Podcast pitch on findings

## Quality Rubric for Data Reports
- Original data
- Methodology disclosed
- Sample size and demographics provided
- Margin of error stated
- Surprising findings highlighted
- Implications clear
- Multimedia comprehensive
- Full report accessible
