# Medium: The Acquisition Press Release Template

## When to Use
- Acquisition completed (not LOI signed)
- Strategic acquisition (not financial only)
- Material to either company's stakeholders
- Customer impact significant

## What Doesn't Work
- "We're combining forces" buzzword
- Financial terms undisclosed when material
- No employee transition clarity
- No customer impact addressed
- Long boilerplates from both companies

## Template Structure

### Headline
"[Acquirer] Acquires [Target] to [Specific Strategic Outcome]"

### Lede
"[Acquirer], [brief description], today announced it has acquired [Target], [brief description], in a [type of] transaction valued at [amount, if disclosed]. The acquisition will [specific strategic outcome]."

### Body Paragraphs
**Para 1: Strategic Rationale**
- Why this acquisition specifically
- What capability is gained
- What market position is achieved

**Para 2: Customer Impact**
- What changes for customers
- What stays the same
- Timeline of any changes
- Support transition plan

**Para 3: Employee Impact**
- Leadership team transitions
- Workforce continuity
- Office locations
- Cultural integration approach

**Para 4: Financial Details**
- Transaction structure
- Deal value if material
- Expected close date
- Regulatory approvals needed

**Para 5: Quotes**
- Acquirer CEO on strategic rationale
- Target CEO on what acquirer brings
- Both should sound substantive

### Boilerplate
- Brief description of both companies
- Combined entity if forming new company
- Headquarters locations
- Total customer base / scale

### Contact Information
- Both companies' communications contacts
- Investor relations if public
- Crisis communications backup

## Distribution Strategy
- Same day across both companies
- Coordinated with regulatory filings
- Customer email same day
- Employee notification before public release
- Wire and direct outreach combined

## Quality Rubric for Acquisitions
- Strategic rationale clear
- Customer impact addressed specifically
- Employee impact addressed
- Financial terms appropriate to materiality
- Substantive CEO quotes from both sides
- Regulatory timing clear
- Coordinated cross-company release
