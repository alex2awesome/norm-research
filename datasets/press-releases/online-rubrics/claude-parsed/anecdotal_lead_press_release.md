# Anecdotal Lead Technique for Press Releases

## Why Use an Anecdotal Lead
- Reporters want stories
- Anecdotes make PR pieces:
  - Easier to believe
  - Easier to understand
  - Easier to remember
- Beats statistics for engagement
- Captures attention immediately

## Best Practice: Find a "Poster Person"
A single human being who can stand for your point. Don't lead with numbers — lead with a person.

## Anecdotal Lead Standards

### Must Be
- Interesting
- Closely illustrative of broader point
- Specific (not generic)
- Concrete detail
- Verifiable (real person, real situation)

### Followed By
- Broader significance explained within first few sentences
- Connection to news angle
- Pivot to data/announcement
- Bigger picture

## Example Structure

### Award-Winning Example (Department 56)
"Twenty-five years ago, on a holiday outing, a group of friends set off for dinner at a small country inn..."
[Continues with specific details...]
"This was the original inspiration for The Original Snow Village Collection."

### Pattern
1. Specific human moment
2. Sensory/specific details
3. Pivot to news connection
4. Broader announcement

## Types of Effective Leads

### Anecdotal
- Story-based opening
- Specific person/situation
- Sensory details

### News (Direct)
- Straight news in first sentence
- Inverted pyramid
- Best for breaking news

### Question
- Provocative question
- Hooks reader curiosity
- Best for thought leadership

### Quote
- Compelling quote first
- Sets tone immediately
- Best for major statements

### Data/Stat
- Striking number
- "1 in 4 Americans..."
- Best for survey/research

### Scene
- Vivid scene-setting
- Cinematic opening
- Best for feature-style

## Implicit Lead Quality Rubric

A lead is high-quality if:
1. Hooks reader in first sentence
2. Specific (not generic)
3. Connects to broader news within 3 sentences
4. Avoids lead-burying clichés ("In a move that...")
5. Uses active voice
6. Has concrete details
7. Pivots smoothly to news
8. Sets appropriate tone
9. Audience-appropriate
10. Verifiable (real)

## When to Use Anecdotal Leads
- Feature-style stories
- Human interest angles
- Cause-related releases
- Long-form releases
- Magazine-style outlets

## When NOT to Use
- Breaking hard news
- Wire-style for pickup
- Earnings/financial
- Crisis communications
- Time-sensitive announcements (use direct lead)
