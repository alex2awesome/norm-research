<h2>Planning Your Nonprofit Annual Report</h2>
<p>First of all, if creating your nonprofit&#8217;s annual report is a dreaded chore, take heart and read this case study: <a href="https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/how-i-came-to-love-my-nonprofits-annual-report-and-how-you-can-love-yours-too/">How I Came to Love My Nonprofit&#8217;s Annual Report &#8212; and How You Can Love Yours Too!</a>&nbsp; &nbsp;We know from our research that <a href="https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/your-favorite-and-least-favorite-projects-this-year-word-clouds/">you either love it or hate it!</a></p>
<p>Before you get too far, you need to <a href="https://www.nonprofitmarketingguide.com/answer-these-three-questions-before-starting-your-nonprofit-annual-report/">answer these three questions before getting started:</a></p>
<ul>
<li>What are your accomplishments for the year?</li>
<li>What do you want supporters to remember about this year?</li>
<li>What format will the report take?</li>
</ul>
