Press & Media

National media relations are handled by National Headquarters in Alexandria, VA, and its public relations consultant.

Missouri and Southern Illinois media relations are handled through the Divisional Headquarters, based in St. Louis, MO.

All local media are asked to first contact their local Salvation Army representative for requests for spokespersons and other inquiries.

The Salvation Army Mission Statement

The Salvation Army, an international movement, is an evangelical part of the universal Christian Church. Its message is based on the Bible. Its ministry is motivated by the love of God. Its mission is to preach the gospel of Jesus Christ and to meet human needs in His name without discrimination.
