IOC/IPC Social Media Guidelines

Athlete Marketing

The IOC and IPC encourage athletes and other accredited persons to engage in social and digital media activities, sharing and celebrating their experiences with their global audiences. Please note, however, that some conditions apply, especially surrounding commercial use and the sharing of audio and video.

For an overview on the Social Media Guidelines for the Paris 2024 Games, please watch this video. As of Fall 2025, the Milano Cortina Games guidelines were not yet available, however there is a strong likelihood the same principles from Paris will apply for these Winter Games as well. Please check the IOC and IPC websites for latest updates.

Dates Effective 

These guidelines apply during Games times. Both the IOC and IPC guidelines are in effect from the opening to the closing of the Olympic Villages.
