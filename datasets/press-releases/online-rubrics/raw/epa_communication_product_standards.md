    <article class="node node-document clearfix view-mode-full" role="article">

  
  <p>This stylebook is intended to help EPA staff prepare and review Agency <span>communications. Our main focus is to guide the style, format and presentation of EPA’s content and communications. </span><span>The EPA Stylebook provides guidance for most media, including print documents, audiovisual, broadcast, presentation </span><span>and exhibit work. </span></p>

<div>
	<p><span>The 2009 EPA Stylebook and the Stylebook Index are available for download below. </span></p>
</div>
