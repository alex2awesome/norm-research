					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content"><div class="mw-subjectpageheader">
</div><div class="mw-content-ltr mw-parser-output" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">U.S, Securities and Exchange Commission form</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1320445320">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}@media print{body.ns-0 .mw-parser-output .hatnote{display:none!important}}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For other uses, see <a href="/wiki/8K_(disambiguation)" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="8K (disambiguation)">8K (disambiguation)</a>.</div>
<p><b>Form 8-K</b> is a very broad form used to notify investors in United States public companies of specified events that may be important to <a href="/wiki/Shareholder" title="Shareholder">shareholders</a> or the <a href="/wiki/United_States_Securities_and_Exchange_Commission" title="United States Securities and Exchange Commission">United States Securities and Exchange Commission</a>. This is one of the most common types of forms filed with the SEC. After a significant event like <a href="/wiki/Bankruptcy" title="Bankruptcy">bankruptcy</a> or departure of a <a href="/wiki/CEO" class="mw-redirect" title="CEO">CEO</a>, a public company generally must file a Current Report on Form 8-K within four business days to provide an update to previously filed quarterly reports on <a href="/wiki/Form_10-Q" title="Form 10-Q">Form 10-Q</a> and/or Annual Reports on <a href="/wiki/Form_10-K" title="Form 10-K">Form 10-K</a>. Form 8-K is required to be filed by <a href="/wiki/Public_company" title="Public company">public companies</a> with the SEC pursuant to the <a href="/wiki/Securities_Exchange_Act_of_1934" title="Securities Exchange Act of 1934">Securities Exchange Act of 1934</a>, as amended.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Academic researchers make this report metadata available as structured datasets in the <a href="/wiki/Dataverse#Harvard_Dataverse" title="Dataverse">Harvard Dataverse</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="When_Form_8-K_is_required">When Form 8-K is required</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Form_8-K&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: When Form 8-K is required"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p>Form 8-K is used to notify investors of a current event.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These types of events include:
</p>
<ul><li>signing, amending or terminating material definitive agreements not made in the ordinary course of business, bankruptcies or <a href="/wiki/Receivership" title="Receivership">receiverships</a></li>
<li>mine shutdowns or violations of mine health and safety laws</li>
<li>consummation of a material asset acquisition or sale</li>
<li>results of operations and financial condition, creating certain financial obligations, such as incurrence of material debt</li>
<li>triggering events that accelerate material obligations (such as defaults on a loan)</li>
<li>costs associated with exit or disposal plans (<a href="/wiki/Layoff" title="Layoff">layoffs</a>, shutting down a plant, or material change in services or outlets)</li>
<li>material impairments</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Delisting_(stock)" class="mw-redirect" title="Delisting (stock)">delisting</a> from a securities exchange or failing to satisfy listing requirements</li>
<li>unregistered <a href="/wiki/Stock" title="Stock">equity</a> sales (private placements)</li>
<li>modifications to shareholder rights</li>
<li>change in <a href="/wiki/Accountant" title="Accountant">accountants</a></li>
<li>determinations that previously issued financial statements cannot be relied upon</li>
<li>change in control</li>
<li>senior officer appointments and departures</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Board_of_Directors" class="mw-redirect" title="Board of Directors">director</a> elections and departures</li>
<li>amendments to certificate/articles of incorporation or bylaws</li>
<li>changes in fiscal year</li>
<li>trading suspension under employee benefit plans</li>
<li>amendments or waivers of code of ethics</li>
<li>changes in shell company status</li>
<li>results of shareholder votes</li>
<li>disclosures applicable to issuers of asset-backed securities</li>
<li>disclosures necessary to comply with Regulation FD</li>
<li>other material events</li>
<li>certain financial statements and other exhibits.</li></ul>
<p>Investors should always read any 8-K filings that are made by companies in which they are invested. These reports are often material to the company, and frequently contain information that will affect the <a href="/wiki/Share_price" title="Share price">share price</a>.
</p>
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Reading_Form_8-K">Reading Form 8-K</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Form_8-K&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Reading Form 8-K"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p>Typically an 8-K filing will only have two major parts: the name and description of the event and any exhibits that are relevant. The name and description of the event contains all the information that the company considers relevant to shareholders and the SEC. It is important to read this information, as it has been deemed "material" by the company. Any exhibits that are relevant may include financial statements, press releases, data tables, or other information that is referenced in the description of the event.
</p>
