PR Newswire Makes Press Release Distribution Easy in 4 Simple Steps

1. Upload Your Press Release

When you submit your news release, it is sent to our team of industry experts who serve as trusted advisers. In addition to proofreading every release, we will also look for broken links, ensure proper tagging, and provide SEO-related content best practices for immediate and long-term search performance.

2. Choose Your Target Audience

You decide how specifically or broadly you want to distribute your press release. Select from the most accurate, comprehensive, and inclusive targeting lists: from locations to industries, verticals, beats (e.g., small business, regulatory compliance), demographics, and more.

3. Add Multimedia

Our research shows press releases that include multimedia receive up to 6x greater engagement. Our platform easily allows you to embed photos, videos, logos, infographics, and other dynamic content into your release with just a few clicks.

4. Send It and Track the Results

Understand the impact of your release with our Visibility Report. This complimentary report provides press release analytics that allows you to see on line pickup, traffic, audience data, engagement activity, and other key metrics for every release you send.
