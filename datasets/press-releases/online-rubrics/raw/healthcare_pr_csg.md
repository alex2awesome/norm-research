class="entry-content-wrap"><div class="entry-content single-content"><h6>Updated: February 19, 2026</h6><h2><b>What is </b><b>Healthcare Public Relations?</b></h2><p><a href="https://wearecsg.com/industries/health/">Public relations in healthcare</a> is simply the use of communications tools—such as press releases, social media accounts and traditional media pieces—to spread a strategic message relevant to medicine or public health management. Although healthcare PR is defined as a subcategory of PR and shows similarities to many other forms of PR, it entails unique challenges and therefore calls for unique expertise. We will walk you through the basics of this niche so that your next public relations campaign can reach the right people, convey the right message and achieve the right result. Just what the doctor ordered!</p><h2><b>Why PR Is Crucial in Healthcare Today: Top 4 Reasons</b></h2><p>It’s no secret that healthcare PR campaigns have a profound impact on healthcare consumer behavior. By using a suite of PR techniques to establish thought leadership in one or several specialties, medical facilities in the digital age are drawing patients from outside their practice areas; <a href="https://www.invoca.com/blog/healthcare-marketing-statistics">those patients usually sift through a number of options</a> using narrow online searches before booking an appointment.</p><p>The days have passed when people, were more or less obliged to assume that one hospital, one doctor, was as good as the next. The ever-savvy, smartphone-brandishing consumer devours social media, testimonials, reviews, broadcast media placement, and other signals of distinction or trustworthiness, not just when seeking medical help but upon every occasion of consumption. The significance of this change for healthcare providers and public health entities is clear: either you come up with a medical PR plan, or your facility, practice, organization, etc., no matter how excellent, disappears from public view under a torrent of competing communications.</p><p>But here’s the good news. Public relations in healthcare does not have to be complicated to be effective, and it can bring about several benefits in the public’s relationship with you whether you’re a healthcare policymaker or provider. These benefits include:</p><ul><li aria-level="1">Education</li><li aria-level="1">Trust</li><li aria-level="1">Community</li><li aria-level="1">Thought leadership</li></ul><p>Let’s get into those in more detail.</p><h3><b>1. Educate Beyond the Hospital</b></h3><p>In many commercial sectors, it might be a stretch to describe a public relations campaign as a public service. In healthcare, however, the line between public interest and organizational interest is far more intertwined. Healthcare PR often centers on medical advancements, emerging health challenges, treatment options and preventative care—information that can directly impact people’s lives.</p><p>Because of this, education is not an add-on to healthcare PR; it is inherent to it. Effective healthcare communications inform communities, clarify complex medical topics and empower patients to make better decisions. That educational role makes healthcare PR valuable not only for its impact on an organization’s reputation or bottom line, but also for its broader contribution to public understanding and well-being.</p><h3>2. <b>Build Trust</b></h3><p>The medical world is filled with specialized terminology, complex protocols and deeply rooted philosophies that can feel inaccessible to the average person. When patients don’t fully understand what’s happening behind the scenes, uncertainty can easily turn into distrust. Thoughtful healthcare public relations helps close that gap, translating complexity into clarity and restoring confidence in providers, treatments and healthcare systems as a whole.</p><p>Effective medical PR prioritizes clear, straightforward language that meets audiences where they are. By breaking down complicated concepts into their most essential points, a well-crafted communications campaign empowers patients to feel informed, included and respected. An experienced PR partner can help hospitals and healthcare organizations build trust and strengthen community goodwill by spotlighting clinical outcomes, innovative contributions, patient stories and credible third-party validation, bringing together proof points that reinforce both expertise and compassion.</p><h3>3. <b>Create Communit</b>y</h3><p>Public relations in healthcare can bring people together by showing them that their lives are part of a larger tapestry. If you collect diverse patient experiences on a single platform like your blog, for example, or publish articles on health conditions that people struggle to find solid information about elsewhere, you can cultivate a sense of hope and togetherness that <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3122271/">people tend to find compelling</a>.</p><h3>4. <b>Grow Thought leadership</b></h3><p>Public relations in the healthcare space helps organizations earn the recognition they deserve for their ongoing innovations and cutting-edge solutions and positions them as thought leaders in their fields. Through strategic media coverage, relevant speaking engagements and savvy publishing, PR professionals can help medical and educational organizations show their expertise, tell their unique stories and continue to respond to the needs of their audiences to solidify their leadership position in the industry.</p><h2><b>Best Practices for Healthcare PR In 2024</b></h2><p>With all that in mind, it’s time to discuss how you should approach your next healthcare PR endeavor. Our best practices for these types of campaigns are:</p><ul><li aria-level="1">Understand your audience and medium</li><li aria-level="1">Engage press decision-makers</li><li aria-level="1">Localize your story</li><li aria-level="1">Proofread before publishing</li><li aria-level="1">Check for HIPAA and FDA compliance</li></ul><h3>Understand Your Audience and Medium</h3><p>The better you understand the people you’re speaking to, the better you’ll predict their emotional responses to what you’re putting out and the sharper your decisions will be throughout your healthcare public relations campaign.</p><h3>Engage Press Decision Makers</h3><p>If you’re trying to get a healthcare story featured in traditional media such as newspapers or broadcast, propose the story to reporters and producers. These are the people who will actually grant life to the content you want publicized. Getting your pitch as close as possible to the ultimate source of the message ensures that the impact of your pitch isn’t lost in translation. Maybe read other articles the reporter has written, or do research about their preferred audience and weave that reconnaissance into your proposal.</p><h3>Localize Your Story</h3><p>It’s only natural that <a href="https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2018/01/11/publics-around-the-world-follow-national-and-local-news-more-closely-than-international/">people respond more strongly</a> to news that concerns them, their families and their communities than to news from far afield. A story’s proximity to the reader’s own life can change it from an interesting abstraction to a deeply emotional issue. You can harness this rule in your medical PR campaign by highlighting the aspects of your PR pieces that hit close to home for your audience. But this requires that you understand your audience first (see above).</p><h3>Proofread Your Press Release Before Hitting Send</h3><p>Especially when it comes to medical information, accuracy is key. The last thing you want when health is at stake is to mislead people, overstate a point or misinterpret a piece of scientific evidence in the public eye. Make sure your healthcare public relations plan includes a proofreading step for every item you put out; this will keep your campaign on track and prevent a communications crisis.</p><h3>Beware of HIPAA and FDA Rules</h3><p>One wrinkle in healthcare public relations is the requirement of keeping provider and patient information private. The <a href="https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/marketing/index.html">HIPAA Privacy Rule</a> sets out safeguards against unauthorized disclosure that you will need to comply with in your medical PR pieces. Generally, material defined as “marketing” under HIPAA criteria will require more steps in advance of publication to stay on the right side of the law.</p><h2><b>Examples of Public Relations In Healthcare We Love</b></h2><p>Looking for inspiration? These healthcare public relations examples speak for themselves when it comes to the power and potential of a well-crafted PR campaign.</p><h3><b>Spring Health’s Data-Driven Strategy</b></h3><p>Spring Health, a New York-based B2B behavioral health organization, is on a mission to eliminate barriers to mental health. They work to make mental healthcare accessible and achievable by leveraging clinically validated AI technology.</p><p>To secure a database of skilled professionals and meet the growing demand for mental health services, Spring Health leveraged marketing recruitment tactics and digital marketing efforts to attract providers who met their criteria.</p><p><b>Key Takeaways</b></p><p>Those campaign efforts resulted in a <a href="https://nogood.io/2023/07/05/healthcare-marketing-campaigns/">113% increase in provider-qualified leads</a> and a 58% increase in official hires. What’s the key takeaway here? Data has an enormous potential to help you find, message and resonate with your target audience to achieve results.</p><h3><b>Non-Opioid EXPAREL</b></h3><p>A subsidiary of Pacira Biosciences, EXPAREL is a cutting-edge, long-acting non-opioid anesthetic that effectively manages postsurgical pain. Since its introduction in 2012, over 9 million patients have received EXPAREL as a safe and effective alternative to opioids.</p><p>When they wanted to create a PR campaign to increase high-quality traffic that converts to leads, they focused on user engagement journeys, conducting stakeholder interviews to better understand processes, needs and opportunities. </p><p>To achieve this, clear audience segments and mapped engagement journeys were established to guide strategy. Comprehensive website analytics and in-depth stakeholder interviews were also conducted to gain a deeper understanding of existing processes, user needs and areas of opportunity for improvement.</p><p><b>Key Takeaways</b></p><p>The campaign resulted in <a href="https://nogood.io/2023/07/05/healthcare-marketing-campaigns/">307% more healthcare providers</a> connected directly with EXPAREL to integrate into their practice, a 106% increase in direct traffic and a 46% increase in organic sessions. Benefit-driven ad creative is an effective tool to educate and resonate with patients and providers.</p><h3><b>Patient Stories (New York-Presbyterian Hospital)</b></h3><p>One of the largest and most prestigious healthcare facilities in the United States, New York-Presbyterian Hospital is known for providing exceptional healthcare to its communities. To capture and share its patient-centered approach, the hospital launched a “Patient Stories” series, which showcased real-life patient stories to strike an emotional connection to its larger audience.</p><p>This commitment was evident in the “<a href="https://nogood.io/2023/07/05/healthcare-marketing-campaigns/">Patient Stories”</a> series, which effectively captured the essence of the hospital’s compassionate and patient-centered approach. By showcasing real-life stories and experiences of patients who received care at the hospital, the campaign achieved its goal: to spark an emotional connection in its audience and underline the hospital’s commitment to providing exceptional healthcare services.</p><p><b>Key Takeaways</b></p><p>Personal narratives are incredibly powerful. Real, raw stories tap into an emotional response that elicits hope, raises awareness and garners a positive impact.</p><h2><b>Ready to take your </b><b>healthcare public relations strategy </b><b>to the next level?</b></h2><h3><b>Schedule a Consultation With Healthcare PR Pros</b></h3><p>Communications Strategy Group® (CSG) has a <a href="https://wearecsg.com/what-we-do/public-relations/">wealth of experience</a> in healthcare public relations.
