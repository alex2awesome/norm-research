It's time to celebrate the extraordinary

Get ready - entries are officially open for the 2026 Mumbrella Awards! These awards are media and marketing's ultimate gala celebrating the standout work, ideas and teams that, over the past 12 months, have shaped culture, driven commercial impact and pushed the industry forward.

Across 36 categories spanning marketing, advertising, media, production, PR and communications sectors, Mumbrella has introduced a clearer thematic structure, grouping categories across Campaign Excellence, Craft and Innovation, Specialist Disciplines, Agency and Team Awards, and Culture and Values. The result is a more intuitive framework for entrants and jurors alike, while preserving the breadth and prestige the Mumbrella Awards are known for.

New pinnacle awards, including Brand of the Year and Holdco of the Year, raise the bar at the top end of the program. The introduction of a live component for Industry Leader of the Year at Mumbrella360 further reinforces transparency, credibility and community engagement, while keeping the majority of scoring firmly in the hands of our expert jurors.

The ambition is simple: to recognise work that not only stands out creatively, but stands up commercially, culturally and strategically. Make sure you carefully review the refreshed entry criteria before you commence crafting your success stories.
