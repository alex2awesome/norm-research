<div class="part" id="part-243"><h1 data-hierarchy-metadata="{&quot;path&quot;:&quot;/current/title-17/part-243&quot;,&quot;citation&quot;:&quot;17 CFR Part 243&quot;}">PART 243—REGULATION FD
</h1>

<div class="authority"><h4 class="inline-header">Authority:</h4><p class="inline-paragraph"><a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78c" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78c</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78i" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78i</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78j" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78j</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78m" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78m</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78o" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78o</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78w" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78w</a>, <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78mm" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78mm</a>, and <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-29" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">80a-29</a>, unless otherwise noted.
</p></div>

<div class="source"><h4 class="inline-header">Source:</h4><p class="inline-paragraph"><a href="https://www.federalregister.gov/citation/65-FR-51738" class="fr-reference" data-reference="65 FR 51738">65 FR 51738</a>, Aug. 24, 2000, unless otherwise noted.
</p></div>
<div class="section" id="243.100">
<h4 data-hierarchy-metadata='{"path":"/current/title-17/section-243.100","citation":"17 CFR 243.100"}'>§ 243.100 General rule regarding selective disclosure.</h4>
<div id="p-243.100(a)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.100(a)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>a<span class="paren">)</span></span> Whenever an issuer, or any person acting on its behalf, discloses any material nonpublic information regarding that issuer or its securities to any person described in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(1)" class="cfr external">paragraph (b)(1)</a> of this section, the issuer shall make public disclosure of that information as provided in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.101#p-243.101(e)" class="cfr external">§ 243.101(e)</a>:</p>
<div id="p-243.100(a)(1)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.100(a)(1)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>1<span class="paren">)</span></span> Simultaneously, in the case of an intentional disclosure; and</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(a)(2)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.100(a)(2)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>2<span class="paren">)</span></span> Promptly, in the case of a non-intentional disclosure.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.100(b)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>b<span class="paren">)</span></span> </p>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(1)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.100(b)(1)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>1<span class="paren">)</span></span> Except as provided in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(2)" class="cfr external">paragraph (b)(2)</a> of this section, <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(a)" class="cfr external">paragraph (a)</a> of this section shall apply to a disclosure made to any person outside the issuer:</p>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(1)(i)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(1)(i)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>i<span class="paren">)</span></span> Who is a broker or dealer, or a person associated with a broker or dealer, as those terms are defined in Section 3(a) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78c" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78c(a)</a>);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(1)(ii)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(1)(ii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>ii<span class="paren">)</span></span> Who is an investment adviser, as that term is defined in Section 202(a)(11) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80b-2" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80b-2(a)(11)</a>); an institutional investment manager, as that term is defined in Section 13(f)(6) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78m" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78m(f)(6)</a>), that filed a report on Form 13F (<a href="/current/title-17/section-249.325" class="cfr external">17 CFR 249.325</a>) with the Commission for the most recent quarter ended prior to the date of the disclosure; or a person associated with either of the foregoing. For purposes of this paragraph, a “person associated with an investment adviser or institutional investment manager” has the meaning set forth in Section 202(a)(17) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80b-2" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80b-2(a)(17)</a>), assuming for these purposes that an institutional investment manager is an investment adviser;</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(1)(iii)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(1)(iii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>iii<span class="paren">)</span></span> Who is an investment company, as defined in Section 3 of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-3" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-3</a>), or who would be an investment company but for Section 3(c)(1) (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-3" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-3(c)(1)</a>) or Section 3(c)(7) (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-3" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-3(c)(7)</a>) thereof, or an affiliated person of either of the foregoing. For purposes of this paragraph, “affiliated person” means only those persons described in Section 2(a)(3)(C), (D), (E), and (F) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-2" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-2(a)(3)(C)</a>, (D), (E), and (F)), assuming for these purposes that a person who would be an investment company but for Section 3(c)(1) (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-3" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-3(c)(1)</a>) or Section 3(c)(7) (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-3" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-3(c)(7)</a>) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 is an investment company; or</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(1)(iv)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(1)(iv)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>iv<span class="paren">)</span></span> Who is a holder of the issuer's securities, under circumstances in which it is reasonably foreseeable that the person will purchase or sell the issuer's securities on the basis of the information.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.100(b)(2)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>2<span class="paren">)</span></span> <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(a)" class="cfr external">Paragraph (a)</a> of this section shall not apply to a disclosure made:</p>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(i)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(i)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>i<span class="paren">)</span></span> To a person who owes a duty of trust or confidence to the issuer (such as an attorney, investment banker, or accountant);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(ii)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(ii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>ii<span class="paren">)</span></span> To a person who expressly agrees to maintain the disclosed information in confidence;</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>iii<span class="paren">)</span></span> In connection with a securities offering registered under the Securities Act, other than an offering of the type described in any of Rule 415(a)(1)(i) through (vi) under the Securities Act (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.415#p-230.415(a)(1)(i)" class="cfr external">§ 230.415(a)(1)(i)</a> through <a href="/current/title-17/section-230.415#p-230.415(a)(1)(vi)" class="cfr external">(vi) of this chapter</a>) (except an offering of the type described in Rule 415(a)(1)(i) under the Securities Act (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.415#p-230.415(a)(1)(i)" class="cfr external">§ 230.415(a)(1)(i) of this chapter</a>) also involving a registered offering, whether or not underwritten, for capital formation purposes for the account of the issuer (unless the issuer's offering is being registered for the purpose of evading the requirements of this section)), if the disclosure is by any of the following means:</p>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(A)">
<p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(A)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>A<span class="paren">)</span></span> A registration statement filed under the Securities Act, including a prospectus contained therein;</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(B)">
<p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(B)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>B<span class="paren">)</span></span> A free writing prospectus used after filing of the registration statement for the offering or a communication falling within the exception to the definition of prospectus contained in clause (a) of section 2(a)(10) of the Securities Act;</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(C)">
<p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(C)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>C<span class="paren">)</span></span> Any other Section 10(b) prospectus;</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(D)">
<p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(D)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>D<span class="paren">)</span></span> A notice permitted by Rule 135 under the Securities Act (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.135" class="cfr external">§ 230.135 of this chapter</a>);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(E)">
<p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(E)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>E<span class="paren">)</span></span> A communication permitted by Rule 134 under the Securities Act (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.134" class="cfr external">§ 230.134 of this chapter</a>); or</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.100(b)(2)(iii)(F)"><p class="indent-4" data-title="243.100(b)(2)(iii)(F)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>F<span class="paren">)</span></span> An oral communication made in connection with the registered securities offering after filing of the registration statement for the offering under the Securities Act.</p></div>

<div class="section" id="243.101">
<h4 data-hierarchy-metadata='{"path":"/current/title-17/section-243.101","citation":"17 CFR 243.101"}'>§ 243.101 Definitions.</h4>
<p>This section defines certain terms as used in Regulation FD (<a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100" class="cfr external">§§ 243.100-243.103</a>).</p>
<div id="p-243.101(a)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(a)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>a<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Intentional.</em>  A selective disclosure of material nonpublic information is “intentional” when the person making the disclosure either knows, or is reckless in not knowing, that the information he or she is communicating is both material and nonpublic.</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(b)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(b)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>b<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Issuer.</em>  An “issuer” subject to this regulation is one that has a class of securities registered under Section 12 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78</a><em>l</em>), or is required to file reports under Section 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78o" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78o(d)</a>), including any closed-end investment company (as defined in Section 5(a)(2) of the Investment Company Act of 1940) (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-5" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-5(a)(2)</a>), but not including any other investment company or any foreign government or foreign private issuer, as those terms are defined in Rule 405 under the Securities Act (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.405" class="cfr external">§ 230.405 of this chapter</a>).</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(c)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(c)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>c<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Person acting on behalf of an issuer.</em>  “Person acting on behalf of an issuer” means any senior official of the issuer (or, in the case of a closed-end investment company, a senior official of the issuer's investment adviser), or any other officer, employee, or agent of an issuer who regularly communicates with any person described in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(1)(i)" class="cfr external">§ 243.100(b)(1)(i)</a>, <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(1)(ii)" class="cfr external">(ii)</a>, or <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(1)(iii)" class="cfr external">(iii)</a>, or with holders of the issuer's securities. An officer, director, employee, or agent of an issuer who discloses material nonpublic information in breach of a duty of trust or confidence to the issuer shall not be considered to be acting on behalf of the issuer.</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(d)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(d)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>d<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Promptly.</em>  “Promptly” means as soon as reasonably practicable (but in no event after the later of 24 hours or the commencement of the next day's trading on the New York Stock Exchange) after a senior official of the issuer (or, in the case of a closed-end investment company, a senior official of the issuer's investment adviser) learns that there has been a non-intentional disclosure by the issuer or person acting on behalf of the issuer of information that the senior official knows, or is reckless in not knowing, is both material and nonpublic.</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(e)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(e)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>e<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Public disclosure.</em> </p>
<div id="p-243.101(e)(1)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.101(e)(1)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>1<span class="paren">)</span></span> Except as provided in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.101#p-243.101(e)(2)" class="cfr external">paragraph (e)(2)</a> of this section, an issuer shall make the “public disclosure” of information required by <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(a)" class="cfr external">§ 243.100(a)</a> by furnishing to or filing with the Commission a Form 8-K (<a href="/current/title-17/section-249.308" class="cfr external">17 CFR 249.308</a>) disclosing that information.</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(e)(2)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.101(e)(2)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>2<span class="paren">)</span></span> An issuer shall be exempt from the requirement to furnish or file a Form 8-K if it instead disseminates the information through another method (or combination of methods) of disclosure that is reasonably designed to provide broad, non-exclusionary distribution of the information to the public.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(f)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(f)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>f<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Senior official.</em>  “Senior official” means any director, executive officer (as defined in <a href="/current/title-17/section-240.3b-7" class="cfr external">§ 240.3b-7 of this chapter</a>), investor relations or public relations officer, or other person with similar functions.</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(g)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.101(g)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>g<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Securities offering.</em>  For purposes of <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100#p-243.100(b)(2)(iv)" class="cfr external">§ 243.100(b)(2)(iv)</a>:</p>
<div id="p-243.101(g)(1)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.101(g)(1)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>1<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Underwritten offerings.</em>  A securities offering that is underwritten commences when the issuer reaches an understanding with the broker-dealer that is to act as managing underwriter and continues until the later of the end of the period during which a dealer must deliver a prospectus or the sale of the securities (unless the offering is sooner terminated);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(g)(2)">
<p class="indent-2" data-title="243.101(g)(2)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>2<span class="paren">)</span></span> <em class="paragraph-heading">Non-underwritten offerings.</em>  A securities offering that is not underwritten:</p>
<div id="p-243.101(g)(2)(i)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.101(g)(2)(i)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>i<span class="paren">)</span></span> If covered by Rule 415(a)(1)(x) (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.415#p-230.415(a)(1)(x)" class="cfr external">§ 230.415(a)(1)(x) of this chapter</a>), commences when the issuer makes its first bona fide offer in a takedown of securities and continues until the later of the end of the period during which each dealer must deliver a prospectus or the sale of the securities in that takedown (unless the takedown is sooner terminated);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(g)(2)(ii)">
<p class="indent-3" data-title="243.101(g)(2)(ii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>ii<span class="paren">)</span></span> If a business combination as defined in Rule 165(f)(1) (<a href="/current/title-17/section-230.165#p-230.165(f)(1)" class="cfr external">§ 230.165(f)(1) of this chapter</a>), commences when the first public announcement of the transaction is made and continues until the completion of the vote or the expiration of the tender offer, as applicable (unless the transaction is sooner terminated);</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.101(g)(2)(iii)"><p class="indent-3" data-title="243.101(g)(2)(iii)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>iii<span class="paren">)</span></span> If an offering other than those specified in <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.101#p-243.101(a)" class="cfr external">paragraphs (a)</a> and <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.101#p-243.101(b)" class="cfr external">(b)</a> of this section, commences when the issuer files a registration statement and continues until the later of the end of the period during which each dealer must deliver a prospectus or the sale of the securities (unless the offering is sooner terminated).</p></div>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="section" id="243.102">
<h4 data-hierarchy-metadata='{"path":"/current/title-17/section-243.102","citation":"17 CFR 243.102"}'>§ 243.102 No effect on antifraud liability.</h4>
<p>No failure to make a public disclosure required solely by <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100" class="cfr external">§ 243.100</a> shall be deemed to be a violation of Rule 10b-5 (<a href="/current/title-17/section-240.10b-5" class="cfr external">17 CFR 240.10b-5</a>) under the Securities Exchange Act.</p>
</div>

<div class="section" id="243.103">
<h4 data-hierarchy-metadata='{"path":"/current/title-17/section-243.103","citation":"17 CFR 243.103"}'>§ 243.103 No effect on Exchange Act reporting status.</h4>
<p>A failure to make a public disclosure required solely by <a href="/current/title-17/section-243.100" class="cfr external">§ 243.100</a> shall not affect whether:</p>
<div id="p-243.103(a)">
<p class="indent-1" data-title="243.103(a)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>a<span class="paren">)</span></span> For purposes of Forms S-3 (<a href="/current/title-17/section-239.13" class="cfr external">17 CFR 239.13</a>), S-8 (<a href="/current/title-17/section-239.16b" class="cfr external">17 CFR 239.16b</a>) and SF-3 (<a href="/current/title-17/section-239.45" class="cfr external">17 CFR 239.45</a>) under the Securities Act of 1933 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/77a" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 77a</a> <em>et seq.</em>), or Form N-2 (<a href="/current/title-17/section-239.14" class="cfr external">17 CFR 239.14</a> and <a href="/current/title-17/section-274.11a-1" class="cfr external">274.11a-1</a>) under the Securities Act of 1933 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/77a" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 77a</a> <em>et seq.</em>) and the Investment Company Act of 1940 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/80a-1" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 80a-1</a> <em>et seq.</em>), an issuer is deemed to have filed all the material required to be filed pursuant to Section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78m" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">15 U.S.C. 78m</a> or <a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/78o" class="usc external" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">78o(d)</a>) or where applicable, has made those filings in a timely manner; or</p>
</div>
<div id="p-243.103(b)"><p class="indent-1" data-title="243.103(b)"><span class="paragraph-hierarchy"><span class="paren">(</span>b<span class="paren">)</span></span> There is adequate current public information about the issuer for purposes of <a href="/current/title-17/section-230.144#p-230.144(c)" class="cfr external">§ 230.144(c) of this chapter</a> (Rule 144(c)).</p></div>
