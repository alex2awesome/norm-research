About PRSSA

Advancing the Profession and the Future Professional

Founded in 1967, the Public Relations Student Society of America (PRSSA) is the foremost organization for students interested in public relations and communications. We advocate rigorous academic standards for public relations education, the highest ethical principles and diversity in the profession.

Who We Are

Since its founding, PRSSA has served hundreds of thousands of student members, offering educational programs, resources, scholarships, and mentorship opportunities that prepare students for a successful career in communications. Chapters are located on university and college campuses across the U.S. (including Puerto Rico) and in Argentina, Colombia, Peru and Mexico. We are headquartered in New York City, and led by an International Committee of PRSSA and PRSA members.

Membership Benefits

Enhance Your Education

PRSSA can help you gain the experience and practical knowledge employers are seeking. We offer internship listings, competitions, scholarships, awards and leadership opportunities that allow you to develop your skills as both a communications professional and a leader. In addition, PRSSA provides publications, news and social media to keep you updated on organization and industry trends.

Broaden Your Network

PRSSA will connect you to a network of peers and professionals who will become lifelong friends and mentors. We facilitate the development of valuable relationships with future colleagues through PRSSA International and District events, PRSSA Chapters and social media. We also provide the opportunity to meet current professionals who are eager to help you navigate your career. You’ll be exposed to the best of the best through your PRSA sponsor Chapter, Champions for PRSSA, by searching the PRSA member directory or by attending District or International events.

Launch Your Career

If you want to jumpstart your career in public relations and communications, there is no better place than PRSSA. As a PRSSA member, you have access to the PRSA Jobcenter and PRSSA Internship Center to find public relations internship or employment opportunities all over the world and across a number of industries. When you graduate, you also may join PRSA for just $60 a year as an Associate Member, connecting you to your local chapter or one of 14 Professional Interest Sections.
