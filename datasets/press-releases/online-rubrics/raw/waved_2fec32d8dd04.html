CAPIO—Are you ready to Make Waves?
Registration Deadline - April 27

We can’t wait to welcome you to sunny San Diego, May 11–14, 2026 for the California Association of Public Information Officials’ 2026 Annual Conference.  Our agenda is packed with amazing sessions and registering by March 6 ensures you the best pricing!

This is your opportunity to showcase your work, connect with colleagues from across the state, and learn from industry experts leading the way in public sector communications. Dive into sessions covering media relations, strategic communications, social media, emergency response, branding, emerging technologies (hello, AI!), analytics, public speaking and more.

Post April 21, 2026

CAPIO Chirp Episode 28
Make Waves at CAPIO 2026 in San Diego

The countdown is on! ⏳ The annual CAPIO Conference returns the week of May 11 in San Diego! Are you ready to make some waves?

In this episode of the CAPIO Chirp, we preview what’s ahead in conversation with CAPIO President-Elect Sonja Dosti, APR. Conference highlights include sessions on AI, social media, emergency response, media relations, and building public trust. Plus networking opportunities, tips for first-time attendees, and updates to this year’s EPIC Awards celebration.

Don’t miss your chance to connect, learn, and be inspired.

We also have some bonus material for you. At the conference, be sure to connect with our sponsors/vendors. We had an opportunity to talk with a couple of them at last year’s conference in Napa.

Register by April 27 at ⁠capio.org
