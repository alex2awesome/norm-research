                                    </h2>
                        <div id="s-lg-box-collapse-34738931" >

                    <div id="s-lg-content-81754539" class="  clearfix">
    <p>Here are recent news items that may affect Northwestern&#39;s NIH research community or are notable:</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2026/04/2026-pilot-data-management-and-sharing-plan-format-available" target="_blank"><strong>2026 Pilot Data Management and Sharing Plan Format Available</strong></a>&nbsp;- April 29, 2026

	<ul>
		<li>The NIH has released a <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/sites/default/files/dms-blank-format-page-2026.docx" target="_blank">new &quot;pilot&quot; DMSP format</a>. The purpose of the new format is to simplify the DMSP for both applicants and study section reviewers. More info on the new format may be found on the <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/forms-directory/data-management-and-sharing-plan-format-page" target="_blank">NIH&#39;s Data Management and Sharing Plan Format </a>page.</li>
		<li><strong>Did you know?</strong>&nbsp;At GalterLibrary, our Senior Data Librarian Sara Gonzales has already updated her <strong><a href="https://libguides.galter.northwestern.edu/NIH-Data-Management-Plan" target="_blank">GalterGuide on the NIH DMSP</a></strong> and she has updated her <strong><a href="https://galter.northwestern.edu/course_info/265" target="_blank">class on the NIH DMSP</a></strong> as well. Both are excellent resources that will help you find all the necessary tools and information to complete the new format DMSP.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li>The NIH Extramural Nexus news site released a &quot;You Ask, We Answer&quot; article entitled <strong><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2026/04/what-should-i-know-about-the-current-and-pending-other-support-cpos-common-form" target="_blank">What Should I Know About the Current and Pending (Other) Support (CPOS) Common Form?</a></strong>&nbsp;- April 10, 2026
	<ul>
		<li>Many of Northwestern&#39;s senior research administrators already know the information provided in this short informational news item. It reminds applicants of the purpose of the CPOS and the application types it is required for.&nbsp;</li>
		<li>It also emphasizes the importance for all applicants to ensure that their ORCID ID is linked to both their eRA Commons ID and NCBI / NCBI account to ensure successful completion of the forms.&nbsp;</li>
		<li>The news post has links to helpful resources and the CPOS FAQs.
		<ul>
			<li><strong>The NIH is doing an outstanding job of updating <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm" target="_blank">FAQs for SciENcv Common Forms</a></strong>, so be sure to check them frequently for answers to your questions.&nbsp;</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57875" target="_blank"><strong>Individuals serving as senior/key personnel must certify annually to their participation or non-participation in a Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Program (MFTRP) by uploading a certification statement in RPPR Section G.1, Special Notice of Award and Funding Opportunity Announcement Reporting Requirements </strong></a>- Updated Febrary 4, 2026 - <span style="color:#ee0000;"><strong>Updated again</strong></span> February 20, 2026
	<ul>
		<li><s>This requirement is IN ADDITION to the certifications individuals make within the NIH Common Forms for biosketch and Other Support (OS) documents.&nbsp;</s>
		<ul>
			<li><span style="color:#ee0000;"><strong>Update in February, 2026</strong></span>: The NIH has clarified this issue in an updated FAQ:<br />
			<em><strong>Individuals who have submitted a Common Form biosketch or Other Support document in which they have certified for MFTRP do <u>NOT</u> have to complete the annual MFTRP as a separate document for progress reports in section G.1. Certification in the Common Forms is sufficient.&nbsp;</strong></em></li>
			<li>See the <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57890" target="_blank"><strong>updated NIH FAQ on this requirement</strong></a>.&nbsp;
			<ul>
				<li>However, If an investigator is not submitting Common Form documents (under the period of leniency through May 2026), they will be required to submit a separate MFTRP certification document and should follow the instructions in the notice linked above.&nbsp;</li>
			</ul>
			</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
		<li>&quot;The certification statement language included in the RPPR must align with the statement within the Common Forms for Biosketch and Current and Pending (Other) Support, and state: I [insert name] certify that, at the time of submission, I am not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program.<br />
		The file for each senior/key person must be named &lsquo;MFTRPcert_[Name].pdf&rsquo; without quotations, where &lsquo;[Name]&rsquo; is the name of the senior/key person and the PDF must be <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/how-to-apply-application-guide/format-attachments#flattened-pdfs" target="_blank">flattened</a> prior to upload. (<a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-018.html" target="_blank">NOT-OD-26-018</a>).&quot;</li>
		<li>The NIH also <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/sites/default/files/rppr_instruction_guide.pdf" target="_blank">updated the RPPR Instruction Guide in January 2026.</a> Information on this annual certification can be found on page 116 of this guide.&nbsp;</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-033.html" target="_blank"><strong>Adjusted Timeline for NIHs Implementation of Common Forms</strong></a> - February 4, 2026
	<ul>
		<li>Because of the number of help requests to the eRA Commons and NCBI / SciENcv help desks, and the delays in correcting technical problems in both platforms placing a &quot;severe burden on the community complying with the original timeline&quot;, <strong>the NIH is allowing a &quot;period of leniency&quot; through May 2026</strong>. Durinig this time, applicants may submit either the Common Forms or the older forms for biosketch and other support documents. A warning will still be issued when submitting the older forms, but applications will not be blocked or withdrawn from submission during the period of leniency. This period also allows for the certifications on the Common Forms to align with the Research Security Training outlined in <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-017.html" target="_blank">NOT-OD-26-017.</a>&nbsp;T<a href="https://grants.nih.gov/faqs#/common-forms-biographical-sketch-current-pending-support.htm?anchor=57833" target="_blank">his period of leniency will also apply to Research Performance Progress Reports (RPPRs), Just-in-Time (JIT) and Prior Approval (PA) submissions.</a></li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2026/01/check-out-the-latest-how-to-apply-application-guide" target="_blank"><strong>Check Out the NIH&#39;s Updated How to Apply - Application Guide</strong></a>&nbsp;- January 16, 2026
	<ul>
		<li>In December 2025, the NIH updated its <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/how-to-apply-application-guide" target="_blank">How to Apply - Application Guide</a> to incorporate significant changes to the application process, including
		<ul>
			<li>the NIH&nbsp;Biosketch Common Form and NIH Biosketch Supplement</li>
			<li>the Current and Pending (Other) Support (CPOS) Common Form</li>
			<li>the ORCID requirement</li>
			<li>Foreign justification attachments</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
		<li>See the news announcement for more details and links to resources and materials to guide the application process.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong><a href="https://osp.od.nih.gov/2026internship/" target="_blank">OSP Now Accepting Applications for 2026 Summer Internship Program</a></strong> - January 15, 2026
	<ul>
		<li>The OSP Summer Internship Program provides an opportunity for two (2) undergraduate, graduate, and/or professional students to gain tangible science policy experience in support of the NIH mission. Interns will work side-by-side with leading policy experts to learn strategies for advancing national science and technology policy and obtain practical experience in policy analyses and development. OSP will be hosting interns for an 8&ndash;12-week period beginning in May or June 2026. Interns will receive a stipend commensurate with their level of education.</li>
		<li>Applications are accepted until February 27, 2026</li>
		<li>For more information please see the OSP announcement at:&nbsp;<a href="https://osp.od.nih.gov/2026internship/" target="_blank">https://osp.od.nih.gov/2026internship/</a></li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2025/12/what-if-i-forgot-to-include-a-required-attachment-of-an-application" target="_blank"><strong>What if I Forgot to Include a Required Attachment of an Application?</strong></a> - December 22, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>The NIH will contact applicants in limited situations when files or attachments have been omitted, including:
		<ul>
			<li>Leaving out required sections of applications (e.g. foreign component justifications and support letters from institutions).</li>
			<li>Incorrect files being uploaded.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
		<li>The NIH will NOT accept requests for changes to already submitted sections (research section, budget, etc.); nor will it accept unsolicited attachments or applicant requests to replace materials.</li>
		<li>The NIH will contact the applicant&#39;s Authorized Organization Representative (AOR) who must provide the missing materials within a timeframe. Applicants should not contact the NIH directly.</li>
		<li>Details on&nbsp;providing complete and compliant application materials are provided in <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-025.html" target="_blank">NOT-OD-26-025</a>.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-023.html" target="_blank">NIH Issues Request for Information on Draft Proposal Harmonizing Research Participant Data Policies</a></strong> - December 19, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>The&nbsp;NIH is proposing:</li>
	</ul>

	<ol style="margin-left: 40px;">
		<li>A new NIH Controlled-Access Data Policy to support the research community in fulfilling NIH data sharing expectations, specifically for human subject data. The proposed policy &quot;specifies human participant data types required to be managed via controlled-access and provides criteria for assessing the need for controls for other data types. It also provides a standard set of expectations across NIH Institutes, Centers and Offices to promote maximal responsible human participant data sharing through controlled access while simultaneously responding to emergent privacy and security risks...&quot;</li>
		<li>To revise the NIH Genomic Data Sharing (GDS) Policy to reduce duplicative policy requirements that have been released in recent directives and policy developments.</li>
	</ol>

	<ul>
		<li>The draft policy is included in the notice. The NIH is requesting input on the draft policy with a <strong>deadline of March 4, 2026</strong>.&nbsp;</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2025/12/updated-nih-fellowship-resources-are-available" target="_blank">Updated NIH Fellowship Resources are Available</a>&nbsp;</strong>- December 17, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>The NIH has updated its &quot;<a href="https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOEUwSnjvqBKiRa5iTl7LpOY1MJgiZF0F" target="_blank">Focus on Fellowships</a>&quot; video series. Fellowships are open to early career scientists. Applicants are reminded to consult <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/write-application/how-to-apply-application-guide" target="_blank">instructions for current application forms</a> (Forms-I) when preparing fellowship proposals.&nbsp;</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>NIH Releases <a href="https://osp.od.nih.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Compiled-Public-Comments-on-the-RFI-on-Maximizing-Research-Funds-by-Limiting-Allowable-Publishing-Costs.pdf" target="_blank">&quot;Compiled Public Comments on the Request for Information on Maximizing Research Funds by Limiting Allowable Publishing Costs&quot;</a></strong> - December 5, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>The NIH released the compiled comments from 914 individuals and organizations in response to&nbsp;<a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-25-138.html" target="_blank">NOT-OD-25-138 Request for Information on Maximizing Research Funds by Limiting Allowable Publishing Costs</a>. The publication is long:&nbsp;comments are provided in full, with links to documents attached by respondents. Comments were made by a wide array of respondents, including individuals, publishers, university entities (medical centers, libraries, faculty organizations, university press), and professional societies. The NIH has not yet published a policy decision on this matter.&nbsp;</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2025/11/emergency-modifications-to-nih-peer-review"><strong>Emergency Modifications to NIH Peer Review </strong></a>- November 24, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>Since &quot;370 peer review meetings were cancelled between October 1, 2025 and November 14, 2025,&quot; the NIH announced plans to speed up the peer review process to address the backlog of proposals awaiting peer review and remain committed to three full rounds of peer review for the&nbsp;fiscal year. Review panels will consider proposals in three groups:&nbsp;</li>
	</ul>

	<ol style="margin-left: 40px;">
		<li>Competitive and discussed (at the meeting) and considered for funding</li>
		<li>Competitive and not discussed, but considered for funding</li>
		<li>Not competitive and not discussed - not considered for funding</li>
	</ol>

	<ul>
		<li>Additionally, peer review summary statements will be simplified.</li>
		<li>The announcement also encourages researchers to &quot;say yes&quot; when asked to participate in review sections.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong><a href="https://grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2025/11/implementing-a-unified-nih-funding-strategy-to-guide-consistent-and-clearer-award-decisions" target="_blank">Implementing a Unified NIH Funding Strategy to Guide Consistent and Clearer Award Decisions</a></strong> - November 21, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>This announcement pulls together several decisions made by the NIH to implement &quot;
		<p>steps towards a unified strategy that will help guide clearer and consistent funding decisions across all Institutes, Centers, and Offices (ICOs).&quot; This strategy will take effect in January 2026. ICOs should adhere to several tenets, such as considering budget, NIH&#39;s priorities and merit (among others). Citing an August 7, 2025 Executive Order entitled <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/improving-oversight-of-federal-grantmaking/" target="_blank">&quot;Improving Oversight of Federal Grantmaking&quot;</a>, the NIH will still consider peer review, but move away from paylines. The NIH also updated information on the Grants &amp;&nbsp;Funding <a href="https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/award/funding-decisions" target="_blank">Funding Decisions</a> page.&nbsp;</p>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><a href="https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-establishes-nations-first-dedicated-organoid-development-center-reduce-reliance-animal-modeling" target="_blank"><strong>NIH establishes nation&#39;s first dedicated organoid development center to reduce reliance on animal modeling</strong></a> - September 25, 2025
	<ul>
		<li>The NIH announced the award of &quot;contracts for launching the <a href="https://www.nih.gov/som" target="_blank">Standardized Organoid Modeling (SOM) Center</a>, a national resource that will be dedicated to using cutting-edge technologies to develop standardized organoid-based new approach methodologies (NAMs) that deliver robust, reproducible, and patient-centered research findings.&quot; The center will be housed at the Frederick National Laboratory for Cancer Research (FNLCR), a facility supported by NIH&rsquo;s National Cancer Institute (NCI).</li>

There is also an advanced search that provides more fields to narrow your search.&nbsp;</li>

Since that launch the NIH has added more topics and notices for proposals, along with an FAQ and a subscription feature that can be customized to deliver emails on all topic announcements or targeted announcements by keyword or funding program (ICO).</li>
	</ul>

	<ul>
