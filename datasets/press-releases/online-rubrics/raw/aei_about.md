<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center" id="h-about">About</h2>



<div style="height:22px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer is-style-small-spacer"></div>



<p class="has-text-align-center has-large-font-size">The American Enterprise Institute is a public policy think tank dedicated to defending human dignity, expanding human potential, and building a freer and safer world. The work of our scholars and staff advances ideas rooted in our belief in democracy, free enterprise, American strength and global leadership, solidarity with those at the periphery of our society, and a pluralistic, entrepreneurial culture.</p>



<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer is-style-medium-spacer"></div>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center" id="h-leadership">Leadership</h3>



<p class="has-text-align-center">The American Enterprise Institute’s governance is overseen by our Board of Trustees, composed of leading business and financial executives. The Institute’s external development and outreach activities are bolstered by entrepreneurs from across the country who serve on our National Council.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center" id="h-organization-and-purposes">Organization and Purposes</h3>



<p class="has-text-align-center">In all our endeavors, AEI trustees, scholars and fellows, and officers and staff are responsible for maintaining the highest standards of integrity, intellectual rigor and excellence–and for sustaining AEI’s founding commitment to open inquiry, lucid exposition, vigorous debate and continuous improvement in the institutions of American liberty.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center" id="h-our-research-areas">Our Research Areas:</h3>



<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer is-style-small-spacer"></div>



<p class="has-text-align-center">AEI’s research is organized into four core areas that reflect the breadth of its policy work: Economics, Foreign and Defense Policy, Social, Cultural, and Constitutional Studies, and Domestic Policy Studies. Each area brings together leading scholars to conduct independent, empirical research aimed at strengthening free enterprise, expanding opportunity, and safeguarding America’s role in the world. This structure enables AEI to address complex challenges with interdisciplinary insight and a commitment to democratic governance, individual liberty, and a robust national defense.</p>
