CDP's 25th Year

25 years since CDP pioneered disclosure, the number of investors backing CDP’s annual request has ballooned from just 35 to financial institutions representing a quarter of all global institutional financial assets.

Now, companies representing two-thirds of global market capitalization – from 130 countries – disclose critical environmental data through CDP. Within two years of an investor request, companies disclosing through CDP reduce their direct emissions by 7-10% on average.
