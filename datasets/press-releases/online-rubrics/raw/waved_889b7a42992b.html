Public Information Officers (PIOs)

Each institution and parole region designates an appropriate staff member as a Public Information Officer (PIO). The PIO coordinates public information and community relations programs within state prisons and parole regions. The PIO is also the Warden/institution head’s Administrative Assistant and the institution’s designee to respond to media requests.
