Title IX & Sexual Abuse Lawyers

They Protect Predators. We Protect Survivors.

Our firm holds schools, colleges, and universities accountable for the profound betrayal of sexual assault and abuse. We are experienced Title IX lawyers who dismantle the institutional cover-ups and give survivors a powerful voice in the courtroom.

Schools have a system for burying the truth. We have a record of unearthing it.

When sexual assault occurs, the institution’s first move is to protect its reputation. Their lawyers and crisis teams are experts at silencing victims, denying responsibility, and hiding evidence. Our firm exists to expose that strategy and hold them accountable for every failure that led to your harm.

Accountability for Abuse at Every Level of Education

Perpetrators and the negligent institutions that enable them must be held responsible, whether the abuse occurred in a K-12 school or on a university campus.

A Proven Record of Exposing Institutional Betrayal

Our investigation goes far beyond the perpetrator. We uncover the systemic negligence and deliberate choices that allowed the abuse to happen and continue. We find the truth by exposing:

  • Negligent Hiring & Supervision: Schools failing to perform adequate background checks or ignoring complaints made against teachers, trainers, and coaches.
  • Ignoring Reports & Warnings: Clear evidence that students, parents, or staff reported concerns about a perpetrator that were deliberately dismissed or covered up by administrators.
  • Failure to Report to Authorities: Institutions illegally concealing incidents of sexual abuse from law enforcement to avoid scandal and protect their public image.
  • Title IX Violations: Colleges and universities failing to follow federal law in responding to claims of sexual assault, thereby creating a dangerous environment for students.

Exposing these failures is the first step toward securing justice.

Sexual Assault & Abuse Litigation: Frequently Asked Questions

Can a school be held liable if the abuser was another student?

Yes. If the school knew or should have known about a threat and failed to take reasonable action, or if it fostered a hostile environment that allowed the assault to occur, it can be held legally responsible for the harm you suffered.

What if the school made me sign something or offered a settlement?

Do not let an institution silence you. Any agreement or waiver you may have signed could be invalid, especially if it was signed under duress. We can review any documents and advise you on the best path forward to hold them accountable.

Will my story and identity be made public?

We understand the importance of privacy. Our firm takes every available legal measure to protect the confidentiality of our clients. We will fight to ensure your identity is shielded from public disclosure throughout the legal process.
