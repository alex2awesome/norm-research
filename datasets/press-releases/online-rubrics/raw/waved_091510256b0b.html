For nearly 70 years, PRISA has set the benchmark for PR excellence in Africa. As the sole SAQA accredited PR body, we shape the narratives behind the continent’s most influential brands driving societal trends and industry innovation. From corporate leaders to emerging voices, PRISA is the hub for Southern Africa’s PR industry, championing ethics, diversity, and growth.

The PRISMS Summit & Awards 2025

Join us from 16 to 18 October 2025 for the PRISMS Summit and Awards Gala. Over three inspiring days, the Summit will bring together industry leaders, strategists and communicators to explore trends, share insights and strengthen the future of communications under the theme The Human Factor. The experience culminates in the prestigious PRISMS Awards Gala on 18 October, celebrating excellence, creativity and impact across the profession. Be part of Africa’s most anticipated communications event, where learning, networking and recognition come together to move our industry forward.

Young Voices Programme

PRISA's Young Voices initiative empowers the next generation of PR professionals through mentorship, skill transfer, and hands-on experience. As Africa's youth drive the continent's rapid growth, our young protégés stand ready to seize unprecedented opportunities and shape the future of PR in Africa's thriving markets.
