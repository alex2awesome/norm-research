<p>RIKEN is active on a wide variety of fronts communicating to the public, both within Japan and around the world, the research results achieved at its many centers and labs. The main channels for this communication include press and news releases, various events and symposia, publications such as annual reports and pamphlets, and videos and other multimedia content.</p>
