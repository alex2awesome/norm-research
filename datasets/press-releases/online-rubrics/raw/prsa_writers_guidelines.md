    <div class="sfContentBlock sf-Long-text" ><h2>Writer’s Guidelines</h2><p>PRSA produces a variety of content for PR professionals, including the award-winning <em>Strategies &amp; Tactics</em>.</p><p><em>Strategies &amp; Tactics</em>&nbsp;will help you stay up-to-date with the latest news, best practices and how-to information about everything from employee communications to crisis management to social media. The publication also provides feature-length commentary on the strategic importance of public relations  and insights on changing concepts in communications.</p><p>Our goal is to offer lifelong learning to help you enhance your job skills, remain competitive, and progress during all stages of your career.</p><h3><strong>We’re interested in articles that:</strong></h3><ul><li>Explain new trends in public relations</li><li>Discuss new technologies that are changing the way that professionals practice public relations</li><li>Interview exceptional communications practitioners</li><li>Convey timely news analysis of issues impacting the profession</li><li>Analyze case studies of successful PR campaigns with measurable outcomes</li><li>Reflect the growth and change in crucial areas of professional development in the form of how-to pieces</li></ul><h3><strong>Key elements for&nbsp;<em>Strategies &amp; Tactics</em>&nbsp;submissions:</strong></h3><p>All columns must be written in a lively, easy-to-read format. For the how-to angle, topics should focus on how professionals can best execute a tactical skill. Place emphasis on the steps required to successfully complete a typical PR task.
