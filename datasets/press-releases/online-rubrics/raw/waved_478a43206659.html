UK and Ireland media enquiries

For UK and Ireland media enquiries please contact The Salvation Army United Kingdom and Ireland Territory.

UK and Ireland news centre

All other media enquiries

For routine media enquiries, please contact the IHQ Communications team using this form.

Our office hours are 08.30 - 17.00 UK time, Monday to Friday. Outside of these hours, urgent media enquiries requiring IHQ support should be sent to our out-of-hours mailbox.

 

Only media-related enquiries will be responded to.

All other correspondence or requests for help to The Salvation Army should be directed to the appropriate national or regional office.
