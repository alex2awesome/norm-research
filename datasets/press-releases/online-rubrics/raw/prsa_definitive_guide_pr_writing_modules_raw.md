    <div class="sfContentBlock sf-Long-text" ><p>The only thing worse than finding out your PR writing isn’t good enough is&nbsp;<strong>NOT</strong>&nbsp;finding out.</p><p>If no one ever tells you what’s wrong, if no one ever gives you the tools to fix it, then you’ve got to live with the consequences.</p><p>And the consequences for bad PR writing are&nbsp;<span style="text-decoration: underline">not</span>&nbsp;pretty. You get passed over for promotions. Your writing gets ignored. Your writing gets called out by a snarky journalist. Your writing even&nbsp;<strong>stops</strong>&nbsp;people
