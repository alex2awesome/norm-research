    to the success of overall programs or campaigns.<br><br></h4></div>

    <div class="sfContentBlock sf-Long-text" ><h4 style="text-align: center"><strong>SILVER ANVIL STRATEGIC CAMPAIGN AWARDS</strong></h4><p><strong>1. COMMUNITY RELATIONS</strong> <br>1A. Associations/Nonprofit Organizations<br>1B. Government<br>1C. Business<br>Includes programs that aim to improve relations with, or seek to win the support or cooperation of, people or organizations
    in communities in which the sponsoring organization has an interest, need or opportunity. “Community” in this category refers to a specific geographic location or locations.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>2. CONTENT MARKETING</strong> <br>2A. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>2B. Business to Business<br>2C. Business to Consumer<br>Programs that effectively demonstrate a strategic program that includes creating and distributing
    valuable content to attract, acquire, and engage target audience(s). Include examples and metrics.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>3. CRISIS COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>Includes programs undertaken to deal with an unplanned event that required an immediate response.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>4. EVENTS AND OBSERVANCES</strong> <br>4A. More Than Seven Days<br>4AA. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>4AB. Business<br>4B. Seven Or Fewer Days<br>4BA. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>4BB. Business<br>Includes virtual or in-person programs or events, such as commemorations, observances, conferences, openings, yearlong anniversaries, celebrations or other special activities. Events that took place for longer than a one-week period should be entered
    in “4A. More Than Seven Days” and events occurring within a time span of one week should be entered in “4B. Seven Or Fewer Days.”</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>5. FINANCIAL COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>Includes programs directed to shareowners, other investors and the investment community; includes communication programs related to decentralized currencies.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>6. GLOBAL COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>Includes any type of program, such as Reputation/Brand Management, Marketing or Events and Observances, that demonstrates effective global communications implemented in more than one country.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>7. INFLUENCER MARKETING</strong> <br>7A. Macro-Influencers<br>7B. Micro-Influencers<br>Macro-influencers: Celebrities, executives, bloggers, and journalists who have 10,000–1M followers. They have the highest topical relevance on the
    spectrum, with category-specific influence – such as lifestyle, fashion or business.<br>Micro-influencers: Everyday consumers or employees or industry experts who have 500–10,000 followers. They have the highest brand relevance and resonance
    on the spectrum of influencers, with influence driven by their personal experience and strength of relationship with their networks.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>8. INTEGRATED COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>8A. Associations/Nonprofit Organizations<br>8B. Government<br>8C. Business to Business<br>8D. Consumer Products<br>8E. Consumer Services<br>Includes creative and effective integrated campaigns,
    along with other marketing or communications including paid, earned, shared and owned efforts. Demonstrate the meaningful PR/communications components and how they integrated with other disciplines.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>9. INTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>9A. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>9B. Business<br>Includes programs targeted specifically to special publics directly allied with an organization, such as employees, members, affiliated
    dealers and franchisees.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>10. ISSUES MANAGEMENT</strong> <br>For programs undertaken to deal with issues that could extraordinarily affect ongoing business strategy.</p><p><strong>11. MARKETING</strong> <br>11A. Business to Business<br>11B. Consumer Products – Regional Focus<br>11C. Consumer Products – National/International Focus<br>11D. Consumer Services<br>Includes programs designed to introduce
    new products/services or promote existing products/services to a particular audience.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>12. MOST EFFECTIVE CAMPAIGN ON A $5,000 TO $10,000 BUDGET</strong> <br>Effective campaign delivered within parameters of a limited budget. This does not include staff time and overhead.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>13. MOST EFFECTIVE CAMPAIGN ON A SHOESTRING BUDGET ($5,000 OR LESS)</strong> <br>Successful campaign despite constraints of a small budget. This does not include staff time and overhead.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>14. MOST EFFECTIVE DIVERSITY, EQUITY AND INCLUSION (DEI) CAMPAIGN</strong> <br>Efforts devoted to promoting inclusivity and/or dismantling discrimination for racial, ethnic, religious or sexual orientation and gender differences.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>15. CLIMATE ACTION &amp; ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY</strong> <br>This honors excellence in energy efficiency, natural resources, programs and campaigns that limit greenhouse gas emissions, minimize waste and contamination, protect and restore
    ecosystems, and contribute to the adaptation to climate change.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>16. CORPORATE CITIZENSHIP</strong> <br>This celebrates campaigns and programs that ignite individuals, technology, and partnerships to create positive impact for society at the local, national or international level; including but not limited
    to areas such as poverty, hunger, housing, health and well-being, climate, equity, education, and human rights.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>17. MULTICULTURAL PUBLIC RELATIONS</strong> <br>For any type of program, such as institutional, marketing and community relations, specifically targeted to a cultural group.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>18. PUBLIC AFFAIRS</strong> <br>Includes programs specifically designed to influence public policy and/or affect legislation, regulations, political activities or candidacies — at the local, state or federal government levels — so
    that the entity funding the program benefits.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>19. PUBLIC SERVICE</strong> <br>19A. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>19B. Business<br>19C. Partnerships (Funded jointly by businesses and other organizations, including nonprofit and government)<br>Includes programs
    that advance public understanding of societal issues, problems or concerns.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>20. REPUTATION/BRAND MANAGEMENT</strong> <br>20A. Associations/Government/Nonprofit Organizations<br>20B. Business<br>Programs designed to enhance, promote or improve the reputation of an organization with its publics or key elements of
    its publics, either proactively or in response to an issue, event or market occurrence.</p></div>

PAID PROMOTIONS</strong> <br>Use of sponsorships, paid placements, or other marketing communications tactics that complement a broader public relations strategy or approach and generate exceptional ROI. These efforts should be targeted and

 programs and events driven entirely by media relations. Submit press releases, media advisories, pitch letters, requests for coverage, etc., along with the one-page summary that includes measurable objectives and results, including media impact. Upload
    or provide YouTube/Vimeo links to any television or radio coverage. The Non-Packaged Goods subcategory refers to consumer products, such as clothing, appliances, furniture, etc. The Packaged Goods subcategory refers to traditional consumer products
    sold in packages, such as food products, pet products, household goods, toiletries, cosmetics, etc.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>3. FEATURE STORIES</strong> <br>Feature articles that have been written by a practitioner and submitted and published through his/her efforts. Articles must be written in their entirety or substantively by the entrant, and not merely “pitched.”</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>4. EXECUTIVE COMMUNICATIONS</strong> <br>Positioning of an executive at any level across earned, owned, and share platforms.     as well as copies of significant placements, social media, posts or memos.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>SHARED:</strong></p><p><strong>5. SOCIAL MEDIA</strong> <br>5A. Single Channel Use<br>5B. Multichannel Use<br>How did you use social media to tell a story or connect with an audience? Share screenshots of links to your work and provide detailed results focusing on engagement
    and conversions, where applicable.</p><p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>6.
