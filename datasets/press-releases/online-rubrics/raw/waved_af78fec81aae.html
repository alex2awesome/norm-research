Winners of first Mumbrella Asia Awards revealed

Alex Hayes
May 8, 2015 7:57

An idea to tackle prejudice against people living with HIV in Hong Kong has won the Mumbrella Award for Bravery at the inaugural Mumbrella Asia Awards overnight.

TBWA\Hong Kong claimed the accolade for charity AIDS Concern at the ceremony at the Sheraton Hotel in Singapore this evening.

Campaign of the year was awarded to APD Interact for its Merdeka 2014 campaign for Malaysian telco Maxis.
