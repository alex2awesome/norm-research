  <h1 style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>Submission Information</strong></h1><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">To better help you pitch your op-eds, we offer the information for the top online and print publications in the country. This list is far from exhaustive. Many publications are open to op-ed and freelance submissions.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">We suggest that you choose a publication and then read recent issues/posts to see if your idea/topic has been covered recently. If so, another outlet might be a better fit.&nbsp;</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">If you’re interested in a publication not listed here, we suggest searching the name of the news outlet and the words “opinion” and “editor” on X’s (formerly known as Twitter) search bar and looking under the “people” vertical when the search results come up. Most editors list their contact information in their X/Twitter bio.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">If you are an editor of a publication that is not listed and would like to be listed or if you know information for an outlet that is not listed, please notify us at <a href="mailto:info@theopedproject.org" target="_blank">info@theopedproject.org</a>. We update this information regularly and are always looking for new thought leadership publications to add to the list. [<em>Updated as of October 2025</em>]</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>To increase your chances of publication, </strong>join us any Thursday at 5 pm ET for our <a href="https://www.theopedproject.org/webinars">Ask A Journalist office hours</a>. One of our master editors will live-edit a few drafts (chosen from among those you submit) and will answer your questions in real time.</p><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;"><strong>To go deeper,</strong> attend one of our <a href="https://www.theopedproject.org/workshops">workshops</a>. You will emerge with an op-ed draft, access to our national network of journalist mentors for individual follow-up, and a clearer idea of your potential impact on the world.</p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: Letters to the editor are welcome on topics of local or state interest. They should be no longer than 400 words and include the name, daytime phone number and e-mail address of the author. Email: <a href="mailto:letters@theadvocate.com">letters@theadvocate.com</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: 250 words or less. Letters may be edited for publication in The Huntsville Times, The Birmingham News, or Mobile Press Register. You can also submit online here. Must include hometown.  Email: <a href="mailto:letters@al.com">letters@al.com</a>. </p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: Complete this <a href="http://www.mcall.com/services/mc-letter-to-the-editor-ngux-htmlstory.html">online form</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Op-eds</strong>: 600 words max. Fully disclose relationship with issue and expertise. Facts should be annotated. Write, edit, and save copy before copying and submitting to this <a href="http://speedway.tucson.com/letters/?template=&amp;action=opinion">online form</a>. </p>

<p><strong>Op-eds</strong>: All submissions for publication in the Arizona Republic must be accompanied by your full name, your mailing address, and your day phone. May be distributed through print and online. Subject matter must be relevant, provocative, constructive, and timely.  </p>
<p><strong>Letters</strong>: No longer than 200 words. No anonymous letters. Submit <a href="http://static.azcentral.com/opinion/letters/">here</a>.  </p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: Email: <a href="mailto:letters@ajc.com">letters@ajc.com</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: 150 words. Email: <a href="mailto:views@statesman.com">views@statesman.com</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Op-eds</strong>: 650-750 words. Local topics and authors preferred. Email <a href="mailto:commentary@baltsun.com">commentary@baltsun.com</a>. Or submit to their <a href="http://www.baltimoresun.com/news/opinion/bal-oped-submission-ngux-htmlstory.html">online form</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Letters</strong>: To submit a letter to the editor, use their [online form](<a href="http://www.baltimoresun.com/news/opinion/bal-letter-submission-ngux-htmlstory.html">http://www.baltimoresun.com/news/opinion/bal-letter-submission-ngux-htmlstory.html</a> or email <a href="mailto:talkback@baltimoresun.com">talkback@baltimoresun.com</a>).</p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: Timely, original, and short. Respond to pieces in Globe. Letters must be signed and include a daytime number. Letters should be 200 words or less. Email: <a href="mailto:letter@globe.com">letter@globe.com</a>. </p>

<p><strong>Op-eds</strong>: <a href="mailto:letterstoeditor@bostonherald.com">letterstoeditor@bostonherald.com</a><br><strong>Letters</strong>: Submit to their online form <a href="http://www.bostonherald.com/about/herald_history/2012/12/send_letter_editor">here</a>.</p>

<p><strong>Letters</strong>: 250 words max. Letters must be written to editors of the News. Must be able to provide source material for your arguments. Email: <a href="mailto:lettertoeditor@buffnews.com">lettertoeditor@buffnews.com</a>. </p>
