				<p>If certain voices or experiences are missing from the news, we don&#8217;t get the full picture. Look for voices less commonly heard in society, often because of race, class, generation, gender, sexual orientation, ideology or the region they live in.</p>

				<button class="tj-ti-toggle-btn">Questions To Ask<span>&nbsp;</span></button>

				<div class="tj-ti-rule"></div>

				<div data-open="0" class="tj-ti-card--questions tj-ti-toggle">

					<ul>

						<li>Does the newsroom commit to bringing in diverse perspectives?</li>

						<li>Is there evidence that the journalist pays attention to diversity?</li>

						<li>Are many types of communities represented accurately and fully? Are some completely missing?</li>

					</ul>

				</div>

			</div>

		</div>

	</div>

	<div class="tj-ti-card tj-ti-card-8">

		<div class="tj-ti-card--header">

			<div class="tj-ti-card--header__order">

				<p>8</p>

			</div>

			<div class="tj-ti-card--header__text">

				<h2>Actionable Feedback</h2>

			</div>

		</div>

		<div class="tj-ti-card--text-container">

			<div class="tj-ti-card--sub-header">

				<p>Does this news site listen to me?</p>

			</div>

			<div class="tj-ti-card--text">

				<p>By inviting and listening to public feedback, journalists can make sure their work is accurate and complete. The public also might help them find important news they had overlooked.</p>

				<button class="tj-ti-toggle-btn">Questions To Ask<span>&nbsp;</span></button>

				<div class="tj-ti-rule"></div>

				<div data-open="0" class="tj-ti-card--questions tj-ti-toggle">

					<ul>

						<li>Does the news site invite the public to participate?</li>

						<li>Do they ask for feedback?</li>

						<li>Do they correct errors quickly, clearly and prominently?</li>

					</ul>

				</div>

			</div>

		</div>

	</div>
