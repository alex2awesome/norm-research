<p>The <b>Council of Science Editors</b> (<b>CSE</b>), formerly the <b>Council of Biology Editors</b> (<b>CBE</b>; 1965–2000) and originally the <b>Conference of Biology Editors</b> (<b>CBE</b>; 1957–1965),<sup id="cite&#95;ref-:0&#95;1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> is a United States–based nonprofit organization that supports editorial practice among scientific writers. In 2008, the CSE adopted the slogan "CSE: Education, Ethics, and Evidence for Editors (E4)".
</p><p>A volunteer board of directors leads the Council, with the assistance of several committees. CSE is managed by Riggs Enterprise Corp, located in New Jersey. 
</p>

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="History_and_organization">History and organization</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Council_of_Science_Editors&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History and organization"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p>The organization was established in 1957 by the <a href="/wiki/National_Science_Foundation" title="National Science Foundation">National Science Foundation</a> and the <a href="/wiki/American_Institute_of_Biological_Sciences" title="American Institute of Biological Sciences">American Institute of Biological Sciences</a> as the Conference of Biology Editors (CBE).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-:0&#95;1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In 1965, the organization incorporated as the Council of Biology Editors "and soon thereafter expanded membership to include all scientific publishing endeavors from science editors to copy editors."<sup id="cite&#95;ref-:0&#95;1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> On January 1, 2000, it was renamed the Council of Science Editors.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-:0&#95;1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The membership of CSE comprises editorial professionals, mainly in the United States.
</p><p>As well as providing services and advice online, CSE holds an annual meeting that includes short courses on topics such as journal editorship, publication management, manuscript editing, and journal metrics.
</p>
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Publications">Publications</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Council_of_Science_Editors&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Publications"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p>CSE publishes a <a href="/wiki/Style_guide" title="Style guide">style guide</a> for <a href="/wiki/Scientific_paper" class="mw-redirect" title="Scientific paper">scientific papers</a>, <i>The CSE Manual, Ninth Edition: Scientific Style and Format for Authors, Editors, and Publishers, Council of Science Editors.</i>
</p><p>As of 2024, it is in the 9th edition, and has been renamed <i>The CSE Manual</i> <sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The 8th edition was published in 2014,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the 7th edition in 2006,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the 6th in 1994.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>CSE has partnered with the <a href="/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Press" title="University of Chicago Press">University of Chicago Press</a> to use the successful online platform of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Chicago_Manual_of_Style" title="The Chicago Manual of Style">The Chicago Manual of Style</a></i> (which provides users with search and personal <a href="/wiki/Annotation" title="Annotation">annotation</a> of the manual) to publish the CSE Manual online. Through this partnership, the eighth edition<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and ninth edition<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are available online.
</p><p><i>Science Editor</i> is the quarterly publication of the CSE; after one year, articles are available as open access (<a href="/wiki/Delayed_open_access" class="mw-redirect" title="Delayed open access">delayed open access</a>).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
