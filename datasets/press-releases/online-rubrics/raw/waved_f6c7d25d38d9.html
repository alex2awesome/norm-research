Green OA and Self Archiving

As mentioned in the "Open Access Does Not Equal Public Access" page of this guide, green open access (OA) refers to the authors accepted manuscript, not the final, copy edited, published journal's version.

Under green OA, authors are allowed to deposit the authors manuscript into their repository of choice -- an institutional repository or a funder-specified respository like PubMed Central (PMC). This action is usually completed by the authors or their delegates, not the journal or the publisher. Look for instructions on "self archiving" or "green open access" at publishers' sites. 

Publishers and Journals that Allow Zero Embargo Green OA

Some publishers or journals will allow authors to deposit their manuscript (usually the authors accepted manuscript (AAM)) into a federal or institutional repository with no embargo period and with no fees. These policies are compatible with the NIH Public Access policy's requirement for deposit of the AAM with no embargo to PubMed Central (PMC), usually via the NIH Manuscript Submission system (NIHMS) if authors or grantees are depositing the manuscripts themselves. Be sure to read the publisher or journal's instructions for self archiving the AAM, as some publishers require you to include a link to the published version of record in your uploaded version, or may have other language you should include in your manuscript.

We are working on a list of publishers and journals that have zero embargo green open access policies. This list is displayed here and will be updated as frequently as we are made aware of these publishers' or journals' zero embargo / green OA policies.

Also, Galter Library directly contacted many publishers and asked them questions regarding author self-archiving and any policies they have that relate to the NIH Public Access Policy's requirement for no embargo deposit of manuscripts to PubMed Central. Quoted text under each publisher is taken from 1) the publisher / journal instructions for authors pages AND / OR 2) publisher responses to our emailed inquiries.

Publishers

AAAS / Science

"Authors of peer-reviewed research can post the Accepted Version to the public repository of their choice. Additionally, authors of peer-reviewed research articles can post the Accepted Version of their work, along with a link to the Final Published Version on the Science Website, to the public repository or their choice for public release immediately upon publication by AAAS... authors should include a hyperlink to the Final Published Version on the Science Website, along with an explanatory note that includes the full citation, as follows: “This is the author’s version of the work. It is posted here by permission of the AAAS for personal use, not for redistribution. The definitive version was published in [Science
Journal Title] on [date]; DOI: [insert DOI number].” "

 

American Academy of Pediatrics

Specific policy for NIH-funded manuscripts: 

"US federally-funded authors are granted permission to deposit the Author Accepted Manuscript in PubMed Central (PMC) or CDC Stacks immediately upon publication.
No 12-month Embargo.
Authors are required to state NIH or CDC funding at time of submission. [Bold emphasis added by Galter Library - please note that you must inform the journal of NIH funding at submission, not acceptance]
Authors are responsible for depositing the "Author Accepted Manuscript" (final Word file) in PMC"

 

American Association for Cancer Research (AACR)

"The Author Accepted Manuscript is permitted to be deposited in PMC and made available to the public on or after the first online publication date of the Version of Record...If the corresponding author is an AACR Active Member and the article is not published under an open access license, AACR will, upon author request, deposit the Author Accepted Manuscript in PMC on the author's behalf. All other authors must self-deposit the Author Accepted Manuscript in PMC." 

 

American Chemical Society (ACS)
(only via Northwestern's participation in CARLI - the Consortium of Academic and Research Libraries in Illinois)

Effective January 2026, a "read and green" agreement is in place between participating CARLI (Consorium of Academic and Research Libraries in Illinois) institutions, including Northwestern University, and American Chemical Society.

For hybrid journals, authors of accepted manuscripts may deposit in a repository of choice, with no embargo and a Creative Commons license of their choice (CC BY or CC BY-NC-ND).

  • When completing their Journal Publishing Agreement, authors who would like to participate in the program will need to opt-in by selecting the option to retain copyright and be allowed to deposit their manuscript .
  • There is no fee for authors to participate in the program.

More information and step-by-step instructions.

 

American Society for Microbiology

Green Open Access: "Authors retain the right to post their accepted manuscripts to their institutional repository and/or on their personal or university-hosted websites (e.g., institutional repositories, etc.) without embargo, under a CC-BY Creative Commons license provided that a URL linking to the published article on the Journal's website appears and proper credit is given to the Journal."
Additional language for NIH-funded manuscripts: "For manuscripts accepted on or after July 1, 2025, the NIH requires public access in PMC upon acceptance. As a Gold Open Access publisher, we make articles freely available on our site and deposit them in PMC on the official date of publication. NIH-funded authors who publish in an ASM primary research journal are automatically in compliance with this policy, and no action is required from them. Articles published under a Creative Commons license are available in the PMC Open Access Subset."

 

American Society for Tropical Medicine and Research

For the society journal: the American Journal of Tropical Medicine and Hygiene: "For articles for which the open access fee is not paid, authors must honor a 12-month embargo on current content, however they may deposit their paper in a public repository. Please email the managing editor to notify them after the deposit."

 

American Speech-Language-Hearing Association

"ASHA deposits to PubMed Central, on behalf of authors, any articles that have received NIH funding... Articles accepted on or after July 1, 2025, are made publicly available upon publication in issue."
"Important Note Regarding Perspectives of the ASHA Special Interest Groups: At this time, Perspectives of the ASHA Special Interest Groups is not indexed by PubMed, and ASHA Journals cannot process the deposit of Perspectives articles into PubMed Central. Authors of these NIH funded articles are still obliged to comply with the public access mandate, however, by depositing the accepted manuscript version of their article to PubMed Central themselves as soon as the article has been accepted. NIH funded Perspectives authors should consult their funding information for more details on how to comply or go directly to the NIH Manuscript Submission System to begin that manuscript deposit process."

 

American Thoracic Society

"If any of the authors have been supported financially by the NIH to conduct the research that is reported in the article, they should indicate this on the copyright form and provide the grant number and contact name on the title page of the manuscript. Per updated NIH requirements, the Author Accepted Manuscript (AAM)/ahead-of-print version of an article should be uploaded by the authors to the NIH Manuscript Submission (NIHMS) system (https://www.nihms.nih.gov/login/?next=/submission/) after the final acceptance email has been received. This mandate applies to NIH-funded articles accepted on or after July 1, 2025. Authors will not be charged an associated fee to upload these articles. [Note: When authors upload their NIH-funded articles, these should include an ATS copyright; in addition, these articles are not OA unless the articles have additional funders who require OA status.]"

 

Annual Reviews

"NIH-funded authors may deposit their Author Accepted Manuscript in NIHMS immediately upon acceptance with no embargo or fee, and the existing Copyright Transfer Agreement already permits compliance. Annual Reviews does not submit to NIHMS on behalf of authors but will provide the accepted manuscript, metadata, and DOI to support author submissions."

 

Association for Computing Machinery

"All ACM published authors of magazine articles, journal articles, and conference papers retain the right to post the pre-submitted (also known as "pre-prints"), submitted, accepted, and peer-reviewed versions of their work in any and all of the following sites: Author's Homepage; Author's Institutional Repository; Any Repository legally mandated by the agency or funder funding the research on which the work is based; Any Non-Commercial Repository or Aggregation that does not duplicate ACM tables of contents. Non-Commercial Repositories are defined as Repositories owned by non-profit organizations that do not charge a fee to access deposited articles and that do not sell advertising or otherwise profit from serving scholarly articles. (bold emphasis added by Galter Library)
Authors should include an appropriate citation and attribution statement on all Submitted or Accepted versions of the Work similar to the following:
"© {Owner/Author | ACM} {Year}. This is the author's version of the work. It is posted here for your personal use. Not for redistribution. The definitive Version of Record was published in {SourcePublication}, http://dx.doi.org/10.1145/{number}." "

 

BMJ Group

Updated July 2025: "AAMs may be posted on PMC with zero embargo from Issue Publication (the date the article is ‘published online’) if required by funder...BMJ Group deposits the AAM of all NIH-funded research to NIHMS upon acceptance with zero-embargo."
Galter Library note: BMJ journals are considered a PMC Journals, but note that BMJ deposits the AAM to NIHMS, not directly to PMC, so action may be required on the authors' or PIs' behalf to complete the NIHMS processing steps for deposit to PMC for these journals. 
Details on non-open access manuscript publishing and funder requirements can be found in the table at this link: https://www.bmj.com/company/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Non-Open-Access-Articles-July-2025.pdf. See the row labeled "PubMed Central and its mirror sites ("PMC")."

 

Cambridge University Press

Mixed policies: From the publisher response to our inquiries: "Some of our journals allow immediate deposit of the Accepted Manuscript whereas others have an embargo period. For journals with an embargo period, if authors are unable to pay an APC to publish open access in one of our hybrid journals, we will support authors subject to the NIH mandate to publish open access with an exceptional waiver of the fee."
For Gold Open Access articles "We deposit the Version of Record (VoR) of relevant articles to PubMed Central directly upon publication, without embargo...Authors whose VoRs are deposited by us do not also need to deposit their Accepted Manuscripts."
A sample Cambridge University Press exclusive license was provided to us September 3, 2025. 
From the publisher's Green Open Access page: "For articles that are not published Gold OA, our Green Open Access policy provides another way for authors to comply with funders’ open access requirements, by allowing authors to make pre-final versions of their journal articles publicly accessible in non-commercial websites."

 

Canadian Science Publishing

"All authors who publish in our subscription-based journals can post their accepted (post-print) manuscripts, immediately upon acceptance, on their own website, an institutional repository, a preprint server, or their funding body's designated archive."

 

Company of Biologists

While the Company of Biologists is not a green open access publisher, "For authors who need to comply with the NIH Public Access policy, we do allow self-archiving of the author accepted manuscript in PMC with zero embargo upon the date of publication. If such authors choose and/or are eligible for one of our Gold Open Access options, we will continue to deposit the final published version of the article in PMC on their behalf; we encourage this option to ensure full compliance with the policy."

 

Human Kinetics

"Human Kinetics allows authors to submit the accepted version of their files as required by NIH policy. Please note that Human Kinetics does not have an agreement with PubMed Central, meaning that Human Kinetics cannot deposit articles to PubMed Central on behalf of authors. However, authors can ensure compliance with their funding agencies by self-archiving with PubMed Central." Human Kinetics does not impose an embargo on authors to self-archive to PMC via NIHMS.

 

Institute of Mathematical Statistics

Authors retain the right to deposit manuscripts in PMC. For their hybrid/subscription journals, the Copyright Transfer Agreement (CTA) states authors may provide the final peer-reviewed manuscript to NIH upon acceptance for PMC archiving “as soon as possible but no later than 12 months after publication.” Additionally, their fully OA titles are under CC BY 4.0 licenses, allowing free deposit to PMC.

 

JAMA and JAMA Network

"JAMA and all the journals in the JAMA Network permit authors of original research articles to deposit their accepted manuscript in a public repository of their choosing on the date of acceptance provided that it is not made publicly available until the date of publication."

 

Karger

"As a service to authors, Karger Publishers submits the accepted, unedited version of NIH-funded manuscripts to PMC on the authors’ behalf. Where the authors have made their paper Open Access under Karger’s Author’s Choice™ service, the Version of Record will be submitted upon publication."

 

National Academy of Sciences

For their journal Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, "Since 2004, PNAS has offered green open access to everyone. Immediately upon publication, authors may deposit their accepted manuscript in their funding body’s archive or designated noncommercial institutional repository, under the same license as the published article, provided that a link to the published article in PNAS is included. The accepted manuscript is the version of the article that was accepted for publication after peer review and prior to copyediting by PNAS. Authors may make their deposited accepted manuscripts publicly available immediately upon publication by PNAS."  Deposit upon acceptance is not explicitly allowed; deposit is allowed upon publication, though there is no embargo period imposed.

 

Portland Press

"Green Open Access. An Author’s Accepted Manuscript may be deposited in institutional or subject-specific repositories without embargo. Immediate Green deposition is supported as a way for authors to achieve funder and institutional compliance across all our journals. When archiving your Author Accepted Manuscript, please make clear that it is not the final Version of Record,  and make sure to include a link to the Version of Record along with attribution to the author and journal."

 

Rockefeller University Press

"Authors have the right to deposit the Author Accepted Manuscript with any repository if they are required to do so pursuant to a grant or funding agreement." Rockefeller University Press (RUP) uses a License to Publish rather than a traditional copyright transfer, allowing authors to retain copyright. NIH-funded authors who choose the non–open access option may deposit their Author Accepted Manuscript in NIHMS immediately upon acceptance with no embargo, though Journal of Cell Biology and Journal of Experimental Medicine charge a $2,000 page fee for this “green” route.

 

Sage

"Sage's Green OA policy permits authors depositing their Original Submission or Accepted Manuscript at any time and in any format in PMC.

NIH funded authors publishing their article open access (OA) at Sage do not need to take any action because their final published PDF will be deposited in PMC by Sage on publication. NIH funded authors that are not publishing their article OA will need to self-archive their AAM upon publication via NIHMS.

Some Sage journals have arranged automatic PMC deposit of the Author Accepted Manuscript (AAM) of articles indicating NIH funding, meaning NIH funded authors do not need to deposit via NIHMS because Sage will deposit on their  behalf. This list of journals can be found under the NIH / HHS heading at Sage's funder compliance page.

 

Wolters Kluwer / Lippincott

"Authors supported by funders who mandate open access publication, but do not fund the Gold open access route may deposit their final peer-reviewed manuscript (or “author accepted manuscript (AAM)”) in their institutions or other non-commercial repository. This allows them to make it publicly available after an embargo period."

HOWEVER, for the NIH:
"Authors can comply with this policy by publishing their article as open access. In such cases, the journal will deposit the final published article to PubMed Central on behalf of the authors, ensuring public availability without embargo on the official date of publication.
If the article is not published as open access, authors can comply by depositing their accepted manuscript to PubMed Central upon acceptance for publication."
