<h1 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center" id="h-aei-collegiate-network">AEI Collegiate Network</h1>



<div style="height:20px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>



<p>The AEI Collegiate Network is an intellectual home for current undergraduates to engage with AEI scholarship, connect with AEI scholars, and cultivate relationships with other public-spirited students from campuses around the nation and world. Membership in the network provides students with educational and professional opportunities with one of America’s preeminent think tanks. Through a series of educational and networking opportunities, the AEI Collegiate Network exposes the next generation of leaders in public policy, media, business, academia, law, and other fields to the research and people of AEI and prepares them to model a competition of ideas on their campuses and beyond.</p>



<div style="height:30px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-about-us">About Us</h2>



<div class="wp-block-media-text alignwide is-stacked-on-mobile" style="grid-template-columns:41% auto"><figure class="wp-block-media-text__media"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" width="1024" height="662" src="https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-1024x662.jpg?x97961" alt="" class="wp-image-1008740260 size-full" srcset="https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-1024x662.jpg 1024w, https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-300x194.jpg 300w, https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-768x497.jpg 768w, https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-1536x993.jpg 1536w, https://www.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/240913_AEI_FallSummit_083-2048x1324.jpg 2048w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure><div class="wp-block-media-text__content">
<p>AEI’s outreach to college campuses began in 2013 through the launch of the Executive Council Program. Starting from 25 campuses, the program expanded to more than 75 universities across the nation with over 400 members annually. AEI’s outreach to the higher education community will continue to grow, while deepening the experience of each student in our network. Our mission to improve intellectual discourse on public policy on college campuses begins with empowering individuals to engage in substantive dialogue and civil debate and continues by fostering connections between such public-spirited leaders across the country.&nbsp;</p>
</div></div>



<p></p>



<p>Students in the Collegiate Network are offered a variety of educational and professional development opportunities, including:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Hosting AEI scholars and other nationally renowned leaders on campus for lectures, panels, and roundtable discussions</li>



<li>Leading reading groups with other students on-campus and online</li>



<li>Attending exclusive public policy summits and conferences at AEI’s headquarters in Washington, DC and at partner campuses across the country</li>
</ul>



<p>Join a network of peers committed to civil debate and free inquiry. Alumni of the program have gone on to attend some of the nation’s top graduate schools, work on Capitol Hill and various DC public policy think tanks, and have been published in leading newspapers and magazines.</p>
