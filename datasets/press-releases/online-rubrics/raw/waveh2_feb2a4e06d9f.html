CIPR appoints Professor Susan Kinnear as new Chair of Examiners

27
April
2026
| 09:44 Europe/London

The Chartered Institute of Public Relations (CIPR) is pleased to announce that Professor Susan Kinnear Chart.PR FCIPR has been appointed as the Institute's new Chair of Examiners.

Susan takes over from Ruth Matthews who is retiring after more than 11 years in the role and is responsible for maintaining standards across the different CIPR qualifications delivered in the UK and overseas by CIPR Accredited Teaching Centres (ATCs). The Chair of Examiners has a strategic advisory role to ensure that CIPR Awarding Body assessment processes and course regulations are fit for purpose including responsibility for developing assessment processes, course regulation and policy to ensure alignment with best practice in the area of Awarding Body services.

Susan is Deputy Vice-Principal (Science, Engineering and Business) at the University of Dundee and a Chartered Practitioner and Fellow of the CIPR. Her recruitment is for an initial three-year term and her tenure will begin in June 2026.

CIPR opens #StateofPR 2026 survey as industry navigates AI and workforce change

13
April
2026
| 11:02 Europe/London

The Chartered Institute of Public Relations (CIPR) has launched its State of the Profession 2026 survey, aimed at capturing insights on how PR professionals work, what challenges they're facing, and the latest trends in the industry.

The survey captures practitioner perspectives on the health of the profession including skills and training needs, salaries, the reputation of PR, and how practitioners are responding to developments in artificial intelligence and the global news agenda.

Now in its fourteenth year, the State of the Profession survey is the largest and longest-running survey of its kind. The insight gathered supports the Institute to benchmark standards, guide its policy agenda, and make effective representations on behalf of the profession to business, partners, and legislators.

The survey is open to all, takes approximately 10 minutes to complete, and all participants can enter a prize draw for a £100 gift card on completion.

CIPR introduces compulsory EDI learning as part of annual CPD requirement

07
April
2026
| 10:29 Europe/London

The Chartered Institute of Public Relations (CIPR) has announced that from 2026–2027, members that engage in the Institute’s CPD cycle will be required to log a minimum of five Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) points as part of their annual professional development.

The change positions EDI learning alongside the existing ethics requirement within the 60-point annual CPD framework. Members' CPD cycles will only be marked as complete once both components are fulfilled, and the MyCPD dashboard will track progress against the new requirement in the same way it does for ethics points.

Over 80 free EDI activities are already listed on the CIPR’s CPD database, covering areas such as inclusive communications, understanding bias and behaviour, and building inclusive organisations. Members will also be able to log bespoke or external EDI learning as a custom activity.

The change was recommended by the CIPR's EDI Committee and approved by its Council and Board. It follows the Institute becoming the first professional body to achieve the National Equality Standard in 2023 and was identified as a priority point of consideration in its EDI Strategy.
