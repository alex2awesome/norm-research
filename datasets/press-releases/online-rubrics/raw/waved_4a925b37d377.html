Brand Guidelines for B Corps

Whether you’re looking to communicate more effectively, or driving a cohesive communication strategy towards our collective vision of economic systems, here are some tools, tips and resources to help you improve your branding.

  • Explore New Brand Book here

New Brand Book for B Corps

A clear, compelling, and compliant way to communicate about being a B Corp

The new B Corp Brand Book provides Certified B Corps with a consistent, up to-date set of tools to communicate about certification clearly and confidently. As the B Corp community continues to grow globally, a unified approach to branding and messaging is essential to build trust, avoid confusion, and reduce risk.

This updated guidance replaces the previous Brand Guidelines for B Corps and reflects how B Corp Certification is evolving — and so is how we communicate about it. It is informed by testing with B Corps, consumers, and experts in legal and standards compliance, and aligns with today’s regulatory and consumer expectations.

Why a new Brand Book?

The B Corp movement spans industries, regions, and cultures. The new Brand Book is designed to support this diversity while strengthening collective clarity and credibility.

The new Brand Book:

  • Reflects today’s B Corp movement, community, and global reach
  • Supports long-term brand recognition and trust
  • Provides clarity for companies across different industries, regions, and communications capacities
  • Helps companies explain what B Corp Certification means — without ambiguity or overstatement

By using shared guidance and approved language, B Corps can communicate responsibly while contributing to a strong, consistent global brand.

New B Lab Standards, new ways to communicate

The transition to the B Lab Standards V2.1 introduces a new way of measuring and communicating impact. As a result, the Brand Book now includes topic-specific, approved claims linked directly to meaningful action.

What’s new:

  • New approved claims connected to the seven Impact Topics
  • Clear labeling of claims suitable for:
    • All B Corps
    • B Corps certified under V1.6
    • B Corps certified under V2.1
  • More ready-to-use messaging than ever for companies certified under V2.1
  • Clear guidance to ensure companies use language aligned with the version of standards they are certified against

Only companies certified under the new standards will be eligible to use certain updated claims and brand assets.

Communicating responsibly in a changing regulatory landscape

Expectations for sustainability communication are rising worldwide. In the EU, the Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition (ECGT) Directive requires sustainability claims and labels to be specific, substantiated, and verifiable.

The new Brand Book helps B Corps navigate this environment with confidence.

It:

  • Provides compliant, pre-approved messaging reviewed against ECGT expectations and global best practice
  • Helps prevent overstatement and reduce greenwashing risk
  • Supports transparency — a core principle of B Corp Certification
  • Ensures consumers receive clear, accurate information about what the B stands for

 

This guidance enables B Corps to communicate impact in a way that is both credible and legally robust.

Brand Guidelines for B Corps

The new Brand Book is the single source of truth for how Certified B Corps should communicate about certification today.

It provides:

  • Clear guidance on language, claims, and positioning
  • Tools to explain certification consistently and accurately
  • Support for responsible, compliant communication across markets

As the B Corp community grows, using these shared guidelines helps protect the integrity of the certification and strengthens trust in the movement as a whole.
