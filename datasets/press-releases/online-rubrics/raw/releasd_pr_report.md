<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="PR_Reporting_Templates_-_Why_Do_They_Matter">PR Reporting Templates – Why Do They Matter?</span></h2>



<p>PR reports can make or break client relationships. The best PRs in the business are master storytellers with a talent for crafting compelling narratives. Experienced professionals should also know <a href="https://www.prdaily.com/3-ways-pr-pros-can-write-compelling-narratives/">how to present themselves</a> through a meticulously crafted PR report. While PRs excel at doing PR for clients, many often struggle at ‘PRing’ themselves through compelling reporting.</p>



<p>With access to the right PR report template, communicating accomplishments to clients and executives will become far easier. An effective public relations report should help <a href="https://overit.com/blog/client-relationships">nurture the client relationship</a>. Let’s explore the core components of PR reporting.</p>



<p>To summarise, here’s why PR reporting templates matter:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Kickstart the process of ‘PRing’ yourself.</li>



<li>Shape the narrative on campaign performance.</li>



<li>Communicate accomplishments with clients and executives.</li>
</ul><h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="The_Core_Components_Of_Effective_PR_Reporting">The Core Components Of Effective PR Reporting</span></h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Start_Off_Strong">Start Off Strong!</span></h3>



<p>It’s important to hit the ground running and front-load the report with the most valuable stuff. It can be a mistake to assume that busy clients or executives will have the patience to sit down and read through the entire report. We believe in showcasing the best coverage, metrics and activity at the beginning of the report (according to our data, clients typically have 1-2 minutes of attention to read PR reports).</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Provide_Context">Provide Context</span></h3>



<p>It’s often surprising to see how few reports explain the <a href="https://marxcommunications.com/pr-measurement-important-metrics/">relevance of specific results</a>. The key is to circle back to <em>why</em> the coverage is important, and how it fits into the bigger picture. If clients or execs do not have a strong understanding of PR, it’s critical to continually provide context and reinforce objectives.</p>



<p>Add context to key points in a PR report by considering the following questions:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>How does this piece of coverage show that we are achieving our objectives?</li>



<li>How does this metric demonstrate that we are making meaningful progress?</li>



<li>How does this activity show that we are providing value for money?</li>
</ul><h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Include_Supporting_Activity">Include Supporting Activity</span></h3>



<p>We all know there’s far more to modern public relations than coverage – and it’s essential that this is communicated in the report. If there are various projects or strategies in the pipeline, mention these in the report. If there is little coverage for the month, talk about what the team is in the process of planning and how this fits into the overall strategy.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Focus_On_The_Visuals">Focus On The Visuals</span></h3>



<p>A strong visual presentation is about conveying a clear message to clients in an enticing way. If a PR agency is firmly committed to delivering results, a report with a smart design will reflect this. It’s best to avoid low-quality screenshots of online coverage and dense copy. Inconsistent formatting in the report will make it challenging for the client to navigate through the document.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="673" src="https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image.png" alt="This image has an empty alt attribute; its file name is Screenshot-2022-03-15-at-15.47.23-1-1024x673.png" class="wp-image-906" srcset="https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image.png 1024w, https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-300x197.png 300w, https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-768x505.png 768w, https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-680x447.png 680w, https://releasd.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-800x526.png 800w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" data-pagespeed-url-hash="1000111848" onload="pagespeed.CriticalImages.checkImageForCriticality(this);"></figure><p>Releasd PR reports are designed to showcase content in a visual way.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Measure_What_Matters">Measure What Matters</span></h3>



<p>While it’s tempting to include as many metrics as possible, this may overwhelm the reader and lessen the impact of the metrics that matter. When dealing with readers who are often short on time, it’s important to focus on only the most relevant KPIs. Dedicating pages and pages to inconsequential data provides little value to the reader.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Provide_A_Table_Of_Contents">Provide A Table Of Contents</span></h3>



<p>Good PR reports are easy to navigate and structured logically, and a table of contents can be a helpful feature. If the client cannot dedicate enough time to read the report from front to back, they will benefit from a table of contents in order to identify the most relevant information.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Set_The_Scene_For_The_Next_Month">Set The Scene For The Next Month</span></h3>



<p>Taking a forward-looking approach makes it easy to explain how everything is building towards hitting broader objectives. In other words, clients and execs need to know what is on the horizon and how the PR efforts are pushing them closer to key milestones. When this is detailed in the report, it can illustrate the month-to-month <a href="http://www.natapr.com/en/blog/public-relations-and-the-compound-effect-">compounding effects of the PR strategy</a>.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span id="Use_The_Right_Format">Use The Right Format</span></h3>



<p>PDF or Word documents were not built to showcase the work of modern PR professionals in an engaging way. For PRs to prove their worth to clients and executives, they must leverage digital tools to showcase their work. This is where <a href="https://releasd.com/">Releasd</a> comes in – as a next-generation PR activity reporting tool.</p>



<p>Releasd showcases modern PR activities in a clear, engaging format that busy clients and executives can quickly absorb.</p>



<p>To summarise, here are a few key takeaways:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Front-load the report with the most valuable insights.</li>



<li>Explain the relevance of specific PR metrics.</li>



<li>Avoid using dense copy and highlight key wins.</li>



<li>Focus on the bigger picture and talk about the future.</li>
</ul><p>As a PR reporting tool, Releasd enables public relations teams to prepare evergreen coverage reports that update in real-time and include all the PR metrics needed to <a href="https://releasd.com/blog/public-relations-roi/">communicate campaign performance</a>.</p>
