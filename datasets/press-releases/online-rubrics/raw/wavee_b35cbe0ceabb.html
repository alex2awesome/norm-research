Randy developed and teaches the online Data Visualization and Storytelling Certificate program at Southern Methodist University (SMU). This 12-week program is offered twice a year to a small cohort in a hybrid format that combines pre-recorded videos with weekly live sessions. The course is held online so students can attend from anywhere.

Upon completion, students earn a Certificate of Completion from SMU and create a verified Digital Badge to add to their resume and LinkedIn profile.

 

Randy is the Organizer for both the DFW (Dallas/Fort Worth), Austin, and Silicon Valley Data Visualization Meetup Groups that hold free events for the data visualization community.

In 2021, Randy also started coordinating Global, Joint, Online Data Visualization Meetup events with 20+ data visualization groups around the world. This helps provide events to groups that need speakers or couldn’t meet in person.
