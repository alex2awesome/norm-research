        <h1 class="latest-news">About Newswise</h1>
        <h2><b>Newswise is where journalists choose, connect,and use smart news.</b></h2>
        <p>A free newswire for journalists.&nbsp; A press release distribution service for public relations professionals.&nbsp; A news site for the intellectually curious. Newswise means different things to different people, and each group participates in different ways.</p>
        <br/>
        <ul style="list-style-type:disc;color:#545E6C;margin:15px">
            <li><b>Journalists</b></li>
        </ul>
        <p>Journalists can find embargoed research news, expert sources, and feature ideas to help them do their jobs. <a href="https://www.newswise.com/journalists">Learn more about Services for Journalists</a></p>
        <ul style="list-style-type:disc;color:#545E6C;margin:15px">
            <li><b>Public relations professionals</b></li>
        </ul>
        <p>Public relations professionals post and distribute their news releases, reaching thousands of users through the Newswise website and email wires. <a href="http://newswise.com/contributors">Learn more about Services for Contributors</a></p>
        <ul style="list-style-type:disc;color:#545E6C;margin:15px">
            <li><b>Public subscribers</b></li>
        </ul>
        <p>Public subscribers can share articles in social media platforms and start discussions in the comment sections. <a href="https://www.newswise.com/public-users">Learn more about Services for Public Users</a></p>
        <br/>
        <h3 class="six_heading"><b>How to Participate</b></h3>
        <p><a href="/register">Register as a journalist, a media relations professional, or a public user</a>, and select your preferred wire subscription. Newswise Wires are available daily, or semi-weekly.</p>
