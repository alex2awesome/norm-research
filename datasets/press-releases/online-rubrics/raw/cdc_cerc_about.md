	<a class="onThisPageAnchor" id="cdc_training_summary_overview-about-cerc" name="About%20CERC" title="About CERC"><!-- --></a><h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-rgtquhhy-v">About CERC</h2>
<p>CDC&rsquo;s Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) program provides trainings, tools, and resources to help health communicators, emergency responders, and leaders of organizations communicate effectively about risks during emergencies. CERC draws from lessons learned during past public health emergencies and research in the fields of public health, psychology, and emergency risk communication.</p>

</div>
<div class="dfe-section" data-section="cdc_training_summary_training">
	<a class="onThisPageAnchor" id="cdc_training_summary_training-cerc-training" name="CERC%20training" title="CERC training"><!-- --></a><h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-zalveaqu34">CERC training</h2>
<h3 id="heading-wrgfl2lzsn">Introduction to CERC</h3>
<p>Introduction to CERC is a full-day (8-hour) CERC training that helps participants understand CERC principles and recommended practices and how applying them can help reduce harm during an emergency response. Training includes in-depth practice on a variety of skills and techniques relevant to crisis and emergency risk communication. When conducted virtually, this course usually takes place over the course of two consecutive days in four-hour sessions.</p>
<h3 id="heading-tqm3o6ylyt">CERC for Leaders</h3>
<p>CERC for Leaders is a half-day (4-hour) training designed to help emergency response leaders who are not communication experts understand the role of CERC in reducing harm during emergencies.</p>
<h3 id="heading-1dvdmfwsda">CERC Workshop</h3>
<p>A CERC workshop lasts 2&ndash;3 hours and provides an overview of the core CERC and plain language principles. Workshops include limited practice on applying the concepts presented.</p>
<h3 id="heading-0uieorljxq">CERC Presentation</h3>
<p>A CDC CERC trainer can talk with your organization for 60 minutes about how CERC can enhance emergency response efforts, protect health, and improve lives.</p>

</div>
<div class="dfe-section" data-section="cdc_training_summary_target">
	<a class="onThisPageAnchor" id="cdc_training_summary_target-who-this-training-is-for" name="Who%20this%20training%20is%20for" title="Who this training is for"><!-- --></a><h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-yrodxhxvg9">Who this training is for</h2>
<h3 id="heading--tdsvev9g_">For Organizations</h3>
<p>Contact the CERC program at <a href="mailto:cercrequest@cdc.gov">cercrequest@cdc.gov</a> to learn if CERC training is right for your organization. If so, we can discuss options for how to meet your organization's needs.</p>

</div>
<div class="dfe-section" data-section="cdc_training_summary_credit">
	<a class="onThisPageAnchor" id="cdc_training_summary_credit-resources" name="Resources" title="Resources"><!-- --></a><h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-7qb9ssffvi">Resources</h2>
<h3 id="heading-xr7chsafbe">CERC manual</h3>
<p>The <a href="/cerc/php/cerc-manual/index.html">CERC manual</a> provides an evidence-based framework and recommended practices for anyone who communicates on behalf of an organization responding to a public health emergency. The manual is the basis for all other CERC materials and trainings.</p>
<h3 id="heading-wxe0yc1am-">CERC webinars</h3>
<p>The CERC webinars last about one hour each and cover material similar to what is presented in a live CERC training.</p>
