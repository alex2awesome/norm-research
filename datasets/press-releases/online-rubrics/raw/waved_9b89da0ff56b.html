Electronic Press Kit

Thank you for your interest in Habitat for Humanity of Greater Los Angeles (Habitat LA). Our experts are available to the media on a wide array of topics, including affordable housing, residential home construction and repairs, neighborhood revitalization, community development, volunteerism, philanthropy and nonprofit fundraising, disaster recovery and recycling with the Habitat LA ReStore.

Media Library

Over the last thirty years, Habitat LA has built, repaired or renovated over 1,000 homes locally, serving thousands of individuals and families in need of affordable housing. 2020 marks Habitat LA's 30th anniversary of building better futures in Los Angeles thanks to the support of volunteers, donors, and communities. Check out our media library of photo and video galleries to learn more about how Habitat LA offers a hand up, not a hand out!
