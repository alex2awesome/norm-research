About Us

The Public Relations Society, Delhi regional chapters regularly organize seminars, lectures and discussions on various aspects of public relations creating a wide network for members to enhance their knowledge and skills. The society also promotes professional training for students from various fields. Public Relations Society, Delhi collaborates with various universities and colleges to run successful training programs.
