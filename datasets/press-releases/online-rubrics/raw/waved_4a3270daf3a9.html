Get to Know Us

Connecting Communications Leaders Worldwide to Drive Innovation, Excellence, and Impact.

Our Mission

To unite PR consultancies worldwide, raise professional standards, support learning, and provide a collective voice for the global communications industry.

Our Vision

To build a globally connected public relations profession that leads with trust, ethics, collaboration, and long-term value for society

Our Services

What we offer

Profession Representation

We act as a collective voice for PR consultancies worldwide, bringing together national associations to represent shared interests and industry perspectives at a global level.

Membership & Global Network

We connect PR consultancies and associations across countries, enabling collaboration, knowledge exchange, and a sense of belonging within a trusted international community.

Training & Professional Development

We provide access to education through webinars, courses, and the Global Diploma, supporting learning and skill development at every stage of a PR career.

Events, Awards & Recognition

We organise global summits, forums, and awards that bring the industry together to share insight, recognise excellence, and discuss the future of communications.

The best thing about ICCO is its global reach. Another great value of membership is the common ethical framework and the work to combat disinformation, which both helps to strengthen trust in the industry.

Joining ICCO is simple. You can apply as a PR consultancy, national association, or affiliate partner. Membership gives you access to global networks, industry insights, advocacy initiatives, and flagship events. Start by choosing the membership category that best fits your organisation and completing the online application form.

Members gain access to global thought leadership, international best-practice standards, industry research, networking opportunities, speaking platforms, and participation in global initiatives such as ethics frameworks, diversity programmes, and NextGen development.

ICCO hosts several major global events each year, including the Global Summit, NextGen PR World Cup, industry webinars, and leadership roundtables. These events offer opportunities to connect with senior industry leaders, share insights, and stay ahead of global communications trends.

National associations can join as member bodies to represent their country within ICCO’s global network. This includes participation in global policy discussions, collaborative research, knowledge sharing, and opportunities to showcase local industry achievements on an international stage.
