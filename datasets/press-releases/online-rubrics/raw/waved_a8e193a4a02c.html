About the ICCO Global Summit

The International Communications Consultancy Organisation (ICCO) is the voice of public relations consultancies around the world. The ICCO membership comprises associations representing 82 countries across the globe: from Europe, Africa, Asia, the Middle East, the Americas, and Australasia. Collectively, these associations represent over 3,000 PR firms. Visit the main ICCO Website

ICCO GLOBAL SUMMIT 2024

The 2024 ICCO Global Summit will be taking place in Istanbul, Turkey on 9th and 10th October. Delegates can expect to hear from a variety of international speakers, as well as enjoy social functions and networking sessions with colleagues from across the globe.

For over 25 years our summit has offered PR experts and leaders with international remits to share insight and discuss the critical issues facing the global industry. Previous Summits have included: Dubai, Lisbon, Dublin, Helsinki, Milan, Poland, New Dehli, Paris, Prague, Berlin , San Francisco, Lausanne and London.
