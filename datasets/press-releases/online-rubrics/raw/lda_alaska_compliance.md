                     <h1 class="page-title pb-2">Federal Lobbying Disclosure Act (LDA) Compliance </h1>
                     <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Lobbying Disclosure Act (LDA) of 1995 (as amended in 2007) requires certain organizations
                           to register as lobbying entities with the U.S. House of Representatives and U.S. Senate
                           and report quarterly on all lobbying activities and expenses. Based on the requirements
                           outlined in the law, the University of Alaska (UA) system must comply with the registration
                           and reporting obligations in the LDA.&nbsp;</span></p>
                     
                     <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Office of Government Relations, Federal Relations team in the UA System Office
                           is tasked with compliance with the LDA. Any LDA-related questions should be directed
                           to John Latini (</span><a href="mailto:jalatini@alaska.edu"><span style="font-weight: 400;">jalatini@alaska.edu</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">), Executive Director of Federal Relations for the UA system.&nbsp;</span></p>
                     
                     <div class="box-wrapper custom-bx gradient-border normal-width">
                        <div class="row">
                           <div class="col-md">
                              
                              <h2 class="h3">Faculty &amp; Staff LDA Reporting Obligations</h2>
                              
                              <div>
                                 
                                 <p><span><i>ALL</i></span><span>&nbsp;UA faculty and staff that engage in lobbying in their official capacity as UA system
                                       employees should report lobbying activities and expenses. Faculty and staff that contact,
                                       urge, or lobby federal officials in their personal or professional capacity do not
                                       have to report these activities.&nbsp;</span></p>
                                 
                                 <p>The Federal Relations team sends out quarterly emails to request that faculty and
                                    staff who have engaged in lobbying report appropriate expenses and activities. If
                                    you are not receiving quarterly emails, please contact John Latini (<a href="mailto:jalatini@alaska.edu" target="_blank" rel="noopener">jalatini@alaska.edu</a>), Executive Director of Federal Relations for the UA system.&nbsp;</p>
                                 
                                 <p><i>UA system employees are strongly encouraged to contact the Office of Government Relations,
                                       Federal Relations team before engaging in any lobbying activities.&nbsp;</i></p>
                                 </div>
                              </div>
                        </div>
                     </div>
                     
                     <div class="box-wrapper custom-bx default normal-width">
                        <div class="row">

                              <h2 class="h3">Lobbying Activities &amp; Other Definitions</h2>
                              
                              <p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lobbying activities (including Lobbying Contacts)</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> are defined as any oral, written, or electronic communication to a covered official
                                    regarding any aspect of federal legislation (including funding), rules, regulations,
                                    Executive Order, program, policy, confirmation, or position of the United States Government.
                                    This includes any preparation or planning activities, research, and other background
                                    work intended to support lobbying.&nbsp;</span></p>
                              
                              <p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Covered officials</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> are defined in two categories.&nbsp;</span></p>
                              
                              <ul>
                                 
                                 <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Covered Legislative Branch Officials include all of the following:</span></li>
                                 
                                 <ul>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Members of Congress (House and Senate);</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Congressional staff;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Congressional Committee staff;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Joint Congressional Committee staff;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Congressional working groups or Caucuses; and&nbsp;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Officers in the House and Senate (e.g. Clerk).</span></li>
                                    </ul>
                                 </ul>
                              
                              <ul>
                                 
                                 <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Covered Executive Branch Officials include any of the following:</span></li>
                                 
                                 <ul>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">President of the United States;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Vice President;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Employees of the Executive Office of the President;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Employees serving in a position in level I, II, III, IV, or V of the Executive Schedule;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Members of the uniformed services whose pay grade is at or above O-7; and&nbsp;</span></li>
                                    
                                    <li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Employees serving in a position of a confidential, policy-determining, policy-making,
                                          or policy-advocating character.&nbsp;</span></li>
                                    </ul>
                                 </ul>
                              
                              <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/administration/executive-office-of-the-president/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Executive Office of the President</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> includes the Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP), Climate Policy Office,
                                    Office of Management and Budget, Office of National Drug Control Policy, and National
                                    Space Council among others.&nbsp;</span></p>
                              
                              <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As a general rule, any federal official subject to Senate confirmation will be a covered
                                    executive branch official. Any questions on the classification of federal officials
                                    should be directed to John Latini (</span><a href="mailto:jalatini@alaska.edu"><span style="font-weight: 400;">jalatini@alaska.edu</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">), Executive Director of Federal Relations for the UA system.&nbsp;</span></p>
                              
                              <p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Lobbying expenses</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> include travel costs, employee salaries, office overhead, and payments to vendors
                                    (including professional societies and organizations if university funds are used to
                                    pay for membership).&nbsp;</span></p>
                              </div>
                        </div>

                                       <div class="h5 font-weight-bold mb-0"><a class="collapsed" aria-controls="collapse6d13e683" aria-expanded="true" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse6d13e683"><span title="&#xA; Using my UA email address, I emailed an Alaska Member of Congress, or their staff, to urge the Member to protect funding or request funding be released for a federal program. Is this considered a lobbying activity? &#xA;">
                                                Using my UA email address, I emailed an Alaska Member of Congress, or their staff,
                                                to urge the Member to protect funding or request funding be released for a federal
                                                program. Is this considered a lobbying activity?
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
                                    </div>
                                    <div aria-labelledby="heading6d13e683" class="collapse" data-parent="#accordiond13e683" id="collapse6d13e683" role="region">
                                       <div class="card-body">
                                          
                                          <p>Yes, this communication needs to be reported as lobbying.&nbsp;</p>
                                          </div>
                                    </div>
                                 </div>
                                 <div class="card">
                                    <div class="card-header" id="heading7d13e683">
                                       <div class="h5 font-weight-bold mb-0"><a class="collapsed" aria-controls="collapse7d13e683" aria-expanded="true" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse7d13e683"><span title="&#xA; Using my personal email address, I emailed an Alaska Member of Congress, or their staff, to urge the Member to project&nbsp;funding or request funding be released for a federal program. Is this considered a lobbying activity? &#xA;">
                                                Using my personal email address, I emailed an Alaska Member of Congress, or their
                                                staff, to urge the Member to project&nbsp;funding or request funding be released for a
                                                federal program. Is this considered a lobbying activity?
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
                                    </div>
                                    <div aria-labelledby="heading7d13e683" class="collapse" data-parent="#accordiond13e683" id="collapse7d13e683" role="region">
                                       <div class="card-body">
                                          
                                          <p>No, since you reached out using personal resources you do not need to report this
                                             activity. You have a First Amendment right to contact your federal elected officials
                                             on your own time as often as you wish.&nbsp;</p>
                                          </div>
                                    </div>
                                 </div>
                                 <div class="card">
                                    <div class="card-header" id="heading8d13e683">
                                       <div class="h5 font-weight-bold mb-0"><a class="collapsed" aria-controls="collapse8d13e683" aria-expanded="true" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse8d13e683"><span title="&#xA; I attended a conference in Alaska, or Washington, DC. While I was at the conference I talked with an Alaska Member of Congress and/or their staff about the importance of my work including the need for federal investment. Do I need to report this engagement? &#xA;">
                                                I attended a conference in Alaska, or Washington, DC. While I was at the conference
                                                I talked with an Alaska Member of Congress and/or their staff about the importance
                                                of my work including the need for federal investment. Do I need to report this engagement?
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
                                    </div>
                                    <div aria-labelledby="heading8d13e683" class="collapse" data-parent="#accordiond13e683" id="collapse8d13e683" role="region">
                                       <div class="card-body">
                                          
                                          <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Most likely yes. You may not need to report the travel expenses related to attending
                                                the conference but you will need to report the topics discussed, provide your hourly
                                                rate, how long the engagement lasted, who you lobbied, and if other UA personnel participated.</span></p>
                                          </div>
                                    </div>
                                 </div>
                                 <div class="card">
                                    <div class="card-header" id="heading9d13e683">
                                       <div class="h5 font-weight-bold mb-0"><a class="collapsed" aria-controls="collapse9d13e683" aria-expanded="true" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse9d13e683"><span title="&#xA; I had students join me in a meeting with a covered official on behalf of our program, do I need to report their time as well? &#xA;">
                                                I had students join me in a meeting with a covered official on behalf of our program,
                                                do I need to report their time as well?
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
                                    </div>
                                    <div aria-labelledby="heading9d13e683" class="collapse" data-parent="#accordiond13e683" id="collapse9d13e683" role="region">
                                       <div class="card-body">
                                          
                                          <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">No, in general LDA obligations do not apply to students.&nbsp;</span></p>
                                          
                                          <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">&nbsp;</span></p>
                                          </div>
                                    </div>
                                 </div>
                                 <div class="card">
                                    <div class="card-header" id="heading10d13e683">
                                       <div class="h5 font-weight-bold mb-0"><a class="collapsed" aria-controls="collapse10d13e683" aria-expanded="true" data-toggle="collapse" href="#collapse10d13e683"><span title="&#xA; I participated in or led a campus tour that included covered officials. During the tour, I educated the officials about my activities and described challenges including limited federal funding but I did not request any action be taken in response to those challenges. Is this considered a lobbying activity?&nbsp; &#xA;">
                                                I participated in or led a campus tour that included covered officials. During the
                                                tour, I educated the officials about my activities and described challenges including
                                                limited federal funding but I did not request any action be taken in response to those
                                                challenges. Is this considered a lobbying activity?&nbsp;
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
                                    </div>
                                    <div aria-labelledby="heading10d13e683" class="collapse" data-parent="#accordiond13e683" id="collapse10d13e683" role="region">
                                       <div class="card-body">
                                          
                                          <p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><span>No, educating and talking about challenges are not considered lobbying. If you asked
                                                   the covered official to take any actions such as supporting increased federal funding
                                                   for your activities then that would be considered lobbying and reportable.</span></span></p>
                                          </div>
                                    </div>
                                 </div>

Who should I contact to have my questions answered?&nbsp; &#xA;">
                                                I am not sure how to handle a potential federal issue or if I need to engage in lobbying.
                                                Who should I contact to have my questions answered?&nbsp;
                                                </span><span class="fas fa-angle-down rotate-icon" style="float: right;"></span></a></div>
