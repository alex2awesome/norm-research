<div class="row" id="lede">
<div class="lede-float-fix"> 

<p>The core working hours for Multichannel News Releases are 08:00 to 17:30  GMT, Monday to Friday. Your Multichannel News Release order can commence as soon as three of the  components and assets listed below have been submitted to the Multichannel News Release team.</p>
      
<p>Upon receipt of a client's order we will be in  touch with turn-around times. When sending us your Multichannel News Release assets, please use one of the following file sharing services, WeTransfer, Dropbox etc. </p>

<h2>What is a Multichannel  News Release?</h2>

     
<p>A Multichannel&nbsp;News Release (MNR) is a dynamic HTML5  page with the ability to encompass text, images, video, audio, documents, live  Twitter &amp; Facebook feeds and much more. PR Newswire's Multichannel News Release makes the  distribution and sharing of multimedia content extremely simple and  uncomplicated. Below is further information to help you get started with your  Multichannel News Release. </p>

</p>

<p>  That your video is in English, PR Newswire can  offer distribution in multiple languages (English subtitles recommended);  however, most of our portals are global. English language videos will maximise pick-up. </p>

<h2>Photos</h2>


    
<p>Images can make your story come alive. Images can  be located in various locations on the MNR page based on your chosen MNR  design, all Images should have a <strong>title, caption and file name </strong>to allow for optimum  engagement.</p>

<p>Titles: 100 Characters including spaces<br>
Captions - 250 Characters including spaces </p>

<p>Acceptable File Formats: </p>

<p>  We recommend your photos to be 2700 pixels on the  longest side and 300 dpi. We also recommend that you supply photo captions with  each image which should be a maximum of 250 characters long with spaces. </p>

<p>  These are the following formats that we will  accept: JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF. </p>

<h2>The Press Release</h2>


    
<p>This text portion of your Multichannel News Release will be the same that  goes out to all of your desired distribution points. After the first paragraph  of your release there will be a link to your Multichannel News Release page, which users can click  through to the Multichannel News Release.</p>

<p>The  idea, the sale, the product or your message should be accurately and concisely  conveyed. Our editorial service will always be on hand to provide proofreading  and editorial suggestions for your release. </p>



<h2>Translations &amp; Languages</h2>

    
<p>PR Newswire's distribution service is a truly  global platform. We distribute to 70+ countries in localised languages where  possible. Translations can take up to 48 hours to complete. Alternatively,  clients are encouraged to supply their own translations for their Multichannel News Release.</p>



<h2>Language Toggle</h2>

    
<p>Language toggles can be added to any Multichannel News Release to easily  switch and view your text in different languages. Each toggle can be displayed  as a link, which replaces one language with another. We currently offer the  language toggle in 23 different languages, listed below. </p>

<p>Chinese  (Simplified), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English (UK), English (US), Finnish, French  (Canadian), French (European), German, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean,  Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Slovakian, Spanish (European), Spanish  (LatAm), Turkish and Swedish.<br>
<br> Additional charges apply.</p>

</p>

<p>The reporting for your campaign is available 24/7  within our online reporting platform - Visibility Reports. You can simply log  on anytime that&rsquo;s convenient for you and analyse the success of your campaign  in an easy online intuitive format. </p>

<p>
<strong>With Visibility Reports you can expect:</strong>
</p>

<p>  Robust Data&nbsp;&ndash; Evaluate the full distribution  of your Multichannel News Release along with the specific page interactions, showcasing comprehensive  reach and engagement.</p>

<p>  Visuals&nbsp;&ndash; Enhanced, dynamic graphics  effectively tell your Multichannel News Releases story plus additional data is available via &ldquo;mouse  over&rdquo; charts and graphs.</p>

<p>  Export and Sharing Features&nbsp;&ndash; Download graphs  in a variety of formats, to incorporate data into your own custom reports.</p>
