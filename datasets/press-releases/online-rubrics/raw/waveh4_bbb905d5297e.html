James Madison Jr.
(1751–1836)
→
  • related portals: Presidents of the United States
  • sister projects: Wikipedia article, Commons gallery, Commons category, quotes, Wikidata item

Fourth president of the United States (1809–1817); fifth United States secretary of state (1801–1809); Democratic-Republican U.S. representative from Virginia (1789–1797); the “father of the Constitution”
