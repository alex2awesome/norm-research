The most prestigious PR & communications awards

The CommsCon Awards return for 2026 to recognise excellence, innovation and impact across Australia and New Zealand’s PR and communications industry. Communications professionals now sit at the heart of business leadership, tasked with navigating complexity, building trust, and shaping the narratives that define reputation and public confidence.

These prestigious awards now include new categories recognising this transformation. Best Use of Strategic Communications has been launched to recognise teams driving reputation, stakeholder influence and long-term corporate trust. Non-Traditional Campaign of the Year has been redefined to highlight communications thinking applied to behaviour change, customer experience and innovation.

Plus, this year the Emerging Talent award will be free to enter, reflecting Mumbrella’s commitment to supporting the next generation shaping the future of the profession.

Meet Your MC

Fashion Critical is an anonymous Australian fashion commentator, digital creator and the author of Fashion Critical: Red Carpet Lewks and LOLs from an Undercover Fashion Critic (2025).

Known for her razor-sharp critiques of celebrity red-carpet fashion, she has built a fiercely devoted following including designers, stylists, celebrities themselves and of course, everyday Aussies, all of whom who pour over her verdicts long after the trophies have been handed out.

Her signature catchphrases and sharp wit have turned her unapologetic post-event “albums” into essential scrolling after every major awards night, reaching tens of millions around the world.

Social media aside, FC is regularly called upon by the Australian media to dissect high-profile fashion moments - from the Oscars to the Olympic Opening Ceremony, bringing her trademark wit to every interview.

Live Entertainment

DJ Claire Elliott is a globally sought-after and trusted DJ with over 15 years of experience playing high-end clubs and major events. 

Known for her energy and seamless genre-blending, she’s a top choice for elite brands and large-scale productions. Claire specializes in open-format sets and has performed for stadium events, TV, radio, and collaborations with world-class entertainers. Her standout performances include opening for Katy Perry, Justin Bieber, the AFL Grand Final, and televised events like the AFL Footy Show Final and T20 cricket and a residency for Virgin in Ibiza.
