Welcome to

Public Relations Society of India, Kolkata Chapter

The Public Relations Society of India (PRSI) established in 1958, is dedicated to advancing public relations as a vital profession and strategic management tool. As part of India’s premier PR network, the chapter fosters proactive, need-based communication strategies for professionals across public and private sectors. PRSI, Kolkata Chapter offers a collaborative platform for knowledge exchange and professional growth, enhancing the role of PR in diverse industries.

Core Values

  • Real

    We commit to authenticity and transparency, ensuring that our communications and interactions are genuine and grounded in truth.

  • Relevant

    We stay attuned to the evolving needs of the industry, providing timel and pertinent insights that drive effective and impactful communication.

  • Resourceful

    We leverage creativity and strategic thinking to overcome challenges and deliver innovative solutions, maximizing value for our members.

PRSI, Kolkata Chapter

Pioneering Professional Growth and Industry Collaboration

Leading PR Network

The PRSI, Kolkata chapter is the leading national network for PR professionals, bringing together experts from both public and private sectors.

Collaboration & Knowledge Sharing

PRSI, Kolkata Chapter fosters a collaborative environment where members share innovative strategies and best practices across industries.

Professional Growth & Dialogue

The chapter promotes professional development and facilitates cross-sector dialogue to enhance PR’s impact.

Advancing PR Nationwide

PRSI, Kolkata Chapter drives the growth of the PR profession across India, shaping both organizational success and societal outcomes.

Our value

Empowering PR Professionals for a Dynamic Future

Members are drawn from all sectors of the industry, from corporate to government to community. Individuals are required to meet specific criteria for full professional membership.

  • Our Mission:

    To advance public relations as a strategic profession by fostering proactive communication, ethical practices, and professional development across industries.

  • Our Vision:

    To be the leading network empowering PR professionals to drive impactful, innovative communication strategies nationwide.
