</ul><hr /><h2 id="a">Writing</h2>

<h3>EPA On-line Resources</h3>

<ul><li>AP Stylebook, via the EPA Intranet: an <a href="/stylebook/ap-stylebook">on-line version of the Stylebook</a> (copyright 2003) is available to EPA employees with access to the EPA Intranet.</li>
	<li><a href="/product-review">EPA's Product Review Site</a>: explains product development and review for all media, including print, Web, audio-visual, and displays.</li>
</ul><h3>External to EPA On-line Resources</h3>

<p><span>Some of the following links exit the site  </span><a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/home/exit-epa" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Exit</a></p>

<ul><li><a href="http://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/GPO-STYLEMANUAL-2008/content-detail.html">U.S. Government Printing Office Style Manual</a>: the GPO's printer stylebook contains rules for preparing products to be printed by the GPO:</li>
	<li><a href="http://www.apstylebook.com/epa/?do=ask_editor&amp;pg=faq">AP Stylebook's Frequently Asked Questions</a>: the Associated Press Stylebook's on-line resource for questions and answers.</li>
	<li><a href="http://www.merriam-webster.com/">Merriam-Webster's On-line Resources</a>: includes a dictionary, thesaurus, Spanish-English dictionary, word of the day and more.</li>
	<li><a href="http://www.plainlanguage.gov/">Plain Language</a>: This U.S. government site provides guidance/instruction and training materials for writing in plain language across the federal government.</li>
</ul><h3>Print Resources</h3>

<ul><li>The Associated Press Stylebook, 2007, The Associated Press publisher</li>
	<li>The Elements of Style, by William Strunk, Jr. and E. B. White, Fourth Edition, 2000, Allyn &amp; Bacon</li>
	<li>Merriam-Webster's Collegiate Dictionary, 10th Edition, 1998, Merriam-Webster publisher</li>
</ul><p class="pagetop"><a href="#main-content">Top of page</a></p>

<hr /><h2 id="b">Production/Pre-Production References</h2>

<h3>United States Code and Federal Government Regulations</h3>

<ul><li>Title 44 of the United States Code, Public Printing and Documents, contains the broad legal framework for all Government publishing.</li>
	<li>Joint Committee on Printing, Government Printing and Binding Regulations, contains the authority for Government printing and binding.</li>
	<li>OMB Circular A-25 describes free distribution of Government documents.</li>
</ul><h3>EPA Orders and Transmittals</h3>

<ul><li><a href="https://intranet.epa.gov/ohr/rmpolicy/ads/orders/1000_25.pdf">Order 1000.25, Use of Recycled Paper (PDF)</a> <span class="fileinfo">(1 pg, 55 K) </span> <a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/web-policies-and-procedures/intranet-disclaimer" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Intranet</a>, states: "...contractors use recycled paper for all reports required for delivery to the Agency."</li>
	<li><a href="https://intranet.epa.gov/ohr/rmpolicy/ads/orders/1015_1b.pdf">Order 1015.1B, EPA Identification Signs at Project Sites (PDF)</a> <span class="fileinfo">(2 pp, 97 K)</span> <a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/web-policies-and-procedures/intranet-disclaimer" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Intranet</a>, applies to "sites of projects which are operated or sponsored by EPA and applies to publicly visible construction site and demonstration project". It prohibits the appearance of "names of Program Offices".</li>
	<li><a href="https://intranet.epa.gov/ohr/rmpolicy/ads/orders/1015_2a.pdf">Order 1015.2A, EPA Seal and Agency Identifier (PDF)</a> <span class="fileinfo">(4 pp, 144 K)</span> <a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/web-policies-and-procedures/intranet-disclaimer" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Intranet</a>, governs "visual communications" and emphasizes: "...all organizational units use...this order and those in the EPA Graphic Standards System Handbook..." It names the Director of the Office of Public Awareness (now, Office of Public Affairs) as responsible for:implementation and continuous management of the EPA Graphic Standards System including supplements and revisions to the standards handbook and the granting or denying of exceptions. This Order states in part:</li>
</ul><p class="indented"><span>3a.Use of the Official Agency Seal will continue on all stationery and on flags, buildings, and project identifications signs. Use of the Seal is optional on calling cards and official Agency reports, such as reports to Congress, foreign governments, and other Federal agencies.</span></p>

<p class="indented">3b.The Agency will use the Agency Identifier on all brochures and other printed matter.</p>

<p class="indented">3c.This Agency will not use any visual identification forms other than those in this Order.</p>

<p>The Order also prohibits the use of EPA symbols for commercial purposes or for promotion or sale of private goods/services.</p>

<ul><li><a href="https://intranet.epa.gov/ohr/rmpolicy/ads/orders/1440_7.pdf">Order 1440.7, Hazard Communications (PDF)</a> <span class="fileinfo">(7 pp, 378 K)</span> <a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/web-policies-and-procedures/intranet-disclaimer" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Intranet</a>, establishes minimum requirementsfor the hazard communication program.00.4A, EPA Publication Review Procedures, establishes policy and procedural requirements for the review of materials published or issued by the EPA and that the aforementioned have been developed using methodology which will achieve high quality results. This order seeks to clarify EPA responsibilities for information published or issued and provides for the expeditious approval of publications. With regard to decentralization, it empowers senior staff at Office and Regional level with responsibility for 'substance, form and policy implications of materials and provides these officials with wide latitude for them to establish internal review procedures and controls for quality and provides for use of a formal concurrence form. With regard to centralization, it requires forwarding of any material that has policy implications to the Office of External Affairs, a.k.a. Office of Public Affairs.</li>
	<li><a href="https://intranet.epa.gov/ohr/rmpolicy/ads/orders/2510-1.pdf">Order 2510.1, Policy for the Procurement of Employee Business Cards (PDF)</a> <span class="fileinfo">(5 pp, 341 K)</span> <a class="exit-disclaimer" href="/web-policies-and-procedures/intranet-disclaimer" title="EPA's External Link Disclaimer">Intranet</a>, provides guidance on using EPA appropriated funds to procure employee business cards.</li>
