<ul class="key-takeaways">
<li>Press releases are <strong>structured</strong>, <strong>news</strong>&#8211;<strong>focused</strong> <strong>statements</strong> designed to inform media and secure coverage.</li>
<li>Types include <strong>product</strong> <strong>launches</strong>, <strong>company</strong> <strong>news</strong>, <strong>events</strong>, and <strong>data reports, </strong>each with a specific formatting and audience.</li>
<li>Effective press releases require <strong>strong headlines</strong>, <strong>clear</strong> <strong>CTAs</strong>, <strong>quotes</strong>, and <strong>media assets</strong> for maximum impact.</li>
<li>Tools like BuzzStream streamline <strong>email outreach</strong> for <strong>press release distribution</strong> and follow-up tracking.</li>
<li>Tailor releases to journalists’ needs by prioritizing <strong>concise</strong>, <strong>relevant</strong>, and <strong>well-proofed content</strong> over promotional fluff.</li>
</ul>
<p>Press releases are one of the most impactful tools in a PR toolbox, but how you approach them can mean the difference between great coverage and a waste of time and money.</p>
<p>Just because most journalists agree that press releases are a top priority when looking for trusted sources doesn&#8217;t mean all press releases hit the mark.</p>
<p>There are many different wire services where companies publish thousands of press releases daily, so it is critical to put the time and effort into crafting the best press release to make yours stand out.</p>
<p>If you can nail the press release, you&#8217;ll also have a leg up on crafting <a href="https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/media-pitch/">media pitches</a> because they use many of the same principles.</p>
<p>Let&#8217;s break down exactly what a press release is, the different types of press releases, some industry best practices, and, most importantly, the anatomy of an effective press release.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="What_is_a_Press_Release"></span>What is a Press Release?<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>A press release is an official statement or proclamation announcing news that provides relevant information to the media. The key here is that it is an informative piece of content.</p>
<p>Press releases can take many forms depending on the news you are announcing. Before writing a press release, whether it&#8217;s your first or 100th time, it is essential to set clear expectations on what you want to achieve so that you can better understand what you are writing.</p>
<p>Press releases are announcements. They are not editorial (like this) or inspirational pieces.</p>
<p>Can press releases lead to editorial and inspirational content?</p>
<p>Absolutely!</p>
<p>But that is the job of the media you are trying to reach, and what the media needs is an informational source – a press release.</p>
<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Types_of_Press_Releases"></span>Types of Press Releases<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>Companies issue press releases for various reasons, so it is crucial to understand the different types of press releases; let&#8217;s break down some of the more common types of releases: product or brand launches, company news, event announcements, and reports.</p>
<h3>Product or Brand Launches</h3>
<div class="screen"><img decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6925" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/product-launch.png" alt="product launch" width="1005" height="830" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/product-launch.png 1005w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/product-launch-300x248.png 300w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/product-launch-768x634.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 1005px) 100vw, 1005px" /></div>
<p>Product or brand launch press releases can do wonders when trying to reach a new audience of potential customers about a new product or feature. However, not all product or brand releases warrant a press release.</p>
<p>If you are launching a new brand or product, tailor your press release to resonate with journalists at industry trade publications, so always tie it back to what they find valuable and relevant.</p>
<p>If a new enhancement gets launched and is only relevant to customers already using the product, then an email campaign to your current customer base is the way to go.</p>
<p>Here are a few different ways brands have launched product news:</p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/media-advisory-chrysler-will-unveil-innovative-electric-concept-car-three-teaser-images-to-preview-brands-drive-to-all-electric-future-302054719.html">Media Advisory: Chrysler Will Unveil Innovative Electric Concept Car, Three Teaser Images to Preview Brand&#8217;s Drive to All-electric Future</a></li>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240201200237/en/Found-Advances-Weight-Loss-Standard-of-Care-With-MetabolicPrint-Launch">Found Advances Weight Loss Standard of Care With MetabolicPrint Launch | Business Wire</a></li>
</ul>
<h3>Company News Announcements</h3>
<div class="screen"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6914" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/company-news.png" alt="company news press release example" width="998" height="811" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/company-news.png 998w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/company-news-300x244.png 300w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/company-news-768x624.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 998px) 100vw, 998px" /></div>
<p>A company news announcement encompasses any significant news the media might be interested in, such as new hires, mergers and partnerships, award recognition, company milestones, growth, and more.</p>
<p>But how do you know if your news is …newsworthy?</p>
<p>Jodie Booras from <a href="https://kaionacomms.com/">Kaiona Communications</a> recommends asking: “Does this news impact those outside the organization? For example, if the company is restructuring its internal management structure in a way that only affects employees, it&#8217;s likely not newsworthy. If they bring in a new VP of Sales to focus on customer relationships, that is newsworthy.”</p>
<p>Company news can be as broad as what you see on <a href="https://www.crainsnewyork.com/people-on-the-move">Crain&#8217;s People on the Move</a> when announcing a new CEO or something more specific to your industry, like Healthcare Dive&#8217;s <a href="https://www.healthcaredive.com/topic/hospital-administration/">Hospital Administration News</a>.</p>
<p>In an ideal world, these announcements will lead to interviews or, at the minimum, coverage from various target publications.</p>
<p>Here are a few examples of company news announcements from various newswires:</p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231128512672/en/Orthofix-Names-Massimo-Calafiore-as-President-and-Chief-Executive-Officer">Orthofix Names Massimo Calafiore as President and Chief Executive Officer | Business Wire</a></li>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/02/06/2824765/0/en/Travis-Barker-Launches-Barker-Canna-Co-with-Hardware-Partner-Jupiter-Research.html">Travis Barker Launches Barker Canna Co. with Hardware Partner Jupiter Research</a></li>
</ul>
<h3>Event Announcement</h3>
<div class="screen"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6915" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/event.png" alt="event press release example" width="821" height="630" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/event.png 821w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/event-300x230.png 300w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/event-768x589.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 821px) 100vw, 821px" /></div>
<p>Events have taken on many different forms since COVID-19, but whether virtual, in-person, or hybrid, events are just as essential to promote via a press release.</p>
<p>The primary goal of issuing an event press release is increasing attendance.</p>
<p>To reach the right audience, focus on resonating with the publications your audience reads. Scan through other event press releases they have covered to ensure your press release includes all of the common elements or themes you see.</p>
<p>Here are a few examples of event announcement press releases:</p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231115724366/en/Enterprise-Connect-2024-Expands-Conference-Program-Adds-RingCentral-CEO-Keynote">Enterprise Connect 2024 Expands Conference Program, Adds RingCentral CEO Keynote | Business Wire</a></li>
<li aria-level="1"><a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/02/06/2824764/0/en/Oncternal-Therapeutics-to-Participate-in-Oppenheimer-34th-Annual-Healthcare-Life-Sciences-Conference.html">Oncternal Therapeutics to Participate in Oppenheimer 34th Annual Healthcare Life Sciences Conference</a></li>
</ul>
<h3>Data-Driven Reports</h3>
<div class="screen"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-6916" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/data-driven.png" alt="data-driven report press release example" width="839" height="666" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/data-driven.png 839w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/data-driven-300x238.png 300w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/data-driven-768x610.png 768w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 839px) 100vw, 839px" /></div>
<p>Finally, one of the most intensive yet effective types of press releases is announcing reports with insightful data.</p>
<p>It is no secret that journalists love data, so if you can provide new and proprietary data, along with an analysis, you will quickly become a trusted source to them. A common strategy for <a href="https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/digital-pr/">digital PR</a>, data-driven press releases are a great way to establish your brand as an industry thought leader.</p>
<p>Unlike announcing a product enhancement that may no longer be relevant after 6-months or a year, releasing reports full of valuable data can get cited for many years.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Anatomy_of_a_Press_Release"></span>Anatomy of a Press Release<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<p>There are a few key elements that all press releases should include, regardless of what type of announcement it is.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone wp-image-9700 size-full" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1.png" alt="the anatomy of a press release" width="1500" height="1700" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1.png 1500w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1-265x300.png 265w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1-904x1024.png 904w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1-768x870.png 768w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Anatomy-of-a-Press-Release-1-1355x1536.png 1355w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1500px) 100vw, 1500px" /></p>
<p>Breaking down each section:</p>
<h3>Headline</h3>
<p>The headline is one of the most essential parts of your press release &#8211; it is your opportunity to catch your audience&#8217;s attention and make them want to read more, so it needs to be concise, engaging, and informative. Focus on why this press release is essential to the audience, not why your company is excellent.</p>
<h3>Dateline</h3>
<p>The dateline is one of the most critical elements of the press release, and there isn&#8217;t much to it other than the date you publish it.</p>
<p>Don&#8217;t overcomplicate it.</p>
<h3>Press Release Lead-in</h3>
<p>The lead-in is the beginning of the first sentence of your press release, and for branding consistency, this should always be the same on all press releases. Most lead-ins get written ahead of time as part of a brand&#8217;s approved press kit.</p>
<h3>Key Facts</h3>
<p>Adding key facts lets journalists quickly scan and extract the most crucial information.</p>
<p>How do you know what warrants a key takeaway?</p>
<p>If your report found something that challenges the status quo in your industry, that is a newsworthy takeaway. If some data points reiterate a well-known fact that everyone is already talking about, then leave it out.</p>
<p>Try to shoot for including 8 &#8211; 13 key findings.</p>
<h3>Quote</h3>
<p>Always include at least one quote in all press releases, ideally two to four sentences per quote.</p>
<p>The quote can come from an executive team or someone in charge of the project or event.</p>
<p>If it makes sense for the story, you can include quotes from multiple people. One example would be having a quote from a new partner, hire, or customer and an executive.</p>
<h3>Call-to-action (CTA)</h3>
<p>All press releases should have a call to action. CTAs can be event registration links, landing pages, or as simple as a link company&#8217;s home page.</p>
<h3>Boilerplate</h3>
<p>Boilerplate outlines what exactly your company does for those who have yet to become familiar with the brand. You should be able to find this information in a press kit already.</p>
<h3>Contact Information</h3>
<p>All press releases should include contact information for the press. We recommend using someone on the PR team, whether internal or with an external agency.</p>
<p>Let PR be the gatekeeper of all inquiries instead of letting executives get bogged down by emails.</p>
<h3>Additional Media</h3>
<p>If you want to give your press release the best chance at reaching the most people, think about some unique media assets that can make it stand out and make the lives of your target journalists easier.</p>
<p>Examples of assets include data, infographics, and videos, among others.</p>
<div class="screen"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-6926" src="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/additional-media-1024x593.png" alt="additional assets" width="1024" height="593" srcset="https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/additional-media-1024x593.png 1024w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/additional-media-300x174.png 300w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/additional-media-768x445.png 768w, https://www.buzzstream.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/additional-media.png 1388w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></div>
<p>Although assets aren’t always available, they can make a significant impact. We recommend including them if you can.</p>
<h3>Additional Sections for Launches, News, Events, and Reports</h3>
<p>In addition to the essential elements mentioned above, here are some other specific sections to include based on the type of press release.</p>
<h4>Product Specs for Product Launches</h4>
<p>For product launches, include key differentiators of the product that make it stand out from competitors or prior versions.</p>
<div class="tip">Tip: Look at the product specs or any marketing and sales material.</div>
<h4>Company News Highlights</h4>
<p>When announcing company news, try to incorporate at least one specific key highlight that makes it significant to this news.</p>
<p>For example, if you are announcing a new executive, detail that executive&#8217;s background and what they will bring to the company. When announcing an award the company won, what was the main contributor to that company winning the award?</p>
<h4>Event Specifics</h4>
<p>Event announcements must include vital information, like the 5 Ws (who, what, when, where, and why). Where will the event be held? Who should attend? Where can they register? You get the idea.</p>
<h4>Key Takeaways for Reports</h4>
<p>When announcing the results of a new report or survey, you want to point out the most insightful key takeaways, along with a brief analysis of the findings. Give just enough to intrigue them but not enough where they do not need to take any action.</p>
<p>Your CTA for these releases will likely get them to download or sign up for the full report.</p>
<p>Additionally, provide the survey demographics so they can quickly see the audience used to give this data and ensure it is the right fit for them.</p>

<h2><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Frequently_Asked_Questions_About_Press_Releases"></span>Frequently Asked Questions About Press Releases<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>
<h3>What Are the 7 Parts of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The seven parts of a press release include the headline, dateline, introduction (lead or lead-in), body, boilerplate, call to action (CTA), and contact information. The body contains the key takeaways and the quote in our list above.</p>
<h3>What is the Structure of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The structure of a press release typically follows a straightforward format: it starts with a headline that grabs attention, followed by a dateline, an introduction that summarizes the news, the body with detailed information, a quote, a boilerplate with background on the issuing company, a call to action, and ends with contact information for further inquiries.</p>
<h3>What Should Be in the Body of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The body of a press release should contain detailed information supporting the announcement, including key facts, statistics, and quotes from company executives.</p>
<h3>What Are the 3 Core Components of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The three core components of a press release are the headline, which captures the essence of the news; the lead paragraph, which summarizes the most critical aspects of the announcement; and the body, where you include detailed information and quotes to support the lead.</p>
<h3>What are the 5 Ws in a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The 5 W&#8217;s in a press release are Who, What, When, Where, and Why. These elements ensure the press release communicates who is involved, what is happening, when it’s occurring, where it&#8217;s taking place, and why it&#8217;s important.</p>
<h3>What is a Boilerplate in a Press Release?</h3>
<p>A boilerplate in a press release is a standard paragraph at the end that provides background information about the company or organization. It typically details the company&#8217;s mission, history, or achievements.</p>
<h3>What Not to Include in a Press Release?</h3>
<p>In a press release, avoid irrelevant information, jargon, unverified claims, and personal opinions that do not directly support the news announcement.</p>
<p>The focus should be on newsworthy content that interests the media and your audience.</p>
<h3>What Does 30 Mean at the End of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>The number &#8220;30&#8221; at the end of a press release is a traditional notation used to indicate the end of the document. It is a holdover from press telegraphy days in the US.</p>
<h3>What Does ### Mean at the End of a Press Release?</h3>
<p>Similar to -30-, using ### signifies the end of a press release. Some may also use -END-.</p>
