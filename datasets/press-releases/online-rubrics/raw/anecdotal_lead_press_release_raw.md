<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-33848" src="https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads.jpg" alt="" width="1920" height="1080" srcset="https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads.jpg 1920w, https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads-300x169.jpg 300w, https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.wyliecomm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Tell-a-story-in-press-release-leads-1536x864.jpg 1536w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1920px) 100vw, 1920px" /></p>
<p>Anecdotes make your PR pieces easier to believe, understand and remember. So use an anecdotal lead whenever possible to illustrate your key idea.</p>
<h3>As they rounded a bend on the country road …</h3>
<p>Take a tip from this Department 56 <a title="Silver Anvil Award" href="https://www.prsa.org/conferences-and-awards/awards" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Silver Anvil Award</a>-winning release lead, and tap the <a title="power of storytelling" href="https://www.wyliecomm.com/writing-tips/how-to-write-creative-content/corporate-storytelling/" target="" rel="noopener noreferrer">power of storytelling</a>:</p>
<div class="excerptbox" style="border: 1px solid black;">Twenty-five years ago, on a holiday outing, a group of friends set off for dinner at a small country inn nestled in a quiet river town. As they rounded a bend in the road, they saw a small, quaint village, decorated for the holidays with lights that glowed on the fresh-fallen snow. This was the original inspiration for The Original Snow Village Collection — a nostalgic collection of lighted ceramic buildings that evoked memories of yesteryear.</div>
<h3>Buddy was hit by a car …</h3>
<p>And this Deramaxx <a title="Silver Anvil Award" href="https://www.prsa.org/conferences-and-awards/awards" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Silver Anvil Award</a>-winning release lead is one of my favorites. Note the story in the quote:</p>
<div class="excerptbox" style="border: 1px solid black;">
<p>Even as the 200-foot giant mural was erected, Dallas resident Niki Eichman worried she might not be able to find her dog among the thousands of photographs.</p>
<p>&#8220;Buddy was hit by a car when he was very young,” Eichman says of the 4-year-old Lab mix that shattered his pelvis and dislocated his elbow in the accident. &#8220;To this day, the only reason we can wrestle and play is because his veterinarian recognized his resulting arthritis and did something about it.&#8221;</p>
</div>
