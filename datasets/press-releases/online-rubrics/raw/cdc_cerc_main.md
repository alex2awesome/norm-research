<div class="cdc-page-title cdc-page-offset syndicate">
	<h1>Crisis & Emergency Risk Communication (CERC) Manual</h1>	</div>
	<div id="content" class="cdc-page-content cdc-page-offset syndicate container">
		<div class="cdc-dfe-body ">
	<div class="cdc-dfe-body__top ">
		<div class="dfe-section dfe-section--page-summary summary-blurb summary-paragraph" data-section="cdc_generic_summary">
			<div class="dfe-section__header">
		<h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-6pnsnttoqu">What to know</h2>
		</div>
		<div class="dfe-section__content">
		<p>The CERC manual provides an evidence-based framework and recommended practices for anyone who communicates on behalf of an organization responding to major emergencies such as natural disasters.</p>
		<div class="dfe-section__nav hide" id="dfe-section__nav">
			<div class="dfe-section__nav__header" id="dfe-section__nav__header">
							</div>
			<div class="dfe-section__nav-links" id="dfe-section__nav-links">
							</div>
		</div>
			</div>
			<div class="dfe-section__image" id="page-summary-image">
			<figure class="dfe-image dfe-image__large"><div class="image-container calculated-ratio-16-9 aspect-ratio-16-9"><img src="/cerc/media/images/2024/11/CERC-logo-DFE_1.jpg" alt="Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC)" title="Crisis &amp; Emergency Risk Communication (CERC)"></div></figure>
		</div>
	
	</div>
	</div>
		<div class="cdc-dfe-body__center ">
		<div class="dfe-section" data-section="cdc_generic_section_1">
	<a class="onThisPageAnchor" id="cdc_generic_section_1-cerc-manual-chapters" name="CERC%20Manual%20Chapters" title="CERC Manual Chapters"><!-- --></a><h2 class="editor-block--title" id="heading-syjtmdsvfc">CERC Manual Chapters</h2>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Introduction.pdf" class="ce-selection" target="_blank">Introduction to Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication (CERC)<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes the core Crisis and Emergency Risk Communication principles and how they apply to each phase of a crisis. <em>Last updated 2018.</em> </p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Psychology_of_a_Crisis.pdf" class="" target="_blank">The Psychology of a Crisis<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes how people process information differently during a crisis, psychological barriers to receiving information, and how to incorporate what we know into strategies for communicators. <em>Last updated 2019.</em></p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Messages_and_Audiences.pdf" class="ce-selection" target="_blank">Messages and Audiences<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes different ways to consider your audiences' needs, the factors that can influence their understanding and acceptance of your messages, and how communicators can tailor their messages for different audiences. <em>Last updated 2018.</em></p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_CommunityEngagement.pdf" target="_blank">Community Engagement<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes how to identify and engage with communities who are affected by crises and promote active community participation in responses. <em>Last updated 2018.</em></p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Crisis_Communication_Plans.pdf" target="_blank">Crisis Communication Plans<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes how to develop and implement a crisis communication plan. <em>Last updated 2014.</em></p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Spokesperson.pdf" target="_blank">Spokesperson<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes the role of a spokesperson in communicating during crises and emergencies and provides recommendations on practices for spokespersons. <em>Last updated 2014.</em></p>
<p><strong><a href="/cerc/media/pdfs/CERC_Working_with_the_Media.pdf" target="_blank">Working with the Media<span aria-label=" pdf icon" role="img" class="sr-only"></span><span class="cdc-link-icon cdc-link-icon--pdf" aria-hidden="true"></span></a></strong></p>
<p>This chapter describes the media's role in a crisis and recommended practices for facilitating positive relationships with the media. <em>Last updated 2014.</em></p>
