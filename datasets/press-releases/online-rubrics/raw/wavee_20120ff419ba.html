Quickly amplify your story to a national TV and radio audience

Satellite, Radio and Virtual Media Tours

Virtual Media Tours let your spokesperson reach a large audience quickly—with back-to-back television, radio, or online interviews on top outlets, over the course of several hours in a single day. It's the ideal format for amplifying special events, product launches or campaign kickoffs to your target audience. And you can customize your tour to prioritize target markets, including nationally syndicated and local shows. 

Choose a Virtual Media Tour (VMT) to have your spokesperson participate in interviews with TV and radio stations across the country, via satellite, without leaving their home, using Zoom or Skype as the intermediary.

Leverage decades of media relations expertise
Our team produces more than 200 media tours every year. We’ll work together with you on everything from initial consultation to audio and video production services, media notification and pitching, and post-event reporting.

Generate more visibility and engagement for your multimedia assets

Multichannel News Release—only from Cision

A Multichannel News Release (MNR) combines your press release and up to 6 multimedia assets—video, images, audio and more—into a single, Cision-created and hosted, branded landing page experience focused on your story and message. Then we provide:

  • Distribution of your content across PR Newswire’s industry-leading extensive audience generating network, including full national wire transmission to thousands of websites, media outlets, and journalists, with guaranteed placement on 200 local broadcast media websites via Cision-exclusive partnerships
  • Opportunities to extend promotion of your content to international outlets and influencers
  • 10,000 landing page views, guaranteed
  • Lead generation features that drive traffic to your website and social media channels
  • Posting of one video to YouTube, Vimeo or the video portal of your choice

MNRs get your content greater visibility with your target audience, and more social media engagement from influencers and customers. It elevates your press release to a powerful, sharable pitching tool.

MNR Pro

A Multichannel News Release plus Native Articles on websites your target audience trusts
MNR Pro adds paid placement of your press release as a native article on relevant websites, and native ads promoting the article on other high-traffic pages within those sites. Amplify your story with guaranteed publication and 150K guaranteed impressions on the web sites your target audiences follow and trust.

MNR Interactive

News your audience interacts with
MNR Interactive transforms your news into engaging quizzes, flip-books, ‘live’ charts and more. Your audience will spend more time with your news, and you’ll gather more data on the information and experiences that interest them most

MNR Deluxe

An engrossing audience experience
MNR Deluxe combines all our MNR options—branded multimedia landing page, paid placement, and interactive content—into a single seamless experience that keeps influencers, journalists, customers, and prospects engaged from start to finish. It's a complete content marketing strategy for your news, in one package.
