            <td valign="top">
            <h4>Item 1.01 &mdash; Entry into a material definitive agreement</h4>
            <p><strong>Event</strong>&mdash;Registrant enters into a material definitive agreement not
            made in the ordinary course of business or into an amendment of such agreement that is
            material to registrant; see Reg S-K Item 601(b)(10) (excluding (iii)(A) and (B),
            management or compensatory agreements or arrangements, which, as to the principal
            executive officer, principal financial officer or other named executive officers, are
            covered under Item 5.02)</p>
            <p><strong>Filing period</strong> &mdash; Within 4 business days</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact</strong> &mdash; No impact</p>
            <hr />
            <h4>Item 1.02 &mdash; Termination of a material definitive agreement</h4>
            <p><strong>Event</strong>&mdash; A material definitive agreement not made in the ordinary
            course of business of registrant terminates (other than by expiration on a stated
            termination date, or the completion of all parties&rsquo; obligations) where such
            termination is material to registrant; see Reg S-K Item 601(b)(10) (excluding (iii)(A)
            and (B), management or compensatory agreements or arrangements, which, as to the
            principal executive officer, principal financial officer or other named executive
            officers, are now covered under Item 5.02)</p>
            <p><strong>Filing period </strong>&mdash; Within 4 business days of termination, or notice
            of termination pursuant to the terms of the agreement</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact </strong>&mdash; No impact</p>
            <hr />
            <h4><strong>Item 1.03 </strong>&mdash; Bankruptcy or receivership</h4>
            <p><strong>Event </strong>&mdash; Appointment of a receiver, fiscal agent or similar officer
            for registrant or its parent in a bankruptcy proceeding (federal or state)</p>
            <p>Entry of an order confirming a plan of reorganization, arrangement or liquidation
            relating to registrant&rsquo;s or its parent&rsquo;s assets or business by a court or
            governmental authority</p>
            <p><strong>Filing period</strong> &mdash; Within 4 business days</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact</strong> &mdash; Yes. Failure to timely file will impact S-3
            eligibility</p>
            <hr />
            <h4><strong>Item 1.04</strong> &mdash; Mine safety: reporting of shutdowns and patterns of
            violations</h4>
            <p><strong>Event </strong>&mdash; If the registrant or a subsidiary receives, with respect
            to a coal or other mine (&ldquo;Mine&rdquo;) of which the registrant or subsidiary is an
            operator::</p>
            <ul>
                <li>an imminent danger order issued under Section 107(a) of the Federal Mine Safety and
                Health Act of 1977 (&ldquo;Mine Safety Act&rdquo;);</li>
                <li>a written notice from the Mine Safety and Health Administration (&ldquo;MSHA&rdquo;)
                that the Mine has a pattern of violations of mandatory health or safety standards
                that are of such nature as could have significantly and substantially contributed to
                the cause and effect of mine health or safety hazards under Section 104(e) of the
                Mine Safety Act; or</li>
                <li>a written notice from the MSHA that the Mine has the potential to have such a
                pattern</li>
            </ul>
            <p><strong>Filing period &mdash; </strong>Within 4 business days</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact &mdash;</strong> No impact</p>
            <hr />
            <h4><strong>Item 1.05</strong> &mdash; Material Cybersecurity Incidents</h4>
            <p><strong>Event </strong>&mdash; If the registrant experiences a cybersecurity incident
            that is determined by the registrant to be material</p>
            <p><strong>Filing period &mdash; </strong>Within 4 business days of the company making the
            determination that the cybersecurity incident was material</p>
            <p>Registrant may delay filing in cases where the US attorney general has notified the SEC
            in writing that the disclosure poses a substantial risk to national security or public
            safety </p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact &mdash;</strong> No impact</p>

            <h4>Item 2.01&mdash;Completion of acquisition or disposition of assets</h4>
            <p>EventRegistrant or a subsidiary consolidated completes an acquisition or disposition of a
            &ldquo;significant amount of assets&rdquo; (otherwise than in the ordinary course of
            business), or a "significant amount of assets" that constitute a real estate operation
            as defined in Regulation S-X</p>
            <p><strong>Filing period</strong>&mdash;Within 4 business days</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact</strong>&mdash;Yes. Failure to timely file will impact S-3 eligibility </p>
            <p><strong>Instruction</strong>&mdash;&ldquo;Significant amount of assets&rdquo; is involved
            if:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>the equity in the net book value of assets or the amount paid or received for the
                assets upon acquisition or disposition exceeded 10% of the total assets of
                registrant and its consolidated subsidiaries, or</li>
                <li>it involved a business that is significant to registrant or a majority-owned
                subsidiary (encompasses acquisition of an interest in a business accounted for by
                the equity method or, in lieu of that method, the fair value option), or</li>

            S-X</p>

            obligation that is material to it</p>

            the contingent obligation</p>
            <p><strong>S-3 impact</strong>&mdash;No impact</p>

                <li>as obligations that arise under the facility that are immaterial individually become
                material in the aggregate to registrant</li>
            </ul>

            financial obligation of the registrant (including an accrual for probable loss under
            FASB ASC Topic 450), the consequences of which are material to registrant</p>

            <p><strong>S-3 impact</strong>&mdash;No impact</p>

            disclosed in the timely-filed periodic report for that period</p>
            <p id="section-3"><strong>S-3 impact</strong>&mdash;No impact</p>
