               <div class="inner-content" id="middle_column_td">	<h2>Code of Ethics</h2>
	
<h3>Code of Professional Standards for the Practice of Public Relations</h3>
<h4>Member Statement of Professional Values</h4>
<p>This statement presents the core values of PRSA members and, more broadly, of the public relations profession. These values provide the foundation for the Member Code of Ethics and set the industry standard for the professional practice of public relations. These values are the fundamental beliefs that guide our behaviors and decision making process. We believe our professional values are vital to the integrity of the profession as a whole.</p>
<h4>Advocacy</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We serve the public interest by acting as responsible advocates for those we represent.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We provide a voice in the marketplace of ideas, facts, and viewpoints to aid informed public debate.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Honesty</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We adhere to the highest standards of accuracy and truth in advancing the interests of those we represent and in communicating with the public.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Expertise</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We acquire and responsibly use specialized knowledge and experience.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We advance the profession through continued professional development, research, and education.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We build mutual understanding, credibility, and relationships among a wide array of institutions and audiences.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Independence</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We provide objective counsel to those we represent.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We are accountable for our actions.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Loyalty</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We are faithful to those we represent, while honoring our obligation to serve the public interest.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Fairness</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">We deal fairly with clients, employers, competitors, peers, vendors, the media, and the general public. We respect all opinions and support the right of free expression.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Provisions and Guidelines</h4>
<p>Within the following provisions, a member shall:</p>
<h4><br />Free Flow of Information</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Preserve the integrity of the process of communication.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Be honest and accurate in all communications.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Act promptly to correct erroneous communications for which the practitioner is responsible.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Preserve the free flow of unprejudiced information when giving or receiving gifts by ensuring that gifts are nominal, legal, and infrequent.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Competition</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Follow ethical hiring practices designed to respect free and open competition without deliberately undermining a competitor.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Preserve intellectual property rights in the marketplace. Disclosure of Information</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Be honest and accurate in all communications.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Act promptly to correct erroneous communications for which the practitioner is responsible.</span></div>
</li>
<li><span style="color: #333333;">Investigate the truthfulness and accuracy of information released on behalf of those represented.</span></li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Reveal the sponsors for causes and interests represented.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Disclose financial interest (such as stock ownership) in a client's organization.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Avoid deceptive practices.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Safeguarding Confidences</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Safeguard the confidences and privacy rights of present, former, and prospective clients and employees.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Protect privileged, confidential, or insider information gained from a client or organization.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Immediately advise an appropriate authority if a member discovers that confidential information is being divulged by an employee of a client company or organization.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Conflicts of Interests</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Act in the best interests of the client or employer, even subordinating the member's personal interests.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Avoid actions and circumstances that may appear to compromise good business judgment or create a conflict between personal and professional interests.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Disclose promptly any existing or potential conflict of interest to affected clients or organizations.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Encourage clients and customers to determine if a conflict exists after notifying all affected parties.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Enhancing the Profession</h4>
<ul>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Acknowledge that there is an obligation to protect and enhance the profession.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Keep informed and educated about practices in the profession to ensure ethical conduct.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Actively pursue personal professional development.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Decline representation of clients or organizations that urge or require actions contrary to this Code.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Accurately define what public relations activities can accomplish.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Counsel subordinates in proper ethical decision making.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Require that subordinates adhere to the ethical requirements of the Code.</span></div>
</li>
<li>
<div><span style="color: #333333;">Report ethical violations, whether committed by PRSA members or not, to the appropriate authority.</span></div>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><br />Member Code of Ethics Pledge</h4>
<p><strong>Pledge:</strong></p>
<p>To conduct myself professionally, with truth, accuracy, fairness, and responsibility to the public; to improve my individual competence and advance the knowledge and proficiency of the profession through continuing research and education; and to adhere to the articles of the Member Code of Ethics 2000 for the practice of public relations as adopted by the governing Assembly of the Public Relations Society of America.<br /><br /> I understand and accept that there is a consequence for misconduct, up to and including membership revocation. And, I understand that those who have been or are sanctioned by a government agency or convicted in a court of law of an action that is in violation of this Code may be barred from membership or expelled from the Society.</p>
