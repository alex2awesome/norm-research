# Publons Academy / Web of Science Academy Peer Review Training

## Overview

The Publons Academy is a free, online peer review training course for early career researchers. It was developed together with researchers, peer reviewers, journal editors and Nobel Laureates to help practice and master the core competencies of peer review.

## Course Structure

The course consists of **10 modules**, covering:
- Industry knowledge
- Review skills
- Practical activities

Participants practice writing actual reviews of papers published in their field.

## Module Topics (typical)

1. The role of peer review in scholarly communication
2. Journal selection and types of peer review
3. Conflicts of interest and ethical responsibilities
4. Reading the paper critically
5. Identifying contributions and assessing novelty
6. Methodological evaluation
7. Statistical review principles
8. Writing constructive review comments
9. Decision recommendations
10. Working with editors and post-review process

## Mentorship and Completion

The Publons Academy facilitated co-review with a mentor. Upon finishing the course with the mentor's endorsement, participants build a verified peer review profile.

## Note on Transition

The Publons Academy content was moved onto a different platform and renamed the **Web of Science Academy** in June 2021 to better represent the expanded course offerings covering research integrity in general, and not just peer review.
