# The Open Marketplace of Ideas (Recht / arg min)

## Main Arguments on Peer Review

- "It doesn't elevate great work, it doesn't catch errors, it doesn't catch fraud, it is susceptible to collusion rings."
- Defenders cannot articulate what peer review accomplishes well, nor that it's superior to alternatives.

## The Scale Problem in ML

- ICML / NeurIPS receive ~30,000 submissions annually.
- Reviewers get six weeks to review six or more papers.
- Volume makes meaningful evaluation impossible at this scale.

## Organic Reform Over Mandates

- Devalue peer-reviewed papers through cultural shift, not new rules.
- Hiring committees already distrust candidates with excessive publication counts.

## The Twitter Caveat

- Social media bypasses peer review but conflates scientific debate, public communication, and personal opinion.
- Endorses Kevin Munger's critique of single-platform conflation.

## Core Reform Principle

"Adding requirements without resources creates worse outcomes." Mandatory ethics statements, preregistration plans, or additional paperwork without funding strangle creativity and enable authoritarian control.

## Implication for Reviewer Norms

A reviewer should resist treating their own review as a definitive certification — it is a quick filter at scale, not an oracle. Stop asking authors to do work the venue won't fund.
