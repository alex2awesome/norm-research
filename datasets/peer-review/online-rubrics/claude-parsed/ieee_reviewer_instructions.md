# IEEE Reviewer Instructions: Complete Overview

## Role & Benefits
Reviewers help "improve the technical quality of a colleague's work" and contribute to advancing research through expert evaluations.

## Signup Process
Create an account on the publication's submission site and select keywords matching your expertise to enter the reviewer pool.

## Peer Review Structure
"At least two independent, qualified reviewers" evaluate articles after prescreening.

## Evaluation Criteria for New Articles

**Key Assessment Areas:**
- "Contribution to the field and novelty"
- Literature review completeness and reference appropriateness
- Methodology soundness and experimental design
- Result quality, clarity, and reproducibility
- Logical conclusions and organizational flow

**Section-by-Section Standards:**
- Abstract: ≤250 words, self-contained, accurately reflects content
- Introduction: establishes context and research questions
- Methodology: detailed and reproducible
- Results: appropriately analyzed and presented
- Discussion: addresses research questions, highlights significance
- Conclusion: supported by findings only
- References: relevant, recent, balanced

## Ethics Requirements

**Author Misconduct to Report:**
- Plagiarism, duplicate publication, parallel submission
- Data fabrication or image manipulation
- Inappropriate authorship, citation stacking

**Reviewer Misconduct to Avoid:**
- Breaching confidentiality or contacting authors directly
- Citation coercion or conflicts of interest

## Review Writing Structure
1. Summary of purpose and findings
2. Major concerns (methodology, data, analysis)
3. Minor concerns (clarity, structure, presentation)
4. Overall feedback with recommendation

## Critical Restrictions
- "Do **not** use AI tools...to generate or draft reviews"
- Maintain strict confidentiality of unpublished content
- Decline if conflicts of interest exist
- Only suggest relevant references with justification

## Recommendation Categories
- **Accept**: Meets standards with no changes required
- **Revise**: Major or minor revisions needed
- **Reject**: Fundamental issues present
