# AER Reviewer Guidelines

## Anonymity

"Manuscripts submitted to the _American Economic Review_ are reviewed in a single-blind fashion." Reviewers must exclude their names from report text and filenames to maintain anonymity.

## Conflict of Interest Handling

The journal collects disclosure statements from all coauthors. However, evaluation should focus on scientific merit rather than conflicts of interest—specifically whether "the conclusions in the paper are robust, credible, and worthy of publication."

## Submission Process

Reviews are managed through ScholarOne Manuscripts. Reviewers access submission instructions via their Reviewer Center after logging in.

## Recommended External Resources

The AER references external guidelines that reviewers may find valuable:
- *Econometrica* provides a brief referee guidelines document
- The *Journal of Economic Perspectives* published more comprehensive guidance by Berk, Harvey, and Hirshleifer

## Evaluation Approach

The AER referee model emphasizes:
- Evaluation of robustness of empirical results
- Credibility of identification strategy
- Substantive contribution to the literature
- Quality of theoretical analysis where relevant
- Worthiness of publication in a top general-interest economics journal
