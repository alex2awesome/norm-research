# How to Be a Great Reviewer for a Research Paper (Cell Mentor)

## Key Qualities

Effective reviewers are "thorough and balanced" and deliver feedback "in a timely manner."

## Essential Tips

### Learn to Decline Appropriately
- Say no promptly — within a day of receiving an invitation.
- Suggest alternative experts, especially early-career researchers.
- Request extensions early if you need more time.

### Provide Clear, Specific Feedback
- Vague comments don't help authors or editors.
- "Honestly and clearly state the pros and cons" and explain reasoning.
- When suggesting experiments, justify why they're necessary and assess feasibility.
- Authors have limited resources and may address concerns differently than you envision — accept that.

### Consider Your Reputation
- Many journals keep identities confidential, but editors know who you are.
- Some journals publicly credit reviewers; behave professionally — your reputation is at stake.

### Adopt a Collaborative Mindset
- View peer review as teamwork between authors, reviewers, editors.
- Treat editors as partners, not just gatekeepers.
- Use your author experience to guide constructive feedback.

### Apply Empathy
- Be "timely, thoughtful, and above all empathic."
- Remember what feedback felt like when you were on the receiving end.
