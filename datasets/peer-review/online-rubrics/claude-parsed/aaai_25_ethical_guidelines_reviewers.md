# AAAI-25 Ethical Guidelines for Reviewers

## Core Commitments

Reviewers must maintain fairness and objectivity while treating authors respectfully. They should "provide constructive feedback in a professional and courteous manner."

## Conflict of Interest Management

Reviewers must disclose potential conflicts and decline assignments where objectivity may be compromised. This includes papers from their own institution or close collaborators. They should "promptly inform the chairs and exclude" themselves when conflicts arise.

## Review Standards

Reviews must focus solely on academic merit, excluding "the authors' personal characteristics, affiliations, or backgrounds." Reviewers commit to offering "suggestions for improvement" rather than personal criticism.

## Confidentiality and Integrity

Reviewers must protect "the confidentiality of the review process" and not use information for personal gain. They must "carefully check the submissions for any indications of plagiarism" and report suspected instances immediately.

## Professional Responsibility

All reviewers must meet deadlines and provide thorough feedback while upholding "the highest standards of ethical conduct" throughout the review process.

## Five Key Principles

1. **Fairness and Professionalism**: Reviewers strive to be fair, unbiased, and objective.
2. **Conflict of Interest Disclosure**: Reviewers must disclose any conflicts and decline if not qualified or objectivity may be compromised.
3. **Avoiding Bias from Personal Relationships**: Don't review submissions suspected to be authored by colleagues or close collaborators.
4. **Plagiarism Detection**: Carefully check submissions for plagiarism and inform chairs immediately.
5. **Adherence to Guidelines**: Adhere to the guidelines and deadlines set forth.
