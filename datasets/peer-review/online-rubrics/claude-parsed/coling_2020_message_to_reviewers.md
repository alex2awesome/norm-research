# COLING 2020 PC-Chairs Message to Reviewers

## Five Main Evaluation Criteria

The COLING review form consists of five main sections:

1. **Relevance**: Is this paper relevant to COLING?

2. **Readability/Clarity**: Is the paper well-written and well-structured?

3. **Originality**: How original and innovative is the research described? Originality could be in:
   - The linguistic question being addressed
   - The methodology applied to the linguistic question
   - The combination of the two

4. **Technical Correctness/Soundness**: Is the research described in the paper technically sound and correct? Can one trust the claims of the paper—are they supported by the analysis or experiments and are the results correctly interpreted?

5. **Accessibility**: Have you personally checked the accessibility of the resource, data or code?

## Key Review Principles

Reviewers should take a balanced approach when reviewing papers:
- COLING seeks a solid technical program with high-quality papers describing complete pieces of work
- COLING also wants a broad and interesting program—reviewers should keep an open mind when evaluating and recommending papers

## Mission Focus

The mission of COLING is to present research at the intersection of linguistics and computation. COLING seeks to select papers with an in-depth analysis of results, going beyond mere figures for leaderboards and, where appropriate, providing analysis of results using diagnostic datasets designed to evaluate performance with respect to motivated linguistic phenomena.

## Process

For COLING 2025, the review process is double-blind.
