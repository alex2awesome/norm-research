# To Be or Not To Be (a Reviewer 2) — PhD-student perspective (The PhD Place)

## Pros of Reviewing as a PhD Student

- **Skill development** — sharpens critical analysis; "Reviewing manuscripts requires more concentration and attention to detail" than casual reading; helps you spot methodological issues in your own work.
- **Career building** — strengthens academic job applications; demonstrates "versatility, critical analysis skills, and a selfless attitude to supporting the research community."
- **Networking** — connecting with journal editors and academics in your field opens doors.
- **Contributing to the field** — supports research-community integrity.

## Cons

- **Timing** — early-stage doctoral students may lack sufficient expertise.
- **Time investment** — unpaid work competing with your own research and writing.

## Recommendations

- Wait 12+ months into your PhD before volunteering.
- Review sparingly — roughly one paper per publication you produce.
- Strategically target journals where you want to build connections.
- Balance the unpaid duty with PhD priorities.
