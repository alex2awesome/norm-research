# SciPost Peer-Witnessed Refereeing

## Overlay Journal Model

SciPost is often thought of as an overlay journals system. Preprint handling and circulation occurs through arXiv; the rest of the publication process — peer refereeing through publication and post-publication commenting — is offered by SciPost.

## Peer-Witnessed Refereeing Process

SciPost uses an open procedure known as **peer-witnessed refereeing**. Submitted manuscripts must be picked up for editorial processing by one of the Fellows of an Editorial College.

Key features:
- Reports on Submissions are anonymous by default
- Anonymity can be waived if the Contributor providing the Report wishes it
- SciPost encourages Referees to sign their Reports
- All Reports become publicly visible with the published paper

## Quality Standards

Manuscripts undergo "extremely stringent peer-witnessed refereeing" guaranteeing that papers published in SciPost Journals meet the highest possible academic standards.

## Acceptance Criteria

SciPost focuses on:
- Originality and novelty of contribution
- Significance of advance to the field
- Validity of the research and arguments
- Quality of writing and presentation

## Transparency

Unlike traditional models, SciPost makes:
- Submitted manuscript publicly available
- All Reports publicly available (anonymous or signed)
- Author responses publicly available
- Editor decisions publicly available

This fosters accountability and quality in the refereeing process.
