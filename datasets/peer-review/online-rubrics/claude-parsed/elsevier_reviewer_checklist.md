# Elsevier Reviewer Checklist

## Before Accepting a Review
- Verify expertise aligns with article scope
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest
- Confirm availability to meet deadlines
- Review peer review process fundamentals
- Familiarize yourself with "reviewer policies"

## Managing Your Review

**Confidentiality Requirements:**
- Treat manuscripts as "confidential documents"
- Do not share with anyone without editor authorization
- Avoid uploading content to generative AI tools
- Never disclose peer review information

**For Original Research Articles, Evaluate:**
- Title accuracy and discoverability
- Abstract completeness and clarity
- Introduction's background and objectives
- Methods sufficiency for replication
- Results presentation and statistical reporting
- Discussion support from data
- Reference currency and relevance
- Language quality and readability
- Novel or important contributions
- Ethical/integrity concerns

**For Review Articles, Assess:**
- Relevance justification
- Historical development coverage
- Reference comprehensiveness
- Methodological transparency
- Statistical method appropriateness
- Overall structure and clarity
- Language quality

**Additional Considerations:**
- Sex and gender reporting practices
- Research data and visualization quality
- Ethical approval documentation

## Finalizing Your Review

**Do:**
- Summarize the article briefly
- Provide specific, constructive feedback
- Use clear paragraph structure
- Check conformity to journal guidelines
- Raise plagiarism/falsification suspicions
- Consider unconscious bias

**Don't:**
- Focus on minor spelling issues
- Make personal attacks
- Dismiss alternative viewpoints
- Share reviews without authorization
- Self-promote citations

**Recommendation Options:**
- Reject
- Accept without revision
- Revise (major or minor)
