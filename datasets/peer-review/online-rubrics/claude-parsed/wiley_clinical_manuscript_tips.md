# Wiley's Clinical Manuscript Review Tips

Nine considerations specific to clinical manuscripts.

1. **Basic peer review principles** apply to all manuscript types.
2. **Initial assessment** — skim for content/objectives; evaluate novelty in methodology or conclusions.
3. **Target audience focus** — work must address a clinical problem (not just a research question) and use practitioner-appropriate language.
4. **Structural clarity** — clearly organized and easy to understand for busy clinicians.
5. **Clinical applicability** — emphasize implementation potential; include clinically applicable screening tools where relevant.
6. **Case study evaluation** — only essential information, patient privacy protected, no over-generalized conclusions.
7. **Conflict-of-interest disclosure** — verify authors disclose all potential conflicts including ghostwriting.
8. **Ethical participant protection** — confirm a statement of ethical standards exists, even when research was exempted from informed consent.
9. **Clinical trial standards** — proper recruitment, ethical procedures, randomization, statistical analysis, EQUATOR network compliance.
