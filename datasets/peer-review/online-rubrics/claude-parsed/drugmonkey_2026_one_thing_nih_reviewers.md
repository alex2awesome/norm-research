# Please, NIH Reviewers, If You Do One Thing, Do This (DrugMonkey)

## Core Recommendation

"If you do one productive thing, people, do what you can to communicate to NIH what a total disaster it has been not to have in-person study section meetings."

## How

Use the post-study-section survey, which has questions on:
- Attention span and concentration during virtual meetings.
- Reviewer engagement levels.
- Productivity of panel discussions.
- Preferences between in-person and Zoom formats.

## Norm Encoded

A "good reviewer" duty includes giving honest infrastructure feedback to the funding agency, not just scoring proposals. The reviewer's role extends to helping fix the review system.
