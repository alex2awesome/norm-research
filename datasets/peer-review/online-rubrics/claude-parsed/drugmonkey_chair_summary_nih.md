# What is the Chair Summary for in NIH Peer Review? (DrugMonkey)

A working scientist's critical look at the Chair summary in NIH study sections.

## Official Purpose

NIH guidance: the Chair summary should "focus the panel's attention on the set of major strengths and weaknesses" and "overview the score driving comments." The summary notes areas of consensus and disagreement without substituting for panel members' active engagement.

## DrugMonkey's Concerns About Bias

- **Recency effect**: summary occurs immediately before voting, potentially shifting scores via timing and the Chair's subjective interpretation.
- **Discrepancies with reality**: summaries sometimes emphasize written critiques that never came up in discussion, or mischaracterize where the panel actually landed.
- **Authority influence**: Chairs hold field standing, which carries persuasive weight regardless of intent.
- **Systemic, not individual**: Chairs are time-pressured and managing many responsibilities; this is structural.

## Underlying Question for Reviewers

Does the Chair summary serve any legitimate function beyond shepherding the vote into line and promoting conformity? Reviewers should weigh their own scores against the panel discussion, not the chair-summary recap.
