# ASAPbio Crowd Preprint Review

A community-based, public-comment alternative model to journal-administered peer review.

## Program Overview

ASAPbio coordinates groups of researchers to provide public feedback on preprints. The initiative addresses the low participation in preprint review across platforms.

## Structure

- Crowd leads coordinate groups within a discipline.
- ASAPbio provides "a guide and support for logistics" to crowd leads.
- Reviewers learn through participation in the collaborative process (no formal training course).

## Reviewer Expectations

- Reviewers are *not* required to comment on every preprint — choose based on expertise and availability.
- Comments are submitted via a collaborative Google Doc over 14 days.
- Reviewers may comment on the full paper or specific parts based on their knowledge.

## Current Disciplines

- Cell biology preprints.
- Meta-research preprints.

## Output

Comments are consolidated by section for ease of reading and posted as a public review attached to the preprint. The "report" is a synthesis, not an individual review.

## Implicit Norms

- Public, named, collaborative review is treated as a legitimate alternative to closed journal review.
- Selective participation (review only when in your wheelhouse) is encouraged rather than penalized.
