# 10 Steps to Review a Scientific Paper (Proteintech)

A reagent vendor's blog distilling the standard biomedical review checklist for new reviewers.

## The 10 Steps

1. **Understand the purpose of the review** — why have you been asked, what should you focus on.
2. **Familiarize yourself with the paper** — read thoroughly; grasp question, methodology, analysis, results, conclusions.
3. **Evaluate structure and clarity** — organization, abstract quality, intro effectiveness, logical flow.
4. **Evaluate research methods** — appropriateness and rigor of design and statistical analyses.
5. **Evaluate results and analysis** — does data support the question? statistics appropriate?
6. **Assess discussion and conclusions** — balanced analysis; conclusions aligned with objectives.
7. **Ethical considerations** — guidelines, informed consent, confidentiality, harm minimization.
8. **Verify references and citations** — accurate, relevant, up-to-date.
9. **Provide constructive feedback** — strengths, weaknesses, specific improvements; respectful.
10. **Summarize your review** — main points, strengths, weaknesses, overall recommendation.
