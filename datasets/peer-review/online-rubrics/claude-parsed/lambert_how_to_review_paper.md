# How to Review a Paper (Nathan Lambert)

A practical guide for ML/AI conference reviewing from an industry researcher (Allen AI / interconnects.ai).

## Two-Phase Process

1. **Deep Reading Phase (1–2 hours)** — maintain curiosity and openness while engaging the paper's content and methodology.
2. **Clear Feedback Phase (1–4 hours)** — turn margin notes into coherent, actionable commentary without rushing.

## Markup Strategy While Reading

Color-coded annotation:
- **Green** — contributions and positive elements.
- **Blue / neutral** — general understanding, questions, content feedback.
- **Red** — grammar and language issues, kept *separate* from substantive critique.

"If you don't have any markup in the margins for about half of a page, you probably did not read closely enough."

## Three Often-Missing Elements of Good Reviews

1. **Score-improvement pathways** — specify what revisions would strengthen the rating (weak reject → weak accept → strong accept).
2. **Curiosity / open comments** — non-scored section for exploratory ideas and questions; fosters collaborative dialogue.
3. **Transparency about confusion** — request clarification rather than penalizing unclear content.

## Additional Recommendations

- Number feedback points (i1, i2, …) by section for easier author response.
- Edit reviews after completion to synthesize key weaknesses.
- Use shorter templates for clear accepts/rejects; reserve detailed reviews for borderline or award-nomination papers.
- Adopt supportive tone rather than adversarial positioning.
