# APS Physical Review Information for Reviewers

## Overview

As a reviewer, you play a crucial role in upholding the integrity of science by ensuring that published research is valid, rigorous, and credible. Thorough and insightful reviews help authors improve their papers while upholding the high standards of Physical Review.

## Ethical Guidelines

For information on the proper conduct of referees, the APS recommends consulting the COPE Ethical Guidelines for Peer Reviewers.

## Peer Review Process

The peer review process is directed by:
- A team of staff editors employed by APS
- Active scientists (academic editors), ultimately responsible for publication decisions
- Advised by experts in the field serving as referees and members of the Editorial Board

## Evaluation Criteria

Reviewers should assess submissions on:
- **Validity** — are the claims and methods sound?
- **Novelty** — is the work original?
- **Importance** — does it advance the field meaningfully?
- **Quality of presentation** — is it clearly communicated?

## Reviewer Responsibilities

### Report Structure
Using the provided sections facilitates the peer review process. There are specific guidelines for structuring your report.

### Data Sharing
Reviewers may comment on whether and how data are shared, though authors are ultimately responsible for data sharing.

### Supplemental Material
Reviewers should assess whether supplemental material is relevant to the main text and scientifically sound, and indicate whether any of it should be included in the main text.

## Journal-Specific Standards

Different APS journals have different acceptance thresholds:
- **Physical Review Letters**: Highest selectivity; emphasis on broad significance and surprise
- **Physical Review X**: Open access, highest novelty and impact
- **Physical Review A-E**: Topical journals; technical excellence
- **Physical Review Research**: Open access, broader scope

## Confidentiality

- Treat manuscripts as confidential
- Do not share with non-reviewers
- Do not use information for personal advantage
- Do not contact authors directly
