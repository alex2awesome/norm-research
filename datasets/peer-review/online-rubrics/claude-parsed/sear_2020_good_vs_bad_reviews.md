# What Makes a Good or Bad Peer Review (Royal Society — Sear, 2020)

## Characteristics of Good Reviews

1. **Structure and clarity** — start with a (very) brief summary of the paper, then editorial overview, then detailed comments organized by severity.
2. **Balanced feedback** — "A good review does not just criticise but also highlights what the authors have done well."
3. **Constructive tone** — clear, constructive, consistent, professional.
4. **Justified recommendations** — final verdict aligns with detailed comments so editors can justify decisions.

## Characteristics of Bad Reviews

1. **Unprofessional language** — insulting or derogatory terms undermine credibility.
2. **Poor organization** — "An unstructured stream of consciousness" that fails to distinguish major from minor issues.
3. **Insufficient detail** — comments too brief for editors to understand your reasoning.
4. **Excessive demands** — requiring unnecessary revisions beyond what's needed for publishability.
5. **Unreasonable expectations** — recommending changes authors cannot feasibly implement.

## Key Rule

Write reviews "in a tone you would be happy to receive," keeping both editors and authors as your audience.
