# 12 Tips for Writing a Peer Review (Wilkinson / Social Science Space)

A widely-cited 12-item checklist originally published on the Publons blog.

## The 12 Tips

1. **Verify your expertise** before accepting the review assignment.
2. **Check journal guidelines** — go to the journal page for any specific reviewer instructions and confirm format compliance.
3. **Skim the paper first** — quickly read through to understand general content; underline key arguments.
4. **Read critically** — ask whether the research question is valuable, data appropriate, results novel.
5. **Document major issues** — take notes about major, moderate and minor revisions needed.
6. **Create a checklist** — verify citations actually support the claims authors make.
7. **Assess writing quality** — language, grammar, flow, clarity, fit for the journal.
8. **Check for prior publication** — review author histories and related research to ensure no previous publication.
9. **Identify common errors** in methodology or analysis.
10. **Summarize for the editor** — overview, contribution, strengths, weaknesses, acceptability.
11. **Provide specific recommendations** — actionable suggestions, target two to three pages (four max).
12. **Make a recommendation** — accept / revise / reject, clearly stated to the editor.
