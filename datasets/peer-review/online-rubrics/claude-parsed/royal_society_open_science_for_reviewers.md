# Royal Society Open Science - For Reviewers

## Soundness-Based Review (Not Impact)

Royal Society Open Science publishes research across the entire range of science on the basis of objective peer review. The journal accepts all articles that are "scientifically sound and useful to the community without assessing novelty or potential impact."

This is similar to the PLOS ONE / Royal Society Open Science / Scientific Reports model that focuses purely on technical correctness rather than perceived importance.

## Review Process and Anonymity

The journal operates a **single-anonymized peer review** policy: reviewers are aware of the author(s) identities but reviewer identities are withheld unless reviewers waive their right to anonymity. The journal encourages referees to waive their traditional right to anonymity and sign their reports.

## Reviewer Standards

Reviewers should be:
- Objective and constructive
- Focused on the content of the work
- Refraining from being hostile or inflammatory
- Refraining from libellous or derogatory personal comments

## Transparent Peer Review

Royal Society Open Science operates open peer review via Web of Science Transparent Peer Review:
- Reviewer reports made public alongside published articles
- Decision letter and author responses also accessible
- Referee reports made public under an open access licence (CC-BY)

## Number of Reviewers

At least two peer reviewers will be sought, though the Editors may request that additional reviewers assess the manuscript.

## Constructive Review Approach

The journal's approach focuses on whether the methodology and conclusions are rigorous, with importance/novelty assessment left to readers post-publication.
