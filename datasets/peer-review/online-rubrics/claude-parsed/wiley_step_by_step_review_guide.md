# Wiley Step-by-Step Manuscript Review Guide

Two-pass workflow with section-level checklists.

## First Read-Through

- Note thoughts with line numbers for easy reference.
- Assess research relevance, originality, contribution.
- Evaluate clarity and whether conclusions align with evidence.
- Review effectiveness of tables, figures, images.

**Major Flaws to Identify**
- Conclusions contradicting evidence.
- Outdated or unreliable methodologies.
- Overlooked processes with known influence.
- Insufficient or contradictory data.
- Methodological gaps affecting replicability.

**Initial Summary**: opening paragraphs contextualizing research question, methodology, and field contribution before deciding rejection or further review.

## Second Read-Through

For potentially-publishable manuscripts, refine via:
- Argument construction and logical flow.
- Clarity, title accuracy, abstract quality.
- Language issues affecting comprehension.

## Section-by-Section Assessment

- **Introduction**: research argument and recent literature establish originality and necessity.
- **Materials & Methods**: replicability, repeatability, robustness, adherence to standards like CONSORT.
- **Results & Discussion**: clear coherent narrative, statistical analyses, contextual significance.
- **Conclusions**: evidence-based statements reflecting achieved aims.
- **References**: accurate, adequate, current, balanced; no over-reliance on self-citation.
- **Data & Images**: plausibility, presentation clarity, no undisclosed manipulation.

## Report Structure

1. **Summary** — start with strengths, contextualize within literature.
2. **Major issues** — flaws, severity, required revisions.
3. **Minor issues** — ambiguities, citation concerns, labeling errors.

## Recommendations

- **Accept** — outline justifications and improvable areas.
- **Revise** — specify major or minor changes.
- **Reject** — provide constructive improvement pathways.

## Key Principle

"Treat the author's work the way you would like your own to be treated."
