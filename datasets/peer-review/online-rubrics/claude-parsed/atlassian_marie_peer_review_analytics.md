# The Art of Peer Review in Analytics (Atlassian / Marie)

Industry-side peer-review framework from Atlassian's data org.

## Definition and Purpose

"Evaluation of work by one or more people with similar competencies" to maintain quality and catch errors. Definition was kept flexible rather than prescriptive — teams have autonomy in implementation.

## Four Goals

- Error reduction via mistake-catching and approach validation.
- Continuous improvement via knowledge-sharing with senior peers.
- Best-practice dissemination across organizational areas.
- Scalability through consistent methodologies.

## Four Review Formats

1. **Sparring sessions** (most popular) — regular meetings where analysts present early work for team feedback.
2. **Code reviews** — formal pull-request reviews for productionized pipelines.
3. **Slack channels** — informal or structured feedback threads.
4. **Office hours** — designated weekly slots with assigned reviewers available for consultation.

## Critical Success Factor

"The reviewee explicitly asks for the type of feedback that they are looking for." Directs reviewer focus toward specific areas (messaging, analytical methods, etc.) — keeps feedback actionable.

## Outcome

>90% of Atlassian's analytics teams engaged with at least one peer-review format, despite initial time and siloing concerns.
