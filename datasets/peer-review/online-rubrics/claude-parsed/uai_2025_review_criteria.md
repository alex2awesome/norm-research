# UAI 2025 Review Criteria

## Four Core Assessment Criteria

Papers submitted to UAI 2025 will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

1. **Technical Correctness** - The soundness and validity of the research methodology and findings

2. **Novelty** - The originality and innovation of the contribution to the field

3. **Convincing Claims** - "Whether claims are backed up convincingly"

4. **Clarity of Writing** - The quality and readability of the paper's presentation

## Reproducibility

The conference encourages authors to enhance their submissions by making code and data publicly available, which supports reproducibility and strengthens the overall contribution to the research community.

## Process

UAI uses a double-blind review process via OpenReview. Reviewers are under no obligation to look at supplementary materials and base reviews primarily on the main paper.

Application-focused submissions are reviewed according to appropriate criteria and by reviewers with appropriate backgrounds.
