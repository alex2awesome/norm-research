# AAAI-24 Review Process

## Two-Phase Reviewing Structure

**Phase 1:** "Every paper will be allocated two reviewers. If a paper receives two reviews that are both sufficiently negative, it will be rejected without any opportunity for author feedback."

**Phase 2:** "Each paper will be allocated additional reviewers. These reviewers will not be given access to Phase 1 reviews until after submitting their own reviews."

## Author Feedback and Discussion

A unified feedback opportunity permits Phase 2 authors to respond to all evaluations from both phases between November 2-5, 2023. Following this window, the program committee engages in deliberations that incorporate author responses.

## Double-Blind Review Requirements

The reviewing process maintains anonymity standards: "AAAI-24 reviews are double blind (i.e., neither reviewers nor authors should be able to identify each other)." Papers must exclude author names, affiliations, and identifying acknowledgments from initial pages.

## Citation Guidelines for Blind Review

Authors should reference prior work using third-person attribution rather than first-person claims. Anonymous citations are permissible when appropriate. Preprint versions on platforms like arXiv do not violate blind review policies if submissions do not reference this material.

## Ethical Framework

The document references "Ethical Guidelines for AAAI-24 Reviewers" and a "Publications Ethics and Malpractice Statement," though full details are not provided in the excerpt.

## Reviewer Standards

Reviewers are fully responsible for the entire content of their reviews. Tools can be used to improve the wording of the review but not to generate its content.
