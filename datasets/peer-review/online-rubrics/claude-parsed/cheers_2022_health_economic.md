# CHEERS 2022: Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards

## Overview

Health economic evaluations are comparative analyses of alternative courses of action in terms of their costs and consequences. The CHEERS statement was created to ensure health economic evaluations are identifiable, interpretable, and useful for decision making.

## CHEERS 2022 Update

The CHEERS 2022 statement replaces previous CHEERS reporting guidance. It reflects:
- The need for guidance applicable to all types of health economic evaluation
- New methods and developments in the field
- The increased role of stakeholder involvement including patients and the public

## Checklist Structure

CHEERS 2022 includes a **28-item checklist**, an update from the original 2013 version with 24 items.

## Sections of the Checklist

1. **Title and Abstract** — clear identification of the type of analysis
2. **Introduction** — background and objectives
3. **Methods** —
   - Health economic analysis plan
   - Study population
   - Setting and location
   - Comparators
   - Perspective
   - Time horizon
   - Discount rate
   - Selection of outcomes
   - Measurement of outcomes
   - Valuation of outcomes
   - Measurement and valuation of resources and costs
   - Currency, price date, and conversion
   - Rationale and description of model
   - Analytics and assumptions
   - Characterising heterogeneity
   - Characterising distributional effects
   - Characterising uncertainty
   - Approach to engagement with patients and others affected by the study
4. **Results** —
   - Study parameters
   - Summary of main results
   - Effect of uncertainty
   - Effect of engagement with patients
5. **Discussion** —
   - Study findings, limitations, generalizability, current knowledge
6. **Other** —
   - Source of funding
   - Conflicts of interest

## Purpose and Audience

The CHEERS 2022 statement is primarily intended for researchers reporting economic evaluations for peer-reviewed journals as well as the **peer reviewers and editors assessing them for publication**.

## Reviewer Use

When reviewing health economic evaluations, reviewers should check the manuscript against the 28-item CHEERS checklist for completeness.
