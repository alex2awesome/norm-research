# WACV 2026 Reviewer Guidelines

## Two Paper Types

WACV papers can either be **"application"** or **"algorithms"** papers, and have different reviewing criteria.

### Application Papers
Applications papers will be evaluated on:
- **Systems-level innovation**
- **Novelty of the domain**
- **Comparative assessment**

They should NOT be evaluated solely on the basis of algorithmic novelty (i.e., it is okay to have algorithmic novelty, but it is also okay not to have it).

### Algorithms Papers
Algorithms papers will be evaluated according to standard conference criteria:
- **Algorithmic novelty**
- **Quantified evaluation against current, alternative approaches**

## Responsible Reviewing Expectations

Reviewers are expected to provide fair and thoughtful reviews. Examples of highly irresponsible behavior include:
- One-sentence reviews
- Reviews generated by Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Reviews submitted late
- Reviews that lack engagement with the paper's actual content

## Confidentiality and Ethics

As a reviewer for WACV, you have the responsibility to protect the confidentiality of the ideas represented in the papers you review. WACV submissions are not published documents. The work is considered new or proprietary by the authors.

## Strict Enforcement Policy

WACV will strictly enforce a Responsible Reviewing Policy and a Reviewing Deadline Policy. Any reviewer whose review fails to comply with these policies may face possible **desk rejection of all papers on which they are an author**.

## Conflict of Interest

Common conflicts include:
- Same institution
- Recent collaborators (within 3 years)
- Advisor/advisee
- Family members
- Close personal relationships

## Review Quality Standards

Each review should:
- Engage substantively with the paper's content
- Identify both strengths and weaknesses
- Provide specific, actionable feedback
- Justify scores with concrete reasoning
- Maintain a professional tone
