# Points to Consider When Reviewing Articles (YJBM)

YJBM's article-type-specific rubrics.

## General

- Is the article of interest to YJBM readers?
- Identify strengths and weaknesses throughout.
- Determine how editors can collaborate with authors for improvement.

## Original Research Articles

- Clarity of overall research aim and stated findings.
- Abstract quality regarding aims and results.
- Sufficiency of background information.
- Evidence quality supporting claims.
- Acknowledgment of similar published work.
- Data quality and analytical correctness.
- Appropriateness of figures and tables.
- Methodology clarity for reproducibility.
- Alignment between conclusions and data.
- Discussion of implications with balanced literature review.
- Accessibility for unfamiliar readers.
- Proper abbreviation explanation.

## Case Reports

- Abstract clarity on aim, findings, implications.
- Introduction accessibility for unfamiliar readers.
- Clear case presentation description.
- Figure and table clarity and utility.
- Accurate data analysis and reporting.
- Conclusion alignment with presented data.
- Discussion of similar cases and treatment relevance.

## Reviews

- Accessibility for unfamiliar YJBM readers.
- Abstract accuracy.
- Clear introduction focus.
- Factual accuracy throughout.
- Recent literature utilization.
- Logical organization with subheadings.
- Balanced perspectives on debated topics.
- Relevant figures and tables.
- Forward-looking conclusions addressing research gaps.

## Perspectives, Analyses, Profiles, Interviews

Each article type has its own checklist (abstract accuracy, contextual background, balanced viewpoints, logical conclusions, lessons learned). Common throughout: abstract conciseness, accessibility, logical conclusions, balanced treatment, helpful organization.
