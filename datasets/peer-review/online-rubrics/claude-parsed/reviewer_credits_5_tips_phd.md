# 5 Tips for PhD Students to Be Effective Peer Reviewers (Reviewer Credits)

PhD-student-targeted reviewer onboarding.

## The 5 Tips

1. **Don't try to change everything about the paper** — focus on constructive feedback rather than rewriting. "Only point out issues if they are grammatically wrong, poorly structured, or illogical." Don't impose your style.
2. **Consider your supervisor's approach** — reflect on the kind of feedback you've valued from mentors; balance critique with encouragement; respect the author's voice.
3. **Adopt an "expert" mentality** — "You know more than you think. Embrace your expertise." Your specialized knowledge from years of study has value.
4. **Use reviews to reinforce your own learning** — treat peer review as an opportunity to apply your training; ask the same critical questions you'd ask of your own work.
5. **Focus on the positives** — balance criticism with strengths. Use respectful language like "This section would benefit from…" rather than harsh feedback. Stay objective; avoid personal attacks; complete reviews within 3–4 weeks.
