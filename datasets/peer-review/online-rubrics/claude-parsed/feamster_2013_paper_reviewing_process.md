# The Paper Reviewing Process (Nick Feamster)

A Princeton CS professor's overview of the systems / networking review process.

## Core Insight

"Learning how to review papers not only (obviously) makes you a better reviewer, but it can also help you as an author."

Understanding reviewer criteria helps authors tailor submissions for acceptance — strategic advantage in the submission process.

## Scope

Feamster directs readers to several established resources:
- Articles explaining the review process and its limitations.
- Guidance on writing effective reviews.
- Conference-specific summaries from CS programs.
- Program committee chair guidelines.

The article focuses on "the creation of a paper review" rather than the paper-selection process for accept/reject decisions.

## Implicit Norms

- Review is a teachable skill, not innate.
- Norms are venue-specific (systems / networking has its own conventions).
- Authors should learn to think like reviewers; reviewers should remember they were authors.
