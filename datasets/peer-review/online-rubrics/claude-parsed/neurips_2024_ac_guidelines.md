# NeurIPS 2024 Area Chair Guidelines

## Core Responsibilities

Area Chairs ensure assigned submissions receive "high quality reviews and good discussions" and become familiar with technical content to make initial acceptance decisions with Senior Area Chair guidance.

## Main Tasks

**Preparation:** Update OpenReview profile, read Code of Conduct, understand conflict-of-interest policies, and familiarize yourself with reviewer guidelines.

**Reviewer Management:** Ensure suitable, diverse reviewers matched to each paper. Invite emergency reviewers if needed. Monitor review quality and request improvements for substandard reviews.

**Quality Assurance:** Flag ethically questionable papers for ethics review. Request additional reviews when current ones are unhelpful.

**Discussion Leadership:** Initiate and moderate OpenReview discussions across three phases, ensuring reviewers respond to author feedback and engage substantively.

**Metareview Writing:** Explain your decision (accept/reject/borderline), explain how reviews and discussions informed your choice, and avoid dismissing reviews without justification.

## Meta-Review Best Practices

- Prioritize reviewer **comments over numerical scores**
- Acknowledge author responses explicitly
- Make **decisive judgments on borderline papers**
- Counter perceived biases in reviews
- Consider unconventional approaches fairly
- Avoid overruling unanimous reviewer opinions without additional expert review

## Professional Standards

- Maintain strict confidentiality of all submissions
- Respect reviewer and author deadlines
- Communicate promptly and professionally
- Notify SAC immediately of conflicts of interest
- Never discuss assignments with other ACs without SAC involvement
- Report suspected unethical behavior to SACs and program chairs
