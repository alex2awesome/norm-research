# Bits of DNA — Post-Publication Peer Review (Pachter)

Caltech computational biology professor whose blog is a working example of post-publication critical review.

## Stated Principle

"Neither poetry nor prose…is of any great value, if…they are only recited in order to be believed, and not with any view to criticism or instruction." — Plato, *Phaedrus*, quoted as the blog's epigraph.

## Practice (from posts, not the about page)

- Public, named, technical critiques of high-profile published papers.
- Demands statistical and methodological transparency (e.g., the "P-value Prize" for justifying claims).
- Treats post-publication review as a legitimate continuation of the peer-review process — not as harassment.

## Comment Policy

- Valid email required (commenter can be otherwise anonymized).
- Reserves the right to reject inappropriate content.

## Implicit Reviewer Norms

- A reviewer's job doesn't end at acceptance.
- Public critique with attribution is acceptable when methodologically grounded.
- Statistical claims must be defensible on demand.
