# ACL Rolling Review (ARR) Form - Complete Structure

## Core Review Sections

**Paper Summary**: Reviewers describe the paper's subject matter to ensure understanding and identify potential misinterpretations.

**Summary of Strengths**: Identify publication reasons including novel methodology, empirical results, theoretical analysis, and literature organization.

**Summary of Weaknesses**: Detail concerns about correctness, limited impact, clarity issues, or other factors that might deprioritize the paper among competing submissions.

**Comments/Suggestions/Typos**: Provide constructive feedback for author improvements beyond addressing major concerns.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### Reviewer Confidence (1-5 scale)
- **5**: Positive evaluation; careful reading with familiarity of related work
- **4**: Quite sure; important points checked carefully
- **3**: Pretty sure; general area knowledge but incomplete detail verification
- **2**: Willing to defend evaluation; likely missed details or novelty concerns
- **1**: Outside expertise area or paper very difficult to understand

### Soundness (1-5 scale with half-point options)
- **5**: Exemplary thoroughness for paper type
- **4**: Sufficient support for claims; minor experiments could enhance
- **3**: Adequate main claim support; minor points need strengthening
- **2**: Main claims lack sufficient support; major technical/methodological issues
- **1**: Insufficient for publication or irrelevant to ACL

### Excitement (1-5 scale with half-point options)
Subjective assessment of transformational potential, community impact, or personal research relevance.
- **5**: Highly exciting; would recommend to others
- **4**: Exciting; would mention to colleagues
- **3**: Interesting; might attend presentation
- **2**: Potentially interesting; may resonate with others
- **1**: Not exciting; lacks computational linguistics relevance

### Overall Assessment (1-5 scale with half-point options)
- **5**: Consider for Award (top 2.5%)
- **4**: Conference acceptance
- **3**: Findings acceptance
- **2**: Resubmit next cycle with revisions
- **1**: Do not resubmit or not relevant

## Additional Assessment Areas

**Best Paper Justification**: Required for award-level recommendations

**Limitations & Societal Impact**: Evaluation of author acknowledgment regarding potential harms, bias, exclusion, and dual-use concerns

**Ethical Concerns**: Review against ACL Code of Ethics

**Ethics Review Recommendation**: Indicate if in-depth committee review needed

**Reproducibility** (1-5 scale): Assess availability of parameters, data, and methodological clarity

**Datasets** (1-5 scale): Rate newly released dataset value from enabling to documentary

**Software** (1-5 scale): Rate newly released software utility similarly

**Author Identity Knowledge** (1-5 scale): Disclose any identifying information sources
