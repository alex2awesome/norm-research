# Peer Review Demystified: What, Why, and How (Pareek / TDS)

## What Is Peer Review

Evaluates "the quality, validity, and relevance of research or scholarly work before it is published." Volunteer reviewers absorb enormous submission volumes — CVPR received 11,532 submissions in 2024.

## Why You Should Review

1. Academic responsibility — keep a 3:1 review-to-publication ratio.
2. Stay current — see cutting-edge unpublished research.
3. Build a professional network with editors and PIs globally.
4. Improve your own writing by seeing peer mistakes.
5. Career advancement — review experience strengthens immigration applications (e.g., EB Green Cards).

## How to Get Involved

- Email journal editors directly expressing interest.
- Create a Web of Science profile to track verified reviews.
- You don't need prior publications; smaller conferences help build experience.
- Time commitment: 1–2 papers/month; individual reviews "a couple of hours to several days."

The article includes a cold-email template for contacting editors.
