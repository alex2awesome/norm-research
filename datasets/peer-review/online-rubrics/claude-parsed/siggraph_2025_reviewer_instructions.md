# SIGGRAPH 2025 Reviewer Instructions & Ethics

## Key Criteria

**Journal vs. Conference Track:**
- **Journal Papers**: Must meet ACM TOG excellence standards with "original, well validated, and comprehensively described research"
- **Conference Papers**: Accept "original research" with potentially less comprehensive validation, "novel solutions to existing problems," or work introducing "new interesting problems"

## Types of Contributions Valued

Reviewers should embrace diverse submission types:
- Systems papers integrating multiple techniques
- Experimental studies and perceptual research
- Novel datasets and benchmarks
- Analysis of existing techniques likely to inspire follow-on work

## Scoring Scale

Six-tier evaluation (applies uniformly to all submissions):
- Strong Reject
- Reject
- Borderline Reject
- Borderline Accept
- Accept
- Strong Accept

Uniformly strong scores indicate Journal track suitability; varying scores suggest Conference track placement.

## Core Review Principles

**Fairness**: "Be fair, promote a broad range of substantial contributions to the area, and look for what is good or stimulating about the paper."

**Specificity Required**: Simply stating something is "well known" is "not sufficient nor acceptable"—cite specific publications or sources.

**Minor Flaws**: Should not trigger rejection if the paper remains "technically sound and make[s] a substantial contribution."

**Inspiring follow-on research**: An important factor in evaluating a paper is the degree to which it will inspire follow-on research.

**Different validations**: Different types of contributions require different types of validation; not everything needs to be demonstrated on an end-to-end application or with a user study.

**Comparison burden**: Weigh requiring comparisons (especially complex/time-consuming ones) against the likelihood that these comparisons will yield a result affecting the evaluation.

## Critical Ethics Standards

- **Confidentiality**: Never share papers with non-reviewers
- **Conflict of Interest**: Recuse yourself from same-institution authors, collaborators from past three years, or advisor/advisee relationships
- **Professionalism**: Avoid sarcasm or personal attacks; evaluate work, not authors
- **Anonymity**: Maintain reviewer anonymity permanently
