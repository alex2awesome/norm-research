# ICLR 2025 Area Chair Guidelines: Meta-Review & Decision Framework

## Meta-Review Structure

ACs should write meta-reviews of at least 60 words containing three key elements:

1. **Content Summary**: "A concise description of the submission's main content (scientific claims and findings)" including strengths, weaknesses, and what's missing.

2. **Discussion Summary**: ACs should synthesize weeks of reviewer-author dialogue, listing points raised by reviewers, how authors addressed them, and the AC's assessment of their merit.

3. **Recommendation with Justification**: The meta-review must conclude with a clear accept/reject recommendation grounded in the paper's content and discussion.

## Decision Criteria

The guidelines emphasize basing recommendations on:
- Quality of the paper itself (not score curves)
- Alignment with ICLR 2025 reviewer guidelines
- Code of Ethics compliance
- Virtual meeting discussions for borderline papers

ACs are instructed: "base your recommendations for accept/reject based solely on the reviews and the quality of papers."

## Calibration Approach

Rather than sharing rating distributions, the document states SACs provide calibration support. ACs should contact their Senior Area Chair or program chairs if uncertain about recommendations, ensuring decisions reflect paper quality rather than predetermined acceptance quotas.
