# ASAPbio FAST Principles for Preprint Feedback

## Overview

ASAPbio has developed 14 principles for creating, responding to, and interpreting preprint feedback, organized under four broad themes: **Focused, Appropriate, Specific, and Transparent (FAST)**.

## **Focused** (3 principles)
- Focus on the content of the preprint
- Focus on science, not journal suitability
- Focus on scientific rather than personal critique

## **Appropriate** (5 principles)
- Polish the tone to remain constructive
- Reflect on possible biases in feedback
- Engage in scientific discourse responsibly
- Behave with integrity and responsibility
- Address inappropriate behavior when observed

## **Specific** (4 principles)
- Make feedback useful with concrete details
- Indicate whether concerns are critical or optional
- Assess whether claims are supported by data
- Be candid about both strengths and weaknesses

## **Transparent** (3 principles)
- Sign feedback or provide attribution context
- Acknowledge oversights when pointed out
- Credit all contributors to feedback

## Use Cases

These principles apply to:
- Preprint reviews on platforms like Review Commons, eLife, ASAPbio Crowd
- Public comments on preprint servers
- Author responses to reviewer feedback
- Reader interpretation of preprint feedback

The principles aim to elevate the quality, civility, and usefulness of preprint feedback.
