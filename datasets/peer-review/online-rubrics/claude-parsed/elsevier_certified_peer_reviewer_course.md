# Elsevier Researcher Academy: Certified Peer Reviewer Course

## Overview
The Certified Peer Reviewer course provides a thorough grounding in the principles and practice of refereeing. The course is designed to give those who have not yet reviewed — or who feel they would like additional training — the skills and confidence to accept a request to review.

## Course Structure and Format
The course is delivered via directed self-learning in the form of webinars, podcasts and questionnaires, and can be tackled in your own time. Several core modules must be completed to gain certification, but you can "pick and choose" additional elements to suit your individual learning needs and interests.

## Module Outline (selected)

1. **Introduction to peer review** — fundamentals of the system
2. **Different peer review models** — single/double blind, open, transparent, post-publication
3. **The role of the reviewer** — responsibilities and ethics
4. **How to get involved** — registering with journals, building a profile
5. **Considering review requests** — expertise, time, conflicts
6. **Reading the manuscript critically** — first-pass and detailed reading
7. **Identifying common issues** — methodological, ethical, presentation
8. **Writing the review report** — structure, language, recommendations
9. **Reviewing different article types** — original research, reviews, case studies
10. **Reviewing review articles** — special considerations
11. **Reviewing methods/protocols** — reproducibility focus
12. **Statistical review basics** — what to flag for specialist review
13. **Tone and constructive feedback** — saying difficult things diplomatically
14. **Confidentiality and ethics** — including AI tool restrictions
15. **After the review** — revisions, post-publication, recognition

## Benefits of Certification
- Publicly demonstrate expertise as a referee
- Clear signal to journal editors of valuable reviewer pool addition
- Possible institutional recognition as evidence of continuing professional education

## Important Note
Completion of the Certified Peer Reviewer course does not guarantee invitation or selection as a reviewer.
