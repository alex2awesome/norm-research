# ICML 2024 Reviewer Instructions

## Overview
The instructions emphasize that reviewer commitment is "essential to the success of ICML," with a blog post providing additional context on review considerations.

## Key Dates
- Papers Due: February 1
- Review Period: February 16 - March 14
- Author Rebuttal: March 21 - March 28
- Final Decisions: May 2

## Main Track Review Form

Reviewers assess papers across thirteen components:

**Qualitative Assessments:**
1. "Briefly summarize the paper and its contributions" without critique
2. Evaluate originality, quality, clarity, and significance with broad definitions
3. Pose questions that could "change your opinion, clarify a confusion"
4. Address whether limitations and societal impacts are "adequately addressed"
5. Flag ethical concerns for specialized review

**Numerical Ratings (1-4 scale):**
- Soundness: Technical validity of claims and methodology
- Presentation: Writing quality and contextual framing
- Contribution: Importance and originality for the research area

**Overall Score (1-10 scale):**
Ranges from "Award quality: Technically flawless paper" (10) to "Very Strong Reject" (1), with detailed descriptors at each level.

**Confidence Score (1-5):** Indicates assessment certainty level

## Position Paper Track Review Form

Position papers undergo distinct evaluation focusing on whether they "clearly state a position" on machine learning topics and examining support quality through six numerical ratings and an overall score using similar 1-10 scaling.

Both tracks require Code of Conduct acknowledgment.
