# APA Peer Review Information

## Core Purpose
"Positive peer reviews contribute to increased funding opportunities, academic advancement and a good reputation."

## Key Resources Offered

**Guidance & Training:**
- Best practices for co-reviewing manuscripts with senior mentors
- Step-by-step guidance on conducting peer reviews
- Reviewer mentorship programs across APA journals
- Editor conversations about emerging review formats like registered reports

**Recognition Programs:**
- Web of Science Reviewer Recognition Service enables reviewers to maintain verified records for promotions and funding applications
- Publons service provides instant recognition for peer review contributions

## Standards & Guidelines

APA references several regulatory frameworks:
- **JARS (Journal Article Reporting Standards)** — guidelines for authors, reviewers, and editors to enhance scientific rigor
- **NSF Grant Proposal Guide** — NSF's proposal and award process policies
- **NIH Peer Review Policies** — regulations governing peer review integrity, conflicts of interest, and appeals
- **OMB Information Quality Bulletin** — requires federal agencies to peer review important scientific information

## JARS Highlights

JARS provides reporting standards in psychology covering:
- JARS-Quant (quantitative research)
- JARS-Qual (qualitative research)
- MARS (meta-analytic reporting standards)

## Getting Involved

APA encourages participation through:
- Reviewer opportunity listings
- Career pathway information for becoming a journal editor
- Resources on manuscript review best practices and common reviewer responsibilities
