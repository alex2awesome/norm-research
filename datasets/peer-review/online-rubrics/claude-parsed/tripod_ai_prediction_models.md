# TRIPOD+AI: Transparent Reporting of Prediction Models

## What is TRIPOD?

TRIPOD (Transparent Reporting of a multivariable prediction model for Individual Prognosis Or Diagnosis) is a statement that includes a checklist aimed at improving the reporting of studies developing, validating, or updating a prediction model, whether for diagnostic or prognostic purposes.

## Purpose

The TRIPOD Statement aims to improve the transparency of the reporting of a prediction model study regardless of the study methods used. Reviews have shown that the quality of reporting in published articles describing the development or validation of multivariable prediction models in medicine is poor.

## TRIPOD+AI (2024)

TRIPOD+AI is a **27-item checklist** that aims to harmonise the landscape of prediction model studies and provide transparent reporting regardless of whether regression models or machine learning methods have been used. **TRIPOD+AI supersedes the TRIPOD 2015 checklist, which should no longer be used.**

## TRIPOD+AI Checklist Sections

- **Title and Abstract**
- **Background and Objectives** — including target population, predictors, outcomes, intended use
- **Methods**
   - Source of data
   - Participants
   - Outcome
   - Predictors
   - Sample size
   - Missing data
   - Analytical methods
   - Class imbalance handling (new)
   - Fairness considerations (new)
- **Open Science** (new for AI version)
   - Code availability
   - Data availability
   - Pre-registration
- **Patient & Public Involvement**
- **Results** — Participants, model development, model performance, model updating
- **Discussion** — Limitations, usability of the model in context, implications

## TRIPOD-LLM Extension

TRIPOD-LLM is an extension addressing the unique challenges of LLMs in biomedical and healthcare applications. TRIPOD-LLM provides a comprehensive checklist of 19 main items and various subitems, emphasizing:
- Explainability
- Transparency
- Human oversight
- Task-specific performance reporting

## Reviewer Use

When reviewing studies developing or validating prediction models (regression or ML), reviewers should check completeness against TRIPOD+AI items, especially:
- Outcome definition and timing
- Predictor measurement
- Performance measures and uncertainty
- External validation status
- Calibration assessment
- Decision curve / clinical utility analysis
