# INFORMS Operations Research Reviewer Guidelines

## Peer Review Process

Operations Research uses a **double-anonymous** peer review process where authors' identities are not disclosed to reviewers, and reviewers' identities are not disclosed to authors.

## Confidentiality

- Reviewer comments to editors are strictly confidential
- Comments shared with authors will be made anonymous
- Reviewer names known only to editor-in-chief, area editor, associate editor, and editorial staff
- Author names confidential to reviewers

## Editorial Decision Criteria

Editorial decisions will be based on:
- **Importance** of the contribution
- **Originality** of the approach
- **Clarity** of the writing
- **Validity** of the methods and conclusions
- **Relevance** to the journal's editorial statement

Operations Research will never consider an author's race, gender, sexual orientation, religious beliefs, ethnic origin, citizenship, or political philosophy in making editorial decisions.

## Review Timeline

The goal is to have a **2-cycle review process**.

The Associate Editor receives the manuscript and within two weeks determines if the paper has the potential to be over the bar for OR. The AE may ask reviewers specific questions:
- Asking for proofs to be double-checked
- Comparing methodological novelty to the literature
- Assessing simulations and numerics for robustness

## Ethical Standards

- Authors must not publish manuscripts describing essentially the same research in more than one journal
- Citations to previously published own work required when relevant
- Submitting the same manuscript to multiple journals concurrently constitutes unethical behavior

## Reviewer Best Practices

- Provide constructive criticism
- Justify recommendations with specific examples
- Distinguish major and minor concerns
- Be timely
- Decline if conflict of interest or insufficient expertise
- Maintain confidentiality
- Suggest alternative reviewers if declining
