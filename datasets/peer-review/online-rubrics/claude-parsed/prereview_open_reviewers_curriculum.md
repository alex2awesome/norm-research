# PREreview Open Reviewers — Training Curriculum

A 3-part workshop or 14-week to 8-month program training early-career researchers in constructive, equity-aware peer review.

## Three Foundational Resources (on Zenodo, CC-BY 4.0)

- **Reviewer Guide** — structured framework for writing reviews; includes adapted PLOS Peer Review Center material plus quotes from journal editors and a printable review template.
- **Bias Reflection Guide** — non-judgmental, self-reflective process for any reviewer.
- **Review Assessment Rubric** — 10 statements the evaluator scores plus a written comment, used to *evaluate the quality of a review*.

## Four Core Competencies

1. **Constructive Feedback Skills** — "clear, constructive, and actionable feedback."
2. **Systems Awareness** — how systems of oppression manifest in peer review.
3. **Bias Recognition** — identify and address biases in peer review.
4. **Practical Review Writing** — write a manuscript review for a journal or for a preprint.

## 14-Week Cohort Focus

- Critical evaluation of emerging scientific literature.
- Strategies for constructive review writing.
- Understanding oppression within scientific enterprises.
- Recognizing and challenging internal biases affecting objectivity.
- Peer-to-peer support and mentorship.

All participants publish reviews on PREreview.org under open-access licensing.

## Notable Norm Set

Treats *bias-reflection* as a discrete, audited step — not as something a reviewer naturally does. Treats *review evaluation* as itself a separate task.
