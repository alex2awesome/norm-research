# PLOS ONE Reviewer Guidelines

## Seven Publication Criteria

Manuscripts must satisfy all criteria:

1. **Original Research**: "The study presents the results of original research."

2. **Novelty**: "Results reported have not been published elsewhere"

3. **Technical Rigor**: "Experiments, statistics, and other analyses are performed to a high technical standard and are described in sufficient detail."

4. **Supported Conclusions**: "Conclusions are presented in an appropriate fashion and are supported by the data"

5. **Clear Writing**: "The article is presented in an intelligible fashion and is written in standard English"

6. **Research Ethics**: "The research meets all applicable standards for the ethics of experimentation and research integrity"

7. **Data & Reporting Standards**: "The article adheres to appropriate reporting guidelines and community standards for data availability"

## Assessment Framework

Unlike journals focused on "importance," PLOS ONE evaluates whether papers demonstrate "technical rigor" and meet scientific and ethical standards for the published record.

## Review Structure (Editorial Manager Form)

**Comments to Authors** (required, shared with authors):
- Questions 1-4: Yes/No/I don't know/N/A responses with details
- Question 5: Main review text

**Comments to Editor** (confidential):
- Competing interests declaration
- Collaborator names
- Publication highlight recommendations

## Key Reviewer Responsibilities

- Complete reviews within 10 days
- Declare competing interests
- Maintain confidentiality
- Credit co-reviewers officially
- Consider methodology, data support, literature context, protocol adherence, reproducibility, and clarity
