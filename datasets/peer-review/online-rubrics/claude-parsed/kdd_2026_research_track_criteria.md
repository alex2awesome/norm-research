# KDD 2026 Research Track: Evaluation Criteria

## Primary Evaluation Criteria

Decision-makers will consider:

"A range of factors including technical merit, originality, potential impact, quality of execution, quality of presentation, related work, reproducibility of results, and ethics"

## Specific Quality Dimensions

### Novelty/Originality
Papers must present innovative contributions. The CFP explicitly welcomes "visionary papers on new and emerging topics" and emphasizes that work should avoid being merely comprehensive surveys without novel intellectual contributions.

### Technical Quality
Reviewers assess the soundness of methodology, rigor of experiments, and validity of conclusions. Submissions should demonstrate strong execution and robust reproducibility practices.

### Clarity
Papers must be well-written and clearly presented in English, following ACM formatting standards. The first 8 pages should be self-contained since reviewers aren't obligated to read beyond that.

## Additional Considerations

- **Impact potential:** The significance and applicability of findings
- **Related work:** Proper positioning within existing literature
- **Ethical considerations:** Responsible data use and informed consent where applicable
- **Reproducibility:** Code, data availability, and methodological transparency

## Reviewer Requirements

Candidate reviewers should have demonstrated subject matter expertise, with at least three papers in KDD (Research or ADS track) or similar machine-learning, AI or data science conferences.

## Review Process

The review process involves at least three independent reviewers overseen by an Area Chair, providing comprehensive evaluation across these dimensions. If any author of a submission who is also a reviewer does not carry out the reviewing task properly, no author of that submission will see the reviews during rebuttal.

Any use of generative AI tools during the reviewing process must be disclosed in the review form.
