# 8 Pointers for Producing a Helpful Peer Review (Hurley Write)

Workplace-writing oriented but consistently cited in academic peer-review training material.

## The 8 Pointers

1. Find out what the writer is trying to accomplish and who the reader is before commenting.
2. Read the entire piece before giving advice — questions might be answered later in the work.
3. Get back to your colleague quickly — they may be under deadline.
4. Tell the truth, but be sensitive at the same time.
5. Make initial comments positive and identify the best parts — keeps the review pleasant and gives the colleague a clear example of what's correct.
6. Organize comments in an inverted pyramid — start with big issues (effectiveness), then organization, then word choice / grammar.
7. Give as many specific examples as possible.
8. Tell the writer what to extend and what to cut — keep your peer brief.
