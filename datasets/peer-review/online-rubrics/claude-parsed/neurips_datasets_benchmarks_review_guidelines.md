# NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks Track Review Guidelines

## Key Review Criteria

### For Datasets
Reviewers should assess whether there is sufficient detail on:
- Data collection and organization
- Availability and maintenance
- Ethical and responsible use

Dataset submissions should include:
- Documentation and intended uses
- A URL for reviewer access to the dataset
- A hosting, licensing and maintenance plan

### For Benchmarks
Reviewers should evaluate whether there is sufficient detail to support reproducibility.

## Special Submission Mode

In the Datasets and Benchmarks track, authors can choose to submit single-blind since it may be impossible to share hosted and well-documented datasets entirely anonymously.

## Reviewer Scoring

Reviewers must provide:
- An "overall score"
- A "confidence score"

## Ethical Review

If there are ethical issues with a paper, reviewers should flag it for an ethics review, and should reference the NeurIPS ethics guidelines for guidance on when this is appropriate.

## Reviewer Responsibilities

Reviewers must:
- Keep everything relating to the review process confidential
- Not use ideas, code, or results from submissions in their own work until they become publicly available
- Not talk about or share submissions with anyone without prior approval from the program chairs

## Distinct from Main Track

The Datasets & Benchmarks track has different evaluation criteria than the main NeurIPS track:
- More emphasis on documentation and accessibility
- More emphasis on long-term maintenance and licensing
- More emphasis on ethical sourcing of data
- More emphasis on community impact (will the dataset/benchmark be widely used?)
- Less emphasis on novel ML methods
