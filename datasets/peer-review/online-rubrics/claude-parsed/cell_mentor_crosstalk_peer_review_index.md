# Cell Mentor / CrossTalk Peer Review Blog Series

Cell Press's running blog of editorial guidance on peer review for early-career researchers.

## Posts (representative — accessible from index)

1. **The art of suggesting reviewers** — how to recommend reviewers usefully when submitting.
2. **Learning the ropes of peer review** — emphasizes that peer review is a skill that needs training.
3. **High-quality peer review and where to find it** — practical approaches to thorough scientific evaluation.
4. **Sneak Peek 2.0** — sharing manuscripts publicly while in peer review.
5. **Beyond Reviewer 3: A peer review success story** — reframes the process as collaborative dialogue rather than adversarial.
6. **How I learned to stop worrying about peer review and love collaboration** — how editors balance multiple reviewer perspectives.
7. **What changed? Transparency in a table to highlight the value of peer review** — Cell Press transparency initiatives.
8. **What to do when your paper is out for review** — author-side guidance.
9. **Towards a more transparent and collaborative review process** — Cell Press cross-review pilot, reviewer consultation.
10. **How to be a great reviewer for a research paper** — self-assessment for reviewers.

## Recurring Norms Across the Series

- Review is a *skill* that requires deliberate development.
- Reviewer-to-reviewer cross-consultation is a feature, not a leak.
- Transparency about the process strengthens, not weakens, peer review.
- "Reviewer 2" is a culture problem, not an inevitability.
