# How to Write Constructive Peer Review Comments (Scholastica)

Seven tips journals can give referees to help them deliver constructive feedback.

## The 7 Tips

1. **Avoid decision fatigue** — review early in the day, one review per sitting, never when tired or hungry.
2. **Be aware of conscious and unconscious biases** — focus on facts not feelings, slow decision-making, root feedback in evidence; double-anonymized review helps.
3. **Null or negative results aren't a basis for rejection** — combat positive-results bias; evaluate on quality and methodology, not outcome favorability.
4. **List positives as well as negatives** — overly harsh reviews can cause authors to dismantle working sections; avoid sarcasm.
5. **Give concrete examples and advice** — back opinions with specific manuscript instances; only recommend additions you would feasibly complete yourself.
6. **Don't be afraid to seek support** — early-career reviewers can find mentors or co-reviewers for their first reviews.
7. **Follow the Golden Rule** — "Would I be satisfied receiving this same review for my work?" If no, revise.
