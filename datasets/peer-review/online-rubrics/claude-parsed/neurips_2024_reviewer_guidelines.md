# NeurIPS 2024 Reviewer Guidelines

## Overview
The guidelines establish expectations for reviewers across the 2024 conference cycle, spanning from May through September.

## Key Timeline
- **Bidding**: May 23-30
- **Review Period**: June 10 - July 12
- **Discussion Phase**: August 6-23
- **Decision Making**: August 30 - September 19
- **Author Notifications**: September 25

## Core Responsibilities

**Preparation Requirements:**
Reviewers must acknowledge the Code of Conduct, maintain updated OpenReview profiles, and understand conflict-of-interest policies before beginning assignments.

**Review Quality Standards:**
The guidelines emphasize that "the community depends on your high quality reviews to uphold the scientific quality of NeurIPS." Reviewers should provide substantiated, informative assessments rather than superficial evaluations.

## Review Form Components

Reviewers evaluate papers across these dimensions:

1. **Summary** - Objective paper description
2. **Strengths/Weaknesses** - Assessed on originality, quality, clarity, and significance
3. **Questions for Authors** - Areas where author responses could alter assessment
4. **Limitations** - Adequacy of authors' discussion of constraints
5. **Ethical Concerns** - Flagging for ethics review when appropriate
6. **Numerical Ratings** - Soundness, presentation, and contribution (1-4 scales)
7. **Overall Score** - Range from 1-10 with detailed descriptors
8. **Confidence Level** - Assessment certainty (1-5 scale)

## Essential Practices

The guidelines recommend reviewers: be thoughtful with early-career researchers, remain fair and impartial, provide constructive feedback, avoid vague statements, and respond promptly to deadlines.

## Critical Policies

- Double-blind reviewing maintained at reviewer/AC level
- No sub-reviewers permitted
- Code must run in isolated environments only
- Complete confidentiality required
- No dual submissions allowed
