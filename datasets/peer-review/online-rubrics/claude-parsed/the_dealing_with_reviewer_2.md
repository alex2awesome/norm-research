# Dealing with "Reviewer 2" (Times Higher Education)

A catalog of bad reviewer behaviour and what to do about it.

## Real Examples of Bad Reviewer Comments

- "This work is useless. Nothing is presented."
- "The author writes like a drunken after-dinner speaker."
- Reviews in ALL CAPS stating someone "should never be allowed to publish."
- Comments described as "rude, obnoxious, verging on abusive, and personal."

## Author-Side Responses

- Contact the editor to lodge a complaint about unjust feedback.
- Consider withdrawing the paper if comments are sufficiently damaging.
- Recognize "academics can be brutal and also wrong."
- Share these stories with early-career researchers for perspective.

## Reviewer Norms (Alastair Sloan)

- Stay within your expertise area.
- Actually read the manuscript thoroughly.
- Dedicate adequate time.
- Remain impartial throughout.
- Focus on "facilitating" scholarly conversation rather than adopting "an adversarial role."

## Systemic Solutions

- More transparent peer-review processes (publish reviewer comments publicly).
- Better editorial oversight of comment tone.
- Protect editor time in university workload models.
- Compensate reviewers appropriately.
