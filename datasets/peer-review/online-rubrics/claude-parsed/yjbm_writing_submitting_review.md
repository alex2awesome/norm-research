# Writing and Submitting a Review (YJBM)

Yale Journal of Biology and Medicine's reviewer-side guidance.

## Submission Process

- Reviews are submitted through Scholastica.
- Reviewers create a free Scholastica account; manuscript and supporting docs are kept there.

## Timeline

- Submit reviews 1–2 weeks after receipt of the manuscript.
- If you can't meet the deadline, contact editors *before* it expires so they can reassign or warn the authors.

## Required Components

- A statement of any competing interests.
- A section for the editor with concerns about the manuscript or your ability to review it.
- Comments to authors are sent to the author directly; reviewer identity is kept confidential.

## Conflict-of-Interest Definitions (YJBM-specific)

A reviewer has a competing interest if they:
- Are a direct competitor.
- Dislike a specific author or topic.
- May profit from the publication of a specific manuscript.

Reviewers should state any competing interest in the comments to the editors.
