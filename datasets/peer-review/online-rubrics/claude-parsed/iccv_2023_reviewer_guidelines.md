# ICCV 2023 Reviewer Guidelines

## Core Responsibilities

Reviewers must maintain the conference's high technical standards by evaluating papers fairly and thoroughly.

## Blind Review Requirements

"Author Guidelines have instructed authors to make reasonable efforts to hide their identities" including names and affiliations. Reviewers should similarly maintain anonymity. ArXiv preprints don't violate anonymity policies, but reviewers "DO NOT attempt to discover" author identities through web searches if unknown initially.

## Conflict of Interest Review

Upon assignment, verify no conflicts exist and confirm comfort reviewing each paper.

## Evaluation Standards

Papers should demonstrate technical soundness and field contribution. The guidelines encourage valuing novel approaches even without extensive benchmark testing. "Minor flaws that can be easily corrected should not be a reason to reject a paper."

## Special Assessment Areas

**Reproducibility**: Reviewers may optionally verify submitted code but aren't required to do so.

**Datasets**: If papers claim dataset contributions, public availability by camera-ready deadline is expected.

**Data Attribution**: Papers must properly cite data assets used.

**Ethics**: Authors using personal data or human subjects require institutional review board clearance or ethical principle descriptions.

**Societal Impact**: Discussion encouraged but not mandatory for acceptance.

**Limitations**: Honest limitation discussions should be weighted positively, not penalized.

## Review Quality

Be specific with detailed strength/weakness lists. Support recommendations thoroughly, as discussion helps authors and chairs understand your reasoning. Avoid vague claims—cite specific publications when discussing related work.

## Tone

The tone of reviews is important — harshly written reviews will be resented by authors regardless of accuracy, but it's always possible to word reviews constructively. Belittling, sarcastic, or overly harsh remarks have no place in the reviewing process.
