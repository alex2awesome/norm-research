Recommenders

A recommender manages preprint evaluation. Their role is very similar to that of a journal editor (handling preprints, finding reviewers, collecting reviews, making editorial decisions based on reviews), with the article eventually being recommended (or not) after one or more rounds of reviews. PCI Evolutionary Biology recommenders can recommend up to five articles per year. They are expected to comply with the code of conduct of PCI Evolutionary Biology, are eligible for selection as a member of the Managing Board for a period of two years, and can propose the nomination of new recommenders to the Managing Board. See the PCI Evolutionary Biology Guidelines for Recommenders for more information on the key roles and responsibilities.
