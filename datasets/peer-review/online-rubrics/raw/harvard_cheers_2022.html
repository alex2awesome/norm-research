Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards (CHEERS) Statement

2022

The article introduces the CHEERS 2022 statement, an updated set of guidelines for reporting health economic evaluations. Health economic evaluations compare alternative actions in terms of costs and consequences. The original CHEERS statement, published in 2013, aimed to make these evaluations clear, interpretable, and useful for decision-making by providing a standardized reporting format.

Methods involved reviewing advancements in health economic evaluation methods and the need for inclusive stakeholder engagement, including patients and the public. The CHEERS 2022 statement is designed to apply universally across various health interventions and contexts, such as healthcare, public health, education, and social care.

The new 28-item checklist offers detailed recommendations for reporting economic evaluations, emphasizing clarity and transparency. While primarily intended for researchers, peer reviewers, and journal editors, the checklist also serves as a valuable tool for analysts planning studies and health technology assessment bodies focusing on transparent decision-making. The updated guidance ensures that economic evaluations are more accessible and applicable to a broad range of interventions and contexts.

Source:

Husereau D, Drummond M, Augustovski F et al. Consolidated Health Economic Evaluation Reporting Standards 2022 (CHEERS 2022) Statement: Updated Reporting Guidance for Health Economic Evaluations. Value in Health 2022; 25 (1): 3-9. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jval.2021.11.1351
