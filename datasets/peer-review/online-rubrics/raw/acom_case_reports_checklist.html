CARE Guidelines

                                                                            

A checklist of 13 things to include in a case report.  The CARE guidelines were developed to increase accuracy, transparency, and usefulness of case reports.  Per the CARE guidelines, a case report must include the following:

  1. Title
  2. Keywords
  3. Abstract
  4. Introduction
  5. Patient Information
  6. Clinical Findings
  7. Timeline
  8. Diagnostic Assessment
  9. Therapeutic Intervention
  10. Follow-up and Questions
  11. Discussion
  12. Patient Perspective
  13. Informed Consent

You can download a PDF version of the checklist too.

SCARE Guidelines

                                                                           

Surgical Case Report Guidelines

A checklist of 14 things to include in a surgical case report.  The SCARE guidelines were developed to tailor the above CARE Guidelines to surgical case reports. The SCARE Guidelines include:

  1. Title
  2. Keywords
  3. Abstract
  4. Introduction
  5. Patient Information -- including
    • demographic details
    • presentation
    • past medical and surgical history
    • other relevant history
  6. Clinical Findings
  7. Timeline
  8. Diagnostic Assessment -- including
    • diagnostic methods
    • diagnostic challenges
    • diagnostic reasoning
    • prognostic characteristics
  9. Therapeutic Intervention -- including
    • pre-intervention considerations
    • intervention
    • intervention details
    • who performed the procedure
    • changes
  10. Follow-up and Questions -- including
    • intervention adherence and compliance
    • complications and adverse events
  11. Discussion -- including
    • strengths
    • weaknesses and limitations in approach
    • relevant literature review
    • "take-away" lessons
  12. Patient Perspective
  13. Informed Consent
  14. Additional Information
