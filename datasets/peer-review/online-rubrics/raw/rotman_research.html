Research

Research at Rotman

The institute integrates core humanities scholarship — especially philosophy — into interdisciplinary research in the sciences, social sciences, and more. We unite scholars across disciplines locally and globally to address major scientific, technological, and societal challenges shaped by what we consider to be deep and wicked problems.

Learn more about "deep and wicked" problems

Our Research Goals

  • To foster success for institute researchers at all levels. 
  • To create a research environment that fosters innovative solutions to deep and wicked problems. 
  • To provide distinctive interdisciplinary training for students in the sciences and humanities. 
  • To integrate philosophical and ethical considerations into science, technology, and policy. 
  • To inform and engage the public on deep and wicked problems.

Our Research Priorities

  • Knowledge in the 21st Century 
  • Societal Impact of Emerging Technologies 
  • Challenges in Public Health 
  • Foundations of Physics, Neuroscience, and the Mind

Think Tank Projects

Interdisciplinary Think Tank Projects are projects that address deep and wicked challenges that are supported by our Interdisciplinary Think Tank Program. Think Tank projects foster collaboration across disciplines and sectors, develop innovative research programs, and position teams for major external funding.

Institute Supported Projects

Institute Supported Projects encompass a wide range of interdisciplinary initiatives at various stages of development. The institute provides strategic, organizational, and collaborative support to help research teams refine ideas, build partnerships, and advance research agendas.

Learn more

Research Activities

The institute supports various research activities, including reading groups, working groups, and research meetings and events. These initiatives bring together scholars and partners from diverse disciplines and sectors to explore shared questions, exchange ideas, and develop collaborative research projects.
