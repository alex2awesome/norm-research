Peer Review Training Opportunities

  • Certified Peer Reviewer Course This link opens in a new window
    The Elsevier Researcher Academy's Certified Peer Reviewer course is designed to give you the skills and confidence needed to accept a request to peer review. Free access upon registration.
  • Nature MasterClass: Focus on Peer Review This link opens in a new window
    For students and researchers who are new to peer review or wish to refresh their skills. Free to access upon registration.
  • Wiley Peer Review Training This link opens in a new window
    If you would like to get involved in peer review or are looking for tips on how to deliver a great review, Wiley has many resources for you including a 3-part introductory video series on the basics of peer review. Free.
