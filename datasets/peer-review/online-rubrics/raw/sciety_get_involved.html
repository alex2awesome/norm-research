Get involved

At Sciety, we’re building a community around the peer evaluation of preprints, and we want you to be a part of it. Whether you’re a researcher, reviewer, and/or someone passionate about improving the way scientific discoveries are shared, there are multiple ways to get involved. Here’s how you can contribute to the future of scholarly communication with Sciety:

1. Highlight Preprints You Care About

You can contribute directly by sharing preprints that have caught your attention. Whether it’s a groundbreaking study or a topic you're passionate about, highlighting preprints on social media or by curating a list on Sciety helps amplify the the work, bringing attention to important research and encouraging further discussion within the community.

Create a list on Sciety

or tag #PreprintHighlights when you share preprints on social media

1. Participate In UX Research Calls

We’re constantly working to improve Sciety, and your feedback is invaluable. By joining a call, you can share your experiences, tell us what’s working, and help shape new features. These interviews are a great way to make your voice heard and directly influence the development of our platform. Where possible we will offer compensation for your time.

Sign up for a call

2. Community Member Spotlights

We’re proud to have a diverse and growing community, and we love highlighting the people who make Sciety special. Do you want to share your story and how you’re using Sciety in your work? Whether you’re part of a preprint review community or a dedicated reader, you could be our next featured member in our community member spotlight series.

Want to be featured? Let us know

3. Join Group Networking Calls

Our networking calls are a great way to meet others who are passionate about preprints and peer evaluation. These informal calls offer a chance to discuss best practices, share insights, and learn from other community members. Whether you’re already an evaluating community partner or just intrigued to learn from those that are already evaluating preprints, these calls are an excellent opportunity to connect and collaborate.

Join our next call

4. Follow Us on Social Media

Keep up to date with the latest news, discussions, and preprint highlights by following Sciety on social media. We regularly post updates, share interesting articles, and feature community content. This is the quickest way to stay in the loop and engage with others in the field.

Follow us on Twitter, Mastodon, LinkedIn, or Bluesky

5. Subscribe to Our Newsletter

Want Sciety news and insights delivered straight to your inbox? By subscribing to our newsletter, you’ll receive updates on new features, community events, and the latest in preprint evaluation. It’s the easiest way to stay informed about what’s happening at Sciety and beyond.

Subscribe

Join us in elevating community-driven peer evaluations and making preprints easier to navigate for everyone.
