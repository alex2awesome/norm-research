Submit research to the eLife Model

Under the eLife Model, we no longer make accept/reject decisions after peer review. Instead, every preprint sent for peer review is published on the eLife website as a Reviewed Preprint.

  • Please use this submission system for new submissions to be considered for publication under the eLife Model.
  • All submissions invited for peer review will be published as a Reviewed Preprint that includes an eLife Assessment, public reviews, and a response from the authors (if available). There will be no accept/reject decision after review.
  • Please use this submission system to submit revisions of Reviewed Preprints. The manuscript will be re-reviewed and the Reviewed Preprint, eLife Assessment and public reviews will be updated.
  • Please use our legacy submission system for new submissions of Feature Articles and Scientific Correspondence, and for resubmissions of research papers submitted before January 31, 2023.
