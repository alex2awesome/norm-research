About PCI Evolutionary Biology

What is PCI Evolutionary Biology​?

PCI Evolutionary Biology is one of PCI's thematic peer-review platforms for evaluating and recommending preprints in the field of evolutionary biology. 

Following the authors' submission, PCI Evolutionary Biology evaluates preprints through rigorous peer review. In case of positive evaluation, PCI Evolutionary Biology validates and publicly recommends those peer-reviewed preprints, providing citable documentation for the preprint's methodological rigour and the legitimacy of its results and conclusions. The entire process is free of charge for authors and readers. 

Authors can transfer their PCI-recommended article for publication, also free of charge, to the diamond open-access Peer Community Journal or submit it to a PCI-friendly journal or to another journal.

 

Advantages/benefits of using PCI

  • PCI reviews offer valuable feedback to enhance your preprint's quality.

If your preprint is positively evaluated and recommended:

  • PCI transforms your preprint into a validated research article.
  • PCI publishes a public recommendation, creating a citable document that attests to the methodological rigour of the study and the validity of its results and conclusions.
  • You may publish your recommended preprint in the diamond open-access, indexed Peer Community Journal, supported by many research institutions, or in 50 category 1 PCI-friendly journals.
  • You can also use the PCI evaluation when submitting to any journal.
  • Your preprint is tagged as peer-reviewed by bioRxiv, medRxiv, and various open repositories.
  • Your preprint becomes indexed and discoverable through Europe PMC, Sciety, and Early Evidence-Based.

Editorial policy​

Quality Standards

​PCI Evolutionary Biology recommends only preprints that meet high scientific, methodological, and ethical standards regardless of impact. To this end, ​PCI Evolutionary Biology: 

  • Requires authors to share data, code, and analysis scripts with reviewers upon submission and with readers after recommendation.
  • Expects clear justification of sample sizes in empirical studies.
  • Welcomes replications, preregistered research, and negative results.
  • Does not accept submissions with financial conflicts of interest; other conflicts must be minimal and declared.
  • Ensures, as much as possible, that reviewers and recommenders are free from conflicts of interest related to the study or its authors.

Only preprints that attract the interest of at least one recommender (similar to an associate editor) are sent for peer review. Recommenders select work that stands out (to them) for its context, questions, methods, or findings. Submitted preprints that do not attract the interest of a recommender within a period of 20 days are not considered for evaluation.

Scope​​​

PCI Evolutionary Biology will evaluate preprints dealing with all fields of Evolutionary Biology and in particular with these subjects: Adaptation, Behavior & Social Evolution, Bioinformatics & Computational Biology, Evo-Devo, Evolutionary Applications, Evolutionary Dynamics, Evolutionary Ecology, Evolutionary Epidemiology, Evolutionary Theory, Experimental Evolution, Expression Studies, Genetic conflicts, Genome Evolution, Genotype-Phenotype, Human Evolution, Hybridization / Introgression, Life History, Macroevolution, Molecular Evolution, Morphological Evolution, Non Genetic Inheritance, Paleontology, Phenotypic Plasticity, Phylogenetics / Phylogenomics, Phylogeography & Biogeography, Population Genetics / Genomics, Quantitative Genetics, Reproduction and Sex, Sexual Selection, Speciation, Species interactions, Systematics / Taxonomy. 

The preprints submitted to ​PCI Evolutionary Biology may describe empirical or theoretical studies and be based on observations from experiments or nature, or previously acquired data. They may also report the results of verbal, computer, or mathematical models. Studies of methodologies are also appreciated. Perspectives, reviews and opinions and comments on previously published articles are also welcome.

Ethics​

Peer Community In subscribes to the principles of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and has its own code of conduct that its users must comply with.

 Inclusiveness and equity

PCI is attentive to equity and inclusion at all stages of the scientific article evaluation process. PCI focuses on bringing more people who are underrepresented in academia among authors submitting to PCI, and on recruiting reviewers, recommenders, and Managing Board members. Underrepresentation is linked to many factors, including career stage, gender, and geography.

Specific recommendations are made to reviewers, recommenders, and Managing Board members to increase equity and inclusiveness in their respective tasks.

Tools to increase equity and inclusiveness:

  • Possibility to submit articles anonymously
  • Transparency in the evaluation of articles
  • Managing Board members take into account underrepresentation in academia when appointing new recommenders
  • Template messages to recommenders and reviewers include recommendations about equity and inclusiveness 
  • Possibility to review anonymously

PCI is a signatory of the Joint Statement of Principles of the Coalition for Diversity and Inclusion in Scholarly Communication (C4DISC)​​​​.
