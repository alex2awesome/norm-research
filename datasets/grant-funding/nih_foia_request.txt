Re: Request under 5 U.S.C. § 552 for de-identified NIH grant impact/percentile scores linked to APPLICATION_ID for funded research project grants, FY1985–FY2024

To Whom It May Concern:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I respectfully request the following agency records in electronic form.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
RECORDS REQUESTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------

For every NIH research project grant (activity codes R01, R21, R03, R15, P01, U01) that was FUNDED in fiscal years 1985 through 2024, a tabular dataset containing the following fields for each funded application:

  1. APPLICATION_ID          (as appears in the NIH RePORTER Project File)
  2. CORE_PROJECT_NUM        (as appears in RePORTER)
  3. FY                      (fiscal year of award)
  4. ACTIVITY                (mechanism code, e.g. R01, R21)
  5. STUDY_SECTION           (review group code)
  6. OVERALL_IMPACT_SCORE    (numerical; 10–90 post-2009 scale, 100–500 pre-2009 priority score)
  7. PERCENTILE              (where assigned)
  8. SUFFIX                  (A0/A1/A2 resubmission indicator; already public in RePORTER)

I am NOT requesting:
  - PI names, PI IDs, or any personally identifying information
  - Institutional names or addresses
  - Summary statement narrative text
  - Criterion scores (Significance, Investigators, Innovation, Approach, Environment)
  - Reviewer identities or reviewer-level scores
  - Any information about UNFUNDED applications
  - Any proprietary scientific or budgetary detail from the proposal text

Every record requested is already linked to a publicly disclosed funded grant in the NIH RePORTER system. This request seeks only the numeric overall review outcome that determined the funding decision for applications NIH has already made public.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
PRECEDENT FOR RELEASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Individual-level funded-grant percentile scores have previously been released to external academic researchers in a form materially identical to what is requested here. Specifically, the dataset of 102,740 funded R01 grants from FY1980–FY2008, each linked to its percentile score, that was used in:

  - Li, D. & Agha, L. (2015). "Big names or big ideas: Do peer-review panels select the best science proposals?" Science, 348(6233), 434–438.
  - Fang, F. C., Bowen, A., & Casadevall, A. (2016). "NIH peer review percentile scores are poorly predictive of grant productivity." eLife, 5, e13323. DOI: 10.7554/eLife.13323.

The present request covers records of the same type and character as those already disclosed to the above researchers, extended forward through FY2024.
