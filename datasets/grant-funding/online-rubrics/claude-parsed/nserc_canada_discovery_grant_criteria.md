# NSERC Discovery Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Three Weighted Evaluation Criteria

The current evaluation framework uses three weighted criteria:

### 1. Excellence of the Researcher
- Knowledge, expertise, and experience the researcher brings to the proposed program of research
- Quality of contributions to and impact on the proposed and other areas of research
- Importance of contributions
- Sustained commitment to research

### 2. Merit of the Proposal
- Originality and innovation
- Significance of the expected contributions to research
- Clarity and scope of objectives
- Clarity and appropriateness of the methodology
- Feasibility
- Appropriateness of the budget

### 3. Contribution to Training of Highly Qualified Personnel (HQP)
- Quality and impact of past contributions to training of HQP
- Appropriateness of the proposed strategy and methodology for training HQP
- Expected contribution to training of HQP

## Selection Committee Structure
- Applications assigned to one of **twelve Evaluation Groups (EGs)**
- Staffed by peer reviewers from the Canadian academic community
- Every year, NSERC engages experts from universities, government, and industry
- National and international experts donate considerable time on a voluntary basis

## Review Process
- Evaluation Groups, each covering a specific discipline, assess applications against the three Discovery Grants selection criteria
- At the Notice of Intent (NOI) stage, applicants suggest which Evaluation Group is most appropriate
- Final decision of EG assignment is made by NSERC

## Merit Indicators (Bins)

Each criterion is rated on a 6-point scale with detailed indicators:
- **Exceptional** - top researchers globally
- **Outstanding** - very well-established, internationally recognized
- **Very Strong** - well-established with strong reputation
- **Strong** - solid record of contributions
- **Moderate** - average for stage and discipline
- **Insufficient** - below the standard for funding

## Funding Outcome
Each application receives a "bin assignment" (1-6 scale across each criterion), and these are mapped to funding levels and durations (typically 5 years).

## EDI Considerations
NSERC has policies on Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion that are integrated into the review process. Researchers can describe career interruptions, special circumstances, and EDI activities related to their research and HQP training.
