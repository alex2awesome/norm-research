# Cornell OSP — Grant Writing Themes

Cornell University OSP provides grant writing resources across Cornell's broad research portfolio (Ithaca campus + Weill Cornell Medicine in NYC).

## Cornell Funder Mix

### Federal
- NIH (Weill Cornell + Ithaca)
- NSF (engineering, CS, agriculture, basic sciences)
- USDA (College of Agriculture and Life Sciences strong)
- DOE
- NASA (Cornell strong in astronomy/aerospace)

### Foundation
- HHMI Investigators
- Atkinson Center for Sustainability
- Various foundation grants

## Cornell-Specific Themes

### Land-Grant + Private Hybrid
- Statutory colleges (Agriculture, Veterinary Medicine, ILR, Human Ecology) have land-grant funding obligations
- Endowed colleges (Arts and Sciences, Engineering, etc.) operate as private
- Differs in some grant administration aspects

### Cross-Campus Collaboration
- Ithaca + Weill Cornell collaborations encouraged
- Cross-disciplinary centers (Atkinson, Cornell Tech, etc.)

### Cornell Tech (NYC)
- Engineering and CS focus
- Industry partnerships
- Different funding model and culture

## Cornell OSP Implicit Rubric

### What Wins
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Cornell-specific facilities leveraged
6. Cross-campus or cross-disciplinary collaborations where genuine
7. Stakeholder/extension engagement (USDA-relevant)
8. Industry partnerships (Cornell Tech, engineering)
9. Realistic budget with Cornell indirect rate
10. Compliance addressed

### What OSP Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors
2. Compliance gaps (especially for Weill Cornell with NYC IRB)
3. Subaward complications (Ithaca-NYC subawards)
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections
