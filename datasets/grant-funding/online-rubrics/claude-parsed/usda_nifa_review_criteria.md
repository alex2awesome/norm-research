# USDA NIFA - Peer Review Process and Criteria

## NIFA Grant Review Process Overview
NIFA reviews all proposals accepted in individual competitive programs through the peer review process. Specific details on the panel meeting, review format, and evaluation criteria vary among programs.

## Standard Adjectival Rating Scale
Evaluation criteria are aligned to scores of:
- **Excellent**
- **Very Good**
- **Good**
- **Fair**
- **Poor**

## AFRI (Agriculture and Food Research Initiative) Review Criteria

For the largest NIFA program (AFRI), proposals are evaluated for:

### 1. Scientific Merit
- Originality, novelty, and significance of the proposed research
- Conceptual adequacy and clarity of the project
- Potential for scientific advancement of the field
- Appropriateness of the experimental design and methodology

### 2. Qualifications of Project Personnel
- Qualifications, training, and experience of the PI and team
- Adequacy of facilities and resources
- Time committed to the project
- Past productivity

### 3. Relevance to Program Priorities
- Importance of the topic for agriculture
- Alignment with USDA mission and program priorities
- Contribution to mission needs

### 4. Project Plan/Approach
- Soundness of the project plan
- Feasibility of timeline
- Risk mitigation
- Stakeholder engagement (where relevant)

### 5. Outcomes and Impact
- Expected products and outputs
- Anticipated outcomes for stakeholders
- Long-term impact on agricultural systems

## Application Requirements
The Request for Applications (RFA) includes:
- Program purpose and legislative mandates
- Award types
- Eligibility requirements
- Evaluation criteria
- Submission instructions
- Program goals and funding priorities
- Proposal submission deadlines
- Application submission instructions

## Panel Review Process
Following the evaluation and initial ranking of each proposal:
- A "panel summary" document is written by a panel member reflecting the panel consensus
- Details the salient points of the panel's assessment of strengths and weaknesses
- Contains a synthesis comments section describing areas for improvement
