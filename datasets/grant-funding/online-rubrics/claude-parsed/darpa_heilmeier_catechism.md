# The Heilmeier Catechism

The eight questions that researchers and engineers answer to understand project context, costs, and effort:

1. **What are you trying to do? Articulate your objectives using absolutely no jargon.**

2. **How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?**

3. **What is new in your approach and why do you think it will be successful?**

4. **Who cares? If you are successful, what difference will it make?**

5. **What are the risks?**

6. **How much will it cost?**

7. **How long will it take?**

8. **What are the mid-term and final "exams" to check for success?**

---

**Background:** Former DARPA director George H. Heilmeier created this framework during his tenure from 1975-1977. The questionnaire emerged from his extensive technology research background, including pioneering liquid crystal display work at RCA. Today, innovative research organizations worldwide employ this evaluation method to assess high-risk innovation projects, and the Catechism is integrated into review criteria for many other funding agencies.
