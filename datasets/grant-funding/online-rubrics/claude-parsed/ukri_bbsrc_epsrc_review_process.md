# UKRI BBSRC and EPSRC - Grant Review Criteria

## Universal UKRI Scoring (1-6 Scale)
- **6 - Exceptional**: outstanding, addresses all criteria at exceptional level
- **5 - Excellent**: very high quality, very minor weaknesses
- **4 - Good**: addresses criteria well with some weaknesses
- **3 - Fair**: some merit but significant weaknesses
- **2 - Weak**: flaws that prevent funding
- **1 - Poor**: unsuitable for funding

## BBSRC Review Process

### Process Steps
1. Proposals considered by **three or more peer reviewers**
2. Applicants provide a response (PI response) to peer reviewer comments
3. Proposals, reviews, and PI responses considered by the assessment panel
4. Panel provides a rank-ordered list of proposals in order of funding priority
5. Panel recommendations considered internally by BBSRC
6. Final decision on awards to be supported

### BBSRC Standard Research Grant Criteria
- Importance/significance of the research
- Quality of the research proposal
- Track record of investigators
- Research environment
- Strategic alignment with BBSRC priorities
- Value for money
- Ethics and responsible research and innovation considerations

## EPSRC Review Process

### Process Steps
1. **At least three peers** review your application independently
2. Reviewed against published criteria (areas of assessment) for the funding opportunity
3. If application gains enough support, it goes to the most appropriate prioritisation panel
4. Panel scores it against assessment criteria and ranks it alongside other applications

### EPSRC Standard Research Grant Criteria
- Quality (importance, originality, ambition)
- Importance and timeliness
- Applicant capability
- Resources and management
- National importance
- Ethical considerations

### Note on Panel Function
EPSRC and BBSRC panels act as **moderating panels** and do not re-review proposals — they synthesize peer reviews and rank proposals.

## UKRI Eight Core Principles of Assessment and Decision Making

1. Excellence
2. Diversity
3. Mission alignment
4. Trust
5. Transparency
6. Impartiality
7. Efficiency
8. Continuous improvement

## Common Across UKRI Councils
- Three or more peer reviewers per proposal
- PI response opportunity
- Panel moderation rather than re-review
- Adjectival/numerical scoring
- Strategic alignment considered
- Equality, diversity, and inclusion policies applied
- Reviewers cannot use generative AI for assessments
