# Wikipedia — Grant Writing Article Themes

The Wikipedia "Grant writing" article aggregates conventional wisdom on grant writing across academic, nonprofit, and government sectors.

## Wikipedia's Definition and Scope

### Definition
- The practice of writing proposals to obtain financial support from foundations, government, corporations, and other funders
- Distinct from fundraising (which includes donor cultivation)
- Distinct from grant management (which is post-award administration)

### Scope
- Academic research grants (NIH, NSF, etc.)
- Foundation grants
- Government grants (federal, state, local)
- Nonprofit organizational grants
- Corporate giving

## Common Components Wikipedia Lists

### Cover Letter / Letter of Intent
- Introduction of organization or PI
- Statement of need
- Brief project description
- Amount requested

### Project Summary / Abstract
- 1-page overview
- Problem, solution, impact
- Often the most-read section

### Statement of Need
- Documents the problem
- Cites credible data
- Establishes urgency
- Connects to funder priorities

### Project Description
- Goals and objectives
- Methodology
- Timeline
- Personnel
- Expected outcomes

### Budget
- Personnel
- Direct costs
- Indirect costs (where allowed)
- Justification

### Evaluation Plan
- Outcome measures
- Data collection
- Reporting

### Supporting Documents
- Letters of support
- Compliance approvals
- Organizational documents
- CVs

## Wikipedia's Themes on Grant Writing as a Profession

### Grant Writing as a Career
- Professional grant writers (especially for nonprofits)
- Grant writing certifications (Grant Professionals Association)
- Hourly or per-project compensation
- Some on staff at nonprofits/universities

### Skills Required
- Research and analysis
- Writing and editing
- Project management
- Knowledge of funder landscape
- Attention to detail

### Distinguishing Good from Bad Grant Writing
- Clear, persuasive, specific
- Aligned with funder
- Realistic budget and timeline
- Strong evaluation plan
- Professional presentation

## Funder Types Wikipedia Distinguishes

### Government
- Federal, state, local
- Often very specific RFAs
- Strict format requirements
- Multi-stage review

### Foundations
- Private and community foundations
- Annual report establishes priorities
- Often require letter of inquiry first
- Program officer engagement valued

### Corporations
- Cause marketing alignment
- Strategic philanthropy
- Often local focus
- Sometimes employee-engagement linked

### Crowdfunding
- Public donation platforms
- Different conventions (storytelling, video)
- Smaller award sizes typically
- Direct community engagement

## Wikipedia's Implicit Rubric

### What Wins Across Funder Types
1. Clear problem statement with data
2. Alignment with funder's priorities
3. Specific goals and objectives
4. Realistic methodology
5. Budget aligned with scope
6. Evaluation plan with metrics
7. Letters of support
8. Professional presentation
9. Compliance addressed
10. Submission on time

### Common Pitfalls
1. Vague problem statement
2. Mismatched funder priorities
3. Unrealistic budget
4. No evaluation plan
5. Missing required documents
6. Format violations
7. Late submission
8. Generic letters of support

## Sectoral Conventions Wikipedia Notes

### Academic Research
- Specific Aims / Project Description
- Hypothesis-driven (sciences)
- Argument-driven (humanities)
- Investigator productivity central
- Institutional environment

### Nonprofit
- Statement of Need with community data
- Goals, objectives, activities
- Logic models
- Sustainability plan
- Organizational capacity

### Government
- Strict format requirements
- Compliance-heavy
- Multi-stage review
- Formal evaluation criteria

### Foundation
- Alignment with foundation priorities
- Often informal pre-engagement
- Varies widely in formality
- Trust relationships matter
