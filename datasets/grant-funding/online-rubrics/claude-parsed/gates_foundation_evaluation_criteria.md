# Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation - Grant Evaluation Criteria

## Selection Criteria
The foundation prioritizes initiatives that:
- Address significant health challenges faced by vulnerable populations
- Target diseases with high morbidity and mortality rates
- Particularly focus on diseases affecting children and women in low-income countries
- Demonstrate innovation and scalability

## Evaluation Process

### Initial Screening
- Proposals undergo screening to ensure alignment with the foundation's strategic priorities

### Detailed Evaluation
A team of experts assesses:
- Scientific validity
- Feasibility
- Potential for impact

### Evidence-Based Metrics
The foundation uses evidence-based metrics to measure potential outcomes, emphasizing data in decision-making. Proposals must include:
- Robust plans for monitoring and evaluation
- Clear objectives
- Key performance indicators
- Outlined methods for data collection and analysis

## Post-Award Monitoring
From the earliest phases of the grantmaking process, the foundation works with grantees to define:
- Overall results
- Data needed to measure progress
- How to evaluate that data

The foundation employs independent evaluators to conduct assessments of funded projects at various stages, providing objective analysis to ensure accountability and transparency.

## Grand Challenges Initiative
For the Grand Challenges programs, the foundation seeks bold ideas to specific stated problems with high innovation, applying:
- Direct relevance to defined Grand Challenges goals
- Novel scientific approach
- Measurable milestones within short timeframes (Phase I)
- Advancement potential for follow-on Phase II funding

## Funding Restrictions
- Does not fund tuition or K-12 education
- Does not provide direct unrestricted funding for individuals
- Grants are designated for charitable and/or educational purposes
