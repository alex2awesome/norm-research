# ERC Advanced Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Primary Evaluation Criterion: Excellence
Proposals are evaluated by selected international peer reviewers who assess them on the basis of excellence as the sole criterion.

## Two Main Elements of Evaluation
At each evaluation step, each proposal is evaluated and marked for each of the two main elements of the proposal:

### 1. The Research Project
- Ground-breaking nature
- Ambition
- Feasibility

### 2. The Principal Investigator
- Intellectual capacity
- Creativity
- Commitment

ERC panels primarily evaluate the ground-breaking nature, ambition, and feasibility of the research project. They also evaluate the intellectual capacity, creativity, and commitment of the applicant principal investigator, with a focus on the extent to which the applicant has the excellence and required scientific expertise to successfully execute the proposed project.

## Principal Investigator Profile Assessment

Applicants must be exceptional leaders in terms of originality and significance of research with an excellent track record and achievements during the last 10 years (this time window can be extended in case of eligible career breaks). The track record requirements include:
- Significant research output (e.g., publications, monographs, books)
- Notable invitations to international conferences as keynote speaker
- Significant inventions, patents
- Recognition through major prizes and awards
- Major leadership/membership of established research institutions

## Evaluation Process and Outcomes

At the end of step 1 of the evaluation, applicants are informed that their proposal:
- **[A]** is of sufficient quality to pass to step 2 of the evaluation
- **[B]** is of high quality but not sufficient to pass to step 2 of the evaluation
- **[C]** is not of sufficient quality to pass to step 2 of the evaluation
