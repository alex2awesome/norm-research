# NCI Cancer Moonshot - Grant Evaluation Criteria

## Overview
Cancer Moonshot funding supports research aimed at accelerating progress against cancer. Applications evaluated by Scientific Review Groups convened by NCI, in accordance with NIH peer review policy and procedures.

## Four Core Features Across All Cancer Moonshot Funding

1. **Strong commitment to collaborative research**
2. **Embrace of open access publications**
3. **Robust policy of complete data sharing**
4. **Intense focus on the elimination of cancer health disparities**

## Standard Review Process
- All applications receive a written critique as part of scientific peer review
- Applications may undergo selection process where only those deemed highest scientific and technical merit (generally top half) discussed and assigned an overall impact score

## Specialized Criteria

### Diversity and Inclusion
The NCI measures and evaluates efforts to achieve diversity within NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Centers. Four aims:
1. Having the right infrastructure
2. Growing the pipelines of future clinicians and researchers
3. Recruiting individuals from under-represented populations
4. Accountability

### Public Access and Data Sharing (PADS) Policy
- Applicable to all Cancer Moonshot funding applications submitted on or after October 1, 2017
- Requires all Cancer Moonshot grant applicants to submit a PADS plan
- Describes how they will meet goals for immediate public release of published research results and data

## Specific Cancer Moonshot Initiatives

### Pediatric Cancer Initiative
- DCI / RACE for Children Act
- Focus on pediatric oncology innovations

### CANCER Moonshot Scholars Diversity Program (CMSDP)
- R01 mechanism
- Support for early-stage investigators from underrepresented groups
- Cancer-relevant research

### Other Initiatives
- Drug Resistance and Sensitivity Network
- Adult Immunotherapy Network
- ATOM Initiative
- Cancer Immunotherapy Translational Network
- Patient-Derived Models Repository

## Special Review Considerations
- Translational potential to clinic
- Patient and community engagement
- Health equity considerations
- Multi-institutional collaboration
- Use of existing Moonshot networks/resources
