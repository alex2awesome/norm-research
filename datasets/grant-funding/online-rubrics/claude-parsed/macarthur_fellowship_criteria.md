# MacArthur Fellowship - Selection Criteria

## Three Core Selection Criteria

The MacArthur Fellowship is awarded to individuals based on three criteria:

1. **Exceptional Creativity** - Extraordinary originality and dedication in their creative pursuits

2. **Promise for Important Future Advances** - Based on a track record of significant accomplishment

3. **Potential for the Fellowship to Facilitate Subsequent Creative Work** - Investment-like nature; not a reward for past work

## Personal Qualities Sought

The foundation seeks to support people who express:
- Ability to transcend traditional boundaries
- Willingness to take risks
- Persistence in the face of personal and conceptual obstacles
- Capacity to synthesize disparate ideas and approaches
- Marked capacity for self-direction

## Important Distinction
The fellowship is NOT a reward for past accomplishments but rather an investment in a person's originality, insight, and potential, based on a track record of significant accomplishments.

## Selection Process
- Individuals **cannot apply** for this award
- Must be nominated by individuals identified through a process of broad and diverse outreach
- Selectors consider each nominee with respect to the program's selection criteria based on:
  - The nomination letter(s)
  - Numerous evaluations from subject matter experts collected by the program Staff
  - Examples of the nominee's work
- Typically 20 to 30 Fellows are selected each year
- Represents a broad range of fields and endeavors

## Award Details
- $800,000 stipend (as of 2023)
- Paid out over 5 years in equal quarterly installments
- No restrictions on how the money is used
- No requirement for reports or specific outcomes
