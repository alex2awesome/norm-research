# IARPA - Proposal Evaluation Criteria

## Mission and Approach
IARPA (Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity) invests in high-risk, high-payoff research that has the potential to provide the United States with overwhelming intelligence advantage over future adversaries. IARPA is the IC's research counterpart to DARPA.

## Heilmeier Catechism Adoption
IARPA adheres to the **Heilmeier Catechism**, named after an early DARPA Director. These guidelines base funding decisions upon answering questions that define objectives using absolutely no jargon:
1. What are you trying to do?
2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
3. What's new in your approach and why do you think it will be successful?
4. Who cares?
5. If you're successful, what difference will it make?
6. What are the risks?
7. How much will it cost?
8. How long will it take?

## Evaluation Process Features

### Independent Test and Evaluation (T&E)
IARPA has an independent testing and evaluation (T&E) team that confirms the results reported by the performers to ensure that they are delivering products that faithfully and reproducibly meet program metrics.

### Multiple Performers per Goal
IARPA programs normally have multiple performers addressing each technical goal, ensuring that:
- Multiple solutions are developed
- Solutions can be tested against one another
- Best approaches emerge through competition
- Reduces risk of single-point failure

### Open Research Standard
The majority of IARPA projects are conducted through open, unclassified research, with performers encouraged to publish research results in open, peer-reviewed literature that can be referenced and examined by the broader research community.

### Conflict of Interest Procedures
Panel members are required to:
- Declare potential conflicts of interest
- Sign non-disclosure agreements prior to gaining access to technical proposals

## Standard Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Scientific and Technical Merit
- Innovation and approach
- Likelihood of meeting metrics
- Soundness of methodology

### 2. Programmatic Relevance
- Direct relevance to BAA topic
- Contribution to IARPA mission
- Impact on intelligence community problems

### 3. Cost Realism
- Reasonableness of proposed budget
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Resource allocation

### 4. Team Capability
- Past performance
- Expertise of proposed team
- Available facilities and infrastructure

### 5. Test and Evaluation Plan
- Quantifiable milestones
- Metrics that can be independently verified
- Realistic phase planning

## Notable IARPA Programs (Past and Current)
- ACE - Aggregative Contingent Estimation (forecasting)
- FUSE - Foresight and Understanding from Scientific Exposition
- REASON - Reasoning support tools
- HFC - Hybrid Forecasting Competition
- TrojAI - Detect AI trojans
- BETTER - Better extraction from text

## Typical Program Structure
- Multi-phase (Phase 1, 2, optional Phase 3)
- Phase-gated with go/no-go milestones
- Quarterly progress reports
- Major reviews at phase boundaries
- Final demonstration with measurable outcomes
