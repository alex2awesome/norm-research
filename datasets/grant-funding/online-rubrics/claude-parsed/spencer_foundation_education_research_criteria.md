# Spencer Foundation - Research Grants on Education Criteria

## Foundation Goals
The Spencer Foundation's goal for all research grants is to support:
- Rigorous, intellectually ambitious and technically sound research
- Relevant to the most pressing questions and compelling opportunities in education
- Creative and open-minded scholarship
- Deep inquiry
- Robust questions related to education
- Scholarship that develops new foundational knowledge with potential lasting impact on policy-making, practice, or educational discourse

## Six Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Significance of the Project
Reviewers evaluate:
- Centrality of education in the research
- Importance of the topic to its field
- Quality of the research question(s)
- Direction of inquiry

### 2. Connection to Research and Theory
Reviewers evaluate:
- Adequacy of the description of how other researchers have treated the same topic
- How well the proposal responds to prior work and theory
- Theoretical framework

### 3. Research Design
Reviewers evaluate:
- Overall quality, sophistication, and appropriateness of the research design
- Alignment with the research question(s)
- Alignment with conceptual framing

### 4. Budget and Timeline
Reviewers evaluate:
- Adequacy of the budget
- Adequacy of the timeline
- Strength of proposal within their funding tier

### 5. Project Team
Reviewers comment on:
- Potential of the investigator(s) to complete the study as described
- Potential to share the results or other findings

### 6. Dissemination Plans
Reviewers evaluate proposed plans for sharing the results, which may include:
- Traditional submissions to conferences or publications
- Other efforts aimed at influencing research, policy, practice, or public discourse
- Stakeholder engagement
- Open access and data sharing

## Spencer Grant Programs

### Small Research Grants ($50K)
- For exploratory or pilot work
- Single PI typically
- 1-2 year timeline

### Large Research Grants (up to $500K)
- For substantive research projects
- Multi-year (typically 3-5 years)
- Single PI or team
- 3 funding cycles per year

### Vision Grants
- Large strategic grants
- For transformative work in education

### Racial Equity Research Grants
- Specifically for racial equity research in education
- Distinct call

### Lyle Spencer Research Awards
- Distinguished mid-career scholar awards
- Up to $1 million

## Application Process
- Letter of Intent (LOI) typically required
- Selected applicants invited to submit full proposal
- External peer review
- Spencer Foundation board approval

## Notable Features
- Strong emphasis on intellectual ambition over incremental work
- Welcomes diverse methodologies (qualitative, quantitative, mixed)
- Field of education broadly construed
- Prioritizes research that bridges scholarship and practice/policy
