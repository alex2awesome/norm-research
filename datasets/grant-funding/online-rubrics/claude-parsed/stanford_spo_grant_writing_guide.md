## Stanford SPO Process

### Pre-Award Services
- Budget development with PI
- Indirect cost calculation
- Subaward agreements
- Compliance review
- Submission via Stanford eRA

### Faculty Resources
- Grant writing workshops (school-specific)
- Mock review services (department-level)
- Specific Aims page consultations
- Foundation grant strategy via Office of Foundation Relations

### Timeline
- 6 weeks before deadline: budget, admin docs
- 2 weeks: full draft
- 1 week: final compliance and formatting
- 5 days before: SPO submits

## Stanford SPO Implicit Rubric

### What Wins
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims (no failure cascade)
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Stanford interdisciplinary centers leveraged
6. Industry/clinical partnerships where relevant
7. Realistic budget with Stanford indirect rate
8. Compliance addressed
9. Letters of support specific
10. Innovation framed as feasible

### What SPO Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors
2. Compliance gaps
3. Subaward complications
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections

## Stanford Academic Voice

### Confidence Without Arrogance
- Cite competitor work generously
- Position contribution within field
- Avoid hype
- Acknowledge limitations

### Interdisciplinary Framing
- Bridge fields where genuine
- Cite Stanford interdisciplinary centers
- Multi-departmental collaborator letters
- Cross-school appointments leveraged

### Translational Where Applicable
- Stanford Hospital connection
- Patient-facing potential
- Clinical translational research themes
- Industry technology transfer where relevant
