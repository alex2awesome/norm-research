## Duke ORS Themes

### Specific Aims Page Conventions
- Standard four-paragraph structure
- Hypothesis-driven aims
- Aim independence
- Conceptual figure encouraged
- Iterate 10+ times

### Significance and Innovation
- Frame for non-specialist reviewers
- Cite high-impact recent papers
- Connect to NIH institute mission
- Innovation as novel application

### Approach
- Each aim with subsections
- Statistical analysis plan with power
- Sample size justification
- Rigor and reproducibility
- Authentication of resources
- Sex as biological variable
- Alternatives

### Investigator and Environment
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Duke-specific resources cited
- Letters of support specific

## Duke ORS Implicit Rubric

### What Wins
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Duke-specific resources leveraged (DTMI, DCRI, DCI)
6. Translational framing where applicable
7. Community engagement where applicable
8. Realistic budget
9. Compliance addressed
10. Letters of support specific

### What ORS Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors
2. Compliance gaps
3. Subaward complications
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections
