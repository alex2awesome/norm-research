## Penn ORS Implicit Themes

### Specific Aims Page Conventions
- Standard four-paragraph structure
- Hypothesis-driven aims
- Aim independence
- Conceptual figure encouraged
- Iterate 10+ times

### Significance and Innovation
- Frame for non-specialist reviewers
- Cite high-impact recent papers
- Connect to NIH institute mission
- Innovation as novel application

### Approach
- Each aim with subsections
- Statistical analysis plan
- Power calculation
- Sample size justification
- Rigor and reproducibility
- Authentication
- Sex as biological variable
- Alternatives

### Investigator and Environment
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Independence narrative for ESI
- Penn-specific resources cited (cores, centers, CHOP)
- Letters of support specific

## Penn ORS Pre-Submission Process

### Timeline
- 6 weeks: budget + admin docs
- 2-4 weeks: full draft for review
- 1 week: final compliance check
- 5 days before deadline: ORS submits

### Internal Review
- Department-level mock reviews
- Cross-department for interdisciplinary work
- ORS formatting and compliance check

## Penn ORS Implicit Rubric

### What Wins
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Penn-specific resources leveraged (CHOP, Wistar, ITMAT)
6. Translational framing where applicable
7. Realistic budget with Penn indirect rate
8. Compliance addressed
9. Letters of support specific
10. Innovation framed as feasible

### What ORS Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors
2. Compliance gaps (CHOP IRB if relevant)
3. Subaward complications (Penn-CHOP-Wistar subawards)
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections
