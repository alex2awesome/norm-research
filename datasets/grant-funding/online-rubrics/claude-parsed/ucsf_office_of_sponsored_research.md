# UCSF Office of Sponsored Research — Grant Writing Themes

UCSF OSR provides grant writing resources, mock review services, and workshops for one of the largest NIH-funded biomedical research institutions.

## Core Resources OSR Provides

### Mock Review Committees
- Faculty review of full applications before submission
- NIH-style scoring (1-9)
- Written summary of strengths/weaknesses
- Discussion meeting with PI
- Lead time: 4-6 weeks before deadline

### Specific Aims Page Workshops
- Multi-week iterative workshop
- Peer feedback rounds
- Senior faculty mentor pairing
- Final draft polish session

### Grant-Writing Office Hours
- Walk-in advising on Specific Aims
- Strategic advice on mechanism choice
- Program officer contact strategy
- Resubmission planning

## What UCSF OSR Emphasizes

### Specific Aims Page Conventions
- Four-paragraph structure (Russell-Morrison style)
- One page; iterate 10+ times
- Bold the aims; clear visual hierarchy
- Conceptual figure where helpful
- Closing impact statement

### Significance Section
- Lead with the problem (clinical, public health, scientific)
- Cite high-impact recent papers
- Connect to NIH institute mission
- "So what?" test for every paragraph

### Innovation Section
- Frame as "novel application" not "first ever"
- Cite the methods you adapt
- Acknowledge precedent honestly
- Innovation in concept + feasibility in approach

### Approach Section
- Each aim broken into: Rationale, Approach, Expected Outcomes, Pitfalls, Alternatives, Rigor
- Statistical analysis plan with power calculation
- Sample size justification with citation
- Replicates: biological + technical
- Authentication of cell lines, antibodies
- Sex as biological variable

### Investigator and Environment
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Independence narrative for ESI
- Specific named collaborators with roles
- UCSF-specific resources cited
- Letters of support that are specific

## UCSF-Specific Themes

### Leveraging Institutional Resources
- Cite specific UCSF cores (e.g., genomics, imaging, biostatistics)
- Mention specific collaborators in disease centers
- Reference UCSF training programs (PSTP, MSTP, etc.)
- Highlight clinical-research integration where relevant

### Translational Framing
- UCSF emphasizes translational impact
- Connect basic to clinical where possible
- Cite UCSF clinical resources
- Highlight patient-facing potential

### Diversity and Inclusion
- UCSF emphasizes diverse cohort recruitment
- Address health equity in human-subjects research
- Multi-ancestry inclusion in genomics
- Community-engaged research framing

## Common Pre-Submission Process at UCSF

### Timeline
- 6 months out: Specific Aims draft
- 4 months out: Full draft + mock review
- 2 months out: Revisions based on mock review
- 1 month out: Polish + OSR submission review
- Submit 1+ week before deadline

### Internal Review Cycle
- PI drafts Specific Aims
- Mentor reads, provides feedback
- Specific Aims workshop (peer review)
- Full draft to mock review committee
- Mock review meeting
- Revisions
- OSR formatting check
- Submit

### What Mock Review Looks For
- Specific Aims clarity and structure
- Hypothesis-driven aims
- Aim independence
- Preliminary data sufficiency
- Methodology specificity
- Statistical analysis plan
- Investigator credibility for scope
- Budget realism
- Timeline realism

## OSR's Implicit Rubric

### What Wins Per OSR Mock Reviews
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims (no failure cascade)
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Statistical analysis plan with power
6. Investigator productivity proportionate
7. UCSF-specific resources leveraged
8. Translational framing where applicable
9. Diverse cohort plans where applicable
10. Realistic timeline and budget

### What Mock Reviewers Ding
1. Co-dependent aims
2. Vague Significance
3. Insufficient preliminary data
4. Missing power calculation
5. No rigor language
6. Investigator overreach
7. Padded budget
8. Generic letters
9. Unrealistic timeline
10. No alternative if primary fails
