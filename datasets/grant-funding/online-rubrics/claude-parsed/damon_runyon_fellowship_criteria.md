# Damon Runyon Cancer Research Foundation - Fellowship Award

## Primary Selection Criteria

The primary criteria used to evaluate applicants:

### 1. Quality of the Research Proposal
- Importance of the problem
- Originality of approach
- Appropriateness of techniques
- Clarity of presentation

### 2. Qualifications, Experience, and Productivity
- Of both the candidate and the Sponsor
- Strong publication record
- Track record of research excellence

### 3. Quality of the Research Training Environment
- Where the proposed research will be conducted
- Potential for broadening and strengthening the candidate's ability to independently conduct innovative and substantive research

## Eligibility Requirements

### Level 1 (Basic and Physician-Scientists)
- Received degrees no more than 18 months prior to application deadline
- Must not have been in their Sponsors' labs for more than one year prior to application deadline

### Level 2 (Physician-Scientists)
- MD, MD/Ph.D., DDS, DVM, DO, or equivalent
- Completed residencies and clinical training
- Board-eligible in the United States at the start of the Damon Runyon Fellowship
- Devote at least 80% of their time to Damon Runyon-supported research activities

## Sponsor Requirements
- Tenured, tenure-track or equivalent position
- Capable of providing mentorship to the Fellow
- Track record of mentoring postdoctoral researchers

## Research Requirements

### Novel Research Direction Required
- The proposed research and training environment should represent a new opportunity for the candidate to expand their scientific skill set
- **Direct extensions of the candidate's graduate work (in approach, technique or exact area of study) will NOT be funded**
- Encourages mobility between labs and institutions

## Award Structure
- Damon Runyon Postdoctoral Research Fellowships granted for a four-year term
- Second-, third- and fourth-year funding contingent upon satisfactory progress reports
- Annual progress reports required

## Damon Runyon Quantitative Biology Fellowship
- Specifically for postdocs with quantitative training (math, physics, computer science, engineering)
- Applying quantitative skills to cancer research
- Same selection criteria with quantitative emphasis

## Innovation Award
- For early-career investigators with innovative high-risk projects
- Different from Fellowship; targets independent investigators

## Selection Process
- Application reviews by scientific committee
- External reviewers consulted for some applications
- Committee deliberation and final selection
- Announcement typically 4-6 months after submission

## Notable Features
- Strong emphasis on cancer research relevance
- Independence and mobility encouraged
- Multi-year support with milestones
- Continued community engagement after award
