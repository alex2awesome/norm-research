# NIH K Award - Career Development Review Criteria

## Key Distinction from R-Series
Unlike an R01, where the science drives the evaluation, a K-Award is fundamentally about the **applicant** — their potential, training needs, and career trajectory.

## Major Review Criteria

### 1. Candidate
- Academic record and research experience
- Motivation and commitment to a research career
- Likelihood that the K award will lead to research independence
- Capacity to develop required skills

### 2. Career Development Plan (CDP)
The most important element of any K award application. Reviewers evaluate:
- Whether the CDP fills knowledge/skill gaps for the candidate
- Specific courses, workshops, conferences, didactic training
- Plans for protected research time
- Logical progression toward research independence
- Mentor meetings and structured oversight

Applications that relegate the CDP to merely "a perfunctory set of courses and meetings" leave reviewers little recourse other than to evaluate the applicant's potential based on the likely success of the research plan.

### 3. Research Plan
- Specific Aims and Research Strategy
- Whether it is appropriate for and tailored to the applicant's experience level
- Should be ambitious but feasible within the K timeframe

### 4. Mentor (and Co-Mentors)
For mentored positions, reviewers assess:
- Mentor's career and research supervision record
- Whether mentor's work and experience are relevant to the proposed Research Plan
- Track record of training individuals (especially K awardees) for successful independent research careers
- Mentor's commitment of time and effort

### 5. Environment and Institutional Commitment
- Whether the institution has suitable facilities
- Institution's commitment to the applicant's development as an investigator
- Protected time guarantee (typically 75% effort)
- Departmental support letter

## K Award Mechanisms Covered
- K01 - Mentored Research Scientist
- K08 - Mentored Clinical Scientist
- K23 - Mentored Patient-Oriented Research
- K99/R00 - Pathway to Independence
- K24 - Mid-Career Investigator
- K25 - Career Award for Quantitative Scientists
