# ERC Starting Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Panel Structure
For each call there are 28 panels, each covering a sub-section of one of three domains:
- Life Sciences
- Physical and Engineering Sciences
- Social Sciences and Humanities

Each ERC panel consists of a chair and 10-16 members. Panel members are selected by the ERC Scientific Council on the basis of their scientific reputation. The majority of panel members may be generalists and thus not experts in the specific area(s) of your project.

## Sole Evaluation Criterion: Excellence

Excellence has two main components:

### 1. Excellence of the Research Project
- Ground-breaking nature
- Ambition
- Feasibility (NOTE: From 2026, feasibility is no longer assessed at Step 1)

### 2. Excellence of the Principal Investigator
- Intellectual capacity
- Creativity
- Commitment

The panels primarily evaluate the ground-breaking nature and ambition of the project.

## Two-Step Evaluation Process

**Step 1:** Panel members review only Part B1 (synopsis) and the CV. Panels of generalists.

**Step 2:** A new panel of specialists reviews both B1 and B2 (full description). Interviews take place at Step 2.

The Panels may be assisted by independent external experts working remotely, who have access to Part I, Part II, and the proposed budget.

## 2026 Key Changes
- Feasibility no longer assessed at Step 1
- Part I of the Scientific Proposal should no longer include details on the feasibility of the scientific approach but present a convincing overall research strategy
