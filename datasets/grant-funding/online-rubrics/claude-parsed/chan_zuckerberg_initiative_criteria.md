# Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) - Grant Evaluation Criteria

## Grant Evaluation Approach
CZI adheres to its core values in proposal selection and progress evaluation. CZI evaluates all applications for scientific merit and seeks independent expert review, with final decisions made by CZI staff in consultation with scientific advisors.

## Science Grantmaking Focus
CZI's grantmaking is aligned with its **four scientific grand challenges**:
- Imaging
- Cell Science
- Single Cell Biology
- Open Science Tools/Software

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative focuses its grantmaking on:
- The use of artificial intelligence in biomedicine
- Technology and biomedical research driven by generative AI
- K-12 education

## Funding Mechanisms
CZI utilizes a range of funding mechanisms:
- **Targeted grantmaking** (invited applicants)
- **Open competitions** (Requests for Applications or RFAs) for specific issue areas

Each RFA includes:
- Information on the opportunity
- Project requirements
- Eligibility
- Application process
- Specific evaluation criteria

## Example: Open Source Software for Science (EOSS)

For specific biomedical programs, reviewers evaluate:
- Value to the biomedical community of proposed work
- How the output will advance adoption among biomedical researchers
- Value the work will produce for biomedical researchers' work
- Unmet biomedical user needs that will be addressed
- Improvements or integration with other tools that will improve adoption, usability, functionality, or performance

## Reporting Requirements

CZI evaluates progress and impact through:
- Delivery of intermediate outputs
- Annual reports submitted and approved to ensure continued delivery of funds
- Description of the awardee's progress in their research program

## Distinct CZI Programs

### CZ Biohub
- Independent non-profit research institutes (SF, Chicago, NY)
- Recruit interdisciplinary investigators
- Collaboration across institutions

### Imaging Hardware
- Multi-million-dollar funding for innovative imaging tools
- Collaborative team grants

### Single Cell Biology
- Cell Atlas projects
- Methodology development

### Neurodegeneration Initiative
- Multi-disciplinary teams tackling neurodegenerative disease

### Pediatric Cancer
- Through AACR partnership
- Childhood/adolescent cancers

### Science Diversity Leadership Award
- Senior URM faculty
- $1.15M over 5 years
- Through HHMI partnership

## CZI Distinct Features
- Heavy emphasis on tools, infrastructure, and open science
- Cross-cutting interdisciplinary work valued
- Speed and agility (faster decisions than NIH)
- Public/private partnerships welcomed
- AI/ML applications increasingly emphasized
- Ambitious "moonshot" thinking encouraged

## Compared with NIH
- Less emphasis on individual investigator track record
- More emphasis on tools/infrastructure
- More open to early-stage and high-risk work
- Faster funding decisions
- More flexibility in deliverables
