# Wellcome Discovery Award - Evaluation Criteria

## Overview
Wellcome Discovery Awards provide funding for established researchers from any discipline who want to pursue bold and creative research ideas to deliver significant shifts in understanding that could improve human life, health, and wellbeing.

## Three Main Assessment Areas

The committee assesses across a set of criteria rather than one specific aspect of the proposal. Wellcome reviews:

### 1. Research Proposal Quality
Applications should aim to:
- Deliver a **significant shift in understanding**
- Provide a significant advance over existing methodologies, conceptual frameworks, tools or techniques
- Have the potential to stimulate new and innovative research

The proposed approach should be **novel**:
- Develops and tests new concepts, methods or technologies
- Combines existing ideas and approaches in a new way

### 2. Researcher Skills and Experience
- Research experience and track record
- Educational background
- Career stage and trajectory
- Leadership in the field
- Demonstrated ability to deliver ambitious projects

### 3. Research Environment
- Quality of institutional environment
- Support and resources available
- Collaborations
- Career development opportunities for team members

## Application Process

### Stage 1: Application
- Full application
- Includes detailed research plan
- Track record of investigators
- Environment description

### Stage 2: External Review
- Selected applications sent for external expert review
- Reviewers chosen based on expertise (not seniority)
- Multiple independent reviews

### Stage 3: Interview
- Discovery Award Interview Panel interviews shortlisted candidates at Wellcome offices in London
- Presentation at start of interview, with focus on questions and answers
- Panel assesses scientific depth, ambition, and ability to deliver

### Stage 4: Final Decision
- Discovery Research Decision Board makes final funding decisions

## Notable Wellcome Principles

### Open Research
- Strong commitment to open access
- Data sharing required
- DORA signatory

### People-Centered
- Holistic assessment of researchers
- Career disruption considered fairly
- Diverse career paths valued

### Ambition Premium
- Bold ideas valued over incremental work
- Long-term vision over short-term deliverables
- Risk-taking encouraged

## Funding
- Up to £8 million over 8 years
- Suitable for established researchers leading independent research programs
- Any discipline relevant to Wellcome's mission

## Comparison with Career Development Award
- Discovery Award: established researchers (typically 7+ years post-first independent position)
- Career Development Award: mid-career researchers establishing independence

## Wellcome Mission Areas (Discovery research can be in any area, but priority for):
- Mental Health
- Infectious Disease
- Climate and Health
- Discovery Research (basic biology)
