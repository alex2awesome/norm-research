# Simons Foundation - Grant Evaluation Criteria

## Targeted Grants in Mathematics and Physical Sciences

The Simons Foundation's Targeted Grants in MPS are intended to support **high-risk projects of exceptional promise and scientific importance** on a case-by-case basis. The division's primary focus is on theoretical mathematics, theoretical physics, and theoretical computer science.

## Evaluation Process
- Letter of Intent (LOI) stage assesses suitability and novelty
- Recommends submitting an LOI if applicants believe their research meets the criteria outlined in the RFA
- A review of the LOI may lead to a request for a full proposal

## Quality-Based Criteria
- Quality and significance of the applicant's previous research
- Likely impact the grant will have on future research
- Publication in high-quality journals
- The applicant's current and recent Ph.D. students
- The applicant's travel goals and general research activity

## Scientific Impact Criteria for Collaborations

For Simons Collaborations in Mathematics and Physical Sciences:
- A collaboration should address a mathematical or theoretical topic of fundamental scientific importance
- A significant, new development creates a novel area for exploration or provides a new direction for progress in an established field
- The questions addressed by the collaboration may be concrete or conceptual
- There should be little doubt that answering them would constitute a major scientific milestone
- The project should have clearly defined initial activities and goals by which progress and success can be measured

## Simons Investigators
For the Simons Investigator awards:
- Outstanding theoretical scientists who receive a stable base of research support
- Selection focuses on track record of accomplishment and continued promise
- Tier-1 senior investigator status with no specific deliverables, allowing risk-taking
