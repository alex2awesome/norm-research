# NIH R01 Research Strategy - Writing Guide

## Overview
The Research Strategy is a description of the rationale for your research and your experiments in 12 pages for an R01. A strong Research Strategy is the heart of your application where you describe your proposed project, its significance, and how you will conduct your research.

## Required Section Structure
Start each of the Research Strategy's sections with a header:
- **Significance**
- **Innovation**
- **Approach**

Then organize the Approach section around your Specific Aims. Use bold headers or an outline or numbering system — or both — that you use consistently throughout.

## Three Main Components

### Significance
Although significance will be emphasized throughout the application, the Significance section should give the most details:
- Background on the basic biology
- Importance of the research area
- Research opportunities
- New findings
- Knowledge gaps your work will fill
- The farther removed your reviewers are from your field, the more information you'll need to provide

### Innovation
- Note the significance and innovation of your research
- Describe novel concepts, approaches, methodologies, instrumentation, or interventions
- How your work shifts current paradigms
- Whether innovation is in concepts, methods, applications, or perspectives

### Approach
The Approach section is where the experimental design is described. Reviewers will want to know:
- **What** you plan to do
- **How** you plan to do it
- **Whether** you can do it (preliminary data)

NIH data show that of the peer review criteria, **Approach has the highest correlation with the overall impact score**.

## Required Elements of Rigorous Study Design

### Bias Minimization
- Methods for blinding
- Treatment randomization
- Selection of controls
- Allocation concealment

### Biological Variables
- Sex as a biological variable
- Age, race, weight (where relevant)
- Authentication of key biological resources

### Statistical Considerations
- Sample size justification
- Power calculations
- Pre-specified analysis plan
- Multiple comparison correction

### Reproducibility
- Methods to ensure reproducibility
- Data sharing plan
- Resource validation

## Preliminary Data
- If submitting a new application to a NOFO that allows preliminary data, strongly encouraged to include preliminary studies
- Demonstrate competency in the methods and interpretation
- Well-designed and robust preliminary studies provide a strong scientific rationale for the proposed follow-up experiments

## Common Approach Section Issues to Avoid
- Insufficient methodological detail
- Failure to discuss alternative strategies
- No contingency plans for negative results
- Unrealistic timelines
- Missing benchmarks for success
- Lack of integration between aims
- Unaddressed weaknesses in prior research

## Each Aim Should Include
1. Brief restatement of aim and rationale
2. Detailed approach/methods
3. Expected outcomes
4. Potential problems and alternative strategies
5. Benchmarks for success
6. Timeline within the budget period
