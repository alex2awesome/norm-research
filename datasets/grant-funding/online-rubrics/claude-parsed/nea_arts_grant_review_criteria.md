# NEA - Grants for Arts Projects Review Criteria

## Two Primary Review Criteria (Equal Weight)

Applications are reviewed on the basis of criteria with equal weight assigned to:
- **Artistic excellence**
- **Artistic merit**

These two primary review criteria are specified by Congress in NEA's authorizing legislation.

### 1. Artistic Excellence
The quality of the:
- Artists involved in the project
- Other key individuals involved
- Creative process
- Works of art
- Organizations
- Arts education providers
- Artistic partners
- Services involved in the project
- Their relevance to the audience or communities the project aims to serve

### 2. Artistic Merit
- Importance and appropriateness of the project to:
  - The organization's mission
  - The artistic field
  - The artists
  - The audience
  - The community
  - The constituency

## Review Process

### Closed-Session Panel Review
- Applications reviewed by advisory panelists
- Each panel comprises a diverse group of arts experts and other individuals
- Includes at least one knowledgeable layperson
- Broad knowledge in the areas under review
- Meet by discipline (Dance, Music, Theater, Visual Arts, etc.)

### National Council on the Arts
- Second-level review
- Provides recommendations to the NEA Chair

### Final Decision
- NEA Chair makes final funding decisions

## Discipline-Specific Considerations
The NEA reviews applications in 13 artistic disciplines:
- Artist Communities
- Arts Education
- Dance
- Design
- Folk & Traditional Arts
- Literary Arts
- Local Arts Agencies
- Media Arts
- Museums
- Music
- Musical Theater
- Opera
- Presenting & Multidisciplinary Works
- Theater
- Visual Arts

Each panel may apply discipline-specific norms but must address the two primary criteria.

## Strategic Plan Alignment
Review criteria reflect the NEA's priorities and are aligned with its strategic plan, which currently includes:
- Strengthening communities through the arts
- Engaging the public with diverse and excellent art
- Promoting public knowledge and understanding about the contributions of the arts

## Specific NEA Programs
- Grants for Arts Projects (main grant program)
- Challenge America (smaller grants for first-time applicants/underserved communities)
- Our Town (creative placemaking)
- NEA Big Read
- Research Grants in the Arts
- Folk and Traditional Arts Apprenticeships

## Application Components
- Project narrative (description, schedule, costs)
- Artistic statement (work samples)
- Audience/community description
- Budget
- Documentation of artistic excellence (work samples, reviews)

## Common Strengths Reviewers Look For
- Strong artistic vision
- Engagement with diverse audiences
- Innovation in artistic practice
- Community partnerships
- Sustainability of organization

## Common Weaknesses
- Generic, unfocused project description
- Weak work samples
- Mismatch between budget and project scope
- Unclear audience/community engagement
