# Burroughs Wellcome Fund - Career Awards

## Career Awards for Medical Scientists (CAMS)

### Award Details
- $700,000 over 5 years
- Bridges advanced postdoctoral training and the first three years of faculty service

### Selection Criteria
Competitive candidates will:
- Have at least two years of research experience
- Be up to two years away from becoming an independent investigator
- Be a first author on at least one publication in a peer-reviewed journal
- Have a significant publication record

### Evaluation Factors
Recipients evaluated based on:
- Quality of their previous research
- Potential impact of their proposed projects
- Overall commitment to advancing medical science
- Interdisciplinary approach (encouraged)
- Translation potential

## Career Awards at the Scientific Interface (CASI)

### Award Details
- $560,000 over 5 years
- Bridges advanced postdoctoral training and the first three years of faculty service

### Eligibility
Designed to support those working in biology with backgrounds in:
- Physical sciences
- Mathematical sciences
- Computational sciences
- Engineering sciences

### Selection Criteria
- At least one first-author publication in a peer-reviewed journal
- Evidence of significant accomplishment or competence in a quantitative discipline (papers, advanced coursework, undergraduate major)
- Proposal must draw on quantitative background
- Cross-disciplinary research bridging quantitative methods with biology

## Common BWF Selection Themes

### Innovation and Risk
- Bold, ambitious research questions
- Willingness to pursue high-risk/high-reward
- Willingness to develop new tools/methods

### Career Trajectory
- Clear path toward independence
- Strong mentorship arrangement
- Institutional commitment to PI's career

### Quantitative Background (CASI specifically)
- Mathematical, computational, or physical science training
- Application to biological problems
- Cross-disciplinary collaborations

## BWF Investigators in Pathogenesis of Infectious Disease (PATH)
- Different mechanism, similar criteria
- $700,000 over 5 years
- Support for early-career researchers studying microbe-host interactions

## Application Process
- Limited submission (institutional nominations)
- Pre-application screening at home institution
- Full application with detailed research plan and CV
- Reference letters
- Detailed budget justification

## Review Process
- BWF Scientific Advisory Committee
- External expert review
- Final selection by BWF Board

## Program Goals
BWF programs aim to:
- Support exceptional scientists at critical career transitions
- Foster independent research careers
- Bridge funding gaps
- Encourage interdisciplinary work
- Support translation of basic to clinical research
