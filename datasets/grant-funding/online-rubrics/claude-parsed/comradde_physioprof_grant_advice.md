# Comradde PhysioProffe — NIH Grant Tactics

Comradde PhysioProffe (CPP) is an anonymous senior NIH-funded PI blogger known for blunt, tactical advice on R01 writing and study section behavior. Often paired with DrugMonkey content.

## Core Tactical Principles

### "Make Reviewer Rage Disappear"
- Reviewers are looking for reasons to dock points
- Every page should anticipate and disarm common critiques
- Add a half-paragraph addressing each predictable objection
- Don't wait for the resubmission to address known weaknesses

### The Power Move: Preliminary Data in Significance
- Move your best preliminary figure into the Significance section
- It establishes that you can do the work AND the problem matters
- Reviewers form an impression in the first 2 pages

### Aims Independence Doctrine
- "If Aim 1 fails, can Aim 2 still succeed?" must be YES
- If not, restructure
- This is the #1 reason approach scores get penalized
- Use parallel rather than serial logic where possible

### The Methods Tax
- Every method gets a "rigor and reproducibility" paragraph
- Sample size justification with citation
- Replicates, blinding, randomization
- Pre-registered analysis plans where applicable
- Authentication of cell lines, antibodies, reagents

### Budget Justification Reality
- Reviewers do read budget justifications
- Don't pad — reviewers will cut
- Justify each personnel role in scientific terms
- Equipment requests need vendor quotes for big-ticket items

### Investigator Section Strategy
- For ESIs: emphasize independence from postdoc mentor
- For mid-career: emphasize productivity per grant dollar
- For senior: emphasize mentorship and field leadership
- Always reference your most recent high-impact publication

## Study Section Behavior CPP Has Documented

### The Discussion Order
- Top half discussed, bottom half triaged (not discussed)
- Discussed grants get 15-20 minutes
- Reviewer 1 (assigned) speaks first, gives strengths/weaknesses
- Reviewer 2 and 3 follow
- Anyone in room can comment
- Final scores converge or stay split

### What Causes Score Spread
- Methodological disagreement
- Different reviewer expertise (mechanism vs. translation)
- Significance vs. Approach weighting differences
- Personality / writing style reactions

### What Saves a Borderline App
- Strong scientific premise + minor methods quibbles = often funded
- Innovative approach + execution concerns = often unfunded
- Solid execution + incremental advance = depends on payline

## CPP's Most-Quoted Maxims
- "The Specific Aims page is the entire grant for triage purposes"
- "Reviewers reward the appearance of having thought carefully"
- "Never give the reviewer an easy nit to pick"
- "Hypothesis-driven beats descriptive every time"
- "If you have to explain why your project is significant, it isn't"

## Writing Workflow
1. Specific Aims page first, iterated to perfection over 4-6 weeks
2. Significance section, anchored in 5-10 key papers
3. Innovation section, framed as "novel application"
4. Preliminary Studies, with quantitative figures
5. Approach, broken into Aim-specific subsections
6. Each Aim with: Rationale, Approach, Expected Outcomes, Pitfalls, Alternatives, Rigor
7. Future Directions paragraph
8. Final pass for tone, hedging removal, white space

## What CPP Says About Resubmissions
- Use the Introduction page tactically
- Quote reviewer language verbatim when responding
- Even unfair critiques get acknowledged with substance
- "We have addressed this concern by..." appears 10+ times
- Bold/italic the changes in the body of the resubmission
