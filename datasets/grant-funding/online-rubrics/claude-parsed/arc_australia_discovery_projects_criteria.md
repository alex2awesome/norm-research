# ARC Discovery Projects - Evaluation Criteria

## Two-Stage Application Process

### Expression of Interest (EOI) Stage Criteria
- **Investigator(s)/Capability** (30%)
- **Project Quality and Innovation** (70%)

EOIs are assessed by three ARC College of Expert members. Following moderation, highly-ranked projects are invited to submit a full proposal.

## Standard Selection Criteria for Full Proposal

### 1. Project Quality and Innovation (40%)
- Significance and innovation
- Likelihood of producing innovative and internationally competitive research
- Sound conceptual framework
- Methodological rigor
- Position relative to international state of the art

### 2. Investigator(s)/Capability (30%)
- Capacity of the investigator/team to undertake the proposed research
- Track record relative to opportunities
- Demonstrated ability to attract HDR (Higher Degree by Research) candidates
- Demonstrated ability to lead/train others
- Past contributions to advancement of the field

### 3. Feasibility and Benefit (30%)
- Sound feasibility, including budget justification
- Project timeframes
- Adequacy of facilities and infrastructure
- Plans to disseminate research outcomes
- National benefit

## Career Stage Adjustments
Assessment against the 'investigator' criterion includes consideration of:
- Opportunities the researcher has had to build their research profile
- Early-career researchers receive appropriate consideration
- Researchers with interrupted research careers receive appropriate consideration
- Career disruptions can be explained in the application

## Scheme Objectives
- Support excellent basic and applied research and research training by individuals and teams
- Support national and international research collaboration
- Enhance the scale and focus of research in Australian Government priority areas

## Excellence in Research for Australia (ERA)
- ERA assessments inform the broader research evaluation context but are not directly used for individual grant scoring

## Other ARC Schemes (similar criteria with variations)
- DECRA (Discovery Early Career Researcher Award)
- Future Fellowships (mid-career)
- Laureate Fellowships (top tier)
- Linkage Projects (industry collaboration)
- LIEF (infrastructure)
