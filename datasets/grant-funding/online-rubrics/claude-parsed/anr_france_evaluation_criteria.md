# ANR (Agence nationale de la recherche) - Evaluation Criteria

## Two-Phase Evaluation

### Phase 1 (Pre-proposal)
The evaluation process is guided by **two key criteria**:
1. **Quality and scientific aims of the proposal** (determining criterion)
2. **Organisation and implementation** of the project (including several sub-criteria)

### Phase 2 (Full proposal)
The evaluation process is guided by **three key criteria**:
1. **Quality and scientific aims of the proposal**
2. **Organisation and implementation** of the project
3. **Impact** considerations

## Evaluation Process and Principles

- Panel members and external experts use the same set of criteria to evaluate pre-proposals and full proposals
- Projects assessed independently based on criteria specific to the call
- No discussion with the panel before initial scoring
- Before accessing project documents, panel members commit to:
  - Respect ANR code of ethics and scientific integrity
  - Respect the French National Charter for Research Integrity
  - Protect confidentiality of information
  - Declare no conflicts of interest

## Key Sub-criteria for Quality and Scientific Aims
- Scientific and technical quality
- Originality and innovation
- Soundness of approach
- Risk assessment
- Position relative to state of the art
- Scientific ambition

## Key Sub-criteria for Organisation and Implementation
- Quality and complementarity of the consortium (PRC) or individual investigator
- Adequacy of resources requested
- Suitability of timeline
- Project management and coordination
- Risk mitigation strategies

## Key Sub-criteria for Impact (Phase 2)
- Scientific impact (advancement of the field)
- Economic and social impact
- Dissemination plan
- Open science practices
- Training of young researchers

## Adjectival Ratings
ANR uses adjectival ratings, generally with categories such as A+ (excellent) through C (insufficient).

## Project Categories (Generic Call - AAPG)
- JCJC (Young Researchers)
- PRC (Collaborative Research)
- PRCE (Collaborative Research with Companies)
- PRCI (International Collaboration)
