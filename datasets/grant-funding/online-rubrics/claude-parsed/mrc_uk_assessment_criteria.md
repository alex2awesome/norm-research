# MRC UK - Grant Assessment Criteria

## Three Core Evaluation Criteria

Reviews are based around three core criteria that apply across MRC funding schemes:

### 1. Importance
- Significance of the research question
- Relevance to MRC's strategy
- Health/disease/societal impact
- Why this research, why now
- How the work will fill a critical gap

### 2. Scientific Potential
- Quality of the research plan
- Originality and innovation
- Soundness of methodology
- Likelihood of achieving stated objectives
- Contribution to knowledge

### 3. Resources Requested
- Appropriateness of staffing
- Justification for equipment
- Realistic budget
- Value for money
- Adequate time allocation
- Suitable institutional environment

## Assessment Process
- Proposals peer reviewed by independent scientific experts from the UK and overseas
- Detailed information provided on what reviewers, boards and panels are looking for

## Application Guidance
Before applying, applicants should consider:
- Each of the specific criteria
- Whether they fully address each area
- Whether they, their co-applicants, and their research environment fulfil the required criteria
- Whether the application meets the strategic priorities

## Specific MRC Schemes
- Programme grants (longer-term, larger)
- Research grants (standard project funding)
- New Investigator Research Grants (early career)
- Career Development Awards
- Centres of Excellence
- Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund collaborations
- Clinical Research Training Fellowships

## Scoring System
MRC uses standard UKRI scoring (1 = poor to 6 = exceptional):
- 6: Exceptional - the application is outstanding
- 5: Excellent - very high quality, addresses most criteria to an excellent level
- 4: Good - addresses criteria well with some weaknesses
- 3: Fair - some merit but significant weaknesses
- 2: Weak - flaws that prevent funding
- 1: Poor - unsuitable for funding

## Boards and Panels
MRC has multiple Research Boards covering:
- Infections and Immunity Board
- Molecular and Cellular Medicine Board
- Neurosciences and Mental Health Board
- Population and Systems Medicine Board
- Methodology Research Programme
