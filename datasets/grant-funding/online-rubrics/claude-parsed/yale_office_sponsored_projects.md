## Yale OSP Themes

### Specific Aims Page Conventions (NIH-relevant)
- Standard four-paragraph structure
- Hypothesis-driven aims
- Aim independence
- Conceptual figure encouraged
- Iterate 10+ times

### Significance and Innovation
- Frame for non-specialist reviewers
- Cite high-impact recent papers
- Connect to NIH institute mission
- Innovation as novel application

### Approach
- Each aim with subsections
- Statistical analysis plan with power
- Sample size justification
- Rigor and reproducibility
- Authentication of resources
- Sex as biological variable
- Alternatives

### Investigator and Environment
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Yale-specific resources cited
- Letters of support specific

## Humanities Grant Themes (Yale Strong)

### NEH Conventions
- Significance to humanities scholarship
- Methodology specific to discipline
- Project narrative + work plan + budget
- Letters of support from senior scholars
- Final product (book, edition, digital project) specified

### Mellon Foundation
- Long-term, transformative
- Often institutional rather than individual
- Strategic alignment with foundation priorities
- Senior leader letters of support

### Yale Humanities Resources to Cite
- Beinecke Library archives
- Yale Whitney Humanities Center
- Yale Center for British Art
- Department-specific resources

## Yale OSP Implicit Rubric

### What Wins (NIH/Sciences)
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. Yale-specific resources leveraged
6. Translational framing where applicable (YCCI)
7. Realistic budget
8. Compliance addressed
9. Letters of support specific
10. Innovation framed as feasible

### What Wins (Humanities)
1. Clear, novel scholarly contribution
2. Engagement with relevant historiography
3. Specific methodology
4. Clear timeline with deliverables
5. Yale archives/resources cited
6. Letters from senior scholars
7. Final product specified (book, edition, digital project)
8. Realistic budget
9. Cohort/institutional alignment
10. Sustainable scholarly engagement

### What OSP Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors
2. Compliance gaps
3. Subaward complications
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections
