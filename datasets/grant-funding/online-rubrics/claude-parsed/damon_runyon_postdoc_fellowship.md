# Damon Runyon Cancer Research Fellowship — Selection Criteria

Postdoctoral fellowship for cancer research, ~$280,000 over 4 years. ~10-15 fellowships awarded per year.

## Three Primary Selection Criteria

### 1. Quality of the Research Proposal
- **Importance of the problem** — what cancer-relevant question is addressed?
- **Originality of approach** — how novel is the experimental strategy?
- **Appropriateness of techniques** — methods well-matched to questions?
- **Clarity of presentation** — proposal well-written and accessible?

### 2. Qualifications, Experience, and Productivity
- Of the **candidate** — academic record, publications, capability
- Of the **sponsor** (host PI) — reputation, prior mentorship outcomes
- Letters of recommendation
- Productivity relative to career stage

### 3. Quality of the Research Training Environment
- Host laboratory's resources and infrastructure
- Departmental and institutional environment
- **Potential for broadening the candidate's ability** to independently conduct innovative research
- Career-development opportunities at the host site

## Distinctive Programmatic Constraint (the anti-criterion)

**"Direct extensions of the candidate's graduate work (in approach, technique or exact area of study) will not be funded."**

Penalizes continuity with PhD work — applicants must demonstrate movement to a new area, technique, or approach. Most agencies are silent on or even reward continuity.

## DEI Statement

Damon Runyon explicitly notes uncompromising commitment to excellence AND values diversity, equity, inclusion. Foundation seeks to:
- Promote gender equality
- Increase diversity in all forms throughout programs

## Implications for Rubric Design

Damon Runyon's "no direct extension of graduate work" rule is an unusual explicit criterion that pre-commits to selecting candidates making intellectual transitions. Similar to HFSP's interdisciplinary requirement — designed to reward category-crossing rather than depth-within-category.
