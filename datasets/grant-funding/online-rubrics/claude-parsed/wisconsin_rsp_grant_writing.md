# UW-Madison RSP — Grant Writing Themes

University of Wisconsin-Madison RSP provides extensive grant writing resources, with a particularly strong tradition of NIH grant writing support.

## UW-Madison Funder Mix

### Strong NIH Portfolio
- Major medical school
- WiCell Research Institute (stem cell)
- Wisconsin Institutes for Discovery
- Morgridge Institute affiliation

### USDA / Agriculture
- Strong land-grant agricultural research
- USDA NIFA grants
- Sea Grant program
- Cooperative Extension

### NSF
- Strong engineering, CS, basic sciences
- Wisconsin Energy Institute

### Grant Library
- RSP maintains library of funded applications

- Department-level mock reviews

- Written feedback + meeting

### Workshops

- Foundation grant strategies

## UW RSP Themes

### Specific Aims Page Conventions
- Standard four-paragraph structure
- Hypothesis-driven aims
- Aim independence emphasized
- Conceptual figure encouraged
- Iterate 10+ times

### Significance and Innovation
- Frame for non-specialist reviewers
- Cite high-impact recent papers
- Connect to NIH institute mission
- Innovation as novel application

### Approach
- Each aim with subsections
- Statistical analysis plan
- Power calculation
- Sample size justification
- Rigor and reproducibility
- Authentication of resources
- Sex as biological variable
- Alternatives

### Investigator and Environment
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Independence narrative for ESI
- UW-specific resources cited
- Letters of support specific

## USDA NIFA Themes

### AFRI (Agriculture and Food Research Initiative)
- Specific RFAs each year
- Stakeholder engagement valued
- Practical application emphasized
- Multi-state collaborations
- Extension partnerships

### Hatch Act / McIntire-Stennis
- Formula funds for land-grant institutions
- Less competitive but smaller awards
- Project review at state level
- Annual reporting required

### What USDA Reviewers Look For
- Connection to USDA mission and priorities
- Stakeholder/producer engagement
- Practical/translational outcomes
- Multi-disciplinary teams
- Education/extension components

## UW RSP Implicit Rubric

### What Wins
1. Strong Specific Aims with clear hypothesis
2. Independent aims
3. Quantitative preliminary data
4. Rigor language explicit
5. UW-specific resources leveraged
6. Stakeholder engagement (USDA-relevant)
7. Realistic budget
8. Letters of support specific
9. Innovation framed as feasible
10. Significance landed in 30 seconds

### What RSP Catches Pre-Submission
1. Budget errors (effort, indirect cost)
2. Compliance gaps
3. Subaward complications
4. Format violations
5. Missing required sections
