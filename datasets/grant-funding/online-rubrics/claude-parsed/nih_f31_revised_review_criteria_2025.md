# NIH Revised Fellowship Review Criteria (2025)

## Effective Date and Scope
Fellowship applications submitted on or after January 25, 2025 follow revised application and review criteria. The revised peer review criteria apply to: F30, F31, F32, F33, F99/K00.

## Restructured Three Criteria

The revised criteria reorganize the previous five regulatory criteria (Fellowship Applicant; Sponsors, Collaborators, and Consultants; Research Training Plan; Training Potential; Institutional environment and Commitment to Training) into THREE criteria:

### 1. Candidate's Preparedness and Potential
Reviewers evaluate the candidate's:
- Academic preparation
- Research experience and skills
- Motivation and commitment to a research career
- Productivity and accomplishments to date
- Potential to contribute to the biomedical workforce

### 2. Research Training Plan
- Quality of the proposed research project
- Appropriateness of the training environment for proposed work
- Activities to address training needs
- Quality of mentorship plan
- Plan to enhance reproducible research practices

### 3. Commitment to Candidate
- Sponsor's track record of mentorship
- Sponsor's research support and resources
- Institutional commitment
- Career development opportunities provided

## Key Changes
- Better focus reviewer attention on the three key assessments
- Ensure a broad range of candidates and research training contexts can be recognized as meritorious
- Reduce bias in review by emphasizing the commitment to the candidate without undue consideration of sponsor and institutional reputation

## Other Notable Changes
- **Academic grades will no longer be a component of fellowship applications**
- The candidate, sponsor, and referees may provide a variety of information for the review panel to determine the candidate's preparedness and potential
- More detailed instructions for reference letters

## Scoring
Each criterion scored 1-9 (1=exceptional, 9=poor). Overall impact score is determined as a separate assessment of the likelihood the fellowship will promote the candidate's potential for, and commitment to, an independent scientific research career.
