# Sloan Research Fellowships — Detailed Selection Criteria (Wave 2 supplement)

Wave 1 had a brief Sloan entry; this Wave 2 file extracts the operational selection rubric.

## Three Selection Bases (the explicit criteria)

1. **Independent research accomplishments** of the candidate
2. **Creativity** of the candidate's work
3. **Potential to become leaders in the scientific community** through future contributions

## Eligibility (the threshold rubric)

- **PhD or equivalent** in eligible field
- **Faculty position at college/university** in U.S. or Canada
- **Tenure-track but untenured** as of Sept 15 of nomination year
- **Several years past PhD** to have accumulated independent research record
- **Regular teaching obligation** as part of position

## Eight Eligible Fields (one fellowship per field selection)

1. Chemistry
2. Computer Science
3. Earth System Science
4. Economics
5. Mathematics
6. Neuroscience
7. Physics
8. Ocean Sciences (added recently)

## Nomination Process

- **Nomination from a department head or senior researcher** required (no self-nomination)
- Nominators must speak knowledgeably about candidate's quality and significance of work
- **Letters of support** are critical — committee weights heavily
- Letters from **co-authors, mentors, personal associates** are explicitly LESS useful than letters from those with professional distance

## Materials Required

1. **CV** — full record
2. **Publication list** — typically 5-10 most significant
3. **Research statement** — 3 pages
4. **Three letters of support** — including the nomination letter

## Selection Committee Structure

- **Seven separate selection committees** (one per field)
- Each committee composed of established experts in that field
- Committee reads all field nominations and ranks
- Committee chairs from each field meet to finalize portfolio

## Implicit Criteria (per Foundation guidance)

1. **Demonstrated independence** from PhD advisor / postdoc mentor
2. **Original research direction** distinct from training environment
3. **Productivity appropriate to career stage**
4. **Recognition by field peers** (citations, invitations, awards)
5. **Trajectory toward field leadership**

## What Distinguishes Sloan from Other Early-Career Awards

- **Open-ended use of funds** (~$75,000 over 2 years) — no specific project required
- **No project proposal** in application — the candidate is the unit being evaluated, not a proposal
- **No reporting requirements** on how funds are used
- **Career-trajectory focus** rather than specific-project focus

## Implications for Rubric Design

Sloan's rubric is among the most "person-centric" of major fellowship rubrics: there is essentially no project evaluation. The committee is asked to identify future field leaders based purely on past trajectory and independent research record. This stands in contrast to most grant rubrics that center the proposed work.
