# MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships - Evaluation Criteria

The MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship proposal is evaluated across three main sections, each with a different weight:

## 1. Excellence (50% of the mark)
- Quality and pertinence of the project's research and innovation objectives
- Soundness of the proposed methodology
- Quality of supervision, training, and two-way knowledge transfer (three-way for Global Fellowships) between the researcher and the host
- Quality and appropriateness of the researcher's professional experience, competencies, and skills
- Match between the researcher's profile, training-through-research, and the project objectives

## 2. Impact (30%)
- Credibility of measures to enhance the career perspectives and employability of the researcher
- Suitability and quality of measures to maximize expected outcomes and impacts (dissemination, exploitation, communication of project results)
- Quality of activities to communicate the project to different audiences
- Public engagement strategy

## 3. Quality and Efficiency of the Implementation (20%)
- Quality and effectiveness of the work plan, assessment of risks and appropriateness of the effort
- Quality and capacity of the host institutions and participating organizations, including hosting arrangements

## Scoring System
Each criterion scored 0-5 with half-point resolution. Threshold:
- Each individual criterion: minimum 3.0
- Total threshold: 70% (i.e., 10.5 points overall when out of 15)

## Evaluation Process
MSCA proposals are evaluated by at least 3 independent external expert evaluators. The experts evaluate each proposal against the award criteria defined in the work programme. Success rates hover around 10%.
