# Whitaker International Fellows and Scholars Program - Selection Criteria

## Program Overview
The Whitaker International Program supports US biomedical engineers in international research and educational opportunities, with the goal of strengthening their professional careers and the field of biomedical engineering.

## General Selection Criteria

### Achievement Requirements
Applicants must:
- Be highly qualified
- Have demonstrated significant achievement in research, education, or other societal activity related to biomedical engineering

### Synergy with Career Goals
Applications should demonstrate **synergy between career goals and the proposed overseas experience**.

### Citizenship
- Must be U.S. citizens or have U.S. permanent residency

### Nomination Required
- Must be nominated by the Dean of a School or Chair of the biomedical engineering department at their current or most recent degree granting institution

### Language Ability
- Must have language ability to carry out the proposed project in the host country at the time of departure

## Two Award Categories

### Whitaker Fellows (Junior)
- Biomedical engineers ranging from graduating seniors to those conducting doctoral studies
- Should have a BS or MS degree in biomedical engineering or a closely related field
- Designed to enhance and accelerate the career trajectories of those who will become future leaders in academic, industrial, governmental, clinical, or other endeavors

### Whitaker Scholars (Senior)
- Biomedical engineers who have/will have a PhD by the beginning date of their awards
- Should have a degree in biomedical engineering AND a doctorate (not more than three years prior to the start of the grant)
- OR will receive the PhD prior to the start of the grant
- Designed to enable PhD-holding researchers to lay foundations for international collaborations

## Application Components

### References
- Three references required
- Recommended that all references be from academics or professionals in biomedical engineering or a closely-related field

### Host Letter
- The Whitaker Program requires a letter of support from the host affiliation as part of the application process

### Project Description
- Detailed plan for proposed activities
- Justification for chosen host institution
- Demonstration of how project advances candidate's career

### Personal Statement
- Career trajectory
- Why this international experience
- Long-term goals

## Evaluation Criteria

### Quality of Candidate
- Academic record
- Research accomplishments
- Leadership potential

### Quality of Proposed Project
- Scientific/technical merit
- Feasibility
- Potential impact

### Quality of Host Affiliation
- Reputation of host institution
- Mentorship plan
- Resources available

### Strategic Value
- Alignment with candidate's career goals
- Potential to broaden the field of biomedical engineering
- International collaboration value
- Cultural exchange dimension

## Key Strategic Considerations
- Choose a host that provides genuinely new opportunities (not extensions of current work)
- Demonstrate cultural and intellectual readiness for international experience
- Articulate why this specific host and country
- Show how experience advances biomedical engineering field broadly

## Award Details
- Multi-month to multi-year placements
- Living stipends, travel, project support
- Benefits package varies by award type
- Required final report on impact
