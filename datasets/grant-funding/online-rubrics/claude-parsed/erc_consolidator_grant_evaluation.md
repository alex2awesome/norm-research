# ERC Consolidator Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Sole Evaluation Criterion: Excellence

The Consolidator Grant is evaluated on the sole criterion of Excellence, which includes both the excellence of the research project and the excellence of the principal investigator.

### For the Research Project
- Ground-breaking nature
- Ambition

### For the Principal Investigator
- Intellectual capacity
- Creativity
- Commitment

The panels primarily evaluate the ground-breaking nature and ambition of the project, while also evaluating the intellectual capacity and creativity of the PI, with a focus on the extent to which the PI has the required scientific expertise and capacity to successfully execute the project.

## Eligibility Window
PI must be 7-12 years post-PhD (with extensions for eligible career breaks).

## Panel Review Process

Two-step evaluation:

**Step 1:** A panel of generalist reviewers reads only B1.

**Step 2:** If selected, the proposal moves to Step 2, where a new panel of specialists reviews both B1 and B2.

Panels may be assisted by independent external experts working remotely, recommended by panel members collectively, with access to Part I, Part II, and the proposed budget.

## 2026 Key Change
Feasibility will no longer be assessed at Step 1; thus, Part I of the Scientific Proposal should no longer include details on the feasibility of the scientific approach but present a convincing overall research strategy.
