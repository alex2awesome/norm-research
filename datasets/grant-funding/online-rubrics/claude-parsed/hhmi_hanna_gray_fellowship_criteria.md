# HHMI Hanna H. Gray Fellows Program - Selection Criteria

## Program Mission
The selection is of outstanding early career scientists who have demonstrated:
- A commitment to making foundational discoveries
- Building an inclusive culture in academic science

## Eligibility Requirements

### Career Stage
- Applicants must have been accepted to join a lab in the U.S.
- No more than 24 months of postdoctoral research experience at the time of application due date
- Hold a PhD and/or MD (or equivalent) which is conferred by the start of the grant term

### Effort Commitment
- Commit 75% of their total effort to research in a postdoctoral training position by the grant start date
- Commit to a minimum of two years of postdoctoral training in this position after the grant start date

### Citizenship
- Open to all
- Non-U.S. citizens and applicants with other nationalities must have a degree from a research institution in the U.S.

## Eligible Research Disciplines

The competition is open to those dedicated to **basic research** from both doctoral and/or medical training paths in the biomedical and life science disciplines, including but not limited to:
- Plant biology
- Evolutionary biology
- Biophysics
- Chemical biology
- Biomedical engineering
- Computational biology
- And more biomedical/life sciences

## Program Structure

### Phase 1: Postdoctoral Phase (up to 4 years)
- Mentored research
- Career development
- Publication of work

### Phase 2: Faculty Phase (up to 4 years)
- Continued support upon transition to faculty position
- Resources to launch independent lab
- Mentoring and community

## Award Details
- Up to 8 years total support
- Up to $80,000/year postdoc salary + benefits
- $20,000/year research allowance
- Faculty phase: substantial multi-year support including startup
- Total package can exceed $1.5M

## Selection Criteria

### Scientific Excellence
- Track record of research accomplishment
- Quality of proposed research project
- Potential to make foundational discoveries
- Letters of recommendation

### Independence and Vision
- Ability to develop independent research program
- Compelling research vision
- Potential to lead an independent lab

### Commitment to Inclusion
- Demonstrated commitment to making academic science more inclusive
- Mentorship history
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion activities
- Personal narrative on diversity contributions

### Research Plan Quality
- Originality and ambition
- Feasibility within fellowship period
- Match between candidate skills and proposed work
- Choice of host laboratory and mentor

## Application Components
- Research statement (proposed work)
- Personal statement (career trajectory + DEI commitment)
- Three letters of recommendation
- Mentor's commitment letter
- Diversity statement

## Comparison with Similar Programs
- More inclusive eligibility than HHMI Investigator (career stage)
- Longer duration than typical postdoc fellowship
- Faculty phase ensures continued support
- Strong DEI emphasis distinct from many other fellowships
