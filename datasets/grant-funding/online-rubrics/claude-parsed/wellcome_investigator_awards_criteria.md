# Wellcome Discovery Awards & Investigator Awards — Selection Criteria

Wellcome's flagship grants for established researchers, providing up to £3 million over up to 8 years for "discovery research."

## Wellcome Discovery Awards (current scheme replacing Investigator Awards)

### Program Goals
Funding for senior researchers to ask "the most important questions" in their field with the time and flexibility to pursue ambitious agendas.

### Three Primary Selection Criteria

1. **Research Vision and Approach**
   - Originality and ambition of the research vision
   - Importance of the questions being addressed
   - Soundness of the proposed approach
   - Potential for transformative outcomes
2. **Research Environment**
   - Quality of the host institution and team
   - Resources available
   - Track record of training and developing researchers
3. **You as a Researcher**
   - Track record relative to career stage
   - Demonstrated ability to lead a major research program
   - Vision and leadership in the field

## Wellcome's "Researcher Inclusion" Criterion (DEI rubric)

Wellcome explicitly requires applications to address how Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) considerations factor into:

1. **Research planning** — does the research question itself consider equity?
2. **Research development** — diverse perspectives included in design?
3. **Research implementation** — diverse team composition?
4. **Research dissemination** — outputs accessible to diverse audiences?

EDI considerations should be appropriate to:
- The research proposal context
- The disciplines involved
- The location of research
- The composition of the team

## Three-Stage Evaluation Process

### Stage 1: Shortlisting
- Expert Review Groups (independent UK and international scientists)
- Assess vision, ambition, and track record
- Reduce applicant pool

### Stage 2: External Peer Review
- Shortlisted applications sent to UK and international referees
- Detailed scientific review
- Reviewer comments shared with applicant for response

### Stage 3: Selection Panel Interview
- Shortlisted candidates invited to interview
- Present vision and respond to reviewer concerns
- Final funding decisions

## Funding Structure

- Up to **£3 million** per award
- Up to **£425,000 per year**
- **Up to 7-8 years** duration
- Can be joint applications by 2 researchers with complementary expertise

## Distinctive Rubric Elements

1. **"Compelling research vision"** as core criterion — emphasizes long-term agenda over specific project
2. **EDI considerations as required application section** — formalizes equity criteria absent from most other major funders
3. **Joint applications welcome** for complementary teams (similar to ERC Synergy)
4. **Long award duration** allows for long-term high-risk programs
5. **Interview stage** allows holistic judgment beyond written application

## Implications for Rubric Design

Wellcome's rubric is among the most explicit major-funder rubrics in REQUIRING applicants to address equity/diversity/inclusion as a scored criterion. Most other major funders treat EDI as compliance requirement or aspirational goal; Wellcome makes it central to evaluation.
