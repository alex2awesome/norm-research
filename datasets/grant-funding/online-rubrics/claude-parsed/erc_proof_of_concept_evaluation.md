# ERC Proof of Concept - Evaluation Criteria

## Sole Evaluation Criterion: Excellence

The Proof of Concept Grant is evaluated based on the sole criterion of excellence, which comprises three evaluation elements:

### 1. Breakthrough Innovation Potential
Proposals must demonstrate that the proposed Proof of Concept activity has the potential to drive innovation and business inventiveness and/or tackle societal challenges, and that the proposed expected outcomes are innovative or distinctive compared to existing solutions.

### 2. Approach and Methodology
The proposed Proof of Concept activities and planning are feasible within the proposed timescales and resources, and are appropriate and effective to explore the pathway from ground-breaking research towards innovation.

### 3. Strategic Leadership and Project Management
This element concerns the researcher's strategic leadership and project management.

## Evaluation Process
External experts mark each element on a three-level scale:
- **Very good**
- **Good**
- **Fail**

To be eligible for funding, your proposal must receive a pass ("very good" or "good") from a majority of experts on all three elements.

## Eligibility
The PoC Grant is open only to current or recent ERC grantees, and the activities must build on results from an existing or completed ERC project.
