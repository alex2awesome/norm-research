# Medium — Aggregated ERC Grant Writing Wisdom

Top-clapped Medium articles on European Research Council Starting, Consolidator, and Advanced grants, by recent winners and grant consultants.

## ERC Specific Grant Structure

### Part B1 (5 pages, "Extended Synopsis")
- Read by all panel reviewers
- Decides Step 1 evaluation (panel only)
- Functions like NIH Specific Aims but longer
- Must convey: question, ground-breaking nature, methodology, feasibility, impact

### Part B2 (15 pages, full proposal)
- Read by ad hoc external reviewers in Step 2
- Section a) State of the art and objectives
- Section b) Methodology (most pages)
- Section c) Resources

### CV and Track Record
- 2 pages CV + 2 pages track record
- 10 publications selected with rationale
- Major contributions narrative
- Awards, invited talks, leadership

## ERC-Specific Themes from Top Articles

### "Ground-Breaking" Is Non-Negotiable
- ERC's defining criterion is high-risk/high-gain
- Incremental work is explicitly disfavored
- Must articulate paradigm shift potential
- Frame: "This will change how we think about X"

### High-Risk/High-Gain Framing
- Reviewers explicitly look for risk
- BUT must demonstrate feasibility
- Standard structure: bold vision + risk mitigation
- "If this works, it transforms field; if not, it still produces X, Y, Z"

### Single PI Excellence
- ERC funds one PI; collaborators are secondary
- PI must be sole intellectual driver
- Track record of independent thinking critical
- "Senior PI parasitism" is a known killer

### Mobility and Originality
- Independence from PhD/postdoc supervisor
- Career trajectory shows originality
- Geographic/institutional mobility valued
- Returning to home country is fine if scientific case strong

## Author-Voice Tips That Repeat

### Starting Grant Specifics
- 2-7 years post-PhD eligibility
- Junior independence narrative critical
- Scope must be ambitious but feasible for early career
- 1.5M EUR over 5 years (rough budget)

### Consolidator Grant Specifics
- 7-12 years post-PhD
- Established lab; consolidating research direction
- 2M EUR over 5 years
- Track record of independent group leadership

### Advanced Grant Specifics
- 10+ years post-PhD, established excellence
- Visionary, transformative project
- 2.5M EUR over 5 years
- Track record at highest international level

### Synergy Grant Specifics
- 2-4 PIs, complementary expertise
- Problem requires multiple PIs (justify why)
- 10M EUR over 6 years
- Each PI's contribution specified

## Step 1 vs. Step 2 Strategy

### Step 1 (Panel Only)
- B1 + CV + track record
- Panel reads, scores, ranks
- ~30% pass to Step 2
- B1 must work as standalone document

### Step 2 (Panel + External Reviewers)
- Full B2 + interview (for some calls)
- External reviewers in PI's exact subfield
- Panel synthesizes external reviews
- Interview for shortlisted candidates

## ERC vs. NIH Cultural Differences (Per Top Articles)

### ERC Values More
- Single-PI vision
- Long-term, transformative work
- Cross-disciplinary boundaries
- European mobility narrative
- Risk-taking

### NIH Values More
- Team science
- Clinical/translational impact
- Established methodology
- Productivity per dollar
- Risk mitigation

## What Wins ERC (Aggregate)
1. Bold vision that could change the field
2. Strong CV with independent track record
3. Methodology with credible feasibility
4. Risk mitigation built in
5. Single-PI intellectual coherence
6. Cross-disciplinary novelty where genuine
7. Clear independence from previous mentors
8. Strong host institution commitment

## What Loses
1. Incremental project framing
2. Methodology too vague to evaluate
3. Track record of dependence on senior collaborators
4. Scope too narrow for ERC ambition
5. Risk without mitigation
6. Generic "impact" claims
7. Mismatch with grant level (junior applying for Advanced, senior for Starting)
