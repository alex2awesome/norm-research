# NEH - Application Review Process

## Overview
NEH conducts more than 160 review panels annually, involving more than 700 outside experts, evaluating approximately 2,500 applications across about 15 grant programs. Knowledgeable persons outside NEH read each application and advise the agency about its merits.

## Evaluation Ratings
Evaluators choose from the following ratings:
- **E** - Excellent
- **VG** - Very Good
- **G** - Good
- **SM** - Some Merit
- **NC** - Not Competitive

## Universal Review Criteria
Review criteria may vary by NOFO, but reviews will generally always address:
- **Significance** of your project
- **Originality** of your project
- **Feasibility** of your project

## Common NEH Review Criteria
Typical criteria across NEH grants:
1. Intellectual significance of the proposed project, including its value to humanities scholars, general audiences, or both
2. Quality of the conception, definition, organization, and description of the project, and the soundness of methodology
3. Likelihood of completion in a reasonable time frame
4. Quality of the staff, including expertise in the relevant scholarly fields
5. Reasonableness of the budget in relation to the proposed activities

## Multi-Level Review Process

NEH's review process has FOUR distinct but fully integrated levels:

1. **Knowledgeable persons independent of the agency** read each application
2. **Panel discussion** - Reviewers convene to discuss applications
3. **NEH staff** organizes the panel results and summarizes the reviews
4. Based on evaluators' ratings and comments, **NEH staff recommends** the most meritorious applications while maintaining an even level of quality across all panels
5. **National Council on the Humanities** advises the Chairman, who makes final funding decisions

## Reviewer Feedback
All applicants can receive upon request the evaluators' ratings and written comments. NEH does not provide the names of the evaluators with their comments.
