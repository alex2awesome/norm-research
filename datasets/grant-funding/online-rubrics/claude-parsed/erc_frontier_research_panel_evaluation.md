# ERC Frontier Research: Panel-Reviewer Operational Rubric

Detailed operational guide that ERC panel members and external reviewers use during evaluation. Wave 1 had general ERC entries; this is the explicit two-criterion panel-evaluation rubric.

## Single Statutory Criterion: Scientific Excellence

By EU regulation, the ERC may use ONLY one criterion: scientific excellence. This single criterion is operationalized as evaluations of TWO objects:

### A. The Research Project — assessed on:
1. **Ground-breaking nature** of the proposed research
   - Goes beyond state-of-the-art
   - Addresses important problems / barriers
   - Tackles new and emerging fields
2. **Potential impact**
   - Significance for advancing knowledge
   - Wider scientific or societal impact
3. **Scientific approach**
   - Soundness of objectives
   - Appropriateness of methodology
   - Existence of testable hypotheses (where applicable)
   - Quality of preliminary work
   - Feasibility within timeframe
   - Risk and risk-management appropriate to ambitions

### B. The Principal Investigator — assessed on:
1. **Intellectual capacity and creativity**
   - Track record demonstrating exceptional capacity
   - Original contributions to the field
   - Intellectual leadership beyond the immediate group
2. **Commitment to the project**
   - Time commitment (≥50% for Starting/Consolidator, ≥30% for Advanced)
   - Independence from previous mentors (for Starting/Consolidator)
3. **Career-stage-appropriate excellence**
   - Starting Grant: 2-7 years post-PhD; "exceptional promise"
   - Consolidator: 7-12 years; "outstanding research achievements"
   - Advanced: any stage; "exceptional leader" with "ground-breaking achievements"

## Two-Stage Evaluation Process

### Stage 1: Panel-Only Review (extended synopsis + CV)
Panel members give individual scores on a 4-point scale:
- **A** — fully meets ERC criteria, recommended for funding if budget allows
- **B** — meets ERC criteria but not in top tier
- **C** — does not fully meet ERC criteria

### Stage 2: External Expert Review (full proposal)
Top-ranked Stage 1 proposals receive:
- 3-5 external expert reviews
- Panel re-evaluation incorporating external reviews
- Interview with the PI (mandatory for Starting/Consolidator finalists)
- Final A/B/C scoring

## Score Definitions (the explicit rating descriptors)

- **A — Outstanding**: ground-breaking, exceptional PI, fully convincing
- **B — Excellent but with some weaknesses**: high quality but not at A level
- **C — Below ERC threshold**: does not meet excellence bar

## Explicit Reviewer Guidance (procedural rubric)

1. Apply ONLY the scientific excellence criterion — no policy, equity, regional, or strategic considerations.
2. Score each proposal on its own merits — no quotas.
3. Risk and ambition are positive features, not negative ones.
4. PI's CV must be assessed in the context of career stage and field norms.
5. Proposals from interdisciplinary work should be assessed by all relevant disciplines on the panel.
