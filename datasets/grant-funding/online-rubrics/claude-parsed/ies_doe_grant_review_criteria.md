# IES - Education Research Grant Review Criteria

## Four Major Review Criteria

Reviewers assess applications on the basis of FOUR criteria:

### 1. Significance
Does the applicant make a compelling case for the potential contribution of the project to the solution of an education problem?

For development projects: Does the applicant present a strong rationale — including a theoretical foundation and prior empirical evidence — justifying the development of the selected intervention or assessment?

### 2. Research Plan
- Quality of the research design
- Appropriate methods to address the research questions
- Sample size and analysis plan
- Plan for data analysis and interpretation
- Validity (internal and external)
- Reliability of measures

### 3. Personnel
- Qualifications of the PI and key personnel
- Time commitment of the team
- Expertise relevant to the proposed research

### 4. Resources
- Availability of resources to support the project
- Adequacy of institutional commitment
- Access to relevant data, sites, populations

## Standards for Excellence in Education Research (SEER)

IES SEER encourages rigorous education research that is:
- Transparent
- Actionable
- Focused on meaningful outcomes

SEER recommends that researchers:
- Pre-register studies
- Make findings, methods, and data open
- Address inequities in learners' opportunities
- Identify interventions' components
- Document treatment implementation and contrast
- Analyze interventions' costs
- Use high-quality outcome measures
- Facilitate generalization of study findings
- Support scaling of promising interventions

## Peer Review Process
- Office of Science oversees peer review
- RFAs include "Recommendations for a Strong Application" for each review criterion
- Peer reviewers consider these recommendations alongside their professional judgment

## What Works Clearhouse (WWC) Standards
The IES What Works Clearinghouse uses Study Review Protocols to evaluate educational interventions. Studies are rated:
- **Meets WWC Standards Without Reservations** (RCT with low attrition)
- **Meets WWC Standards With Reservations** (quasi-experimental designs meeting requirements)
- **Does Not Meet WWC Standards**

These standards influence what evidence IES recognizes as causal.
