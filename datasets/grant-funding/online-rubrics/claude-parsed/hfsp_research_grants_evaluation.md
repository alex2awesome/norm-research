# HFSP: Research Grants & Fellowships Evaluation Criteria

The Human Frontier Science Program funds frontier life-science research with a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary international teams.

## HFSP Research Grants — Three Core Criteria

1. **Novelty / scientific quality**
   - The proposed research must extend the frontiers of knowledge
   - Must NOT be obvious next-step work from the applicant's lab
   - Must NOT continue ongoing collaborations among the team
2. **Interdisciplinarity**
   - Team members must come from genuinely different disciplines
   - Crossing biological/physical/computational boundaries is favored
   - Mere multi-disciplinary collaboration is insufficient — true integration required
3. **Team integration**
   - Synergistic collaboration plan
   - Distinct contributions from each PI
   - Geographic diversity (cross-country teams favored)
   - Track record of team members in their respective fields

## HFSP Explicitly Discouraged Proposal Patterns

- Proposals that are "obvious next steps" in the applicants' labs
- Proposals where the team has previously published together
- Proposals that don't represent significant change in research direction
- Single-discipline proposals dressed up as interdisciplinary
- Established collaborations seeking continuation funding

## HFSP Postdoctoral Fellowships — Three Excellence Criteria

1. **Excellence of the applicant**
   - Accomplishments relative to career stage
   - Publication record (quality over quantity)
   - Letters of recommendation
   - Demonstrated potential
2. **Excellence of the proposed research**
   - Training potential (must teach the applicant new techniques/concepts)
   - Scientific originality
   - Innovative character
   - Clear departure from previous research (cross-disciplinary move required)
3. **Excellence of the host laboratory**
   - Quality of host PI
   - Infrastructure and resources available
   - Supervisor's track record with prior fellows

## Scoring Mechanism

- Two committee members initially review each application
- Alphabetical scores: **A (highest), B, C, D (lowest)**
- A third reviewer evaluates if scores differ significantly between the first two
- Final ranking based on aggregated reviewer scores

## Cross-Boundary Requirement (Fellowships)

For HFSP postdoc fellowships, the applicant's PhD must be in a clearly different discipline than the proposed postdoctoral work — this "discipline change" is itself a scoring criterion. Bench-to-computational moves and physicist-to-biologist moves are common targets.

## Implications for Rubric Design

HFSP's rubric is unusual in actively penalizing continuity (with prior work, prior collaborators, prior discipline). Most agencies reward continuity as evidence of feasibility; HFSP treats it as evidence of insufficient novelty.
