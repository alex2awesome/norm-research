# SNSF - Evaluation Procedure

## Evaluation Criteria
Reviewers assess proposals based on criteria defined by the SNSF, which include:
- **Scientific relevance** of the project
- **Originality** of the project
- **Applicant's expertise** and scientific track record

## Key Principle
The SNSF promotes the view that scientific quality across a researcher's entire output is much more important than publication metrics. The SNSF does NOT rely on:
- Journal-based metrics
- Journal reputation (impact factor)
- H-index
- Other simplistic metrics

(SNSF is a DORA signatory.)

## Selection Process Structure

The SNSF has implemented a standardized evaluation process across funding schemes. The three key steps are:

### 1. External Review
- For most funding schemes, at least two external reviewers assess proposals first
- Reviewers selected for relevant expertise

### 2. Internal Review
- Each application is independently evaluated by at least two members of the evaluation panel
- Recommendations provide the basis for subsequent discussion

### 3. Panel Discussion
- Discussion by the relevant evaluation panel
- Consensus building
- Final ranking

## Rating Scale
Reviewers and panel members rate applications on a uniform scale of **1 to 9**, where:
- **1** = an application has few or no merits and many serious weaknesses
- **9** = projects are outstanding in all respects
- Threshold typically requires high scores given competitive landscape

## Funding Decisions
Based on the ranking of proposals and available funding, the National Research Council determines the "funding line." Proposals with a score above the line will receive funding.

## Standard Sub-criteria

For Project funding (most common scheme):
1. Scientific quality and originality
2. Suitability of methods
3. Scientific significance
4. Topicality and impact
5. Feasibility
6. Quality of the proposal applicant

## Career Funding Schemes
Career-oriented schemes (Ambizione, Eccellenza, etc.) emphasize:
- Demonstrated independence
- Personal achievements
- Research vision
- Mobility and broadening of experience
