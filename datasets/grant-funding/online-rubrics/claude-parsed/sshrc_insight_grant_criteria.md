# SSHRC Insight Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Three Primary Evaluation Criteria with Weights

SSHRC's merit review process adjudicates applications based on three primary criteria:

### 1. Challenge — The aim and importance of the endeavour (50%)
- Originality, significance, and expected contribution to knowledge
- Appropriateness of the literature review
- Appropriateness of the theoretical approach or framework
- Appropriateness of the methods/approach
- Quality of the training and mentoring to be provided to students, emerging scholars and other highly qualified personnel
- Potential for the project results to have influence and impact within and/or beyond the social sciences and humanities research community

### 2. Feasibility — The plan to achieve excellence (20%)
- Appropriateness of the proposed timeline, plan and probability of completion
- Appropriateness of the requested budget and justification of proposed costs
- Quality of, and commitment to, training to be provided
- Strategy for ensuring outputs and other results are available to interested users

### 3. Capability — The expertise to succeed (30%)
- Quality, quantity, and significance of past experience and published and/or creative outputs
- Relevant experience and training of any team members and collaborators
- Potential to make important contributions
- Quality of past contributions to the training of students, emerging scholars and other highly qualified personnel

## Scoring
- Merit review committee members assign a score for each of the three criteria
- Appropriate weighting is then applied to arrive at a final score
- Applications must receive a score of **3.0 or higher for each of the three criteria** to be recommended for funding
- Score scale: typically 1 (poor) to 6 (excellent), with intermediate scores

## Review Process
- Applications reviewed through competitive merit review process
- Merit review committee makes recommendations
- SSHRC bases funding decisions on committee recommendations and available funds

## Feedback
All applicants receive:
- SSHRC's notice of decision
- Any external assessments received
- A summary of the merit review committee's evaluation

## EDI Considerations
While not explicitly listed as scoring criteria, the following are considered during peer review:
- Equity, diversity, and inclusion in research design
- Access considerations
- Indigenization
- Decolonization
- Intersectionality

## Application Categories
- Insight Grants ($7K-$400K over 2-5 years) — established researchers
- Insight Development Grants ($7K-$75K over 1-2 years) — exploratory/early stage
