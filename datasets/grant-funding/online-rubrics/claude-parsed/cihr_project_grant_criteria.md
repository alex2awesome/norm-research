# CIHR Project Grant Program - Evaluation Criteria

## Project Grant Evaluation Components

### Concept (assessed using sub-criteria)

**Significance and Impact of the Research**
- What is being proposed
- Why the project is worth doing
- Originality
- Anticipated contributions

### Feasibility (assessed using two sub-criteria)

**1. Approaches and Methods** (HOW and WHEN the project is expected to be completed)
- Soundness of approach
- Methodology
- Timeline and milestones
- Risk mitigation

**2. Expertise, Experience, and Resources** (WHO is involved and WHERE)
- Qualifications of the team
- Track record
- Available resources
- Institutional environment

## Review Process
Applications follow a committee-based peer review process:
- Group of reviewers with required experience and expertise
- Assess quality and potential impact of proposed research
- Within the context of the program's objectives

## Contextual Considerations
Reviewers consider the context of the applicant:
- Leave history
- Career stage
- Areas of research
- Experiential knowledge
- Diverse career paths
- Family responsibilities
- Pandemic impact

Reviewers consider how this context may have affected the applicant's progress and productivity when assessing each criterion.

## Rating Scale

Applications are scored on a numerical scale where:
- **4.5 to 4.9** = innovative, filling an important critical gap in knowledge, with very few flaws and investigators having a comprehensive complement of expertise, experience and resources
- **4.0 to 4.4** = highly competitive, addresses important question
- **3.5 to 3.9** = competitive but with notable weaknesses
- **3.0 to 3.4** = adequate
- Below 3.0 = significant weaknesses

## CIHR Foundation Grants (legacy program)
Different criteria emphasizing:
- Leadership and significance of research
- Research approach and quality
- Track record of leadership in field
- Mentorship and training of HQP

## EDI and Indigenous Health
CIHR integrates considerations of:
- Sex and gender-based analysis (SGBA+)
- Indigenous health research principles
- Equity, diversity, and inclusion in research teams
- Patient and community engagement (where relevant)
