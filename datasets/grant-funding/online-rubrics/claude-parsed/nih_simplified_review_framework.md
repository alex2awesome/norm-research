# NIH Simplified Peer Review Framework

## Overview
The NIH simplified peer review framework reorganizes five regulatory criteria into three factors for applications with due dates January 25, 2025 or later. Two factors receive numerical scores (1-9), while one is evaluated for adequacy.

## Factor 1: Importance of the Research
**Scoring: 1-9**

Combines Significance and Innovation elements. Reviewers assess:

**Significance:** Evaluate how the proposed work addresses critical gaps or creates conceptual advances. Consider "the importance of the proposed research in the context of current scientific challenges and opportunities" and whether rigorous scientific background justifies the study.

**Innovation:** Determine if the project employs novel concepts or applies existing methods in new ways. Note that critical research may not require technical innovation.

## Factor 2: Rigor and Feasibility
**Scoring: 1-9**

Examines Approach, including both rigor and feasibility components plus clinical trial timelines and inclusion adequacy.

**Rigor elements:** Reviewers evaluate potential for "unbiased, reproducible, robust data," experimental design quality, sample size justification, analysis plans, and biological variable considerations (sex, age). For human/animal studies, assess intervention quality, outcome variables, generalizability, and population diversity appropriateness.

**Feasibility elements:** Determine if approaches are "sound and achievable" with contingency planning. For human studies, evaluate recruitment/retention capacity and enrollment likelihood across demographic categories.

## Factor 3: Expertise and Resources
**Evaluation: Sufficient or Gaps Identified (No Numerical Score)**

**Investigators:** Assess whether researchers demonstrate appropriate "background, training, and expertise" for their career stage. For multiple PI applications, evaluate leadership coordination plans.

**Environment:** Confirm institutional resources "are appropriate to ensure the successful execution of the proposed work."

## Additional Review Criteria
Considered in overall impact but without separate scores:
- Human subjects protections (when applicable)
- Vertebrate animal welfare
- Biohazard evaluation
- Resubmission/renewal/revision appropriateness

Human subjects protections specifically evaluate five areas: risk levels, protective measures, benefits, knowledge importance, and safety monitoring.
