# Doris Duke Charitable Foundation - Grant Criteria

## Five Core Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Funding Gap / Critical Opportunity
Does the project address a significant funding gap or support a critical opportunity related to the foundation's mission?

### 2. Mutual Alignment
Is the project designed to achieve both the objectives of the applicant organization AND the program goals of the foundation?

### 3. Long-term Impact Potential
What is the project's potential for long-term impact in terms of:
- Replication
- Reach
- Visibility
- Changes in the field

### 4. Organizational Capacity
Does the applicant organization have the capacity to effectively execute the project?

### 5. Leverage Potential
What opportunity exists to leverage additional resources as a result of foundation funding?

## Three Evaluation Strategies

### 1. Monitoring and Assessment (Ongoing)
- Site visits
- Grantee consultations
- Review of annual progress reports
- Determines compliance with grant agreements
- Assesses initial outputs, outcomes and indicators
- Conducted by internal staff and outside consultants as needed

### 2. Initiative Evaluation (Periodic)
- Specific initiative or cluster of related grants selected for intensive review
- Usually involves outside consultants
- Determines whether funded initiatives appear to be achieving desired goals
- Outcomes-focused

### 3. Field Analysis (Strategic)
- Starting point in creating new initiatives
- Analyzes a field or sectors within a field
- Identifies potential funding opportunities
- Conducted every 5 years for systematic review

## Funding Priorities

The Doris Duke Charitable Foundation focuses on:
- Performing arts
- Environmental conservation
- Medical research
- Child wellbeing
- Building bridges between Muslim and non-Muslim Americans
