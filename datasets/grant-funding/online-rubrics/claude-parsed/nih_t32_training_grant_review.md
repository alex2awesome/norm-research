# NIH T32 Institutional Training Grant - Review Criteria

## Scoring Scale
Each of the review criteria receives an individual score of 1 to 9 (1=outstanding, to 9=poor). An overall impact/priority score is determined by considering the scored criteria, plus additional review criteria.

## Seven Major Review Criteria

T32 training grant applications are evaluated based on:

### 1. Training Program
- Goals and objectives of the training program
- Training program structure (didactic, research, professional development)
- Duration and intensity
- Specific cohort sizes and characteristics
- Coordination among program components

### 2. Training Program Director/Principal Investigator (PD/PI)
- Leadership experience in training
- Time commitment
- Research expertise
- Track record of mentorship
- Vision for the program

### 3. Preceptors/Mentors
- Quality and experience of mentors
- Funding levels (active grants supporting trainees)
- Diversity of expertise
- Track record of training successful trainees
- Time commitment to training

### 4. Past Training Record
Reviewers consider:
- How successful the program has been in attracting and retaining individuals from diverse populations
- How successful trainees are in completing the program
- How productive trainees are in research accomplishments and publications
- How successful trainees are in obtaining further training appointments, fellowships, and career development awards
- Achievement of productive scientific careers (research grants, honors, high-impact publications, scientific leadership)

### 5. Institutional Training Environment, Commitment, and Resources
- Quality of facilities
- Institutional commitment (financial, in-kind)
- Coordination with other training programs
- Trainee support services
- Mentorship infrastructure

### 6. Trainee Recruitment, Selection, and Retention Plan
- Diversity recruitment plan (URM, persons with disabilities, women)
- Selection criteria
- Application/admission process
- Retention strategies

### 7. Short-Term and Research Training Positions (where applicable)
- Justification for short-term experiences
- Quality of summer/intensive programs

## Additional Review Criteria

### Plan for Instruction in the Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR)
- Format
- Subject matter
- Faculty participation
- Frequency
- Plan for evaluation of trainees' RCR understanding

### Plan for Instruction in Methods for Enhancing Reproducibility
- Required for biomedical training programs
- Coverage of rigorous research design

### Recruitment and Retention Plan to Enhance Diversity
- Specific strategies
- Goals and metrics
- Evaluation plan

### Inclusion of Special Populations (where applicable)
- Children
- Older adults
- Sex/gender considerations

## Application Materials
- Training Program Plan
- Tables (Table 1: Census; Table 2: Participating Faculty; Tables 3-8: Trainee outcomes; etc.)
- Letters of support from key institutional officials
- PD/PI biosketch and other key personnel biosketches
- Detailed budget and justification
