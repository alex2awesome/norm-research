# EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowship - Selection Criteria

## Primary Selection Criteria
The primary selection criteria applied by the EMBO Fellowship Committee are:
- **Scientific excellence of the candidate**
- **Scientific excellence of the project**
- **Scientific excellence of the host laboratory**

## Selection Process Overview
- All eligible applications sent to the Fellowship Committee for pre-screening
- About twice the number of available fellowships are selected for an interview (~25% of applications pass pre-screening)
- Final selection by independent evaluation by five members of EMBO Fellowship Committee

## Three-Stage Selection Process

### Stage 1: Pre-screening
The committee reviews a condensed application form, which includes:
- Summary of the applicant's main scientific achievements
- Three most important publications
- Summary and biological significance of the proposed work
- Justification for choice of the host lab
- Reference and acceptance letters

### Stage 2: Interview
For applicants who passed pre-screening:
- An interviewer with expertise in the area of the application is selected from EMBO Members and EMBO Young Investigators
- Interviews conducted remotely via videoconferencing
- Last 90 minutes
- The applicant is responsible for contacting the interviewer
- Tests scientific depth, presentation, ability to defend the project

### Stage 3: Final Selection
- Applications selected to the 2nd stage independently evaluated by five members of EMBO Fellowship Committee
- Each member evaluates the full application
- Consensus or vote determines final list

## Important Evaluation Considerations

### Discouraged Application Types
- Applications to work with host laboratories with which the candidate has collaborated in the past generally receive lower priority for funding
- Projects that are a continuation of an applicant's PhD research will not be considered

### Mobility Required
- Strong emphasis on changing host institution after PhD
- Cross-country/cross-continent moves valued

## Timeline
The outcome of the selection is known between 16 to 18 weeks after the evaluation deadline.

## What Makes a Strong Application

### Candidate
- Excellent PhD productivity
- Strong publications (preferably first author in respected journals)
- Personal initiative and impact
- Clear research vision

### Project
- Original and ambitious
- Well-defined hypothesis
- Sound methodology
- Clearly differentiated from PhD work
- Feasible within fellowship duration

### Host Lab
- Renowned international research group
- Different from PhD lab
- Provides new techniques/approaches
- Strong record of training
- Letter of acceptance with detailed plan

## Evaluation Bias Considerations
- EMBO is a DORA signatory
- No reliance on journal impact factors as sole metric
- Quality of contributions over quantity
- Career interruptions considered fairly
