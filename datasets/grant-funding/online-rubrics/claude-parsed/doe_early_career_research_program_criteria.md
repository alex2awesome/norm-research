# DOE Office of Science - Early Career Research Program Review Criteria

## Program Overview
Provides support to exceptional researchers during the crucial early career years when many scientists do their most formative work. Funding is for at least five years at $750,000+ per year for university-based researchers.

## Eligibility
- Within 10 years of receiving a Ph.D.
- Untenured Assistant or Associate Professor at U.S. academic institution OR full-time employee at DOE National Laboratory
- U.S. or foreign national in the U.S.

## Review Process
- Funding competitively awarded on the basis of peer review
- Selection based on peer review by outside scientific experts
- Two-stage process: pre-applications reviewed first
- Only applicants whose pre-application is encouraged may submit full applications

## Research Topic Requirements
- Proposed research topics must fall within the programmatic priorities of DOE's Office of Science
- Programs include: Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR), Basic Energy Sciences (BES), Biological and Environmental Research (BER), Fusion Energy Sciences (FES), High Energy Physics (HEP), Nuclear Physics (NP), Isotope R&D and Production, Accelerator R&D
- Seeks to support BASIC research to advance understanding rather than to address commercial opportunities
- Applications proposing research related to current commercial activity or current customer needs may be declined without merit review

## Evaluation Criteria
DOE uses standard merit review criteria including:
- Scientific and/or technical merit of the project
- Appropriateness of the proposed method or approach
- Competency of personnel and adequacy of proposed resources
- Reasonableness and appropriateness of the proposed budget
- Relevance and importance to mission needs and program priorities
- Likelihood of success and development of the early-career investigator
