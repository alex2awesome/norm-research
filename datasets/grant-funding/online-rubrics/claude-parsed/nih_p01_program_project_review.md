# NIH P01 Program Project Grant - Review Criteria

## Overview
A Program Project Grant (PPG or P01) supports synergistic research and is made up of:
- At least three subprojects
- One or more cores (serving some or all subprojects)

The PPG allows investigators with differing areas of expertise to collaborate in research by pooling their talents and resources, to produce results that would not be possible with individual, independent projects.

## Three-Component Review

For NICHD program project (P01) applications, peer review of scientific and technical merit focuses on three areas:

### 1. Review of Individual Research Project Components
Each subproject is reviewed using the same five criteria as standalone research projects:
- **Significance**
- **Investigator(s)**
- **Innovation**
- **Approach**
- **Environment**

Each subproject receives an overall impact score (1-9).

### 2. Review of Individual Core Units
Cores are evaluated for:
- Whether they are essential and justified for the proposed research
- Whether they have the capability to fulfill the proposed function
- Whether they service two or more Projects (typically required)

Cores are rated as **"Recommended"** or **"Not Recommended"** rather than receiving numerical scores.

Common core types:
- Administrative Core
- Biostatistics Core
- Imaging Core
- Genomics Core
- Animal Core
- Clinical Core
- Data Management Core

### 3. Review of the P01 Program as an Integrated Whole

Reviewers assess:
- Is the Program as a whole **scientifically compelling**?
- Is there **coordination and synergy** of the individual research Projects and Cores towards the achievement of the central objectives of the Program?
- Does the program leverage the cores effectively?
- Does the program create value beyond what the individual projects could achieve separately?

The overall P01 receives an Overall Impact Score (1-9).

## General Evaluation Principle
"An application does not need to be strong in all categories to be judged likely to have major scientific impact. For example, a program that by its nature is not innovative may be essential to advance a field."

## What Reviewers Look For

### Synergy
- Projects that genuinely depend on each other
- Cores that provide unique value
- Coordinated leadership
- Shared resources that wouldn't otherwise exist

### Coordination
- Strong overall PI leadership
- Regular meetings/communication structure
- Internal advisory committee
- External advisory committee
- Annual planning processes

### Critical Mass
- Multiple investigators of high caliber
- Established collaboration history
- Institutional commitment
- Resources beyond what individual grants would provide

## Common P01 Weaknesses
- Lack of true synergy (could be parallel R01s)
- Cores that don't justify their cost
- Single project that dominates (not really multi-project)
- Weak overall PI leadership
- Insufficient internal coordination plan
- Cores not integrated into projects

## P01 Strategic Considerations
- Significant institutional commitment required
- Multi-year planning (often 5+ years lead time)
- Investigators must have demonstrated track record together
- Often used to renew/expand existing collaborations

## Variations Across Institutes
- NCI P01s often emphasize translational components
- NIA P01s focus on aging-relevant integration
- NICHD P01s emphasize child health applications
- NHLBI P01s often have clinical components
- Each institute may have specific announcements with additional criteria
