# Quora — NSF GRFP Top-Voted Answer Aggregate

Top-voted Quora answers on winning the NSF GRFP, by recent winners, honorable mentions, and faculty reviewers.

## Universal Top-Voted Themes

### Personal Statement Wins or Loses Most Applications
- 60-70% of weight per multiple winner accounts
- Your story, not your CV
- Specific moments that shaped you
- Connect identity to research interests
- Concrete broader impacts plans

### Research Plan Demonstrates Capacity, Not Commitment
- Doesn't have to be your thesis
- Show ability to formulate and propose research
- Plan should fit your training and resources
- Include Intellectual Merit and Broader Impacts subheadings explicitly

### Reviewers Are Generalists
- Your panel covers a broad area, not your subfield
- Define jargon
- Explain why your question matters
- Use accessible analogies

### Letters of Recommendation Matter
- Three letters, mix research and academic
- Best: 2 research mentors + 1 academic
- Provide writers with CV, drafts, talking points
- 6-8 weeks lead time

## Personal Statement Frameworks

### "The Hero's Journey" (Most Common Winning Structure)
1. Origin moment (specific anecdote)
2. Educational journey (degrees, formative experiences)
3. Research experiences (each with role, contribution, lesson)
4. Mentors who shaped you
5. Vision (concrete future career goals)
6. Broader contribution plans (specific, with named activities)

### "Identity-Centered" Framework
- Open with your background
- Connect identity to research interests
- Outreach as continuation of personal commitment
- Vision as service to your community

### "Three Research Experiences" Framework
- Each experience: lab, role, contribution, lesson, output
- Show progression and increasing independence
- Connect to current and future interests

## Research Plan Best Practices

### Structure (Quora Aggregate)
- Title that signals the question
- 2-3 sentence hook
- 1 paragraph background
- 1 paragraph research question and hypothesis
- 1 paragraph methodology with timeline
- 1 paragraph expected outcomes
- 1 paragraph Intellectual Merit (subheading)
- 1 paragraph Broader Impacts (subheading)

### Common Research Plan Mistakes
- Too literature-review-y
- No clear methodology
- No timeline
- Missing IM/BI subheadings
- Plan outside your training
- Plan that requires unavailable resources

## Tactical Tips That Repeat

### Format
- 11pt Times New Roman or similar
- 1-inch margins
- Single-spaced
- Section headings bold
- IM/BI subheadings explicit and bold

### Read Funded Examples
- Alex Lang's archive
- Mallory Ladd's resources
- ProFellow database
- Departmental fellowship office samples

### Reuse Across Years
- First-year and second-year grad students can apply
- Save and revise
- Track which letter writers, which experiences
- Each year improves with feedback

## Broader Impacts (Quora-Cited Effective Activities)

### Effective BI Activities (with Specifics)
- Named outreach programs (with hours, students reached, outcomes)
- Mentorship of underrepresented students (numbers, names where possible)
- Public communication (specific blog, podcast, articles)
- Curriculum development (specific courses)
- Service in scientific societies
- Open science contributions (code, data, protocols on GitHub/OSF)

### Ineffective BI Claims
- "I will mentor undergrads" (no specifics)
- "I will engage in outreach" (no specifics)
- "I will publish my work" (that's standard)
- Generic diversity statements without personal grounding

## What Top-Voted Quora Answers Say

### "First Year vs. Second Year Application"
- First year: research plan based on undergrad/early grad work
- Second year: research plan based on actual current work
- Both can win; different framing
- Don't apply third year (eligibility limit)

### "Honorable Mention vs. Award"
- Both meaningful for academic CV
- HM funding rates similar to top 10-15%
- HM still eligible for graduate study without funding obligation
- Award provides 3 years of funding

### "How Much Time to Spend"
- 80-150 hours for competitive application
- Personal Statement: 30-50 hours over 6-8 drafts
- Research Plan: 20-40 hours over 4-6 drafts
- Letters: 6-8 weeks lead time

## On Multi-Fellowship Strategy

### Apply In Parallel
- NSF GRFP, Ford, NDSEG, Hertz, DOE SCGSR, EPA STAR
- Same content, different formatting
- Different funders weight differently:
  - NSF GRFP: balanced IM/BI
  - Ford: diversity-centered narrative
  - NDSEG: defense-relevant research
  - Hertz: long-form interview
  - DOE SCGSR: specific lab placement at national lab
