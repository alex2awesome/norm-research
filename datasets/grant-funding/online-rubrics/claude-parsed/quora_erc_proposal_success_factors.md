# Quora — ERC Proposal Top-Voted Answer Aggregate

Top-voted Quora answers on writing successful ERC proposals (Starting, Consolidator, Advanced), by recent winners and ERC panel members.

## Universal Top-Voted Themes

### "Ground-Breaking" Is Non-Negotiable
- ERC's defining criterion
- Must articulate paradigm shift potential
- Frame: "This will change how we think about X"
- Incremental work explicitly disfavored

### High-Risk / High-Gain Framing
- Reviewers explicitly look for risk
- BUT must demonstrate feasibility
- Standard: bold vision + risk mitigation
- "If this works, transformative; if not, still produces X, Y, Z"

### Single PI Excellence
- ERC funds one PI; collaborators secondary
- PI must be sole intellectual driver
- Track record of independent thinking critical
- Senior PI parasitism is a known killer

### B1 Synopsis Is Crucial
- 5 pages, read by all panelists
- Decides Step 1 evaluation
- Functions like NIH Specific Aims but longer
- Must convey: question, ground-breaking nature, methodology, feasibility, impact

## Tactical Tips That Repeat

### Step 1 Strategy
- B1 + CV + track record only
- Panel reads, scores, ranks
- ~30% pass to Step 2
- B1 must work standalone
- Strong CV can save weak B1; weak CV can't be saved

### Step 2 Strategy
- Full B2 + interview (for some calls)
- External reviewers in PI's exact subfield
- Panel synthesizes external reviews
- Interview for shortlisted candidates
- 8-15 minute presentation + Q&A

### Track Record Selection
- 10 publications selected with rationale
- Major contributions narrative
- Independent contribution must be clear
- Awards, invited talks, leadership

### CV Conventions
- Career narrative showing independence
- Mobility (institutions, countries)
- Major fellowships, awards
- Plenary talks, keynotes
- Editorial roles

## ERC-Specific Sections

### B1 Section a) State of the Art and Objectives (1-2 pages)
- Where the field is
- What's missing
- Your bold question
- Specific objectives (3-5)

### B1 Section b) Methodology (2-3 pages)
- How you'll address each objective
- Why these methods
- Risks and mitigation
- Feasibility evidence
- Timeline

### B1 Section c) Impact (1 page)
- Scientific impact (paradigm shift)
- Broader societal/economic impact (less weighted than NSF BI)
- Career trajectory benefit (especially for Starting/Consolidator)

## Career Stage Specifics

### Starting Grant
- 2-7 years post-PhD
- Junior independence narrative
- Scope: ambitious but feasible for early career
- 1.5M EUR over 5 years (rough)
- CV shows post-PhD independent contribution

### Consolidator Grant
- 7-12 years post-PhD
- Established lab, consolidating direction
- 2M EUR over 5 years
- Track record of independent group leadership

### Advanced Grant
- 10+ years post-PhD, established excellence
- Visionary, transformative project
- 2.5M EUR over 5 years
- Highest international standing

### Synergy Grant
- 2-4 PIs, complementary expertise
- Problem requires multiple PIs (justify why)
- 10M EUR over 6 years
- Each PI's contribution specified

## What Quora Aggregate Says Wins

### Winning Themes
1. Bold vision that could change the field
2. Strong CV with independent track record
3. Methodology with credible feasibility
4. Risk mitigation built in
5. Single-PI intellectual coherence
6. Cross-disciplinary novelty where genuine
7. Clear independence from previous mentors
8. Strong host institution commitment

### Losing Themes
1. Incremental project framing
2. Methodology too vague to evaluate
3. Track record of dependence on senior collaborators
4. Scope too narrow for ERC ambition
5. Risk without mitigation
6. Generic "impact" claims
7. Mismatch with grant level

## ERC vs. NIH Cultural Differences

### ERC Values
- Single-PI vision
- Long-term, transformative work
- Cross-disciplinary boundaries
- European mobility narrative
- Risk-taking

### NIH Values
- Team science
- Clinical/translational impact
- Established methodology
- Productivity per dollar
- Risk mitigation

## Interview Tips (Step 2)

### Format
- 8-15 min presentation + Q&A
- Panel + sometimes external expert
- Slides expected
- English (most panels)

### What Wins Interviews
- Clear, confident articulation of vision
- Direct answers to questions
- Acknowledgment of weaknesses with mitigation
- Engagement with feedback
- Energy and conviction

### What Loses Interviews
- Reading slides
- Defensive posture
- Ignoring panel concerns
- Vague answers
- Lack of conviction
