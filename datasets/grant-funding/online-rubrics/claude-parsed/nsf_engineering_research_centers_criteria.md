# NSF Engineering Research Centers (ERC) - Review Criteria

## Overview
NSF Engineering Research Centers (ERCs) are large-scale, multi-institutional centers conducting use-inspired, convergent research that addresses pressing societal problems. Gen-4 ERCs require interacting foundational components.

## Four Foundational Components

Each ERC requires interacting foundational components that go beyond the research project:

### 1. Convergent Research Project
- Use-inspired
- Engineered system focus
- Convergent (multi-disciplinary integration)

### 2. Engineering Workforce Development (EWD)
- Workforce development at all participant stages
- K-12 outreach
- Undergraduate research
- Graduate training
- Postdoctoral development
- Industry engagement

### 3. Diversity and a Culture of Inclusion (DCI)
- Diversity at all levels of participation
- Mutual benefit for all participants
- Inclusive environment
- Recruitment and retention of underrepresented groups

### 4. Innovation Ecosystem (IE)
- Value creation that will outlast the lifetime of the ERC
- Industry partnerships
- Translation pathways
- Entrepreneurship support
- Sustainable post-ERC innovation

## Review Approach

### Multi-Disciplinary Reviewers
All appropriate experts are brought in to review all aspects of the proposal:
- Research expertise
- Workforce development
- Innovation ecosystem
- Diversity and culture of inclusion

One should NOT assume that only engineers will be reviewing the proposals.

### Reviewer Preparation
The reviewers are prepared through:
- Webinars
- Dedicated training sessions
- Explicit instructions to respond to solicitation specific review criteria
- So they are in-tune with the expectations of Gen-4 ERC program

## Strategic Plan Requirements

The Strategic Plan must define:
- The engineered system
- How the features of the ERC will be integrated to achieve the vision
- Cohesive plan for involving participants at all levels in the four foundational components
- Vision and goals for each foundational component

## Management and Leadership Requirements

The proposal must describe the proposed management:
- Functions of key personnel
- Role of any advisory committee
- Required Student Leadership Council
- Council of Deans (institutional commitment)

## Required Plan Components

1. Vision and Goals
2. Research thrusts (interconnected)
3. Engineering Workforce Development plan
4. Diversity and Culture of Inclusion plan
5. Innovation Ecosystem plan
6. Strategic Management Plan
7. Sustainability Plan
8. Industry partnerships
9. Education partnerships
10. Budget across components

## Stages of ERC Competition

### Pre-Proposal Stage
- Letter of Intent
- Pre-proposal (concept)
- Selected for full proposal invitation

### Full Proposal Stage
- Comprehensive proposal across all components
- Strategic plan
- Management plan
- Site visit if shortlisted

### Site Visit
- Multi-day visit by NSF and external panel
- In-depth evaluation of plans, capacity, partnerships
- Final ranking

## ERC Award Details
- 5-year initial award (renewable to 10 years total)
- Typically $25-35M total (~$5-7M/year)
- Multi-institutional collaborative
- Substantial cost share required
- Strong industry partnerships expected
