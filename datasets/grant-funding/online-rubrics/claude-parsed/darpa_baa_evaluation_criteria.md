# DARPA - Broad Agency Announcement (BAA) Evaluation Criteria

## Mission Context
DARPA's mission is to make pivotal investments in breakthrough technologies for national security. Proposals are evaluated against a BAA's specific criteria designed to support DARPA's mission to **create or prevent technological surprise**.

## Standard DARPA Evaluation Criteria

DARPA evaluates proposals based on the following criteria:

### 1. Technical Approach
- Innovation: Is the approach genuinely novel?
- Feasibility: Can it actually be done?
- Clarity: Is it well-defined?
- Connection to state of the art

### 2. Mission Alignment
- Work supports DARPA's mission to create or prevent technological surprise
- Aligns with stated BAA technical goals
- Addresses explicit metrics in the BAA

### 3. Team Capability
- Strong expertise and execution capability
- Track record relevant to proposed work
- Necessary facilities and resources
- Multi-disciplinary team if needed

### 4. Risk Assessment
- Technical risks identified with credible mitigation strategies
- Acceptance of high risk for high reward
- Realistic risk register

### 5. Cost Realism
- Costs are realistic and aligned with the proposed technical approach
- Detailed budget justification
- Cost effectiveness

## Evaluation Process

### Individual Proposal Review
- Government evaluates each proposal in its entirety
- Documents strengths and weaknesses relative to each evaluation criterion
- Proposals are evaluated on their own individual merit
- NOT directly compared to other proposals during initial evaluation

### Selectability Standard
A "selectable" proposal is one that:
- Has been evaluated by the Government against the evaluation criteria
- The strengths of the overall proposal outweigh its weaknesses
- No accumulated weaknesses requiring extensive negotiations or resubmitted proposal

### Award Decisions
Awards made to proposers whose proposals are determined to be most advantageous to the Government, consistent with:
- Instructions and evaluation criteria specified in the BAA
- Availability of funding
- Programmatic balance

## BAA Structure
A program BAA defines:
- Area of interest
- Type of award (contract, grant, or cooperative agreement)
- Specific topics within the program
- Required proposal sections
- Evaluation criteria

BAAs are open to all proposers who can meet the agency's needs (industry, academia, FFRDCs, individuals, foreign organizations under specific conditions).

## DARPA-Unique Features
- Heavy emphasis on Heilmeier Catechism
- Program Manager-driven
- Time-bounded programs (typically 3-5 years)
- "Go/no-go" milestones
- Phase-gated funding
- Government-furnished property/data
- Test and Evaluation (T&E) by independent teams

## Typical Proposal Sections
- Executive Summary (no jargon)
- Technical Approach
- Statement of Work
- Schedule and Milestones
- Cost Proposal
- Risks and Risk Mitigation
- Team Qualifications and Experience
- Past Performance
