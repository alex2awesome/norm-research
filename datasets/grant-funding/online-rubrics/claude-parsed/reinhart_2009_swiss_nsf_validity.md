# Reinhart 2009: Reliability, Fairness, Validity at SNSF

Empirical study of 496 SNSF biology and medicine grant applications from 1998, applying the three classical psychometric criteria.

## Three Evaluative Dimensions Tested

1. **Reliability** — agreement between reviewers on the same application.
2. **Fairness** — absence of bias on irrelevant applicant characteristics.
3. **Validity** — predictive accuracy for future research outcomes.

## Reliability Results (intraclass correlation)

- **Overall: ICC ≈ 0.41** (fair, but low absolute level).
- **Biology: ICC ≈ 0.45**.
- **Medicine: ICC ≈ 0.20** — strikingly poorer agreement among medicine reviewers.

## Fairness Results (variables tested as potential bias sources)

Using multiple logistic regression on funding decisions, the following were tested as predictors:

1. Applicant **gender**
2. Applicant **age**
3. Applicant **nationality**
4. Applicant **academic status / rank**
5. **Requested funding amount**
6. **Institutional affiliation**

**None of these were significant predictors** of funding outcome — only scientific performance indicators were. Important counter-evidence to broader gender-bias claims.

## Validity Results

- Grant review outcomes only weakly predict future bibliometric performance of the applicant.
- Funding decisions ARE in line with publication success in aggregate, but at individual proposal level the signal is noisy.

## Implicit SNSF Quality Criteria

The SNSF's evaluation rubric in this period included:

1. Scientific quality / originality of the proposal
2. Methodology
3. Feasibility / work plan
4. Applicant track record
5. Importance of expected results

## Implications for Rubric Design

- Reliability differences between fields (medicine vs biology) suggest field-specific calibration is needed.
- Negative finding on bias is significant: well-designed European review processes may not exhibit the gender/institutional biases found elsewhere.
- Validity is real but weak — rubrics should not promise more precision than the underlying signal supports.
