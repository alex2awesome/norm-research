# DFG (German Research Foundation) - Grant Evaluation Criteria

## Core Evaluation Criteria
DFG funding proposals are assessed based on scientifically relevant criteria alone. Reviewers assess proposals on a voluntary basis according to:
- **Scientific excellence**
- **Relevance**
- **Originality**

## Three-Stage Process: Review, Assessment, Decision
The DFG has developed a differentiated three-stage process designed to ensure decisions are based on professional and fair assessment of all relevant aspects of a proposal compared to other proposals:

### Stage 1: Review
- Approximately 17,000 reviewers consulted annually from Germany and other countries
- Selected based on specialist expertise
- Independent assessments

### Stage 2: Assessment (Evaluation)
- DFG review boards evaluate the reviews
- Apply standardized assessment criteria across proposals

### Stage 3: Decision
- Joint Committee/Senate makes final funding decisions
- Separation of review, evaluation, and decision functions

## Special Considerations

### Equity and Diversity
In DFG's coordinated programmes, equity and diversity in research are also relevant to the review.

### Career Circumstances
Any special and unavoidable personal circumstances pertaining to an applicant are taken into account exclusively in their favour:
- Periods of childcare
- Periods of absence due to health
- Care of family members
- Other documented circumstances

These details are supplied voluntarily by applicants.

## Standard Evaluation Dimensions for Individual Research Grants

1. **Scientific quality and originality** of the project
2. **Relevance** to current research questions
3. **Methodological approach** and feasibility
4. **Qualifications and expertise** of the applicants
5. **Track record** including publications, projects, and research environment
6. **Cooperation** plans (where applicable)
7. **Risk assessment** and contingency plans

## Open Science and Data Practices
DFG signed DORA (Declaration on Research Assessment) and emphasizes:
- No reliance on journal impact factors
- Quality of research over quantity
- Open data and reproducibility
