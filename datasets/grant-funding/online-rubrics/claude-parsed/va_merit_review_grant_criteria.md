# VA - Merit Review Grant Criteria

## Merit Review Process Overview
Merit Review is an objective process of evaluating competing Federal award applications in accordance with written standards set forth by VA and in accordance with 2 C.F.R. § 200.205.

## Application Evaluation
- Applications are reviewed/scored in accordance with evaluation criteria included in the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO)
- Reviewers individually review and score each application using scoring guidelines outlined in the original NOFO
- Reviewers serving on Scientific Merit Review Board assign each application a single score reflecting their assessment of overall scientific merit

## Scoring Process
- Individual reviewer scores are averaged
- Then multiplied by 100 to determine final score
- Lower score = better
- Threshold scores vary by service

## Categories of Reviewer Scores

### Scored Applications
- Applications discussed at panel meeting
- Each receives a final overall score
- Score informs funding decisions

### Unscored Applications (Streamlining)
- Any proposal with average preliminary score in the lower (least meritorious) 25-30%
- Not discussed in panel
- May be appropriate for resubmission, depending on comments in written critiques

## Major VA Research Services and Their Mechanisms

### BLR&D - Biomedical Laboratory Research and Development
- I01 Merit Review (the standard grant)
- IK1, IK2 Career Development Awards
- I21 pilot/exploratory

### CSR&D - Clinical Science Research and Development
- I01 Merit Review (clinical research)
- IK1, IK2 Career Development Awards
- I50 Center grants

### HSR&D - Health Services Research and Development
- IIR (Investigator-Initiated Research) Merit Review
- IK2 Career Development Awards
- Quality Enhancement Research Initiative (QUERI)

### RR&D - Rehabilitation Research and Development
- I01 Merit Review (rehab focus)
- IK1, IK2 Career Development Awards

## Standard Review Criteria

### 1. Significance to Veterans
- Direct relevance to Veteran health or disability
- Mission alignment with VA priorities
- Potential to improve veteran care

### 2. Scientific Merit
- Quality and innovation of the research
- Clear hypotheses and aims
- Sound experimental design
- Rigor and reproducibility

### 3. Investigator Qualifications
- Track record
- Expertise
- VA appointment status (typically required - 5/8ths)

### 4. Approach
- Methodology
- Feasibility
- Statistical analysis
- Power calculations

### 5. Environment
- VA facility resources
- Institutional commitment
- Collaborations
- Access to veteran populations

## Key VA-Specific Considerations
- PI must hold a paid VA appointment (typically 5/8ths or more)
- Research must be conducted at VA facility (or via approved arrangements)
- Veteran patient population must be appropriately accessible
- Alignment with VA Office of Research and Development priorities
- Research relevant to military service-related conditions
