# Quora — NIH R01 Top-Voted Answer Aggregate

Top-voted Quora answers on writing a competitive NIH R01 application, by NIH-funded PIs and former study section reviewers.

## Universal Top-Voted Themes

### Start With the Question, Not the Method
- Reviewers want to know "what are you trying to find out?"
- Methods come later; question and significance up front
- "We will use technique X to study Y" is backward
- "We will determine Y by using technique X" is forward

### Specific Aims Page Is Everything
- 1 page, but most-iterated document
- 90% of reviewer impression formed here
- Triaged grants get only this read
- Iterate 20+ times before submitting

### Hypothesis-Driven > Descriptive
- "We will characterize" = death sentence
- "We will test the hypothesis that X drives Y" = win
- Hypothesis must be specific, falsifiable, mechanistic
- Each Aim should have a hypothesis

### Aim Independence Is Non-Negotiable
- "If Aim 1 fails, Aim 2/3 still works"
- Parallel logic > serial logic
- Three independent threads, not one chain
- Most-cited reason for low Approach scores

### Preliminary Data Must Be Quantitative
- Effect sizes, error bars, N
- Box plots with individual points
- Replication evidence (N=3+ biological)
- Power calculation tied to preliminary effect size

## Quora Specific Tactical Wisdom

### Work Backwards From the Aims Page
- Draft Specific Aims first
- Get senior feedback
- Iterate 5+ times before drafting Significance/Approach
- Then draft Significance, then Approach
- Aims page is your contract with the reviewer

### Find a Senior Mentor or Mock Reviewer
- Senior PIs read drafts at multiple stages
- Mock review committees score 1-9 NIH style
- Outside-field reader for clarity check
- Editing services for non-native English speakers

### Engage Program Officers Early
- POs accessible by email
- Send Specific Aims for fit feedback
- Ask about mechanism, payline, RFA opportunities
- Build relationship over time

### Read Funded R01s in Your Area
- NIH RePORTER for titles, abstracts
- Ask senior PIs for full applications
- NIAID sample applications publicly available
- Pattern-match successful structures

## Career Stage Considerations

### ESI (Early Stage Investigator)
- More forgiving payline
- Independence narrative important
- Productivity proportionate to career stage
- Don't propose senior-PI scope

### Mid-Career
- Productivity per dollar matters
- Diversification of funders strategically valued
- Service as reviewer builds credibility
- Mentorship of trainees noted

### Senior
- Track record of mentorship valued
- Risk-taking can be rewarded
- Productivity per dollar still matters
- Avoid "old boy" trap of complacency

## Common Top-Voted Mistakes

### Top Mistakes Quora Aggregate Lists
1. Co-dependent aims
2. Vague Significance
3. Insufficient preliminary data
4. Missing rigor language
5. No statistical analysis plan
6. Investigator overreach for career stage
7. Padded budget
8. Generic letters of support
9. Unrealistic timeline
10. Methodological vagueness

### Specific Avoidance Tactics
- Don't write "we will explore" or "we will characterize" — use hypothesis verbs
- Don't propose 4 aims (3 max for R01)
- Don't list every paper in your field — cite the high-impact recent ones
- Don't pad the budget — reviewers cut what looks padded
- Don't ignore reviewer concerns in resubmission — address every one

## On Resubmission

### Strategy
- Most R01s funded after resubmission
- Address every critique substantively
- Use reviewer language verbatim
- Quote critique, respond with substance
- Bold changes in body text
- Introduction page (1 page) for response summary

### Tone
- Diplomatic, not defensive
- Acknowledge fair criticism
- Politely push back where critique is wrong
- Always offer substance, not just rebuttal
