# ERC Synergy Grant - Evaluation Criteria

## Sole Evaluation Criterion
Proposals are evaluated on the sole criterion of scientific excellence, which takes on the additional meaning of "outstanding intrinsic synergetic effect."

Peer reviewers assess distinct features when assessing the excellence of the proposal:
- Synergy
- Complementarity of the Principal Investigators
- Collaborative working arrangements
- Risk

## Three-Stage Evaluation Process

Unlike the two-step evaluation of personal grants, the SyG proposal review consists of three steps, including an interview:

**Stage 1:** Only part B1 is evaluated (5-page project summary, CVs, track records).

**Stage 2:** The entire proposal is evaluated, i.e., B1 and B2 (15-page project description). The complete proposal is reviewed.

**Stage 3:** The interview is the final stage of the evaluation. The aspects demonstrated in the presentation should clearly convey the synergistic nature of the project.

## Proposal Scoring
The evaluation of the Principal Investigators and the project is carried out with a grading: A/B/C.

## Panel Composition
To account for complex interdisciplinary projects, the panels' composition is dynamic and subject to change in order to fit the multidisciplinary nature of the proposals.

## Group Composition
Synergy Grants support 2-4 Principal Investigators (Corresponding PI + 1-3 PIs) who jointly address ambitious research problems. The team must demonstrate that the science is genuinely synergistic - not just collaborative.
