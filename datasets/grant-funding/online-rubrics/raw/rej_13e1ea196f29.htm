Submit & Manage Word Critiques, Scores

Instructions for submitting critiques and scores for assigned applications during the submit and edit phases in IAR

Reviewers have to submit critiques and scores electronically for assigned applications during the Submit and Edit phases in IAR. Reviewers may also submit critiques and scores for applications that they are not assigned.

Note that reviewers will be unable to submit critiques and scores if they have not signed their pre-meeting conflict of interest certification in IAR. An alert will notify reviewers and they will need to click a link to go ahead and certify the pre-meeting COI before they can submit critiques and scores.

IMPORTANT: The instructions below are for critiques submitted on Microsoft Word critique templates. For meetings with online critiques, please see the Fill Out and Submit Online Critiques webpage.

Basic Tasks (step-by-step instructions and screenshots)*

  • Locating Word critique templates — Microsoft Word templates can be found in IAR’s meeting materials folder, or by clicking on the application’s ‘Critique Template’ link on the List of All Applications screen. Reviewers can also download all critique templates for all applications specifically assigned to them by using the zip file link on the List of My Assigned Applications screen or the List of All Applications screen (See Reviewers: Locating Critique Template link in IAR). If you have any questions, please contact your SRO.

Note: Only Word documents (doc, docx) can be uploaded, not pdfs
