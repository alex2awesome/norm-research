Funding

What do we offer?

On this page you’ll find information about our main grants, as well as some additional opportunities for researchers who are thinking of applying. This is also your first port of call if you’ve decided to apply but you are not sure where to start from.

ERC STARTING GRANTS

These grants are for researchers with 2-7 years of experience since the completion of their PhD, a scientific track record showing great promise and an excellent research proposal.

ERC Consolidator Grants

These grants are for researchers with 7-12 years of experience since completion of PhD, a scientific track record showing great promise and an excellent research proposal.

ERC Advanced Grants

These grants are for active researchers with a track-record of significant research achievements in the last 10 years.

ERC Proof of concept

These grants are for researchers who have already received an ERC grant for their frontier research project and now want to explore the commercial or societal potential of it.

ERC Synergy Grants

These grants are for two to maximum four researchers working together and bringing different skills and resources to tackle ambitious research problems.

Additional opportunities

There are other initiatives linked to ERC funding. If you are not planning to apply for a grant, or it’s too early to, you may still be interested in these.

How to find more help?

Decided you’d like to give applying a try but you don’t know where to start? Here is some information that will help.

National contact points

These are some of the first people you should contact about your ERC application. There is one based in every EU and Associated Country, as well as in some countries outside Europe. They are designated to help applicants out in their own countries.

frequently asked questions

Have a question about your ERC application? Chances are you’re not alone. Throughout the years, we have collected some of the most frequently asked questions to guide you through the process.

Documents and publications

If you are considering applying, you'll have several key documents to refer to, like the Work Programme and the Information for Applicants. These highlight the specifics of the calls every year, as well as providing some useful information to think about before you embark on the process itself.

For non EU-researchers

If you are a researcher from outside the EU and you wish to apply check out some of the things you should consider.

Step-by-step

For a short introduction to ERC grants, watch our step-by-step video.

ERC Classes

This series of 5 explanatory videos will give you concrete information about the ERC application process, including frequently made mistakes, tips and rumours.
