Army Research Office

Leading and shaping scientific discovery worldwide to create future transformational Army capabilities

The Army Research Office is the Army’s extramural basic research arm operating under U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command. Founded in 1951 and based in Research Triangle Park, North Carolina, ARO’s team of scientists, engineers, and staff help drive the nationwide research agenda to create revolutionary new Army capabilities.

ARO’s unique focus, prioritizing high-payoff scientific breakthroughs, keeps the Army at the forefront of technological innovation. By cultivating partnerships, supporting talent, and funding bold ideas, ARO delivers the scientific discoveries that will safeguard national security.

What We Do

Deliver Disruption
Create, direct and protect scientific discovery for the Army and drive the future operational superiority of our Soldiers

The Army’s Front Door to Universities
Build partnerships with more than 7,000 researchers and 300 institutions of higher learning that accelerate game-changing future technology

Securing the Future
Mentor and educate the best and brightest to establish the Army’s future science and engineering workforce, and shape the US industrial base

Why ARO

Scientific Breakthroughs: ARO-supported projects have led to world-changing discoveries, 27 Nobel Prizes, and countless innovations in fields such as biotechnology, materials science, and information technology. These advances strengthen Army capabilities and deliver benefits to the nation and the world.

Long-Term Vision: ARO program managers are national experts who anticipate future challenges and facilitate scientific discoveries.

Global Reach: ARO funds research in 46 U.S. states and territories and 30 countries, leveraging global expertise.

Trusted Partnership: ARO leads public/private and academic collaborations, ensuring discoveries transition rapidly.

How to Engage

Explore funding opportunities and connect with ARO Program managers via the year-round ARL Broad Agency Announcement (BAA).

Participate in Army STEM outreach and apprenticeship programs.
