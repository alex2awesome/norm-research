The Office of Research Development provides strategic and practical support for the planning, development, and launch of large-scale sponsored research projects at Georgia Tech.  These projects typically include:

  • Formation of a center or institute with multiple functions
  • Engagement of teams that span disciplines and institutions
  • Proposed budgets greater than $10 million

Plan

The Office of Research Development supports teaming during project conceptualization and planning.

Collaboration Planning

  • Guided sessions to develop a working team collaboration plan

Connection to Partners

  • Connection to partners to expand team expertise and capacity
More on Collaboration Planning:

Increasingly, funding agencies are looking for strong management and effective collaboration approaches to ensure project success.  Collaboration Planning is a team science-based approach that is widely recognized to improve project outcomes.  Over the course of several short sessions, we will guide your team through the development of a Collaboration Plan.  The Collaboration Planning program is designed for teams in the early stages of team building through early proposal development.  Contact Emily Carpinone (ecarpinone@gatech.edu) to inquire about this service. 

 

Develop

The Office of Research Development provides tailored, strategic support for proposal development and site visit preparations.

Strategic Guidance

  • Framing relative to solicitation and funding agency goals
  • Identification of strengths and gaps
  • Communication with agency officials
  • Preparation for site visits

Project Management

  • Development and management of checklists and timelines
  • Facilitation of team meetings
  • Coordination of proposal support team

Development and Review of Proposal Content

  • Development of writing templates based on solicitation and review criteria
  • Management of internal and external (e.g., red team) reviews
  • Proposal review and editing

Connection to Resources

  • Consultations with experts and partners
  • Proposal services (e.g., graphic design)
  • Curated written resources for specialized proposal elements
GT Facilities Library:

The GT Facilities Library is a centralized repository of descriptions of campus resources. The Library was established by the Office of Research Development to assist faculty and staff with descriptions of Georgia Tech facilities and other resources in proposals. Access the Library through SharePoint or Teams (requires GT credentials) for more information.

 

Launch

The Office of Research Development provides administrative support and guidance for the initial center launch period.

Interim Support to Initiate Center Activities

  • Guidance on operational best practices and institutional resources and processes
  • Oversight of administrative, financial, hiring, communications, and other workflows
