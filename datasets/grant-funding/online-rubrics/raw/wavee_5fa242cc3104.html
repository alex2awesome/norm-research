NIH Grants Policy Statement (10/01/1998) - Effective for all NIH grants and cooperative agreements with budget periods beginning on October 1, 1998 through February 28, 2001. See 03/01 NIHGPS and 12/03 NIHGPS for subsequent budget periods. Download or View the complete 10/98 NIH GPS (PDF - 1 MB). See 10/21/1998 NIH Guide for description and notable changes since the 1994 PHS GPS.
