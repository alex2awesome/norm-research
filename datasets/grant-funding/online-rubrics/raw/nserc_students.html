Students and postdoctoral researchers

NSERC offers scholarships and awards for every stage of study, from undergraduate to postdoctoral.

The program guides for students and postdoctoral researchers, as well as the award holder’s guides, provide information on application procedures and guidelines for award holders. These guides supersede all previous versions.

NSERC may alter the programs or the terms and conditions of the awards at any time, without notice. Any major changes will be announced immediately on NSERC's website, and the effective date will be the date indicated at the bottom of the relevant web page.
