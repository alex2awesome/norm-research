NIA Award Sample Applications

Several NIA Investigators have graciously agreed to share their exceptional applications and summary statements as samples to help the research community. The F99/K00 and K99/R00 resources below represent the initial submissions and subsequent resubmissions of applications from applicants who received an excellent score and were funded upon resubmission. These are only examples of successful applications and are not meant to be a template for future applicants. Please make sure that your application is tailored to your specific research and career development plan and seek individual advice from mentors and peers as you prepare your application.

  • Reminders:

    • Always follow your funding opportunity's instructions for application format. Although these applications demonstrate good grantsmanship, time has passed since these grantees applied. The samples may not reflect the latest format or rules.
    • Copyright Notice. The awardee allows you to use the material (e.g., data, writing, graphics) it shared in this application only for nonprofit educational purposes provided the material remains unchanged and the principal investigator, awardee organization, and NIH NIA are credited.
