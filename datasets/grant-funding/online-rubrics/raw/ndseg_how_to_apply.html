How to Apply

Application Status

FY2026 applications are currently under review. Applicants can track the progress of their application through the NDSEG portal. We expect to update the portal between late April and late May, when selections will be made. There will be no updates on the portal until that time.

The next application cycle opens August 15 – November 15

Ensure to check the eligibility, requirements and FAQ page prior to creating an application.

1 - Check your eligibility here: Eligibility - NDSEG (sysplus.com)

2 - Check Application requirements here: https://ndseg.sysplus.com/NDSEG/Applicants/Application-Evaluation-Award

3 - Check Application FAQs here: https://ndseg.sysplus.com/NDSEG/FAQ/application

4 - APPLY HERE: https://applyndseg.sysplus.com/

Questions related to applications should be directed to NDSEG.PMO@sysplus.com.
