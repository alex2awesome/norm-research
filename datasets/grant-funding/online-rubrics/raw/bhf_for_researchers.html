Information for researchers

We fund vital research into the causes, prevention, diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. Learn more about BHF research grants and how to apply for funding.

Apply for funding and grants

What we fund

We provide personal support for clinical and non-clinical cardiovascular researchers. We also provide grants for research projects and other awards.

Discover our research grant options

How to apply for funding

We're here to support you throughout the grant application process. If you’re a new applicant, please ask a senior colleague for advice first.

Read our tips on preparing a grant application

Managing your grant

Find out how to successfully manage your BHF grant for its duration.

Read our guidance on managing your grant

Get more advice with your application

Need help with your grant application? Contact us and we'll be happy to help.
