OUR TOWN

Our Town grants support creative placemaking projects that integrate arts, culture, and design in efforts to advance local economic, physical, and other community-desired outcomes. Projects must be place-specific (such as a community, neighborhood, or cultural district) and should focus on community priorities.

To apply, select the Design & Our Town discipline within the Grants for Arts Projects  program.
