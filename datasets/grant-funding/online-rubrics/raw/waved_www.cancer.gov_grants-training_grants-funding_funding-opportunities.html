Research Funding Opportunities

The NIH posts all notices of funding opportunities (NOFOs) on grants.gov and grants.nih.gov, the NIH’s single official source for grant and cooperative agreement funding opportunities. For more information, see NOT-OD-25-143.

Find Funding Opportunities

Use the NIH Grant search tool to find opportunities for NCI funding. To browse all NIH grant opportunities, clear the "NCI" preset filter.

Explore NCI Research Priorities

Review NCI Interest Areas and Highlighted Topics to learn about NCI priorities for cancer research and support.

Policy Notices and Updates

Find specific notices of policies, guidelines, and other information in the NIH Guide for Grants and Contracts. Users can subscribe for updates on this page.
