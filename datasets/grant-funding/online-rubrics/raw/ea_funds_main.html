Funds

Animal Welfare Fund

Funding organizations and projects that will help alleviate the suffering of millions or billions of animals.

Effective Altruism Infrastructure Fund

Funding organizations or people that aim to grow or improve the effective altruism community.

Global Health and Development Fund

Funding outstanding, evidence-based opportunities to provide better access to healthcare and economic development.

Long-Term Future Fund

Funding people or projects that aim to improve the long-term future, such as by reducing risks from artificial intelligence and engineered pandemics.

Effective Ventures Foundation (UK) (EV UK) is a charity in England and Wales (with registered charity number 1149828, registered company number 07962181, and is also a Netherlands registered tax-deductible entity ANBI 825776867). Effective Ventures Foundation USA Inc. (EV US) is a section 501(c)(3) organization in the USA (EIN 47-1988398). EV UK and EV US are separate charities that collaborate closely under the “Effective Ventures” name.
