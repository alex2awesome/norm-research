Events

ARPA-H Events

Through presentations, workshops, and other opportunities to connect, the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H) looks forward to connecting with everyone who has a stake in our health – from patients, researchers, and nonprofits, to technologists, investors, and entrepreneurs. 

For Proposers' Day events, reference the program's Special Notice on SAM.gov for event details and then visit the ARPA-H Solutions site to register.

For speaking engagement requests, please fill out the engagements form.

ARPA-H Comprehensive Other Transaction (OT) Training - Jan 2026

Virtual

January 28, 2026 - January 29, 2026

ARPA-H will host its Comprehensive Other Transaction (OT) training event January 28-29. This virtual event is open to government and industry personnel.
