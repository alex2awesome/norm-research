Applying for funding

The Swedish Research Council fund research by issuing calls for grant applications in open competition. In order to provide Swedish research with the best possible prerequisites, we fund research infrastructure, both in Sweden and abroad.

Print

In connection with the calls that closed on April 14, technical issues have occurred in Prisma. The issue concerns the PDF version of the application that is generated, which in some cases lacks an attachment that was uploaded in the application form.

However, applications that have already been registered and submitted are complete. At present, no action is required from applicants who may have been affected by this issue. We are working on a technical solution.
