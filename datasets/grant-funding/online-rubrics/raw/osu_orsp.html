Cultivating leaders to grow Ohio’s future

Whether you want to improve your community, help solve climate challenges or advance global food systems, the College of Food, Agricultural, and Environmental Sciences is where your purpose takes root. Become a change-maker through science and hands-on learning that delivers real-world results.

  • 96% of grads are employed or enrolled in grad or professional school within 6 months of graduation
  • $4M in scholarships awarded annually
  • 2.5M Ohioans served yearly by Ohio State University Extension

Rooted in Ohio. Reaching for the world.

From our land-grant mission to our global partnerships, CFAES is where innovation begins at home and grows across borders. Our students are leading change in every Ohio county and making an impact across the globe.
