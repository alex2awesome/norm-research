Welcome to the Office of Research Services

The Office of Research Services (ORS) provides full life cycle administrative support to facilitate innovative research that positively impacts the world. ORS provides excellent customer service through knowledge-based leadership and stewardship. This includes timely clear communication to build partnerships with the Penn research community and our external sponsors.

Research Security Training - Reminder

Both the NSF and NIH have implemented new requirements for Research Security Training. All Principal Investigators and Senior/Key Personnel must complete the training within 12 months prior to the submission of a proposal.
