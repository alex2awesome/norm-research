Research

With world-class research facilities and people dedicated to improving life, health and technology for all, Cornell is at the forefront of research that benefits society.

Innovation & External Engagement

A network of incubators, seed funding opportunities, competitions, mentorship programs, and other resources give researchers and entrepreneurs the training and support they need to translate ideas and discoveries into innovations that benefit people and communities around the world.

Collaborating Across Cornell

Research & Innovation fosters collaborative, multidisciplinary research across Cornell’s campuses in Ithaca, Geneva, and New York City. Our integrated research and innovation ecosystem, anchored by shared user facilities and multidisciplinary centers, is uniquely designed for groundbreaking discoveries with real-world impact.
