Stay Connected

NIA supports aging research from basic science through clinical studies. Here you will learn about extramural research opportunities and how to apply for NIA funds. Specific grant mechanisms are available for individuals at different career stages, from trainees to senior investigators. NIA provides support for aging research and Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias in older adults.
