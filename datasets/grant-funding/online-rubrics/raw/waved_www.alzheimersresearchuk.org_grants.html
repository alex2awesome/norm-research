Grant schemes

For researchers

Alzheimer’s Research UK has a large number of different grant schemes through which you can apply for funding.

All applications must fall within Alzheimer’s Research UK’s remit which covers biomedical research in Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias. Alzheimer’s Research UK funds research into treatment, diagnosis and prevention.  

Of particular importance is research which has translational potential to benefit people with or at risk of dementia. This includes basic preclinical as well as clinical research, but not care or service delivery research.

Read more about our Research Strategy. 

Current open funding calls: Currently, there are no open funding calls. 

Alzheimer’s Research UK has now migrated to Flexigrant and has ceased to use GrantTracker.

Advanced Clinical Fellowship

For clinicians who’ve delivered previous dementia research projects and have clear plans to establish their own research programme.

Early Career Researcher Bridge Fund

This scheme aims to enable the retention of early career researchers in dementia research or to address particular pinch points in their career path.

Inspire Fund – public engagement grant

The Inspire Fund provides funding for projects that engage the public with the topic of brain health and reducing the risk of dementia.

Race Against Dementia Fellowship

For early-career scientists in their pursuit of innovative solutions to the big questions in dementia research.

Pilot Projects

Meant for novel research ideas that, if successful, would lead to a Major project or programme application to Alzheimer’s Research UK or other funding body.

Major Projects

Meant for intermediate to large-scale research projects.

Senior Research Fellowship

For PhD-qualified researchers in transition to lead independent groups.

Research Fellowship

For PhD-qualified non-clinical researchers who have clear plans to establish their own research niche.

Doctoral Clinical Fellowships

For clinically qualified applicants wishing to develop a career that combines dementia research and professional practice.

Grants review process

Grant application undergo a rigorous review process and are awarded on the basis of scientific merit in relation to Alzheimer's Research UK’s remit, suitability for the scheme applied for, and open competition.
