Funding Opportunities

A fundamental goal of the Office of the Vice President for Research is to enhance the research climate at Colorado State University. We are here to help students and faculty in all disciplines by generating internal and external funding for scholarly and creative activities. In addition to funding opportunities, the Office of the Vice President provides grant writing resources, proposal development services, and technical assistance.
