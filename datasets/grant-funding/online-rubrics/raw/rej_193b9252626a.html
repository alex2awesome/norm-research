            Here are some general interview questions I have been asked, every. single. time. 

            They will also start with asking for a quick overview of yourself so I wrote out a general elevator speech of who I am and how it led me to prep

            - Why PREP? What do you most want to get out of this program?

            - Have you had research experience? (if you say yes) What did you do and find from the research.

            - Have you had good mentorships before? What made that relationship successful? What do you look for in a mentor

            - How have you responded to a hard situation during your undergad?

            - What do you expect your greatest challenges to be in grad school?

            -"what will you bring to the PREP cohort",

            - "where do you see yourself after your PhD"

            - "what specific skills do you hope to gain from PREP"

            - Make sure to look at the faculty as they will ask if you have any in mind 

            - They will ask what specific research you are interested in 

            etc etc
