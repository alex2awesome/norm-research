HHMI Pauses Applications for Hanna Gray Fellowship Program Supporting Early-Career Scientists

by Mark Chiang
Share To

The Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) has announced a pause in accepting applications for its Hanna Gray Fellowship program, a funding initiative aimed at supporting early-career scientists during their postdoctoral training. This decision reduces available opportunities for young researchers seeking financial support, prompting some to explore options abroad.

The Hanna Gray Fellowship program was established to provide funding and mentorship to promising scientists from diverse backgrounds as they transition into independent research careers. The suspension of new applications comes amid broader concerns about limited resources for postdoctoral researchers in the United States. With fewer domestic funding opportunities available, many young scientists are reportedly considering international programs as alternatives to continue their work. HHMI has not provided specific details regarding the reasons behind the pause or when applications might resume.
