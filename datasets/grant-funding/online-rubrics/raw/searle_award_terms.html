Searle Scholars Program Award Terms

The Searle Scholars Program provides institutions with a grant of $300,000 for the support of the Searle Scholar’s research during the three-year grant period. Funding for each of the three years will be $100,000, with the first year payable in late June and future payments in September. This grant is subject to the conditions outlined in the Terms of Award.

Frequently Asked Questions for awardees

Read more below to answer your questions about the Searle Scholars Program.

Does the Searle Scholars Program allow indirect costs to be charged to the grant?

Funds provided under this grant may not be used to pay costs associated with general facilities or administrative overhead, such as costs of providing space, utilities, library facilities, and administrative services.

Can investigator or personnel salaries be charged to the grant?

The Program does not recommend that Funds be used to support a Scholar’s salary. If the Scholar chooses to allocate a portion of the Award to their salary, said Scholar must email or mail a request to the Program’s scientific director justifying the allocation. In no event may the sum of the salaries and benefits charged for the Scholar exceed $25,000 in any grant fiscal year. Salaries, wages, and benefits may be charged for other individuals working on the Searle Scholars Program funded research project in proportion to the effort expended. Salaries may not be charged for others with faculty level appointments.

What is the Searle Scholars Program policy on intellectual property if funding from the award helps support an invention?

If an invention is made, wholly or in part, with support received from the Searle Scholars Program, the Program shall be notified promptly by email or mail. Disposition of patents shall be in accordance with the policies of the grantee institution. However, any net income from licensing and royalties shall be divided so that a proportionate amount, which would otherwise accrue to the Searle Scholars Program, shall be set aside and used for the support of biomedical research at the institution. Should publications result from research so supported, the Searle Scholars Program shall be cited as providing financial assistance.
