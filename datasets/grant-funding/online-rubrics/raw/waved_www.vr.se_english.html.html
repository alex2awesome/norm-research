Research for a wiser world

The Swedish Research Council is Sweden’s largest governmental research funding body and supports research of the highest quality in all scientific fields. We advise the Government on research policy and work to ensure that research benefit society.

Calls and decisions

Initiative on excellence clusters for groundbreaking technologies

The Swedish Research Council and Swedish Governmental Agency for Innovation Systems (Vinnova) are collaborating on an initiative for clusters of excellence in groundbreaking technologies. The purpose is to enable Sweden, in the long term, to become a world leader in groundbreaking technologies.
