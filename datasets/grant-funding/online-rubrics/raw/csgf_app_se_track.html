DOE CSGF - Science and Engineering Track

Registered users

Welcome to the DOE CSGF Science and Engineering Track online application site.

This application is intended for graduate students pursuing a Ph.D. in fields of study that advance the use of high-performance computing (HPC) to solve specific, complex science and engineering problems.

The deadline for online submission was January 15, 2026. Fellowship offers will be extended by mid-April. All applicants with a complete final submission will, in that same period, receive an email regarding the outcome of their application.

Transcripts will be accepted after the January 15th deadline. Flexibility will also be allowed for references if out of the applicant's control. However, submission of supplemental materials should not exceed the below deadline dates.

Deadline Dates:

  • Letters of recommendation are due by January 22, 2026
  • Official transcripts are due by January 31, 2026

You can still log in to check on the status of your supporting materials.

The 2027-2028 application will be available late October 2026; if you would like to be notified by email, click here.

If you have any questions, please email csgf@krellinst.org.
