Researcher Resources

CIRM advances regenerative medicine in California through collaboration, innovation, and support for all stages of research. CIRM provides awardees and the broader research community with access to the following resources.

Explore Funding Opportunities

 CIRM provides funding across all stages of research, from basic science to translational studies and clinical trials. This support drives best-in-class research aimed at developing innovative therapies for patients in need.

Photo Credit: UCLA Broad Stem Cell Research Center

How to Apply for Funding

Whether you’re a first-time applicant or a returning awardee, learn how to submit an application through CIRM’s Grants Management System.

Photo Credit: UCLA Broad Stem Cell Research Center

Manage Your CIRM Grant

Find information to support with managing your CIRM grant including regulations, forms, policies, and FAQs.
Photo Credit: UCLA Broad Stem Cell Research Center

Data Sharing and Management Plan

CIRM requires awardees of R&D awards to develop a Data Sharing and Management Plan (DSMP) and make data and metadata available to the broader scientific community. CIRM publicly shares information about CIRM-funded data in the Data Explorer dashboard.

Photo Credit: City of Hope

Shared Resources Laboratories

CIRM’s Shared Labs aim to foster collaboration among California researchers, break down research silos, and provide students and researchers access to top-notch resources and training in using stem cell-based models.

Photo Credit: City of Hope

Alpha Clinics Network

First-of-their-kind specialty clinics at top California medical centers offering access to clinical trials for regenerative medicine treatments including those that come from CIRM-funded research. 

Industry Alliance Program (IAP)

Explore direct opportunities to partner with Industry to accelerating the most promising cell and gene therapy projects to commercialization.

Industry Resource Partners (IRP)

Explore critical resources provided by industry organizations and institutions that support with overcoming potential bottlenecks in cell and gene therapy development.

CIRM-funded clinical trials are advancing stem cell and gene therapies to address a variety of diseases and conditions.
