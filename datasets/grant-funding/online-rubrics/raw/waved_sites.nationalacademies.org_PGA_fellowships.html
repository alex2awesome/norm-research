About

The Office of Fellowships at the National Academies administers prestigious fellowship awards on behalf of government agencies, private foundations, and other sponsoring organizations. These programs are designed to support and enhance the career development of doctoral and postdoctoral researchers and scholars, providing them with unique opportunities to advance their expertise. Through this programming, the Office of Fellowships plays a vital role in preparing the next generation of leaders in science, engineering, and medicine. Through these fellowships, participants gain valuable experience, build professional networks, and make significant contributions to their fields, driving progress and discovery across a diverse range of disciplines.

A unique aspect of the Office of Fellowships is its ability to draw on the extensive membership and volunteer database of the National Academies. This distinguished network includes some of the most respected experts and leaders in science, engineering, and medicine. By leveraging this resource, the Office of Fellowships ensures that each fellowship application undergoes a rigorous and comprehensive review process. Expert reviewers from the National Academies' network provide invaluable insights and evaluations, ensuring that sponsors receive highly qualified and thoroughly vetted awardees.
