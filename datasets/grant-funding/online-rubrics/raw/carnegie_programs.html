Our Approach

Overview

Carnegie Corporation of New York’s philanthropic goal is to reduce political polarization through grants spanning program initiatives in Education, to promote socioeconomic mobility and civic participation; Democracy, to develop policy solutions for legal immigration and uphold voting rights; and International Peace, to advance peace with a focus on movements of people, non-state actors, and managing and preventing conflict.

  •  
    Education

    Our grantmaking helps students of all ages and backgrounds access learning that leads to meaningful careers, upward mobility, and civic participation. In this way, Education works to support the forces of cohesion that build trust in public institutions, reduce political polarization, and strengthen our democracy.

  •  
    Democracy

    Carnegie’s Strengthening U.S. Democracy program supports nonpartisan organizations that are working to reduce political polarization in the United States through initiatives focused on elections and governance, state community service, universal civic learning, and legal immigration pathways. In addition, Carnegie continues to make investments in opportunities to bring people together to solve problems across differences, including core grantees such as the Trust for Civic Life. Across these areas of focus, Carnegie supports research, convenings, public education, and other activities aimed at strengthening American democracy and public trust in government and institutions.

  •  
    International Program

    Our grantmaking is guided by Andrew Carnegie’s founding vision: to advance peace as both the absence of armed conflict and the creation of conditions that sustain nonviolent growth and change. We focus on three areas: preventing and managing conflict, movements of people, and non-state actors. Together, these priorities reflect Carnegie’s commitment to fostering policy-relevant research, strengthening institutions and practices, impacting lives, and amplifying voices to shape more peaceful futures.
