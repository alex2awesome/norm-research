Earthjustice is the nonprofit law firm for the environment, representing—without charge—hundreds of public interest clients, large and small. Earthjustice works through the courts to safeguard public lands, national forests, parks, and wilderness areas; to reduce air and water pollution; to prevent toxic contamination; to preserve endangered species and wildlife habitat; and to achieve environmental justice.

Founded in 1971 as Sierra Club Legal Defense Fund, Earthjustice has played a leading role in shaping the development of environmental law. From nine offices across the country Earthjustice attorneys take on powerful special interests and win. Throughout our history, we have achieved many landmark legal victories.

When Congress tries to overturn these courtroom victories, Earthjustice's Policy and Legislation program in Washington DC, works to halt legislative backlash and protect environment laws such as the Clean Air Act, and the Endangered Species Act.

Earthjustice's International Program addresses trade and the environment, human rights and the environment, and helps build environmental law in other countries.

Earthjustice also runs environmental law clinics at Stanford and the University of Denver, training students in public interest environmental law and increases our service to organizations throughout the country.
