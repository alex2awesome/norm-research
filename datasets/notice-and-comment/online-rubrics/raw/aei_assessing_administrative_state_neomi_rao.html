Event Summary

On Tuesday, June 19, Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) Administrator Neomi Rao came to AEI to discuss the current administration’s regulatory reform agenda. Her keynote address detailed her office’s role in a centralized approach to deregulation and how OIRA enhances the unitary direction of the executive branch.

After the conversation, Administrator Rao spoke with AEI’s John Yoo about how the president’s emphasis on deregulation has helped her office become more effective. She analyzed some of the worst cases of overregulation that she has witnessed and explored the possible benefits of applying the centralized review process to independent agencies.

— James Wyderko

Event Description

President Donald Trump came into office promising “the most far-reaching regulatory reform in American history.” He put forward an ambitious agenda, overturning regulations; issuing an executive order requiring that, for every new regulation issued, at least two be eliminated; and approving far fewer new rules than any other president in recent memory.

A year and a half into President Trump’s first term, what has been accomplished? What has been learned? And what tools are available for the executive to confront what some have called the “fourth branch of government”?

Join AEI for an address and conversation with AEI’s John Yoo and Neomi Rao, administrator of the Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs, the agency responsible for reviewing the president’s regulatory program.
