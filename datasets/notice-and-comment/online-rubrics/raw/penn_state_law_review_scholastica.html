Penn State Law Review is the flagship publication of Penn State Law. The journal can trace its origins to 1897 with the first publication of The Forum, making it one of the oldest law reviews in continuous print in the United States. As a general-interest journal, Penn State Law Review does not limit submissions to any specific topic. The journal publishes three issues per year.
