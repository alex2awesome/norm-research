Devin Judge-Lord

Devin Judge-Lord
Post-Doctoral Scholar in Government

Devin Judge-Lord is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Government and Center for the Environment. His research focuses on social movements, bureaucratic policymaking, congressional oversight and representation, and environmental policy. His dissertation examined how public pressure campaigns affect agency rulemaking. He also has major collaborative projects on legislator behavior, interest-group lobbying, and private governance.
