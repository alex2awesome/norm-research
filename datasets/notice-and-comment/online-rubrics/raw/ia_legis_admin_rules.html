Iowa Administrative Rules

Administrative rules implement or interpret law; prescribe policy; or describe the organization, procedure, or practice requirements of an executive branch agency.
  • Iowa Administrative Code (IAC) (Current & Archive) - Composite of all adopted administrative rules organized by publication date and agency (Official - PDF format and Unofficial - PDF and RTF formats).
  • Iowa Administrative Bulletin (IAB) and IAC Supplement (Current & Archive) - Biweekly publication of all proposed and adopted changes to administrative rules and biweekly compilation of replacement IAC chapters (Official Version - PDF format).
  • Current IAB
  • Comment - On proposed administrative rules.
  • Emergency Rulemaking Documents - Rules filed without public notice and participation and with an early effective date.
  • Uniform Rules on Agency Procedure - Agency procedures include petitions for rulemaking, declaratory orders, agency procedure for rulemaking, fair information practices, and contested cases.
  • IAC Index - Index of general subject matters and concepts within the IAC.
  • Search Waivers of Administrative Rules - Search recorded waivers by agency, by time frame, by keyword or phrase, or by rule number.
  • Iowa Departmental Rules (IDR) - Rules that preceded the current IAC (1952-1975).
  • Request Certified Copy of Iowa Administrative Code - Iowa Code §2B.18 authorizes the Iowa Administrative Code Editor to certify IAC provisions, for admission in a court of law or governmental office outside Iowa, by attesting to and authenticating such provisions appeared in the IAC as of a certain date.
