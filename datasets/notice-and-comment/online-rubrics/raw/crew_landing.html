An earthquake can happen anytime. It takes teamwork to make communities ready and resilient.

CREW works with partners across Cascadia and the western U.S. to raise awareness, foster collaboration, and develop, sponsor, and support earthquake studies and risk mitigation activities.

CREW is a non-profit coalition of private, public, and academic partners working together to improve the ability of communities, businesses and households across Cascadia and the western U.S. to reduce the effects of earthquakes and related hazards, including tsunami.
