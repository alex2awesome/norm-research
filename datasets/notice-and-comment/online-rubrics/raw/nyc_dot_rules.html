The laws and rules of the City of New York are published in the New York City Charter, the New York City Administrative Code and the Rules of the City of New York. The most updated versions of these publications are available on the American Legal Publishing (ALP) website. Visitors may use the "Search" button at the top of the ALP website to find topics across all three publications.

The NYC DOT Rulemaking Process flowchart presents the process by which NYC DOT promulgates a rule pursuant to the City Administrative Procedure Act (CAPA). To learn more about NYC’s rulemaking process or to comment on proposed rules, visit the NYC Rules website.

New York City Charter

The New York City Charter defines the organization, functions, and essential procedures and policies of NYC government. NYC DOT's authority can be found in Chapter 71 of the New York City Charter.

New York City Administrative Code

The New York City Administrative Code contains the codified local laws of New York City enacted by the New York City Council and Mayor.

The majority of local laws pertaining to NYC DOT can be found in Title 19 of the New York City Administrative Code, however, other titles—including, but not limited to—Title 7 and Title 10 of the New York City Administrative Code contain local laws relevant to NYC DOT.

Rules of the City of New York

The Rules of the City of New York contain the rules and regulations for New York City Agencies. NYC DOT's rules can be found in Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York.

Staten Island Ferry Rules

Staten Island Ferry rules can be found in Chapter 1 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York.
Review Ferry Terminals and Vessels rules on ALP

Highway Rules

Highway Rules can be found in Chapter 2 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York. Chapter 2 includes rules regarding street construction permits, permit fees, requirements for sidewalk, curb and roadway work, flood mitigation systems, and other related information. Review Highway Rules on ALP

Penalty Schedule

NYC DOT's Penalty Schedule can be found in Chapter 3 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York. Chapter 3 lists penalties associated with NYC DOT violations that are adjudicated at the Office of Administrative Trials and Hearings (OATH) through the Environmental Control Board (ECB). Review NYC DOT’s Penalty Schedule on ALP

Traffic Rules

Traffic Rules can be found in Chapter 4 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York. Chapter 4 includes rules regarding traffic signals, parking, bicycles, moped share and carshare, pedestrian plazas, buses, taxi/for-hire vehicles, trucks, and other related information. Review Traffic Rules and Regulations on ALP

Revocable Consents Rules

Rules about Revocable Consents can be found in Chapter 7 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York. Chapter 7 includes rules about applications, requirements, and fees related to obtaining a revocable consent. Review all Revocable Consents on ALP

Notices Archive (2024-2025)

Notice of Adoption - Busways

The adopted rule amends sections 4-01 and 4-12 of Chapter 4 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York (“Traffic Rules”) to clarify bus lane restrictions.

The Notice was published in the City Record on October 3, 2025. The rule goes into effect on November 2, 2025. Notice of Adoption - Busways

Notice of Adoption - Concession Seating in NYC DOT Pedestrian Plazas and Open Streets

This adopted rule authorizes NYC DOT’s pedestrian plaza and open streets partners, through their concession agreements with NYC DOT, to permit their subconcessionaires to designate areas of NYC DOT pedestrian plazas and open streets for exclusive use by their patrons subject to certain restrictions and the review and approval of NYC DOT.

The Notice of Adoption was published in the City Record on July 28, 2025. The rule will go into effect on August 27, 2025. Notice of Adoption - Concession Seating in NYC DOT Pedestrian Plazas and Open Streets

Notice of Adoption- Jaywalking

This adopted rule amends Chapter 4 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York to decriminalize jaywalking, aligning New York City’s traffic rules with section 19-195 of the New York City Administrative Code, as amended by Local Law 98 of 2024.

The Notice was published in the City Record on May 27, 2025. The rule goes into effect on June 26, 2025. Notice of Adoption- Jaywalking

Notice of Adoption- Mopeds on Bridges

This adopted rule amends Chapter 4 of Title 34 of the Rules of the City of New York to remove the prohibition on the operation of mopeds (limited use motorcycles) on the Brooklyn Bridge and the lower roadway of the Ed Koch Queensboro Bridge.

The Notice was published in the City Record on May 27, 2025. The rule goes into effect on June 26, 2025. Notice of Adoption- Mopeds on Bridges

Notice of Adoption - Obstructed Plates

This adopted rule updates provisions of the Rules of the City of New York relating to license plate display requirements to align with the New York State Vehicle and Traffic Law. Notice of Adoption - Obstructed Plates

Notice of Adoption- Microhubs
This rule establishes and implement a permit for the use of microhubs as part of a pilot program mandated by Local Law 166 of 2021 to aid in congestion reduction and increase freight sustainability. This rule goes into effect on February 13, 2025 and expires on October 30, 2027. Notice of Adoption- Microhubs

Notice of Adoption – Revocable Consent for Electric Micromobility Device Battery Swapping and Charging Cabinets
The adopted rule authorizes property owners and tenants to apply for and be granted a revocable consent for the installation of an electric micromobility device battery swapping and charging cabinet on sidewalks within the public right of way in front of their properties. The Notice of Adoption was published in the City Record on January 2, 2025. The rule will go into effect on February 1, 2025. Notice of Adoption – Revocable Consent for Electric Micromobility Device Battery Swapping and Charging Cabinets

Notice of Adoption - Trade-In Program for Electric Powered Mobility Devices and Lithium-Ion Batteries
The adopted rule establishes a trade-in program for powered mobility devices or lithium- ion batteries used in powered mobility devices. The Notice of Adoption was published in the City Record on October 22, 2024. The rule went into effect on November 21, 2024. Notice of Adoption - Trade-In Program for Electric Powered Mobility Devices and Lithium-Ion Batteries

Notice of Adoption - Pedal-Assist Commercial Bicycles
The adopted rule allows for the use of pedal-assist commercial bicycles (previously “Cargo Bikes”) on city streets. The Notice of Adoption was published in the City Record on March 27, 2024. The rule will go into effect on April 26, 2024. Notice of Adoption - Pedal-Assist Commercial Bicycles

Notice of Adoption - Open Streets Program
NYC DOT adopted a rule that establishes requirements relating to NYC DOT’s Open Streets program. The rule will become effective on March 29, 2024. Notice of Adoption - Open Streets Program

Notice of Adoption - Dining Out NYC
NYC DOT adopted a rule that establishes requirements relating to NYC DOT’s Dining Out NYC program, including the granting of licenses and revocable consents for sidewalk and roadway cafes, operational requirements and siting and design criteria for sidewalk and roadway seating areas, and enforcement procedures. The rule will become effective on March 3, 2024. Notice of Adoption - Dining Out NYC

Notice of Adoption - Vending on Bridges
NYC DOT adopted a rule that clarifies that an elevated pedestrian walkway or a bicycle lane on a bridge or a bridge approach may not be used for the vending of merchandise or services. The rule will take effect immediately - January 3, 2024 Notice of Adoption - Vending on Bridges
