Built for Local and State Government

Become more strategic, responsive, resilient, and efficient with OpenGov.

Enterprise Asset Management

Your infrastructure—roads, bridges, water mains, parks, and more—says everything about how your city grows and serves its people. OpenGov Enterprise Asset Management helps you plan smarter, respond faster, and make data-backed decisions with tools built for the unique demands of local and state governments.

KEY FEATURES

  • Work Order Management
  • Asset Lifecycle Tracking
  • Infrastructure Planning
  • Reporting & Dashboards
  • Real-Time GIS Integration
  • Mobile App

Learn More

Permitting & Licensing

Permits, licenses, and inspections are where government meets daily life—whether someone’s opening a business, building a home, or hosting an event. OpenGov Permitting & Licensing helps you cut through the red tape with the most adaptable AI-enabled software built for the real-world needs of local and state governments. Less manual work, fewer delays, and a better experience for everyone on both sides of the counter.

KEY FEATURES

  • Intuitive Applicant Portal
  • Workflow Automation and Approvals
  • Self-Service System and Tools
  • Virtual Front Desk
  • Mobile App for Inspections
  • Automated Renewals

Learn More

Budgeting & Performance

Budgeting isn’t just numbers—it’s how governments turn vision into action. OpenGov Budgeting & Performance helps local and state governments set goals and manage operational, capital, and workforce budgets with confidence. With built-in transparency, seamless ERP integration, and powerful reporting tools, you can move faster, plan smarter, and align every dollar to what matters most.

KEY FEATURES

  • Performance Management
  • Advanced Workflow Management
  • Operating Budget
  • Capital and Workforce Planning
  • Powerful Analytics and Reporting
  • Digital Budget Book Builder

Learn More

Procurement & Contract Management

Procurement touches everything from public safety equipment to park renovations—but too often, governments are stuck juggling emails, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. OpenGov Procurement & Contract Management brings the entire process together with workflow automation and AI built for local and state governments. From requests to contracts, you’ll gain speed, visibility, compliance and control—without the paper chase.

KEY FEATURES

  • Request Management
  • Intake Management
  • Solicitation Assembly
  • Supplier Engagement
  • Group Evaluations
  • Contract Management

Learn More

Tax & Revenue Collection

From business taxes to property taxes, revenue fuels the services residents count on. But managing it all across siloed systems and manual processes slows everything down. OpenGov Tax & Revenue Collection centralizes tax billing, filing, and payments in one place—so you can improve collections, reduce administrative headaches, and deliver better service with fewer resources.

KEY FEATURES

  • Revenue Workflows
  • Self-Service Portal
  • Dynamic Calculations
  • Real-Time Analytics
  • State & Local Data Interfaces
  • E-Delivery Options

Learn More

Financial Management

Strong financial management is key to earning public trust—but many governments are stuck with outdated, inflexible ERP systems. OpenGov Financial Management brings it all together in one modern platform, including general ledger, accounts payable, budgeting, procurement, and reporting. Built for local and state governments, it helps you operate strategically, respond faster, and manage every dollar with confidence.

KEY FEATURES

  • Inquiry, reporting, and analysis
  • Digital  workflows
  • Digital documentation
  • Auto-validations for Departments 
  • Integrated Spend Controls
  • Expert Services Support
