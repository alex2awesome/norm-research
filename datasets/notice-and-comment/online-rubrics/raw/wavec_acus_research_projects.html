Projects

ACUS undertakes many types of projects to carry out its statutory mission to improve federal administrative procedure. ACUS consultants and staff regularly prepare research studies, some of which inform the development of recommendations for action by federal agencies, the President, Congress, and the judiciary. ACUS also publishes reference guides, organizes public forums and roundtables, and prepares other resources on an ongoing basis.
