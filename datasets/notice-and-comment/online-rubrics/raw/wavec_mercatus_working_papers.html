Research

Mercatus conducts research on a wide range of subjects in law, regulation, and economics. Publications include working papers and research papers as well as “bridging products”—policy briefs, testimonies, public interest comments—that provide a high-level overview of key issues facing policymakers and elaborate upon relevant policy positions.
