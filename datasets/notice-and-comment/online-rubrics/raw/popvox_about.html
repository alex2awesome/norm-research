Mission, Vision, and Focus

About POPVOX Foundation

POPVOX Foundation is a nonpartisan nonprofit organization with a mission to help democratic institutions keep pace with a rapidly changing world. Through publications, events, prototypes, and technical assistance, the organization helps public servants and elected officials better serve their constituents and make better policy.

Governing Capacity & Technology

Helping democratic institutions keep pace with technological advancements

Through technical assistance, research, prototyping, and maintaining collaborative communities of practice, POPVOX Foundation helps governments understand and incorporate emerging technologies into their work.

Legislative Strengthening

Making legislatures around the world more effective, efficient, and transparent

POPVOX Foundation supports global democratic resilience by helping legislatures leverage emerging technologies and open data platforms to modernize legislative workflows, enhance real-time information sharing, and ensure data sovereignty.

Constituent Engagement

Strengthening the feedback loop between the public and their representatives

POPVOX Foundation helps legislators in the US and abroad better connect with their constituents to ensure public policies prioritize public needs and reinvigorate civic participation in the democratic process.

Policy Vision

The POPVOX Foundation team views the US Congress as a fulcrum for policy and process evolution with influence on all branches and levels of American governance, and ripple impacts around the world. We believe that a modernized Congress can “lift all boats” with more informed policymaking, increased public engagement, and transparent, trustworthy processes. This not only means investing in technology and continuous learning but also fostering a governance structure that remains resilient and informed in the age of AI and other exponential technologies. 

POPVOX Foundation engages with Congress through a four-pronged approach: 

  1. providing cooperative assistance

  2. exerting constructive external pressure 

  3. facilitating institutional knowledge sharing and networking 

  4. de-risking technology projects through prototypes, open source development and technical assistance 

Through informal advising, expert testimonies, training sessions, publications, and informal dialogues, the team aids Congress in its own evolution. Simultaneously, by identifying deficiencies, highlighting improvements, and celebrating significant advancements, POPVOX Foundation helps maintain a momentum of positive change and encourages the onward, ongoing march of modernization.

Focus on the First Branch

Co-founded in 2021 and led by proud former Congressional staffers from both sides of the aisle, the team at POPVOX Foundation brings empathy, a deep respect for the Legislative branch, and diverse expertise to its efforts to modernize Congress and other governing institutions.

NB: POPVOX Foundation is a separate entity from POPVOX, Inc., a civic tech platform Harris co-created in 2010 to connect the people to those who serve them in government.
