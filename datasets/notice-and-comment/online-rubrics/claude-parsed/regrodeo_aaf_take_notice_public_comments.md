# American Action Forum: Take Notice of Public Comments

## Overview

The American Action Forum (AAF) is a center-right policy institute that produces extensive analysis of federal regulations, including the role and effectiveness of public comments in shaping rules.

## RegRodeo Database

AAF's RegRodeo (regrodeo.com) tracks every proposed and final rule published in the Federal Register that includes a quantified economic impact or paperwork burden estimate. Available since 2005.

## AAF's Perspective on Effective Comments

AAF emphasizes the importance of:
- Quantified economic impact analysis in comments
- Paperwork Reduction Act burden estimates
- Cost-benefit framework engagement
- Cumulative regulatory cost considerations
- Small business impact (Regulatory Flexibility Act)
- Federalism considerations
- Unfunded mandate considerations

## Effective AAF-Style Comments

For comments aimed at influencing significant economic regulations:
- Cite OMB Circular A-4 standards explicitly
- Provide cost estimates with verifiable assumptions
- Engage with the agency's RIA methodology
- Address the agency's discount rate choice
- Identify benefits/costs the agency missed
- Cite Information Quality Act standards (Data Quality Act)
- Address impacts on innovation and competition
- Reference specific OIRA-tracked metrics

## Quality Indicators

AAF analyses identify high-quality comments by:
- Specific section citations
- Quantitative impact estimates
- Engagement with agency's RIA
- Independent data or analysis
- Suggestion of alternatives with comparative analysis
- Plain-language clarity

## What AAF Considers Low-Quality

- Pure expressions of preference
- Generic concerns about overregulation
- Uncited claims about economic impact
- Ideological framing without substantive analysis
- Comments not tied to specific provisions

## Practical Resources

AAF tracks:
- Total annual regulatory costs
- Major rulemakings with economic significance
- Paperwork hours imposed
- Rules struck down on judicial review
- OIRA review statistics
