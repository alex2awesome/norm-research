# Department of Energy: How to Participate or Comment

## Submission Methods

Comments may be submitted through:
- Regulations.gov website
- Email
- Postal mail
- Hand delivery/courier

## Primary Platform

You can participate in rulemakings by viewing Federal Register notices, supporting documents, and public comments in the rulemaking docket found on Regulations.gov, which is described as a web-based repository of federal regulatory documents.

## Participation Options

DOE holds public meetings and webinars related to rulemakings to gather information and present analysis results, with stakeholders invited to comment on design options. Anyone can participate either in-person or via a webinar, and in-person participants are allowed to make oral presentations.

## DOE-Specific Effective Comment Strategies

For energy efficiency standards, electricity sector, and DOE rulemakings:
- Cite specific statutory authority (Energy Policy and Conservation Act, FERA, etc.)
- Engage with the technical support documents (TSDs)
- Address energy savings calculations
- Provide manufacturer impact data (for appliance standards)
- Include life-cycle cost analyses
- Address consumer impact (energy bills, product performance, choice)
- Reference DOE's NIA (National Impact Analysis) methodology
- Engage with discount rate assumptions
- Address technology substitution scenarios
- Cite Test Procedure data where applicable

## Process Rule (10 CFR § 430)

DOE's "Process Rule" sets out the procedures for energy efficiency standard rulemaking, including:
- Framework documents
- Preliminary analyses
- NOPR
- Final rule
Comments at each stage have distinct functions and influence opportunities.
