# Cuéllar, "Rethinking Regulatory Democracy" (2005)

Empirical study coding citizen vs. sophisticated comments across financial privacy, nuclear
regulation, and campaign finance proceedings. Cuéllar finds that lay commenters **can produce
sophisticated, substantive comments**, but agencies systematically under-credit them and
formalistic procedures exclude many lay voices.

## Cuéllar's Coding Criteria for Comment Sophistication

Cuéllar developed an empirical coding scheme for comment "sophistication." Comments are scored
on whether they:

1. **Identify the specific provision of the proposed rule** they address
2. **Cite legal authorities** (statutes, regulations, court decisions)
3. **Cite empirical evidence or data**
4. **Articulate a clear position** (support, oppose, suggest modification)
5. **Engage with the agency's stated rationale** rather than merely asserting preferences
6. **Propose alternative regulatory language** or specific modifications
7. **Recognize tradeoffs** acknowledging costs of the commenter's preferred position
8. **Address likely opposing arguments**

## Distinctive Findings on Lay Comments

- More than 70% of citizen comments in the financial privacy proceeding were **substantively
  responsive** to the proposed rule, contradicting agency assumptions that lay comments are
  uninformative.
- Citizen comments **frequently identified real-world impacts** (e.g., downstream effects on
  vulnerable populations) that sophisticated commenters omitted.
- Agencies systematically **under-credit citizen comments** even when they meet sophistication
  criteria — a procedural injustice Cuéllar argues undermines democratic legitimacy.

## Recommendations for Improving Comment Quality (Cuéllar)

- Agencies should provide **plain-language summaries** of proposed rules
- Comment forms should include **structured prompts** to elicit specific information
- Agencies should be required to **explicitly respond to substantive lay comments**
- Comment evaluation should treat **experiential evidence** (lived impact) as substantive

## Rubric Implications

A good comment, even from a lay person, can hit Cuéllar's sophistication markers:
- Pin to specific text
- Provide an experiential or data basis
- Acknowledge tradeoffs
- Engage with the agency's stated rationale rather than ignore it
