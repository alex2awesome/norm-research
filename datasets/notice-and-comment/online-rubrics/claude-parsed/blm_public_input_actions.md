# BLM Public Input on Proposed Rules

## Methods for Submitting Comments

Comments on the proposed rule can be submitted via:
- Federal eRulemaking Portal at http://www.regulations.gov
- Personal or messenger delivery
- Mail

## Comment Deadlines and Requirements

A notice that is open for comment will specify a deadline and methods for submitting comments.

The BLM is not obligated to consider or include in the Administrative Record for the final rule:
- Comments that are received after the close of the comment period identified in the DATES section of the proposed rule
- Comments delivered to an address other than those listed in the ADDRESSES section of the proposed rule

## Guidelines for Effective BLM Comments

**Make your comments on the proposed rule as specific as possible, confine them to issues pertinent to the proposed rule, and explain the reason for any changes you recommend.** Where possible, your comments should reference the specific section or paragraph of the proposal that you are addressing.

## Privacy and Disclosure

The BLM does not require commenters to disclose their identity when submitting comments on rulemakings. Before including your address, telephone number, email address, or other personal identifying information in your comment, be advised that your entire comment—including your personal identifying information—may be made publicly available at any time.

## Effective BLM Comments

For public lands rules:
- Identify your specific use/interest (recreation, grazing, mining, energy, conservation, hunting/fishing)
- Reference specific BLM-administered lands by name and unit
- Cite Federal Land Policy and Management Act (FLPMA) provisions
- Engage with NEPA/Endangered Species Act compliance issues
- Provide local economic data (rural community impacts)
- Cite Resource Management Plans (RMPs) and Land Use Plans
- Address tribal consultation considerations where relevant
- Engage with multiple-use vs. dominant-use framing
- Reference Wilderness Act, Wild and Scenic Rivers Act, etc.
