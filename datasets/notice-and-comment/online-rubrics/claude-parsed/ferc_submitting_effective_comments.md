# FERC: Submitting Effective Comments

## Overview

FERC provides specific guidance on how to participate effectively in its rulemaking and project review processes. Every comment FERC receives is entered into the record, read, and considered.

## Why Comments Matter

Public comments strengthen Commission decisions by providing FERC with essential facts, perspectives, and data that may have been lacking in regulatory changes, policy proposals, and company filings.

## How to Submit Comments

### eComment System
- For brief, text-only comments – up to 10,000 characters
- No account required
- Quickest way to file online

### eFiling System
- For longer comments, attachments, or filings on behalf of an organization
- Requires an eRegister account

## Step-by-Step Process

1. Receive email with detailed instructions
2. Click on link in email to display "Submit eComment" screen
3. Query and select the docket or project number(s)
4. Key or paste text comments in the large text box
5. Counter shows characters remaining

## Best Practices for FERC Comments

Effective FERC comments:
- Reference the specific docket number
- Engage with the technical analysis (e.g., transmission planning, rate design)
- Include data on energy markets, reliability impacts, environmental effects
- Cite Federal Power Act, Natural Gas Act provisions
- Address Commission precedent
- Suggest specific tariff or regulatory text changes

## For Energy Project Reviews

For pipeline and transmission line reviews:
- Identify yourself as a landowner/affected party where applicable
- Cite NEPA-relevant impacts
- Address specific environmental justice considerations
- Reference cumulative impacts of related projects
