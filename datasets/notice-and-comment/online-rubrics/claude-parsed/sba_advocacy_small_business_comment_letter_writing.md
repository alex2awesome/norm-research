# SBA Office of Advocacy: Small Business Guide to Comment Letter Writing

## Overview
The SBA Office of Advocacy provides comprehensive guidance for small businesses seeking to influence federal rulemaking through effective comment letters.

## Why Comment Letters Matter
"It is invaluable...for the agency to hear directly from small businesses impacted" by rules, since associations may not capture every business's specific perspective.

## Key Steps for Writing Effective Comments

**1. Understand the Rule**
- Carefully review proposed regulations in the Federal Register
- Identify areas with the most significant business impact

**2. Be Clear and Concise**
- State your purpose early in the letter
- Use plain language to ensure regulatory agencies understand your concerns

**3. Explain Business Impact**
- Provide concrete examples of operational effects
- Include supporting data, cost estimates, and industry benchmarks

**4. Offer Solutions**
- Suggest specific, practical alternatives to problematic provisions
- Emphasize how changes benefit both business and consumers

**5. Maintain Professionalism**
- Use respectful, constructive tone
- Follow each agency's submission guidelines

## Comment Letter Structure

A strong letter includes:
- **Introduction**: Identify yourself, the rule name, your business relevance, and key concerns
- **Body**: Describe supported/opposed provisions with specific explanations and supporting data
- **Conclusion**: Summarize main points and thank the agency

## Argument Framework
Effective arguments combine three elements:
1. A clear claim
2. Supporting data (quantified impact)
3. Explanation of why the evidence matters

## Professional Tips
- Be specific with details and data
- Cite sources clearly
- Establish credibility through relevant experience
- Use accessible language and professional tone
- Proofread carefully

**Submission Timing**: Submit comments before the deadline (typically 30-60 days) to allow adequate agency review.
