# How to Submit a Public Comment Against the Immigrant Registry (United We Dream)

## Eight Steps
1. Prepare your comment using the provided template
2. Access Regulations.gov at the rule page (USCIS-2025-0004-0001)
3. Click the Comment button
4. Enter your comment in the text field
5. Paste your prepared text
6. Select Anonymous under the identity question
7. Complete the reCAPTCHA
8. Submit

## Key Guidance
- **Deadline**: April 11, 2025, 11:59 PM EDT
- **Anonymity**: explicitly recommended to protect commenters' safety
- **Personalization**: "Don't forget to make it personal — your story and feelings matter."

## Template Framework
Emphasizes opposition to the registry's discriminatory nature, historical parallels to harmful registration systems, the rule as a "registration trap" forcing self-identification, and calls for rescinding the rule and expanding immigrant protections.

## Practitioner Insight
For vulnerable populations, the standard advice ("personalize, identify yourself, give specifics") collides with safety. United We Dream's solution: anonymized but personalized comments — preserve the texture of lived experience without the identifying metadata.
