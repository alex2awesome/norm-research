# CFPB Guide on Commenting on Notices

## Posting and Access
Bureau notices open for public comment are posted to the Notice and Opportunities to Comment section when publicly released, which may precede Federal Register publication by days to weeks. All notices requesting comment and published in the Federal Register receive dockets on Regulations.gov, typically opened on the publication date.

## Submission Process
If a notice has been released but not yet published in the Federal Register, commenters can either wait for the official version (which is preferred) or submit comments through alternate means listed in the notice's ADDRESSES section.

## Public Record Policy
"The Bureau currently has a policy of generally posting all comments received (by any means specified in the notice) to the docket on Regulations.gov." All comments, attachments, and supporting materials become part of the public record subject to disclosure.

## Exceptions to Posting
The Bureau may decline to post or withdraw certain materials, including duplicate submissions, copyrighted content not owned by the submitter, confidential business information, spam, threats, and disavowed comments.

## Privacy Caution
Commenters should avoid including proprietary information, sensitive personal data (like account or Social Security numbers), or other individuals' names, as the Bureau generally posts all comments without independent verification or standard redaction of identifying information.

## Use in Rulemaking
The Bureau uses public comments submitted in response to Notices of Proposed Rulemaking to inform decision-making by considering their substantive content.
