# Executive Order 13563: Improving Regulation and Regulatory Review (Obama, 2011)

## Purpose

The order supplements and affirms the requirements of Executive Order 12866 "Regulatory Planning and Review" and sets out principles and requirements designed to promote public participation, improve integration and innovation, increase flexibility, ensure scientific integrity, and increase retrospective analysis of existing rules.

## Key Provisions

**General Regulatory Principles:**
The regulatory system must:
- Protect public health, welfare, safety, and environment while promoting economic growth
- Be based on the best available science
- Allow for public participation and open exchange of ideas
- Promote predictability and reduce uncertainty
- Use the best, most innovative, and least burdensome tools
- Take into account benefits and costs (including non-quantifiable values like equity, human dignity, fairness)
- Ensure regulations are accessible, consistent, written in plain language, and easy to understand

**Retrospective Review:**
The order directed federal agencies to develop a Preliminary Plan on the Retrospective Review of Existing Regulations, which outlined the agency's approach for periodically reviewing regulations.

**Public Participation:**
"Regulations shall be adopted through a process that involves public participation, based on the open exchange of information and perspectives among State, local, and tribal officials, experts, affected stakeholders in the private sector, and the public."

## Implications for Public Comments

Effective comments under EO 13563 should:
- Engage with cost AND benefit dimensions
- Acknowledge non-quantifiable values (equity, fairness, human dignity)
- Use plain language (model the standard the rule is held to)
- Suggest more flexible, less burdensome alternatives
- Cite best available science
- Incorporate state/local/tribal perspectives where relevant
- Address whether existing rules should be modified or repealed
