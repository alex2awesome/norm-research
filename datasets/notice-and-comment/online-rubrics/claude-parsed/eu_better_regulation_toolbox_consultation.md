# EU Better Regulation Toolbox: Public Consultation Framework

## Overview

The better regulation guidelines and toolbox provide concrete guidance to Commission services involved in preparing new initiatives and proposals, or managing and evaluating existing legislation. Consulting stakeholders is at the heart of the better regulation agenda.

## Public Consultation Tools

The toolbox includes several consultation-related tools:
- **Tool #51**: Consulting stakeholders
- **Tool #52**: Consultation strategy
- **Tool #53**: Conducting consultation activities
- **Tool #54**: Analysing data and informing policymaking
- **Tool #55**: Horizontal matters (publication of responses, data protection, access to documents, transparency register)

## Recent Changes (Call for Evidence)

The process of consulting with stakeholders has been simplified. The Call for Evidence (CFE) replaced Inception Impact Assessments and Evaluation Roadmaps and consolidated all public consultations into a single call. This aimed at providing flexibility to Commission services in organising consultation activities and reducing consultation fatigue.

## Key Principles

The Commission should:
- Ensure wide participation throughout the policy cycle
- Seek and consider a wide range of views and input
- Ensure that all relevant parties have had the opportunity to express their opinions
- Use web-based public consultations together with targeted consultations as key elements of strategy

## Quality Indicators for Effective Consultation Responses

EU consultation quality assessment looks for:
- Relevance to the scope of the consultation
- Substantive engagement with the questions posed
- Evidence-based reasoning
- Stakeholder identity transparency (in the Transparency Register)
- Clear positioning on the policy options presented
- Proposed alternative options where relevant
