# ILRC Guide: Submitting Comments on Federal Regulations

## Overview
The Immigrant Legal Resource Center emphasizes that "Commenting on regulations is one of the most direct and important ways that the public can influence federal policy." Agencies must legally review and consider public submissions.

## Finding Open Regulations
Search two primary sources:
- **Regulations.gov** (official submission portal)
- **Federalregister.gov** (daily publication with email alerts)

Filter by agency, docket number, or posting date.

## Submission Basics

**Timeline:** "You can submit comments any time during the comment period, which is usually 30 or 60 days from the date that the regulation is published."

**Critical deadline:** Comments must arrive before midnight Eastern Time on the specified deadline date—the portal closes at that exact time regardless of your local timezone.

## Who Can Submit
Anyone may participate anonymously or representing an organization. No specialized credentials required.

## Comment Content Guidelines

**What to include:**
- Personal impacts on you, family, or community
- Data and survey results
- Stories from affected community members
- Any relevant information for agency consideration

**Restrictions:** Agencies only must consider comments directly relevant to the specific regulation. Off-topic submissions and late comments won't be reviewed.

## Submission Methods
Electronic submission (in English) via regulations.gov or Federal Register is standard. Follow specific instructions provided—some rules require mailing instead. You may type content directly or upload a file.
