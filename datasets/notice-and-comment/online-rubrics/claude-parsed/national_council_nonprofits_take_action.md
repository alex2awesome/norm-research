# National Council of Nonprofits: Public Comment Guidance

## Overview

The National Council of Nonprofits offers a comment guide which includes talking points, instructions, and more information for submitting public comments. Advocacy tools can help submit public comments, integrating directly with government agency forms like regulations.gov.

## Advocacy Framework

Advocacy is a broad term encompassing any type of communication that advances a nonprofit's mission, including:
- Legislative lobbying on public policy issues
- Nonpartisan voter engagement activities
- Issue-based rallies
- Storytelling
- Media outreach
- Conversations with donors, volunteers, and business leaders
- **Submitting comments on proposed regulations**

## Key Advocacy Concepts for Comments

For nonprofits commenting on proposed federal rules:
- Use the organization's mission as the framing
- Speak from sector expertise (e.g., human services, arts, environmental)
- Cite empirical data from your direct service experience
- Aggregate impacts across the population you serve
- Frame harm/benefit in terms of the public the rule affects
- Avoid partisan framing (501(c)(3) restrictions)
- Be specific about regulatory provisions
- Coordinate with peer organizations through sign-on letters when appropriate

## Templates and Tools

The Council provides:
- Sample comment templates customizable for nonprofits
- Issue-specific talking points
- Action alerts when major rules are proposed
- Coalition coordination resources
- Lobbying limit calculators (501(c)(3) compliance)

## Distinct Considerations for Nonprofit Commenters

- Nonprofits speaking as institutions can endorse/oppose specific rules without 501(h) lobbying limits
- Aggregating client/beneficiary perspectives adds weight
- Nonprofit comments should preserve client confidentiality
- Sign-on letters with multiple organizations amplify smaller organizations
