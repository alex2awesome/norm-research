# Guidance on Commenting on EPA Dockets

## Primary Submission Method
Regulations.gov is the EPA's preferred method of receiving comments, though alternative formats are accepted through various mailing addresses.

## Tips for Effective Comments

**Identification & Clarity**
Include the docket ID number and identifying information in your subject line to ensure your comment reaches the right location and isn't lost or misdirected.

**Understanding & Research**
Read the document thoroughly before commenting. If confused, contact the EPA representative listed in the document and describe your difficulty in your response.

**Follow Instructions**
Adhere to any specific directions provided by the Agency, including responding to particular questions or organizing comments in requested formats.

**Quality Over Volume**
The comment process is not a vote – one well-supported comment is often more informative to the agency than a thousand form letters. Focus on substantive arguments rather than expressing simple agreement or disagreement.

**Support Your Claims**
Explain your position clearly using specific examples. Avoid profanity or personal threats. Suggest alternative language or solutions when appropriate.

**Use Evidence**
Base arguments on sound reasoning, scientific evidence, or documented personal impact. If citing costs or burdens, explain your calculations in reproducible detail.

**Address Counterarguments**
Acknowledge and respond to opposing viewpoints and trade-offs relevant to your position.

**Meet Deadlines**
Submit comments by the deadline specified in the Federal Register document.

## Rules & Restrictions

Comments with threatening language or profanity are rejected. Multimedia submissions require accompanying written comments. Copyrighted material needs proof of redistribution rights. Confidential Business Information should not be submitted electronically; contact the EPA Docket Center (202-566-1744) for guidance.
