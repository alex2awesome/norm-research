# Bunea - Responsive to What? Explaining the Information Quality of Public Comments on Bureaucratic Policymaking

Adriana Bunea, *Governance*, 2026.

## Research Question

What drives the quality of information that public comments on bureaucratic policymaking provide? Using a text-as-data approach to develop and validate four measures of information quality.

## Methodology

Applied to an original dataset covering more than 20,000 public comments across 1,036 policy acts issued by the European Commission between 2016 and 2021.

## Four Measures of Information Quality (Rubric)

The paper validates four measurable dimensions of comment quality:
1. **Substantive depth** - level of detail and specificity
2. **Information density** - signal-to-noise ratio
3. **Syntactic complexity** - sophistication of argumentation
4. **Topical relevance** - alignment with consultation questions

## Key Findings

The interplay of information demand and supply and institutional factors matter for varying information quality of public comments.

**Specifically:**
- More informationally dense and syntactically complex EC policy documents generate public comments of higher quality
- Stakeholder type predicts information quality (industry vs. NGO vs. citizen)
- Repeat-player commenters submit higher-quality comments
- Consultation framing affects responsiveness

## Implications for What Makes a "High-Information" Comment

Per Bunea's measures, a high-quality comment:
- Has substantial substantive content (not boilerplate)
- Uses topic-specific terminology appropriately
- Engages with the specific questions posed by the agency
- Has structured argumentation rather than diffuse claims
- Provides verifiable factual claims
- Does not rely solely on emotional appeals or pure preference statements
