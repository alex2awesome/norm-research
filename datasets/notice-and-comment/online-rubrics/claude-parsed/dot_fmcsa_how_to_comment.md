# FMCSA Guide on Commenting on FMCSA Rulemakings

## Where to Submit Comments

Comments may be submitted via:
- **Federal eRulemaking Portal**: http://www.regulations.gov, identified by the docket number
- **Mail**: Docket Operations, U.S. Department of Transportation
- **Hand Delivery**: 1200 New Jersey Avenue SE, West Building Ground Floor, Room W12-140, Washington, DC 20590-0001
- **Fax**: 1-202-493-2251

## Best Practices

To make your comment most effective:
- Submit through Regulations.gov (preferred)
- Reference the specific docket number in the body
- Address the questions FMCSA poses in the NPRM
- Include data on safety impacts, motor carrier costs, driver impacts
- Cite specific FMCSA regulations (49 CFR §§ 350-399)
- Explain technical operational impacts

## What Makes Comments Effective for FMCSA

- Driver safety data
- Crash statistics with verifiable sources
- Cost-of-compliance estimates for motor carriers
- Hours-of-service / fatigue research
- Technology integration considerations (ELDs, AOBRDs)
- Inspection and audit experience
- Small motor carrier perspectives (RFA implications)

## Notes
- Comments are publicly available
- Do not submit confidential business information except as marked
- Late comments may not be considered
