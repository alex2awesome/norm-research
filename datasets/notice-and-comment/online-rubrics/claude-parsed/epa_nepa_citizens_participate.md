# EPA NEPA Citizens Participation Guide

## Key Participation Opportunities

The EPA identifies two primary moments for public involvement:
- "When an agency begins the NEPA analysis"
- "When a NEPA document is published for public review and comment"

Agencies use varied notification methods, including Federal Register publications, agency websites, and newspaper notices.

## The Scoping Process

Scoping allows residents to shape environmental review priorities. When an Environmental Impact Statement is required, agencies publish a Notice of Intent in the Federal Register, initiating scoping and describing "the proposed action and a description of the scoping process, including any scheduled meetings."

Agencies employ multiple engagement formats:
- Public meetings
- Conference calls
- Formal hearings
- Informal workshops
- Written comment opportunities

## Commenting on NEPA Documents

Draft Environmental Impact Statements remain open for public feedback for "a minimum of 45 days." Citizens can submit comments via email or attend in-person meetings to voice concerns.

## What Makes NEPA Comments Effective

EPA's general guidance on NEPA comments:
- Comments should be **clear, concise, and relevant** to the analysis of the proposed action
- Comments that are **solutions-oriented**, provide **specific examples**, and contribute to developing alternatives will be more effective than those that simply convey opposition
- General comments that state an action will have "significant environmental effects" will not help an agency make a better decision unless the comment explains the relevant causes and environmental effects and why the issues raised are significant

## Active EIS Database

The EPA maintains an online database of active Environmental Impact Statements, displaying:
- Project names
- Lead agencies
- Locations
- Comment deadlines
- Contact information for each proposed action

For additional information, the Council on Environmental Quality offers "A Citizen's Guide to NEPA" describing the full process and engagement pathways.
