# No Kid Hungry: How to Comment on Federal Regulations

## Overview

No Kid Hungry, the campaign of Share Our Strength, provides commentary guidance specifically tailored to nonprofit advocates working on child nutrition (school meals, SNAP, WIC, summer EBT, summer meal programs).

## Key Recommendations

### Before You Write
- Read the proposed rule and accompanying preamble
- Identify the specific provisions affecting child nutrition programs
- Coordinate with your state agency contacts and partner organizations
- Consider sign-on letters for amplification

### Structure of an Effective Comment
1. Identification of organization/individual and connection to child nutrition
2. Statement of position (support, oppose, conditional)
3. Specific section-by-section comments
4. Stories from those affected (anonymized if appropriate)
5. Data on program participation, hunger rates, child outcomes
6. Specific suggested changes
7. Conclusion with clear ask

### What Makes Comments Effective for Child Nutrition Rules
- Cite specific USDA FNS instructions, memos, and prior guidance
- Reference the Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act and Russell-Ginnis programs
- Provide state participation data
- Cite USDA's own ERS food security data
- Address impacts on specific child populations (rural, urban, AI/AN, etc.)
- Engage with administrative burden on schools, states, and providers
- Cite peer-reviewed research on nutrition program effectiveness
- Reference cost-effectiveness analyses
- Address food waste, nutrition standards, and access dimensions

## Sample Topics for Child Nutrition Comments

- School meal pattern and nutrition standards
- Community Eligibility Provision thresholds
- SNAP work requirements and time limits
- Summer EBT eligibility and amounts
- WIC food package contents
- Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) reimbursement
- Summer Food Service Program (SFSP) site eligibility

## Action Tips

- Submit comments early in the period
- Save your tracking number
- Share with partners to amplify
- Track agency response in the final rule
- Prepare for litigation if final rule departs from comment record
