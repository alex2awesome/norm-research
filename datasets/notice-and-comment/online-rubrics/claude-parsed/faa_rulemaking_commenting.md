# FAA Guide on Commenting on Aviation Proposed Rules

## Where to Find Proposed Rules

You can find FAA proposed regulations (NPRM) currently available for comment in the Federal Docket Management System at regulations.gov.

## How to Submit Comments

Comments can be submitted through the Federal eRulemaking Portal at https://www.regulations.gov/ and by following the online instructions for sending your comments electronically. Reference the specific docket number assigned to the proposed rule.

## Sensitive Security Information

You should not submit sensitive security information to the rulemaking docket, unless you are invited to do so in the request for comments. If the FAA asks for this information, they will tell you how to submit it and will provide a separate non-public docket.

## Effective FAA Comment Strategies

For aviation rulemakings, effective comments:
- Cite specific FAA regulations (14 CFR Parts 21, 25, 91, 121, 135, etc.)
- Address pilot/operator/manufacturer impacts
- Include safety data with verifiable sources (NTSB reports, ASRS, etc.)
- Engage with FAA Advisory Circulars and Orders
- Reference international aviation standards (ICAO, EASA)
- Provide cost estimates for affected segments
- Distinguish impacts on commercial operators, GA, UAS, manufacturers

## FAA-Specific Considerations

- AC 11-2A provides information on participation in FAA rulemaking
- Aviation Rulemaking Committees (ARCs) provide pre-NPRM input opportunities
- The Aviation Rulemaking Advisory Committee (ARAC) is the formal advisory body
- For airworthiness directives, comment timing is often compressed
- Special conditions and exemptions follow distinct comment processes
