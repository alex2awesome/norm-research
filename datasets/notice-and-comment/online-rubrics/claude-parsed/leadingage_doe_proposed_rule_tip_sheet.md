# Tip Sheet: Commenting on Department of Education Proposed Rule

## Best Practices for Comments

When submitting comments, agency reviewers look for sound science and reasoning in the comments they receive. Recommended practices:

- Make your comments unique and include how this rule will personally impact you and/or your community.
- Address trade-offs and opposing views in your comments, and include data on the qualitative and quantitative effects the rule may have, if available.

## How to Submit Comments

To provide your comments, go to the Department's comment page for this rule, where you can either copy and paste your comments into the provided space or upload a file into the comment web form. More specifically, comments must be submitted through the Federal eRulemaking Portal at regulations.gov.

## Timeline

The comment period varies by rule. For example, comments on the Department of Education proposed rule were due Monday, March 2, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET.
