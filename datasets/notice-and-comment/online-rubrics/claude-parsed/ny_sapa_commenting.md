# New York State Administrative Procedure Act (SAPA): Public Commenting

## Comment Period and Timeline

The public has a minimum period of **60 days after publication** to comment on a proposed rule (or if a hearing is required, the comment period extends to 5 days after the last public hearing).

## Where to Find Proposed Rules

A Notice of Proposed Rule Making is published in the State Register. The Secretary of State publishes an electronic copy of the state register which is freely available on the internet.

## How to Submit Comments

When an agency provides an electronic mail address for the submission of comments, the Secretary of State provides a direct link between the electronic copy of the state register and that electronic mail address. This means you can typically submit comments electronically through the links provided in the State Register notice.

## What Agencies Are Subject to SAPA

Most actions taken by state agencies are subject to SAPA requirements, though the Office of Court Administration and the Judiciary are exempt.

## Effective Comment Strategies (NY-Specific)

- Engage with the Regulatory Impact Statement (RIS) the agency must publish
- Address the Regulatory Flexibility Analysis for Small Businesses and Local Governments
- Engage with the Job Impact Statement if the rule may displace workers
- Note Rural Area Flexibility Analysis if applicable
- Cite NY State law and prior agency guidance
- Reference the SAPA Section 202 procedural requirements
- Demand the Assessment of Public Comment in the final rulemaking address your points
