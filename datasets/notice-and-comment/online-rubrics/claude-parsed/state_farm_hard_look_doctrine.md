# State Farm Hard Look Doctrine

## Origin

The U.S. Supreme Court's 1983 decision in *Motor Vehicle Manufacturers Ass'n v. State Farm Mutual Automobile Insurance Co.* announced a framework for judicial review of agency actions that are challenged as arbitrary and capricious. While *Vermont Yankee* forbids courts from adding procedures to agency decision-making beyond what the APA requires, courts use the State Farm hard look review to require detailed explanations for agency behavior.

## Core Requirements (Implications for Comments)

The hard look standard requires the agency to:

1. **Articulate the policies** that it purports to be furthering
2. **Show statutory grounding** for those policies
3. **Address potential alternative means** to achieve them
4. **Demonstrate factual support** in the rulemaking record (no inventing facts)
5. **Show reasonableness** of its choice in light of relevant policies, alternatives, and facts
6. **Respond to relevant and significant comments**

## Comments Responsiveness

Where an agency engages in notice-and-comment rulemaking, it must demonstrate that it reasonably considered the comments that were submitted, although it "need not address every comment." An agency must demonstrate the rationality of its decision-making process by responding to those comments that are **relevant and significant**.

## What Makes a Comment "Relevant and Significant"

For purposes of compelling agency response under State Farm:
- Comments that raise data or evidence undermining the agency's factual basis
- Comments that propose alternatives the agency hasn't considered
- Comments that identify legal authority issues
- Comments that show the agency hasn't considered an important aspect of the problem
- Comments that show the agency's explanation runs counter to the evidence

## Practical Lesson for Comment Writers

To maximize impact:
- Frame comments to trigger the agency's duty to respond
- Cite the specific factual or analytical claim being challenged
- Provide the alternative analysis or evidence
- Make the comment hard to ignore on judicial review
