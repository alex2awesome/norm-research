# Protect Democracy, "What Makes an Effective Public Comment on a Federal Regulation" (2025)

Issue brief grounded in 5 U.S.C. § 553 and case law on agency response obligations. Frames
comment quality around **what triggers an agency's legal obligation to respond**.

## Core Quality Elements

### 1. Specificity & Evidence
Comments must reference the **specific regulation being addressed** and support positions with
factual data, research, or concrete examples rather than general statements.

### 2. Clear Position-Taking
Commenters should **explicitly state** whether they support, oppose, or propose modifications,
avoiding ambiguous language.

### 3. Substantive Analysis
Agencies must "respond to all significant issues raised in the comments," making **thorough
engagement with the regulatory rationale** essential to triggering response obligations.

## Recommended Elements

| Element | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------|
| Citations to relevant legal authorities (5 U.S.C. § 553) | Frames the comment as legally cognizable |
| Reference to existing studies or precedents | Provides agency with a vetted basis for change |
| Personal expertise or professional qualifications | Establishes the commenter's epistemic authority |
| Specific alternative regulatory text | Gives the agency something to adopt |
| Acknowledgment of counterarguments | Demonstrates good-faith engagement |
| Implementation feasibility and cost discussion | Addresses agency implementation concerns |

## Structural Quality Indicators

- Logical flow from problem identification to proposed solution
- Distinction between opinion and verifiable fact
- Connection between proposed changes and statutory requirements
- Accessible format with clear organization, professional tone, concise language

## Rubric Items

- [ ] Does the comment cite 5 U.S.C. § 553 or other governing authority where relevant?
- [ ] Does the comment cite empirical studies or precedents?
- [ ] Does the commenter identify their professional or experiential qualifications?
- [ ] Does the comment propose specific alternative regulatory text?
- [ ] Does the comment acknowledge counterarguments?
- [ ] Does the comment discuss implementation feasibility and cost?
- [ ] Does the comment connect requested changes to statutory requirements?
- [ ] Does the comment distinguish opinion from verifiable fact?
