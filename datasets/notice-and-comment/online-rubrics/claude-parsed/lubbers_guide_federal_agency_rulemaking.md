# Lubbers - A Guide to Federal Agency Rulemaking

Jeffrey S. Lubbers, ABA Section of Administrative Law and Regulatory Practice, 6th Edition.

## About

Jeffrey S. Lubbers is a Professor of Practice in Administrative Law at American University's Washington College of Law. He is the author of the ABA's Guide to Federal Agency Rulemaking. The 6th edition brings the Guide up-to-date with respect to recent cases and changes during the latter half of the Obama Administration and the early years of the Trump Administration.

## Scope

The guide aims to inform agency rule-makers, participants and others interested in the subject with a stage-by-stage view of the rulemaking process, focusing on **'informal' rather than 'formal' proceedings**. The guide provides a time-saving reference for the latest case law, and the most recent legislation affecting rulemaking.

## Topics Covered Relevant to Public Comments

- The Administrative Procedure Act §553 framework
- The "logical outgrowth" doctrine and what makes a final rule a permissible variant of the proposed rule
- The "concise general statement" requirement and how agency response to comments shapes review
- Pre-rulemaking proceedings (ANPRMs, RFIs)
- Negotiated rulemaking under the Negotiated Rulemaking Act
- Direct final rulemaking and interim final rules
- The role of OMB/OIRA review
- Petitions for rulemaking (5 U.S.C. § 553(e))
- Hard look review (State Farm) and what it means for the comment record
- Ex parte communications and disclosure rules
- Treatment of confidential information in dockets

## Practical Implications

For commenters, the guide highlights:
- The importance of preserving issues in the docket for judicial review
- The "duty to respond" doctrine - agencies must respond to significant comments
- Strategic timing of submissions across rule stages
- How docket records become the basis for litigation
