# State Department/ATF Arms Export Proposed Rule Commenting

## Submission Methods

Comments can be submitted via:
- **Federal e-rulemaking portal**: regulations.gov (will not accept comments after midnight Eastern Time on the last day)
- **Mail**: ATF Rulemaking Comments; Mail Stop 6N-518, Office of Regulatory Affairs; Enforcement Programs and Services; Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives; 99 New York Ave, NE; Washington, DC 20226

Reference the specific RIN in the subject (e.g., RIN 1140-AA77).

## Effective Arms Export Comments

For ITAR/EAR rulemakings affecting export controls, effective comments:
- Cite specific USML categories or CCL ECCNs being amended
- Reference Arms Export Control Act (22 USC § 2778) provisions
- Address national security implications
- Include manufacturer/exporter compliance cost estimates
- Address impacts on foreign policy objectives
- Reference Wassenaar Arrangement, MTCR, and other multilateral regimes
- Provide industry impact data (jobs, exports, R&D)
- Address congressional notification requirements
- Identify dual-use considerations
- Consider non-proliferation impacts

## Common Topics

- Transfer of items between USML and CCL
- Definitions of "defense articles," "components," "parts"
- Brokering controls
- Re-export and retransfer controls
- License application procedures
- Country-specific embargoes
