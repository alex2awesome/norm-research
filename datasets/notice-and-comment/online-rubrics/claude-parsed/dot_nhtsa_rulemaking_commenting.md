# DOT/NHTSA Rulemaking Process and Commenting Guide

## Where to Submit Comments

Comments can be submitted through https://www.regulations.gov, or at the Docket Management Facility, located at the U.S. Department of Transportation.

## Comment Period Timeframe

Generally, agencies allow 60 days for public comment. Sometimes they provide much longer periods, and they may use shorter periods where they can provide justification for them. The public may also request more time; if you provide justification, that will help the agency make a decision on whether to extend or reopen the comment period.

## What Makes Comments Effective

Comments that get the most attention are those that provide empirical evidence to support the commenter's position. Comments that state an opinion without any supporting evidence are reviewed but generally have little impact on the agency's decision for the final rule.

## The Comment Process

Once the comment period closes, the agency reviews all the comments in the docket. NHTSA reviews these comments and may use them to inform its decisionmaking. For example, the agency may reconsider a rule if a comment addresses material errors of fact or law, raises new evidence, or cites practicality issues in complying with the new rule.

The specific procedures and comment deadlines for each proposed rule are typically included in the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) published in the Federal Register.
