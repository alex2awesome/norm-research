# USPTO Public Comments Guide

## How to Submit Comments

Find, review, and submit comments online for all proposed Federal regulations at Regulations.gov. Comments must be submitted through the Federal eRulemaking Portal at https://www.regulations.gov.

## Alternative Submission Methods

For specific USPTO patent proceedings, comments can also be submitted through other channels:
- By electronic mail message over the Internet
- By postal mail addressed to: Mail Stop Patent Board, Director of the United States Patent and Trademark Office, P.O. Box 1450, Alexandria, VA 22313-1450

## Important Notes

- If electronic submission is not feasible due to lack of access to a computer/internet, contact the USPTO for special instructions.
- Comments will be made available for public inspection. Information that the submitter does not desire to be made public, such as address or phone number, should not be included.
- Each proposed rule has its own comment deadline.

## Specific Recommendations for USPTO Comments

For USPTO rules (patent or trademark), effective comments should:
- Cite specific patent statute provisions (35 U.S.C. § 100 et seq.)
- Reference the USPTO's Manual of Patent Examining Procedure (MPEP) where relevant
- Engage with prior USPTO interpretations and Federal Circuit case law
- Provide data on inventor/applicant impact (especially small inventors)
- Address cross-Atlantic harmonization with EPO/JPO/other offices where applicable
- Suggest specific regulatory text changes
- Address fee/cost impacts on different applicant categories
