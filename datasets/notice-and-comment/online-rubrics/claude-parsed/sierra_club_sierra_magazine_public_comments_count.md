# Public Comments: How to Make Yours Count (Sierra Club)

## Key Principles

**Be Specific**: Identify what you're responding to and clearly state what changes you want. Reference particular sections or paragraphs of the proposal.

**Use Data**: Support your argument with quantitative information, studies, citations, and analysis of applicable laws or regulations.

**Be Original**: Write in your own words to explain why the issue matters to you personally, rather than relying solely on form letters.

## Strategic Tactics

- Individual submissions matter more than bundled ones: "If you deliver them as one big file...they are going to count as one, but if people send individual emails, then they count those separately."
- Physical letters carry weight because of the time investment required to write them.
- Customize form letters by selecting resonant points and rewriting in your own words — this takes only minutes longer but makes a significant difference.
- Mix up your approach: vary submission methods and timing to maintain agency attention.

## NEPA-Specific Mechanics

Agencies responding to NEPA comments must address each distinct issue raised. **One letter covering five different issues requires five separate responses**, while 100 identical comments require just one response to that single issue. This is a key reason original, multi-issue comments are operationally more demanding for agencies than form letters.
