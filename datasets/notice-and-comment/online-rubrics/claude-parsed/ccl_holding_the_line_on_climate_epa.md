# Holding the Line on Climate: EPA (Citizens' Climate Lobby)

## Key Issue
On July 29, 2025, the EPA proposed rescinding its 2009 endangerment finding — the foundation for all federal climate pollution regulations affecting power plants and vehicle emissions.

## CCL's Actions
Citizens' Climate Lobby submitted formal comments on this EPA proposal, despite typically focusing on congressional advocacy rather than agency matters. The organization identified this as an opportunity where its voice could meaningfully contribute.

## Public Comment Process
The EPA received over 44,000 public comments within the first week. Comment period open until September 15, 2025.

## Key Arguments
CCL's comment contends the EPA has misinterpreted the Clean Air Act and congressional intent: "Congress' decadeslong support for the EPA's mandate to regulate greenhouse gas emissions" reflects legislative backing for these regulations, including through the Inflation Reduction Act.

## CCL's Strategic Perspective
Executive regulations are fragile and shift with administrations. CCL prioritizes pursuing durable, congressionally-enacted climate solutions over agency rules. Filed comment as a defensive backstop, not the primary lever.

## Practitioner Takeaway
Organizations with a primarily legislative theory of change can still file targeted comments at moments of legal exposure (e.g., when an agency action will define the future statutory baseline) without abandoning their main strategy.
