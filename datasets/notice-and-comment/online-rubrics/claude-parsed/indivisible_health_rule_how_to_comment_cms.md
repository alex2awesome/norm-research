# Trump's Rulemaking Attack on Healthcare and How to Fight Back (Indivisible)

## Why Comments Matter
"All submitted comments become part of the public administrative record. Judges, policymakers, and journalists review these records." Agencies are required to read every comment, and if finalizing the proposal, the final rule "must address all substantive concerns raised by commenters or face potential legal challenges to strike down the rule on procedural grounds."

## Minimal Viable Comment
"Your comment can be as simple as 'I strongly oppose [this rule or these provisions] because [your reason]; I urge CMS [to withdraw the rule/not to finalize these provisions].'"

## Key Tips
- The more unique and detailed your comment, the better. Submitting any kind of comment is enough — but more substance gets more weight.
- You don't need to comment on everything. Pick the provisions you understand best.
- Use your expertise — as a provider, immigrant, patient, or advocate.
- Don't just copy-paste. Unique, personalized comments carry more weight.

## Submission Process
Go to https://www.regulations.gov/document/ — type comment in the box (max 5,000 characters) or upload a PDF/Word document.
