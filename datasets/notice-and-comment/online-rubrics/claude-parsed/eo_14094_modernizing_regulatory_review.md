# Executive Order 14094 - Modernizing Regulatory Review (Biden, 2023)

President Biden issued EO 14094 on April 6, 2023. The order was intended to modernize the regulatory process to advance policies that promote the public interest and address national priorities.

## Key Changes

### Definition of Significant Regulatory Actions
The executive order doubled the cost threshold for "significant regulatory action" from $100 million to $200 million per year of impact. EO 14094 omitted language about "novel legal or policy issues arising out of legal mandates" and instead covered only "legal or policy issues for which centralized review would meaningfully further the President's priorities."

### Regulatory Analysis and Cost-Benefit Assessment
OMB was directed to revise Circular A-4 within one year to ensure that regulatory analysis recognizes "distributive impacts and equity." The proposal replaced traditional discount rates of 3 percent and 7 percent with a discount rate of 1.7 percent.

### Public Engagement Reforms
The order required OMB to reform the process for meetings with OIRA on proposed rules, including:
- Facilitating meeting requests to ensure access for those who have not historically participated
- Increasing transparency about which groups are granted meetings

## Implications for Comments

EO 14094 emphasized:
- **Equity considerations** as legitimate analytical factors
- **Underserved community participation** as an explicit goal
- **Distributional impacts** being valued (not just net benefits)
- **Lower discount rates** giving more weight to long-term/intergenerational benefits

Comments under EO 14094-era rules should:
- Address distributional impacts on identifiable groups
- Highlight effects on underserved communities
- Engage with non-quantified equity benefits
- Acknowledge the lower discount rate's effect on future-oriented analysis

## Status

President Donald Trump revoked Executive Order 14094 on January 20, 2025, via Executive Order 14148.
