# Practical Guide for Submitting FTC Noncompete Comments

## Key Deadlines
- **Comment Period:** January 8 – April 19, 2023
- **Original deadline mentioned:** March 20, 2023

## Submission Methods

**Physical Mail:**
Federal Trade Commission, Office of the Secretary, 600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Suite CC-5610 (Annex C), Washington, DC 20580

**Online:**
FTC's Rulemaking Docket at regulations.gov (Docket FTC-2023-0007)

## Required Information
Each submission must include:
- Reference to "Non-Compete Clause Rulemaking, Matter No. P201200"
- Submitter's name and state

## Content Recommendations

The guide advises that "business submissions should be supported with pertinent details about the industry in which the business operates, the anticompetitive or procompetitive effects of noncompete clauses in that industry" and positions on the proposed rule and alternatives.

## Priority Comment Topics

The FTC specifically requests input on:

1. **Rule vs. Alternatives** – Whether to categorically ban noncompetes or adopt modified approaches with rebuttable presumptions
2. **Worker/Employer Definitions** – Whether current definitions require adjustment
3. **Differentiation by Worker Type** – Whether standards should vary by job function, earnings, or seniority
4. **Franchisee Coverage** – Whether franchisor-franchisee noncompetes should be included
5. **Related Covenants** – NDAs, non-solicitation agreements, and training-repayment agreements
6. **Reasonable Alternatives** – Other protective mechanisms besides noncompetes
7. **Choice-of-Law Evasion** – Employer use of jurisdictional strategies
8. **Ownership Thresholds** – Whether the 25% business interest exception should adjust
9. **Worker Notification** – Whether employers should notify workers of rescinded clauses

## Critical Timing
This comment period represents "the only opportunity for businesses to provide input on the proposed rule," making substantive participation crucial.
