# USDA Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) Guide

## Where to Submit Comments

You can submit comments through the Federal eRulemaking Portal by going to www.regulations.gov and entering the relevant APHIS docket number in the Search field.

Alternatively, you can submit comments by postal mail or commercial delivery to:
Docket No. [specific docket number]
Regulatory Analysis and Development, PPD, APHIS
5601 Sunnyside Avenue, Beltsville, Maryland 20705

## The Public Comment Process

For most rulemaking, the process includes publication in the Federal Register and an opportunity for—and agency response to—public comments. APHIS considers all public comments on the proposed rule and prepares responses.

## Effective APHIS Comments

For animal/plant health and biotechnology rules:
- Cite specific APHIS regulations (7 CFR, 9 CFR)
- Reference Plant Protection Act, Animal Health Protection Act provisions
- Provide producer/grower economic impact data
- Address pest/disease risk assessments
- Cite relevant scientific studies (peer-reviewed plant pathology, veterinary medicine)
- Engage with the Pest Risk Assessment (PRA) where applicable
- Address international trade implications (WTO SPS Agreement)
- Consider organic and non-organic operator impacts
- Address impacts on small farms (RFA)
