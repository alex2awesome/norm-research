# California APA: Commenting on Proposed Regulations

## Comment Period Requirements

The California APA requires an agency to provide for a minimum **forty-five day** written comment period wherein interested persons can submit written comments. This Public Comment Period begins when the Notice of Proposed Action (NOPA) is published in the Notice Register and ends when specified in the NOPA.

## Where to Find Proposed Regulations

All NOPAs are published in the California Regulatory Notice Register (Notice Register), which is issued every Friday. The agency must also mail the notice to those persons who have requested notice of regulatory actions, and post the notice and Initial Statement of Reasons on the agency's website.

## Two Ways to Participate

1. **Written Comments**: Interested persons or parties may submit written comments directly to the agency about the proposed rulemaking.

2. **Public Hearing**: The agency must accept both written and oral comments at the hearing. If an agency does not schedule a public hearing, any interested person can make a request for a hearing. The request must be made in writing within 15 days of the close of the written comment period.

## Effective Comment Standards (CA-Specific)

California's APA has a stronger requirement than federal: agencies must respond to "relevant" comments specifically. To maximize impact:

- Address the specific authority and reference statutes cited in the NOPA
- Engage with the Initial Statement of Reasons (ISOR)
- Note alternatives the agency must consider per Government Code §11346.2(b)
- Address the agency's economic impact analysis
- Cite to specific California Code of Regulations sections being amended
- Demand the Final Statement of Reasons address your specific points

## What Happens to Your Comments

The APA requires the rulemaking agency to consider all relevant and timely comments. The agency is only required to respond to comments that are directed at the proposed regulations or procedures followed (i.e., relevant) and that are received during the applicable comment period (i.e., timely).
