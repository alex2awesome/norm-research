# Naughton et al. - Understanding Commenter Influence During Agency Rule Development

Naughton, Schmid, and colleagues, *Journal of Policy Analysis and Management*, March 2009.

## Methodology

The researchers analyzed data from 36 Department of Transportation rules and almost 500 comments, with each rule beginning with an Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking—a procedure that allowed them to track formal participation of interested parties during rule development. Their analyses were supplemented by face-to-face interviews with agency officials.

## Key Findings on "Early-Bird" Commenters

The researchers found a strong agenda-setting role for early commenters—both in terms of affecting the content of future regulations and in thwarting unwanted regulations. They concluded that **"early-bird" commenters are well positioned to frame public policy debates** as rules move through the regulatory process.

## Practical Lesson for Comment Effectiveness

- Submit comments at the ANPRM/RFI stage, not just NPRM
- The earliest commenters set the framing - subsequent comments respond to that framing
- Engaging multiple times across the rule lifecycle increases influence
- Early framing can prevent unwanted rules from being proposed at all
