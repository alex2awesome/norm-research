# OECD Best Practice Principles for Regulatory Policy

## Overview

The OECD Best Practice Principles for Regulatory Policy series brings together OECD work related to the 2012 Recommendation of Council on Regulatory Policy and Governance, offering information about the policies, practices and institutions needed to improve regulatory policy and to support better regulatory governance.

## Core Framework

Better regulation requires that governments consistently follow principles of good rulemaking at every stage of the policy cycle:
- Focusing on issues that matter to people
- Listening to their ideas for solutions
- Using evidence to compare different options
- Getting the details of rules right to ensure they improve outcomes for people

## Suite of Best Practice Principles

The OECD has developed Best Practice Principles on:
- The governance of regulators
- Enforcement and inspections, and its toolkit
- Regulatory impact assessment
- Ex post reviews of regulatory stock
- One-stop shops for citizens and business
- International regulatory cooperation
- Stakeholder engagement

## Quality Indicators for Effective Consultation

Good consultations:
- Are timely and at the right stage of policy development
- Use multiple channels (online, in-person, targeted)
- Provide accessible documentation
- Welcome diverse perspectives, including from underrepresented groups
- Synthesize and respond publicly
- Demonstrate impact on policy

## Implications for Comment Writers (International Context)

Effective comments under OECD principles should:
- Cite evidence (preferably independent)
- Engage with the regulator's stated objectives
- Propose specific, workable alternatives
- Address proportionality (regulatory impact vs. problem scale)
- Consider international consistency where relevant
- Acknowledge cross-border or international cooperation dimensions
