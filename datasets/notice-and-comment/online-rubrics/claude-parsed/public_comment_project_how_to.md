# Public Comment Project's How-To Guide

## Core Principles
The most valuable public comments are **unique, fact-based, and succinct**. Scientists should leverage their ability to synthesize data and research as a key strength.

## Seven Key Guidelines

1. **Report Scientific Evidence**: Present data supporting or opposing the regulation's underlying theory to strengthen the agency's scientific foundation.

2. **Include Proper Identification**: "Include a heading that states the regulation name and the docket ID number" to demonstrate thorough review.

3. **Establish Credibility**: Open with a sentence identifying yourself and relevant expertise on the topic.

4. **Summarize Research First**: Begin with a discussion-style summary of your data before expanding on details for easier agency response.

5. **Clarify Implications**: Communicate how your research relates to the regulation rather than leaving inferences to the agency.

6. **Cite Sources**: Include specific paper citations to help agency staff locate references.

7. **Connect to Mission and Statutes**: Reference the agency's mission statement and relevant statutes to strengthen your argument's alignment with their mandate.
