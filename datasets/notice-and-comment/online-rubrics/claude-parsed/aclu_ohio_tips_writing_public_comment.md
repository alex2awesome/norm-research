# ACLU Ohio's Public Comment Tips

The organization offers four key recommendations for effective public comment:

1. **Share personal stories**: "If you have a personal story, share it." This humanizes your issue and helps officials understand its importance. Use identifying phrases like "As a teacher" or "As a parent."

2. **Keep it concise**: While there are no strict length requirements, brevity is encouraged to maintain focus and impact.

3. **Maintain respect**: "Overtly negative language may diminish your credibility and undermine your message." Format comments professionally as a letter, avoiding editorializing.

4. **Encourage others**: Share information with people in your network who might also provide valuable input to the process.

The ACLU emphasizes that anyone can submit public comment—no special credentials required—and these comments are welcomed at various government levels statewide.
