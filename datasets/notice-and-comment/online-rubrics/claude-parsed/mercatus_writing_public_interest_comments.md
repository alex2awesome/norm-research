# Mercatus Center's Guide to Writing Public Interest Comments

## Core Framework

The guide presents **six fundamental actions** for maximizing comment effectiveness:

1. **Understand the regulatory process** – Recognize how Congress delegates authority to agencies and the procedures they must follow
2. **Know agency definitions of "public interest"** – Align comments with how regulators define this concept
3. **Identify key economic analysis topics** – Address the substantive economic issues relevant to the regulation
4. **Recognize legal authority and constraints** – Understand what powers and limitations agencies face
5. **Use appropriate format, language, and tone** – Present comments professionally and clearly
6. **Share your comment** – Inform other interested parties about your submission

## Core Principle

The guide emphasizes that "a constructive, information-rich comment that clearly communicates and supports its claims is more likely to have an impact on regulatory decision-making."

## Key Insight

The guide states the guide is "not intended to promote any particular viewpoint" but rather serves as a resource for anyone seeking to improve regulators' understanding of proposed regulations' economic consequences through substantive economic research.

The full guide (available as a PDF download) provides detailed explanations of the regulatory process, including the federal government's **Unified Agenda** of proposed and ongoing regulatory actions.
