# VA Step-by-Step Guide for Providing Comments on Federal Register

## Overview

The VA published a step-by-step guide for providing comments on Federal Register documents. The guide provides visual instructions on how to submit comments both as an individual and as an organization.

## How to Comment on VA Proposed Rules

Comments on rules, proposed rules, and notices should be submitted through Regulations.gov, to agency dockets on Regulations.gov, or to other places identified under the "Addresses" heading in Federal Register documents.

### Step-by-Step Process

To leave a comment, click the comment button. You will be directed to a document open for comment and can click the comment button to leave a comment.

### Comment Periods

National standards of practice are typically available for comment for 60 days.

### VA Employee Considerations

VA employees should not comment via the Federal Register. If you are a VA employee and would like to comment, navigate to the National Standards of Practice Employee Participation and Review site page via VA SharePoint. **VA employee comments made via Regulations.gov will be withdrawn.**

### After Submission

You can save your Comment Tracking Number for future reference by:
- Printing the webpage and emailing the number to yourself
- Clicking the email opt-in button on the comment form prior to finalizing

## Effective VA Comments

For VA proposed rules:
- Identify yourself as veteran/service member/family/provider
- Reference specific service-connected conditions where applicable
- Cite specific 38 CFR provisions being amended
- Engage with veterans' health, benefit, or burial program impacts
- Provide data on veteran population impacts
- Reference VA's Office of Inspector General reports where relevant
- Cite GAO and CRS reports on VA programs
- Address Choice/MISSION Act implementation considerations
- Provide community provider perspective if applicable
