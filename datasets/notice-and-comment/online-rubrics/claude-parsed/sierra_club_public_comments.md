# Sierra Club Guide to Public Comments

## Key Recommendations for Writing Public Comments

- **Personalized letters are more effective than utilizing pre-written templates.**
- Keep your comments brief; one page or shorter recommended.
- Be specific and explain how the issue affects you and, if possible, how it impacts your community and/or state.

## Communication Best Practices

Sierra Club emphasizes several communication principles when advocating for environmental issues:
- Be concise and direct (e.g., "100% renewable energy will keep our air and water clean")
- Connect to widely held values identified in your community
- Make messages compelling and meaningful

## Additional Advocacy Resources

- Grassroots lobbying days
- Public comment workshops (some chapters provide slide decks)
- Calls to Members of Congress
- Coordinated comment letters
