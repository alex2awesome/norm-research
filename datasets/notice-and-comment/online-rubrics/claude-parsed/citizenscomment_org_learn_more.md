# Learn More About the Notice and Comment Process (Citizens Comment)

## Overview
Federal agencies must follow the "Notice and Comment" procedure (established in the Administrative Procedure Act over 70 years ago) for creating, changing, or eliminating regulations. The process requires agencies to "publish a 'Notice of Proposed Rulemaking' in the Federal Register, at least 30 days prior to the effective date."

## Key Process Steps
1. **Publication** — agencies must post proposed changes in the Federal Register.
2. **Comment Period** — citizens typically have at least 30 days to submit written feedback.
3. **Agency Response** — the agency publishes "a summary of public comments it has received, along with written responses to relevant points raised."

## Citizen Participation Rights
Most citizens are unaware they can participate; "most people do not follow the publication of Notices in the daily Federal Register" and remain unaware of their commenting rights. Citizens Comment exists to surface this gap and connect citizens to relevant dockets.

## Resources
Regulations.gov (built under the 2002 E-Government Act) is the central online portal for receiving comments. Citizens Comment provides curated alerts and walks users through how to use the platform; it also points to Save EPA's online guide for deeper involvement.
