# SEC Investor Bulletin: Commenting on SEC Rulemaking

## Overview

The SEC's Office of Investor Education and Advocacy published this May 2024 bulletin to guide individual investors on participating in the agency's rulemaking process. The bulletin emphasizes that public input—including simple statements of support or opposition—helps the SEC understand investor concerns.

## Key Phases of Rulemaking

The bulletin describes three main stages:

1. **Concept Release**: The SEC seeks preliminary feedback by outlining topics and presenting potential approaches before formal proposals.

2. **Proposing Release**: The SEC publishes a formal proposal containing the proposed rule text, its purpose, and economic analysis for public comment.

3. **Adopting Release**: After reviewing comments, the SEC issues a final rule with explanations of how feedback influenced the decision.

## Effective Comment Strategies

The bulletin recommends investors:

- Study the proposal and available summaries beforehand
- Describe their investor background and how the rule affects them personally
- Clearly identify specific sections being discussed with supporting reasoning
- Propose alternatives to opposed provisions
- Include factual evidence when available

## Submission Process

Comments are submitted through online forms on SEC.gov's Rulemaking Activity page. The bulletin cautions that all comments become public, so investors should avoid personal identifying information.

## Important Note on Transparency

"Comments received through this process are considered in the rulemaking" and subsequently posted publicly. Duplicate submissions result in only the first being published with a tally of repetitions.
