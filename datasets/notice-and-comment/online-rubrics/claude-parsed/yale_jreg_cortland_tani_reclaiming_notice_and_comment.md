# Reclaiming Notice and Comment (Cortland & Tani, Yale J. on Reg. - Notice & Comment Blog)

## Strategic Purpose
Comments should be crafted with awareness of audience. While administrators may dismiss them, courts reviewing agency decisions examine comment records carefully. "Comments that, to one decision maker, demonstrate ignorance might to another register as informed evidence."

## Practical Tips
Effective comments should be grounded in real-world expertise and lived experience. The piece highlights the value of submissions that draw on the commenter's domain knowledge — counselors, home health aides, advocates — about how a rule will operate on the ground.

## Mobilizing Grassroots Expertise
Organize community members who possess practical knowledge of policy areas. Collective commenting "claims and affirms this expertise" among populations often excluded from formal political processes. The act of commenting itself is constitutive of standing as a knowledgeable participant.

## Creating an Evidentiary Record
Comments serve as historical documentation of policy impacts. Personal testimonies about concrete hardships — "unpredictable earnings, inaccessible public transportation, unaffordable child care" — create a public archive demonstrating real consequences of agency choices.

## Key Insight
The immediate goal isn't always persuading current decision-makers; it can be building a judicial record and historical witness to support legal challenge later. A comment that fails politically can still succeed legally if it preserves a precise factual record the agency must explain away on review.
