# Launching DearFCC: The Best Way to Submit Comments to the FCC (EFF Deeplinks, 2017)

## Why Personal Comments Matter
Original, individual voices carry weight. Over a million people used DearFCC to speak out in 2014, demonstrating how personal testimonies accumulate into significant public pressure on regulators.

## Key Structuring Tips
The DearFCC tool helps users craft a unique comment using custom-generated text and submit a personal comment to the official docket with two clicks. Effective comments should be:
- **Personalized rather than generic templates**
- Address how proposed changes would specifically affect you
- Submitted through the official FCC docket
- Connected to broader democratic values

## What to Focus On
Tie comments to concrete concerns about how ISP throttling or blocking would harm your community's access to information and services. Avoid abstract arguments — the FCC has heard those. Real-world stakes are what differentiate signal from noise.

## Net Neutrality Framing
"Net neutrality — the right to access all Internet content freely without your Internet provider slowing down or even blocking content — is fundamental to our democracy." Pair the policy frame with personal stakes.
