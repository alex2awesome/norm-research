# NLRB Rulemaking and Commenting Guide

## How to Submit Comments

Public comments should be submitted either:
- Electronically to **regulations.gov**
- By mail or hand-delivery to: Roxanne L. Rothschild, Executive Secretary, National Labor Relations Board, 1015 Half Street S.E., Washington, D.C. 20570-0001

## Important Deadlines

Reply comments should be limited to replying to comments previously filed by other parties. **No late comments will be accepted.** Requests for extensions of time will be granted only for good cause shown.

## Effective NLRB Comments

For National Labor Relations Act (NLRA) rules:
- Cite specific NLRA sections (29 USC §§ 151-169)
- Reference 29 CFR provisions being amended
- Engage with Board precedent (citing case names and citations)
- Cite federal court of appeals cases reviewing Board decisions
- Provide industry-specific data on union representation, collective bargaining
- Address impact on different employer types (small business, healthcare, educational, federal contractors)
- Reference Bureau of Labor Statistics union data
- Engage with right-to-work law interactions
- Address gig economy / platform worker NLRA coverage
- Cite the Taft-Hartley Act and Landrum-Griffin Act provisions

## Common NLRB Rule Topics

- Election procedures (representation petition handling, voter eligibility)
- Joint employer standard
- Independent contractor status under NLRA
- Property access rights for organizing
- Union dues collection
- Confidentiality and trade secret protection in elections
- Mail vs. in-person ballot procedures
- Bargaining unit determination
- Collective bargaining duty
- Unfair labor practice charges

## NLRB-Specific Considerations

- The Board oscillates between administrations - effective comments engage with both prior precedent and current Board's stated rationale
- Three-member quorum requirements affect rule timing
- Reply comment opportunity is unique - rebut other commenters' factual claims
- Election rules have particular fact-intensive empirical implications
- Board panel composition can affect interpretation - frame for both sides
