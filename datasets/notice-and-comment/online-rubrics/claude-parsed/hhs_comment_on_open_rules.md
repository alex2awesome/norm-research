# HHS Guide on Commenting on Open Rules

## Tips for Effective Comments

The most helpful comments clearly communicate and support your position, whether you are for or against the proposed action.

### Six Key Recommendations

1. **Plan Ahead**: The comment period closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the date comments are due, and planning ahead helps to ensure that you submit your comments well before the deadline.

2. **Add Detail**: Constructive, detailed comments (whether positive or negative) are the most helpful.

3. **Identify the Issues**: Clearly identify which issues you are commenting on within the regulatory action, and provide the page number, column, and/or paragraph from the Federal Register if you are commenting on a particular word, phrase, or sentence.

4. **Provide Evidence and Alternatives**: If you disagree with a proposed action, suggest an alternative (including not regulating at all) and include an explanation of how the alternative might meet the same objective or be more effective, as evidence-based information is particularly helpful.

5. **Contact the Agency**: If you have questions or do not understand a part of the regulatory document, reach out to the agency contact person listed on the document before submitting your comment.

6. **You Don't Have to Comment on Everything**: You do not have to comment on every issue in a rule. You may select the issues on which you wish to comment or provide a general comment.

## Where to Submit Comments

You can provide your comments by going to the federal government's regulations website (Regulations.gov).

## Focus on Specific Requests

The Department often requests comments on specific parts of proposed rules. This may be a helpful place to focus your comments.

## CDC-Specific

You can email CDCRegulations@cdc.gov with questions, comments, or suggestions on how to improve CDC's regulations.
