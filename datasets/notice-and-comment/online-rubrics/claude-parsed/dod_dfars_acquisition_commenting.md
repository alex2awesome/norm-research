# DOD DFARS / Defense Acquisition Rulemaking Commenting

## How to Submit Comments

Comments on proposed rules should be submitted in writing to the address shown in the Federal Register notice on or before the specified comment deadline. Comments should be submitted identified by the specific DFARS Case number.

## Comment Periods

DOD generally requires proposed rules to provide not less than a 30-day public comment period.

## Communication Methods

DOD has identified multiple communication techniques for public and industry participation:
- Web-based email account for the public and industry officials to ask questions, recommend changes, or comment on DFARS rules
- Advanced Notices of Proposed Rulemaking used to receive early public input
- Identifying DOD points of contacts in Federal Register notices
- Public meetings

## Effective DFARS Comments

For defense acquisition rules, effective comments:
- Cite specific DFARS sections and subsections
- Reference Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) parallel provisions
- Address contractor implementation costs
- Distinguish small business and large prime contractor impacts
- Engage with security clearance and CMMC requirements
- Cite Defense Science Board, NIST, and other technical guidance
- Address supply chain risk management dimensions
- Reference international supplier impacts (CFIUS, TAA, BAA)
- Engage with cybersecurity and IP protection requirements
- Provide cost data on compliance with new requirements
- Address Buy American Act implications

## Common DFARS Topics for Comment

- Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC)
- Sourcing restrictions (Section 889 prohibitions)
- Cost accounting standards
- Audit requirements
- Conflict mineral disclosures
- Anti-counterfeit electronic part requirements
- Past performance reporting
