# Boundless Guide: Submitting Public Comments to Federal Agencies

## What is a Public Comment?

A public comment is feedback submitted to a federal agency regarding proposed rules or regulations. The Administrative Procedures Act of 1946 requires agencies to keep the public informed and allows citizens to participate in rule-making beyond voting.

## Who Can Submit?

"Any person, groups, organizations — including non-profit organizations and lobbying groups — or other federal agency" may submit comments, anonymously or through a representative.

## The Process Timeline

- **60-day comment period** follows publication (closes 7:00 PM ET for written, 11:59 PM ET for digital submissions)
- **30-day reply period** follows, during which agencies must respond to comments
- Agencies must address all public comments in final regulations, explaining whether each was incorporated or out of scope

## How to Submit

Four primary methods exist:

1. **Regulations.gov** - Visit www.regulations.gov, search your docket, click "Comment," and submit feedback with up to 20 files (10MB each max)
2. **Agency websites** - Navigate to specific agency sites for comment portals
3. **Mail** - Send to addresses listed in Federal Register notices; must be postmarked by deadline
4. **Public hearings** - Deliver comments in-person at scheduled hearings

## Key Tips for Effective Comments

- **Know the regulation thoroughly** before commenting
- **Use the docket ID number** in your heading (e.g., "FDA-2017-D-6580-4894")
- **Establish your expertise** with relevant credentials or experience
- **Be clear and organized**: define objectives, use precise language, present data-supported arguments
- **Cite sources** when referencing outside materials
- **Address specific questions** posed within the proposed rule

## File Submission Details

Valid file types include: .pdf, .docx, .xlsx, .txt, .jpg, .png, and several others. Each file cannot exceed 10MB.

## Tracking Your Comment

Use "find a comment" at https://www.regulations.gov/help to locate submitted comments using your confirmation number.
