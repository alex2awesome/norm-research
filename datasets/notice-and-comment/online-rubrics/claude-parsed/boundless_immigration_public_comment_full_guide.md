# How to Submit a Public Comment to a Federal Agency (Boundless, full)

## Overview
Public comments are formal feedback mechanisms allowing citizens to influence federal rule-making. Per the APA of 1946, agencies must inform the public and allow participation in regulatory processes.

## Who Can Comment
Any individual, organization, non-profit, lobbying group, or federal agency with legitimate interest — anonymously or through a representative.

## Timeline
- **60-day comment period** (7 PM ET written / 11:59 PM ET digital deadlines)
- **30-day reply period** during which agencies must respond
- Agencies must address all public feedback in final regulations

## Four Submission Methods
1. **Federal Register (regulations.gov)** — most common
2. **Agency websites** — navigate to specific agency portals
3. **By Mail** — postmarked by deadline; include docket/rule ID
4. **In-Person Hearings** — when scheduled

## Effective Comment Tips
- Establish credibility — ground comments in relevant expertise/experience, supported by research and data
- Use clear structure — define objectives, precise language, supporting evidence, citations
- Include identifying information for the regulation — name and docket ID

## Technical Requirements
File uploads limited to 20 attachments, 10MB max each. Accepted formats: .pdf, .docx, .xlsx, .jpg, .png, etc.

## Tracking
Comments can be tracked via regulations.gov's help section under "find a comment."
