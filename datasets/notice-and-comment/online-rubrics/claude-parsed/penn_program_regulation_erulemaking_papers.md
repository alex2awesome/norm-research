## Significance

This collection represents the most complete academic bibliography on rulemaking participation, used as foundational citations for understanding what makes comments effective and how agencies process them. Key authors cited repeatedly:

- **Coglianese** (Penn) - Citizen participation, e-rulemaking
- **Shulman** (UMass) - Mass email campaigns, comment quality
- **Balla** (GW) - Mass/computer-generated comments
- **Cuellar** (Stanford) - Regulatory democracy
- **Yackee** (Wisconsin) - Interest group influence
- **Wagner** (Texas) - Asymmetries in comment process
- **Libgober** (Northwestern) - What comments do
- **Farina** (Cornell) - Regulation Room
