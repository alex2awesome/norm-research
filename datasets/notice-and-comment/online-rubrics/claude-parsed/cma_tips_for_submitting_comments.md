# Center for Medicare Advocacy's Tips for Commenting on Regulations.gov

## Key Starting Points

The CMA emphasizes that "comment periods close at 11:59 eastern time on the date" specified. Commenters should identify relevant credentials and experience. The organization notes that "a single, well-supported comment may carry more weight than a thousand form letters."

## Comment Structure Recommendations

- Begin with background on your organization or expertise
- Organize comments by rule sections with clear headings
- State your position explicitly (support or opposition)
- Include the proposed rule docket number on the first page
- Attach detailed comments rather than using the comment box
- Add a brief conclusion with contact information

## Positioning Your Input

Commenters should clearly articulate their stance. Examples provided show effective language like supporting specific proposals while also identifying concerns about implementation details.

## Using Template Comments

While original submissions are preferred, templates can help frame issues. The CMA advises against simply copying templates verbatim. Instead, personalize them by adding "stories and experiences through your work" or coordinate with other organizations through sign-on letters.

## Additional Strategies

- Comment on specific proposals only—you need not address every element
- Include beneficiary impact stories and unintended consequences
- Provide supporting research and data
- Request agency clarification where needed
- Note that comments become public records
