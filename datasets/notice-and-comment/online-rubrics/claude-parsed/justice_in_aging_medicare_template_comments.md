# Justice in Aging's Medicare Part C & Part D Rule Comments Guide

## Overview
Justice in Aging provides a template and instructions for submitting public comments on CMS's proposed rule affecting Medicare Advantage (Part C) and Prescription Drug Plans (Part D).

## Key Comment Areas

The guide addresses 11 areas where advocates can submit feedback:

1. **SHIP Program Awareness** – Opposing removal of State Health Insurance Assistance Program references from marketing disclaimers
2. **Enrollment Pressure Protections** – Supporting separation between educational and sales activities
3. **Marketing Standards** – Maintaining prohibitions on inaccurate, misleading, and superlative advertising language
4. **Language Access** – Preserving notice requirements for interpretation services
5. **D-SNP Care Coordination** – Supporting enhanced coordination for dually eligible individuals
6. **State D-SNP Oversight** – Endorsing improved state monitoring mechanisms
7. **C-SNP/I-SNP Concerns** – Addressing potential gaps in care coordination
8. **Supplemental Benefits Notices** – Opposing removal of mid-year notice requirements
9. **Prior Authorization Transparency** – Supporting continued public reporting
10. **Equitable Access Standards** – Opposing deletions of specific protected group references
11. **Broad RFI** – Providing feedback on integration, provider networks, accessibility

## Submission Strategy

Comments should include specific examples, client stories, and organizational experiences while preserving enrollee privacy.

## Template Comment Structure (Justice in Aging Approach)

For each topic addressed, the template suggests:
- Start with the proposed change being addressed
- State support, opposition, or partial support
- Provide a brief justification (2-4 sentences)
- Include 1-2 specific examples or client stories where possible
- Suggest specific regulatory language amendments
- Reference relevant statutory authority (Social Security Act sections, etc.)
