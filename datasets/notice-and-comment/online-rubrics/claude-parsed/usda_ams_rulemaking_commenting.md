# USDA Guide on Commenting on Proposed Rules

## Where to Submit Comments

Public feedback to notices open for comment is gathered via www.regulations.gov. Comments are more often submitted to an agency through www.regulations.gov.

## What to Include

Please include your full name and mailing address (street, city, state and zip code) with your comments.

## Comment Period and Deadlines

All notices, except for final rules, are open for public comment until the deadline stated within the notice. Comment periods include weekends and holidays – if a 60-day period closes on Saturday, Monday submissions are untimely.

## How Comments Impact the Final Rule

The notice-and-comment process enables anyone to submit a comment on any part of a proposed rule, and an agency must base its conclusions on the rulemaking record, consisting of the comments, scientific data, expert opinions, and facts accumulated during the pre-rule and proposed rule stages. If the rulemaking record contains persuasive new data or sound policy arguments, the agency may decide to terminate the rulemaking, or the agency may decide to continue the rulemaking but change aspects of the rule to reflect these new issues.
