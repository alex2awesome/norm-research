# NACHC Comment Letters and Templates

## Overview

NACHC (National Association of Community Health Centers) provides updates and tools for submitting comments on proposed health center regulations, including customizable template letters to enable organizations to submit their own unique comments.

## Resources Available

When comment opportunities become available, NACHC's team shares customizable comment letter templates to ensure health centers, PCAs (Primary Care Associations), and HCCNs (Health Center Controlled Networks) voices are heard.

## Recent Comment Letter Examples

- CY27 Notice of Benefit and Payment Parameters
- Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act (PRWORA)
- Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) Teleprescribing of Controlled Substances
- Health Center Program Policy Guidance on Reentry of Justice Involved Individuals

## Effective Health Center Comments

For Section 330 health center rules, effective comments:
- Identify your health center type (CHC, MHC, HCH, PHPC, PCA, HCCN)
- Cite specific Section 330 statute provisions and Compliance Manual
- Provide patient population data (numbers served, demographics, conditions)
- Address rural/urban access dimensions
- Engage with FQHC reimbursement methodologies
- Cite UDS (Uniform Data System) outcomes data
- Address sliding fee scale and underinsured/uninsured populations
- Reference HRSA program guidance and program assistance letters
- Engage with 340B Drug Pricing Program implications
- Address workforce shortage and recruitment challenges
- Provide Medicaid managed care interactions
- Engage with Section 1115 waiver implications
- Cite state Medicaid program differences

## Template Customization

NACHC templates leave space for:
- Specific health center identification
- Local population data
- State-specific Medicaid program details
- Particular service line impacts
- Workforce considerations
- Quality outcomes data
