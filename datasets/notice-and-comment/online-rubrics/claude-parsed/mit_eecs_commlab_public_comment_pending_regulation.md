# MIT EECS Communication Lab: Public Comment on Pending Regulation Guide

## Purpose
Public comments allow individuals to provide input on federal agency regulations. A single well-written, evidence-backed comment can outweigh numerous poorly constructed form letters and influence final rulings.

## Criteria for Success
1. "Read and understand the proposed regulation"
2. Address any specific questions in the regulatory prompt
3. "Establish your credentials by stating who you are and why the proposed regulation is relevant to you"
4. "Provide concrete suggestions and changes to the proposed regulation"
5. "Support all claims and arguments with evidence"

## Recommended Structure

**Introduction:** Establish credentials (identity, expertise, organizational affiliation, personal relevance)

**Overview:** Summarize major issues, principal arguments, and specific recommendations

**Major Concerns:** Detail arguments with supporting evidence (organize by regulatory questions, concerns, or alternative solutions with pros/cons)

**Conclusion:** Re-summarize recommendations (skimmers may read only this section)

## Key Skills & Tips

**Understand the Document:** Read thoroughly before writing; contact agency staff if clarification needed

**Establish Credibility:** State relevant professional experience and explain personal investment in the regulation

**Make Specific Recommendations:** Provide concrete alternatives with data support; refute contradictory viewpoints with evidence; explain assumptions

**Analyze Your Audience:** Address the specific agency/official; avoid technical jargon; focus on real-world impacts (economic effects, health outcomes); assume non-scientific background; remember comments may become public
