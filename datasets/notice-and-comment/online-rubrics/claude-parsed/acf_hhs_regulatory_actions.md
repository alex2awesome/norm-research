# HHS Administration for Children and Families: Commenting on Proposed Rules

## How to Submit Comments

1. **Find the Notice**: The Children's Bureau maintains a page containing both regulations and notices for public comment at acf.gov/cb/laws-policies/federal-laws/regulatory-actions

2. **Use Regulations.gov**: The docket on https://www.regulations.gov will include a plain language summary of the NPRM as required by 5 U.S.C.

3. **Follow Comment Deadlines**: Proposed rules are typically open for a 60-day public comment period

4. **Specific Areas for Comment**: While ACF welcomes comment on any topic related to the proposed rule, there are often key areas within the NPRM where ACF is specifically requesting input from commenters

## Effective ACF Comments

For child welfare, foster care, adoption, child care, refugee resettlement rules:
- Identify experience with affected populations (foster youth, providers, caregivers)
- Cite specific Title IV-B/IV-E, CCDF, or other authorizing statutes
- Reference Adoption and Safe Families Act, Multi-Ethnic Placement Act, ICWA
- Provide state-level program impact data
- Address tribal consultation for Indian Child Welfare Act-related rules
- Engage with Family First Prevention Services Act framework
- Cite federal IM (Information Memorandum) and PI (Program Instruction) prior guidance
- Reference Children's Bureau performance metrics (CFSR, AFCARS)
- Provide data on outcomes (permanency, safety, well-being)
- Engage with State plan/preprint requirements where applicable
