# George Polk Awards — Criteria for Distinguished Journalism

The George Polk Awards (named after a CBS reporter killed in Greece in 1948) honor reporters individually rather than news organizations.

## Selection Criteria

Awards focus on "intrepid, bold, and influential work of reporters." Place a premium on:
- **Investigative work** — original, resourceful, thought-provoking
- **Resourcefulness** — pursuit of stories despite difficulty
- **Courage in journalism** — willingness to face danger or pushback
- **Originality** — first to surface a story
- **Impact / influence** — visible effect on policy or public discourse

## Categories
- Foreign Reporting
- National Reporting
- Local Reporting
- Business Reporting
- Environmental Reporting
- Photojournalism
- Science Reporting
- Political Reporting
- International Reporting
- Podcast / Audio Reporting

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Holding power accountable
- Exposing wrongdoing or hidden truth
- Distinctive reporter voice / individual achievement
- Persistence through obstacles
- Stories that produce change
