# Apple News — Editorial Curation Criteria

Apple News uses a hybrid model: human editors curate "Top Stories" while algorithms drive "Trending" and "For You" sections.

## Top Stories (Human Curation) — Criteria

- **Originality** — Original reporting, not aggregation
- **Factuality** — No misinformation
- **Free of bias / sensationalism** — Avoid one-sided framing
- **Established publication** — Strong track record
- **High-quality journalism** — Editorial craft
- **Eligible publishers** — Recognized brands

## Disqualifying Factors
- Hate speech
- Discrimination
- Violation of Apple policies
- Sensational or misleading headlines

## Trending (Algorithmic) — Signals
- Click volume
- Read-through rates
- Social engagement
- Recency

## For You (Personalized) — Signals
- User-followed topics
- User-followed channels
- Read history
- Topic affinity

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Brand reputation as gatekeeper
- Editorial preference for original reporting
- Algorithmic preference for engagement
- Diversity of sources (Apple editors known for source diversity)
- Avoidance of clickbait
- Topic balance across sections
- Mobile-friendly format
- Visual quality

## Note
Research has shown Apple News human editors choose sources more evenly than algorithms; they prefer source diversity and contextual quality.
