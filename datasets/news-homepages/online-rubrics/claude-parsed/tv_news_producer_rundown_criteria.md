# TV News Producer — Rundown / Story Selection Criteria

How TV news producers decide what runs in a newscast and in what order.

## TV News Judgment Criteria (7)

1. **Timeliness** — Recency, breaking developments
2. **Proximity** — Local relevance
3. **Prominence** — Notable people involved
4. **Impact** — Number affected
5. **Conflict** — Tension, dispute
6. **Novelty** — Unusual, surprising
7. **Visuals** — Strong video available (TV-distinctive)

## The Rundown Document

A chronological list of every newscast element:
- Story slug
- Format (package, anchor read, live shot, interview, VO, VOSOT)
- Duration
- Anchor / reporter assignment
- Graphics / video associated
- Tease / promo placement

## Story Ordering Logic

- **Lead story** = highest news value
- **Group like topics** (crime cluster, health cluster) but
- **Avoid 5+ minutes of one topic**
- **A-block** = top stories, hardest news
- **B-block** = developing, important but lower priority
- **C-block** = lighter, kicker
- **Hard-soft alternation** to retain audience

## Visual Newsworthiness for TV (Implicit)

- **Live shot capability** — boost news value
- **Compelling video** — fire, crash, protest
- **Newsmaker on camera** — interview, quote
- **Action footage** — vs. static building shots
- **Aerial / drone** — large-scale events
- **Eyewitness footage** — authenticity
- **Tease-ability** — promotable visual

## Format Decisions Affect Newsworthiness

- **Package** (1:30) — top stories, full reporter narration
- **VOSOT** (~30s with anchor + soundbite) — supplemental
- **VO** (~20s with anchor) — light coverage
- **Anchor read** — least visual demand
- **Live shot** — story still developing

## Real-Time Adjustments

- Producer ready to scrap rundown for breaking news
- Newscast typically finalized 1-2 hours before air
- "Float" stories ready to fill if A-block thin

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for TV

- **Visual potential** — primary differentiator from print
- **Audio quality** — interviewable, soundbite-worthy
- **Length-appropriateness** — fits 30s-1:30 windows
- **Live capability** — can we be there now?
- **Newsmaker availability** — can we get an interview?
- **Pace/variety contribution** — fits flow of show
- **Graphics-friendly** — can we make a chart?
