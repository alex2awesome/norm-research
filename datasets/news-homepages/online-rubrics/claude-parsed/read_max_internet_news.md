# Read Max (Max Read) — Newsworthiness in the AI / Crypto / Internet Era

Max Read's newsletter offers a self-aware framework for what to take seriously vs. what to deflate.

## Read's Newsworthiness Filters
- **Real product changes** vs. marketing announcements
- **Adoption data** vs. founder claims
- **Material consequences** vs. discourse-only events
- **Bubble pattern recognition** — recurring hype cycles flagged
- **Power and money flows** — who benefits from a narrative
- **Long-tail vs. peak-hype timing** — when to write the deflation

## Read's Tests
- "Is anyone actually using this?"
- "Whose investment thesis is this story serving?"
- "Is the breathlessness warranted by the substance?"
- "What would a sophisticated reader 5 years from now find embarrassing about how this is being covered?"

## Demoted Categories
- AGI / "everything will change" essays without empirical content
- Founder / VC narrative pieces
- Token / coin price stories without structural significance
- Influencer takes recycled

## Elevated Categories
- Adoption-rate measurement
- Energy / labor / supply chain accountings of new tech
- Critical histories of similar past hypes
- Cultural reception / vernacular use of new tools
- Legal / regulatory inflections

## Note
Read Max embodies skeptical-but-curious newsworthiness: take the phenomenon seriously enough to interrogate it without inflating it.
