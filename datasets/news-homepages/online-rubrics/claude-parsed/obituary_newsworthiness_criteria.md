# Obituaries — Who Gets a Newspaper Obituary?

A specialized newsworthiness rubric for whose death qualifies as news.

## Two Distinct Forms

1. **Death notices** — Paid family announcements (Births/Marriages/Deaths section)
2. **Obituaries (proper)** — Editorial decision; tribute to lives of public significance

## Categories of Obituary Subjects

### Local Newspaper Obituaries (Paid)
- Any local resident upon death (if family pays)
- Death notices (briefer; sometimes free as "courtesy")

### Editorial Obituaries (Major Newspapers)

NYT-style assignment criteria (advance obits):
- **Lives long enough** — significant duration
- **Accomplished enough** — public achievement
- **Complex enough** — narrative-rich biography

## Newsworthiness Criteria for Editorial Obit

1. **Public achievement** — Career significance
2. **Influence** — Impact on field/society
3. **Recognized in lifetime** (awards, official roles)
4. **Newsworthy biography** — Compelling life story
5. **Historical witness** — Connection to major events
6. **First / pioneering** in field
7. **Cultural figure** (artist, writer, musician)
8. **Political figure**
9. **Business titan**
10. **Sports legend**
11. **Scientist / inventor**
12. **Activist / reformer**
13. **Survivor of historic event**
14. **Local notable** (in local papers)

## Format Conventions

- Lead with most significant achievement
- Cause of death (if known)
- Survivor list
- Career arc
- Anecdotes / quotes
- Family background
- Distinguishing features (eccentricities, signature work)

## NYT Approach

- Maintains pool of advance obits for prominent figures
- Updated periodically while subject is alive
- Released within hours of confirmed death
- Smaller pool of "feature" obits — overlooked lives, marginalized communities

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Lifetime impact** vs. momentary celebrity
- **Substantive contribution** vs. fame-only
- **Local significance** in local press
- **Historical context** — did they shape an era?
- **Storytelling potential** — biography rich enough?
- **Survivor / family willingness** to share
- **Distinct voice / personality**
- **Inflection point** in field

## "Overlooked" Project (NYT)
Belated obituaries for women, people of color, LGBTQ figures historically excluded — a recognition that traditional obit criteria were biased.
