# Food Journalism — Restaurant Review Criteria

Standards from the Association of Food Journalists (AFJ) and major newspaper critics. Distinct rubric for food/restaurant coverage.

## Restaurant Review Process Standards

### Anonymity
- Reviews conducted anonymously when possible
- Reservations under non-reviewer name
- Pay with non-identifying methods
- Avoid being recognized

### Multiple Visits
- Two visits recommended; three preferred
- Different days, times
- Service, food, atmosphere vary day-to-day

### Comprehensive Tasting
- Full range of menu (apps to desserts)
- Taste everything ordered
- At minimum: every item mentioned in column

## Star Rating Systems

### Michelin
- ★ — "Very good restaurant"
- ★★ — "Worth a detour"
- ★★★ — "Exceptional cuisine, worth a special journey"

### Newspaper systems (NYT, WaPo)
- 0-4 stars typically
- Combine food, service, atmosphere, value

## Review Evaluation Criteria

1. **Food quality** — flavor, technique, ingredients
2. **Menu** — coherence, originality, range
3. **Service** — professionalism, warmth, knowledge
4. **Atmosphere** — ambiance, design, comfort
5. **Value** — quality relative to cost
6. **Concept** — restaurant's vision and execution
7. **Execution** — consistency, plating, timing
8. **Beverage program** — wine, cocktails, NA
9. **Cultural significance** — what restaurant represents

## What Restaurants Get Reviewed (Newsworthiness)

1. **Major opening** of acclaimed chef
2. **High anticipation** based on pedigree
3. **Hot trend** representation
4. **Major reopening** after revamp
5. **Ethnic cuisine** newly visible
6. **Underrecognized neighborhood** standout
7. **Closing announcement** — final review
8. **Award attention** (James Beard, Michelin)
9. **Restaurant publishing cookbook**
10. **Anniversary** of legendary spot

## Implicit Newsworthiness for Food Stories

- **Chef prominence**
- **Cuisine authenticity / innovation**
- **Cultural moment alignment**
- **Reader-actionable** (dining decision)
- **Visual potential** (food photography)
- **Local economic impact**
- **Industry trend representation**
- **Story behind food** (immigrant chef, family heritage)
- **Sustainability angle**
- **Health/nutrition implications**

## Food Beat Beyond Reviews

- Restaurant industry news (closures, openings, ownership)
- Recipes
- Food trends
- Agricultural / supply chain
- Health / nutrition science
- Food policy / politics
- Personality profiles (chefs)
- Cookbook reviews

## Ethical Standards (AFJ)

- Disclose press meals / comped meals
- Disclose any chef relationships
- Don't review friend's restaurant
- Don't accept gifts
- Distinguish reviews from puff pieces
