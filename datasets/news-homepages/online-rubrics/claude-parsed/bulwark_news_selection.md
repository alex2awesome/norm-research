# The Bulwark — News Selection from a Pro-Democracy Conservative Lens

The Bulwark's stated mission shapes its newsworthiness criteria.

## Front-Page Criteria (Stated and Implicit)
- **Threats to constitutional norms** — especially from the right
- **Authoritarian behavior** — patterns over single events
- **Disinformation tracking** — how false narratives propagate
- **Republican primary dynamics** — internal GOP battles
- **Election integrity** — both administrative and rhetorical attacks
- **Foreign policy stakes** — Ukraine, Taiwan, NATO

## Editorial Tests
- "Does this story matter for the survival of liberal democracy?"
- "Is this normalized behavior that should be denormalized?"
- "Are mainstream outlets pulling punches because of access concerns?"
- "Does this advance the public's understanding of stakes, or trivialize them?"

## What Bulwark Demotes
- Day-to-day legislative process without democratic stakes
- Cultural controversies orthogonal to power
- Centrist horse-race coverage
- Both-sides framing of asymmetric threats

## What Bulwark Elevates
- January 6 / accountability stories
- Federal court rulings on executive power
- Authoritarianism abroad as warning
- Defections / dissent within conservative movement
- Suppressed truths from formerly Republican officials

## Note
The Bulwark exemplifies "stakes-based" newsworthiness — the rubric is filtered through "what threatens democratic continuity."
