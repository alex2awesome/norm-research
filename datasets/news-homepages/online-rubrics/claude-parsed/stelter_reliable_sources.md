# Brian Stelter — Media Industry Newsworthiness Rubric

Stelter's CNN newsletter and Reliable Sources framing.

## Stelter's Daily Selection Criteria
- **Media-industry shifts** affecting how news reaches audiences
- **Cable news executive moves**
- **Press freedom incidents** — both abroad and domestic
- **Disinformation case studies** — especially right-wing media ecosystems
- **Newsroom labor / unionization stories**
- **Journalist safety / detention worldwide**

## Editorial Tests
- "Will this affect tomorrow's news cycle?"
- "Is this a structural shift or a one-off?"
- "Are we obligated to cover this because we depend on it for our credibility?"

## Stories Stelter Elevates
- Layoffs / restructurings
- Newsroom diversity reports
- Public-affairs decisions (FCC, corporate)
- Threats against journalists (especially overseas)
- Exec succession at networks / publishers

## Stories Stelter Demotes
- Talent gossip lacking business implication
- Niche industry minutiae
- Repetitive partisan-media talking points

## Note
Stelter's rubric is industry-insider newsworthiness — what the people who run TV news need to know each morning.
