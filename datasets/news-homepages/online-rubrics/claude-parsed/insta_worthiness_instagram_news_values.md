# Insta-Worthiness — News Values on Instagram

Adapts traditional newsworthiness frameworks for Instagram. Coined the term "insta-worthiness" to describe the criteria that make news successful on the platform.

## Core Argument

Concepts like share, like, comment have become significant for newsworthiness in the digital age. News values must be reformulated for visual-first platforms.

## Insta-Worthiness Components

1. **Reference to elite/famous people** — Major driver of attention
2. **Frequency / Instantaneity** — Immediacy is key (replaces traditional "frequency")
3. **Exclusiveness** — Being first or unique
4. **Interactivity** — Number of comments under a post
5. **Viewing rating** — Video view counts
6. **Visual aesthetic appeal** — Composition, color, framing
7. **Positive content preference** — Instagram audiences prefer general news, human interest, positive stories
8. **Personalization** — Individual stories over institutional
9. **Identity-signaling** — Stories that affirm user values
10. **Share-ability** — Quotable, screenshot-able

## Distinct from Traditional News Values

- Politics underperforms on Instagram (vs. traditional outlets)
- Human interest dominates
- Negative news still gains attention but positive does well
- Visual newsworthiness diverges from textual

## Key Implication

News organizations using Instagram must reframe stories visually and emotionally; traditional inverted-pyramid + headline approach fails.
