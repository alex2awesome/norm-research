# Edward R. Murrow Awards (RTDNA) — Categories and Criteria

Recognize "local and national news stories that uphold the RTDNA Code of Ethics, demonstrate technical expertise and exemplify the importance and impact of journalism as a service to the community."

## Three Pillars
1. Adherence to RTDNA Code of Ethics
2. Technical expertise (production craft)
3. Importance and impact as community service

## Major Categories
- **Overall Excellence** — 40 minutes of past-year coverage plus a full newscast
- **Newscast** — One regularly scheduled newscast
- **Breaking News Coverage** — Up to 20 minutes covering a single unscheduled event
- **Continuing Coverage** — Up to 30 minutes following a major developing story
- **Feature Reporting** — Up to 10 minutes of human-interest or profile (not breaking, not investigative)
- **Investigative Reporting** — Up to 15 minutes of journalistic enterprise on an important issue
- **Hard News** — Single hard news report
- **News Series**
- **News Documentary**
- **Sports Reporting**
- **Use of Video / Audio**
- **Writing**
- **Excellence in Innovation**
- **Excellence in Social Media**
- **Excellence in Diversity, Equity and Inclusion** — bringing awareness to inequality issues
- **Digital category** — Excellence in Web Site / Digital Journalism

## Implicit Newsworthiness Dimensions
- Service to community (impact)
- Technical/craft quality
- Ethical practice
- Innovation in form
- Diversity and inclusion of perspectives
- Persistence (continuing coverage)
- Public significance
