# NPPA Best of Photojournalism — Visual Newsworthiness Criteria

National Press Photographers Association's flagship competition criteria. Combines technical, ethical, and journalistic standards.

## Three-Part Judging

1. **Journalism demonstrated** — The story-telling, news significance (key factor)
2. **Technical excellence** — Exposure, focus, clarity
3. **Composition** — Visual structure, framing

## Ethical Standards (Visual Integrity)

NPPA Code of Ethics requires:
- "Be accurate and comprehensive in the representation of subjects"
- Two areas of accuracy: making the image (no staging) and post-production (no manipulation)

## Implicit Visual Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Community relevance** — "Stories that are relevant to their communities"
- **Help understanding trends and issues** for informed decision-making
- **Bringing communities together** through stories
- **Deeply researched** photographic enterprise
- **Illuminate and resonate**
- **Visual storytelling power** — moment, gesture, emotion
- **Decisive moment** capture
- **Truthfulness / lack of manipulation**
- **Subject dignity**
- **Newsworthiness of subject matter** — same news values apply visually
