# Film Criticism — Movie Review Criteria

Criteria for film reviews drawn from Roger Ebert's influential framework and standard journalism practice.

## Film Reviews vs. Film Criticism

- **Film Review**: General public; published soon after release; describes plot, characters, director; rating/recommendation
- **Film Criticism**: Scholarly; study, interpretation, evaluation; place in cinema history

## Roger Ebert's Three Functions of a Review

1. **Inform the reader** — give some notion of what the movie is about and what it is like
2. **Convey the experience** — not just deliver a verdict; reader could disagree with rating but still make own decision
3. **Be worth reading on its own terms** — not just a footnote to a rating

## Newsworthiness — What Movies Get Reviewed

1. **Theatrical release** in major markets
2. **Festival debut** (Cannes, Sundance, TIFF)
3. **Streaming premiere** of significant project
4. **Major director** behind it
5. **Major star** featured
6. **Award contender**
7. **Cultural moment** alignment
8. **Genre significance**
9. **Independent / international** highlighting
10. **Anniversary screening** of classic
11. **Restoration / 4K release**
12. **Distinctive marketing event**

## Evaluation Criteria

### Story / Narrative
- Comprehensive story arc
- Plausibility within film's logic
- Themes identifiable
- Themes connect with audience
- Pacing fits tone

### Craft
- Direction
- Acting performances
- Cinematography
- Editing rhythm
- Sound design
- Music / score
- Production design
- Costumes
- Visual effects

### Engagement
- Holds attention throughout
- Emotional resonance
- Surprises / subverts expectation
- Memorability

### Cinema Context
- Place in genre
- Place in director's oeuvre
- Place in cultural conversation
- Originality vs. derivative
- References / influences

## Reviewer Practices
- Often watch film more than once
- Avoid spoilers in reviews of new releases
- Disclose biases / preferences
- Match review length to film significance

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Film

- **Cultural significance**
- **Major release** budget/distribution
- **Director / star** prominence
- **Festival prestige**
- **Genre impact**
- **Critical anticipation**
- **Audience interest** (commercial pull)
- **Award potential**
- **Conversation-starting** (controversial, novel)
- **Visual / aesthetic** distinction
