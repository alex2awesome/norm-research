# Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2024 / 2025

YouGov survey of 95,000+ people in 47 countries on news consumption. De facto evidence base for what news audiences want.

## Key Findings on News Consumption

### Platform Shifts
- **Facebook for news declined** 4 percentage points across all countries
- Growth in: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, private messaging apps
- Visual / video-led platforms dominant for younger audiences
- Print: only 10% access news on print (down from 50% in 2013)
- Podcasts: 10% (similar to print)
- AI chatbots: 9% (rising new category)

### Topics — What Audiences Want
- **Audiences feel well-served**: politics, sports
- **Coverage gaps identified**: 
  - **Local news** in many countries
  - **Health news**
  - **Education news**

### Engagement Patterns
- Declining engagement overall
- Stagnating digital subscriptions
- Low trust in news (but variable by country)
- News avoidance rising

### Country Differences
- Nordic countries: high trust, high subscription
- US, UK: low trust, polarized
- Global South: mobile-first, messaging app-centered
- China, India: own platform ecosystems

## Implicit Newsworthiness Findings

### What Drives Engagement
- **Visual / multimedia content** outperforms text
- **Short-form video** dominates younger demographics
- **Personalized algorithm feeds** drive discovery
- **Trusted brands** matter more amid misinformation
- **Niche topics** + **community signals** drive engagement

### What's Underserved (Coverage Gaps)
- Local issues / hyperlocal
- Health practical information
- Education updates
- Constructive / solutions journalism
- Underrepresented communities

### What Drives News Avoidance
- Negativity overload
- Polarization fatigue
- Repetitive coverage of conflict
- Sense that news doesn't help

## Implications for Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Coverage of underserved topics** = unmet audience need
- **Constructive framing** = audience preference
- **Visual + short-form** = format-driven newsworthiness
- **Trust signals** = critical distinguisher
- **Local depth** = high audience value, low supply
