# Sparks / Esser — Tabloidization and News Values

Foundational works on "tabloidization" of mainstream press. Frank Esser (1999) and Colin Sparks (multiple) argue tabloid news values are spreading.

## Tabloidization Defined

The shift toward tabloid-style news values in mainstream media:
- Personalization
- Sensationalism
- Trivialization
- Emotional framing
- Visual prominence
- Entertainment over information
- Less politics, more lifestyle
- Shorter stories

## Esser's 3-Level Model

Tabloidization happens at three levels:
1. **News selection** — which stories chosen
2. **News writing** — how stories told
3. **News presentation** — visual/format

## Drivers (per Sparks)

1. **Profit orientation** of media organizations
2. **Audience competition** for declining attention
3. **Advertising-driven editorial** decisions
4. **Decline of public-service ethos**
5. **Cross-pressures from digital platforms**
6. **Cost reduction** (cheap to do)

## Tabloid News Values (per scholarship)

1. **Personalization** — events through individuals
2. **Sensationalism** — emotional shock
3. **Drama** — narrative tension
4. **Visualization** — image dominance
5. **Brevity** — short attention spans
6. **Entertainment** — lighter touch
7. **Celebrity** — famous-people focus
8. **Sex / scandal**
9. **Crime spectacle**
10. **Sports** — entertainment dimension
11. **Lifestyle / consumer** content
12. **Local / parochial** focus

## Quality / Broadsheet News Values (Contrasted)

1. **Public policy** depth
2. **Institutional accountability**
3. **Foreign affairs**
4. **Analytical complexity**
5. **Long-form** investigations
6. **Source diversity**
7. **Issue framing** over personality
8. **Numerical literacy** in reporting

## Implications

- Democratic implications debated:
  - Critical view: tabloidization undermines informed citizenship
  - Defenders: tabloid values reach wider audience, can mobilize publics
- "Dual convergence" — tabloids and broadsheets meeting in middle
- Digital platforms accelerate tabloidization

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria from Tabloidization Trend

- **Personalizable** — can it be told through individual?
- **Visualizable** — strong image potential
- **Brief** — short attention span friendly
- **Emotional hook** — feeling before fact
- **Entertainment value**
- **Celebrity attached**
- **Scandalous angle**
