# Welbers & van Atteveldt — News Selection in the Digital Age

Journalism, 2016. Empirical study of how online audience clicks affect news selection in 5 Dutch national newspapers, using cross-lagged analysis over 6 months.

## Core Finding

**Audience clicks affect news selection.** Storylines of most-viewed articles were more likely to receive attention in subsequent reporting.

## The Tension Identified

Editors interviewed considered the **use of audience metrics for news selection to conflict with professional norms**. So:
- Behavior shows audience-driven selection
- Stated norms reject this
- Discrepancy between practice and ideal

## Methodology

- Mixed methods: content analysis + interviews
- 5 Dutch national newspapers (print + online)
- Cross-lagged panel design over 6 months
- Tracked which articles got clicks → did similar stories follow?

## News Values Identified

### Traditional (Galtung-Ruge derived)
- Negativity
- Conflict
- Eliteness
- Personalization
- Magnitude
- Geographic proximity
- Cultural proximity
- Timeliness

### Digital-Era Additions
- **Shareability** — emerging news value
- **Entertainment** — most common value among shared stories
- "Stories shared by online readers because they are fun"

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria from Findings

- **Predicted clickability** as new news value
- **Predicted shareability**
- **Entertainment value** rising in importance
- **Audience-driven topic prioritization**
- **Pile-on dynamics** — clicked stories spawn follow-ups
- **Tension** between metrics and norms still unresolved

## Implications for Newsworthiness Theory

- Newsworthiness is increasingly **audience-defined** in practice
- Editors maintain professional norm of editorial gatekeeping
- But behavior shows audience signals are weighted
- Hybrid newsworthiness: editorial values + click signals

## Citation
Welbers, K., van Atteveldt, W., Kleinnijenhuis, J., Ruigrok, N., & Schaper, J. (2016). News Selection Criteria in the Digital Age: Professional Norms versus Online Audience Metrics. Journalism, 17(8), 1037-1053.
