# Knight Commission on Trust, Media and Democracy

A panel of thinkers and practitioners examining the collapse in trust in media and democratic institutions, established 2017 with the Aspen Institute.

## Recommendations for Newsworthiness / Trust

### For News Organizations
1. **Transparency in development** — How a story was reported
2. **Transparency in writing** — Why this story, what's the scope
3. **Transparency in correction** — Acknowledging mistakes openly
4. **Disclosure of identity** — Who runs the organization
5. **Disclosure of funding**
6. **Disclosure of mission/values**
7. **Original reporting valued** over aggregation
8. **Inclusive reporting** of diverse communities
9. **Audience engagement** as core practice
10. **Local presence** as quality marker

### For Distributors (Platforms)
1. Responsible determination of what surfaces
2. Algorithm transparency
3. Quality signals over engagement-only signals
4. Combat misinformation actively
5. Promote authentic local news

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- **Trust-building practices** as story criterion
- **Transparency** about process
- **Inclusivity** of who/what is covered
- **Originality** vs. aggregation
- **Local presence** as quality
- **Public service mission**
- **Verifiability**
- **Ethical conduct**
- **Audience accountability**

## Investments
Knight invests in:
- AI-assisted reporting tools
- Visual / data journalism
- Workflow tools
- Audience engagement
- Local news capacity
