# Public Broadcaster Editorial Values — ABC, CBC, NHK

Editorial frameworks of major non-Anglo-American public broadcasters. International perspectives on newsworthiness criteria.

## Common PSB Principles

1. **Non-partisan** operation
2. **Public interest mandate** — serve citizens, not advertisers
3. **Editorial independence** from government (despite government funding)
4. **Balance and impartiality**
5. **Accuracy and integrity**
6. **Comprehensive coverage** — domestic and international
7. **Diversity** — voices, regions, perspectives
8. **Accountability** to audiences

## ABC (Australia) — Editorial Values

The Australian Broadcasting Corporation:
- Politically independent statutory organization
- Government-appointed board
- Code of Practice covering accuracy, impartiality, fair dealing, privacy
- 4 Editorial Standards: independence, integrity, responsiveness, public interest
- Required to provide: news, current affairs, entertainment, drama, comedy, sport, music, arts, education

## CBC (Canada) — Journalistic Standards & Practices

The CBC Journalistic Policy Handbook:
- "Duty to provide consistent, high-quality information"
- "Upon which all Canadians may rely"
- Pillars: accuracy, fairness, balance, impartiality, integrity
- Specific guidance on:
  - Conflict of interest
  - Anonymous sources
  - Hidden cameras
  - Children
  - Reconstructions
  - Political coverage
- Public hearings on standards

## NHK (Japan) — Domestic Broadcasting Law

NHK Editorial Guidelines:
- Truthful and accurate reporting
- Fair and impartial
- Comprehensive coverage including local Japan
- Free of bias
- Special attention to wartime memory
- International broadcasting through NHK World

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for PSBs

- **National significance** — what matters to country
- **Diverse regional coverage** (not metropolitan only)
- **International events with national relevance**
- **Cultural / educational value**
- **Underserved community coverage**
- **Comprehensive (not narrow)** coverage
- **Less commercial pressure** — can pursue less-popular but important
- **Democratic deliberation** support

## Distinct from Commercial Broadcasters

- Don't optimize for clicks/ratings alone
- Mandate to cover unprofitable but important
- Investment in foreign bureaus
- Investment in cultural/educational programming
- Emphasis on minority languages (Welsh BBC, Inuktitut CBC, Ainu NHK)
