# PR Pitch / Press Release — Newsworthiness Criteria

How PR practitioners evaluate whether content is "newsworthy" enough for media pitch. Inverse view of editorial newsworthiness.

## Core PR Newsworthiness Test

A story is newsworthy if it has:
- **Impact** — affects many people
- **Timeliness** — relevant right now
- **Prominence** — notable people/orgs
- **Proximity** — local relevance
- **Oddity** — unusual angle

## When to Pitch Media (PR Calendar Triggers)

1. **Product / service launch** — focus on what's enabled
2. **Mergers & acquisitions**
3. **Awards & endorsements** (especially industry-respected)
4. **New executive hires** (especially senior)
5. **Expansion / new offices**
6. **Layoffs / restructuring** (proactive)
7. **Anniversary milestones** (10th, 25th, 50th, 100th)
8. **New research / data** company commissioned
9. **Partnership announcements**
10. **Charity / philanthropy**
11. **Event sponsorship / hosting**
12. **Crisis response** (limited)
13. **Trending topic commentary** (expert source offer)
14. **Survey results**

## What Makes a Pitch Effective

### Story Has
- **Genuine news value** (not press release)
- **Exclusive data** journalists can't get elsewhere
- **Expert source** explains things clearly
- **Timely angle** matching journalist's beat
- **Compelling story arc**
- **Visual assets** (photos, video, graphics)

### Pitch Format
- Subject line: clear, specific, news-y
- Brief body (under 200 words)
- Why this journalist specifically
- Why this story now
- Offer interview / data / visuals
- Embargo proposal if appropriate

## Implicit "Newsworthy for Press Release" Criteria

1. **First** in some category (first-ever, first-to-market)
2. **Biggest / largest** in some category
3. **Industry-changing** implication
4. **Counterintuitive finding**
5. **Data-driven angle**
6. **Tied to news cycle**
7. **Tied to seasonal moment**
8. **Tied to legislation / policy**
9. **Tied to celebrity / public figure**
10. **Holiday or anniversary peg**

## What's NOT Newsworthy (Common PR Mistakes)

- Generic announcement without angle
- Internal achievement (org-only relevance)
- Self-congratulatory press release
- Stale news (>48 hours)
- Untimely seasonal pitch
- No data or evidence
- No human story
- All-staff promotion of leadership
- Vague "leading" or "innovative"
- Pre-announcement of pre-announcement

## Evolution: Content Marketing as News

PR-driven content increasingly fills news space:
- Branded content
- Sponsored features
- "Provided by" articles
- Influencer-amplified pitches
