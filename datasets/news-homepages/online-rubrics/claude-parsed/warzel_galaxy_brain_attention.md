# Galaxy Brain (Charlie Warzel) — Newsworthiness in the Attention Economy

Warzel's recurring theme: the dissonance between what algorithms surface and what is genuinely important.

## Warzel's Newsworthiness Heuristics
- **Signal vs. noise** — does the story tell us something durable or is it ephemeral discourse foam?
- **Real-world stakes** — not just "online" stakes (what happens off-platform?)
- **Information vs. content** — is there new information, or just commentary on commentary?
- **Asymmetry of consequence** — small online events with large offline impact get prioritized
- **Manufactured vs. organic** — flag stories whose "virality" was engineered

## What Warzel Argues Should NOT Drive A1
- Trending topics on X / TikTok (often manipulated)
- Outrage cycles without underlying news
- "Everyone's talking about it" (audience-generated agenda)
- Discourse about discourse
- Tech executive proclamations as inherent news
- AI hype cycles disconnected from product reality

## Tests Warzel Applies
- **The week-from-now test**: will anyone remember this story in seven days?
- **The off-platform test**: does this story exist outside the platform that surfaced it?
- **The new-information test**: is there reporting, or just reaction?
- **The power-mapping test**: who benefits if this becomes the story?

## Note
Warzel's rubric is essentially anti-virality: good news judgment requires resisting platform-amplified salience.
