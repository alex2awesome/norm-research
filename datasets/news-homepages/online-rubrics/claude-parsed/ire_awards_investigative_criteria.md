# IRE Awards — Investigative Reporting Criteria

The IRE definition of investigative reporting and the criteria for excellence in the genre.

## IRE Definition of Investigative Reporting

Entries are judged on the basis of the IRE definition of investigative reporting, which includes:

1. **Substantially the product of the reporter's own initiative and effort** — not handed to the journalist by a source
2. **Uncovering facts** that someone or some agency may have tried to keep from public scrutiny
3. **Issues of public importance** to readers/viewers/listeners
4. **Enterprise** — going beyond the press release / official announcement
5. **Excellence in documentation** — paper trails, records
6. **Excellence in data** — quantitative analysis where appropriate
7. **Storytelling** — narrative that engages readers
8. **Production** — multimedia and presentation craft
9. **Online presentation** — digital experience
10. **Reader/viewer involvement** — engagement, action, response

## Process
- Screeners review each category
- IRE Awards Committee (IRE Board members and elected/appointed investigative journalists)
- Strict conflict-of-interest rules

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Hidden vs. public knowledge — uncovering what was concealed
- Public importance threshold
- Original reporting (not re-aggregated)
- Documentation/evidence quality
- Storytelling craft
- Quantitative rigor
- Public impact / response generated
