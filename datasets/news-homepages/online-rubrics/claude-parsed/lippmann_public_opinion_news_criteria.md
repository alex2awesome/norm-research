# Lippmann — Public Opinion (1922) — News and Newsworthiness

Foundational text on news, public opinion, and democracy. Identifies the structural conditions that make events newsworthy.

## Lippmann's Definition of News

"News is precise in proportion to the precision with which the event is recorded. Unless the event is capable of being **named, measured, given shape, and made specific**, it either fails to take on the character of news, or it is subject to the accidents and prejudices of observation."

## Four Conditions for Newsworthiness (Lippmann)

1. **Nameable** — Has a clear identifier
2. **Measurable** — Quantifiable in some dimension
3. **Given shape** — Has discernible boundaries
4. **Specific** — Concrete, not vague

## The Searchlight Metaphor

"Like the beam of a searchlight that moves restlessly about, bringing one episode and then another out of darkness into vision."
- Journalism is selective, not comprehensive
- Stories illuminated when they meet certain conditions
- Most events stay in darkness

## Implicit News Selection Criteria

- **Definite events** preferred over slow processes
- **Crisis episodes** over chronic conditions
- **Specific persons** over abstract groups
- **Measurable outcomes** over qualitative changes
- **Bounded events** over diffuse trends
- **Visible events** over hidden processes
- **Already-named institutions** (police, courts) easier to cover

## Why Slow / Diffuse / Chronic Issues Underperform

- Climate change (slow)
- Inequality (chronic)
- Cultural shifts (diffuse)
- Public health trends (vs. acute outbreaks)
- All struggle to become "news" by Lippmann's criteria

## Manufacture of Consent

Lippmann argued public opinion can be (and must be) shaped by experts because:
- Public lacks time/expertise to assess complex issues
- News provides only fragmentary information
- "Common interests" require expert analysis to surface

## Implications for Newsworthiness

- Bias toward the **discrete, dated, dramatic event**
- Bias against **structural / systemic** stories
- Episodic framing favored
- "News" ≠ "what's important"
