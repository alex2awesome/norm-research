# Society for Features Journalism — What Makes a Feature Newsworthy

The SFJ supports feature writing as a journalistic genre with its own newsworthiness criteria.

## Three Key Qualities of Excellent Features

1. **Sense of celebration / sharing of journalistic platform** — Lifting up subjects, communities, voices
2. **Sense of urgency** — Why this story now? Stakes that compel attention
3. **Portrait of the human experience** — Universal resonance through specific lives

## Four Essential Ingredients (per SFJ guidance)
- **Character** — Compelling subjects readers care about
- **Action** — Things happening, scenes
- **Setting** — Place that grounds the story
- **Theme** — Larger meaning beyond facts

## Feature Categories
- Features beat writing portfolios (3 stories on one specialty: arts, fashion, food, health, religion, technology, travel)
- Narrative video features
- Utility features — educate readers about topics affecting daily life
- Profile writing
- Trend pieces

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Features
- Strong central character / subject
- Vivid scene-building
- Emotional truth
- Larger thematic resonance
- Practical utility (in service features)
- Cultural/social significance
- Distinctive voice
