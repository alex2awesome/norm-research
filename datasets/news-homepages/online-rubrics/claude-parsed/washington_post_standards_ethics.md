# The Washington Post — Policies and Standards

The Post's newsroom standards. Foundational for understanding US-establishment newsroom newsworthiness.

## Core Mission
"The highest duty is to its readers and to the truth." Committed to **transparency, innovation and accuracy** in delivering authoritative reporting and commentary.

## Conflict of Interest
- No gifts from news sources
- No free trips
- No preferential treatment sought or accepted
- No active involvement in partisan causes (politics, community, demonstrations)
- Stricter than typical private business norms

## Fairness Standards
- "**No story is fair if it omits facts of major importance or significance.** Fairness includes completeness."
- Disclose information sources to maximum extent
- Right of reply for subjects of negative coverage
- Multiple sources for damaging claims

## Accuracy
- Verify before publishing
- Correct errors promptly
- Avoid speculation framed as fact

## Anonymous Sources
- Sparingly used
- Editor approval required
- Reasoning explained to readers

## Standards Desk (2022)
Dedicated team to update, explain and enforce policies; create new standards.

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Significance** of facts (omitting important facts = unfair)
- **Completeness** of context
- **Source diversity / multi-sourcing**
- **Public significance** over commercial appeal
- **Truth over haste**
- **Independence from sources**
- **Authoritative voice**
- **Innovation in form**
- **Transparency about methods**

## Social Media Conduct
Same values apply: professionalism, empathy, focus on facts.
