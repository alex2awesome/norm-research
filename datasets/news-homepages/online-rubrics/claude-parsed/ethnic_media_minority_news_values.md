# Ethnic / Minority Media — News Values

Distinct newsworthiness criteria for media serving specific ethnic, racial, or immigrant communities.

## Definition

"Media by and for ethnics in a host country with content in ethnic languages" (Yu Shi). Includes both large outlets and small organic community publications.

## Functions of Ethnic Media (Implicit Newsworthiness)

1. **Serve community information needs**
2. **Bridge community to host society**
3. **Counter mainstream invisibility** of group
4. **Preserve cultural identity / language**
5. **Connect diaspora to homeland**
6. **Advocate for community interests**
7. **Make ethnic community visible to civic leaders**

## Distinct News Values

### Community-Centered
- **Community success stories** (entrepreneurs, students, leaders)
- **Cultural events** — festivals, observances
- **Religious news** of community faith traditions
- **Immigration policy** developments
- **Discrimination / rights** issues
- **Community organizations** activities

### Homeland Connection
- **Diaspora-relevant homeland news**
- **Transnational politics**
- **Migration stories** — both directions
- **Remittance / family ties**

### Service / Practical
- **Visa / immigration practical info**
- **Tax / legal info** in language
- **Business / commerce** in ethnic economy
- **Health resources** in language
- **Education for immigrant families**

### Identity Issues
- **Generational tensions**
- **Hybrid identity** stories
- **Religious / cultural debates**
- **Language preservation**

## Yu Shi's 7-Pronged Test for Ethnic Media's Alternative Role

1. Individual place of origin
2. Historical evolution
3. Production process
4. Distribution pattern
5. Ownership
6. Self-identity (non-profit/for-profit; alternative/commercial)
7. Connection to power groups (local + transnational)

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Community impact** weighted heavily
- **Mainstream coverage gaps** filled
- **Language access**
- **Cultural specificity**
- **Counter-narrative** to stereotypes in mainstream
- **Practical utility** for immigrants
- **Transnational relevance**
- **Interpretive context** for host-society events
- **Identity affirmation**
- **Civic participation enabling**

## Examples (US)
- Spanish-language: La Opinión, El Diario
- Korean: Korea Daily
- Chinese: Sing Tao, World Journal
- Black press: NY Amsterdam News, Atlanta Daily World
- Hindi/Indian: India Abroad
- Vietnamese, Filipino, Polish, etc.
