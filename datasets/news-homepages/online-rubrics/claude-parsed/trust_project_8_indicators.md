# The Trust Project — 8 Indicators of Trustworthy Journalism

A global consortium of news outlets that adopts standardized "Trust Indicators." Built by asking audiences what wins/loses trust.

## The 8 Trust Indicators

1. **Best Practices** — Transparency about ownership, funding, and editorial policies (mission statements, ethics codes, corrections policies, masthead)

2. **Journalist Expertise** — Bios, qualifications, experience of reporters

3. **Type of Work / Labels** — Clear labels distinguishing news, opinion, analysis, sponsored content, advertorial

4. **Citations & References** — Sources, documents, data linked or cited within stories

5. **Methods** — How was the story reported? Why this story? Disclose process for investigations

6. **Locally Sourced** — Was reporter present in the location? Reliance on local voices

7. **Diverse Voices** — Newsroom efforts to include diverse perspectives across social, ideological, demographic differences

8. **Actionable Feedback** — Mechanisms for audience to engage, challenge, suggest, correct

## Adoption Process

- Voluntary
- Eligibility review
- Cohort training and implementation
- Trust Mark awarded after compliance

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Transparency as quality
- Methodological rigor
- Local presence as credibility
- Diversity as quality
- Audience accountability
- Sourcing rigor
- Distinction of genres

## Reception
Trust in flagship outlets (e.g., UK's Mirror) reportedly jumped 8% after Trust Indicators added.
