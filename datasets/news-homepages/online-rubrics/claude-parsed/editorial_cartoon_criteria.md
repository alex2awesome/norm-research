# Editorial / Political Cartoon — Newsworthiness Criteria

What makes an editorial cartoon publishable, effective, and worth featuring.

## Definition

A political cartoon is a drawing made for editorial commentary on politics, politicians, and current events. Includes caricature, expresses opinion, requires audience knowledge of the subject.

## Newsworthiness — What Subjects Get Cartoons

1. **Major news event** of the day/week
2. **Political controversy**
3. **Government scandal**
4. **Policy debate** topic
5. **Election cycle moments**
6. **International crisis**
7. **Social movement moment**
8. **Cultural figure** in news
9. **Anniversary** of important event
10. **Local political news** (in local press)

## Criteria for Effective Editorial Cartoons

### Clarity
- Subject clearly identified
- Visual references readable
- Audience can decode without explanation
- Caption (if any) clarifies

### Substantive Argument
- "More than a slogan"
- Invites consideration of argument
- Visual + written features make case
- Reasons to support view

### Factual Accuracy
- Cartoonist may exaggerate
- May NOT alter facts
- Quotes accurately attributed
- People depicted as recognizable selves

### Focus
- Single item / issue
- Clearly identified
- Simplification (but not distortion)

### Symbolism
- Effective visual symbols
- Universally understood (within target audience)
- Original or apt cliche

### Caricature
- Recognizable subject
- Exaggerates real features
- Avoids racist / cruel depiction (modern standard)

### Timeliness
- Tied to current event
- Time-sensitive humor
- Shelf-life matched to event significance

## Ethical Considerations
- Avoid stereotypes (race, ethnicity, religion, gender)
- Distinguish satire from libel
- Comment on actions, not appearance unfairly
- Punch up, not down

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Cartoons

- **News event timeliness**
- **Symbolic capacity** of subject
- **Visual potential** (iconic face/situation)
- **Public knowledge** of event
- **Argumentative angle** available
- **Humor / satire** opportunity
- **Restraint from cruelty**
- **Brevity** of message

## Pulitzer Editorial Cartooning Criteria
The Pulitzer Prize for Editorial Cartooning (now "Illustrated Reporting and Commentary") rewards:
- Strong drawing skill
- Pictorial effect
- Editorial effectiveness
- Originality
- Public service value
