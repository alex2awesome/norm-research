# Theater Review — Newsworthiness Criteria

What makes a theatrical production worth reviewing, and how reviews function.

## Opening Night Tradition

- **Reviews not published until after official opening night**
- Critics typically attend preview performances after show "frozen"
- Embargoed until opening
- Pre-2010s: critics attended opening night itself
- Now: critics see previews; reviews drop on/after opening day

## What Gets Reviewed (Newsworthiness)

1. **Major Broadway productions**
2. **Off-Broadway / Off-Off Broadway** with buzz
3. **Regional theater** in major markets (LA, Chicago)
4. **Festival productions** (Edinburgh, etc.)
5. **Star vehicles** (major celebrity attached)
6. **Famous director** projects
7. **New work by established playwright**
8. **Revivals of classics**
9. **Innovative / experimental** work
10. **Revivals at major regional theaters**
11. **Touring productions** in their stop city
12. **High school / community theater** in local press

## Review Criteria

### Production Quality
- Direction (vision, pace, integration)
- Acting performances (lead and ensemble)
- Set design
- Lighting design
- Sound design
- Costume design
- Choreography (musicals)
- Music direction (musicals)

### Text / Material
- Playwright's craft
- Themes addressed
- Dialogue quality
- Structure / pacing
- Originality vs. derivative
- Cultural relevance

### Audience Experience
- Emotional impact
- Intellectual engagement
- Memorability
- Recommendation worthiness
- Ticket-price value

## Newsworthiness Beyond Reviews
- Opening night parties (entertainment news)
- Star casting announcements
- Production drama (firings, walkouts)
- Box office milestones
- Tony nominations
- Closing announcements

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Theater

- **Production scale** (Broadway > regional > community)
- **Attached talent** (star, director, playwright)
- **Cultural significance** (major work, anniversary)
- **Innovation** in form or content
- **Local presence** (audience can attend)
- **Limited engagement** (urgency for review)
- **Box office implications**
- **Award potential**
- **Industry trend representation**
