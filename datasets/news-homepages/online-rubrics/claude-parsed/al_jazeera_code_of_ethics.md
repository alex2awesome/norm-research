# Al Jazeera — Code of Ethics

Code of ethics for the Doha-based global news network. Influential in establishing post-2001 international broadcaster norms.

## The Code's Principles

1. **Honesty, courage, fairness, balance, independence, credibility and diversity** — Core journalistic values; no priority to commercial or political over professional.

2. **Endeavour to get to the truth and declare it** in dispatches/programmes/news bulletins unequivocally with no doubt about validity and accuracy.

3. **Treat audiences with due respect.** Address every issue with attention to clarity, factuality, and accuracy. Give consideration to:
   - Feelings of victims of crime, war, persecution, disaster
   - Their relatives
   - Viewers
   - Individual privacies
   - Public decorum

4. **Present diverse points of view** without bias or partiality.

5. **Acknowledge and correct mistakes** promptly.

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

- **Truth-seeking** as primary
- **Fairness and balance** in framing
- **Independence** from commercial/political pressure
- **Diversity** of perspectives, especially Global South / non-Western
- **Sensitivity to victims and relatives**
- **Privacy considerations**
- **Cultural appropriateness**
- **Editorial courage** — willingness to cover difficult stories

## Distinctive Editorial Stance
Al Jazeera's editorial standards emphasize:
- **Voice for the voiceless** — Marginalized perspectives prioritized
- **Counter-dominant narratives** — Especially regarding Western framing of Middle East
- **Long-form documentary tradition**
- **Live, immediate coverage** from conflict zones
