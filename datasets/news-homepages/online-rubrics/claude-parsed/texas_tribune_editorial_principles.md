# The Texas Tribune — Editorial Principles for Nonprofit News

Pioneer of digital-first nonprofit state-level news. 501(c)3 organization.

## Mission

"Inform and engage Texans through in-depth investigations and reporting on public policy, politics, government, and statewide issues."

Promotes civic engagement through original, explanatory journalism and public events.

## Core Editorial Principles

1. **Editorial Independence** — Editorial decisions made by journalists alone, not influenced by donors. Donations do not entitle preferential treatment or protection from scrutiny.

2. **Aggressive Reporting** — "There are no sacred cows; we aggressively pursue all stories we deem newsworthy."

3. **Transparency** — Truthful, transparent, respectful reporting; facts accurate, complete, fairly presented.

4. **Nonpartisan** — Objective journalism distinguishes the Tribune in a polarized media universe.

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

### Coverage Priorities
- Public policy
- Politics and government
- Statewide issues (not micro-local)
- Issues affecting Texans broadly

### Story Selection Tests
- Civic significance
- Educational value (explanatory)
- Original reporting (vs. aggregation)
- Watchdog potential (no sacred cows)
- Transparency-supportable (sources nameable)
- Nonpartisan framing

### Distinctive Quality Markers
- In-depth investigation
- Public-facing data tools
- Live events as journalism
- Newsletter-driven engagement

## Funding Model & Newsworthiness Implications
- Membership programs
- Major donations (with disclosure)
- Foundation support
- Corporate sponsorships (clearly labeled)
- Events
- Paid newsletters

Funder firewall ensures editorial independence regardless of source.
