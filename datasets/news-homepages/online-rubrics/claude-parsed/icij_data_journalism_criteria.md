# ICIJ — International Consortium of Investigative Journalists

Cross-border collaborative investigative journalism. Best known for Panama Papers, Pandora Papers, FinCEN Files. De facto rubric for transnational data investigations.

## ICIJ Project Selection Criteria

A story qualifies for ICIJ collaboration if it is:

1. **Transnational** — Affects multiple countries
2. **Public significance** — Citizens deserve to know
3. **Reveals concealed information** — Hidden by powerful interests
4. **Backed by documents/data** — Not anecdotal
5. **Verifiable** through structured records
6. **Beyond single-country reach** — Requires collaboration
7. **Holds power accountable** — Officials, oligarchs, corporations
8. **Reform potential** — Could spur policy change
9. **Worth months/years of effort** — Sustained investigation
10. **Original** — Not derivative

## Operating Norms

- Strict source protection
- Cross-border partnerships (often 100+ outlets)
- Coordinated publication day
- Document database stewardship
- Data verification and provenance
- Anonymization of innocent third parties
- Disclosure of methodology

## ICIJ-Style Newsworthiness Criteria

### "Power-Money-Corruption" Triad
- Wealth flows
- Political influence
- Tax avoidance
- Money laundering
- Sanctions violations
- Asset hiding

### Document-Based
- Leaked documents central
- Beneficial ownership records
- Court filings
- Tax records
- Trade data

### Public-Figure Focused
- Heads of state
- Business magnates
- Celebrities
- Officials
- Their families and associates

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Concealment exposed
- Hypocrisy exposed (preachers vs. practitioners)
- Cross-border accountability gap filled
- Data-driven, replicable methodology
- Public-record verifiability
- Symbolic figures named
