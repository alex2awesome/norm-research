# Mathew Ingram — Newsworthiness in the Platform Era

Long-running press critic at CJR / GigaOm; recurring themes form a rubric.

## Ingram's Selection Criteria
- **Platform leverage stories** — how Meta / Google / X squeeze publishers
- **Subscription / membership model dynamics**
- **AI implications for journalism business**
- **Newsroom labor and editorial-independence tensions**
- **International press-freedom incidents**
- **Open-web vs. walled-garden battles**

## Ingram's Tests
- "Does this story affect the structural conditions of journalism?"
- "Is there a power asymmetry being exposed?"
- "Are publishers / journalists acting as a class on this?"
- "What's the long-arc consequence?"

## Stories Ingram Elevates
- Platform algorithm / policy changes that throttle news
- Labor actions at major publishers
- Lawsuits over journalism (training data, anti-SLAPP, defamation)
- Funding mechanism experiments (NewsMatch, foundation models)

## Stories Ingram Demotes
- Tech press hype
- Performative outrage about minor controversies
- Personality-driven media gossip

## Note
Ingram's rubric is structural — newsworthy = changes the political-economic terrain on which journalism operates.
