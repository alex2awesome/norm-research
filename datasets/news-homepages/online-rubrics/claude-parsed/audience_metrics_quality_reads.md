# Audience Metrics — Operationalizing News Value for the Digital Newsroom

Journalism Practice, 2023. How newsrooms operationally translate audience analytics into news selection criteria.

## Metrics Used in Newsrooms

### Volume Metrics
- **Pageviews** — gross visits
- **Unique visitors** — reach
- **Sessions per user** — return visits
- **Pages per session** — discovery within site

### Engagement Quality Metrics
- **Dwell time** — average time on page
- **Scroll depth** — % of article scrolled
- **Quality Reads** (Chartbeat metric) — users reading at least half of article based on word length, time, average reading speed
- **Recirculation** — clicks on related stories
- **Active engaged time** — time with focus on tab

### Distribution Metrics
- **Source of traffic** — search, social, direct, referral
- **Geographic origin**
- **Device type**
- **Time of arrival**

### Loyalty / Subscription Metrics
- **Subscriber stories** — what subscribers read
- **Conversion-driving stories** — stories that prompted subscriptions
- **Email signups**
- **App downloads**

### Social Metrics
- **Shares** by platform
- **Comments**
- **Reactions** (likes, etc.)

## How Metrics Operationalize Newsworthiness

### "What's Worth Covering Tomorrow?"
- Past performance of similar stories
- Topic categories that drive subscribers
- Author/byline performance
- Headline format performance

### "What Goes on the Homepage?"
- Real-time CTR
- Velocity of pickup
- Predicted dwell time
- Cross-section of topics

### "What Should Get Promotional Push?"
- Subscriber-only content
- Original investigations
- Content with high quality reads + low natural traffic

## Tensions Identified

- **Click optimization** vs. **mission alignment**
- **Short-term metrics** vs. **subscriber lifetime value**
- **Quantitative dashboard** vs. **qualitative editorial judgment**
- **Algorithmic homogenization** vs. **distinctive voice**

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria from Metrics

- **Predicted CTR** — clickability
- **Predicted dwell time** — depth value
- **Predicted shareability**
- **Predicted subscriber retention**
- **Predicted recirculation** — pulls people deeper
- **Topic + format match to audience segment**
- **Time-of-day fit**
- **Geographic targeting fit**
- **Headline match to query trends**
