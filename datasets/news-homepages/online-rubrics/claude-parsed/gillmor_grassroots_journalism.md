# Dan Gillmor — Grassroots / Networked Newsworthiness

Gillmor's "We the Media" thesis: the audience is no longer a passive recipient; news values must adapt.

## Gillmor's Newsworthiness Principles
- **Verifiability** — sourced, citable, transparent
- **Linkability** — readers can follow trail to primary sources
- **Conversational** — comments / corrections incorporated
- **Update-able** — story is iterative, not static
- **Niche-relevance** — value is highest for affected community
- **Provenance disclosure** — who produced this and why

## Tests Gillmor Applies
- "Does this story trust the reader to follow links?"
- "Have we shown our work?"
- "Are we open to correction in real time?"
- "Are we using audience expertise as input?"

## Gillmor's Critique of Traditional News Values
- Over-reliance on official sources
- Closed publishing model that hides errors
- Lack of meta-information about how a story was produced
- Treating audience as eyeballs, not collaborators

## Elevated by Gillmor's Framework
- Annotated stories with primary documents
- Crowdsourced reporting (verified)
- Live updating coverage
- Specialized hyper-local reporting
- Fact-check trails visible

## Note
Gillmor's rubric reframes newsworthiness as a process attribute (transparency, verifiability, openness) as much as a content attribute.
