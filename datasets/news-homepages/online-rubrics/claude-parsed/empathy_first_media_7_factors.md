# 7 Key Factors That Make a Story Newsworthy

PR-applied news values framework.

## 1. Timeliness: The Power of "Now"
Recent or current information; "news, by definition, is new information." Connect announcements to trending topics and release information promptly.

## 2. Impact: The Scope and Significance
Number of people affected and depth of consequences. Quantify the audience and illustrate broader implications.

## 3. Proximity: The Local Connection
Geographic or psychological closeness to audiences. Localize broader narratives for specific markets.

## 4. Prominence: The Power of Notable Names
"Well-known individuals, brands, or institutions inherently generate more interest."

## 5. Novelty: The Allure of the Unusual
Unique or unexpected elements capture attention. First-to-market innovations, surprising data, unconventional approaches.

## 6. Conflict: The Draw of Tension and Resolution
Disputes, competitive dynamics, obstacles. Frame challenges as compelling stories with stakes and resolutions.

## 7. Human Interest: The Emotional Connection
Personal stories and universal experiences. Testimonials showing how solutions affect real people.
