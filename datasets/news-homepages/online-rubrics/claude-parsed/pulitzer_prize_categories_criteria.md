# Pulitzer Prize Categories and Criteria

The Pulitzer Prizes serve as a de facto rubric for what constitutes the highest forms of newsworthiness in American journalism. Each category definition implicitly identifies a kind of story journalism should aspire to produce.

## Public Service
"For a distinguished example of meritorious public service by a newspaper, magazine or news site through the use of its journalistic resources, including the use of stories, editorials, cartoons, photographs, graphics, videos, databases, multimedia or interactive presentations or other visual material."
- Often considered the grand prize; awarded as a gold medal
- Implicit criteria: difficulty, novelty, impact, public benefit

## Investigative Reporting
For investigative reporting by an individual or team, presented as a single article or series. Requires original investigation revealing previously hidden wrongdoing, often holding power accountable.

## Explanatory Reporting
For an explanation of complex subjects, demonstrating mastery of the subject, lucid writing, and clear presentation, illuminating significant and complex issues.

## Local Reporting
For local reporting that, by its sustained focus on a community, illuminates significant issues, traces underreported topics, or fosters civic dialogue.

## National Reporting
For coverage of national affairs.

## International Reporting
For coverage of international affairs, including United Nations correspondence.

## Feature Writing
For distinguished feature writing giving prime consideration to quality of writing, originality, and concision.

## Commentary
For distinguished commentary.

## Criticism
For distinguished criticism.

## Editorial Writing
For distinguished editorial writing, the test of excellence being clearness of style, moral purpose, sound reasoning, and power to influence public opinion in what the writer conceives to be the right direction.

## Illustrated Reporting and Commentary
For a distinguished example of illustrated reporting and commentary using any medium that combines text and visual storytelling.

## Breaking News Photography
For a distinguished example of breaking news photography.

## Feature Photography
For a distinguished example of feature photography.

## Audio Reporting
For a distinguished example of audio journalism that serves the public interest.

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria (Pulitzer)
- Public benefit / public service
- Original investigation / exclusivity
- Holding power accountable
- Illumination of complex subjects
- Sustained community focus
- Quality of writing
- Moral purpose
- Influence on public opinion
- Difficulty of execution
- Innovation in presentation
