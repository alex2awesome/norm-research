# The Newswriter's Handbook — Stein, Paterno, Burnett

Wiley-Blackwell, 2nd ed. Widely-used journalism textbook covering news judgment, writing, ethics.

## News Judgment Approach

Develops news judgment through:
- Spot/hard news
- Features
- News features
- Editorials and opinions
- Obituary writing
- Online journalism opportunities

## News Values Taught

The handbook teaches the standard set of news values applied to spot news:

1. **Timeliness** — Recency
2. **Proximity** — Local relevance
3. **Prominence** — Status of subjects
4. **Impact / Consequence** — Number affected
5. **Conflict** — Disputes, tension
6. **Unusual / Oddity**
7. **Human Interest** — Emotional connection
8. **Currency** — Trending issues

## Beyond Spot News

For features:
- **Narrative quality**
- **Character development**
- **Scene-setting**
- **Theme** beyond facts

For editorials/opinions:
- **Argument quality**
- **Evidence basis**
- **Civic significance**
- **Moral force**

For obituaries:
- **Significance of person's life**
- **Achievement**
- **Community connection**
- **Surviving family details**

## Honesty and Ethics Coverage

- Plagiarism (against)
- Fabrication (against)
- Conflicts of interest
- Anonymous sources (sparingly)
- Privacy considerations
- Identifying victims of crime

## Implicit Newsworthiness Across Genres

- News: discrete events, fresh facts
- Features: enduring human interest
- Editorials: civic argument
- Obituaries: completed lives
- Opinion: original viewpoints
