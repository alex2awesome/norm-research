# Music Journalism — Album Review Criteria

Criteria for what makes a music story / album review newsworthy.

## What Makes an Album Worth Reviewing (Newsworthiness)

1. **Major artist release** — Established act
2. **Highly anticipated** debut or follow-up
3. **Critical buzz / pre-release attention**
4. **Genre significance** — Defining moment for a movement
5. **Cultural moment** alignment
6. **Reissue / archival significance**
7. **Posthumous release**
8. **Surprise drop** that creates news
9. **Controversy associated** with album / artist
10. **Local artist** in local press
11. **Demonstrates new style or technology**
12. **Anniversary or retrospective** angle

## Album Review Evaluation Criteria

### Songwriting & Structure
- Strength of melodies, hooks, lyrics
- Verses, choruses, bridges, transitions, dynamics
- Album coherence (not just track-level)

### Vocal & Performance
- Emotional authenticity
- Phrasing and presence
- Instrumental virtuosity

### Production
- Mixing, mastering quality
- Sound clarity, balance
- Sonic identity / palette

### Originality & Listenability
- Distinctive style / sonic choices
- Memorable moments / hooks
- Repeat listen-ability

## Reviewer's Approach

- Place in artist's catalog (where it fits)
- Compare to peers in genre
- Consider intended audience
- Distinguish technical from preferential judgment
- Honesty over politeness
- Ground opinion in audible details

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Music Reviews

- **Artist prominence** drives whether/where review runs
- **Cultural moment** frames context
- **Originality** — pushes form forward
- **Emotional impact** — moves listeners
- **Craft quality** — songwriting, production
- **Place in oeuvre** — better/worse than predecessors
- **Genre fit / departure**
- **Lyrical significance** — themes addressed
- **Live capability** — performable
- **Streaming-era considerations** — singles vs. album

## Genres Apply Different Weights

- Pop: hooks, accessibility
- Hip-hop: lyricism, beats, samples, voice
- Jazz: improvisation, ensemble, originality
- Classical: composition, performance, recording
- Indie: authenticity, craft, vision
- Country: storytelling, voice, tradition
- Electronic: sound design, structure
- Folk: lyrics, voice, intimacy
