# Society of Environmental Journalists (SEJ)

Founded 1990. Mission: "Strengthen the quality, reach and viability of journalism across all media to advance public understanding of environmental issues."

## SEJ Membership Eligibility (Implicit Standards)

- **Active members**: editorially independent journalism, no paid lobbying/PR on environment
- **Associate**: must spend more time on journalism than environmental PR
- **Lobbyists/PR pros**: NOT eligible

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Environmental Stories

### Topic Areas
- Climate change
- Pollution (air, water, soil)
- Species extinction / biodiversity
- Land use / habitat
- Energy (oil, gas, renewables, nuclear)
- Wildfires / extreme weather
- Water quality / quantity
- Toxics / chemical exposure
- Environmental justice
- Indigenous land rights
- Ecology / wildlife
- Conservation / parks
- Agricultural / food systems

### Newsworthiness Drivers
1. **Public health implications** (pollution, toxics)
2. **Long-term consequences** (climate)
3. **Local impact** of global issue
4. **Corporate accountability** (polluters)
5. **Government accountability** (regulation, enforcement)
6. **Environmental justice** — disproportionate impacts
7. **Species iconography** — charismatic wildlife
8. **Disaster scale** — natural events
9. **Scientific findings** — peer-reviewed
10. **Human stories** of impact

### Reporting Standards
- Independent of industry-funded sources
- Source diversity (community + scientific + regulatory)
- Long-term context required
- Quantitative literacy expected
- Climate denial false-balance avoided

## Distinct Challenges
- "Slow news" problem (climate is gradual)
- Visualization challenges (greenhouse gases invisible)
- Audience fatigue
- Industry pushback
- Source intimidation
- Anti-science attacks

## SEJ Annual Conference & Awards
Recognizes excellence in environmental journalism — implicit definition of best work in the field.

## Implicit Quality Newsworthiness for Environment
- Connects local to global
- Names responsible actors
- Provides scientific accuracy
- Engages historically marginalized communities
- Investigates corporate/government records
- Tells human stories within larger systems
- Avoids both alarmism and denialism
