# SABEW Best in Business Awards — Business Journalism Newsworthiness

Society for Advancing Business Editing and Writing (formerly Society of American Business Editors and Writers). Established 1964 to promote superior coverage of business and economic events.

## Award Categories (Implicit Newsworthiness Areas)

- **Breaking News** — Business news as it happens
- **Beat Reporting** — Sustained coverage of a business beat
- **Investigative** — Uncovering corporate / financial wrongdoing
- **Explanatory** — Making complex business concepts understandable
- **Feature**
- **Personal Finance** — Practical impact on individual readers
- **Real Estate**
- **Markets / Investing**
- **Health Care Business**
- **Technology**
- **Energy**
- **Banking / Finance**
- **Retail / Consumer**
- **Workplace / Labor**
- **International Business**
- **Commentary / Editorial**
- **Visual Storytelling / Data Journalism**
- **Innovation / Audience Engagement**

## Implicit Business Newsworthiness Criteria
- Market impact (effect on stocks, sectors, economy)
- Consumer/worker impact (jobs, prices, retirement)
- Corporate accountability (governance, fraud, ethics)
- Industry trend significance
- Explanatory clarity for non-specialists
- Public policy intersections
- Numerical/financial rigor
- Original sourcing and analysis
- Localized economic impact
