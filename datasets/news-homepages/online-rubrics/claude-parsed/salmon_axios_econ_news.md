# Felix Salmon — Numerate Newsworthiness for Business Stories

Long-time business / finance columnist; repeated framings supply a rubric.

## Salmon's Newsworthiness Heuristics
- **The number that matters** — every business story should have a single defining figure
- **Counter-intuitive comparisons** — frame ratios that reframe a story
- **Aggregated-vs-anecdotal trade-off** — prefer aggregates, but anecdote can illustrate
- **Distinguish flow vs. stock** stories
- **Skepticism about "biggest ever" claims** — adjust for inflation, growth
- **Beware single-data-point trend pieces**

## Salmon's Tests
- "Is the headline number meaningful in context?"
- "Has the writer adjusted for size, growth, baseline?"
- "Does the metric tell the story or just illustrate it?"
- "Is the framing pro-cyclical with the news cycle, or independent?"

## Elevated Stories
- Quiet structural shifts in financial markets
- Compensation studies with proper baselines
- Counterintuitive macro pieces
- Long-form on industries undergoing transformation
- Plain-English explanations of complex instruments

## Demoted Stories
- Day-to-day market chatter
- "Stocks did X today because Y" narratives
- Rich-list voyeurism
- Hype-cycle reproduction

## Note
Salmon's rubric inserts numeracy as a newsworthiness criterion — business news should add up before it goes up.
