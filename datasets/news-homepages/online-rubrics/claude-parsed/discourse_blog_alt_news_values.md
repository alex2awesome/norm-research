# Discourse Blog — Anti-Brand-Voice News Selection

Discourse Blog's worker-owned cooperative model produces a distinctly skeptical newsworthiness rubric.

## Newsworthiness Filters (Explicit and Implicit)
- **Class stakes** — labor, housing, healthcare beats
- **Power vs. people** — small actors against institutional ones
- **Discourse satire** — mocking how mainstream outlets cover stories
- **Voice / personality** — first-person authority preferred to "view from nowhere"
- **Cultural specificity** — internet-native, generational, niche references welcome
- **Unfiltered profanity** — register signals authenticity

## Stories Discourse Blog Elevates
- Strikes and labor actions
- Tenant organizing
- Police accountability beat reporting
- Critique of legacy media's framing choices
- Sports as labor / culture
- "Shitposts with reporting underneath"

## Stories They Demote
- Beltway speculation
- "Sources say" pieces
- Both-sides framing of moral issues
- Sanitized institutional voice
- "View from nowhere" objectivity poses

## Implicit Tests
- "Would mainstream outlets soften this take? If yes, lead with the unsoftened version."
- "Whose interest does the standard frame serve?"
- "Is there a working person at the center who'd recognize the piece?"

## Note
Discourse Blog argues newsworthiness should be untangled from professional-class brand-voice norms; first-person and partisan framing can be more honest than fake neutrality.
