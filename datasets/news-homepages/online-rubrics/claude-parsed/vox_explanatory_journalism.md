# Vox & Explanatory Journalism — "Explainer" Newsworthiness

A genre of journalism focused on the "How" and "Why" rather than just "Who, What, When, Where." Vox.com (founded 2014 by Ezra Klein, Matt Yglesias, Melissa Bell) is the most prominent example.

## Core Mission

"Explain the world, add clarity, provide all the information to empower audiences, and do it in an approachable, inspiring, and helpful way."

## What Makes a Good Explainer (Implicit Criteria)

1. **Topic in the news but not understood**
   - High public attention
   - Low public comprehension
   - "Why is X happening?" common reader question

2. **Background-heavy**
   - Provides historical context
   - Defines terms
   - Maps causal chain

3. **Evergreen + Topical**
   - Shelf-life longer than news article
   - Updateable as situation evolves
   - Republished when topic resurges

4. **Visual / Multimedia**
   - Charts, maps, timelines
   - Embedded video
   - Interactive elements

5. **Authoritative source synthesis**
   - Researcher interviews
   - Academic citations
   - Cross-referenced data

## Topics Suited to Explainers

- Foreign policy events (geographic distance)
- Scientific discoveries (technical complexity)
- Economic shifts (jargon-laden)
- Legal cases (procedural complexity)
- Cultural moments (historical context needed)
- Policy debates (terms of art)
- Conflict origins
- Tech / AI developments
- Health / medical issues

## Vox Format Conventions
- Numbered subheads
- Q&A structure ("What does this mean?")
- Bullet point summaries
- Sourcing transparency
- "What you need to know" framing
- Card stack format

## Implicit Newsworthiness for Explainers

- **Comprehension gap** between news event and audience knowledge
- **Stakes** — why audience should care
- **Pattern significance** — connects discrete event to larger system
- **Background dependency** — story can't be told without context
- **Educational value**
- **Reusability** — durable reference

## Distinct from News
- News: First reports of new events
- Explainer: Background and synthesis
- News reports "what happened"; Explainer reports "what it means"
