# Online Journalism — Defining Characteristics

The core elements that distinguish online journalism's newsworthiness criteria from print or broadcast.

## 4 Defining Characteristics of Online Journalism

1. **Immediacy** — Real-time updates, live blogs, push notifications
2. **Multimedia** — Combines text, image, audio, video, interactive graphics
3. **Interactive** — Comments, sharing, participation
4. **Hyper-textuality** — Linked, networked, connected to other content

## 5 Core News Elements (Online)

1. **Timeliness** — Articles timestamped, live blogs in real time, social media instant
2. **Significance** — Public-impact dimension
3. **Impact** — Number affected
4. **Novelty** — Unusual, bizarre, first-ever
5. **Listener / Reader Interest** — Emotional resonance

## Distinct Online Newsworthiness Considerations

### Format Adaptations
- **Headline that works in social feeds**
- **Image that works at thumbnail size**
- **First sentence that works above the fold**
- **Format optimized for mobile** (vertical video, short paragraphs)
- **Search-optimized** (SEO-friendly headlines, keyword density)
- **Shareable** (one-tap sharing, social card preview)

### Engagement Considerations
- **Click-through prediction**
- **Dwell time prediction**
- **Recirculation potential**
- **Subscription-driving potential**
- **Email-newsletter fit**

### Multimedia Considerations
- **Image-rich** to stop scroll
- **Video-embeddable**
- **Interactive element potential**
- **Data visualization opportunity**

### Hyper-textuality
- **Internal links** to related coverage
- **External links** for sources
- **Embed potential** for tweets, videos
- **Connected to live blogs / collections**

## Online Newsroom Dynamics
- Real-time dashboards (Chartbeat, Parse.ly)
- 24/7 desk staffing
- Push notification decisions
- Social platform optimization
- A/B headline testing

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Online

1. **Search-discoverability**
2. **Social-shareability**
3. **Mobile-first formatting**
4. **Real-time update capability**
5. **Multimedia fit**
6. **Interactive component**
7. **Topical SEO match**
8. **Subscription value**
9. **Email-list fit**
10. **Push-notification appropriateness**
11. **Reader engagement metrics**
12. **Linkability** (other sites would link)
