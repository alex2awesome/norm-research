# NASW Science in Society Journalism Awards

National Association of Science Writers' premier awards for science journalism. Independent of professional/commercial subsidies.

## Award Purpose

"To provide recognition for investigative or interpretive reporting about the sciences and their impact on modern society."

## Core Criteria

- **Investigative or interpretive depth** — beyond press release / explanation
- **Science + Society** — winners "demonstrated innovative reporting that goes well beyond the science itself and into ethical problems and social implications for communities and society at large"
- **Diverse perspectives** — "shaped by a variety of perspectives" enabling "more broadly relevant stories"
- **Diversity in topics, sources, audience and authors** considered "a critical component of excellence"
- **For non-specialist audiences** — accessibility

## 6 Categories
1. Books
2. Science Reporting
3. Science Features
4. Longform Narratives
5. Series
6. Commentary

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Science Stories
- Connection to social/ethical implications
- Independence from PR/commercial influence
- Innovative reporting approach
- Accessibility to general public
- Community relevance
- Diversity of voices
- Original interpretation, not just translation
- Going beyond science journals
