# The 8 Values That Will Make Your Content Newsworthy

A content marketing/PR-oriented adaptation of journalism news values.

## 1. Impact
How a story affects readers' lives. "The greater the number of people affected, the greater the impact." Example: New fire engines reduce fire damage risk for community members.

## 2. Timeliness
Answers why information matters now. Requires new information, discovery, or analysis—not necessarily a brand-new event. Example: A newly-discovered dinosaur species, or fresh data from old research.

## 3. Prominence
Why the source telling the story carries credibility. Related to authority and expertise. A state representative's announcements naturally merit coverage because of their position.

## 4. Proximity
Whether the story matters to your specific audience. Geographically or demographically relevant.

## 5. The Bizarre
Unexpected or unusual elements that break the norm. "When a dog bites a man, that's not news... if a man bites a dog, then that's news."

## 6. Conflict
Multiple perspectives on an issue fighting for attention. Generates emotional engagement. Political coverage thrives on disagreement rather than consensus.

## 7. Currency
Is the idea trending or seasonally relevant? "An idea's time has come." Ice Bucket Challenge; Valentine's Day stories.

## 8. Human Interest
Stories featuring people impacted by larger events. Personalizes broader narratives.
