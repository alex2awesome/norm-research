# Constructive Journalism — Solutions-Focused Newsworthiness

A reform movement in journalism (Constructive Institute, Solutions Journalism Network) emphasizing solutions, future-orientation, and engagement over negativity bias.

## Core Premise

Counteract:
- News fatigue
- News avoidance
- Negativity bias
- Disengagement

Through:
- Solution-focused stories
- Contextual reporting
- Future-oriented perspectives

## Solutions Journalism Network's 4 Criteria

To distinguish quality solutions journalism from greenwashing/PR/activism:

1. **Place possible solutions front and center** — focus on response to problem
2. **Examine HOW the solution works** — mechanism, evidence, process
3. **Provide evidence of effectiveness** — data, results
4. **Acknowledge limitations** — what the solution can't do

## Constructive Journalism Principles (Constructive Institute, Bonn Institute)

1. **Future-oriented** — "To what end?" "Where to?" not just "Why?" "From where?"
2. **Solutions-focused** — credible solutions, not just problems
3. **Inclusive dialogue** — diverse perspectives engaged constructively
4. **Engagement over outrage**
5. **Effectiveness over good intentions**
6. **Complementary to critical reporting** (not replacement)

## What Constructive Journalism is NOT
- Positivity for its own sake
- PR / advocacy
- "Good news" boosterism
- Avoiding criticism
- Cheerleading for institutions

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria

### Stories That Qualify
- Communities solving local problems
- Policies that worked elsewhere
- Movements producing change
- Individual actions with broader impact
- Innovation in service delivery
- Restorative responses to harm
- Reconciliation efforts
- Replicable models

### Stories That Don't
- Pure feel-good without substance
- PR campaigns
- Single-organization promotion
- Unverified claims of effectiveness
- Aspirational without action

## Audience Effects (Research)

- Lower negative emotion
- Higher engagement
- Higher behavioral intentions
- Higher trust in news
- Higher subscription willingness
- Less news avoidance

## Implicit Quality Markers

- **Evidence-base** for solution claims
- **Replicability** assessed
- **Limitations** acknowledged
- **Multiple stakeholders** consulted
- **Complexity** maintained (not oversimplified)
- **Long-term outcomes** tracked
- **Failures discussed** not just successes
