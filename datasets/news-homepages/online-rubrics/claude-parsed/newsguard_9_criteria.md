# NewsGuard — 9 Criteria for News Source Reliability

NewsGuard's pass/fail rubric for evaluating news sources. Each criterion is weighted, with sites scored 0-100.

## The 9 Criteria (with Point Weighting)

### Credibility (60 points total)
1. **Does not repeatedly publish false content** (22 points)
2. **Gathers and presents information responsibly** (18 points)
3. **Regularly corrects or clarifies errors** (12.5 points)
4. **Handles the difference between news and opinion responsibly** (12.5 points)
5. **Avoids deceptive headlines** (10 points)

### Transparency (40 points total)
6. **Website discloses ownership and financing** (7.5 points)
7. **Clearly labels advertising** (7.5 points)
8. **Reveals who's in charge, including any possible conflicts of interest** (5 points)
9. **The site provides names of content creators, along with either contact or biographical information** (5 points)

## Score Bands
- **75%+** — Generally credible
- **60-75%** — Credible with exceptions
- **<60%** — Unreliable

## Implicit Newsworthiness/Quality Criteria
- Truthfulness (no false content)
- Verification standards
- Error transparency
- News/opinion distinction
- Headline-content alignment
- Funding transparency
- Ownership disclosure
- Author transparency
- Ad-content distinction

## Note

NewsGuard rates **sources** rather than individual stories, but its criteria function as a rubric for what makes coverage credible enough to belong in the news ecosystem.
