# Google News — Ranking Signals

Google News' algorithmic criteria for ranking and featuring stories. Functions as a de facto rubric for digital newsworthiness.

## Three Primary Factors

1. **Relevance** — How well the article matches reader query, interest, or current trends
2. **Prominence** — How prominent the story is at the moment
3. **Authoritativeness** — Trust signals: bylines, publisher reputation, citations from others

## Detailed Ranking Signals

### Story-Level
- **Freshness** — Recency; how recently published; how often updated
- **Relevance to query** — Topic match
- **Click data** — User behavior
- **Geography** — Localization
- **Image presence and quality**
- **Schema markup** (NewsArticle JSON-LD)

### Publisher-Level
- **Publisher authority** in topic area
- **Volume of original content in category** (e.g., Bloomberg/Reuters in business)
- **Consistent publishing cadence**
- **Transparent authorship** (author bylines, bios)
- **Site speed** and mobile optimization
- **Adherence to Google's content policies**
- **History of original reporting**

### Originality
- Algorithms favor original reporting over aggregation
- "First on the story" gets boost via "Highly Cited" badge
- Cross-reference of citations across publishers

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Speed and recency
- Topical authority
- Brand recognition
- User signal (clicks)
- Geographic relevance
- Original reporting (not rewrite)
- Visual asset presence
- Technical SEO compliance
