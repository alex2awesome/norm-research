# Peabody Awards — "Stories That Matter"

The Peabody Awards recognize excellence in storytelling across electronic media. Tagline: "Stories That Matter."

## Stated Judging Philosophy

- "There are no set criteria for judging the winners."
- Recognize "stories that illuminate social issues with depth and complexity as much as stories that entertain and inspire through their art or voice."
- Honor "stories that matter."
- Each entrant judged on its own merit.
- Only unanimously selected programs receive a Peabody Award.
- Each entry evaluated on the achievement of standards established within its own context.

## Process
- 1300+ entries received annually
- Initial screening round → 3 intensive board meetings
- Diverse Board of Jurors (top-level thought leaders from varied backgrounds)
- 60 nominees → 30 winners per year

## Eight Categories
1. Entertainment
2. Documentary
3. Arts
4. Children's / Youth
5. News
6. Podcast / Radio
7. Interactive & Immersive
8. Public Service

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Social significance / illumination of issues
- Depth and complexity over breadth
- Artistic voice / craftsmanship
- Capacity to inspire or move audiences
- Diverse perspectives
- Public service value
- Originality of approach
- Cross-cultural relevance
