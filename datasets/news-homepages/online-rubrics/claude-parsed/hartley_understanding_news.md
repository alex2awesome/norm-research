# Hartley — Understanding News

Methuen, 1982. Foundational cultural-studies textbook on news. Treats news as a culturally shared language.

## Core Approach

News depends for its effect on a culturally shared language. News values are shared semiotic codes between producers and audiences.

## Topics Covered

- News values
- Mapping reality through consensus
- Mapping dissents as outsiders
- The mode of address (how news speaks to its audience)
- Common sense and audience understanding
- Elements of news narrative — visual and verbal structures
- News narrative vs. fiction

## Hartley's News Values (after Galtung & Ruge)

Hartley reorganizes news values into categories:

### Frequency / Time-Span
- Events fitting publication cycle

### Threshold
- Magnitude required for coverage

### Unambiguity
- Clear meaning preferred

### Meaningfulness (Cultural Proximity)
- Cultural relevance to audience

### Consonance
- Fits what reporters/readers expect

### Unexpectedness
- Within consonance, the surprising

### Continuity
- Already-running stories

### Composition
- Filling out the news whole

### Reference to Elite Persons / Nations
- Famous/powerful actors

### Personification
- Story told through individuals

### Negativity
- Bad news

## Distinctive Contribution

Frames news values not just as journalist criteria but as **cultural codes shared with audience** — both sides participate in defining what counts as news.
