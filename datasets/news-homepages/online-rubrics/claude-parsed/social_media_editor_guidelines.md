# Social Media Editor — Newsroom Guidelines for What to Share

Policies governing how newsrooms decide what to share on social media. Functions as a real-time newsworthiness rubric.

## Core Principles for Social Sharing

### Newsroom Account Conduct
- "Treat each post as your own" — if newsroom wouldn't write it, don't repost
- Major breaking stories: official accounts go first
- Reporter accounts can amplify with own commentary
- Link to developing stories

### What's Shareable

1. **Original reporting** by the newsroom
2. **Breaking news** with verification
3. **Analysis pieces** (clearly labeled)
4. **Long-form features** repurposed
5. **Live event coverage**
6. **Visual content** (photos, video, charts)
7. **Audience engagement** (Q&A, polls)
8. **Behind-the-scenes** (limited)

### What's Not Shareable

- Unverified breaking news
- Partisan commentary
- Personal political views (from work account)
- Disparaging advertisers/competitors
- Disparaging readers
- Anything undermining editorial integrity

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Social

### Formats That Work
- **Short, punchy** sentences
- **Strong visual** asset
- **Quote-able** core fact
- **Counter-intuitive** finding
- **Outrage-inducing** (to be used carefully)
- **Surprise / delight** factor
- **Emoji-friendly** subject

### Topics That Trend on Social
- Breaking events
- Controversial opinions
- Visual spectacle
- Outrage-bait politics
- Celebrity moments
- Sports outcomes
- Cultural memes
- Inspirational stories

## Per-Platform Considerations

### Twitter / X
- Real-time breaking
- Threading for context
- Replies/quote tweets engagement
- Verified-author boost

### Facebook
- Algorithm favors comments and shares
- Video performs well
- Local groups relevant for local news
- Less reach for news pages (post-2018 changes)

### Instagram
- Visual-first (image, video, reels, stories)
- Less news-friendly historically
- Carousel format for explainers
- Stories for ephemeral

### TikTok
- Short video
- Vertical format
- Music / trending sounds
- Younger audience
- Hook in first 3 seconds

### YouTube
- Long-form video
- Searchable
- Title/thumbnail crucial
- Watch-time matters

### LinkedIn
- Professional / business audience
- Thought leadership
- Industry analysis
- Career news

## Implicit Newsworthiness Filters

- **Platform-fit format**
- **Algorithmic favorability**
- **Engagement prediction**
- **Brand-appropriate tone**
- **Time-zone optimization**
- **Hashtag strategy**
- **Cross-post avoidance**
- **Audience-segment targeting**
