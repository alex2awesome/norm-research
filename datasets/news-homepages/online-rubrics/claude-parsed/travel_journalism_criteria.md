# Travel Journalism — Newsworthiness Criteria

Travel writing has its own newsworthiness criteria that combine journalism standards with literary and service elements.

## What Makes a Travel Story (Magazine / Outlet)

### Story Elements (Required)
1. **Strong scenes** — vivid place description
2. **Nut graph** — why this story now
3. **Solid interviewees** — locals, experts
4. **Data** — historical, statistical context

### Beyond "Top 10 Lists"
- "Great travel journalism isn't about ticking off famous landmarks"
- Editors want stories "no one else is telling"
- Unique narratives only the writer can tell
- Counter to listicle saturation

## Newsworthiness Drivers

1. **Destination news** — political change, opening, restriction
2. **Anniversary** — historical milestone in destination
3. **Cultural events** — festivals, openings
4. **Trends** — types of travel (sustainable, slow, etc.)
5. **Hidden gems** — off-the-beaten-path
6. **Practical changes** — visa, currency, transit
7. **Climate / environmental** changes affecting travel
8. **Hotel / restaurant** openings (high-end)
9. **Author's intimate experience** — narrative voice
10. **Identity / intersectional** angle — who travels

## Implicit Newsworthiness for Travel

- **Destination novelty** to audience
- **Cultural depth** — beyond surface
- **Practical utility** for would-be travelers
- **Aspirational quality** — luxury, exoticism
- **Photography potential**
- **Unique angle** — author's distinctive POV
- **Local voice prominence** — not just Western tourist's view
- **Sustainability angle** (rising)
- **Identity intersection** — race, gender, ability, sexuality of traveler
- **Service elements** — what to do, when to go, how much

## Travel Story Types

1. **Service piece** — "How to" / utility
2. **Destination feature** — comprehensive place coverage
3. **Travelogue** — first-person narrative
4. **Trend story** — pattern across many places
5. **Profile** — person tied to place
6. **Adventure** — extreme experience
7. **Food travel** — culinary focus
8. **Cultural critique** — examines tourism's effects

## Ethics in Travel Journalism

- Disclose sponsored trips / press trips
- Pay for own meals when possible
- Avoid promoting unsustainable practices
- Center local voices
- Avoid stereotyping cultures
- Acknowledge colonial / extractive history

## Implicit Quality Markers

- **Narrative arc** in story
- **Sensory specificity** (smells, sounds, textures)
- **Local voice** integration
- **Self-aware tourism critique**
- **Practical without being listicle**
- **Honest about challenges** of place
- **Photographic accompaniment**
