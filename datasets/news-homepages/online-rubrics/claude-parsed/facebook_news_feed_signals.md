# Facebook News Feed — Ranking Signals

Meta's News Feed algorithm signals — the implicit "newsworthiness" criteria that determine which news posts surface to users.

## Top Signals

### Who Posted It
- Frequency of past engagement with the source
- "Friends/family first"
- Trusted page (authentic publisher)
- Subscribed/followed source

### Post Type
- User's past engagement with that content type (video, link, photo, status)

### Quality and Format
- Vertical orientation (mobile-first)
- Clean visuals
- Authentic content (anti-misinformation signals)
- Original sources prioritized

### Engagement Predictions
- Likelihood user will: comment, like, share, save, click
- Likelihood of meaningful interaction (comments > likes)
- Watch time / view duration on video

### Trust Signals
- Whether content is from publishers users have rated as broadly trusted
- Authenticity verification
- Penalty for engagement bait, sensationalism

### Timeliness
- "Timey Posts" — currently popular content
- News value boost for breaking stories
- Decay function on older posts

### Original News Boost (specific to news)
Meta has a "prioritizing original news" model that boosts:
- First-source reporting
- Original investigation
- Penalty for aggregation

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria
- Engagement prediction (comments most weighted)
- Source trust score
- Authenticity (not bot/spam)
- Visual quality
- Recency
- Originality
- User affinity
- Format friendliness
