# YouTube — News Algorithm and Recommendation Criteria

YouTube's algorithm functions as a newsworthiness filter for video content.

## Core Algorithm Question
"Will this specific viewer enjoy this specific video right now?" — predicted via machine learning on billions of signals/sec.

## News-Specific Features

### Breaking News Shelf
- Appears on homepage during major breaking events
- Features relevant videos on a specific topic
- Surfaces during disaster, attack, major political event

### News Search Results
- "News shelf" appears near top of relevant queries
- Pulls from "high quality sources"
- Vetted publishers prioritized

### Information Quality
- News, medical, political videos: special source-reliability assessment
- Health, science, breaking news: boost from vetted publishers and SMEs
- Authority required for sensitive topics

## Trending Criteria (pre-2025)

- Sharp influx of views over short period
- Regional popularity for specific user
- High audience engagement in short period
- Diversity of viewers

## Post-2025 Changes
- YouTube removed Trending Page
- Now tracks micro-trends across niches/fandoms
- Multiple smaller trends instead of single global one

## Recommendation Areas

### Homepage
- Performance factors + previous watch history
- Personalized

### Suggested (Watch Next)
- Watch history
- Broader viewer trends
- Current video topic relevance

### Search
- Query relevance
- Channel authority
- Engagement signals

## Key Ranking Factors (3 main)

1. **Relevance** — query/interest match
2. **Engagement** — watch time, completion, interactions
3. **Quality** — authority signals (especially for news/medical/political)

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Video News

1. **Watch time potential** — long enough hooks
2. **Click-through rate prediction** (thumbnail + title)
3. **Completion rate**
4. **Engagement** (likes, comments, shares)
5. **Channel authority** for news topics
6. **Source vetting** for sensitive categories
7. **Video quality** (production)
8. **Currency** of topic
9. **Personalization fit**

## Tensions
- Engagement maximization vs. quality
- Authority bias vs. citizen journalism
- Algorithmic homogenization
- Filter bubbles
- Misinformation in long-tail content
