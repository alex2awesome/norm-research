# 8 Criteria That Get News Coverage - Newsworthy LLC

PR-pitch-oriented framework derived from journalism news values.

## 1. Impact
"How many people are affected by this story? The more, the better."

## 2. Timeliness
"When did the news happen? The sooner, the better."

## 3. Proximity
"Where is the story located? The closer it is, the more interesting it becomes."

## 4. Prominence
How notable are the key figures involved? A cycling accident becomes newsworthy when the mayor is involved.

## 5. Rarity
"How unexpected are the elements of the story?" Standout stories represent unusual or exceptional occurrences.

## 6. Currency
"Is this pitch about something we're already covering? Is it part of a trend?"

## 7. Conflict
"Opposing forces make a story more compelling."

## 8. Human Interest
"Can you put a face to this story? People care about other people."
