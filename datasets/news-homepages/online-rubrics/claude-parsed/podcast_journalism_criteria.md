# Podcast Journalism — Audio Storytelling Criteria

Audio journalism's distinct newsworthiness criteria, drawing on Knight Foundation reports and the Knight Center's training.

## Why Podcasts Work for Journalism

- **Intimacy** — voice creates personal connection
- **Emotion** — tone, pace, music carry feeling
- **Authenticity** — natural speech, ambient sound
- **Time-shifting** — listeners control when
- **Mobility** — consumed during commute, exercise

## Audio Newsworthiness Criteria

### Subject Suited for Audio
1. **Strong central character** with audible voice
2. **Vivid setting** that can be sonically captured (ambient sound)
3. **Clear narrative arc** — beginning, middle, end
4. **Conflict / tension** — drives listener attention
5. **Emotional truth** — voice reveals what print doesn't
6. **Surprise / mystery** — listeners stay for resolution
7. **Multiple voices** — dialogue, perspectives
8. **Time-rich subjects** — can develop over episodes

### Format Considerations
- **Sound quality** — recording matters
- **Ambient sound** present where possible
- **Voice variety** — multiple speakers
- **Music as emotional cue**
- **Pacing** — variation prevents monotony
- **Length** appropriate to subject

### Distribution Suitability
- **Episodic** — fits serial format
- **Binge-worthy** — encourages continued listening
- **Quotable** — clips for social
- **Niche-defining** — distinct subject matter

## Knight Foundation's Podcast Investment Criteria

- **Public-interest content**
- **Underserved communities**
- **New voices** (not just public radio veterans)
- **Sustainable models**
- **Trust-building potential**
- **Accessibility**
- **Diverse perspectives** — geographic, demographic

## Genre Examples and Their Criteria

- **Daily news pod** — timeliness, brevity, host voice
- **Investigative serial** — story development, mystery, characters
- **Explainer pod** — complex topics simplified
- **Interview show** — personality, expertise, format
- **Narrative documentary** — Wolfe-style scenes in audio

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria for Podcast Journalism

- **Audible voice** of subject
- **Sound-rich environment**
- **Personal story potential**
- **Long enough for development**
- **Universal theme** below specific story
- **Distinct host voice**
- **Listener time investment justified**
