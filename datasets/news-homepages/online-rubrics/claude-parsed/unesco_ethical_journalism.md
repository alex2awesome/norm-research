# UNESCO — Ethical Journalism Principles

UNESCO partners with the Ethical Journalism Network (EJN). Coalition of 60+ groups setting global ethics norms.

## EJN's Five Core Principles

1. **Truth and Accuracy** — Pursuit of accuracy is fundamental; truth is goal even when full truth unattainable.

2. **Independence** — Free from political, corporate, or cultural interest pressure.

3. **Fairness and Impartiality** — Multiple sides; balanced framing; absence of pre-judgment.

4. **Humanity** — Avoid causing harm; consider impact of words and images on others.

5. **Accountability** — Correct mistakes; explain decisions; engage with criticism.

## Implicit Newsworthiness Criteria (UNESCO/EJN)

- **Accuracy** as precondition for newsworthiness
- **Public interest** vs. mere curiosity
- **Independence** of source
- **Multi-perspectival** coverage
- **Avoiding harm** to vulnerable subjects
- **Counter-misinformation** value
- **Counter-hate speech** approach
- **Migration / refugee** dignity
- **Conflict sensitivity** in war zones
- **Diversity** of voices and topics

## Hate Speech Tests
EJN's 5-point test for whether reporting could constitute hate speech:
1. Speaker's status
2. Reach of statement
3. Objective and intent
4. Content and form
5. Context and surrounding climate

## Implicit Quality Criteria
- Universality (applies across cultures)
- Self-regulation over external censorship
- Professional standards as foundation
