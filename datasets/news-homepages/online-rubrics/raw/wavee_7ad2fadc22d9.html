NPPA
The National Press Photographers Association is a professional society that promotes the highest standards in visual journalism and informs people about public events.
Visual Journalist Role
Their primary role is to report significant events to the world.
Visual Journalist Goal
Faithful and comprehensive depiction of the subject at hand.
A visual journalist has responsibility to do what?
Document society and to preserve its history through images.
Photographic and video images can do the following:
Images can reveal great truths, expose wrongdoing and neglect, inspire hope and understanding and connect people around the globe through language of visual understanding.
What is true about photographs?
Photographs can do great harm if they are callously intrusive or manipulated.
What is the purpose of the National Press Photographers Association Code of Ethics (NPPA)?
The purpose of the NPPA Code of Ethics is to promote the highest quality in all forms of visual journalism and strengthen public confidence in the profession.
The NPPA Code of Ethics as an educational tool for who?
The NPPA Code of Ethics serves as an educational tool both for those who practice and for those who appreciate photojournalism.
When representing subjects visual journalists should be what?
Visual journalists should be accurate and comprehensive.
Photojournalists should be this when photographing or recording subjects.
Complete and provide context.
Photojournalist should avoid doing this.
Stereotyping individuals or groups.
Photojournalist should strive to do this
Capture the just the facts in an unbiased manner.
Photojournalist should treat all subjects in what manner?
Treat all subjects with respect, dignity and compassion.
When should a photojournalist intrude on victims of crime or tragedy?
Intrude on private moments of grief only when the public has an overriding and justifiable need to see.
When photographing subjects do not do any of the following:
Intentionally contribute to, alter or seek to alter or influence events.
