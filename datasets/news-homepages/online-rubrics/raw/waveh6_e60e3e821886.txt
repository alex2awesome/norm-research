ways of narrating the event. It Is argued that journalists' beliefs about 
the appropriate format, style, and content for a news story work In concert

ginning readers, and It Is hypothesized that children who are exposed pri-

no paragraphing, relations Inexplicit between events recounted) may fall 
to become good readers because they do not learn to expect connection 
among ideas, and not expecting It, trequently fall to see It, even In 
better-written texts.
