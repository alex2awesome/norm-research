Resources

The Ethical Journalism Network produces resources for working journalists, media trainers and journalism students focusing on how to improve media ethics and ethical communications in the public sphere. On our resources page, you will find our in-depth reports, infographics on hate speech and migration coverage, videos on a wide range of ethical issues, as well as our podcasts and presentations.
