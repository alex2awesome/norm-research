About Us

Longreads, founded in 2009 by Mark Armstrong, is dedicated to finding and sharing the best longform nonfiction storytelling on the web. We publish personal and reported essays, criticism, reading lists, and occasional book excerpts, interviews, and more in-depth features.

Longreads has been nominated for four National Magazine Awards (and won a 2020 ASME for Best Digital Illustration), has won a Canadian National Magazine Award (Gold), and has been cited for digital excellence by the Online News Association and Peabody Awards. Our pieces have won the Pushcart Prize, an ASJA Award, and have been included or notably mentioned in the Best American and Year’s Best anthologies across essays, food writing, science and nature writing, and sports writing. Our podcast series Bundyville, with Leah Sottile and Oregon Public Broadcasting, was named one of the best podcasts of 2018 and won an Edward R. Murrow Award in 2020.

Our stories are funded with support from our readers. For every dollar you contribute to the Longreads Story Fund, our parent company Automattic, matches it. Learn more about how you can contribute.

Longreads does not accept drafts/pitches or publish stories written using generative AI applications such as ChatGPT. We opt out of sharing our site content with third parties whenever possible, and have added all known AI crawlers to the “disallow” list in our robots.txt file.

Interested in writing for us? Visit our Submissions page for guidelines and more on what our editors look for.

Brendan Fitzgerald is a senior editor at Longreads, and previously served as senior editor at Columbia Journalism Review. His writing has appeared in Smithsonian, Literary Hub, and The Morning News, where he wrote the “Press Pause” column. Stories he has edited have won the Mirror Award for Best Commentary and the Los Angeles Press Club’s top prize for minority reporting, and been shortlisted for the Bart Richards Award in Media Criticism. He received his MFA in creative nonfiction from the University of Montana. You can reach him at brendan.fitzgerald@longreads.com.
