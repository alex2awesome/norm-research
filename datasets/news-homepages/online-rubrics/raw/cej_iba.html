iVerify Pakistan

Let’s combat misinformation together via iVerify Pakistan’s tool.

iVerify is a non-partisan fact-checking initiative of the Center for Excellence in Journalism (CEJ) at the Institute of Business Administration University in Karachi, Pakistan, supported by the United Nations Development Programme.

In an age of misinformation and self-censorship, public interest journalism has never been more vital to promoting peace and prosperity and holding power to account. At the World Press Freedom Day event hosted by the Center for Excellence in Journalism at IBA Karachi (IBA-CEJ), journalists, editors, and media advocates warned that threats to journalism are increasingly shifting from physical violence to legal, regulatory, and economic repression.

World Press Freedom Day: Journalists warn of a shift from violence to legal controls on media

CEJ convened the launch of Report: Truth Denied How Pakistani Authorities Built an Unsolvable Case at IBA Karachi, highlighting systemic gaps in journalist protection through the case of Zakir Hussain Dahar. Speakers emphasized the urgent need for accountability, justice, and stronger safeguards to ensure the safety of journalists across Pakistan.

Truth Denied: Report on Impunity for Slain Journalists Launched

As an academic partner for Climate Week Karachi 2026, CEJ hosted the opening ceremony of Pakistan’s first Climate Week and co hosted a two day Green Journalism Workshop with the Climate Action Center and PRIED. Through this partnership, CEJ advanced journalism that informs public debate, deepens understanding, and supports climate action.

Climate and Conflict Displacement Reporting Workshop

The Center for Excellence in Journalism at IBA partnered with AidData to host a specialized training programme on data journalism and development finance in Karachi. The programme brought together 25 journalists for an intensive week of learning focused on strengthening their ability to use data and analysis in reporting.

Strengthening Data Journalism: CEJ and AidData Train Journalists in Karachi
