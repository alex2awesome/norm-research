WHO ARE WE?

The Society for News Design (SND) is an international organization for news media professionals and visual communicators – specifically those who create print/web/mobile publications and products.

Leadership

SND is a volunteer-run organization with industry leaders dedicating their time and expertise to the advancement of visual storytelling around the world.

FUTURE OF VISUAL STORYTELLING

We look to enhance communication through excellence in visual journalism and design.

From the beginning, education has always been important to SND. Our student chapters bring students and educators together in a campus community where they can learn from industry leaders. Student chapter members are able to host events, receive travel grants to workshops, scholarships and benefit from discounts from SND on membership and at SND events

COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY & INCLUSION

Our members represent a range of experiences, backgrounds, platforms,  geographical locations and points of view. It is important to SND that the voices of all our members are represented and celebrated. We aspire to create an organization where equality and inclusion is achievable; where all our members have equal opportunity to learn, grow and thrive. The power of our differences, brought together through our beliefs in design, journalistic values and truth, enable us to build a stronger community and world. We are committed to the work to make this a reality.

SOCIETY FOR NEWS DESIGN

The Society for News Design (SND) is an international organization for news media professionals and visual communicators who create print, web, mobile publications and products. Our members art direct, design, edit, report, illustrate, make photos and video, visualize data and write code.
