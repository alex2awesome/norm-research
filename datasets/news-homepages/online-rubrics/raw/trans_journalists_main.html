Level Up Your Career

The Level Up Program is a stipend from the Trans Journalists Association for journalist members to attend professional development opportunities. Stipends in the amount of $250 and $500 are available. Spring 2026 applications will open Monday, April 6, 2026. The application cycle will close Tuesday, May 5, 2026 at 9a PT.
