From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
This category is often contrasted with Misinformation.

Articles relating to disinformation, false information spread deliberately to deceive. The English word disinformation is a loan translation of the Russian dezinformatsiya, derived from the title of a KGB black propaganda department.
