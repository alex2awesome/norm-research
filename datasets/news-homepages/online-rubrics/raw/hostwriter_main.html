About

Stories cross borders - and journalists should, too.

Hostwriter is an award-winning network that helps journalists to easily collaborate across borders. We support journalists at all career levels to raise the quality of media coverage by providing local contacts around the globe. We organize networking opportunites, host an online platform for journalists and publishers and provide training and capacity building activites. All Hostwriter members provide work samples by way of professional verification. Membership is free of charge.

What we do

Our Projects

Hostwriter implements projects that align with our mission- powering the future of collaborative journalism. Our projects focus on networking, training, and shifting power dynamics in the field. We see cross-border journalism as a tool to overcome national bias and prejudice, contributing toward better informed, more accountable and democratic societies.
