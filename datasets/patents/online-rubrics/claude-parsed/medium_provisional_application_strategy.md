# The Provisional Patent Application Myth: What It Actually Buys You

## What a Provisional Is Not

A provisional is not a patent. It is not examined. It cannot be enforced. It does not, by itself, give you the right to mark "patent pending" on something it does not actually disclose. It does not extend your patent term - the 20-year clock starts from the non-provisional filing.

## What a Provisional Is

A provisional is a priority-date placeholder. For 12 months, you can file a non-provisional that "claims priority" back to the provisional. Prior art that surfaces between the provisional and non-provisional dates does not count against you - but only for subject matter actually disclosed in the provisional.

## The Disclosure Trap

Most provisionals are filed too thin. A common pattern: founder writes a 4-page narrative description, files it, then in month 11 spends $8,000 on a "real" non-provisional with claims and figures. When the examiner cites a reference from month 6, the founder discovers the new non-provisional claims are not supported by the thin provisional - so priority is lost on those claims.

## Rule: Treat Provisional Disclosure As If It Were a Non-Provisional Spec

Every claim element you might one day want should appear, in some form, in the provisional. Include figures. Include alternative embodiments. Include numerical ranges. Include the "Why this is novel" framing. The cost of writing a fat provisional is small compared to the cost of losing priority.

## Multiple Provisionals Are Cheap

You can file additional provisionals during the 12-month window and consolidate them all into a single non-provisional that claims priority to all of them. If your invention evolves between months 1 and 11, file an updated provisional at each milestone.

## Use the Provisional Year to Test the Market

Twelve months is exactly long enough to (a) build a prototype, (b) get customer feedback, (c) learn what really matters, and (d) draft the non-provisional with that knowledge. Many founders waste it.

## The 12-Month Cliff Is Hard

Miss the 12-month deadline by one day and you lose priority forever. There is no extension. Calendar it in three places.

## Foreign Filing Implications

The 12-month deadline is also the Paris Convention deadline for foreign filings. If you want patents in Europe, Japan, or China, you must file PCT or direct national applications by the same date.
