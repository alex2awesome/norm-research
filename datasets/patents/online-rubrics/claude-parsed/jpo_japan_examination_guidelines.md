# JPO Japan Patent Office Examination Guidelines

## Overview

The English texts of the Examination Guidelines for Patent and Utility Model in Japan are provided by the Japan Patent Office. The Examination Guidelines basically apply to applications filed on or after July 1, 1995.

## Available Resources

The JPO provides:
- Main Examination Guidelines
- Examination Guidelines Outline
- Examination Handbook for Patent and Utility Model
- AI/IoT specific examination guidance
- Life sciences sector examination guidance

## Key Sections

The guidelines cover:
- **Prior Art Search and Determination of Novelty**
- **Inventive Step** (analogous to KSR/EPO problem-solution but distinct)
- **Notice of Reasons for Refusal** and Handling of Written Opinion
- **Written Amendment** procedures
- **Disclosure Requirements** (analogous to enablement)
- **Statement of Claims** (clarity requirements)
- **Industrial Applicability**

## Inventive Step Determination at JPO

JPO assesses inventive step considering:
- The level of skill of a person ordinarily skilled in the art
- Comparison with the most similar prior art
- Whether the differences could be easily made by combining cited prior art
- Motivation/incentive in the cited art
- Suggestions or teachings within the cited art
- Common technical knowledge in the relevant field

## Specific Topics

- Software-related inventions guidelines (revised for AI/ML)
- Business method patents
- Medical and pharmaceutical inventions
- Plant and animal patents (different from EPO)
- Treatment methods (excluded for human body, similar to EPO)

## Patent Application Examination Process

Japan operates a deferred examination system: applicants must request examination within 3 years of filing.
