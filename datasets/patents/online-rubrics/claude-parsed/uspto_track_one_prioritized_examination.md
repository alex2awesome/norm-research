# USPTO Track One Prioritized Examination

## Overview

Track One prioritized examination allows applicants to get a final disposition within about twelve months. Available for utility and plant patent applications.

## Key Benefits

- Special status with fewer requirements than the accelerated examination program
- No pre-examination search required
- FY 2023: average final disposition in just over 5 months (vs. 25-month wait for standard applications)
- Higher allowance rates than standard examination

## Filing Requirements

- Available for a fee at the time of filing an original utility or plant application
- Application limited to 4 independent claims and 30 total claims
- Must be filed electronically through Patent Center
- Single request may also be granted for an RCE in a plant or utility application

## Fees (2025)

- Track One prioritized examination fee: $4,200.00
- Small entity: $1,680
- Micro entity: $840

## Eligibility and Limits

- Beginning July 8, 2025: USPTO will increase the annual limit from 15,000 to 20,000 in a fiscal year
- First-come, first-served basis
- Available for Continuing Applications (continuations and divisionals)

## Important Considerations

Any request for an extension of time, including for responding to a pre-examination notice, will cause the application to be **ineligible** for further treatment under the prioritized examination program.

A request for an extension of time prior to the grant of prioritized examination status will prevent such status from being granted.

## Response Time Limits

Applicants under Track One must:
- Respond to office actions promptly (no extensions)
- Avoid actions that delay prosecution
- Maintain compliance with claim count limits

## Strategic Use Cases

- Time-sensitive technology (rapidly evolving fields)
- Imminent product launches
- Investor due diligence requirements
- Threat of competitor entry
- Technology with short commercial life
