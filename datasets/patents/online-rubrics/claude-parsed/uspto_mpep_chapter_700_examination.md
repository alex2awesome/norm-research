# MPEP Chapter 700: Examination of Applications - Table of Contents

## Main Sections Overview

Chapter 700 establishes the foundational procedures for patent application examination, covering statutory authority through final actions and appeals.

### Core Examination Framework

**Sections 701-704** establish examination authority and prerequisites:
- Statutory authority for examination (§701)
- Application requisites (§702)
- Search and information requirements (§704)

**Sections 705-707** address examination workflow:
- Patentability reports (§705)
- Claim rejections (§706)
- Examiner's letters and citations (§707)

### Key Procedural Sections

**Claim Rejection Procedures (§706)** covers:
- Prior art rejections and non-prior art rejections
- Previously allowed claims and rejection after allowance
- Final rejections with proper timing requirements

**Final Rejection Practice (§706.07)** specifies:
- When final rejection is appropriate on second action
- Proper circumstances for first action finals
- "Transitional After-Final Practice" and "Request for Continued Examination (RCE) Practice"

### Response and Amendment Rules

**Amendment Procedures (§714)** mandate:
- Amendments must be "fully responsive" to office actions
- Signature requirements for amendments
- Claims added in excess of previously paid amounts trigger fees

**Special Circumstances** include:
- Amendments after allowance under 37 CFR 1.312
- Amendments filed after reply period expiration
- Entry procedures and partial entry scenarios

### Additional Critical Sections

- **§710**: Period for reply (statutory periods, shortened periods, extensions)
- **§711**: Application abandonment (express, constructive, and revival)
- **§713**: Interview procedures and restrictions
- **§715-718**: Affidavits and declarations to overcome rejections
- **§719**: File wrapper maintenance requirements
