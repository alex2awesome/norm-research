## Key Design Patent Requirements

- Design patents consist of drawings, drawing descriptions, and a single claim
- A brief narrative is optional
- Design patents have a 15-year term from issuance (formerly 14)
- The analogous art for designs is the field for the actual types of design involved, not the field for the functional part of the invention
- The ornamentation has to actually have been made for the purpose of being decorative, and cannot just be a byproduct of the functional design
- Broken lines may be used to show visible environmental structure but may not show hidden planes
- Solid lines define the claimed design; broken lines show unclaimed environment
