# MPEP 2173: Claims Must Particularly Point Out and Distinctly Claim the Invention

## Primary Purpose and Requirements

MPEP 2173 establishes standards for claim definiteness under 35 U.S.C. 112(b). The core requirement is that claims must "particularly point out and distinctly claim the invention" to provide clear public notice of what constitutes patent infringement.

## Test for Definiteness

The fundamental test asks: **Would a person of ordinary skill in the art understand the claim scope when read in light of the specification?**

Key principles:

- **Broadest Reasonable Interpretation**: During prosecution, claims receive their broadest reasonable construction consistent with the specification
- **Clarity Standard**: Claims must inform those skilled in the art with "reasonable certainty" about invention boundaries
- **Context Matters**: Claim terms are evaluated based on the specification, prior art, and ordinary skill level in the field

## Common Indefinite Phrases and Limitations

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Terms of Degree | "substantially," "approximately," "comparable" |
| Relative Terms | "lightweight," "high-speed," "similar" |
| Subjective Language | "aesthetically pleasing," "superior" (without objective standards) |
| Vague Approximations | "about," "essentially," "roughly" |
| Unclear References | "said lever" with no antecedent basis |
| Functional Claims | Results-only language describing problems solved, not structure |

These are **fact-specific**—not automatic per se rejections.

## How Examiners Reject for Indefiniteness

**Procedural Requirements:**

1. **Clear Record Establishment**: Examiners must identify the specific indefinite term and explain in detail why boundaries are unclear

2. **Prima Facie Case**: The Office action must provide sufficient information for applicants to meaningfully respond, including:
   - The exact problematic language
   - Detailed reasoning showing ambiguity
   - Where practicable, suggestions for resolution

3. **Adequate Explanation**: Examiners should avoid mere conclusions and instead demonstrate how the scope becomes unclear

## Response Options for Applicants

- Amend claims to clarify language
- Provide persuasive explanation with evidence that ordinary skilled artisans would understand the term
- Submit declarations or technical dictionary references supporting claim interpretation

## Best Practices

The guidance encourages examiners to initiate interviews before formal rejection when indefiniteness is the sole issue, facilitating resolution through discussion rather than adversarial prosecution.
