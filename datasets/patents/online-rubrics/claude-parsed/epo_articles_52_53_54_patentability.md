# EPO Articles 52, 53, 54 - Patentability Exclusions and Novelty

## Article 52 EPC - Patentable Inventions and Exclusions

European patents shall be granted for any inventions, in all fields of technology, provided that they are:
- New
- Involve an inventive step
- Susceptible of industrial application

### Article 52(2) - Excluded Subject Matter

The following are not regarded as inventions:
- Discoveries, scientific theories and mathematical methods
- Aesthetic creations
- Schemes, rules and methods for performing mental acts, playing games or doing business
- Programs for computers
- Presentations of information

### Article 52(3) - "As Such" Limitation

The exclusions in Article 52(2) apply only to the extent to which a European patent application or European patent relates to such subject-matter or activities **as such**. Computer programs combined with technical character (e.g., computer-implemented inventions) may be patentable.

## Article 53 EPC - Other Exclusions

Article 53 excludes from patentability:

### Article 53(a) - Morality
Inventions immoral or contrary to public order ("ordre public").

### Article 53(b) - Plants and Animals
Plant or animal varieties and essentially biological processes for their production. Microbiological processes and the products thereof are not excluded.

### Article 53(c) - Medical Methods
Methods for treatment of the human or animal body by surgery or therapy and diagnostic methods practised on the human or animal body.

**Important Exception**: This provision does NOT apply to products, in particular substances or compositions, for use in any of these methods. (Allows "Swiss-type" or "EPC 2000 second medical use" claims.)

## Article 54 EPC - Novelty

An invention is considered to be new if it does not form part of the **state of the art**.

### State of the Art (Article 54(2))

The state of the art shall be held to comprise everything made available to the public by:
- Written or oral description
- Use
- In any other way

Before the date of filing of the European patent application.

### Article 54(3) - Earlier-Filed, Later-Published

Extends the state of the art by also including the content of European patent applications as filed:
- With dates of filing prior to the date referred to in paragraph 2
- Which were published on or after that date

(Used only for novelty, not for inventive step assessment.)

## Article 54(4) and (5) - First and Further Medical Use

Allow purpose-limited product claims for known substances/compositions when used for new medical applications.
