# EPO Article 83 EPC - Sufficiency of Disclosure

## Overview

Article 83 EPC requires that the European patent application (or patent) shall disclose the invention in a manner sufficiently clear and complete for it to be carried out by a person skilled in the art.

## Key Requirements

To comply with Article 83 EPC, a European patent application must contain sufficient information to allow a person skilled in the art, using common general knowledge and the information provided in the application, to:
- Perceive the technical teaching inherent in the claimed invention
- Put it into effect accordingly

The disclosure must be reproducible without undue burden, and this must be true over the whole scope of the claim.

## Standard for Assessment

An objection of lack of sufficient disclosure presupposes that there are serious doubts, substantiated by verifiable facts. If the examining division can make out a reasoned case, the onus shifts to the applicant to establish that the invention may be performed and repeated over substantially the whole of the claimed range.

## Examples and Description

Rule 42(1)(e) EPC specifies that the description shall:
- Describe in detail at least one way of carrying out the invention claimed
- Use examples where appropriate
- Refer to the drawings, if any

The application must contain, in addition to examples, sufficient information to allow the skilled person, using common general knowledge, to perform the invention over the whole area claimed without undue burden and without needing inventive skill.

## Special Cases

**Plausibility**: Following G 2/21, technical effects relied upon for inventive step must be derivable from the application as filed; bald assertions are insufficient.

**Black-Box AI**: For AI-related inventions (T 1669/21), training data, model architecture, and hyperparameters may need to be disclosed if necessary to reproduce the technical effect.

**Biological Materials**: Where biological material cannot be sufficiently described, deposit at a recognized depository (Budapest Treaty) is required.

## Article 83 vs. Article 84 vs. Article 56

- Article 83: Whole disclosure enables practice (sufficiency)
- Article 84: Claims are clear and supported (clarity)
- Article 56: Invention is non-obvious (inventive step)
