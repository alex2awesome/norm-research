# CNIPA China Patent Examination Guidelines

## Overview

The CNIPA Guidelines are roughly analogous to the U.S. Patent & Trademark Office's MPEP, providing the framework for examining Chinese patent applications.

## 2026 Revisions Effective January 1, 2026

On November 13, 2025, China's National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA) released revised Guidelines for Patent Examination, effective January 1, 2026.

### Key Areas Covered in Latest Revision

**AI and Big Data**
The amendment addresses emerging technology and business forms, covering:
- The legality and ethics of AI and Big Data applications
- Sufficient disclosure requirements for algorithm/data-related inventions
- Inventiveness assessment of AI/ML inventions

**Inventiveness Assessment**
The claimed technical solution should be subject to the inventiveness assessment test in its entirety, rather than a test of inventiveness of certain technical features.

**Dual Filing Restrictions**
When an applicant files an invention patent application and a parallel utility model application on the same day with mutual cross references ('Dual Filing'), the applicant must, upon receiving a notice of allowance for the invention patent, abandon the parallel utility model application.

**Invalidation Requirements**
A request for invalidation will not be accepted if it is not based on true intention of the petitioner. Reinforces good faith principles in Article 20 of the Chinese Patent Law.

## Three-Step Test for Inventiveness

CNIPA uses a three-step approach similar to EPO's problem-solution:
1. Determine the closest prior art
2. Determine the distinguishing features and the technical problem actually solved
3. Determine whether the claimed invention is obvious to a person skilled in the art

## Patentable Subject Matter Exclusions (Article 25)

- Scientific discoveries
- Rules and methods for mental activities
- Methods for diagnosis and treatment of diseases
- Animal and plant varieties
- Substances obtained by means of nuclear transformation
- Designs that are mainly used for marking patterns/colors/combinations on plane prints

## Utility Models in China

Distinct from invention patents, utility models cover product shape, structure, or combinations. Now (post-2024 amendments) subject to inventiveness examination, lower bar than invention patents.
