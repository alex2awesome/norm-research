# 37 CFR 1.84 - Standards for Patent Drawings

## Drawing Format and Medium

Black and white drawings are normally required, using India ink or its equivalent that secures solid black lines.

Color drawings are permitted in design applications, and on rare occasions, color drawings may be necessary as the only practical medium in utility patent applications, but must be of sufficient quality such that all details are reproducible in black and white in the printed patent.

## Photographs

Photographs are not ordinarily permitted in utility and design patent applications, but the Office will accept photographs if they are the only practicable medium for illustrating the claimed invention. Examples:
- Electrophoresis gels, blots, autoradiographs
- Cell cultures
- Histological tissue sections
- Animals, plants
- In vivo imaging
- Thin layer chromatography plates

## Identification and Margins

Identifying indicia must include:
- Title of the invention
- Inventor's name
- Application number or docket number
- Placed on the front of each sheet within the top margin

## Sheet Requirements

- **Paper size**: A4 (21.0 cm × 29.7 cm) or 8.5" × 11"
- **Margins** (minimum): 2.5 cm top, 2.5 cm left, 1.5 cm right, 1.0 cm bottom
- **Sheet numbering**: Two arabic numerals separated by oblique line: "1/4," "2/4"
- **Figure numbering**: Consecutive arabic numerals starting at 1

## Drawing Quality

- Lines must be black, well-defined
- Uniform in thickness
- Sharp lines without blurring or jagged edges
- Solid lines for claimed features
- Broken lines for unclaimed environment (in design patents) or hidden features
- Sufficient quality for reproduction at 2/3 size

## Views and Figures

- Sufficient views to show the invention completely
- One view suitable for inclusion on the front page
- Cross-sectional, perspective, exploded views as needed
- Reference characters consistent across views

## Lead Lines and Reference Numerals

- Lead lines for each reference numeral
- Reference numerals not less than 0.32 cm (1/8") in height
- Same parts in different views must use same reference characters
- Reference characters must appear in description

## Symbols and Hatching

- Standard graphical symbols (electrical, hydraulic, etc.)
- Cross-hatching to distinguish materials
- Specific patterns for different material types

## Common Drawing Rejection Reasons

- Insufficient quality (blurry, light, broken lines)
- Missing reference numerals
- Incorrect margins or numbering
- Color used without petition
- Photographs without justification
- Inconsistent reference characters between views and description
