# KIPO Korea Patent Examination Guidelines

## Overview

The "Patent Examination Guidelines" serve as a compass for patent examination, guiding patent examiners to conduct more accurate, consistent and unbiased examination at the Korean Intellectual Property Office (KIPO).

## Content and Structure

The guidelines cover:
- General Rules including Capacity
- Article 36 Patent Act of Korea
- Application Requirements
- Examination Methods
- Instructions on Examination
- Unpatentable Inventions

## International Harmonization

KIPO established 39 examination standards based on a comparative study of the IP5 offices (Korea, US, Europe, Japan, China). KIPO increased the number of application examples to 50 to help examiners better understand the standards.

## AI/Fourth Industrial Revolution Guidelines

KIPO announced Patent Examination Guidelines for key technology areas related to the Fourth Industrial Revolution, including machine learning based artificial intelligence, on January 18, 2021.

## Patentability Requirements (Korean Patent Act)

Article 29 (Industrial applicability, novelty, inventive step):
- An invention shall be industrially applicable
- Must not be publicly known/used domestically or in writing/electronic media before filing
- Must involve an inventive step over the publicly known art

## Unpatentable Inventions

Per Korean Patent Act:
- Inventions contrary to public order, morality, or public health
- Methods of treatment of the human body (similar to EPO)

## Inventive Step Standard

KIPO uses a similar test to other jurisdictions:
1. Determine the closest cited art
2. Identify differences
3. Examine whether differences could be easily made by combining cited prior art
4. Consider effects achieved by the invention

## Computer/Software Inventions

KIPO accepts software-related inventions if:
- The invention is a "creation of a technical idea using laws of nature"
- Software performs a technical processing
- Information processing is concretely realized using hardware

## Process Considerations

Korean prosecution typically:
- Faster than US/EPO (12-18 months to first action)
- Strict written argument requirements
- Limited examiner interview practice
- Appeals to Intellectual Property Trial and Appeal Board (IPTAB)
