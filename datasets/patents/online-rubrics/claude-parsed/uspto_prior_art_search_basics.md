# Prior Art Search Strategy

## Key Search Strategies

### Broad to Narrow Approach
The broad to narrow research strategy is the foundation of many successful patent searches:
1. Identify main concepts of an invention
2. Select a primary concept that all search results must have
3. Add secondary concepts to narrow results

## Essential Steps to Conduct a Prior Art Search

### 1. Define Your Invention
- Clear understanding of scope
- Key features, functionalities, and potential applications
- Identify relevant keywords and search terms

### 2. Develop Comprehensive Keywords
Account for all possible keyword combinations:
- Spelling variations (UK vs US English)
- Keywords in foreign languages
- Historical terms for different patent classifications
- Synonyms and related terms
- Different levels of abstraction

### 3. Utilize Multiple Databases

**Patent databases:**
- USPTO Patent Public Search (PPubs)
- Google Patents
- Espacenet (EPO worldwide)
- WIPO PatentScope
- Lens.org
- Patentscope
- DEPATISnet (DPMA)

**Non-patent literature:**
- Peer-reviewed publications
- Dissertations
- Conference presentations
- Trade publications
- Standards documents
- Web archives

### 4. Use Patent Classification Systems

- **CPC (Cooperative Patent Classification)** - used by USPTO, EPO
- **IPC (International Patent Classification)** - WIPO
- **USPC** - older U.S. classification (pre-2013)
- **F-terms / FI** - Japan-specific

### 5. Review and Document Results
Keep a search log to record:
- Search parameters
- Databases searched
- Date searched
- Results reviewed
- Inventor/assignee names searched

## Important Considerations

- Rejected, pending, and abandoned patent applications can also be considered prior art
- Citation analysis (forward and backward) is essential
- Inventor and assignee searches identify related work
- Family members across jurisdictions provide additional context

Incomplete searches can lead to costly mistakes in IP strategy, given the significant amount of money invested in pre-market research and development.
