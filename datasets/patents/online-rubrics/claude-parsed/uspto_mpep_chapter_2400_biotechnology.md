# MPEP Chapter 2400: Biotechnology Table of Contents

## Biological Deposit Rules (Sections 2401-2411)

- **2401-2403**: Introduction and deposit of biological material, including "Material Capable of Self-Replication" and plant material specifications
- **2404**: Circumstances requiring deposits, covering materials "Known and Readily Available to the Public" and those producible "Without Undue Experimentation"
- **2405-2410**: Acceptable depositories, timing, replacement protocols, term duration, viability standards, and sample furnishing conditions
- **2411**: "Examination Procedures" addressing rejections based on deposit issues and applications ready for allowance except for deposit deficiencies

## Sequence Listing Requirements (Sections 2412-2435)

- **2412-2415**: XML-format sequence listing rules under WIPO Standard ST.26, including nucleotide/amino acid definitions, format specifications, and applicability dates
- **2416-2419**: Form paragraphs, compliance guidance, WIPO tools, and publishing procedures for patent grants
- **2420-2435**: Alternative WIPO ST.25 ASCII text requirements, symbols, content standards, and handling of lengthy sequence listings

## Key Examination Provisions

When the invention involves a biological material and words alone cannot sufficiently describe how to make and use the invention in a reproducible manner, access to the biological material may be necessary for the satisfaction of the statutory requirements for patentability under 35 U.S.C. 112.

Biological material includes material that is capable of self-replication whether directly or indirectly. Examples: bacteria, fungi, algae, protozoa, eukaryotic cells, cell lines, hybridomas, plasmids, viruses, plant tissue cells, lichens and seeds.

The examiner shall determine in each application for patent if a deposit is needed, and if needed, if a deposit actually made is acceptable for patent purposes. If a deposit is needed and has not been made or replaced, the examiner shall reject the affected claims under the appropriate provision of 35 U.S.C. 112.
