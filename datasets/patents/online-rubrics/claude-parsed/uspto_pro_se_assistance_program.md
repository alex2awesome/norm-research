# USPTO Pro Se Assistance Program: Guide for Independent Patent Filers

## Program Overview

The Pro Se Assistance Program supports independent inventors and small businesses filing patent applications without legal representation. The program provides outreach and educational resources to help "pro se" applicants—those filing independently—make informed decisions and improve application quality.

## Essential Filing Requirements

Before submitting, pro se applicants need:

- **Specification** (detailed written description of the invention)
- **Claims** (defining the scope of protection sought)
- **Drawings** (when necessary for understanding)
- **Abstract** and title of the invention
- **Appropriate filing fees** (varying by application type)
- **Executed Oath or Declaration** (establishing inventorship)

## Application Types Available

**Utility Patents:** For new, useful processes, machines, manufactures, or compositions of matter

**Design Patents:** For new, original ornamental designs for articles of manufacture

**Plant Patents:** For new plant varieties that are asexually reproduced

**Provisional Applications:** Placeholder applications establishing early filing dates (not examined; must file nonprovisional within 12 months)

## Critical Patentability Requirements

For utility patents, inventions must be:

- **New:** Not identically disclosed in prior art
- **Non-obvious:** Meaningfully different from existing technology
- **Useful:** Accomplishes something with "specific, substantial, and credible" utility

Cannot be patented: Laws of nature, abstract ideas, mere ideas without complete description, or literary/artistic works.

## Pre-Filing Recommendations

Conduct a **prior art search** to determine whether similar inventions already exist. The USPTO provides:

- Public Search Facility in Alexandria, Virginia
- Patent and Trademark Resource Centers at libraries nationwide
- Web-based tutorials and search strategy guides

## Amendment Process Guidance

**For specifications:** Submit marked-up copies showing additions (underlined) and deletions (struck through), plus clean versions

**For claims:** Indicate status of each claim—Original, Currently amended, New, Canceled, etc.

**For drawings:** Submit replacement sheets labeled clearly, explaining all changes in detail

## Important Reminders

Patent pending protection only applies after filing—provisional applications provide "no actual patent protection." Pro se applicants frequently make errors on forms; the USPTO emphasizes reviewing guidance materials and seeking assistance before submitting applications.

The Patent Trial and Appeal Board handles appeals if examiners reject applications, and inventors can request interviews with examiners during prosecution.
