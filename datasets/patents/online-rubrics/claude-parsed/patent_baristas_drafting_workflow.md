# Patent Baristas: Drafting Workflow from Invention Disclosure to Filing

## Phase 1 - Invention Capture (Days 1-3)

Sit with the inventor. Walk through the invention from problem to solution. Take notes. Sketch on a whiteboard. The output of this phase is an invention disclosure that includes:
- Problem statement (technical, not commercial)
- Closest known prior solutions and their shortcomings
- The new solution explained step by step
- Alternative implementations and variations
- Any test data or performance results

## Phase 2 - Prior Art Search (Days 3-10)

A professional search of patents and non-patent literature in the relevant field. The search report should identify the 5-10 closest references with detailed analysis of which claim elements each one discloses.

## Phase 3 - Claim Strategy (Days 10-12)

Based on the search results, decide:
- What is the broadest claim scope that distinguishes over the closest art?
- What are the key narrowing dimensions for dependent claims?
- What claim types (method, system, apparatus, CRM) make sense for this invention?
- What is the overall portfolio architecture (one filing, or a family with multiple filings)?

## Phase 4 - Independent Claim Drafting (Days 12-15)

Draft 3-4 independent claims of varying scope. The broadest is the moonshot; the narrowest is the picture claim. Cross-check each independent claim against the closest art to confirm distinguishing elements.

## Phase 5 - Dependent Claims (Days 15-18)

Build a ladder of dependent claims that systematically narrow on different dimensions. Each rung should be independently meaningful, not a trivial restatement.

## Phase 6 - Specification Drafting (Days 18-30)

Walk through the figures one at a time. Describe what each shows, how the components interact, and what alternatives exist. Ensure every claim term has antecedent support in the spec. Include "in some embodiments" framings throughout.

## Phase 7 - Drawings (Days 25-30)

Engage a patent draftsperson to convert engineering sketches into formal patent drawings (37 CFR 1.84 compliant). Each reference number in the spec should appear in at least one figure.

## Phase 8 - Review (Days 30-35)

Inventor reviews for technical accuracy. Senior attorney reviews for legal sufficiency. Run through a checklist: antecedent basis, consistent terminology, claim ladder, embodiment count, no patent profanity, IDS prepared.

## Phase 9 - Filing (Day 35)

File electronically through Patent Center. Submit specification, claims, abstract, drawings, oath/declaration, application data sheet, and IDS. Pay filing fees.

## Phase 10 - Post-Filing Calendar

Calendar: 12-month foreign filing deadline, 18-month publication, expected first office action date, deadlines for any continuations to be filed before parent issuance.

## Quality Checks Throughout

At each phase, ask: does this still distinguish over the closest art? does the spec still support the claims? would I be comfortable with this in litigation? does this leave room for design-around?
