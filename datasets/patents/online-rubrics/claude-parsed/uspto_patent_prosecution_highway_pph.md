# USPTO Patent Prosecution Highway (PPH) Program

## What is PPH?

The Patent Prosecution Highway (PPH) speeds up the examination process for corresponding applications filed in participating intellectual property offices.

Under PPH, when an applicant receives a ruling from a first patent office that at least one claim is allowable, the applicant may request fast track examination of corresponding claim(s) in a corresponding patent office.

## Key Benefits

1. **Speed**: First substantive Office action received in about 2-5 months (vs. typical 18-24 months)
2. **Cost-Effective**: No added USPTO fee for filing PPH request
3. **Higher Success Rates**: ~10% higher allowance rates for PPH applications
4. **Reduced office actions**: Average fewer rounds of prosecution

## Current Statistics

- PPH applications represent ~2% of USPTO filings
- Average first action pendency: ~7.5 months for PPH
- Average pendency for standard applications: 18-24 months

## Participating Offices

### Global PPH
Larger network covering many participating patent offices.

### IP5 PPH
Five major offices:
- Japan (JPO)
- Korea (KIPO)
- US (USPTO)
- European Patent Office (EPO)
- China (SIPO/CNIPA)

## How to Use PPH

### Requirements:
1. Same earliest priority date in both applications
2. Office of first examination has determined at least one claim allowable
3. Claims in second office must "sufficiently correspond" to allowable claims
4. Office actions and search results from first office available

### Filing Steps:
1. File PPH request with the second office
2. Submit copies of office actions from first office
3. Submit copies of allowed claims from first office
4. Provide claim correspondence table
5. Submit search information

## Strategic Considerations

### When to Use PPH
- When you have a quickly granted European Search Report (EESR)
- After a positive Australia or Israel search result
- When PCT international preliminary examination found claims allowable
- For technology with short commercial life

### When NOT to Use PPH
- When initial application has narrow claims you don't want
- When you want to delay examination
- When initial application was incompletely examined

## PCT-PPH

PCT international applications can use PCT-PPH:
- Based on positive Written Opinion or International Preliminary Report on Patentability
- Avoids waiting for national-stage examination delays

## Comparison with Other Acceleration Options

| Program | Timeline | Cost | Requirements |
|---------|----------|------|--------------|
| PPH | First action ~5 months | $0 (no fee) | Allowable claims in another office |
| Track One | ~12 months total | $4,200 | <30 claims |
| Accelerated Examination | ~12 months | $140 | Pre-exam search + statement |
| First Action Interview | Varies | $0 | Within 60 days of FAOM |

## Limitations

- Only works with participating offices
- Requires correspondence of claims (cannot have claims dramatically different)
- Initial office of examination must conclude examination favorably first
- Quality of underlying foreign examination affects PPH outcome
