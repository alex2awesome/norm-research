# Unity of Invention under PCT

## Basic Requirement

Under the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT), an international application "shall relate to one invention only or to a group of inventions so linked as to form a 'single general inventive concept'" (PCT Rule 13).

## Test for Unity

Where a group of inventions is claimed in one and the same international application, the requirement of unity of invention shall be fulfilled only when there is a technical relationship among those inventions involving one or more of the same or corresponding **special technical features**.

A "special technical feature" is one that defines a contribution which each of the claimed inventions makes over the prior art.

## A Posteriori Lack of Unity

Lack of unity may exist:
- **A priori**: Apparent before considering prior art
- **A posteriori**: Becoming apparent only after considering prior art (the "shared feature" turns out to be known)

## Consequences of Lacking Unity

If the requirement of unity of invention is not met, the International Searching Authority (ISA) is entitled to:
1. Request the applicant to pay an additional search fee for each invention beyond the first
2. Limit the international search to the invention first mentioned in the claims (if no additional fee paid)

## Response Options - Protest Procedure

After payment of additional search fees, the applicant may file a protest:
1. Reasoned statement explaining why unity is met OR additional fees are excessive
2. Pay protest fee
3. Reviewed by review body
4. If protest justified, partial/total reimbursement ordered

## Differences from US Restriction Practice

| Feature | US Restriction (35 USC 121) | PCT Unity of Invention |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Independent and distinct + serious burden | Single general inventive concept (special technical feature) |
| Mechanism | Election among groups | Pay additional search fees |
| Recourse | Petition | Protest with refund possibility |
| Scope | Searches one invention | Can search multiple if fees paid |

## Common Categories of Claims (PCT)

Under PCT, certain combinations are presumed to satisfy unity:
- Product + process for making it + apparatus for making + use of product
- Multiple products with shared "special technical feature"
- Combinations with corresponding contributions

## Strategic Considerations

- For US-bound PCT, plan claims structure to minimize unity objections
- Consider whether to prosecute multiple inventions abroad in same case
- US national stage applications use unity standard, NOT US restriction standard
