# Indian Patent Office Examination Guidelines

## Overview

The Indian Patent Office (IPO) has issued Revised Guidelines for Examination of Computer Related Inventions (CRI) 2025 on July 29, 2025.

## CRI 2025 Guidelines Highlights

### Coverage of New Technologies
The 2025 version introduces structured tests for traditionally complex exclusions:
- Algorithms
- Business methods
- Mathematical methods
- Computer programs

A dedicated section on cutting-edge technologies including:
- AI / Machine learning / Deep learning
- Blockchain
- Quantum computing

### Case Law Integration
The CRI 2025 Guidelines incorporate 19 case laws offering different perspectives on dealing with CRIs, with most cases being from recent years reflecting evolving judicial interpretation.

## Indian Patent Act Section 3 - Non-Patentable Inventions

Includes:
- **Section 3(d)**: New form of known substance not enhancing efficacy (especially impactful for pharma)
- **Section 3(k)**: Mathematical methods, business methods, computer programs per se, algorithms
- **Section 3(b)**: Inventions contrary to public order/morality
- **Section 3(c)**: Discovery of scientific principles
- **Section 3(i)**: Methods of treatment of humans/animals
- **Section 3(j)**: Plants and animals (other than microorganisms)

## Examination Procedure for Computer-Related Inventions

The IPO directs examiners to:
- Focus on whether the invention provides a concrete technical solution using technical means
- Assess technical contribution (over format of claims)
- Distinguish CRIs from purely abstract algorithms
- Examine whether claims provide "technical contribution"

## Key Examination Criteria

For computer-implemented inventions:
1. **Technical character**: Must contribute beyond merely executing on a computer
2. **Concrete technical solution**: Should solve a real-world technical problem
3. **Hardware integration**: Should be tied to specific hardware features
4. **Format-agnostic analysis**: Substance over form

## Section 3(d) - Pharmaceutical Patents

Critical for pharma: New forms of known substances are not patentable unless they show enhanced efficacy.

This affects:
- Polymorphs, salts, esters of known drugs
- Combinations of known compounds
- New formulations of existing actives

## Indian Application Process

- Examination request must be filed within 48 months of priority date
- Pre-grant opposition allowed
- Post-grant opposition within 12 months of grant
- Opposition Board reviews oppositions
- Working Statement (Form 27) required annually

## Fast-Track Examination

Available for:
- Indian applicants
- Female applicants
- Government departments
- Small entities and startups
- Eligible international applicants under PPH

## Strategic Considerations for India

- Section 3(d) requires significant data showing efficacy improvement
- Computer programs "per se" excluded — need technical effect
- Method-of-treatment claims excluded
- Pre-grant opposition can be triggered by competitors during examination
