# MPEP Chapter 1200: Appeal - Table of Contents

## Core Appeal Procedures

The chapter begins with foundational sections on "Introduction" and "Composition of Board," followed by "Administrative Handling" requirements for initiating appeals through a "Notice of Appeal."

## Appeal Brief Requirements

Applicants must file an appeal brief within specified timeframes. The regulations address "Appeal Brief Content" standards and procedures for handling "Non-Compliant Appeal Brief and Amended Brief" submissions.

## Evidence and Amendments

The manual covers "Amendments and Affidavits or Other Evidence Filed With or After Appeal," establishing rules for supplementing the record during appellate proceedings.

## Examiner Response and Board Proceedings

The "Examiner's Answer" section details how examiners respond to appeals, including provisions for "Appeal Conference," answer contents, and procedures for introducing "New Ground of Rejection." The chapter addresses "Reply Briefs and Fee for Forwarding Appeal" and "Oral Hearing" options.

## Board Actions and Decisions

Sections detail actions "Subsequent to Examiner's Answer but Before Board's Decision," including remand procedures, board requirements for appellant responses, and the "Decision by Board" process with provisions for "New Grounds of Rejection."

## Post-Decision Procedures

The manual concludes with procedures following board decisions, including rehearing, reversal scenarios, claim cancellation, reopening of prosecution, appeal withdrawal/dismissal options, and "Judicial Review" through federal courts.

## Who Can Appeal

An applicant for a patent, any of whose claims has been twice rejected, may appeal from the decision of the primary examiner to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board, having once paid the fee for such appeal. A patent owner in a reexamination may appeal from the final rejection of any claim by the primary examiner to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board.

## PTAB Pro Bono Program

Applicants who meet certain financial thresholds may be eligible for free legal assistance through the PTAB Pro Bono Program, which matches volunteer patent professionals with financially under-resourced applicants for ex parte appeals.
