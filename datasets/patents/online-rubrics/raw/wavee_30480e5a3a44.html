Effective patent drafting with Rowan Patents

Create quality patents with unmatched speed and consistency. Access comprehensive AI assistance designed to enhance your expertise at every step.

Quality and consistency

Create quality patent applications faster with integrated claims, specification, and drawings in one purpose-built solution that helps ensure consistency and compliance with diverse drafting requirements.

Integrated efficiency

Streamline and expedite the iterative patent drafting process with revision automation and task-based GenAI assistance.

AI-driven flexibility

Gain flexibility and control with advanced AI technology that allows practitioners to tailor their patent drafting process to meet specific needs and preferences. 

Features

Rowan Patents supports unique workflows for application preparation, review, and prosecution in one desktop application

Seamless patent drafting

The integrated solution that includes all the elements of a patent application – specification, claims and drawings – reducing the time practitioners spend on laborious tasks such as part numbering, tracking claim support, and maintaining consistency throughout the application.

Product features:

  • Leverage GenAI to assist in drafting select parts of the application through your choice of local or cloud-based language models.
  • Import, define and manage your key concepts and terminology consistently throughout the application leveraging the Terms Manager feature.
  • Access the built-in drawing tool, designed for the unique needs of patent application drawings including numbering synchronized with other parts of the application.
  • Ensure consistency and save time with automatic part numbering.
  • Pre-configured templates, and the Drafting Library accelerate drafting and ensure compliance with client guidelines and quality standards.

Proofread your patents with ease

Gain valuable insight into how your application will be received and avoid issues that can cause rejections during patent office examination. Review analyzes consistency of terms in draft applications, published applications, and amended claims for error checking and predictive analysis to improve efficiency and outcomes in patent prosecution.

Product features:

  • Check term usage across claims, specification and figures to ensure consistency across draft application.
  • Leverage Natural Language Processing optimized for patent language to predict art units, potential 112 issues, drawing parts lists, claim trees and more.
  • Check for antecedent basis errors, claim support, profanity lists and acronyms.
Request a demo

Respond with speed and precision

Streamline patent prosecution and strategically address examiner rejections with industry leading drafting capabilities that brings automation, improved efficiency, and enhanced consistency to Office action responses. Save valuable time and help a prosecutor produce strong responses, faster.

Product features:

  • Automate shell package generation, including identifying examiner rejections and citing prior art, to inform a response strategy.
  • Develop a response strategy with an interactive comparison of issues, claims, examiner remarks and cited art leveraging the Rejection Navigator.
  • Amend your claims with automated, USPTO markup styling, claim status rollover, and claim status inserts. Automatically synchronize amendments to parallel claim elements. Validate that amendments are supported in the specification.
  • Respond to rejections using a library of boilerplate text inserts, plus many other automations.
  • View bibliographic data, predictive examination analytics and file history.

The integrated nature of the Rowan Patent solution makes it unique in the market and is instrumental to how Rowan delivers improved efficiency, consistency and quality.

Rowan works through the entire lifecycle of patent application preparation and prosecution. The Drafting module creates a complete application, the Review module analyzes a draft for errors and consistency, and the Prosecution module assists in the preparation of Office action responses.
