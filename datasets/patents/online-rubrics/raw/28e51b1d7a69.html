What are the Patent Bar review course options?

Patent bar review courses come at a range of prices and offer a variety of different study experiences. Merely deciding to take a patent bar review course is, unfortunately, not enough.  For better or worse, not all patent bar review courses are created equally.  It is important to choose a course that will give you the best opportunity to pass the first time.  The patent bar exam is a very difficult examination, so the goal is to pass the first time and then never look back. Always check the sites of the vendors for the most current pricing features. 

  • Elite Patent Prep summarized over 3,000+ pages of source materials into easy-to-follow instructional videos that will walk you through the MPEP, provide common exam day questions, and give common pitfalls you will encounter on test day. Full access until you pass. 
  • PassPatentBar is the lowest priced online training program with simulated tests to pass the U.S. Patent Office’s Bar Exam. You pass, or you enrollment fee will be returned.
  • Practicing Law Institute (PLI): PLI hosts one of the largest and most-respected patent bar review courses. One significant benefit of PLI's course is the inclusion of "Patware," a practice test program that emulates the patent bar's exam. You can choose between an on-demand web course or a schedule of live web courses spread out over two weeks. PLI has been the highest priced option generally approaching $3,000, although students who sign up with a .edu email account can receive a $1,000 discount. Group discounts are also available - 10% off the student price (for students, of course) for every multiple of four registrations, up to a maximum of 50% off (the student price). So, 10% off for a group of 4-7, 20% off for a group of 8-11, 30% off for 12-15, 40% off for 16-19, 50% off for a group of 20 or more. So, through May 2024, most UNH students have been getting the course for about $1000 (list price of the course $2995). 
  • OmniPrep Patent: OmniPrep offers a patent bar review course that promises to refund their fees if you fail to pass the course. The course site indicates they update their practice exam questions monthly, ensuring the materials are up-to-date. The course isn't quite as robust as PLI's but is offered at a much lower price point.
  • Patent Bar Exam Success Coaching : offers a live, highly interactive, rigorous approach to Patent Bar Exam preparation. Founded by Brandy Gamblin, Ph.D., a third-generation college professor with over 20 years of real classroom teaching experience, the program is especially well suited for students who have already studied extensively but need the material explained in a way that finally makes it click. Coaching is current, hands-on, and focused on the Patent Bar’s most heavily tested material. About 90% of Brandy’s students pass the Patent Bar on their first attempt after coaching.
  • PatBar: PatBar offers a comprehensive, self-paced home-study Patent Bar Review course with extensive instructional modules, practice materials, flashcards, and an exam simulator designed to help candidates efficiently prepare for and pass the United States Patent and Trademark Office registration exam. The program, which has been helping students since 1992, emphasizes proven study strategies and includes strong guarantees to support exam success. Their website is very informative  and worth a visit. 
  • Patent Education Series (PES) Bar Review: PES offers several different prep materials for the patent exam, including quiz decks, MPEP audio summaries, hard copy study books, and an online patent training workshop. The quiz decks are also available separately.
  • Wysebridge.com streamlines your study experience by eliminating unnecessary lectures and extraneous content. They focus on deep data analytics to curate content and study materials that target the most tested topics and frequently asked questions on the exam. Pay a one time fee and have access till you pass. Monthly access is also available.

You might find some open web sites like MyPatentBar.com is a Patent Bar Study Guide and resource for questions that currently appear on the Prometric Patent Bar Exam.

You should consider which aspects of the patent bar give you the most concern and choose a company that provides materials and courses that focus on that part of the test.
