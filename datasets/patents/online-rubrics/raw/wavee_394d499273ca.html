Know your odds. Invest with intent.

Make the right call faster

Every office action is a fork in the road. Interview, RCE, appeal, or abandon. Track One or standard. Push now or pivot. Without data, these calls get expensive fast.

‍

Juristat makes the next move clear with examiner-level win rates, portfolio alerts, and a ready-to-use Prosecution Strategy dashboard. Evaluate Track One and other USPTO programs before you spend. And bring evidence to every escalation with the Appeals Module. Standardize decisions across attorneys and outside counsel so you cut actions, time, and money—not corners.

From dashboard to decision in one workflow

Juristat turns a shifting prosecution landscape into a clear, data-backed playbook. Live signals surface where to act—interview, appeal, and continuation opportunities, long prosecutions, and low-allowance examiners—anchored in examiner, art-unit, and tech-center context from a 10M+ application dataset.

‍

When an appeal is in play, the Appeals Module benchmarks reversal patterns across similar cases; forward-citation momentum shows where to protect, license, or prune. Rules and alerts enforce when to push, pivot, or walk away, and exportable briefs align counsel and leadership the same day—so you choose the lowest-cost, highest-confidence path, consistently.

Frequently asked questions

Use data to choose the fastest path to allowance

What data does Juristat use?

Juristat’s industry-leading patent analytics database is based on more than 10 million applications. We examine USPTO data across pending, abandoned, and granted applications. Examiner, art unit, assignee, and firm metrics included. Historical coverage back to 2000.

How often is the data refreshed?

Automatically. Your dashboards and alerts stay current without manual imports.

Can I set custom alerts for risky prosecutions?

Yes. Trigger alerts on OA counts, RCE streaks, missed interviews, low-allowance examiners, and more—using thresholds you define.

Can our team standardize decisions across outside counsel?

Absolutely. Build if-then rules tied to examiner metrics, share them across matters, and enforce with alerts. Export the logic and results for alignment.

Can I evaluate programs like Track One inside Juristat?

Yes. Compare expected pendency and outcomes by tech area and examiner to decide when Track One (or other programs) makes sense.
