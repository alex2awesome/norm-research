Patent Quality Chat

The Patent Quality Chat series was designed to provide information on patent quality topics and gather your input on a regular basis via an easy, webinar format. Each Patent Quality Chat webinar included a speaker(s) presentation for approximately 20 minutes with the remaining time for questions and comments. 

Find a current list of upcoming events on a variety of IP topics.
