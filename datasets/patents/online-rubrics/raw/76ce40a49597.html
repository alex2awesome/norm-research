What is CNIPA?

The China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA) is China’s official government body responsible for patent registration and intellectual property protection. As the world’s largest patent office, CNIPA plays a crucial role in fostering innovation and protecting intellectual property rights in the world’s second-largest economy.

At Yucheng IP Law, we work closely with CNIPA to help both domestic and international clients navigate the complex landscape of Chinese patent law. Our experienced team, led by Tingfei Wang, specializes in patent prosecution and has successfully handled thousands of patent applications.

Types of Patents in China

1. Invention Patents

Invention patents are granted for new technical solutions or improvements in products or processes. These patents:

  • Provide 20 years of protection from the filing date
  • Undergo rigorous substantive examination
  • Offer the strongest legal protection among all patent types
  • Are ideal for breakthrough innovations and core technologies

2. Utility Model Patents

Utility model patents protect improvements in the shape, structure, or combination of products. Key features include:

  • Shorter protection period of 10 years
  • No substantive examination required
  • Lower cost and faster approval process
  • Perfect for incremental technical improvements

3. Design Patents

Design patents protect new aesthetic designs of products. These patents:

  • Provide 15 years of protection
  • Focus on the visual appearance of products
  • Require unique and novel designs
  • Are essential for products where appearance matters

Special Requirements for Foreign Applicants

How long does patent protection last in China?

• Invention Patents: 20 years
• Utility Model Patents: 10 years

Can foreign companies file patents directly with CNIPA?

No, foreign applicants must work through a licensed Chinese patent agency like Yucheng IP Law.

What happens if my patent application is rejected?
