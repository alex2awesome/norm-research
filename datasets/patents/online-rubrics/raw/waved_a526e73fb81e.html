5G Standard Essential Patents Dashboard

Access Dashboard

Too many declared SEPs. No clear view of what’s truly essential.

In the 5G world, declarations to ETSI are easy to make—but hard to trust. Not every declared SEP is truly essential, enforceable, or strategically meaningful. And without context, it’s easy to misread the landscape.

That’s why GreyB built the 5G SEP Dashboard.

Curated by telecom and IP experts, the dashboard is based on a deep, ongoing study of the 5G SEP ecosystem. We’ve extracted, refined, and analyzed thousands of ETSI declarations to reveal the real shape of 5G patent ownership—who holds what, where, and why it matters.

This is the first in a series of continuously updated insights, designed to reflect the ever-evolving dynamics of declaration activity, ownership transfers, and technical standard alignment.

Key Benefits of the GreyB 5G SEP Dashboard

True SEP Essentiality—Verified by Experts

What you get: Over 20,000+ patent families rigorously reviewed to determine whether they are truly essential to 5G technical standards.
Why it matters: Don’t rely on inflated or speculative declarations. This is your shortcut to identifying high-value patents that matter in licensing and litigation.

Global SEP Grant Visibility

What you get: A clear, interactive view of where each patent family is granted—across the US, EU, China, Japan, Korea, and more.
Why it matters: Focus your legal and commercial efforts on jurisdictions with enforceable rights. Spot litigation hotspots and assess global licensing leverage at a glance.

Litigation & FRAND Risk Intelligence

What you get: SEP families are flagged for prior or ongoing litigation and public licensing program participation.
Why it matters: Identify contentious patents early, avoid surprise disputes, and strengthen your position in FRAND negotiations or defensive planning.

Portfolio Ownership & Transfer Tracking

What you get: Insight into M&A activity, portfolio sales, and assignee changes—accurate as of Dec 2024.
Why it matters: Don’t negotiate with the wrong party. Know who controls key SEPs, and track how ownership shifts impact licensing and legal exposure.

Company & Portfolio Rankings

What you get: Instantly identify which companies lead the 5G SEP space by volume, strength, and essentiality of their portfolios.
Why it matters: Make competitive comparisons, assess licensing concentration, or evaluate target partners/acquirers with confidence.

Data You Can Trust—Curated, Not Crawled

What you get: A dashboard built by telecom/IP professionals, not just scraped or auto-processed data.
Why it matters: Accuracy isn’t optional when making strategic decisions. Our analysis is auditable, transparent, and defensible.

No rush—your trial access will be sent via email shortly. Explore it at your convenience and make an informed decision.
