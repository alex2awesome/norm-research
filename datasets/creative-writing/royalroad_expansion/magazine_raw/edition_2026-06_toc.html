Lots of characters and plot threads

Reviewed at: TraumaShock
by Thalaas Wednesday, July 15, 2026 5:03:10 AM

A few too many characters. I wonder if the author can put all those plot threads together!  But I got to say, so many unique and interesting protags!  I do feel having mystery, fantasy, sci-fi, isekai, lit-rpg, romance, comedy, slice of life, detective may be too many genres as well! Trying to do everything all at once can be a bit overwhelming, but I'll be impressed if you pull it off!

Also, not to be nit picky, but I've notice that several of the characters have the same name!  And the magic rules do seem to vary wildly between chapters! Sometimes there's no magic, other times there's lots. Sometimes they use technology. I now wonder if maybe they are setting up some multiversal sort of thing? It could work for this!

I do have to dock some marks for grammar. The author switches between first and third person a lot. And also from past and present tense. I think they need to make up their mind what POV they are going to use. Or maybe they are trying something more experimental. I mean, it's good when an author tries to stretch their metaphorical wings so to speak.

Anyway, I look forward to seeing how it ends. Congrats on such a high word count so fast!  You must write like a machine!
