Best goblin content on RR.

Reviewed at: Not to be a goblin
by ChillyPepper Tuesday, June 11, 2024 5:44:47 PM

This magazine has so much goblin presence of every flavor. Goblin shenanigans everywhere. I've heard things about goblins blowing up, goblins making stuff, goblins goblining and moseying around.

How does an author find two hundred words to fill an advanced review? by telling you more things about goblins! There are green goblins. Brown goblins. Blue goblins. There are goblins with turfs. Goblins with solitary personas. Goblins with Nanas.

But one thing that unites all the goblins, the love of delicious cuisine. (Different goblin tastes may differ.)

You may ask, how does this guy know how grammarish the grammary is grammering around. There are different authors writing different stories and using different styles. How does this guy know that every character is a beacon of perfection, and every story is the peak of literature? 

Fear not, dear reader. 

For a goblin story will always bring emotions to your heart, emotions that make you five star them if you think about it for less than two seconds. 

You might even find something you will become the new hardcore fan of in here. You might find something that makes you want to write, even. Suffer with us, write with us, become us. 

PART OF THE SHIP, PART OF THE CREW.
