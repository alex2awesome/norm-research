 First of all I didn't just review at one story. That's why, If my review is not to anyone's liking or upsets someone, I apologize In advance for that. I haven't done many reviews So, I'll just be honest and speak from heart .

 

I read every single story that took part in the community magazine contest. Every single story has it's own style and flow. While reading the story I felt like living within it . Each story has great world building and writing. Not gonna lie most every story here has potential and is promising. My personal favourites in the community magazine are " Under The Starry sky" and "Heavenfall: Ascension Of A  Primal Hunter". There are other stories that stand out to me too. The story's here has immense depth, emotional impact. The authors here did a fantastic job crafting such deep characters and mind blowing stories. I may have not read all the stories given in the community magazine but I have read most of it and to be honest each of them were good. Some were better than the others however, in the end every single story was worth a shot. I didn't feel like wasting my time at all. In fact I believe if I get more time, I'll read all of them again. 

 

To the authors: You're crafted a masterpiece, full of amazing details, great characters that deserves their own story, fantastic world building. Still, full of potential and room to grow. To every author here looking forward to what you write next. Until then peace.
