# Clarkesworld — Fiction Submission Criteria

## Length & Pay
- 1,000–22,000 words
- Pays above SFWA pro rates

## What They Want
- **Science fiction** — "need not be 'hard' SF, but rigor is appreciated"
- **Fantasy** — "can be folkloric, contemporary, surreal, etc."
- Speculative fiction broadly across the spectrum

## What They Don't Want (from Neil Clarke's "Things I Will Not Buy" lists)
- Stories where a planet is named Tirran or Tellus and turns out to be Earth
- Stories where the cute alien turns out to be evil
- Stories with deep messages about how SF/fantasy is better than mundane fiction
- Stories where the protagonist's hardships exist only for the protagonist to bear them
- Twist endings that don't actually twist
- Stories featuring serial killers
- Most stories about writers
- Stories where the entire plot is technological lecturing
- Lengthy dream sequences
- Stories that deliberately mimic an established style without bringing anything new

## Strict Policies
- **No AI-assisted work whatsoever** — "may result in being banned from submitting"
- No simultaneous submissions
- 7-day cooling period after rejection

## Implicit Quality Hallmarks
- Strong opening — Clarkesworld is famous for tightly-edited, sharp openers
- Surprising ideas + emotional grounding
- Fresh angle on SF tropes
- Diverse perspectives and global settings welcomed
