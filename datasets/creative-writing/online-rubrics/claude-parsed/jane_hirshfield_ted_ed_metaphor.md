# Jane Hirshfield — The Art of the Metaphor (TED-Ed)

## Core Questions
- How do metaphors help us better understand the world?
- What makes a good metaphor?

## Key Principles
- Metaphor brings a scene or emotion to life by linking two seemingly unrelated things.
- Examples drawn from Langston Hughes and Carl Sandburg — masters of evocative metaphor.
- A good metaphor reveals an unexpected resemblance and deepens perception.
- Metaphor is a tool for "reading between the lines" — surfacing meaning that direct statement cannot.

## Practical Exercises
- Notice metaphors in everyday content (web blogs, newspapers, magazines).
- Examine ordinary expressions that are metaphors and similes.
- Search poetry collections to find metaphors.
- Tune ear and mind to hear them in everyday speech.
