# AP English Literature & Composition — Prose Analysis Rubric

College Board rubric for analyzing prose fiction (including short stories). 6-point scale across 3 rows.

## Row A: Thesis (0–1 point)
- **0 pts**: No defensible thesis; restates prompt; describes passage instead of making a claim.
- **1 pt**: "Responds to the prompt with a thesis that presents a defensible interpretation of the passage."

## Row B: Evidence AND Commentary (0–4 points)
- **0 pts**: Simply restates thesis; offers no evidence/commentary.
- **1 pt**: General evidence; minimal/incoherent commentary.
- **2 pts**: Some specific evidence; commentary explains how it relates to thesis but is uneven.
- **3 pts**: Specific evidence supports all claims; commentary explains how it supports the thesis.
- **4 pts**: Specific evidence in service of a line of reasoning; commentary consistently explains how it supports the line of reasoning.

## Row C: Sophistication (0–1 point)
- **1 pt** for one of:
  - Identifying and exploring complexities or tensions within the passage
  - Illuminating the writer's interpretation by situating it in a broader context
  - Accounting for and responding to alternative interpretations
  - Employing a style that is consistently vivid and persuasive

## Implied Quality Criteria for Prose (from rubric)
A "complex" passage rewards analysis that surfaces:
- Shifting, contradictory, or paradoxical elements
- Multi-layered meanings (surface + subtext)
- Stylistic choices that produce specific effects (diction, syntax, imagery, tone, POV, structure)
- Evidence the writer made *intentional* craft decisions
