# 25 Things You Should Know About Storytelling

1. **Stories Have Power** — "Stories move the world at the same time they explain our place in it."
2. **Effect Above Entertainment** — Superior narratives create emotional impact beyond simple amusement.
3. **A Good Story Is Good Regardless Of Genre Or Form** — Quality transcends medium.
4. **Form Still Matters** — Each medium has organic constraints shaping how stories function.
5. **Stories Have Shape, Even Unintentionally** — All narratives possess structure.
6. **The Story Is A Map; Plot Is The Route** — Story = world/characters/atmosphere; plot = sequence chosen within.
7. **Originality In Plot vs. Story** — Plot elements repeat across narratives; unique arrangement creates original stories (LEGO blocks metaphor).
8. **The Bridge Between Author And Audience** — Readers seek connection and self-recognition.
9. **Challenge The Audience** — Great storytellers respectfully defy expectations.
10. **No Tale Survives Without Conflict** — "Conflict is the food that feeds the reader."
11. **Balance Tension and Release** — Narrative builds through pressure punctuated by relief.
12. **Peaks, Valleys, and Varied Pacing** — Successful narratives avoid linear progression.
13. **Tell Only The Story** — Eliminate digressions that distract from core themes.
14. **Big Ideas Need Small Spaces** — Abstract concepts resonate through individual character experiences.
15. **Backstory Requires Delicate Handling** — Excessive exposition overwhelms readers.
16. **Characters Drive The Narrative** — Without interesting people, narratives lose engagement.
17. **Villains Have Mothers** — Antagonists possess humanity and motivations.
18. **Heroes Have Broken Toys** — Protagonists benefit from flaws and moral complexity.
19. **Strip Skin Off Bones** — Study narrative mechanics by crafting minimal stories.
20. **Beginnings Are For Assholes** — Start narratives late; engage readers where action emerges.
21. **Don't Jump Too Deep Too Fast** — Audiences need emotional anchors before dramatic intensity.
22. **Treat Place Like Character** — Settings should seem alive and significant.
23. **Always Ask Why You Tell This** — Knowing your tether to material strengthens delivery.
24. **It's Okay To Bury The Lede** — Subtext allows deeper meaning beneath surface.
25. **Writing Is Craft, Storytelling Is Art** — Storytelling transcends mechanics into artistic expression.
