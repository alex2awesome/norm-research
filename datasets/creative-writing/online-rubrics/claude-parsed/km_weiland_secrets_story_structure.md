# K.M. Weiland's Story Structure Principles

## Primary Plot Points (Three-Act Model)

- **The Hook** — "A question" that engages readers from the opening.
- **Inciting Event** — Initiates the story's central conflict.
- **First Plot Point** — Creates "a seismic shift" launching escalating tension.
- **Midpoint** — Moves protagonists "from reaction to action."
- **Third Plot Point** — Transitions through false victory to the low moment.
- **Climax** — Requires "sacrifice and victory/failure."
- **Resolution** — Provides closure in the "new normal world."

## Supporting Concepts

- **Chiastic Structure** — Links major beats: Hook ↔ Resolution, Inciting Event ↔ Climax, First ↔ Third Plot Points.
- **Four Metaphoric Worlds** — Normal World (Act 1), Adventure World (Act 2), Underworld (Act 3), New Normal (Resolution).
- **Pinch Points** — Critical moments often neglected yet "crucial to your story structure."

## Core Claim
Story structure is "the single most important factor for a writer's success" — allows authors to create stories that work every time.
