# Whiting Award — Emerging Writers Criteria

## Mission
Recognize "exceptional new writers who are just making their mark in the literary culture." 10 awards/year across fiction, nonfiction, poetry, drama.

## Core Criteria
- **Originality** — fresh voice, vision
- **Distinctive voice** — recognizable on the page
- **Potential for lasting impact** — career-trajectory bet
- **Early-career accomplishment** — published work in books or major journals
- *Not* commercial success, sales, or fame

## Process
- No applications accepted
- Confidential nominators (writers, editors, scholars) propose 1 candidate each
- Selection committee of writers/scholars/editors picks 10 winners

## Implicit Quality Hallmarks (from past Whiting fiction recipients)
- Voice is the prize's signature criterion — most winners have a recognizable music
- Often: marginalized perspectives elevated by craft
- Often: experimental form with literary substance
- Often: writers whose later careers vindicate the bet (Atwood, Wallace, Saunders, Hempel, Eugenides, Diaz, Whitehead, Erpenbeck, etc.)

## Quality Bet
Whiting is a *prediction* prize: not just "this work is good" but "this writer will matter." Selectors look for evidence of a sustainable creative project, not just one strong story.
