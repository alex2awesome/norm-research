# Creative Writing Short Story Rubric (iRubric K56455)

Six categories totaling 100 points. Levels: Needs Improvement / Fair / Good / Excellent.

## Overall Presentation (15 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: Structurally inferior; did not follow directions.
- **Fair**: Typed but with incorrect font/size; missing name, date, class.
- **Good**: Typed with possibly missing or misspelled header information.
- **Excellent**: Times New Roman 12 font; name, date, class present.

## Creativity & Use of Literary Devices (10 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: No creative language; lacks feeling and emotion.
- **Fair**: Few creative terms; may be out of context.
- **Good**: Some creative language; devices present but disrupt story flow.
- **Excellent**: "Story is filled with creative and descriptive language" engaging readers.

## Character Development (15 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: Lacks descriptive details; characters lack depth.
- **Fair**: Reader gains basic character understanding.
- **Good**: Some descriptive details; characters somewhat interesting.
- **Excellent**: Thorough development through speech, actions, description.

## Plot Development (30 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: No development; lacks sequence.
- **Fair**: Needs improvement; missing pieces.
- **Good**: Follows sequence but components need better development.
- **Excellent**: Contains exposition, rising action, climax, falling action, resolution.

## Grammar (15 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: 9+ errors interfering with storytelling.
- **Fair**: 7–9 errors.
- **Good**: 3–6 errors.
- **Excellent**: 1–2 grammatical errors.

## Conflict (15 pts)
- **Needs Improvement**: No conflict presented.
- **Fair**: Vague or unclear conflict.
- **Good**: Conflict depicted; story somewhat developed around it.
- **Excellent**: "Conflict is clearly depicted in the story and the story is developed around the conflict."
