# 15 Actionable Tips for Writing Deep Character

1. **Master the 'Believe, Care, Invest' Trifecta** — Readers must believe character is real, care about circumstances, invest in their problem-solving ability.
2. **Define the Dramatic Question** — "Who are you when the chips are down?"
3. **Use Absolute Specificity** — Replace vague traits with precise, measurable details.
4. **Understand the Heroine's Journey** — Strength can come through connection and delegation.
5. **Assign Metaphor Families** — Each character draws from a distinctive linguistic well.
6. **Find the Diagnostic Detail** — One hyper-specific behavioral quirk pulled from real life.
7. **Displace Your Own Trauma** — Channel personal pain into emotionally parallel struggles.
8. **Write Diverse Characters as Real People** — Various backgrounds in normal contemporary roles.
9. **Respect Tribal and Cultural Specificity** — Never treat groups as monolithic.
10. **Give Protagonists Morally Neutral Hero Status** — Compelling problem-solving > likability.
11. **Build Vibrant Side Characters** — Interesting enough for spin-offs.
12. **Use Voice as a Rhythmic Tool** — Sentence structure (periods vs. commas) reflects psychology.
13. **Link Character and Plot Inseparably** — Plot tests and exposes protagonist's particular flaw repeatedly.
14. **Write Out of Order (Discovery Writing)** — Powerful scenes first; assemble later.
15. **Use Research for Sensory Details** — Travel, observation, immersion for authenticity.
