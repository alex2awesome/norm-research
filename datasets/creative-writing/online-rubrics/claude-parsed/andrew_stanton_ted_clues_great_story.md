# Andrew Stanton — The Clues to a Great Story

## Core Principles

1. **Make Me Care** — The supreme command underlying all effective storytelling.
2. **Know Your Ending First** — Storytelling is "joke telling"; understand the destination before starting.
3. **Affirm Meaning** — Effective narratives reinforce that life matters.
4. **Make an Opening Promise** — "This is going to lead somewhere"; signal a worthwhile journey immediately.
5. **2+2 (Don't Spell Out 4)** — Audiences want to assemble meaning themselves; show them 2+2, don't tell them it equals 4.
6. **Wonder** — The true secret ingredient to great storytelling. Find wonder in concept, story, characters; present those magical moments.
7. **Character Spine** — Every character has an inner motor / dominant unconscious goal (WALL-E: find beauty; Marlin: prevent harm; Woody: do what's best for Andy).
8. **Develop Relatable Yet Flawed Characters** — Likable protagonists with unfulfilled need or internal contradiction.
9. **Drama Through Uncertainty** — Build anticipation by making audiences unsure how situations will resolve.
10. **Invite Active Participation** — Audiences enjoy filling gaps themselves rather than being spoon-fed.
