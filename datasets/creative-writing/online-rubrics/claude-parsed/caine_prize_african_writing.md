# Caine Prize for African Writing — Selection Criteria

## Eligibility
- "African writer" = national of an African country, or a parent African by birth/nationality
- Fictional short stories only
- 3,000–10,000 words (indicative)
- Published in English
- Published in the 5 years preceding deadline
- Must be previously published; not self-published
- Submitted by publisher, not author

## Genre Restrictions (Excluded)
- Children's stories
- Factual writing
- Academic essays
- Plays
- Poetry
- Autobiography/biography

## Implicit Quality Criteria (from past winners)
- **Literary craft** — sentence-level mastery
- **Distinctive voice** — often experimental in voice/POV
- **Place specificity** — African settings rendered with detail and refusal of cliché
- **Resistance to single-narrative-Africa** — the prize rewards work that complicates Western expectations
- **Political consciousness** without polemic
- **Form innovation** — many winners take formal risks
- **Emotional weight** — even short pieces carry significant gravity

## Notable Winners' Aesthetic Range
- Realism (Helon Habila, Binyavanga Wainaina)
- Speculative (NoViolet Bulawayo)
- Fragmented/experimental (Henrietta Rose-Innes)
- Diasporic identity (Lesley Nneka Arimah)
