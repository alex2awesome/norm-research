# Maximalism in Fiction (David Foster Wallace, Pynchon, et al.)

## Definition of the Maximalist Novel
"A long novel with a layered structure that exploits sophisticated literary devices." Examples:
- *Infinite Jest* (Wallace)
- *Gravity's Rainbow* (Pynchon)
- *2666* (Bolaño)
- *The Recognitions* (Gaddis)
- *Ulysses* (Joyce)
- *Underworld* (DeLillo)

## Key Characteristics

### Encyclopedic Knowledge
The maximalist novel "incorporates" vast knowledge — media theory, linguistics, science, sport, economics, theology, and so on. Often footnoted, glossed, digressive.

### Length
Substantial — usually 800+ pages — and earned by complexity, not just expansion.

### Multiple Storylines
Interweaving plots, characters, settings, time periods.

### Stylistic Range
Multiple voices, registers, prose modes within a single book.

### Formal Innovation
Footnotes, fragments, lists, found documents, embedded forms.

### Cultural Critique
Often satirizes contemporary excess, capitalism, media, technology, while *enacting* the same excess.

### Moral Earnestness Beneath Irony
Wallace particularly: "white-hot purity of moral purpose" beneath the bells and whistles. The form mirrors the chaos of contemporary life while seeking a way through.

## Quality Criteria for Maximalist Work
- **Justified complexity** — every layer earns its place
- **Architectural coherence** — chaos is organized
- **Voice mastery** — multiple voices distinct yet held together
- **Moral seriousness** beneath formal display
- **Genuine knowledge** of the domains incorporated, not Wikipedia-skimming
- **Heart** — readers must care, even amid difficulty

## Maximalist vs. Minimalist (Carver)
- Maximalist: more is more; abundance, density, range
- Minimalist: less is more; restraint, ellipsis, surface
- Both can produce greatness — opposite means

## Why It Matters for Quality Evaluation
- Maximalist work fails common rubrics that prize "concision"
- Minimalist work fails rubrics that prize "complexity" or "richness"
- A complete quality rubric must accommodate both — judging each work by what it sets out to do

## Wallace's Specific Craft Markers
- 388 endnotes in *Infinite Jest* (some with their own footnotes)
- Unusual coined vocabulary (Subsidized Time, the Entertainment, the Wraith)
- Sentence-level virtuosity within massive structural complexity
- Tension between irony and sincerity
- Long-take psychological description (Avril Incandenza, Hal, Don Gately)
