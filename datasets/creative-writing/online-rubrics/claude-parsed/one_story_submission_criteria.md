# One Story — What Makes a Story Worth Publishing

## Mission
One Story publishes ONE literary story every 3-4 weeks, mailed to subscribers. By design, each issue must stand alone.

## Quality Standard
"Stories that leave readers feeling satisfied and are strong enough to stand alone."

## Submission Specs
- 3,000–8,000 words
- Any style, any subject "as long as they are good"
- Previously unpublished (print outside North America OK)
- $500 + 25 contributor copies

## Implied Criteria for "Good" (from One Story's published catalog)
- **Self-contained** — the story doesn't require sequel/context
- **Satisfying ending** — resolution doesn't have to be tidy but feels earned
- **Strong voice** — distinctive narrator/POV
- **Tight craft** — no filler; every scene matters
- **Memorable** — sticks with the reader
- **Emotional truth** — characters feel like real people in real predicaments

## One Story's "9 Tips for Submitting to Lit Mags"
1. Read the magazine before submitting
2. Submit your strongest, most polished work
3. Follow guidelines exactly
4. Send a clean, professional cover letter
5. Don't query about status before stated response time
6. Take rejection professionally; consider personal notes seriously
7. Simultaneous submissions OK; notify on acceptance
8. Don't resubmit unless explicitly invited
9. Keep writing while you wait
