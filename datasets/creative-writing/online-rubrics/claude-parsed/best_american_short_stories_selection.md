# The Best American Short Stories — Selection Criteria

## Eligibility
1. Original publication in nationally distributed American or Canadian periodicals
2. Author is American or Canadian (or has made the U.S. their home)
3. Original publication as short stories — novel excerpts not considered
4. Must be previously published (no unpublished work)

## Two-Stage Process
1. **Series Editor** (Heidi Pitlor since 2007) selects ~120 notable stories from hundreds of magazines and online publications
2. **Guest Editor** (a leading writer, changes annually) selects ~20 final stories for the volume

## Implicit Criteria
- The series editor's "Notable Stories" list is itself a quality bar
- Guest editor's idiosyncratic vision shapes each year's volume
- Range across literary realism, experimental, genre-adjacent literary work
- Typically character-driven, language-attentive, emotionally resonant
- The "Best" framing connotes critical, not just popular, judgment

## Notable Series Editors
- Shannon Ravenel (1978–1990)
- Katrina Kenison (1991–2006)
- Heidi Pitlor (2007–)
