# 25 Things You Should Know About Worldbuilding

1. **What We Mean When We Say "Worldbuilding"** — Revealing story-world details: money, customs, materials, food, cultural practices.
2. **The World Serves The Story, Not Vice Versa** — Build a world to serve the stories you want to tell.
3. **You're Not Writing A Fucking Encyclopedia** — Excessive worldbuilding distracts; prioritize narrative.
4. **Okay, Wait, You Might Be Writing An Encyclopedia** — Lengthy fantasy epics may use a focused story bible.
5. **Variant Approach: Ninja Genesis** — Build organically as you write, refine in revision.
6. **The Pig In A Purse** — Include only details necessary for the narrative.
7. **Function Beyond Plot** — Worldbuilding supports emotional and thematic elements.
8. **Action And Dialogue Above Description And Exposition** — Convey world details through interaction.
9. **A Rich Tapestry, Not Plain White Toilet Paper** — Build complexity beyond simple good-versus-evil.
10. **The Nature Of "Write What You Know"** — Borrow from understood concepts and translate into fantastical contexts.
11. **Remix Culture** — Combine cultural and mythological influences creatively.
12. **Ew, Stereotypes** — Reject monolithic cultural representations.
13. **Your Heteronormative White Male Gaze** — Examine presuppositions about gender, race, power.
14. **Small Details Are Just As Important As Big Ones** — Food, hygiene, craftsmanship reflect cultural values.
15. **Simple Interactions Pregnant With Worldbuilding Complexity** — Behavior conveys cultural norms.
16. **Your World Must Be Active And Alive** — Individuals have agency to confirm or deny expectations.
17. **"But It's Cool, Shut Up" Is Not An Excuse** — Every element must connect to character/plot/theme.
18. **The Rules** — Establish clear rules but reveal them through story.
19. **Wait, I Need To Research My Made-Up World?** — Research real economics, history, practices.
20. **Imagine A World On The Edge Of Conflict** — Include inherent tensions: war, famine, scarcity.
21. **Everything Affects Everything Else** — Recognize interconnected consequences.
22. **Subtextology** — Let the world communicate through implication.
23. **Preserving Mystery Is Vital** — Leave unexplained elements; resist over-explanation.
24. **Worldbuilding Versus Storytelling** — Robust worldbuilding doesn't guarantee narrative quality.
25. **Construct Worlds Mapped After Your Own Heartsblood Spatter** — Authentic passion shows.
