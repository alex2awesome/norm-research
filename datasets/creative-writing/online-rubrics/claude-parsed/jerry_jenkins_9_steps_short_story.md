# Jerry Jenkins — 9 Proven Steps for Writing Short Stories

1. **Read Extensively** — Study classic and contemporary short stories.
2. **Target Emotions** — Themes that move readers: Love, Redemption, Justice, Freedom, Heroic sacrifice.
3. **Limit Scope** — A short slice of main character's life — often one scene or incident.
4. **Craft a Compelling Title** — Grab attention; distinguish from competitors.
5. **Apply Classic Structure** — Place character "into terrible trouble from the get-go," then escalate.
6. **Imply Rather Than Explain** — Subtle details over lengthy backstories; layered sensory info.
7. **Eliminate Excess** — Remove anything that doesn't serve the story. "Every sentence must count."
8. **Deliver a Satisfying Conclusion** — End with impact that "resounds with the reader" without feeling forced.
9. **Ruthlessly Edit** — Grammar, word choice, clichés, redundancies — maximize impact through compression.
