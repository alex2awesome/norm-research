# Top 10 Tips For Writing Great Short Stories

1. **Understand a short story is not a novel** — Different structure and scope.
2. **Start as close to the end as possible** — Bypass backstory.
3. **Keep up the pace** — "Hit the ground running"; catapult reader headlong.
4. **Keep the number of characters small** — Protagonist, antagonist, one supporting.
5. **Give the reader someone to root for** — Glimpse into psyche.
6. **Create conflict!** — "A single point of conflict."
7. **Suggest a backstory but don't elaborate** — "Every sentence must count."
8. **Appeal to the five senses** — Show don't tell.
9. **Dialogue should bring your story to life** — Advance plot/conflict.
10. **Edit until it hurts** — Ruthlessly cut.
