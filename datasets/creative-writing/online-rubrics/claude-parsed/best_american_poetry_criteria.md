# The Best American Poetry — Selection Criteria

## Mission (David Lehman, founder/series editor since 1988)
"To see whether a single annual volume can accurately reflect the diversity of American poetry and honor excellence regardless of what form it takes, or what idiom it favors, or from which region of the country it comes."

## Process
- 75 poems per year selected from the year's literary journals
- Different guest editor every year (Pulitzer-winning poets, MacArthur fellows, etc.)
- Lehman as series editor curates a long list; guest editor selects 75
- Up to 3 poems per individual poet
- Poets write brief author notes about the poem (form, occasion, method) — included in volume

## Implicit Quality Criteria
- **Form-agnostic** — sonnet, free verse, prose poem, experimental all eligible
- **Region-agnostic** — represents geographic diversity of US
- **Idiom-agnostic** — formal/lyric, slam-influenced, flarf, etc.
- **Excellence** — undefined, intentionally; left to guest editor's vision
- **Originality** — fresh angle on language/form
- **Sound and rhythm** mattering at the line level
- **Authorial voice** distinctive

## What's Eligible
- Magazine publication during the eligibility year
- Both large-circulation and small-press journals
- Books (rarely)
- Poets resident in U.S. (foreign-born OK if community-identified American)

## Not Eligible
- Translations
- Previously published in book before journal
- Works in languages other than English (apart from translations of foreign poetry)

## Why It Matters for Story Quality
Even though *Best American Poetry* is poetry-specific, its principles port to short prose:
- Form-agnostic (any narrative shape can be excellent)
- Idiom-agnostic (genre and literary both)
- Excellence as recognized by accomplished practitioners
- Range and diversity prized over consensus
- The series editor + guest editor structure ensures both consistency and variety

## Companion Series
*Best American Short Stories*, *Best American Essays*, *Best American Mystery Stories*, etc. all follow same model.
