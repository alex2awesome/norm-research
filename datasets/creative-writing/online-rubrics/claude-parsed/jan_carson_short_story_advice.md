# Jan Carson — What Makes a Good Short Story

## Key Criteria
- **Risk-Taking & Innovation** — Stories that subvert rules or push boundaries. "I enjoy a story with notions."
- **Authentic Characters** — Carefully drawn characters over tired tropes. Believability matters most — readers must sense the writer genuinely believes in their characters' reality.
- **Voice** — The most critical element: "an authentic, and ideally intriguing, voice." Carson loves narratives that feel like intimate confessions.

## Writing Approach
- Ignore prescriptive rules.
- "Write from the guts. Take all the risks."
- Read work aloud to verify voice authenticity.
- Edit rigorously, treating revision like "excavating for treasure."
- Trust that each writer possesses unique stories only they can tell.

## Caution
No formula guarantees success. Writing requires intuition, courage, and willingness to challenge conventions.
