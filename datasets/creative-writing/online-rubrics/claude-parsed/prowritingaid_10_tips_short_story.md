# ProWritingAid — 10 Tips for Writing a Short Story

1. **Experiment With Form** — Recipes, text messages, real estate ads.
2. **Start With a Strong Hook** — Each line important, especially the first.
3. **Identify the Inciting Incident and Climax** — Two key plot points.
4. **Evoke a Specific Mood** — Funny, creepy, nostalgic, or heartbreaking; let mood inform decisions.
5. **Keep the Timeline Short** — Begin closer to the climax.
6. **Minimize the Cast of Characters** — Two characters or even one protagonist.
7. **Choose a Specific Theme** — "Short stories are too small to explore every nuance."
8. **Focus on "Knockout"** — "The short story wins by knockout"; excel at one central element.
9. **Give Your Story an Interesting Title** — Hooks readers; "the real first line."
10. **Edit, Edit, Edit** — Polish thoroughly.
