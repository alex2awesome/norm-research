# David Mamet — Three Uses of the Knife: On the Nature and Purpose of Drama

## Central Argument
"The subject of drama is The Lie. At the end of the drama THE TRUTH — which has been overlooked, disregarded, scorned, and denied — prevails."

The dramatist's job is to design a structure in which the audience must reckon with a truth they have been avoiding.

## Three Chapters
1. **The Wind-Chill Factor** — Drama as our way of organizing the chaos of experience; we dramatize the world to make sense of it.
2. **Second Act Problems** — The middle is the crisis of every play (and most stories). Stories falter when the middle becomes mere stalling between setup and payoff. The middle must intensify the central question.
3. **Three Uses of the Knife** — Echoing the song lyric: first to slice bread, then to shave, then to kill. Three ascending uses correspond to escalating dramatic stakes.

## Mamet's Quality Criteria
- **Structure determines meaning** — character is a function of plot, not vice versa
- **Throughline** — every scene must drive the central question
- **Scenes are negotiations** — each character wants something; conflict is bargaining
- **Cut everything that isn't necessary** — Mamet is famously brutal with revision
- **No exposition for its own sake** — only what's needed in the moment
- **Drama isn't about characters' feelings; it's about their actions**
- **Tell the truth** — the audience can sense when the writer is hiding from their own subject

## Mamet's Practical Advice (echoed in his other books)
- Each scene = who wants what from whom; what happens if they don't get it; why now
- Suspense = withheld answer to a question we want answered
- Avoid backstory; what matters is the present action
- Dialogue is action, not explanation

## Quality Tests for Drama
- What is each character pursuing in this scene?
- Why is this scene now? Why not earlier or later?
- What does the audience know vs. what do the characters know?
- What's the lie the play exposes?
- Does the climax force a reckoning with that lie?
