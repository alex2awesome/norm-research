# 25 Ways To Unstick A Stuck Story

1. **Retroactive Outline** — Take the pressure off; create or redo outlines.
2. **Roadblocks & Obstacles** — Add barriers to maintain engagement.
3. **Diversify Character Perspective** — Shift viewpoint to fresh angles.
4. **Recalibrate Motivation** — Understand what characters want and need.
5. **Strengthen Supporting Cast** — Develop secondary characters with own agendas.
6. **Introduce New Characters** — Refresh narrative energy.
7. **Fix Plot Gaps** — Identify missing bridges and fill them.
8. **Add Subplots** — Distract from and energize main plot.
9. **Use Non-Linear Elements** — Flashbacks, hallucinations, dream sequences.
10. **Introduce Mystery** — Plant unanswered questions for gravitational pull.
11. **Steal Protagonist Assets** — Remove weapons, knowledge, loved ones.
12. **Midpoint Twist** — Reveal someone isn't who they claimed.
13. **Strategic Betrayal** — Have a character betray the protagonist.
14. **Secrets & Lies** — Conflict through hidden information.
15. **Kill Important Characters** — Eliminate meaningful characters; raise stakes.
16. **Ill-Advised Romance** — Force inappropriate relationships.
17. **Constant Peril** — Continually place story/characters in danger.
18. **Raise Stakes** — Increase consequences of failure.
19. **Activate Protagonist** — Shift from passive sufferer to active agent.
20. **Triple Axis Conflict** — Physical danger + emotional stakes + philosophical implications.
21. **Determine Ending** — Knowing destination clarifies direction.
22. **"What If?" Brainstorm** — Generate multiple alternative scenarios.
23. **Embrace Chaos** — Make radical narrative choices.
24. **Delete Significant Sections** — Remove problematic passages.
25. **Push Through Resistance** — Write past gaps; solve problems later.
