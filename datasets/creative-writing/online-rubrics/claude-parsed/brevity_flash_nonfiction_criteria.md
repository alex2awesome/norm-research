# Brevity Magazine — Flash Criteria

## Format
- 750 words or fewer ("the 750-words-or-fewer club")
- Concise literary nonfiction (much applies to flash fiction too)
- No AI-generated work

## What Editor Dinty W. Moore Wants
"A fabulous petri dish for experimentation. Everything is dialed up in a shorter piece. You need to:
- Move in and out of scene quickly
- Introduce language, diction and rhythm immediately
- Establish place, character, conflict, voice right away."

## "Working" Test
"A piece of flash is considered to be working if it raises questions of further interest, but not confusion."

## Implicit Quality Criteria for Flash
- **Compression** — every word load-bearing
- **Immediate orientation** — no slow build
- **Resonance density** — high meaning per word
- **Single moment / image / insight** — can't sprawl
- **Sound/rhythm** more crucial than in long form
- **Ending must surprise but feel inevitable**
- **Suggestion over statement** — gestures toward larger meaning
- **Open-endedness** — not closure but aperture

## Often Cited Forms in Flash
- The list essay
- The hermit-crab essay (borrowed form)
- The braided micro-essay
- The single-image meditation
- The compressed memoir
- The scene-as-whole-story
