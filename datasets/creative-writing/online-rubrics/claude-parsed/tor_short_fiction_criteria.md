# Tor.com (Reactor Magazine) — Speculative Short Fiction Criteria

## Length
- < 17,500 words; most stories ~12,000 words

## What They Publish
- **Speculative fiction broadly** — SF, fantasy, horror, alternate history, related genres
- Original short stories and poetry

## Editorial Values
- "Full diversity of speculative fiction"
- Encourages submissions from underrepresented populations
- Welcomes characters and settings reflecting underrepresented experiences

## Implicit Quality Hallmarks (from analysis of accepted work)
- High literary craft within genre frame
- Fresh treatment of speculative concepts
- Strong, distinctive narrative voice
- Resonant emotional core
- Often: novella-length character-driven works (fueled the Hugo/Nebula novella revival)
