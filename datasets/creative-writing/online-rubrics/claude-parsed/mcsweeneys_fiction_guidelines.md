# McSweeney's — Fiction Submission Guidelines

## McSweeney's Quarterly Concern (Print)
- Mostly fiction; some nonfiction; rarely poetry
- "There are no rules" — best test is to read the Quarterly
- Pays $400 per short story
- **Strict no-AI policy** — "McSweeney's accepts no writing aided in any manner by AI"
- Submittable only

## McSweeney's Internet Tendency (Web)
- **Humor and satire**, not traditional fiction
- Conceptual humor pieces
- Maximum 1,200 words; preference for under 1,000
- Often list-format, parody-format, voice-driven monologues

## Implied Quality Criteria
- **Voice** — distinctive, often funny, often a little weird
- **Conceptual hook** — the piece "is about" something at multiple levels
- **Compression** — every line earning its keep
- **Tonal control** — humor that doesn't tip into smug or mean
- **Literary ambition** in the Quarterly — even unconventional formats reward close reading
- **Originality of form** — McSweeney's has historically published epistolary, footnoted, fragmented, list, recipe-format, etc. fiction
