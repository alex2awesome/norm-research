# Edgar Award (Mystery Writers of America) — Short Story Criteria

## Eligibility
- 1,000–22,000 words
- First publication in U.S. during the eligibility year
- Mystery, crime, suspense, or intrigue field
- Published by MWA-approved publisher
- Author must have received ≥ $25 in advance/royalties

## Implicit Quality Criteria for Mystery Short Fiction
- **Strong central puzzle / crime / suspense engine**
- **Clued solution** — fair play (cozy/whodunit) or earned reveal (suspense)
- **Compelling investigator/protagonist** — distinct voice
- **Twist or revelation** that recontextualizes earlier events
- **Atmosphere** appropriate to subgenre (noir darkness, cozy charm, thriller momentum)
- **Pacing** — efficient setup, escalating tension
- **Moral resonance** — crime fiction often carries ethical weight

## Subgenres Recognized
- Hardboiled / noir
- Cozy mystery
- Police procedural
- Psychological suspense
- Historical mystery
- International / crime
- Caper / heist

## Special Award
- **Robert L. Fish Memorial Award** — best first mystery short story by an American
