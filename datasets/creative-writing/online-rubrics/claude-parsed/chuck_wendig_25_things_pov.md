# 25 Things About Narrative Point-of-View

1. **Know Thy Narrator** — Identify who tells the story before writing.
2. **Who's On First, I Don't Know's On Third** — First-person uses "I"; third-person uses "she/he/they."
3. **Ha Ha Ha, Second-Person, That's A Good One** — Second-person ("you") is risky.
4. **Witnessing Versus Experiencing** — Third-person = witness; first-person = experiential immersion.
5. **The Intimacy Of The Reader** — Different POV modes create varying closeness.
6. **Objective: The Reader At The Window** — Restricts readers from internal thoughts.
7. **Subjective: The Reader As A Psychic Monkey** — Grants access to one character's interior.
8. **Omniscient: Reader Drops Acid** — Access to all characters' thoughts simultaneously.
9. **The Limited Lens Of Third Person Subjective** — Witness perspective + intimate access to one mind.
10. **Episodic Third: Monkey Hops From Shoulder To Shoulder** — Chapter-by-chapter character shifts.
11. **The Deeper Plunge Of First Person Subjective** — Risk: narrator voice indistinguishable from author.
12. **The News Report Had Sex With A Screenplay** — Objective POV: details, actions, dialogue, no interpretation.
13. **First Person Omniscient** — Requires justification (ghost, psychic).
14. **Multiple First Person Narrators** — Each voice must be distinctive.
15. **The Cool Kids Of POV High** — First-person subjective and third-person limited episodic dominate.
16. **First And Third Living Together** — Mixing requires logical justification.
17. **Be Consistent, Be Clear** — Maintain POV choices throughout.
18. **The Reader Is Your Puppet And POV Is One Of The Strings** — Manipulative tool controlling info access.
19. **Perspective Creates Tension** — Restricting/revealing info generates suspense and dramatic irony.
20. **Wuzza Wooza Who Now?** — Beware confusing readers through excessive omniscience or subjectivity.
21. **The Danger Of Illuminating Assholes** — Intimate POV with morally questionable characters is tricky.
22. **What Objectivity Misses** — Sacrifices novel's unique power to explore inner experience.
23. **Is The Narrator A Lying Liar?** — Unreliable narrators work best with intimate POV.
24. **This Is All Wrapped Up With Narrative Tense** — POV + tense create additional variables.
25. **When In Doubt, Rewrite To A New POV** — Experimentation can revitalize a struggling manuscript.
