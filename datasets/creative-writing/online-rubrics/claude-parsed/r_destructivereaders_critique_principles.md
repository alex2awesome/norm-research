# r/DestructiveReaders — Critique Principles

## Goal
Improve writing and maintain "the highest standard of critique excellence anywhere on Reddit." Not about writers being nice to writers — about readers being honest with writers. The community deconstructs writing to construct better writers.

## Critique Requirements

**High-Effort:** All critiques must be high-effort and follow the **1:1 ratio** — building a critique with the same word count or more than the story you submit. Google Doc comments don't count toward the 1:1 ratio.

**Two Phases:**
1. **Line edit** — focuses on style, grammar, dialogue, confusing passages.
2. **Critique essay** — focuses on title, style, plot, character, pacing individually.

This format helps writers see boundaries between skills and informs them of techniques used incorrectly and how they affect the reader.

## Common Feedback Patterns
- Unfiltered feedback without regard for feelings.
- Line-by-line breakdown of what bothers them, what to improve, what's missing.
- Tear writing apart if not working.
- Focus on craft elements as discrete diagnosable categories.

## Implicit Rubric (from feedback patterns)
- Title effectiveness
- Opening hook
- Style/voice
- Plot structure and momentum
- Characterization (depth, distinctness, motivation)
- Pacing
- Dialogue
- Grammar/mechanics
- Confusing passages / clarity
