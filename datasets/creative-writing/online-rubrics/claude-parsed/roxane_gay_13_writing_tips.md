# Roxane Gay's Top 13 Writing Tips

1. **Don't Let Anyone Pigeonhole Your Writing** — "Storytelling is storytelling" across genres.
2. **Choose Who You Want to Become on the Page** — Use writing to amplify your authentic voice.
3. **Contribute to the Political Climate** — Writing serves as activism.
4. **Use Writing to Make Others Care** — Help readers understand the world through your perspective.
5. **Decide What You Want to Accomplish** — Clarify purpose before writing.
6. **Be Selective About What to Include** — Narrow focus to one or two ideas; depth over breadth.
7. **Focus on Propulsion** — Maintain steady pacing to keep audiences engaged.
8. **Creatively Multitask** — Multiple projects; switch when blocked.
9. **Win Fairly** — Acknowledge opposing viewpoints; present strongest counter-arguments.
10. **Show Your Work** — Display reasoning rather than stating conclusions.
11. **Set the Right Type of Goals** — Skill-building > external outcomes.
12. **Ask for What You Want** — Publicly express aspirations.
13. **Don't Dim Your Light for Others** — Don't minimize confidence to comfort intimidated peers.
