# Reedsy — How to Write a Short Story in 8 Simple Steps

1. **Pick a simple, central premise** — A brief, powerful concept (e.g., Kafka's man-becomes-insect).
2. **Build a small but distinct cast of characters** — Distinguishable through appearance, behavior, speech.
3. **Begin writing close to the end** — Start *in media res* near the climax; avoid preliminary exposition.
4. **Shut out your internal editor** — Write freely in first draft; "anything goes."
5. **Finish the first draft** — Endings are crucial; avoid being too obvious or confusing.
6. **Edit the short story** — Tighten threads; eliminate unnecessary elements.
7. **Share with beta readers** — After stepping away for weeks.
8. **Submit to publications** — Traditional, contests, or self-publish.

## Key Craft Principles
- **Length:** 1,000–10,000 words.
- **Essential Elements:** Character, conflict, plot structure, POV, setting, theme.
- **Opening Impact:** First sentence must "pique the reader's curiosity, raises questions, and keeps them reading."
- **Editing Philosophy:** "Good writing is rewriting" (Truman Capote).
- Short stories typically encapsulate a mood, covering minimal incidents with limited cast.
