# George Saunders' Craft Principles & Philosophy

## Core Philosophy
Storytelling is central to culture and cognition. "The quality of our scale-model-maker determines the relative accuracy of the resulting model" we create about the world. Stories aren't escape — they're essential tools for understanding reality and developing empathy.

## Key Principles on Reading
- **Sentence-level attention.** Sentences are "the place where originality begins in fiction"; trust your instinctive reactions to language.
- **Reader authority.** Emotional and intellectual responses to stories form "the bedrock of criticism." Honor "micro-opinions" as valid analytical tools.

## Writing & Craft Advice
- Close, page-by-page analysis reveals how stories work mechanically.
- Writing exercises and editing moves develop practical skills.
- The mind creates "scale models" through storytelling — precision in language directly impacts how accurately we perceive reality.
- There's no "right way" to engage with craft; experimentation and permission to fail are essential.

## On Cultural Crisis
A "widespread failure of literary imagination" stems from how modern media delivers stories quickly rather than inviting deep, immersive reading.
