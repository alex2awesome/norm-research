# Short Story Craft Checklist (Writer's Digest / Writers.com)

## Essential Elements
1. **Protagonist with desire/need** — must want something they don't have, to drive story forward
2. **Clear dilemma** — minimal backstory; focus on how the dilemma is resolved
3. **Decisive action** — what does the protagonist *do* about the dilemma?
4. **Setting, plot, POV** — basic fiction elements present
5. **Single central storyline** — no subplots (those push toward novella)

## Revision Checklist
- Each word essential
- Each scene ramps up tension
- Each character clearly defined
- No exposition dumps
- Dialogue distinct per character
- Opening hook is in place
- Ending is earned, not arbitrary

## Tighter Common Lists (Synthesized)

### "5 Key Elements"
1. Plot (with conflict)
2. Character
3. Setting
4. Theme
5. Point of View

### "8 Elements" (some textbooks)
+ Tone, Style, Mood

### Short Story Specific Constraints
- Tight POV (usually one character)
- Single time frame or compressed timeline
- One major reversal
- Resonant ending — ambiguity often more powerful than tidy resolution
- Title often acts as commentary on the story

## Revision Questions
- Does the opening promise something the story delivers?
- Is the inciting incident clear and early?
- Does every scene serve the central conflict?
- Have I cut all explanation/justification I added in draft?
- Is the ending inevitable in retrospect, surprising in the moment?
