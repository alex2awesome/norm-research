# Gabriela Pereira — Mastering Point of View and Voice

## Point of View Fundamentals

**Sphere of Knowledge.** POV is fundamentally about "sphere of knowledge" — narrator has specific knowledge limits that differ from author and characters.

**Three Rules of POV:**
1. Keep it consistent — once you pick a lane, stay in it.
2. Keep it sustainable — works throughout the entire manuscript.
3. Keep it real — understand realistic conventions of each POV form.

## Voice Concepts

**Voice as Literary DNA.** Rather than something to "find," voice is your unique creative expression that already exists. Metaphor: your voice is a sunflower seed — can't force it to grow roses.

**Name Your Voice.** Identify your voice's characteristics. Pereira's: "Professor Valley Girl" — knowledgeable but approachable.

**Multiple Voice Layers:**
- Character voices (how individuals speak)
- Narrator's voice (story's telling perspective)
- Meta voice (in stories within stories)
- Author's voice (distinct expression)

## Revision Strategy
- **Reboot Scenes Rather Than Edit** — Rewrite from memory; preserves authentic voice and energy.
- **TK Markers** — Insert "TK" (rare letter combo) to mark changes for easy search.
