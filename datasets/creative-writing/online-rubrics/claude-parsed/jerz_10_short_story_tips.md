# Jerz — 10 Short Story Writing Tips

1. **Get Started: Emergency Tips** — "What does your protagonist want?" Clear goals, morally significant actions, meaningful obstacles, unexpected consequences, telling details.
2. **Write a Catchy First Paragraph** — Begin with tension; unusual, unexpected, action, or conflict.
3. **Develop Your Characters** — Know extensive details readers won't see; convey via appearance, actions, dialogue, internal thoughts.
4. **Choose a Point of View** — First (easiest), second (engaging but challenging), third (limited or omniscient).
5. **Write Meaningful Dialogue** — "Each speaker gets his/her own paragraph"; show emotions through specifics.
6. **Use Setting and Context** — Sensory details that matter; integrate with characterization and plot.
7. **Set Up the Plot** — Arrange events deliberately to reveal dramatic significance.
8. **Create Conflict and Tension** — "Only trouble is interesting"; mystery, progression, causality, surprise, empathy, universality.
9. **Build to a Crisis or Climax** — Turning point where characters recognize something new or make crucial decisions.
10. **Find a Resolution** — Show characters changing or seeing differently; open endings, clear resolutions, parallel images, monologue, dialogue, or symbolic imagery.
