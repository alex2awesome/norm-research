# The One Rule of Fiction Writing — Lincoln Michel (Counter Craft)

## Core Principle
**Skepticism toward universal writing rules.** "Every claim someone makes about how fiction works can be rebutted with counterexamples." Supposedly fundamental elements — character, plot, worldbuilding — aren't actually mandatory in all successful fiction.

## Stance
Written "for authors who don't want to follow rules." Resist dogmatic writing prescriptions and instead understand context-dependent storytelling. The newsletter's premise (Counter Craft) deliberately attacks conventional advice like "show don't tell" and "make your characters round" — telling is just as important as showing; you need flat characters too.

## Implication
The "one rule" is essentially: there is no one rule. Match craft choices to the specific story's needs, the medium's affordances, and the writer's intentions, not to received wisdom.
