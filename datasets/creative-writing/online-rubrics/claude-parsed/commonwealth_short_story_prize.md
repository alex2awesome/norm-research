# Commonwealth Short Story Prize

Annual prize for short stories from across 56 Commonwealth countries. Particularly aimed at writers from places with little or no publishing industry, bringing voices from underrepresented regions to international attention.

## Stated Judging Values

### Original Voices
"Original voices that reflect diverse experiences across the Commonwealth." Originality is dual: linguistic distinctiveness AND lived-experience distinctiveness.

### Creativity and Depth Across Genres
The prize is genre-open. Literary, speculative, romantic, historical — all eligible if executed with creativity and depth.

### Diversity of Experience
Explicit value placed on perspectives outside the Anglo-American literary mainstream. Cultural specificity is an asset, not a liability.

### Accessibility to Newer Writers
Particularly aimed at writers with "little or no work published." Established writers are not the target. The prize values the not-yet-recognized voice.

## Process

- **Anonymous judging** — judges evaluate without knowing author identity (mitigates known-name bias)
- **Initial screening** by reader team
- **Longlist of 200** → International judging panel
- Five regional judges (one each from Africa; Asia; Canada and Europe; Caribbean; Pacific)
- Each regional judge focuses on stories from their region

## Eligibility (procedural)

- 2,000–5,000 words
- Unpublished short fiction (some flexibility)
- Written by a citizen of any Commonwealth country (56 nations)
- Translated entries accepted (with translator credit)

## Implicit Quality Criteria from Selected Stories

Recurring qualities of winners and shortlisted writers:
- Specificity of place — locality grounded in concrete detail
- Voice that reflects regional speech without exoticizing
- Engagement with contemporary social conditions
- Literary craft (structural unity, language precision) at international standard
- Avoidance of "single-story" stereotyping (Adichie's term) of the writer's region

## Implication for Rubric

A short story can be assessed on:
- Authenticity of voice (regional / cultural specificity without exoticism)
- Originality (does this voice add to what exists?)
- Craft at the international literary standard
- Depth (intellectual and emotional weight)
- Genre fitness (whatever genre is chosen)
- Engagement with the writer's actual context

## Distinction from Other Prizes

Where the BBC NSSA and Sunday Times prizes value craft mastery in established writers, the Commonwealth Prize values **emerging voices from underrepresented regions** — a different but complementary axis. Together they illustrate how prize criteria embed implicit ideologies of literary value.
