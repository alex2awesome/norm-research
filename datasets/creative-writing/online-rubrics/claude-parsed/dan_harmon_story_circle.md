# Dan Harmon's Story Circle — 8 Steps

## The Circle
A simplification of the Hero's Journey, adapted by Dan Harmon (Community, Rick and Morty) for episodic TV writing. Now widely used in fiction.

## The 8 Steps
1. **You** (Comfort Zone) — Establish protagonist in their normal world
2. **Need** — Something they want; something missing/lacking
3. **Go** — Cross the threshold into the unfamiliar
4. **Search** (Adapt/Struggle) — Adapt to the new world; encounter obstacles
5. **Find** — They get what they wanted
6. **Take** (Pay the Price) — A cost; loss; sacrifice; reality check
7. **Return** — Back to the familiar world
8. **Change** — They are transformed; the familiar world reads differently now

## Quality Criteria
- **Symmetry** — steps 1↔5 (You/Find), 2↔6 (Need/Take), 3↔7 (Go/Return), 4↔8 (Search/Change) form mirrored pairs
- **Earned transformation** — the final state must be different from initial state
- **The cost matters** — without sacrifice/loss in step 6, the change feels unearned
- **Threshold crossing is real** — the protagonist must genuinely leave their comfort zone

## Why a Circle?
Harmon: rhythms of biology, psychology, culture move cyclically. Story echoes life's cycles of comfort/discomfort, conscious/unconscious, order/chaos.

## Influence
Adapted from Joseph Campbell (via Christopher Vogler). Used in screenwriting (Community, Rick and Morty episodes) and increasingly in novels and short fiction.
