# Sudowrite — AI Writing Tips for Fiction Authors (Underlying Craft Principles)

## Core Principles
- **Use AI for generating options, not final copy.** Author judgment refines.
- **Build your Story Bible first.** Character details, worldbuilding, genre conventions, style samples.
- **Match tool to need:** describe (sensory depth), rewrite (alternatives), brainstorm (direction), draft (scene expansion).

## Craft-Focused Techniques
- **Maintain voice consistency through customization.** Provide style examples and detailed character entries with speech patterns.
- **Adjust creativity settings strategically.** Low = close to patterns; high = unexpected.
- **Always read AI-generated passages consciously.** Evaluate match to voice, tone, direction.

## Implicit Craft Standards
- Sensory depth across all five senses
- Narrative pacing
- Dialogue authenticity
- Genre conventions
- "Show don't tell"
- Character voice consistency
