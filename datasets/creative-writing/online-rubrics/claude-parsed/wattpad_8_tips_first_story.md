# Wattpad — 8 Tips for Writing Your First Story

1. **Flesh out your ideas** — Set a timer for 15 minutes and get all ideas on paper without editing.
2. **Choose an inspiring title** — Keeps you focused; makes project feel real.
3. **Write your synopsis** — Brief high-level summary of plot points.
4. **Set writing goals** — Break into manageable tasks.
5. **Start small — and not at the beginning** — "A strong beginning often comes after the story is finished."
6. **Be flexible** — Allow story to evolve; modify subplots, characters as needed.
7. **Don't edit while you write** — Save revisions for after completion.
8. **Learn how to build a captivated audience** — Update frequently, edit properly, chapters 1,500–2,000 words, accurate tags, engage with community.
