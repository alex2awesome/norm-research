# Janet Burroway — Writing Fiction (Craft Elements)

The most widely used creative writing textbook in the United States.

## Core Craft Elements (Chapter Topics)
1. **Whatever Works** (the writing process)
2. **Showing Not Telling** (concrete sensory detail)
3. **Building Character** — direct vs. indirect characterization
4. **The Flesh Made Word** (character continued — desire, contradiction)
5. **Long Ago and Far Away** (setting, atmosphere)
6. **Story Form, Plot, and Structure** (Aristotle, Freytag, contemporary forms)
7. **Call Me Ishmael** (point of view)
8. **Is and Is Not** (theme, image, symbol, metaphor)
9. **Play It Again, Sam** (revision)

## Major Quality Categories
- **Significant detail** — concrete, definite, specific, sensory
- **Showing**: scene, image, dialogue, action
- **Telling**: when summary is right
- **Active not passive verbs**
- **Character via desire** — what the character wants drives the story
- **Character via contradiction** — interesting characters are inconsistent
- **Plot = pattern of conflict, crisis, resolution**
- **POV consistency** and intentional shifts
- **Theme as resonance, not message**
- **Image as the load-bearing unit of meaning**

## Burroway's Imaginative Writing (4-genre framework)
Five elements shared across fiction/poetry/drama/CNF:
1. **Image**
2. **Voice**
3. **Character**
4. **Setting**
5. **Story**

## Common Burroway Mantras
- "Specificity is the soul of narrative."
- "A story is a war."
- "Trouble" is the fundamental story unit
- Significant detail engages the senses, conveys atmosphere, conveys story
