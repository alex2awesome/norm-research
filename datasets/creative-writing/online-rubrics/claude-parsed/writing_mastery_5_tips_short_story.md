# Writing Mastery — 5 Tips for Writing a Short Story

1. **Focus on Critical Characters** — Limit cast to essentials; merge or cut.
2. **Center Around One Pivotal Event** — One pivotal moment, not multiple plotlines.
3. **Open with a Strong Hook** — Action, tone-setting, questions, or thematic statements.
4. **Explore Different Writing Styles** — Journal entries, monologues, poetry, dialogue-only.
5. **End on a Powerful Note** — Conclude once message conveyed; leave memorable moment.

## Bonus
1,000–10,000 words; "a singular plot or one main event" within compressed timeframe — distinct from novels.
