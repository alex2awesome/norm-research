# NEA Creative Writing Fellowship — Selection Criteria

## Program
$50,000 awards to published prose (fiction + creative nonfiction) and poetry writers. Two-year cycle: prose one year, poetry the next.

## Single Criterion
"The artistic excellence and artistic merit of the submitted writing sample."

## Process
- Anonymous review (panelists see only the writing sample, no name/bio)
- Approximately 2,000 applicants/year, ~2% awarded
- Different advisory panel each year — diverse by geography, race/ethnicity, artistic POV

## Implicit Criteria (from "artistic excellence")
The NEA leaves quality undefined; panelists judge on:
- **Sentence-level mastery**
- **Distinctive voice**
- **Strong imagination**
- **Risk-taking**
- **Achieved formal control**
- **Resonance and depth**
- **Originality**

## Why It Matters
NEA Fellowship is one of the most rigorous, anonymous-review fiction prizes in the U.S. The fact that quality is judged blind on text-only reflects the assumption that quality is *in the writing itself*, not in the writer's pedigree.

## Implication
A robust quality rubric should be applicable to a writing sample with no biographical context — pure text-based assessment.
