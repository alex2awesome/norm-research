# IB DP English Literature — Written Assignment Rubric

International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme rubric for Language A: Literature.

## Criterion A: Fulfilling the Requirements of the Reflective Statement
Assesses how well the student shows their understanding of cultural and contextual elements developed through interactive oral.
- **Level 1**: Superficial development
- **Level 2**: Some development
- **Level 3**: Adequate development
- **Level 4**: Substantial development
- **Level 5**: Convincing development

## Criterion B: Knowledge and Understanding
Assesses depth of engagement with the work.
- **Level 1**: Some knowledge but little understanding
- **Level 2**: Knowledge and understanding with some insight
- **Level 3**: Detailed knowledge and understanding with perceptive insight
- **Level 4**: Excellent knowledge with persuasive insight
- **Level 5**: Profound knowledge with original insight

## Criterion C: Appreciation of the Writer's Choices
Assesses how well student appreciates form, structure, technique, style.
- **Level 1**: Little appreciation
- **Level 2**: Some appreciation
- **Level 3**: Adequate appreciation
- **Level 4**: Excellent appreciation
- **Level 5**: Outstanding/insightful appreciation of how language, structure, technique, style shape meaning

## Criterion D: Organization and Development
Assesses essay structure, argument development, coherence.
- **Level 1**: Little organization or development
- **Level 5**: Sophisticated structure that effectively develops insights

## Criterion E: Language
Assesses clarity, accuracy, register.
- **Level 1**: Frequent inaccuracies; unclear
- **Level 5**: Clear, varied, sophisticated; appropriate register; accurate vocabulary

## What This Tells Us About IB's Aesthetic
- Form/structure/technique/style are *constitutive* of meaning, not decorative
- Originality of insight matters
- Cultural context is part of literary value
- Language *quality* (not just correctness) is criterial

## Adaptation as a Story-Quality Rubric
For evaluating creative writing rather than analyses:
- Knowledge → Internal coherence of fictional world
- Appreciation → Successful deployment of form/structure/technique/style
- Organization → Story architecture
- Language → Diction, syntax, voice
