# Interactive Fiction — Quality Criteria

(Relevant for prompt-driven, choose-your-own-adventure style work; some r/WritingPrompts continuations function this way.)

## Definition
Software/text simulating environments in which players use commands or choices to influence narrative.

## Quality Criteria

### Meaningful Choice
Each player choice should have *consequences* that ripple through the narrative. Choices that don't matter feel like padding; choices that change endings/scenes/relationships matter.

### Authored Constraint with Player Agency
The author designs the possibility space; the player navigates within it. Quality IF balances structure (you can't do everything) with freedom (your choices matter).

### Branching with Convergence
Pure tree branches grow exponentially and become unmanageable. Quality IF uses bottlenecks (story converges at key beats), state-tracking (game remembers what you did), and weighted variations (small differences across many scenes).

### Replayability
Best IF rewards multiple playthroughs — different choices reveal different sides, reveal hidden content, complicate first impressions.

### Voice and World
Even with player agency, the *implied author's* voice must be distinctive. The world-build, the prose style, the moral universe — all author-controlled.

### Pacing of Reveals
Information unfolds across choices and chapters. The player should always be discovering, not waiting.

### Character Arc Across Branches
Even in branching narratives, the protagonist (and major NPCs) should grow and change.

## Tools
- **Twine** — most popular, web-based, hypertext IF
- **ChoiceScript** (Choice of Games)
- **Inkle** (Inklewriter, Ink language)
- **Inform 7** — natural-language parser IF
- **TADS** — parser IF

## Quality Tests for IF
- Do my choices matter?
- Does the world feel real even within the constraints?
- Does the prose hold up at the sentence level?
- Are the characters memorable?
- Will I want to replay?
- Does the world have internal consistency?
- Are dead-end branches still satisfying?

## Connection to r/WritingPrompts
Many WP responses function as "linear IF" — the prompt is the player's choice; the response is the resulting narrative. The community's most-upvoted serializations operate like IF mini-arcs.
