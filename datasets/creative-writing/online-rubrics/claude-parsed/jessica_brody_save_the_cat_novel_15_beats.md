# Save the Cat! Beat Sheet — 15 Beats for Novel Writing

## Act 1
1. **Opening Image** (0–1%) — Visual snapshot of hero's current world.
2. **Theme Stated** (5%) — A character hints at the life lesson protagonist must learn.
3. **Setup** (1–10%) — Hero's flawed status quo, supporting characters, primary goal.
4. **Catalyst** (10%) — Life-changing event that prevents return to old life.
5. **Debate** (10–20%) — Hero wrestles with whether to accept the call.
6. **Break Into 2** (20%) — Hero accepts change; enters new world.

## Act 2
7. **B Story** (22%) — Helper character introduced.
8. **Fun and Games** (20–50%) — "The promise of the premise."
9. **Midpoint** (50%) — False victory or false defeat; stakes escalate.
10. **Bad Guys Close In** (50–75%) — Circumstances worsen; internal flaws intensify.
11. **All Is Lost** (75%) — Lowest point; "death of the old hero."
12. **Dark Night of the Soul** (75–80%) — Darkest moment before breakthrough.

## Act 3
13. **Break Into 3** (80%) — "Aha!" moment.
14. **Finale** (80–99%) — Hero proves transformation; conquers obstacles.
15. **Final Image** (99–100%) — Mirror of opening; hero's transformed life.
