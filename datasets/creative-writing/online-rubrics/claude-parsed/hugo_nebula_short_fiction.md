# Hugo & Nebula Awards — Short Fiction Criteria

## Categories (by length)
- **Short Story**: < 7,500 words
- **Novelette**: 7,500–17,499 words
- **Novella**: 17,500–39,999 words
- **Novel**: 40,000+ words

## Hugo Award (Reader Popularity)
- Voted by WorldCon supporting/attending members
- "Barometer of reader popularity rather than artistic merit"
- No formal criteria; reflects what genre readers loved most

## Nebula Award (Peer/Literary)
- Voted by SFWA active members (published professional SF/F writers)
- Provides "more literary judgment"
- Authors cannot nominate own work
- Top 6 nominations form ballot; winners chosen by month-long member vote

## Genre-Specific Quality Criteria for SF/F Short Fiction (synthesized)
- **Sense of wonder** — the genre's central affect
- **Conceptual originality** — a fresh idea, twist, or world
- **Worldbuilding economy** — establishes setting in few strokes
- **Internal consistency** — invented rules followed rigorously
- **Character-anchored** — speculative concept made personal
- **Resonant ending** — emotional or intellectual snap
- **Engagement with the genre tradition** — knows what came before
- Often: social/political commentary through speculative metaphor

## Eligibility Notes
- First English publication in a calendar year
- All science fiction, fantasy, or related fiction genres eligible
