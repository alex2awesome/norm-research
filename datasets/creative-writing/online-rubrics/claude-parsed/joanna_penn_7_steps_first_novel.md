# Joanna Penn — 7 Steps to Write Your First Novel

1. **Understand What You're Writing and Why** — Define success criteria; identify target readers; research 5–10 comparables.
2. **Fill Your Creative Well** — Document "sparks" (ideas, quotes, sensations) in notebooks/apps.
3. **Write a Story, Not Just Words** — Learn Three Act Structure; many first-time authors deliver "a load of typing, instead of a story."
4. **Create a Compelling Protagonist** — One main character. Ask: What does she want? What stops her? How does she overcome? How is she changed?
5. **Develop Plot and Setting** — Use setting to create conflict.
6. **Get Words on the Page** — Schedule writing time; ten-minute focused blocks. "The first draft of anything is shit."
7. **Embrace Rewriting** — Self-edit by printing; invest in professional editors. "Writing is rewriting."
