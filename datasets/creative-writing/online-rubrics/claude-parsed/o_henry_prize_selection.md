# O. Henry Prize — Short Story Selection Criteria

## Editorial Philosophy
"The editor has no set criteria nor does she look for something specific in the many short stories." Stories aren't required to have certain kinds of characters, settings, plots, voices, or styles.

## What the Editor Hopes For
"To be moved by stories that feel new and tell something readers didn't know or make them aware of something they knew but wouldn't acknowledge."

## Implicit Quality Criteria
- **Newness** — feels like something not read before
- **Revelation** — illuminates something previously unknown OR articulates something readers know but won't admit
- **Emotional impact** — moves the reader
- **No formula** — radical openness to form, voice, perspective

## Process
- Series editor reads broadly across magazines and online
- Forwards a curated long-list to a guest editor
- Guest editor selects 20 prize stories per year

## Eligibility
- Stories published in U.S. and Canadian magazines (now also translation-eligible)
- Each guest editor has unique vision reflected in selections
