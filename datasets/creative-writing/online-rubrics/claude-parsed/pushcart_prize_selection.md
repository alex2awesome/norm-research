# Pushcart Prize — Short Fiction Selection Criteria

## What the Prize Honors
~20 short stories per year drawn exclusively from independent publications (small presses & lit mags). Emphasizes:
- "Original prose narratives that showcase exceptional craft"
- "Insight into human experience"

## Implicit Quality Hallmarks
- **Standalone short stories** preferred over novel excerpts (highlights "self-contained artistry")
- **Average length ~5,000 words** — favoring complete narratives
- **Exceptional craft** at the sentence and structural level
- **Insight into human experience** — fiction with substantive moral/psychological vision
- Often: literary realism, formally daring work, marginalized voices

## Process
- Editors of small presses nominate up to 6 works
- Pushcart's Contributing Editors also nominate
- Panel of past winners + editors make final selections
- Judges' identities withheld to preserve integrity
