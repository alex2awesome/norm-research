# 25 Ways To Plot, Plan and Prep Your Story

1. **The Basic Vanilla Outline** — Numbered/lettered outline by sections or acts.
2. **The Reverse Outline** — Begin with the ending; work backwards.
3. **Tentpole Moments** — Identify 5–10 keystone events.
4. **Beginning, Middle, End** — Three paragraphs covering three acts.
5. **A Series Of Sequences** — 8–9 sequences, each containing multiple scenes.
6. **Chapter-By-Chapter** — Outline by novel chapters.
7. **Beat Sheet** — Map every beat without dialogue.
8. **Mind-Maps** — Bubbles/spokes connecting to a central hub.
9. **Zero Draft** — "Puke up the story" without quality concerns.
10. **In The Document, As You Go** — Outline the next section before proceeding.
11. **Write A Script** — Draft a screenplay-style version to move quickly.
12. **Dialogue Pass** — Let characters talk in isolation to discover voices.
13. **Character Arcs** — Track emotional/practical journeys.
14. **Synopsis First** — Write synopsis upfront for focus.
15. **Index Cards** — Organize on individual cards arranged visually.
16. **Whiteboard** — Notes, sketches, mind-maps, visual organization.
17. **The Crazy Person's Notebook** — Handwrite messy notes; tape into a journal.
18. **Collage** — Assemble images and quotes for mood.
19. **Spreadsheets** — Track plots/beats systematically in grid.
20. **Story Bible** — Worldbuilding, characters, rules, plot, theme, mood compiled.
21. **The Power Of Templates** — Save the Cat, Hero's Journey.
22. **Stream Of Consciousness Story Babble** — Free writing to discover structure.
23. **Visual Storyboards** — Sketch scenes; gather images.
24. **The Test Drive** — Non-canonical story to find voices.
25. **Pants The Shit Out Of It** — Abandon planning; write intuitively.
