# N.K. Jemisin's Writing Advice & Craft Principles

## Core Writing Philosophy
1. **Write Diverse Stories** — "It's human nature that we come in our own flavours."
2. **Write Observable Truth** — Fiction must be "true" by exploring universal human experiences.
3. **Embrace Discomfort** — Compelling stories place protagonists in peril.
4. **Break the Rules Knowingly** — Learn conventions before subverting them.
5. **Prioritize Wonder** — Magic should inspire awe; never mundane despite prevalence.

## Craft Techniques
6. **Test Different Approaches** — Write sample chapters in various tenses and viewpoints.
7. **Don't Over-Research** — Research informs but shouldn't overwhelm composition.
8. **Show Only Relevant Details** — Keep other research in notes.
9. **Find Your Natural Process** — Plotters and discovery writers both work.
10. **Write from Beginning and End** — Start knowing opening and conclusion.

## Professional & Personal
11. **Seek Human Connection** — Artists need contact with people.
12. **Define Success Personally** — Not bestseller status.
13. **Write for Yourself First** — Authentic work attracts true fans.
14. **Practice Persistently** — Skill develops through repetition.
15. **Accept Imperfect Beginnings** — Early unpublishable work is normal.
16. **Manage Writing Anxiety** — Nervousness indicates novelty; push through.
17. **Persist Through Doubt** — "If you're not good enough yet, work harder."

## Thematic & Political
18. **Challenge Conservative Structures** — Epic fantasy traditionally restores status quo; subvert.
19. **Combat Reactionary Thinking** — Sustaining reactive movements requires new targets.
20. **Foster Connection** — Help readers feel less isolated.
21. **Celebrate Dreaming** — Remind adult readers imagination is valid.
22. **Be Authentically Transgressive** — Permission to be unusual.
