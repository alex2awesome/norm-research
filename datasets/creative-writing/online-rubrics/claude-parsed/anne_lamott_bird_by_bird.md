# Anne Lamott — Bird by Bird: Some Instructions on Writing and Life

## Core Principles

### Shitty First Drafts
"The only way I can get anything written at all is to write really, really shitty first drafts." Perfectionism is the enemy of creativity. Get the words on the page; refine in later drafts.

### Bird by Bird
Take overwhelming projects one small piece at a time — "Bird by bird, buddy. Just take it bird by bird."

### The 1-Inch Picture Frame
Look at writing through a small frame — focus on a tiny scene, a single moment, rather than the entire daunting story.

### Polaroids
A piece of writing is like a Polaroid developing — you don't know what it'll look like until you write it.

### Character
- Know your characters intimately — what they want, fear, love, hate
- Plot grows out of character; characters drive action
- Listen to your characters — they will tell you what they want to do

### Plot
Plot emerges from character; not the other way around. The most authentic plots come from putting characters under pressure.

### Dialogue
- Dialogue should sound like real people speaking
- Read it aloud to test
- Each character must have a distinctive voice

### Setting / Detail
Use the senses. Concrete sensory detail grounds the reader.

### KFKD Radio (Self-Doubt)
The internal voices of self-doubt and self-aggrandizement; recognize and dismiss them.

### Jealousy & Writer Envy
A natural part of the writing life; manage it rather than be ruled by it.

### Reverence for the Material
Treat your subject and characters with respect; honesty matters more than cleverness.

### Broccoli (Trust Intuition)
Listen to your intuition — it knows what the story needs.

### Index Cards & Notebooks
Carry them constantly to capture observations, snippets of dialogue, ideas.
