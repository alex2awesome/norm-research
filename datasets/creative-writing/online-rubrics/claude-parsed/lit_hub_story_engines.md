# Lit Hub — The Engines of a Short Story

A short story can be powered by *different engines*; quality means the chosen engine works.

## Story Engines
1. **Plot-driven** — Desire, antagonism, turning points, spine, progression, crisis, climax (Aristotelian backbone)
2. **Character-driven** — Interiority, psychological revelation, transformation
3. **Voice-driven** — Idiosyncratic narrator who carries the reader by sheer presence (e.g., Lorrie Moore, Denis Johnson)
4. **Setting-driven** — Place itself shapes events; world dictates story
5. **Premise-driven** — A high-concept hook ("what if…"); the implications carry the story
6. **Mood/atmosphere-driven** — Building unease, awe, dread, tenderness — affect over event
7. **Image-driven** — A central recurring image that organizes meaning (Carver's title objects)
8. **Idea-driven** — Story as essayistic exploration of a thought
9. **Form-driven** — Unusual structure (lists, footnotes, fragments) generates meaning
10. **Language-driven** — The pleasure is in the prose itself

## Quality Criterion
Whichever engine drives the story, every other element must *not* fight it. A premise story doesn't need rich character interiority; a voice story doesn't need ornate plotting. Quality = coherence between engine choice and execution.
