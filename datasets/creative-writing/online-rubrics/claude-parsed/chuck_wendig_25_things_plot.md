# 25 Things You Should Know About Plot

1. **What The Fiddly Fuck Is "Plot," Anyway?** — Plot is the sequence of narrative events experienced by the audience.
2. **The Wrong Question** — People asking "What's the plot?" want a summary, not a tedious "and then" list.
3. **A Good Plot Is Like A Skeleton: Critical, Yet Invisible** — Plots function structurally but should remain hidden.
4. **Shit's Gotta Make Sense, Son** — Events must fit together with logical progression.
5. **The Quintessential Plot** — Conflict escalates progressively until resolution.
6. **In Life We Avoid Conflict, In Fiction We Seek It** — Tension drives narrative momentum.
7. **Want Versus Fear** — Plot emerges from desires conflicting with fears.
8. **Grow The Plot, Don't Build It** — Plots develop organically rather than being artificially constructed.
9. **The Tension And Recoil Of Choice And Consequence** — Decisions create branching narratives.
10. **Plot Is Promise** — Events foreshadow eventual payoffs.
11. **Let Characters Do The Heavy Lifting** — Authentic plots emerge from character emotions and actions.
12. **Chart The Shortest Point Between Beginning And End** — Avoid unnecessarily convoluted routes.
13. **On The Subject Of "Plot Holes"** — Stem from inattention, over-complexity, ignorance, or insufficient planning.
14. **Set Up Your Tentpoles** — Identify critical events; ensure smooth connections between them.
15. **The Herky Jerky Plot Shuffle Pivot Point Boogie** — Plots zigzag with multiple pivot points.
16. **Plot Is The Beat That Sets The Story's Rhythm** — Plot establishes pace aligned with emotional needs.
17. **Every Night Needs A Slow Dance** — Place tension release and false victories for respite.
18. **The Name Of My New Band Is "Beat Sheet Manifesto"** — Track beats to identify gaps and inconsistencies.
19. **Beats Become Scenes Become Sequences Become Acts** — Narrative units scale upward.
20. **Your Sexy Mistress, The Subplot** — Secondary storylines complement primary plots.
21. **Beneath Subplot, A Nougaty Layer Of Micro-Plot** — Small conflicts add texture without overwhelming.
22. **Exposition Is Sand In The Story's Panties** — Weave information into action; avoid dumps.
23. **On The Subject Of The "Plot Twist"** — Genuine twists emerge organically from setup.
24. **The Ending Is The Answer To A Very Long Equation** — Endings feel inevitable when they resolve accumulated elements.
25. **Plot Is Only Means To An End** — Plots serve characters and themes; readers care about meaning.
