# NCTE Achievement Awards in Writing — Rubric

National Council of Teachers of English. Awards excellence in writing by high school juniors.

## Holistic Evaluation Categories
"Writings are judged holistically on:"
- **Content** — substance of ideas
- **Purpose** — clear writerly intent
- **Audience** — awareness of and engagement with reader
- **Tone** — appropriate, controlled
- **Word choice** — apt, original
- **Organization** — coherent structure
- **Development** — ideas elaborated meaningfully
- **Style** — voice, sentence rhythm, distinctiveness

## Top-Score Descriptors (paraphrased)
- **Mastery of technical aspects** — control over punctuation, syntax
- **Powerful language use** — apt, original word choice, unique phrasing
- **Strong flow** — syntactical control and variation
- **Effective detail, evocative language, imagery** — to enliven writing and express ideas
- **Audience awareness** — clear sense of who's being addressed
- **Originality** — fresh treatment, not formulaic
- **Control of language over surface polish** — voice and command, not just clean copy

## Implicit Quality Stance
NCTE prioritizes:
- Purpose
- Audience awareness
- Organization
- Originality
- Control of language

…over:
- Surface polish (perfect grammar matters less than vivid voice)
- Mechanical correctness alone
- Five-paragraph essay forms
- Rigid genre adherence

## Forms of Writing Recognized
Personal narrative, journals, written reflections, observations, writing-to-learn — NCTE explicitly values *exploratory* writing, not just polished products.
