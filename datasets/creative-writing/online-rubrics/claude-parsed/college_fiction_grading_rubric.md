# College Fiction Grading Rubric (UCSB ENG 193)

## A-Range
"Stories in this range are smart, articulate, and written in engaging prose."
- Non-predictable plot lines
- Believable characters
- Strong familiarity with genre conventions (e.g., detective fiction)
- Satisfying resolution
- Genuine reader enjoyment
- Original premise or fresh treatment of familiar premise
- Sentence-level mastery

## B-Range
- Coherent plot
- Character consistency
- Demonstrates understanding of genre
- "Basically solid, engaging prose"
- BUT: contains issues that prevent A-level — flat characterization, predictable endings, inadequate setting, uneven pacing

## C-Range
- Basic engagement with story expectations
- Fundamental shortcomings:
  - Awkward writing
  - Underdeveloped characters
  - Unclear narrative arc
  - Unconvincing dialogue
  - Failure to satisfy genre conventions
- C+ is the ceiling for stories with persistent grammatical problems

## D-Range
At least one fundamental problem preventing satisfactory basic-level operation:
- Minimal genre fit (didn't engage the form)
- Severe writing flaws
- Unbelievable characterization
- Disjointed narrative structure
- Extreme lack of engagement

## F
- Plagiarism
- Profoundly inappropriate response
- (Plus institutional penalties beyond grade)

## What This Rubric Reveals
- The line between A and B is *prose* + *character believability* + *plot non-predictability*
- The line between B and C is *consistency* and *grammatical control*
- The line between C and D is whether *fundamental craft* is present at all
- Genre engagement is criterial throughout
