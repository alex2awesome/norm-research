# Kirkus Reviews — Fiction Reviewing Criteria

## Review Format
- 250–300 words per review
- Plot summary + concise unbiased opinion (positive, negative, or mixed)
- Identifies both strengths and weaknesses

## Elements Reviewers Assess
1. **Plot development** — does the story progress in a meaningful way?
2. **Character depth** — multi-dimensional, believable, growing?
3. **Writing style** — prose quality, voice, control
4. **Pacing** — no drag, no rush; chapters propel the reader
5. **Genre execution** — meets and ideally exceeds genre conventions
6. **Originality** — fresh angle on familiar territory
7. **Thematic resonance** — substance beneath surface
8. **Worldbuilding** (where applicable) — internal consistency
9. **Dialogue** — distinct voices, purposeful exchanges
10. **Ending** — satisfying, earned, memorable

## The Kirkus Star
Awarded to ~10% of reviewed books. Marks "exceptional merit." Decision based on:
- Knowledge of contemporary publishing trends
- Appreciation for exceptional writing
- Distinctive voice/perspective
- Craft mastery

## Reviewer Match
Reviewers are matched by editors based on:
- Content/genre expertise
- Style familiarity
- Sub-genre experience

## Famous Bluntness
Kirkus is known for being more critical than other industry trade reviews — a Kirkus star carries weight precisely because the publication is willing to pan books.
