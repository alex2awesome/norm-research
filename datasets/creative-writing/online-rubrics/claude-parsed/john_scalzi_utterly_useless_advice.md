# John Scalzi's Utterly Useless Writing Advice

1. **Writing is Work** — "It takes work. Lots and lots and lots of work." Professionals do it anyway.
2. **Write for Your Audience, Not Yourself** — Professional writing serves editors and readers.
3. **Approach It as a Business** — Commercial endeavor, not a romantic calling.
4. **Don't Quit Your Day Job Early** — Only when freelance income exceeds salary by 30%+.
5. **Accept Rejection as Inevitable** — Not personal; shouldn't derail you.
6. **Submit Work Persistently** — Resubmit rejected work to different markets.
7. **Follow Editor Guidelines Exactly** — Match market requirements precisely.
8. **Listen to Editors** — Don't be difficult about revisions.
9. **Be Flexible with Writing Types** — Success comes from willingness to write diverse content.
10. **Build Contacts Through Professionalism** — Be nice; deliver quality; help others.
11. **Cultivate Broad Knowledge** — Learn about many subjects.
12. **Balance Paid and Personal Work** — Maintain enjoyable writing alongside commercial.
