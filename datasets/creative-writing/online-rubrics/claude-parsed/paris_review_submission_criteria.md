# The Paris Review — Fiction Submission Guidelines & Editorial Standards

## Submission Specs
- Up to 40 pages of prose
- One piece per submission window
- Submittable in February, June, October; mail year-round
- Translations welcome (with original)
- Simultaneous OK (notify if accepted elsewhere)

## Editorial Standards (Implicit, from published fiction)
- **Literary fiction primarily** — character-driven, language-driven
- **Wide stylistic latitude** — from realism to experimental, with high craft demanded throughout
- **Distinctive voice** — recognized within first paragraph
- **Cultural / intellectual ambition** — engagement with ideas, history, art, philosophy
- **Sentence-level mastery** — every line should reward attention
- **Compression and depth** — even 30–40 page novellas hit hard
- **Resonance over plot mechanics** — the lingering effect after reading

## Editor Recommendation
"Read the most recent issues of The Paris Review to acquaint themselves with material the magazine has published."
