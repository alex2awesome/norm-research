# Brandon Taylor's Revision Process

## Core Distinction
Editing corrects errors and refines language. Revision involves fundamental changes to structure, character, plot, pacing, perspective, or thematic foundation. "Revision entails a change in the rule of law of a given piece."

## Seven-Step Revision Method
1. **Read the work** — Push through discomfort; read the entire piece without making changes; note strong emotional reactions.
2. **Create an outline** — Map the actual structure on the page (not imagined).
3. **List desired changes** — Note what you want to alter without judgment.
4. **Map changes onto outline** — Identify clustering patterns and density areas.
5. **Identify deeper issues** — Group changes under categories like "characterization," "pacing," "structure."
6. **Generate action items** — Hierarchical, specific targets.
7. **Execute revisions** — Work through one at a time; allow organic connections.

## Practical Tips
- Print and hand-retype chapters after significant revisions.
- Read aloud or use voice-to-text to catch confusion.
- Allow time away from manuscript for fresh perspective.
- Distinguish between concurrent editing versus true revision.
