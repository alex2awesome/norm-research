# Granta — Fiction Submission Criteria

## Editorial Mission
Granta has "a belief in the power and urgency of the story, both in fiction and non-fiction, and the story's supreme ability to describe, illuminate and make real."

## What They Want
- **Timely work** — pieces "that deal with the world as it is now, not what it was or what it will be"
- **Issue-shaped-by-submissions** — Granta tends to shape its issues around the work it receives, rather than asking authors to write to themes
- **First-publication only** — original material, never previously published online or in print
- **High-craft literary fiction** — descriptive force, urgency, perspective

## Length
- Most submissions: 3,000–6,000 words
- Maximum: 10,000 words

## Implied Quality Criteria (from Granta's editorial profile)
- A *world* must be made real on the page
- Sentences must be load-bearing
- The "urgency" test — does this need to exist now, in the world?
- Distinctive perspective and voice
- Often: hybrid forms, internationalism, political consciousness, reportage-tinged fiction
