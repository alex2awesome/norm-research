# 8 Writing Tips from Jeff VanderMeer

1. **Thinking Time Over Writing Time** — "The amount of time you spend writing isn't necessarily as important as the time spent thinking about what you are going to write."
2. **Capture Every Idea Immediately** — Notebooks/pens everywhere; "hasn't lost a single idea or scene fragment in twenty years."
3. **Train Your Subconscious** — Set intentions before sleep about story problems.
4. **Write During Peak Energy** — Mornings for drafts, afternoons for revision.
5. **Establish Healthy Habits** — Limited drinking, early bedtimes, exercise, less social media.
6. **Love Revision Unconditionally** — Imperfect drafts won't discourage you.
7. **Let Each Novel Guide Its Process** — Adapt approach based on each project's demands.
8. **Experiment with New Techniques** — When stuck: change environment, switch locations, change fonts.
