# Writer's Digest — 5 Tips for Crafting the Perfect Short Story

1. **Consider Your Plot.** Keep storyline straightforward and concept-driven. "Stories are more about concepts...and driven by characters, so you want to keep it straightforward on the plot level, deep on the concept level." Timeframe of days to weeks.

2. **Put Action Over Exposition.** Show character development through actions. Character traits emerge from what they do; this also propels narrative more than telling.

3. **Keep Dialogue Precise.** Have characters respond obliquely or reveal personality through answers. Answering "How are you?" with an unexpected response that hints at situation creates intrigue.

4. **Be Spare with Description.** Select one or two telling details rather than elaborate descriptions. Single distinctive feature lets readers fill in the rest.

5. **Prioritize Characterization.** Demonstrate personality through behavior and speech. "You can tell the reader that a character is nasty, or better yet, you can make them behave or speak in a nasty way."

## Key Principle
Louise Marburg: "economy in all aspects is the key" to successful short story writing.
