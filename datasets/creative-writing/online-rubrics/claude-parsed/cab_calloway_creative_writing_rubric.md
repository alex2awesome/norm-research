# Cab Calloway Creative Writing Rubric

(High-school arts conservatory rubric. PDF was binary; criteria reconstructed from search summaries.)

Categories typically scored 1–4 (Beginning / Developing / Proficient / Exemplary):

1. **Originality / Imagination** — Unique premise, fresh ideas, surprising angles.
2. **Voice / Style** — Distinctive narrative voice, intentional tone, control of register.
3. **Use of Language / Word Choice** — Precise diction, vivid imagery, figurative language.
4. **Structure / Organization** — Clear narrative arc, intentional pacing, scene structure.
5. **Character Development** — Multi-dimensional characters with motivation and arc.
6. **Setting & Atmosphere** — Sensory grounding, mood-establishing detail.
7. **Conflict & Theme** — Meaningful conflict; thematic resonance.
8. **Conventions / Mechanics** — Spelling, grammar, formatting, dialogue punctuation.
9. **Revision Evidence** — Drafts demonstrate considered editing and growth.
