# Asimov's Science Fiction — Writer's Guidelines

## Editorial Mission (from Sheila Williams)
"All fiction should be written to examine or illuminate some aspect of human existence, but in science fiction the backdrop you work against is the size of the Universe."

## What They Want
- **Character-oriented stories** — "characters, rather than the science, provide the main focus for the reader's interest"
- Science fiction primarily; also borderline fantasy, slipstream, surreal fiction
- Stories illuminating "some aspect of human existence" via SF backdrop

## What They Don't Want
- Sword & sorcery
- Explicit sex or violence
- Reprints
- Simultaneous submissions
- AI-assisted work (may be banned)

## Length
- 1,000–20,000 words (rarely outside this range)
- No serialized novels

## Pay
- 8–10¢/word ≤ 7,500 words; 8¢/word for excess
- $1/line for poetry (≤ 40 lines)

## Implicit Quality Criteria
- Human-centered, not gadget-centered, SF
- Speculative element must matter to characters' lives
- Strong, clean prose (Asimov's house style)
- Clear narrative spine even in literary/surreal experiments
