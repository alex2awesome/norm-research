# Henry James — "The Art of Fiction" (1884)

Landmark essay defending the novel as a serious art form against Walter Besant's prescriptive lecture.

## Central Doctrine

The novel is "a personal, a direct impression of life." Its only obligation is to **be interesting**. There are no a priori rules — the only criterion is the quality of the perception and the execution.

## Key Principles

### The Novel as Living Organism
"A novel is a living thing, all one and continuous, like any other organism, and in proportion as it lives will it be found, I think, that in each of the parts there is something of each of the other parts." Character and incident are inseparable: "What is character but the determination of incident? What is incident but the illustration of character?"

### The Air of Reality
"The air of reality (solidity of specification) seems to me to be the supreme virtue of a novel." Without it, all other merits collapse.

### "Try to be one of the people on whom nothing is lost"
The famous injunction to the writer: cultivate the most acute perception possible. The writer's task is to register, observe, and discriminate.

### Freedom from Rules
Reject prescriptive formulas. "A novel is in its broadest definition a personal, a direct impression of life: that, to begin with, constitutes its value." A novel must be free to be what it is.

### Experience Defined
"Experience is never limited, and it is never complete; it is an immense sensibility, a kind of huge spider-web of the finest silken threads suspended in the chamber of consciousness."

### Selection and Form
The novelist's intelligence is shown in **what is selected** for treatment and in the proportions given to each element.

### The Subject Cannot Be Judged
"The execution belongs to the author alone; it is what is most personal to him, and we measure him by that." Critics may not dictate subjects to novelists.

## Evaluative Criteria

A novel can be judged by:
- Solidity of specification (concrete, sensory reality)
- Coherence and organicity (parts reflect the whole)
- Quality of perception evident in the writing
- Honesty of impression
- Execution (the only proper subject of criticism)

## Implication

Fiction is an art equal to painting; subject matter is the writer's province alone; only execution may be judged.
