#3 by The Walrus King
Your story's Ranking is based mostly on your average rating + some adjustments for the number of ratings, and maybe some other things. (No one knows for sure.)

Ranking isn't seen by anyone but you. The only place it really shows up is on the Top Rated listings. 

Ratings go up and down as you jump ahead of other stories, or they jump over you. New ratings affect your Ranking.

#4 by MistOverSnow
This is what I've noticed.

There are around 60,000 fictions on Royal Road, give or take a bunch. New fictions seem to start at around 44000 in rank. If a new fiction is added then rank drops to 44001. If a higher rank fiction is deleted the rank will go up. 

Then you get a rating. If it's a high rating, like 4 or 5 stars, you will jump up thousands to like 25000 or so. And then it will up and down a bit by one or two, but mostly down by 2-20 per day as other fictions get their first ratings and go up in line. When you get another rating it goes up again, maybe to 20000 this time, and then slides again. The smaller the rank number the more ratings it takes to move up the ranks. Bad ratings send you down.

Followers, views, and favorites have an effect, but it is subtle enough that I've not been able to pinpoint what they do beyond preventing the inexorable downward slide.

The only thing this list does is put you on the Best Rated list, and that only happens in the top 100. Nothing you do will have a direct affect on it.

Re: How does this ranking system work?

#5 by KurtMKing
MistOverSnow Wrote: Followers, views, and favorites have an effect, but it is subtle enough that I've not been able to pinpoint what they do beyond preventing the inexorable downward slide.

Followers, views, favorites, etc. are not a part of the algorithm itself. Having more followers generally just means having more Ratings as a result of having more eyes on your fic and so more people likely to rate it. That's it.

Best Rated is based on an algorithm that uses 1) Your story's Rating, 2) the number of Ratings, the number of Reviews, and the number of Advanced Reviews (Reviews are counted more than pure Ratings, Advanced Reviews are counted more than normal Reviews), and 3) that algorithm's result in comparison to all other fictions on the site. Because of that, it's even possible for two or more fictions to share a Ranking - which is what happens at the lower ones and why brand-new fics with zero ratings aren't at 60K+ but much lower. If their score in the algorithm is the exact same, it gives them the same Ranking. It can even happen at higher Rankings - so #1 could actually be shared by several stories, but it's nearly impossible for that to happen.

If your Ranking goes down without you getting any new Ratings/Reviews, it's because a fiction below yours went above yours. Likewise, yours can go up if a fiction ranked above yours goes below yours.

MistOverSnow Wrote: The only thing this list does is put you on the Best Rated list, and that only happens in the top 100. Nothing you do will have a direct affect on it.


Best Rated is every fiction on the site, not just the Top 100. There's more than five pages to it. It's literally just the list of all stories on the site, Ranked.
