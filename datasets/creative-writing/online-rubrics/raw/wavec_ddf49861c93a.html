Poem (5)
Poetic Language and Fluency

Excellent

2

The poem uses figurative language and/or interesting vocabulary to attract the listener. The poem has a structure that makes sense audibly and flows well, with some form of sense and rhythm.
Moderate

1

The poem expresses some poetic language through moderate use of figurative language or vocabulary. The structure has some audible flaws and does not flow perfectly.
Below Average

0

The poem is lacking in most or all poetic language. The poem is difficult to listen to due to structural errors.
Idea and Theme

Excellent

2

This poem has a clear idea and purpose, showing a coherent theme throughout the piece.
Moderate

1

The poem somewhat has an idea or purpose, with some clarity in their theme.
Below Average

0

The poem fails to meet the requirements for ideas or theme, lacking a story or purpose.
Tone and Mood

Excellent

1

The poem portrays a clear emotion from the writer's words/attitude. The poem creates a mood through vocabulary choices and phrasing.
Moderate

.5

The poem partially creates a set emotion from the writer. The poem creates a mood that is understandable but not as clear.
Below Average

0

The poem is lacking in emotion and tone. The poem has little to no set mood.
Performance (5)
Body/Facial Movement

Excellent

2

The poet shows clear emotions through their bodily movements, their face, and perhaps an onstage prop. These emotions enhance the mood and tone of the poem.
Moderate

1

The poet shows some emotions with their body/props. The emotions may be unclear at times or contradictory to the poem, but are relevant for the majority of the time.
Below Average

0

The poet shows little to no emotion. The poet's emotions contradict the poem.
Vocal Strength/Emotion

Excellent

2

The poet has articulate emotion in their voice, reflecting the poem well. The poet projects loudly towards the microphone. The poet uses their voice to help convey the message of the poem.
Moderate

1

The poet has some emotion in their voice, or reflects the poem oddly sometimes. The poet does not project very well.
Below Average

0

The poet has little to no emotion in their voice. The poet does not project loudly enough at all.
Fluency

Excellent

1

The poet performs their poem with little/no stammers. The poet's voice is articulate and easy to understand.
Moderate

.5

The poet stammers more than a few times. The poet's voice is unsteady at times and may be unclear at some points.
Below Average

0

The poet stammers profusely. The poet's voice is undecipherable at many points.
