Congratulations to Professor Aracelis Girmay for being awarded the PEN/Voelcker Award for Poetry Collection for her book Green of All Heads.

Professor Karen Russell’s novel "The Antidote" has been selected as a finalist for The National Book Critics Circle Award in Fiction

Congratulations to Professor Karen Russell for being a finalist for The 2025 National Book Critics Circle Awards. The National Book Critics Circle honors outstanding writing, fosters conversation about reading, criticism, and literature, and gives annual awards in six categories.
