Aristotle
(384 BCE – 322 BCE)
→
  • related portals: Ancient Greek philosophy
  • sister projects: Wikipedia article, Commons gallery, Commons category, quotes, Wikidata item

(Greek: Ἀριστοτέλης Aristotélēs); Greek philosopher; student of Plato and teacher of Theophrastus and Alexander the Great; wrote on many subjects, including physics, metaphysics, poetry, theater, music, logic, rhetoric, politics, government, ethics, biology and zoology
