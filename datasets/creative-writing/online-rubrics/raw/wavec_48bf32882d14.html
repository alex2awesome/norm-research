List of Twitter pitch events 2024

Last updated: 8 November 2024

Hello! Welcome to this list of Twitter pitch events coming up in 2024.

Pitch events (also called pitch contests or pitch parties) are usually one-day events on social media during which writers use a particular hashtag to get eyes on their manuscript pitches. This is often in the hope of grabbing the attention of agents and publishers, but there are also community-only events for cheering each other on.

With Twitter being in a turbulent state at the moment, there is uncertainty around the future of pitch events on the platform. Many have been discontinued, others have moved elsewhere. The list below gathers all the information I could find about what is happening and where. I will update it as more information becomes available throughout the year.

If you’re planning to take part in any of these events, please double-check the details beforehand on the organisers’ social media/websites, since schedules can easily change.

Do your pitches pack a punch?

Get feedback on your pitches from an experienced editor, so you can be sure you’re making a big impact in a short space.

Hi, I’m Jake! I helped my Pitch Wars mentees craft pitches that caught the eye of multiple top SFF agents. I’d love to help you, too. Find out more about my pitch and query critique services, or get in touch with any questions!

Discontinued and inactive Twitter pitch events

  • #APIpit is on hiatus (link opens in a new tab) from 2024 onwards until the organisers can find a better alternative to Twitter
  • #BeRePit (link opens in a new tab) didn’t run as scheduled in January or July 2024 and appears to be inactive
  • #CanLitPit took a hiatus in 2023 (link opens in a new tab) and still appears to be inactive
  • #CompIt (link opens in a new tab) hasn’t run since March 2023 and appears to be inactive
  • #DVpit is taking an indefinite hiatus (link opens in a new tab)
  • #LGBTNPit has been discontinued (link opens in a new tab)
  • #MoodPitch (link opens in a new tab) was cancelled in November 2023 and the next event has not been scheduled
  • #PBPitch has ended (link opens in a new tab) after its last event in June 2024
  • #PitMad ended in 2022 (link opens in a new tab) along with the Pitch Wars mentoring contest
  • #QIApit (link opens in a new tab) hasn’t run since October 2023 and appears to be inactive
  • #SFFPit last took place in 2022 and the next event has not been scheduled (link opens in a new tab)
