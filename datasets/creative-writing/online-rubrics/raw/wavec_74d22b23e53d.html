Each star rating is its own average of what people give it. The other 4 categories other than the main one are from advanced reviews only. The main rating is the average from regular reviews, advanced reviews, and anonymous ratings people leave without a review.

Re: How does a stories rating work

#3 by luda305
It's just a simple average. 

Okay, I think I understand after looking at your page.  Each of the Overall Score, Style Score, Story Score, Grammar Score, and Character Score are independent of each other.  They have no effect on each other.  Within each, it's a simple average.  What this also means is that the last four will be an average from all the advanced reviews, while the first one will be an average of ratings (no reviews), basic reviews, and one component of the advanced reviews.
