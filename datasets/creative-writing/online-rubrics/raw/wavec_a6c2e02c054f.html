Representing Literary Excellence Worldwide

WME’s books division prides itself on operating as a boutique literary agency, providing hands-on representation of our authors while at the same time accessing incredible resources within the WME universe.

WME’s books division is proud to represent bestselling and award-winning authors in all genres including commercial, upmarket, literary fiction and non-fiction, as well as a wide range of children’s books and graphic novels.

SUBMISSIONS

We spend a lot of time reviewing  unsolicited manuscripts and love  championing first-time authors.

You can query the WME Agent of your choice through their profile page on this website, accessible through the “Our Team” pages. On each profile, you’ll find a bit about what each agent represents, as well as their preferred query format. Use the query button provided to write an email to the right address! Please be sure to only query one WME agent at a time —
if you haven’t heard back in six weeks, you can feel free to try another agent in the department.

By sending your materials to our agents, you’re agreeing to our Submission Terms & Conditions, so be sure to read them before you hit send.
