Pep Talks

Pep Talks are inspirational letters written by well-known authors during NaNoWriMo. They’ll be delivered to your NaNoMail inbox throughout November.

Learn more about this year’s pep talkers.

Read pep talks from the past seven years.

2014 Pep Talkers

Jim Butcher is the author of the The Dresden Files, The Codex Alera, and the upcoming Cinder Spires series.

Read Jim’s pep talk.

Kami Garcia is the co-author of the Beautiful Creatures series, and the author of Unbreakable and Unmarked.

Read Kami’s pep talk.

Tamora Pierce is the author of the Song of the Lioness and Immortals quartets, the recent Battle Magic, and many others.

Read Tamora’s pep talk.

Veronica Roth is the author of the Divergent series.

Read Veronica’s pep talk.

Brandon Sanderson is the author of the Stormlight Archive, Mistborn, and the final books of the Wheel of Time series, among many others.

Read Brandon’s pep talk.

Chuck Wendig is the author of the Heartland Trilogy, the Miriam Black series, and The Kick-Ass Writer.

Read Chuck’s pep talk.

Pep Talk Archive
