Finding Work

  • ACES: The Society for Editing
    ACES is an international members' alliance of editors working in digital media, traditional print media, corporate communications, book publishing, academia, government, and more. Its job bank connects those in need of publishing services with experienced communication professionals. There's also the ACES Editors for Hire directory. [online]
  • American Association of University Presses—job listings
    Search for jobs in scholarly publishing. [online]
  • American Medical Writers Association
    This group of medical writers and medical editors provides a member directory and job bank, both accessible by members. Employers and those needing to contract with freelance writers or editors can search the freelance directory and post job openings and project openings. [online]
  • Association of Earth Science Editors
    This group of freelance earth science editors provides a member directory and a job bank, both accessible only by members. [online]
  • Bay Area Editors' Forum
    This group of freelance editors and other communication professionals, based in the the San Francisco Bay Area of California, provides a public member directory searchable in three ways: by name or keywords, by job title, and by ZIP code. [online]
  • Board of Editors in the Life Sciences
    Those who join this organization after passing a rigorous 3-hour examination for certification as an editor in the life sciences, indicated by the "ELS" designation, have access to a members-only e-mail list and to members-only job listings and can be listed in a publicly accessible member directory. [online]
  • Bookjobs.com
    Use this site, sponsored by the Association of American Publishers, to find jobs in all fields of book publishing anywhere in the United States, whether in business, design, editorial, marketing, sales, or technology. [online]
  • Cambridge Academic Editors Network
    This group of freelance editors and other communication professionals, based in the area of Cambridge, Massachusetts, provides a public member directory searchable in three ways: by editor name, by members' skills, and by subject area. [online]
  • Chartered Institute of Editing and Proofreading
    This group of freelance editors and proofreaders in the United Kingdom provides a searchable public member directory. [online]
  • Crafts Publishers' Directory
    This is a directory of publishers of crafts books and magazines. [online]
  • Council of Science Editors Career Center
    Listings of freelance and in-house jobs provided by medical and science journals, publishers, and pharmaceutical companies and hosted by the Council of Science Editors, which serves members in the scientific, scientific publishing, and information science communities by fostering networking, education, discussion, and exchange and by serving as an authoritative resource on current and emerging issues in the communication of scientific information. [online]
  • Directory of CE-L Freelancers
    Copyediting-L (also known as CE-L), an e-mail discussion list for copyeditors from around the world, provides this public directory listing those of its subscribers who are self-employed. On the website, click the "Freelancers" tab, and then use your browser's page-search function to find entries related to specific skills or areas of expertise. [online]
  • Editorial Freelancers Association (EFA)
    This group of freelance editorial professionals, including editors of all kinds (medical, fiction, nonfiction, academic, literary, poetry, developmental/substantive, and more), authenticity readers, proofreaders, indexers, writers, desktop publishers, design and layout specialists, formatters, typesetters, photographers, translators, project managers, researchers and fact-checkers, transcriptionists, writing coaches, subtitlers and captioners, permissions editors, website creators, and marketing and promotion specialists from all over the world provides a public member directory searchable by member name, skills, specialties, hardware used, software used, location, type of materials handled, and style-guide proficiency. Dues-paying members of the association receive emails from the organization's Job List. Publishers, magazines, other businesses, and individual authors needing editorial freelancers can post project opportunities there. Before members contact each potential client, they'll already have access to a description of project scope, plus details on pay and on location, if the project is on-site. Go here to get information about how to post project opportunties for freelancers. Also, see the The EFA Bookshop, which sells booklets that editorial freelancers need for building their skills [online]
  • Editors' Association of Canada
    This group of freelance editorial professionals, including editors, proofreaders, indexers, writers, desktop publishers, translators, project managers, researchers and fact-checkers, and permissions editors, from Canada provides a public member directory searchable by skills, languages, specialties, media, subject matter, keywords, and location. [online]
  • Editors of Color Database
    Employers and clients can use database as tool for increasing diversity among their staff members, freelancers, and consultants. As an editor of color, you can join the database. Also note that the website has the Database of Diverse Databases. [online]
  • Editors Victoria
    This group of freelance editors in Victoria, Australia, provides a public member directory. [online]
  • European Medical Writers Association
    This group of freelance medical writers provides a member directory and job bank. The organization is open to medical editors too. [online]
  • iHirePublishing
    Post your résumé and look for publishing jobs. [online]
  • Institute of Professional Editors Limited (IPEd)
    This group of freelance editors in Australia and New Zealand provides a public member directory searchable by name, product or medium, service, subject area, language other than English, keyword, or company or business name. [online]
  • Investigative Reporters and Editors
    The job listings posted by Investigative Reporters and Editors, a nonprofit organization, are for positions such as investigative reporter, news developer, federal politics report, investigative producer, photographer, and data journalist. [online]
  • Journalism.co.uk
    Find writing and editing jobs in publishing, journalism, and advertising. Most listings are for jobs in the United Kingdom, but a few are for jobs elsewhere in Europe or in India. [online]
  • JournalismJobs
    Find writing and editing jobs in publishing and public relations and with newspapers, television, radio, magazines, and nonprofits. [online]
  • LinkedIn
    Use this national networking site to look for jobs in publishing and journalism. [online]
  • "Marketing Tips for Freelancers"
    Follow the tips in this post from the blog EditorMom to get your name out there in front of clients. [online]
  • MediaBistro's job list
    A list of full-time and part-time job opportunities for U.S. media professionals. [online]
  • Northwest Independent Editors Guild
    This alliance of professional editors in Washington, Oregon, Alaska, and Idaho provides a browsable public member directory. There is also a job board, accessible only to dues-paying members of the guild, that lists part-time, full-time, and freelance work opportunities. [online]
  • Professional Editors Network
    This group of freelance editorial professionals, which includes editors, writers, proofreaders, indexers, and book layout specialists, provides a public, searchable member directory. [online]
  • PublishersGlobal
    This is a directory of publishers around the world, organized by country, subject matter, media, language, city, and publishing-related services. [online]
  • PublishersLunch
    A daily e-mail newsletter about deals in publishing. [online]
  • Publishers Marketplace
    Listings of job openings in publishing. [online]
  • Publishers Weekly
    Compilations of job listings by the highly respected industry magazine. [online]
  • San Diego Professional Editors Network
    This group of freelance editorial professionals, including editors, writers, project managers, photo researchers, marketing specialists, researchers and fact-checkers, indexers, and desktop publishers, provides a public member directory, searchable under the categories of editors, writers, and other services. [online]
  • Science Writers in New York
    This organization for science and medical writers and editors has a public jobs list and offers two e-mail lists, one for members only and one open to both members and nonmembers. [online]
  • Chartered Institute of Editing and Proofreading
    This group of freelance editors and proofreaders in the United Kingdom provides a searchable public member directory. [online]
  • Society of English-Native-Speaking Editors
    This group of English-speaking editors in the Netherlands provides a public member directory. [online]
  • Society of Professional Journalists
    This group of U.S. journalists, made up of both employees and freelancers, provides a jobs directory, searchable by city, state, and specialty. [online]
  • Society of Young Publishers
    This U.K. group of young professionals in publishing and related trades provides a members-only job bank. [online]
  • Society for News Design
    Search for jobs in journalism. [online]
  • Society for Scholarly Publishing—job listings
    Search for jobs and freelance projects in scholarly publishing. [online]
  • The Write Jobs
    Job listings in journalism, publishing, and medical and technical writing and editing. [online]
