What can I do at Scribophile?

Scribophile is a huge writing community with resources for writers of all skill levels.

What stage of your writing journey are you at?

I want to write, but I don’t know how to start.

  • Participate in our free daily online writing sprint, Writing Together.

    The best way to start learning to write is by keeping a daily writing habit. You don’t have to know a thing about writing to show up and put a few words down on the page — it’s the habit that’s important. Writing Together is perfect for building and maintaining a writing habit!

  • Read about the craft in our free Writing Academy.

    To start learning the fundamentals, check out our free Writing Academy for hundreds of articles covering everything from the basics to advanced topics.

  • Chat with writers in our Writing Forums.

    The Forums are where writers of all skill levels meet to chat. Our forums are busy with writers from all over the world chatting 24×7. Fill out your personal profile, see what people are discussing, ask your own questions, and make friends!

  • Practice with our free weekly Writing Challenge.

    Each Monday we post a new writing prompt to inspire you to write a short story. These challenges aren’t graded or critiqued — instead, they’re a way for you to practice your writing muscle in a fun, judgement-free way. Once you’ve submitted your entry, read what other members wrote to see how they approached the same prompt. (By the way, a great time to work on your entry is at Writing Together!)

  • Give feedback on what other writers have posted.

    Giving feedback is one of the best ways of learning how to look at your own writing with a critical eye. You don’t have to be a pro to let a writer know what did or didn’t work in their story — anyone can give feedback, even if they’ve never done it before. Check out our guide to writing a great critique for lots of helpful pointers. (Oh, and you’ll earn karma points while you’re at it!)

  • Once you’re ready, submit some writing for feedback!

    The heart of Scribophile is our feedback system, which gets you guaranteed feedback on your writing from writers from all over the world. You can submit anything at all for feedback — it doesn’t have to be a totally finished work. Poems, flash fiction, short stories, nonfiction, drafts of absolutely any stage — absolutely everything is accepted, and we’d love to help you improve it!

I’m working on a draft of a story or novel.

  • Submit chapters for feedback to the Main Spotlight.

    The heart of Scribophile is Main Spotlight, which is where you can post your work for guaranteed feedback without having to meet or manage readers. You can post anything, from early drafts to polished revisions — our members will help you improve your writing, no matter what stage it’s in. Read about how to post your first work for critique and about how our karma point system works.

  • Join groups to share your writing with writers with the same interests.

    Groups are a great way to meet and chat with other writers who are reading and writing the same kind of stories that you are. You can join as many groups as you like, and each one has its own discussion forum that you can use to chat about anything!

  • Get feedback from a group of trusted critique partners in your Personal Spotlight.

    Once you’ve joined some groups and made some friends, post your work to the Personal Spotlight to get more critiques for your karma point spend. The Personal Spotlight awards maximum karma points to your followers and groupmates, and you get 6 critiques instead of 3 for the Main Spotlight — but the tradeoff is that your posts aren’t visible in site listings, so you have to personally ask your frends and groupmates to critique.

  • Keep up your writing momentum with our free daily online writing sprint, Writing Together.

    Finishing your draft is all about keeping up momentum, and getting some words down every day is the key to maintaining that momentum. Writing Together is perfect for building a writing habit that will help you finish that draft!

  • Perfect your craft by studying our free Writing Academy.

    The best stories are carefully architected according to well-studied principles of storytelling. Learn all about these principles in our free Writing Academy, which is chock-full of articles covering everything your story needs to shine, from character archetypes, to plot arcs, to dialogue, and everything in-between.

  • Discuss the writing life and your progress in our Writing Forums.

    Our forums are busy 24×7 with writers from all over the world chatting about the craft. Fill out your personal profile and dive right in to talk with your fellow writers and make friends!

  • Practice with our free weekly Writing Challenge.

    Each Monday we post a new writing prompt to inspire you to write a short story. These challenges aren’t graded or critiqued — instead, they’re a way for you to practice your writing muscle in a fun, judgement-free way. (By the way, a great time to work on your entry is at Writing Together!)

I have a novel ready for beta reading and...
I want to meet and manage beta readers directly.

  • Post your novel for free in the Beta Read Spotlight.

    The Beta Read Spotlight is a place where you can post your entire novel without having to earn or spend karma points. This spotlight is designed to facilitate full-novel exchanges with your trusted beta readers. Your beta readers can give you feedback chapter by chapter using our critiquing system, and you can keep track of feedback all in one place with your novel’s Critique Overview. Read about how to post your first work for critique.

  • Browse our Beta Reader Directory to directly query beta readers interested in your genre.

    Our Beta Reader Directory lists tens of thousands of readers who want to give you feedback on your complete novel for free. You can search them by interest, genre, and specialty. Once a beta reader accepts your query, you can send them a link to your manuscript that you’ve posted for free in the Beta Read Spotlight. You’ll manage your beta readers directly — keep complete control!

  • Join groups to meet more beta readers and writers in the same genre.

    Groups are where readers and writers with the same interests meet. You can join as many groups as you like to meet other potential beta readers, and to chat with other writers writing in your genre.

I want feedback on my manuscript, but I don’t want to meet or manage beta readers.

  • Submit chapters for feedback to the Main Spotlight.

    The heart of Scribophile is Main Spotlight, which is where you can post your work for guaranteed feedback without having to meet or manage beta readers. You can post anything, from early drafts to polished revisions — our members will help you improve your writing, no matter what stage it’s in. Read about how to post your first work for critique and about how our karma point system works.

  • Join groups to share your writing with writers with the same interests.

    Groups are a great way to meet and chat with other writers who are reading and writing the same kind of stories that you are. You can join as many groups as you like, and each one has its own discussion forum that you can use to chat about anything!

I want to meet other writers and build a supportive group of friends.

  • Participate in our free daily online writing sprint, Writing Together.

    Writing Together is a great way to meet other writers wile getting your writing done. Join us every day for an hour of quiet writing that will help you maintain your writing habit, while giving you time to get that next chapter done.

  • Chat with writers in our Writing Forums.

    Writing can be a lonely passion, but our busy Writing Forums are the cure for feeling isolated. They’re busy with writers from all over the world chatting 24×7. Fill out your personal profile and jump in to meeting writers and discussing the craft — or anything at all!

  • Join groups to meet writers and readers with the same interests.

    There are thousands of groups at Scribophile, and they’re a great way to meet other people who are reading and writing in the same genre as you. Each group has its own discussion forum that you can use to chat with other writers.

I just want to critique writing and beta read.

  • Browse the Main Spotlight for work that you can read and critique immediately.

    The Main Spotlight is the centerpiece of Scribophile — it’s where authors post their writing to get feedback from the Scribophile public. All of the work listed there is up for reading and critique!

  • Fill out your Beta Reader Profile and have authors contact you to beta read their books.

    Our Beta Reader Directory is where authors browse for beta readers to read their books. Authors will contact you based on your genre interests and share books for you to beta read for free!
