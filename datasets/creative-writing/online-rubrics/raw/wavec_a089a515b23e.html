Cleis Press accepting fiction and nonfiction proposals

Posted on November 8, 2022

0


Located in Hoboken, New Jersey, Cleis Press is an indie publisher focusing on LGBTQ, BDSM, romance, and erotic writing for all sexual preferences.

Cleis Press is accepting proposals for the following:

  • LGBTQ+ nonfiction
  • Nonfiction on the topic of sex and sexuality (self-help, sexual guides, LGBTQ+ and/or women’s studies, feminism, dating and relationships, sexual health, memoir)
  • Erotic fiction (erotica/romance)

Check out the Cleis Press catalog here

How to submit: By email only, specific instructions provided at their website

Be sure to follow detailed guidelines at Cleis Press Submission Guidelines
