Tools for Writing LGBTQ+ Fiction

Jul 20, 2025
4 min read
Michael Stewart

Table of Contents

Pride month may be over, but diverse storytelling is always in season. At Sudowrite, we embrace inclusion as a fundamental value, recognizing that stories are the heartbeats of our community. Representation is important—everyone has a story to tell, and deserves the tools that will help them tell it.

Whether you're weaving a tale of queer romance or exploring the complexities of non-binary experiences, Sudowrite is here to help with features that make your writing process easier and more joyful. Below we’ll outline some reasons you may want to use Sudowrite to craft  your bold and beautiful LGBTQ+ fiction.

Story Bible: The secret weapon for Queer storytelling

One of the gems within Sudowrite's toolkit is Story Bible—an ally in your creative process. Imagine having a trusted friend who remembers every detail about your characters, their journeys, and the worlds they inhabit. That’s what the Story Bible does. It’s like a living source of truth that evolves along with your story, holding the threads of your narrative together.

Story Bible is an exceptional resource in LGBTQ+ storytelling It handles the intricacies of gender identities, pronouns, and evolving character arcs with care and precision. It’s a space to explore dynamic relationships, the essence of chosen family, and the vibrant communities we create. The Story Bible supports you in capturing the beautiful complexity of queer life, ensuring your story remains authentic and compelling.

Story Bible helps the AI honor and revel in the vibrant mosaic of your story.Here’s how:

1. Track gender identity, pronouns & arcs

You can assign pronouns and identities to each character and keep them consistent across chapters. The Story Bible also lets you document how a character’s gender identity might evolve, their preferred names at different story stages, and the emotional milestones they hit along the way. This is especially important in stories involving trans or non-binary characters whose arcs include self-discovery and affirmation.

2. Interwoven relationships

Many LGBTQ+ stories thrive on the dynamics of chosen family, queer friendship, and evolving relationships. The Story Bible helps you track not just who your characters are, but how they relate to one another—whether it’s a slow-burn romance between two pansexual leads or the emotional tether between a gay teen and his drag mother.

3. Cultural and community contexts

You can use the Story Bible to build and document the fictional or real-world queer communities your characters live in. Define the norms, support systems, language, and histories that shape their experiences in Worldbuilding. This level of detail deepens authenticity and gives your story a lived-in, resonant feel.

4. Story Bible Remembers

If you’ve used other AI tools, you might have found yourself repeating yourself or correcting the AI. Story Bible creates a source of truth that helps makes sure that Sudowrite won't forget. When names change, you want the AI to remember.

NSFW Generation: affirming Queer intimacy

One of the most liberating aspects of writing LGBTQ+ stories is the ability to explore queer intimacy on your own terms. But many AI writing tools restrict or sanitize these scenes—especially when they fall outside of heteronormative expectations. Often ChatGPT and other tools will refuse to write erotica altogether. Sudowrite takes a different approach.

1. Safe, private, respectful erotica tools

Sudowrite enables opt-in NSFW generation through a private, respectful, and writer-first lens. You can create intimate scenes that feel authentic to your characters—without worrying about judgment, censorship, or AI that refuses to engage. Whether you’re writing tender first kisses between two non-binary characters, sensual moments in polyamorous dynamics, or explicit erotica for queer adults, Sudowrite gives you the freedom and control to go as far as you want.

2. Customizable heat levels

You can tailor the level of heat in a scene—from sweet and suggestive to fully explicit—so that it aligns with your tone, genre, and audience. The AI respects your boundaries while offering fluid, evocative language that enhances emotional intimacy and representation.

3. Inclusive pleasure

Sudowrite helps you write pleasure that isn’t constrained by gender binaries. It understands that intimacy isn’t one-size-fits-all and supports you in crafting moments that reflect a spectrum of bodies, identities, and orientations. Queer intimacy deserves depth, joy, and agency—and Sudowrite supports that at every level.

4. Supportive Community

Sudowrite runs free live classes almost every day of the week, and those classes extend to writing compelling queer erotica. The conversation continues in our community Discord, which is made up of over 15,000 authors.

LGBTQ+ Plugins: tailored tools for Queer stories

Sudowrite has lots of tools, but if it’s ever missing a button for something you need, there may be a Plugin that will do the job. Plugins are like browser extensions, letting Sudowrite do things it didn’t originally.  Lots of Plugins are specifically tailored to support LGBTQ+ writers and narratives. Here are some standouts:

Gender consistency checkup

This is a smart assistant that analyzes your text to ensure consistent usage of pronouns and gendered terms. It’s ideal for maintaining clarity and respect in character portrayal across stories with diverse gender identities.

Series outline generator: gay encounters

This tool helps you create structured, gay-themed series outlines. It generates chapter-by-chapter summaries with built-in LGBTQ+ elements, streamlining the planning process for multi-book narratives.

Sensitivity reader: gender & sexuality

This plugin is a content-sensitive reader that scans your writing for potential pitfalls related to gender and sexuality. It surfaces flagged areas and revision suggestions to help you portray LGBTQ+ identities thoughtfully and inclusively.

Write a gay spicy novel

This utility is a genre-specific generator that crafts romantic or erotic scenes with gay characters. Perfect for authors writing spicy queer fiction, it quickly generates scene ideas, dialogue, and plot details aligned with LGBTQ+ romance tropes.

For a full list, see the Plugins directory, where you'll discover a wide range of bespoke tools to empower your storytelling. And if there’s something you need that you can’t find—you can build it yourself (without code).

Conclusion: writing with pride, power, and possibility

The stories we tell shape the world we live in—and LGBTQ+ stories have always been vital, defiant, and deeply human. Whether you’re crafting a sweeping queer romance, a speculative epic starring non-binary heroes, or a raw personal narrative of transition and self-discovery, Sudowrite is built to support your voice.

Equipped with features like the Story Bible for seamless consistency, NSFW generation for genuine intimacy, and LGBTQ+ plugins that highlight representation, Sudowrite can be more than just a writing tool. It can be your creative ally, celebrating the complexity, beauty, and strength of queer storytelling.

You deserve tools that resonate with your unique voice—tools that empower you to tell the stories only you can tell. With Sudowrite, you're never writing alone; you're creating alongside a partner who truly understands.

Speak your truth. Celebrate your journey. Write boldly — with Sudowrite.
