Embracing Our Commitment

Unleashing the Power of Sensitivity Reading

The essence of representation is undeniable. In an evolving world that seeks true diversity, authors have an unprecedented opportunity to steer this transformation. Authentic representation in literature plays a vital role, painting a broader spectrum of shared human experience. See #representationmatters on Twitter, Geena Davis's groundbreaking studies on gender in media, and USC's eye-opening report on Hollywood's diversity shortfall for a glimpse into the pressing need for change.

Ready to make a difference? Excellent! That's where sensitivity readers step in. These insightful professionals are transforming the author's toolbox, providing nuanced critiques to enrich your writing and promote true representation, rather than reinforcing harmful stereotypes. They scrutinize your work for microaggressions, word choice accuracy, and outdated tropes.

At Salt & Sage Books, we celebrate authors passionate about inclusivity. To empower your journey, we offer a roster of trusted readers, each bringing their unique experiences to the table. Browse through their profiles on our Sensitivity & Expert Reader page.

Diving Deeper into Expertise

Known as "targeted beta readers," expert readers possess real-life experiences or in-depth knowledge aligned with your narrative's core elements. For example, if your protagonist is a horse enthusiast, and equine understanding isn't your forte, our expert reader with horse expertise steps in. They spot intricate details to infuse your writing with an added layer of authenticity and depth.
