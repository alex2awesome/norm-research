Welcome to Absolute Write Water Cooler

Welcome, writers! Absolute Write is a moderated, free, online community of writers, agents, editors, and publishers. Absolute Write provides information about writing as well as editing, publishing, and agents. We welcome writers of all levels of experience and ambition.

PLEASE NOTE: We have a strict zero-tolerance policy for anything AI-generated or AI-assisted, be it creative writing, forum posts, graphics or even your avatar. Use of any material that has been generated with the assistance of AI will be deleted, and the member using it banned.

Please read The Newbie Guide to Absolute Write

Can't log in? You may need to reset your password. Check your email (and spam folder!). The email you register with us must be valid. If you're having troubles, use the Contact Us button at the bottom of the page.
