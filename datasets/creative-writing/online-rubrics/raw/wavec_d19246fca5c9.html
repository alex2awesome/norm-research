Books & Literary Media

Words that shape the world beyond the page

WME is one of the world’s largest and longest-running book agencies with hundreds of titles sold in the U.S., UK, and internationally.

Client authors – a blend of New York Times Best Sellers, household names, and breakout stars – have received some of the most prestigious literary awards. Clients publish all types of fiction and non-fiction books including novels, children’s books, comic books, cookbooks, biographies, and more. Beyond the page, WME represents authors in the sale of their dramatic rights across all media. When clients choose to have parallel careers as screenwriters and/or producers, WME represents them as their literary agency in film and TV. WME strategically brings together creative elements for each project it represents, whether a book, short story, graphic novel, screenplay, script for stage, news article, video game, or podcast.
