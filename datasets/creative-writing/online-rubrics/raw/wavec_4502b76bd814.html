Re: ratings vs reviews

#2 by Aapjuh
Ratings are generally just the star rating (half star all the way to 5 star) by your readers which get combined into an overall rating.

Review is a star rating + feedback to either the author or a potential reader about the story that will be visible to everyone on your story page. The reviewer can choose between an overall star rating (half star all the way to 5 star) or an advanced star rating (separate scores for: Overall, Style, Story, Grammar, Character)

Re: ratings vs reviews

#3 by Artim
You have 3 categories. All of them require user to have RoyalRoad account. You might want to read this from the knowledge base (FAQ). It explains some things.

1. Rating
Reader picks from 0.5 to 5 stars. Ratings are anonymous, you can't see who left what. They have effect in overall score statistic.

Next two both count as review, but have a bit of distinction:
2. Review
Rating as above but user has to leave actually written review. There is required word count and rules that should be followed. You can see who left review and what they had to say.

3. Advanced review
Has effect on all rating categories: Overall Score, Style Score, Story Score, Grammar Score and Character Score. Also has rules that user who left it should follow. As name suggests, it is advanced review. It is split into different categories to rate as well as overall score.
