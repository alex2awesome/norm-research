    Anyone can make a review, as long as they have an account.
    They can either choose to give a single general score (out of 5 stars) or they can give an "Advanced Review", which just lets them score individual points of the story.

    "The Group" is just a group (derp) that give extensive reviews to stories (often on request). But their score is not worth more or less than a regular person's review.
