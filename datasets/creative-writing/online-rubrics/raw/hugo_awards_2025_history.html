2025 Hugo Awards

Presented at: Seattle Worldcon 2025, Seattle, Washington, USA, August 16, 2025

Hosts: K. Tempest Bradford, Nisi Shawl

Base design: Joy Alyssa Day

Lodestar Award trophy design: Lynne Taylor Fahnestalk

Awards Administration: Kathryn Duval, Rosemary Parks, Nicholas Whyte1, Esther MacCallum-Stewart1

 

1,338 valid electronic nominating ballots were received by the deadline of March 14 at 11:59 p.m. PDT and counted from the members of the 2024 and 2025 World Science Fiction Conventions for the 2025 Hugo Awards. ​​Unfortunately, two mailed ballots were received 2.5 weeks later on April 3 after the deadline of receipt. (The initial publication of the finalists had an error of 1,738 ballots instead of the correct number of 1,338.)

1962 final ballots were counted over all 21 categories, including this year’s special category for best poem.

Questions about the Hugo Awards process may be directed to hugo-help@seattlein2025.org.
