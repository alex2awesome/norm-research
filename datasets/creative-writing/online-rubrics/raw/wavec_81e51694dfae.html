WELCOME!

Romance Writers of America/New York City, Inc. is an inclusive organization that welcomes writers from all backgrounds and stages in their career. We strive to promote excellence in romantic fiction and advance the professional interests of career-focused romance writers through networking, advocacy, dissemination of information, professional education, and publications.


Over the years, we have hosted industry and business panels with editors, agents, marketing and media specialists, lawyers, and tax professionals. We have organized craft presentations with bestselling authors, and we run an annual contest for unpublished manuscripts.

The Great Blurb  Game: Building an Irresistible Romance Hook Mad-Libs Style


Think blurbs are harder to write than the book itself? You’re not alone.


In this fast-paced, interactive workshop, we’ll turn blurb writing into a game—while uncovering the key ingredients that make romance readers click “buy now.”

Working in teams, you’ll build a blurb from the ground up using guided prompts, trope challenges, and a simple, repeatable structure. Along the way, you’ll discover how to highlight character, conflict, chemistry, and stakes—and how those elements shift across romance subgenres from romcom to romantic suspense.

Expect collaboration, laughter, and a few surprising “I could actually use this” moments. You’ll leave with a practical framework you can apply to your own books right away—and a clearer sense of how to turn your story into a hook readers can’t resist.

RWA/NYC believes authors should embrace whatever publishing avenue works best for them. Therefore, we have authors who are traditional published, self-published and hybrid. Our members are published in full-length fiction, as well as shorter formats, in a broad array of sub-genres. They have also won numerous industry awards, including the RITA, Golden Heart Awards, and the Prism award. They appear on bestseller lists in the New York Times and USA Today, and on bestseller lists at Barnes & Noble and Amazon.com.
