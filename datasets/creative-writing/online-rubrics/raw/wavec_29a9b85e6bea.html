Frequently Asked Questions

JOB OPENINGS
 How do I apply for a job at your Company?

To view a list of all positions currently available, please click here and follow the instructions to respond to an appropriate position.

 Is there an intern/ work experience position that I can apply for?

Internships are available in all departments of the company. Through bi-weekly rotations, students who participate in the program will have the opportunity to use their strengths to contribute to the work of various departments. Please visit our jobs page, or keep an eye out on our Twitter to find out about these openings.

Authors
 How do I write to an author? Can you give me an author's e-mail address?

We are not able to release the phone number or mailing and e-mail addresses of any of our authors. However, you may be able to make direct contact with an author if he or she has a website.

If you would like to contact an author for an interview, or other publicity/media request, please visit our Publicity Contacts page

 I would like to have my book signed by the author. How can I get that done?

HarperCollins does not have a method of handling requests for signed copies, but you might be able to make direct contact if the author has a website. Look for a website link on the author page of harpercollins.co.uk, or put the name into a search engine such as google.com or yahoo.com to see. 

Books
 I am a book reviewer and would like a copy of a particular book. Whom should I call?

If you are requesting a review copy for publicity purposes, please contact the appropriate division by visiting this page. 

 What is your HarperCollins Donation Policy?

HarperCollins believes strongly in charitable giving and supports organizations dedicated to promoting literacy and a love of reading but we do not accept unsolicited donation requests. We partner with a variety of organisations and donate more than 150,000 books annually.

Writers
 How do I submit a manuscript? Can you send me a copy of your Writer's Guidelines? Do you accept manuscripts from unagented writers?

Unfortunately, HarperCollins does not accept unsolicited submissions. Any unsolicited manuscripts, proposals or query letters that we receive will not be returned, and HarperCollins is not responsible for any materials submitted. We recommend that you consult your local bookstore or library for sources that can direct you in locating an appropriate agent and/or publisher.

 Is there is a web location that I could monitor for updates regarding manuscript submissions?

A few of our UK divisions run Open Submissions throughout the year, which you can usually find via their respective social media accounts. 

Company
 I would like to know more about the history of HarperCollins. How long has the company been in existence?

Please visit the Company Profile page.

Website
 I can't find the book I am looking for using the Search tool on your website, but I know HarperCollins published it. What am I doing wrong?

You can put the title, author’s name, imprint, and series name, into the search tool and find results. If your search returns no results, the book you are searching for may be out of print. HarperCollins.co.uk displays only books that are currently available.

Permissions
 Who can I contact regarding requesting permission to use content or reproduce images in HarperCollins Books?

Written permission is required to reprint or reproduce material from HarperCollins Publishers books. Click here for information on how to request permission.
