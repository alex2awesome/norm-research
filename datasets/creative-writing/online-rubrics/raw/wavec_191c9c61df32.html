Submitting Your Work to Writers House

 Before submitting to Writers House

The Market List recommends you verify the query and/or submission guidelines for each literary agent and literary agency before sending a query or submitting your manuscript. Most agencies require a completed manuscript prior to queries or submissions. Review and verify the agency requirements for working with new or unpublished writers as well as their specific areas of interest.

About Writers House:

Writers House was founded in 1973 and represents authors writing all types of popular fiction and non-fiction. Writers House offers its clients expertise in contributing to all phases of the editorial and publishing processes, with a goal of maximizing the value of its clients' work by providing hands-on editorial and marketing advice, as well as leading the way in branding, licensing, and selling film/TV, foreign, audio, dramatic and serial rights.

Writers House requests hard copy queries to only one agent within the agency at a time.
