SUBMISSIONS

We want to hear from you

We spend a lot of time reviewing unsolicited manuscripts and love championing first-time authors. Thank you for thinking of us and sharing your work – we’re delighted to hear from you.

Query the WME agent of your choice via their profile page, accessible via the “Our Team” tab. On each profile, you’ll find information about each agent including their preferred query format. Use the query button provided to submit your query. By sending your materials, to our team, you’re agreeing to our Submission Terms & Conditions, so please read them thoroughly before you submit.

Please be sure to only query one agent at a time as we work collaboratively internally – if you haven’t heard back in six weeks, please feel free to try another agent in the department and keep us posted with any developments on your projects.

Due to the volume of inquiries received, we’re unfortunately unable to respond to every query. Please note, we do not consider or represent any screenplays.

Combating Fraud

Email fraud is ever more prevalent and sophisticated. There has been an unfortunate influx in scams with impersonators posing as WME agents, WME authors, and editors at publishing houses. Please scrutinize emails that purport to be from WME or others in the publishing industry. WME agents will contact you only from their WME email addresses (@wmeagency.com), not personal domains such as gmail, and will never request upfront payments or fees for editorial advice.

General Query Guidelines

We’re so excited to consider your work. We’ve noted our specific query preferences (how many sample pages, etc.) on our individual profiles, so please review them before hitting send on your email.

Because of the volume of inquiries we receive and our commitment to our clients, we’re unfortunately not able to respond to every query. While you can only send your project to one WME agent at a time, please feel free to query another agent here if you haven’t heard back in six weeks. In the meantime, please do keep us posted with any developments on your project.

Also, our team is books, books, books!   We do not represent or consider screenplays.
