Content crawled via the Wayback Machine Live Proxy mostly by the Save Page Now feature on web.archive.org.

Liveweb proxy is a component of Internet Archive’s wayback machine project. The liveweb proxy captures the content of a web page in real time, archives it into a ARC or WARC file and returns the ARC/WARC record back to the wayback machine to process. The recorded ARC/WARC file becomes part of the wayback machine in due course of time.

Convergence

Welcome to Convergence! This MAA publication offers a wealth of resources to help you teach mathematics using its history.

  • See Featured Items (below) for our newest articles.
  • Browse our annual Tables of Contents (What's in Convergence?).
  • Play Who's That Mathematician? in our Paul Halmos Photo Collection.
  • Explore our ever-expanding Mathematical Treasures collection.

Make Convergence your source for math history and its use in teaching!
