Help out

$\mathsf{Pr} \infty \mathsf{fWiki}$ is a great resource for mathematical proofs but can not maintain itself. If you want to help $\mathsf{Pr} \infty \mathsf{fWiki}$ expand and grow check out the lists of things below.

Things to do

  • Add proofs! See this page and this page for specific proofs that users want added.
  • Give explanations or expand parts of proofs
  • Tell your friends
  • Expand pages that are only stubs
  • If you are particularly knowledgeable, you may be able to resolve an issue which the contributors have so far not been able to sort out.
  • Check existing proofs for mistakes
  • Contribute to pages containing proofs which may not be valid or complete
  • Fix up pages that do not look good
  • Make sure proofs are in the correct categories, and subcategories
