Bridges

The Bridges Organization oversees the annual Bridges conference on mathematical connections in art, music, architecture, and culture. Since 1998, Bridges has traveled to North America, Europe, and Asia, and has attracted participants from over thirty countries. The conference features invited speakers, regular and short paper presentations, hands-on workshops, a juried art exhibition, and arts performance events.

Bridges Galway 2026

The 2026 Bridges conference will take place at the University of Galway in Galway, Ireland, 5–8 August 2026. We look forward to seeing you there!

  • “Inside the Bridges MathArt Conference: Navigating Math, Art and Meaning”: a blog post by the Crearte foundation about Bridges 2025.
  • A blog post about Bridges 2024 by Paula Krieg.
  • George Hart’s article ‘What Can We Say About “Math/Art”?‘ in Notices of the AMS discusses the relationship between mathematical art and the rest of the art world, and references Bridges and other conferences.
  • Richard Hammack’s report on Bridges 2022 in the Nexus Network Journal.
