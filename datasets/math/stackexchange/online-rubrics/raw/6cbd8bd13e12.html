Nagoya Mathematical Journal

The Nagoya Mathematical Journal is published quarterly. Since its founding in 1950 by a group led by Tadashi Nakayama, the journal has sought to publish original research papers of the highest quality and of general interest, covering a broad range of pure mathematics. The journal is owned by Foundation Nagoya Mathematical Journal, which uses the proceeds from the journal to support mathematics worldwide, and it is published by Cambridge University Press in print and electronic versions. The electronic version is hosted online at CambridgeCore.

Authors of articles published in volumes 1–176, who do not wish for their work to be freely available at CambridgeCore, may request that the Foundation remove their work.
