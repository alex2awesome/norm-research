1. Submission of manuscripts

One paper copy of the manuscript intended for publication should be sent to:

Acta Mathematica
Institut Mittag-Leffler
Auravägen 17
SE-182 60 Djursholm
SWEDEN

Please note that only this one paper copy should be submitted at this stage. Electronic submission is not possible.

Normally, only manuscripts prepared using LaTex (preferred) or TeX are accepted. Allowable languages are English, French and German.

Receipt of the submission will be acknowledged by the Institute, and the author will then be asked to send the manuscript electronically to the editor in charge.

Copyright. By submitting a manuscript, an author acknowledges that it is not being considered, nor will be submitted, for publication elsewhere, and agrees that the sole copyright is transferred to the publisher. This transferal is revoked if and when the publisher decides not to publish the manuscript. The publisher's copyright covers the exclusive right to reproduce and distribute the article by any means intended or effectively used to make it available for inspection, reading, or consultation, including, but not limited to, printing, reprinting, photocopying, and electronic means. The publisher grants the author, however, the right to distribute a small number of preprints and reprints, and to have the submitted manuscript available on the author's own homepage.

Colour figures. Acta Mathematica is printed in black/white. If you wish to have figures printed in colour, you have to be prepared to contribute to the extra costs. In the online version colour figures will appear free of charge.


2. Resubmission of accepted manuscripts
Once the author has received notice that the manuscript has been accepted for publication, a final version should be prepared and submitted electronically. For details, please see Instructions for authors.
