# Attend Talks and Conferences — Terry Tao (Career Advice)

## Key Recommendations
"Modern mathematics is very much a collaborative activity rather than an individual one."

### Core Benefits
1. **Broaden perspective.** Exposure to nearby fields provides valuable context for your own research.
2. **Build community.** Conferences help identify key researchers and establish collegial relationships.
3. **Sustain motivation.** Inspiring presentations can reinvigorate enthusiasm.
4. **Realistic expectations.** Don't expect complete comprehension of every talk: "As long as you learn something, the effort is not wasted."

## What Makes Mathematics Productive
- Working diligently within your field.
- Developing broad knowledge across related areas.
- Asking questions actively and seeking dialogue with peers.
- Considering the larger mathematical landscape.
- Combining intelligence with patience and persistence.

The overarching principle: **isolation undermines breakthrough work.**
