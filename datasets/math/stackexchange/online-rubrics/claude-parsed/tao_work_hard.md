# Work Hard — Terry Tao (Career Advice)

## Core Principles

1. **Hard work over genius.** "Mathematical breakthroughs are not powered solely (or even primarily) by 'Eureka' moments of genius, but are in fact largely a product of hard work."
2. **Reading, writing, and thinking together.** Success requires literature engagement, written drafts, and deep thought — not contemplation alone.
3. **Master the details.** Understanding demands reading all relevant literature and writing detailed sketches and complete treatments, not only grasping grand concepts.
4. **Build foundational knowledge.** Progress requires learning and relearning the field, understanding tool strengths and limitations, staying informed.
5. **Quality over quantity.** "Forcing oneself to work even when one is tired, unmotivated, unprepared, or distracted" becomes counterproductive. Focused hours beat numerous distracted hours.

## Supporting Criteria
- Enjoy your work to sustain effort.
- Think strategically ahead rather than obsessing over single problems.
- Maintain multiple projects to prevent burnout.
- Recognise when projects need modification or abandonment.
- Distinguish productive work from merely maximising work hours.

## Implication
A piece of math writing of "good" quality reflects sustained, disciplined effort across reading + writing + thinking, not flash insight alone.
