# Be Flexible — Terry Tao (Career Advice)

## Core Principles

1. **Flexibility in goals.** Long-term goals "should not be set in stone, and should be updated when new developments occur." Research is unpredictable.
2. **Avoid premature commitment.** Announcing work on famous problems before having a feasible plan can make it "harder to gracefully abandon the problem."
3. **Grant proposal wisdom.** Stating "I will solve <Famous Problem X>" lacks credibility without a coherent plan and demonstrated progress on intermediate milestones.
4. **Career decisions.** Don't choose institutions based on single faculty members; they may relocate or your interests may shift.

## What Good Mathematical Practice Looks Like
- Responsive adaptation to emerging developments.
- Willingness to explore new problems and fields that prove more interesting.
- Learning tools and knowledge outside one's initial specialty.
- Realistic, staged approaches to challenging problems.
- Maintaining multiple research directions rather than obsessive focus on one goal.
