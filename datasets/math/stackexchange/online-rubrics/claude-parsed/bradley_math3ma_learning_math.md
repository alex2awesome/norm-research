# Learning How to Learn Math — Tai-Danae Bradley (Math3ma blog)

## Core Principle
"To fully understand a piece of mathematics, you have to grapple with it. You have to work hard to fully understand every aspect of it."

## Do's
1. **Analyse from multiple angles.** Examine concepts from every side, like inspecting a phone from all sides to understand its complete structure.
2. **Investigate foundational ideas.** Identify the core principles underlying mathematical concepts.
3. **Ask critical questions.** "What is it that they all have in common?"
4. **Struggle intentionally.** Wrestle with mathematical ideas to internalise them.
5. **Prioritise understanding over speed.** Comprehension and clarity over rapid computation.

## Don'ts
1. **Don't memorise mechanically.** Avoid rote formula memorisation without grasping underlying concepts.
2. **Don't treat math as symbol-manipulation.** Mathematics involves deep ideas, not arithmetic or notation alone.
3. **Don't rush.** Understanding takes time and cannot be accelerated without sacrificing depth.

## Overall Philosophy
Learning math means transitioning from passive symbol-following to active thinking, where you see mathematics with genuine clarity rather than mere procedural compliance.

## Implication for "Good Math Writing"
A "good" exposition slows the reader down, forces grappling, and reveals the underlying ideas rather than presenting symbol-manipulation as a finished script.
