# What to Publish — Igor Pak (2022)

## Core Principles

1. **Solve major open problems.** The clearest path to publishable work is resolving significant unsolved questions; these stand alone without needing broader narrative.
2. **Build a coherent research story.** Rather than scattered contributions, develop "a series" of interconnected papers that collectively advance understanding. "You start your talk by telling what's the state of the sub-area" and show how your work fits this arc.
3. **Quality over quantity.** Many publications can damage your reputation. A strong job talk requires coherence, not "a patchwork quilt" of disparate papers.

## Criteria for Worth-Publishing

- **Narrative fit.** Does the paper advance your main research story?
- **Community relevance.** Will it be discoverable and useful to others?
- **Distinctiveness.** Genuine contribution, not repetitive variation?
- **Accessibility.** Can you explain its significance in a compelling colloquium talk?

## What NOT to Publish
- One-off results unrelated to your primary research direction.
- Work to inflate publication counts.
- Papers submitted to low-quality or predatory venues.

## Key Warning
Mediocre work can have "negative impact on your job search," as evaluators assess publication portfolios' coherence and significance.

## Implication
"Good math" includes a strategic dimension: which results are worth publishing at all is itself a quality signal.
