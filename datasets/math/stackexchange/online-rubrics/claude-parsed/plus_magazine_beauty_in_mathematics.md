# Beauty in Mathematics — Plus Magazine (UK)

A short piece distilling the working aesthetic vocabulary, anchored on Euler's identity.

## Six Criteria

1. **Unexpected connections.** "Three very strange numbers which are not connected in any evident way combine to give such a normal and familiar result."
2. **Accessibility.** Beauty is enhanced because the explanation "isn't too difficult to see"; advanced expertise not required.
3. **Linking the unfamiliar to the familiar.** "Very strange numbers" with "very ordinary and fundamental ones."
4. **Logical progression.** "A sequence of logical steps and identifying patterns" reveals "incredible truths and interconnections."
5. **Simplicity emerging from complexity.** Arrival at the "seemingly simple numbers 1 and 0" after fairly complex mathematics.
6. **Sense of discovery.** "Treading a little-known path through the mathematical jungle to reach a secret destination."

## Implication
Mathematical beauty is not esoteric but accessible to thoughtful engagement; it surfaces when seemingly unrelated concepts unite elegantly.
