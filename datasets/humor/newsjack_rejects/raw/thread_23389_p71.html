Boom, done!

Some really high quality stuff this series, so well done if you got something on or made the recording. If you didn't, don't worry, it's still a very useful exercise.

Nothing beats having this kind of deadline and the prize of having your work broadcast by the BBC to focus your mind and hone your skills. If you stuck with it throughout the series, I can guarantee you've added some new skills to your repertoire that you didn't have six weeks ago.

I spent this weekend in a massive ball of rage and stress after being screwed on a house purchase, so my mind wasn't on it, and I didn't have as much time as I wanted to dedicate to writing this week, hence only one sketch sent. On Tuesday morning, I didn't really have any one liners to speak of, but I spent an hour at lunchtime going through the news and working out my ideas, and by the end of that I had about 12 I was happy with. Now, I don't think they're my best work, but a year ago, I would've struggled to even find that many jokes in an hour. So, if you didn't get anything on this series, don't be disheartened, there is a lot of competition, and you're in a better position as a writer than you were at the start of the series...
