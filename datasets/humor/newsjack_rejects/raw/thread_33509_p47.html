I managed to reach Tuesday morning with about five number-crunchers and three one-liners (one or two of which were either unsuitable, not funny or no good).

Just as I think I might be starting to get the hang of it, the series comes to an end

(On the other hand, I might not be getting the hang of it at all, as tomorrow's revelatory post will show!)

It would be great if there was another similar open-submission opportunity to keep me honed and occupied now the nights have started drawing in ...
