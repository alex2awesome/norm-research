Touching base after frustrating day otrying to update software on my gas-powered MacBook. According to the so-called genius in the Apple Store it's now technically (or not at all technically) 'vintage'. Which explains why it will only run Betamax.

I had planned to type up what I remembered, but guess there's no point now!

Anyway, yes, I did look out for you, Stephen. In fact, I went as far as asking someone if they were you but he denied it. (A bit too vehemently: are you SURE it wasn't you?? My breath was a bit oniony).

I ended up getting there stupidly early (due to abortive John Lewis mission. Why do I always get tricked into thinking it'll be fun?) so was rewarded with a number 12 sticker and got in the front row.

I think I may have freaked Nadia out because I was sitting right opposite her wearing exactly the same owl-patterned dress only (wait for it) in BLACK AND WHITE. (Actually, she was probably more freaked out that someone (possibly) old enough to be her mum appeared to have gone to extraordinary lengths to be some sort of weirdo fashion stalker. Hey ho.

Big congrats to everyone who was recorded. I had high hopes for my Duffin oneliner (Ladies and Gentlemen, I bring you the Muffnut) but alas, no.
