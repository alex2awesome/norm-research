Hiya!
I'm not sure if I'm allowed to do this, but here goes. Starting Monday at 10am I am hosting regular 2 hour Zoom check-in sessions for writers. No workshop, just a simple check-in at 10am, turn the camera off, get writing- check-in at 11 and again at 12. I'll do it on Wednesday and Friday too.

I have found it helps if there's some accountability as I am a terrible procrastinator! I participated in something similar at the beginning of Lockdown and it really helped to motivate me, so I thought I would start hosting them myself!
You can interact with others or just get on with writing, totally up to you.
