My dubious maths tells me that I got a 40% success rate this series: 1 to script and 1 to broadcast but I'm still Number Crunching. More importantly it was my first success! Who hoo! This week the lovely Angela referenced all my one liners in her intro so they were not going to be repeated. The claim that the show is written by the public is patently not the case when she has a good half of the airtime. Not that I am bitter. Arggggg!
Looking forward to confirmation of payment, which I will frame, to bolster my fragile writing ego. Must get back to worrying if I am a one hit wonder.
