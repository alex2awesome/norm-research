These are Beeb standard rates. Material is rounded up to the nearest minute or half minute thereof - thus a one line quickie attracts the half minute rate and a 35 second piece attracts the full one minute rate etc etc. After that as they say stateside "Do the math."

As has been suggested these are for CV credits, a bit of kudos and generally getting onself noticed, rather than the accumulation of riches beyond the dreams of avarice.

Agree with Badge Ishy. If you were credited for the show then if you email them and point it out you will get your just rewards.
