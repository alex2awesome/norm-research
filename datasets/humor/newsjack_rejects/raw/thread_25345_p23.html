Big Jack

Friday 5th October 2012 4:05pm

467 posts

Quote: DeathbyMonkey @ October 5 2012, 3:28 PM BST

Unless I'm wrong Jack they used your 4G sketch?

View original

Er...I haven't listened to to the show yet - but, per previous post, it's not my sketch. We were looking for an angle on 4G and I just suggested selling the solution to the economic crisis might be selling 5G, 6G, 7G now as well, which then became a sketch idea about selling things that didn't exist, like déjà vu, the spirit world etc. "sensory perception and now with new extra-sensory perception" was mine if it was in, but I don't remember anyone in the meeting laughing at it!

Like Jon said on the webcast, being in the meeting doesn't guarantee a credit - my disappointment is that I've done a Ronny Rosenthal and I'm still only 4th or 5th equal in the #KIPOD NJ credits league. The meeting was immense fun, being in a room of creative people (James and Tom in particular were straight off the cuff hilarious - there's a morsel of hope for non-commers) and I'd go again at the drop of a hat.

I included two of my rejected gags in my set at The Fringe on Tuesday and The Wardrobe Theatre last night, to good reception - I would say 20% of my live performance is now a direct result of writing submissions for NJ that get rejected!
