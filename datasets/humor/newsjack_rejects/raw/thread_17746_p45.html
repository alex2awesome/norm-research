I'm a BCG user, part-time anyway (got a new baby and work's blocked access to the site - what can you do?)

My record with BBC open submissions:-

- Sketch Factor (Play and Record) - sent stuff in and...nowt.

- RFTP (Every series they opened submissions for) - sent stuff in....nothing.

- Tilt - sent stuff in...nada.

- 7 x 7 (pre-newsjack show) - sent stuff in...zilch.

- Laura Solon - sent stuff in...zero.

- Newsjack series one - sent stuff in...blank.

- Newsjack series two - sent stuff in...etc.

- Newsjack series three - sent stuff in.........got one-liners in eps 3,4,5 & 6!!!!!

Obviously when I got the email I replied saying I didn't find the wind-up amusing!

Anyway, I keep sending stuff and always will - even if I'd not got anything in again this series - cos it's a great way to exercise your comedy muscles. If you're serious about writing comedy it's like going to the gym - plus there's a chance you might get paid!

Hope that's in some way helpful to people who're feeling rejected/bitter etc. Just keep at it - you might not win, but you won't lose.
