Hello all. Haven't spent much time on the BCG forums so didn't know this beauty existed. I've been throwing my #newsjackrejects into the Twitterspere! Here are my efforts for last week and this week.

Episode One - 13/02/20

BREAKING NEWS:
1. Schools are giving teachers body cameras in a pilot scheme to tackle pupils' bad behaviour. "This is an excellent idea, you wouldn't believe some of the atrocious language and threats of violence we are subjected to," said one student.

2. A computer scientist who helped the Queen send her first email - in 1976 - has died aged 86. Subsequently, members of the computer fraternity have been greatly impressed by Her Majesty's knowledge following the release of a statement from Buckingham Palace that said: "One was in bits."

GOOD WEEK/BAD WEEK:
1. It has been a bad week for insects as "Climate chaos" has caused widespread losses of bumblebees across continents, according to scientists.

But it has been a good week for parasites with the South Korean film picking up four awards at this year's Oscars and Donald Trump being acquitted in his impeachment trial.

Episode Two - 20/02/20

BREAKING NEWS:
1. Climate Activists, Extinction Rebellion dug up a lawn outside a Cambridge University college over its role in a major development in the Suffolk countryside. Cambridgeshire Police were hopeful of catching members of the group in the act, but it appears they were tipped off as the lawn was already damaged when police arrived. They are now on the lookout for a mole.

GOOD WEEK/BAD WEEK:
1. It has been a good week for Peers, as following a review of their allowances, their daily pay for turning up to the House of Lords will be increased by more than 3% to £323.

But it has been a bad week for Piers Morgan, because, well, basically because he's Piers Morgan.

2. It has been a good week for the fight against obesity as Unilever has agreed that by the end of the year, it will no longer market its ice-creams to children

But it has been a bad week for children, as they now have 99 problems.

I tend to overwrite a bit, as you may have noticed!

Ep 2
BREAKING NEWS:
1. A new Twitter filter has been launched to prevent users from seeing unsolicited nudes. Yet Donald Trump shows up on every second post, and that's an unsolicited dick pic if I ever saw one!

2. It was reported this week that half of Britons take their phone to the toilet with them. Meaning that the other half are lying.

It's been a bad week for Indiana Jones fans who wished the franchise ended right before the professor climbed out that fridge!

It's been a bad week for the underperforming Birds of Prey, proving a bird in the hand isn't always worth two in the bush.

3. It's been a good week for Planet Earth as construction of a new Tesla Gigafactory in Germany began preparations, aiming to produce their most energy efficient and environmentally friendly cars.
It's been a bad week for Planet Earth as they attempted to cut down thousands of trees to do so.
