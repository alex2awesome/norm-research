Here's what I remember:

Government spokesman on the varied uses, or lack of, for graphene.

Parent takes Michael Gove's advice on language learning and teaches her five year old to speak Alan Bennett.

Hague tries to make Tories more attractive to women by starting own louche radio station. Thatcher also does Loose Tories.

Jim Fixes it for the 300 NHS workers who wrote a letter to the Telegraph.

An advert for tourism to Eastern Europe combining the sights with hospital treatment.

Ed Miliband apologises again for forgetting a colleague's name but forgets even more, and ends up saying he's David.

Writer pitches Amanda Knox film idea to producer who wants it to be more upbeat, cutting out the prison etc.

French minister, following ban on ketchup, has further ideas to stop the Anglicisation of French culture.

Teresa May's diary - her everyday frustrations, not being able to get her favourite foods in Sainsburys etc, blamed on Human Rights Act.

Independent bookshop copes with reduced sales by introducing Amazon-type sales features, including the owner's husband giving his own idiosyncratic reviews.

The new powerful telescope that can see to the beginning of time has unexpectedly sighted God laughing and hiding fossils.

Someone in the queue for the new Iphone is interviewed.

Govt. spokesman tells of advantages of new 80 mph limit, such as more work for garages with extra crashes.

Following Bath Railway station's knife scanner, plans for improved railway security lead to Paddington Bear being blown up for carrying a dangerous-looking pot of marmalade.

A student complains about her boring University teacher and introduces her primary schoolteacher as an example of how to teach.

Afterword involving the Olympic track being finished early leading to it developing potholes over the winter, causing the 100 metre final to be diverted via Croydon.
