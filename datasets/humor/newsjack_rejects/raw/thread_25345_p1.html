As it's the start of Week 0 of Newsjack I thought this thread deserved a bump.

That and to say to if you're new to all this please do read their submission rules and writing tips. From doing a bit of reading / editing on Kirrin Island it is amazing how many people don't. (*)

So give yourself an immediate advantage by making sure the first reaction on reading your sketch isn't (1) Grr! Why have they made my life twice as difficult as it needs to be or (2) Grr! They couldn't even be bothered to read the few hundred words I wrote as a brief yet expect me to extend the same curtesy to their work.

As I said doing Kirrin Island really has made me feel much more sympathy for the NJ producers / script editors.

(*) You can follow Simon Mayhew-Archer's twitter feed to see just how much a BBC producer loves people not reading the guidelines
