Quote: Joe86 @ January 18 2010, 12:16 AM GMT

Hi guys,

This is my first post, and I have just finished my first 3 sketches. What I wanted to ask is. Firstly, when do people normally get emails if they got something in, and what does it say? Also, how much do you get paid, I'd do it for free!

Cheers.

View original

Hi Joe, and welcome.

If you haven't got anything in, you probably won't hear anything at all.

If you have got something in it can vary, but you won't hear anything before Wednesday afternoon at the earliest. If you get something then it will say "it's being recorded tonight, well done, but it might not make the final cut". Then some time on Thursday evening you should hear if it's in or out (and they publish the list of writers on the Newsjack website).

You do get paid - it's per minute, rounded up, over the whole series. It was 30-something quid per minute for the last series, so don't give up any day job just yet.
