All good points @deathbymonkey. I always listen with an open mind and whilst I enjoyed much of last night's show it did seem a bit dislocated and didn't add up to anything more than the sum of its parts - wasn't very convinced by that 'Lent' sketch? (not having a pop at the content, more the lack of plot layers and absence of setting which made it very random, but maybe that's being mean and I should give it a second chance). Emma Sidi, who is always great in my opinion (loads of high energy and voice) seemed to propel a lot of the sketches. I've also started to notice the odd well-travelled twitter gag making it onto the oneliners list, which is a bit of a shame but arguably unavoidable: it's most likely coincidence and I might well be unknowingly guilty of it too; of course, it's a big wide world and things happen, but it has had the effect of making me far less stressy about not making it....'every feckin' week gooddammit' But hey, I really love what the show sets out to do - not an easy thing - some genius bits come out of it and the success stories are always encouraging to hear, even if most of us, sadly, are on a road to nowhere.
