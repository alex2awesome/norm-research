Ugh you know when you have a brilliant sketch idea hours after the deadline.

Story: Neil deGrasse Tyson made a video illustrating all the evidence that proves the Earth is round

Sketch premise: Astronaut in space is about to do a moon walk, suddenly notices the Earth is flat. Tells NASA, who quietly inform her they know, it's a conspiracy and she needs to keep it under her helmet. Conspiracy is kept up by the map people. Says 'oh well at least we don't need to worry about mass flooding from climate change as all the water will roll off the side'. NASA goes all quiet, turns out that's a conspiracy as well being funded by big environment. Asks what isn't a conspiracy, moon landings? Never happened. Aliens? Are real. Elvis? Working in a burger shop in Swansea. Some funny twist ending,

I could kick myself I didn't think of this yesterday! It would have taken me ten minutes to write!!
