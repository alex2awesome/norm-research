Quote: Judgement Dave @ 28th February 2014, 11:33 AM GMT

Note that this may also explain why, in the BCG forums, one person (Matt) said that it looked like they could fit a lot more on a page, and so mentioned considering reformatting their Show What You Wrote sketches to the NewsJack template. It's probably because their view of the template was FUBAR'd.

View original

I only use Windows and MS Word. Checking the templates in both documents reveals that the font size in both my template (made from the old NJ blog post measurements) and the new NJ template is Arial 12pt.

The only reason why I can see that I can now enter more in the new template is because when I was writing in earlier shows, I would hit carriage return twice between each line of dialogue to give a full blank line. With the new template (and the instructions within it) seems that they are just happy with line padding as the space between FX/dialogue, so now I can write more.

I'll write in the template they provided because those are the rules, but I'm not too fussed if the formatting looks weird on their machines as it's their template and their issue.

And in the end, I sent my TSWYW sketches off as .doc files using the template that I use for 4am Cab, and the last series of NJ.

The "new" format is standard radio format. The numbers are cue numbers. They do this so, if one line of dialogue doesn't sound right for example, they can say "re-record page 1, cue number 7," and the actor will know which line to redo.

I'm guessing in previous series', they retyped all chosen sketches into this format ahead of recording the show, but now they are figuring out ways to lessen the workload.
