Here we go. My 10th week of attempting NJ. Not feeling incredibly positive after last week's blow but there is such a lottery element to it. One liners anyway.

Best of luck everyone. Really hoping to see some first-timers celebrating this week.

The real part is dislike about writing for NJ is after a while I become so warn down by the news. I generally don't pay too much attention beyond the big stories, but during the series run I cast a pretty wide net and there's so much horrible stuff out there that takes its toll.

It does however make it easier to spot the stories with some sketch potential. I've got a few I've saved to follow over the weekend as they seem promising.
