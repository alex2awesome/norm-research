What's Newsjack's target audience?

View original

At the writers briefing in January, they told us it was younger than average for 4 Extra, but still quite a mixed age group.

I've been to a few of the recordings and, indeed, it has been a mixed age group in attendance - uni students all the way up to retired folk.

Quote: Mrkgrngr @ 13th September 2017, 6:38 PM

Who knows? Who cares?

View original

Presumably, the BBC know. I'd be quite interested to see the viewing figures, especially in comparison to other Radio 4 and 4 Extra shows. Viewing figures play a major part in determining how long this show will run for. It's (as far as I'm aware) the only regular open-door opportunity to have short-form comedy on BBC national radio and it'd be a shame to lose it.
