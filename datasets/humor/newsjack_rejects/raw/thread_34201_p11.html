I've started listening to this week's episode and I did find it an odd one. I think the trouble for open submitters to Newsjack is that they try to write to a style (the Newsjack style), but an episode's style changes depending on who the script editors are, therefore what they'll go for changes from one week to the next.

The sketch I posted in the critique (Olympic Diplomats) clearly retrospectively had no chance of making this week's episode but I can't help feeling had the story come up another week (with a different script editor) it might have been considered.

What's tough is we only hear if we're in the show or made the recording, not if they liked our sketches but they didn't make it to that stage, so we're left not knowing if our submissions were actually any good or not.
