I must admit to struggling this week (and every week) the only vox pop I came up with is:

"Saw the pictures of Haiti this week, it looked like something out of WWII. Perhaps that's why the Americans were so late going in"

Not sure if it's even funny but damned sure it's going nowhere near the show.

I'm a bit confused though about why everyone is so concerned about the poor news week. Last weeks show -just off the top of my head - contained a 3 minute riff on George Michael lyrics from the 80's (possibly 90's I can't consider myself an expert), a self-confessed throwback to 1980's BBC, a 'read my opinion' slot which could have been written anytime since the 9/11 bombings, a Joy Division Newsbullet one-liner and a bunch of chocolate puns amongst many other things where topicality (as opposed to tenuous connection to snow or chocolate) appeared to be a pretty low priority.

I'm not complaining, just observing that the news week doesn't seem to be the most important thing for the show.
