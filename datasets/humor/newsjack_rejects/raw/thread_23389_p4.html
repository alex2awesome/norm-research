Tip, Park Bench. The deadline for oneliners is Tuesday. Unless you have a very good reason to send them on Saturday - like no email access afterwards - then don't. Firstly, you'll probably wake up tomorrow morning thinking of a better wording to one you've already sent; secondly, the story for your best gag might have moved on so it isn't relevant anymore; and thirdly you will have more stories to choose from, and they will be more topical - hence more likely to get in the show.

Good luck everyone.
