J26

View original

I'd leave it until Sunday or Monday to send anything off, partly because on Friday the stories they'll cover probably won't even have happened yet. Unless it's something you know for sure that's going to happen between the Saturday and Wednesday before broadcast (e.g. Oscars, major sporting event) I'd hold back.

You want to make sure it's something that would still be topical by next Thursday when they broadcast and not something that would have been covered in last week's topical shows like News Quiz etc.
