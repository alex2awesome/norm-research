Jesus-titty-fucking-Christ, I got the good email.

Wasn't going to bother this week but figured I'm miss the feeling of righteous indignation, so threw together a few one-liners at the last minute. Easily my weakest submissions from the 5 weeks. Was in such a rush I even included an embarrassing typo that I fear looked deliberate. Somehow, instead of 'criticised' I wrote 'cliticised', and they probably thought it was a pun because it was in a joke about the tulips/vaginas sculpture. Not sure how I did it, the letters 'r' and 'l' aren't even close to each other on the keyboard. They probably assumed I was Japanese and my joke was selected to hit diversity targets.
