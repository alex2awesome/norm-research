I'd agree with what's been said above - if you've got limited time, you're best bending your mind round the freshest news you can.

Except to say that if something you'd been kicking round suddenly turns into the joke it always should have been after the deadline has passed, you only lose one sketch slot in three or a one liner slot in twelve taking a punt on it.

You never know, it might bubble back up into the news again. I had one like this a while ago and it still got on. Actually, it was about same sex marriage, so turns out it would still be fresh-ish two series later. You can't guarantee it will go dead.
