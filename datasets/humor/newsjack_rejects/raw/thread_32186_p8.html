Well done everyone who was recorded for a corking episode 1. And if the owner of the black market GCSE sketch (which didn't make the broadcast) hangs around here, I loved it and am gutted it didn't make the final cut.

One of the very useful things that the script editors said at the writers' meeting in August was that the sketches will go through six or seven sifts before the final selection. So even if it feels like you're not getting stuff on, you may well be getting quite far along in the process, and the producers will remember you.

I can't comment about the edit process, but if it's the best of your 6 then submit it. (If it's the funniest thing the editor heard that week, they would be a fool to exclude it. And/or *they'll* find another word.)

Have you thought about giving the joke a posh voice, so you can use clinical accurate terminology? It might open up the vocab a little. e.g. 'faeces-laden excrement tip'. Posh people getting irate, wanting to swear, but knowing it's becoming, can be funny.

My minimal 2p.
