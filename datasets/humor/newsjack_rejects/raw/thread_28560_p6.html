View original

Oh for sure, jackapps are considered one-liners and should be sent on the Tuesday before the 4pm deadline. And in doing it that way;
1. You don't eat into your three sketch limit (as you recognised)
2. You can cover later, more up to date stories in the jackapps to make them ultra-topical.

The one-liner submission would normally cover any of
Intros, jackapps, corrections and And Finallies... (even though none of them might be one line!)

Alison
