That's a shame. This series tickets have gone crazy for Newsjack. I remember the first recording I went to in Series 2 I got tickets on the night and there were only about 5 rows of people there. Granted that was a 'snow' week so transport had gone crazy but now they're issuing standby tickets for Newsjack recordings, even at BBC Radio Theatre (and I rarely seen them issue standby tickets there).

I got there at 6:15 (door opening time) and it was queued round the block. I was about number 140 in the queue already. And last week at Radio Theatre there were a lot of standby tickets too. The rule is come early, Newsjack is very popular these days!

Anyone know if this is reflected in higher listening figures / downloads?

Should also say it was worth it as I and two others I know had one-liners in last night show. That's the first thing of mine I've ever had recorded at RADA. Here's hoping to make it over the finishing line later today.

I went and so did my wife...

We got in ok having queued for about 30 min outside, the highlight of which was watching Richard Wilson on the other side of the road texting. The bar was small, stuffy and crowded and resembled a Ryanair boarding gate as the time approached to enter the inner sanctum.

I enjoyed the show and all the performers were on good form. I only remember a few of the sketches - Robin Hood, The Pope & God, Milliband wanting to be a fireman, a Justin interview with Godfrey Bloom doing voices, a drugs deal/toilet paper deal, a dirty mag/cigarette prison sketch and one about toad of toad hall. Not all of these worked.

Afraid I cannot remember any of the one liners - but being bitter about rejection can do that I suppose.

All in all a thoroughly enjoyable evening and good to see how it all works.
