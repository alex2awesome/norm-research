Eustace H Plimsoll

Friday 19th February 2016 3:07pm

London

22 posts

I managed to get tickets to the previous two weeks and the upcoming show. It's a big mix actually - there's a moderately-sized chunk of people who are clearly retired that turn up because tickets are free - they arrive hours before and sit at the front; then it's mostly young professional types with a few older (40-50) people. Male/female is 50/50 I'd say, 55/45 at worst. There's not much time to go to the pub between getting your ticket validated and them closing the admissions, though there is a bar within the BBC cafe from which to sup a drink or two.

Like a few on here I only found about this show at the beginning of the last season and managed to get a something in successive shows... nothing this season though, evidently instant success is not representative of what's actually like as a purveyor of lols.

I'd recommend going though, it's very good research!
