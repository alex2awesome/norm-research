Don't know if you're following the Newsjack twitter feed but recommended it as several of the questions on here are answered by them directly.

View original

Here are the relevant questions and answers from their Twitter account. Hope this helps!

Q: Should One Liners and Corrections be sent as one document or two?

A: "No, a single document is absolutely fine. (But if you've split them out, don't worry - that's not so much of a time-drain.)"

Q: Can I send in sketches in the .docx format?

A: "We really prefer not. Some of us can open them, others not. Sticking to the guidelines just means less faff all round...Please use .doc or .rtf only." (two tweet replies merged)

Q: I didn't get the auto-reply. Should I be worried?

A: "Don't worry. We've got your email."

Q: Do you notify people in advance of the broadcast if you're going to use their material?

A: "Yep - all writers who make the edit (plus those who get stuff recorded but cut) receive an email on Thursday early evening."

Q: Can Newsjack link to any old episodes of the show for new writers to hear?

A: "No: because of copyright. Rights agreements only cover 2 transmissions and a weeks podcasting. Not online in perpetuity."
(note: but there are 2 or 3 full episodes on YouTube, uploaded illegally they're not allowed to tell you promote, so you can listen to old episodes there)
