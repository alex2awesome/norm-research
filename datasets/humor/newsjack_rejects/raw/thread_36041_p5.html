3. It's been a bad week for Netflix after Taylor Swift accused it of "degrading women" after one of its shows made a joke about how quickly she allegedly goes through men. It's been a good week for women after Netflix apologised, clarifying that it only meant to degrade Taylor Swift.

We covered a couple of the same topics, Skram! Here are mine.

BREAKING NEWS:
Horse racing trainer Gordon Elliott has been suspended after being pictured sitting on the corpse of a stallion, proving that, if you need a metaphor for the cruelty of racing, you can't beat a dead horse.

Meteorites may have landed in a field in Cheltenham after fragments of a distant comet entered the Earth's atmosphere. An expanse of nothingness with no atmosphere or signs of intelligent life, Cheltenham is a town in Gloucestershire.

Boris Johnson has said that now is "the right time" for the UK to bid to host the 2030 World Cup. A source later clarified that by, 'right time', he means that if the teams set off now, they should just get through the Kent tailbacks in time for kick-off.

GOOD WEEK/BAD WEEK:
A good week for the Orbital Assembly Corporation who announced plans for the first ever space hotel to open by 2027. A better week for Karen from Barnsley who announced plans to complain about pubes floating above the bed later that year.

A good week for Volvo who announced they are going fully electric by 2030. A bad week for older Volvo fans who preferred their earlier acoustic stuff.

A bad week for dog owners who have been buying lockable, steel-core collars and leads after a spate of dognapping. A good week for dogs who say they're flattered but honestly would have been fine with just a card and a cake.
