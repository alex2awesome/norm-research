It's really interesting to listen to a sketch you wrote and compare it to what was submitted to the script editor. Certainly, when writing for RFTP in s1 and 2, only one of my sketches was broadcast pretty much word-for-word and it remains the one I'm least proud of. No-one laughed at any of the jokes I'd written, only the song that was spliced in mid-way through that had bugger all to do with me, and I'm surprised it ever survived the edit! Most of the time, I noticed it was the first 20 seconds or so of my sketches that got re-written, perhaps to hit the first gag earlier than I had.

Il Dustino first of all congratulations on the Teletubbies sketch - definitely one of the stand-out ones last week.

At this stage of a NJ run it's a common question and for those who don't already know there are two shows that run throughout the year that take topical material. They are:
London's Newsrevue at Canal Cafe (More on NR separate thread in Writing Discussion)
Brighton's Treason Show (More on Treason separate thread in Writing Discussion)

What we (Kirrin Island) used to do was indeed make our own, gather together scripts, re-write them and record them into a Kirrin Island podcast. However over time that has evolved and we're doing something different with it (See thread about it here: https://www.comedy.co.uk/forums/thread/31665/ ) Still a chance to submit just not the old NJ sketches!

My tip is to try and find some way to see your stuff being performed whether that's going to NR/Treason (if you get stuff on), try and record it yourself or even get a few friends to read it out. It helps enormously to find what bits work and what bits feel very slow with no jokes.

And there's always the option to post stuff up on critique on here. (I'm less keen on this as sometimes the advice on there might not always be that constructive - others may disagree with me on that).

Anyway don't let the end of NJ be the end of your writing!
