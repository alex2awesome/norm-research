Just discovered this, so the first time I've posted. In the interests of political balance, I'm a Trump supporting brexiteer and my sister is Katie Hopkins
Enjoyed last weeks show, but agree with comments about the newsjackpedia section being weak. Thought it was strong on breaking news with one exception - the one about Sandi Toksvig - could see the punchline / sound ending coming a mile off
Been trying to get stuff on for the last three series. Had two 'made the cuts' and one that made the show - 'Debt ridden Kanye West is to be bailed out by the British government after changing his name from Kanye to Nat' ( Always good to bask in past glories )

Really struggle with the sketch writing, but enjoy the challenge of the one-liners. Anyone have any particular favourites they remember ? I can recall a good one which was roughly about ' vinyl melting on the hottest summers day since records began '

Managed three one liners this week ( Salisbury / Russians - probably too dated by now, though, - Liam/ Lily Allen, and model giving birth after Rihanna fashion show ) and just one 'pedia' - pretty weak one about Justin Welby.

Good luck to all this Thursday. Great to discover this . I'm showing my age now, but I still pine for another series of 'Not The Nine O'clock News '..........
