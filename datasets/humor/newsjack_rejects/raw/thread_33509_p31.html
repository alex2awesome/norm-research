Quote: Chris Hallam @ 24th September 2017, 11:29 AM

Surely all the sketches are submitted by the public?

View original

No, unless something's changed recently. They have a writing room with a few in-house writers, whose names you'll hear credited on several other shows, and added to that, some lucky people who are invited in to write with them. In total, maybe 8 people, each required to write 2 sketches. These sketches are much more likely to be broadcast, because the producer is on call to shape it to their needs, and indeed when I was there, several of the sketches broadcast that week originated from the room....possibly about half the total broadcast, on that occasion.
