Quote: Griff @ February 9 2011, 11:00 AM GMT

The BBC Trust don't seem to be very impressed with Newsjack.

http://www.chortle.co.uk/news/2011/02/08/12721/more_comedy_for_radio_7

"...Radio 7 currently commissions a few new comedy shows of its own, but today's review concluded that 'this output is making little impact' on audiences..."

View original

I've clearly no idea how the BBC trust or BBC executives think but although Newsjack's listeners would probably struggle to fill a double-decker bus it must get some kudos as a stepping stone.

Just looking at the cast and crew over the last few years most of them have done rather well for themselves:

Miles Jupp - Other topical panel shows, sit-coms with posh middle class person in it & sit-coms with a vicar in it. And he gets to work with Tom Hollander.
Andi Osho - Tons of TV stuff at the minute including own spot on Stand Up for the Week
Lewis MacLeod - Probably the busiest impressionist who doesn't have his own show. All over Radio 4 and BBC TV sketch shows
Tilusha Ghelani - Produces Just a Minute. Enough said.
Sam Michell - Produces Elvenquest
Dan Tetsell - Seems to be in everything on Radio 4 (and BBC2)

I say that in the hope that the BBC will find some reason to keep Newsjack going (or at least start another Open Submissions show) even if it isn't listening figures.

Don't know of any writers who've made it though...
