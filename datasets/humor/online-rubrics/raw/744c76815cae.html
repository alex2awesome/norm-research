Stand Up Comedy Routine and Presentation 
Stand up comedy/jokes presentation
Students are to create a 3-5 minute stand up comedy skit and present it to the class. Props are optional, but limited to two. Two of the "comedic joke lines" must be original, using criteria learned about creating jokes. Two of the lines may be borrowed but need to make sense with the overall presentation. They may not not be vulgar, crude, racist or inappropriate in any way. In the written presentation, the students must acknowledge the person(s) whose work they borrowed.

Quantity and Quality of one-liners

Excellent

The joke lines are of advanced quality and cause audience to laugh at key times. The jokes are appropriate to overall skit sequence and theme.
Good

The joke lines are of adequate quality and cause audience to laugh at times. The jokes are appropriate to overall skit sequence and theme.
Fair

The joke lines are of fair quality and the audience listened. The jokes are appropriate to overall skit sequence and theme.
Insufficient/ Poor

The joke lines are inadequate and do not make sense. The jokes are inappropriate to overall skit sequence and theme.
Presentation

Excellent

The presentation lasts for at least 5 minutes and engages the audience the whole time.
The voice used is clear and appropriate.
Dress of speaker and props used are creative and appropriate.
Good

The presentation lasts for at least 3 minutes and engages the audience the whole time.
The voice used is clear and appropriate.
Fair

The presentation is less than 3 minutes and is not as engaging as it could be. The voice used is clear and appropriate.
Dress of speaker and props used are creative and appropriate.
Insufficient/ Poor

The presentation is less than 3 minutes and did not engage the audience at all. The voice used is muffled. There is no special effort used.
Originality and Unique Style

Excellent

The material is original and uses several techniques and comedic strategies, learned during the unit.

The format is clear and follows the model.

The chosen borrowed jokes are appropriate and fit the theme.
Good

The material is original and uses two comedic strategies or techniques, learned during the unit.

The format is clear and follows the model.

The chosen borrowed jokes are appropriate and fit the theme.
Fair

The material is original and uses one technique or comedic strategy, learned during the unit.

The format is clear but does not follow the model.


The chosen borrowed jokes are appropriate, but do not fit with the theme.
Insufficient/ Poor

The material is not original and doesn't use any technique or strategy, learned during the unit.

The format is unclear and doesn't follow the model.

The chosen borrowed jokes are not appropriate and do not fit with the theme
