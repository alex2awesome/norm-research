ABOUT CONTINENTAL FINANCE

Continental Finance Company ("Continental Finance") is one of America's leading marketers and servicers of credit cards issued by partner banks for consumers with less-than-perfect credit. Since the company's founding in 2005, Continental Finance has prided itself on fostering financial inclusion with innovative and flexible technology to deliver best-in-class customer support for those consumers who are excluded by traditional credit card programs.

What We Do

With over 2.6 million credit cards managed since our founding, Continental Finance prides itself on excellent customer service and access to products with innovative features. We specialize in servicing credit cards products for consumers who are largely overlooked by traditional credit card servicers. With a state-of-the-art consumer marketing and servicing platform, we are able to offer a variety of services to consumers when other financial institutions will not accommodate them. Whether you have fair, poor, or limited credit, a Verve credit card issued by The Bank of Missouri can help you access products that are right for you.

Customer Support

As the servicer of the Verve credit card issued by The Bank of Missouri, Continental Finance has a dedicated customer service support team that understands the importance of helping all customers and our goal is to provide each customer with the highest level of customer support, including providing clearly and concisely outlined fees and charges, and payment requirements.

Our Expertise

As a credit card servicer, Continental Finance is committed to providing consumers with superior customer service. We report customer payment performance history to all three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion) every month!

We also support a "GO GREEN" initiative where customers can receive their monthly statement and other notifications electronically via the internet and email.
