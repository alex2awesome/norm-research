LEARN THE ART OF IMPROVISATION FROM SOME OF THE BEST TEACHERS IN THE COUNTRY.

Whether you’re a seasoned performer or you’ve just realized you can’t resist the call of the spotlight, our improv classes cater to all levels of experience. From rookies to improv veterans, we’re here to turn your comedic dreams into reality faster than you can say “Yes, and…”!

Led and curated by some of the most influential instructors in the field of improvisation of the past 30+ years, our structured curriculum is designed with both beginners and experienced people in mind and aims to provide you to with life-changing skills that are immediately applicable on and off stage.

Classes meet once a week for three hours. There are 7 class meetings per level. The average class size is between 10-14 people.

WHAT IS "THE HAROLD"?

In the vast landscape of improv, the “Harold” format reigns as a shining star, earning its stripes as one of the most innovative improv comedy performance pieces known around the world. This signature ‘long-form’ structure  was created and perfected by the legendary Del Close and Charna Halpern right here in Chicago, whose work helped to make America’s “second city” the cool kid on the improv block.

CAN I SKIP A LEVEL IF I HAVE PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE?

If your improv experience consists of having taken more than one level of improv classes from an improv theater (iO, CIC, UCB, etc.), exceptions could be made where you will be allowed to register for any level you think you will be comfortable in, given your prior  experience.

Having said that…
We have a unique approach to improvisation and a progressive training center where each level is built upon the previous one.
In addition, although there could be exceptions made for one to start their curriculum at higher levels, our philosophy is that nothing builds ensemble like going through the trenches together, therefore we highly encourage all new students to start at level 1.
