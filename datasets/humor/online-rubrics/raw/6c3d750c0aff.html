Laugh Factory at Horseshoe Las Vegas

Laugh Factory is a premier stand‑up comedy venue bringing high‑energy laughs to the center of the Las Vegas Strip. Set inside Horseshoe Las Vegas, this renowned comedy club showcases a rotating lineup of nationally touring headliners, rising stars, and surprise guest comedians in an intimate, no‑bad‑seat setting. With nightly shows, a full bar, and a legacy of launching legendary performers, Laugh Factory is a must‑see destination for live comedy and nightlife in Las Vegas.
