British vs American English

500 British vs American English Words with Pictures

9 months ago
12,732 Views

British and American English are both widely used around the world, but they often use different words for the same things. For example, the British say “lift”, while Americans say “elevator”. These small differences can sometimes confuse learners and travelers. That’s why it’s important to understand both versions. In this article, you will learn a clear and simple list of 500 British vs American English words with pictures. Each word is shown with an image and both versions of the word to help you learn easily. This guide is perfect for students, teachers, travelers, and anyone who wants to speak and understand English confidently, no matter which side of the world they’re on.
