At Just For Laughs, we take comedy seriously.

Sous-titre

The Montreal Just For Laughs Festival features ComedyPRO — now a global comedy marketplace, bringing together the industry’s top buyers, sellers, and creators for an unmatched week of dealmaking, discovery, networking, and celebration. 

By day, ComedyPRO is where business gets done — with curated pitching, market-driven showcases, and opportunities to buy, sell, package, and develop comedy across film, television, digital, and live. It’s also home to the Just For Laughs Awards Show, celebrating the biggest names shaping comedy today. 

By night, the festival comes alive with cocktail parties, can’t-miss shows, and after-hours events that bring the global comedy community together. 

From the legendary New Faces of Comedy showcases to the industry’s most influential players, ComedyPRO is where careers launch, partnerships form, and the future of comedy takes shape.
