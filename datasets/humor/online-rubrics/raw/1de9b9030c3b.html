13 Iconic Things To Do When Visiting Hollywood

Hollywood is where movie magic happens and dreams come alive, and also the most iconic spot in Los Angeles that you'll never get tired to explore.

For more than a century, Hollywood has been and continues to be the international capital where the cinematic dreams of fame, success, and global recognition for actors, musicians, and comedians come true. But for locals and tourists alike, there are fortunately many more things to see and do in this iconic corner of the city. And that’s exactly what we’ll explore today: the best things to do in Hollywood!

Where is Hollywood exactly located? 

Hollywood, located in the middle of Los Angeles, is surrounded by the Hollywood Hills, Melrose Avenue, Western Avenue, and Beverly Hills. Here you can see the famous Hollywood Sign, the star-studded Walk of Fame on Hollywood Boulevard, and the Dolby Theatre where the Oscars go down. Plus, it’s surrounded by cool neighborhoods like West Hollywood and Silver Lake.

What is Hollywood like right now?

As it has historically been, Hollywood remains a vibrant and bustling area of the city, teeming with locals and tourists seeking the next attraction and activity. Most of the things to do are related to the film industry, of course. However, if you’re looking for a glamorous or ‘movie-like’ experience, this part of the city might disappoint you. Celebrities and the rich and famous aren’t as common in this area as you might expect.

If you’re seeking what TV portrays as Hollywood (mansions, expensive cars, and designer clothing), then the place you need to visit is Beverly Hills or Rodeo Drive. However, high-end celebrity hangouts and hotel bars feel right at home here, along with tucked-away cocktail bars and bespoke coffee shops—and it seems like there are more and more popping up every day.

Is Hollywood, California worth visiting?

Hollywood, California, is worth visiting for its iconic landmarks like the Hollywood Walk of Fame and the Hollywood Sign, vibrant entertainment scene, diverse culture, lively nightlife, and convenient location for exploring other parts of Los Angeles. Whether you’re a movie buff, a foodie, or simply looking for a fun and dynamic atmosphere, Hollywood offers something for everyone to enjoy.

10 Fun things to do in Hollywood

Catch a movie at the Chinese Theater

Entering the renowned 932-seat theater might be a highlight on any tourist’s list, yet the bustling crowd of vendors and visitors capturing photos with Hollywood handprints adds to its allure. Enhancing the experience further is the fact that the Chinese Theater has one of the world’s largest IMAX auditoriums and its breathtaking architecture.

It encapsulates the quintessential Los Angeles cinema adventure, often serving as a hidden gem amid the bustling surroundings.

📍Location: 6925 Hollywood Blvd, Hollywood, CA 90028

Taste authentic Vietnamese coffee

If you’re wondering what to do in Hollywood, look no further than Nam Coffee, founded by third-generation coffee maker Vince Nguyen with the assistance of third-generation coffee farmers from Vietnam, offering a unique experience.

Originally based in Orange County, home to a large Vietnamese community, Nam Coffee opened its first storefront in Anaheim before expanding to East Hollywood, with their beans already featured in local favorites like Sesame LA and Sarah’s Market.

📍Location: 4876 Fountain, Los Angeles, California 90029

Get amazing views of the Hollywood sign

Originally placed in 1923 as “Hollywoodland,” the iconic sign was meant to stand for only 18 months, yet it remains a century later! For a decent view, head to Ovation Hollywood (formerly Hollywood & Highland) or Beachwood Drive for a direct glimpse. For the best perspective, trek up to Lake Hollywood Park or Sunset Ranch for a 360-degree cityscape view from above the sign on Mt. Lee Drive.

📍Location: Los Angeles, CA 90068

Visit the Hollywood Forever Cemetery

Uncover this historic cinematic gem in Los Angeles, where Hollywood legends rest amid ornate mausoleums. Explore notable grave sites like those of Judy Garland and Douglas Fairbanks on a self-guided tour, for free.

Don’t miss the free outdoor movie screenings, a cherished tradition celebrating film culture. With its rich history and complimentary events, this cemetery offers a unique experience for those seeking free things to do in Los Angeles.

📍Location: 6000 Santa Monica Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90038

Explore the fascinating Hollyhock House

Just outside the Hollywood border, this hilly park is the home of Frank Lloyd Wright’s Hollyhock House, making it one of the must-see things in Hollywood.  In the summer, the park hosts a variety of alfresco cinema nights, wine tastings, and cultural events that bring out a nice mix of singles, couples, and young families.

📍Location: 4800 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90027

Dine at the legendary Musso & Frank

For over a century, the iconic Musso & Frank has been a cornerstone of Hollywood, making it a must-visit spot for things to do in Hollywood. Exuding a timeless charm that transports you to an era of dining with legends like Charlie Chaplin, everything from the decor to the menu resonates with history.

Indulge in classics like juicy New York steak, filet mignon, or ribeye, and savor an unforgettable evening at this historic steakhouse, renowned as Los Angeles’ culinary treasure. 

📍Location: 6667 Hollywood Blvd, Hollywood, CA 90028

Find your favorite celebrity star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame

Even though it might seem romantic or charming, it’s a terrible idea to place your hands on the ground… Nowhere more so than on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. While it may seem like a way to connect with your favorite celebrity, this place is usually packed with people.

You get the picture. It’s better to enjoy this stroll by walking along it, with plenty of patience to navigate through the crowds and gaze at the peculiar characters and superheroes that roam here.

📍Location: 6901 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028

Horseback ride at the Hollywood sign

In addition to hiking to the Hollywood sign or simply admiring it from afar at sunset, an essential activity in this part of the city is horseback riding to the base of this iconic landmark. As you ascend, you’ll be treated to unforgettable vistas of the sprawling L.A. Basin and other iconic and scenic views of LA, creating

📍Location: 3400 Beachwood Dr, Los Angeles, CA 90068

Check out the iconic Yamashiro Hollywood

Yamashiro Hollywood stands as a renowned Los Angeles landmark and an iconic restaurant with amazing views. Originally conceived by two German-born brothers in the early 1900s as an elaborate Asian-inspired estate, it underwent several transformations before evolving into a beloved Japanese-inspired dining destination.

Don’t miss the ancient 14th-century pagoda adjacent to the swimming pool, offering picturesque views of LA, before indulging in a lavish sushi feast.

📍Location: 1999 N Sycamore Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90068

Have a blast at Universal Studios Hollywood

Immerse yourself in Hollywood magic and thrilling attractions at this must-visit L.A. theme park, offering excitement for all. Explore iconic movie sets and enjoy heart-pounding rides—you won’t be disappointed! Don’t forget to hop on the Studio Tour for a peek behind the scenes of your favorite films.

📍Location: 100 Universal City Plaza, Universal City, CA 91608

Sip some wine at Tabula Rasa

A post shared by Tabula Rasa Ba (@tabularasabar)

The cozy venue exudes a minimalist, industrial vibe, perfectly complemented by a relaxed clientele. The wine selection, though concise, is impressive, and they also offer excellent beer options. The highlight? The petite snacks menu. The Cubano, in particular, is surprisingly exceptional.

📍Location: 5125 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90027

LOL at UCB

View this post on Instagram

A post shared by UCB Theatre LA (@ucbtla/)

What could be better than a night filled with laughter alongside friends? A must-visit spot in Hollywood is the UCB, or Upright Citizens Brigade. Founded in 1996 by Amy Poehler, Matt Besser, Ian Roberts, and Matt Walsh, this comedy institution and training center offers a wide variety of performances, including improv, sketch comedy, stand-up, and experimental acts. It’s a hub for new talent and a platform for seasoned comedians alike.

📍Location: 5919 Franklin Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90028

Enjoy a tasty treat at Courage Bagels

View this post on Instagram

A post shared by courage bagels (@courage_bagel)

Courage Bagels, which opened in 2020, is renowned for making the best bagels in Los Angeles. However, it can be a challenge for impatient folks, as the queues can be quite long. This isn’t just a spot to savor the most delicious bagels in town but also an opportunity to practice mindfulness. The bagels are so dense and crispy that every bite releases a satisfying, sharp crack, making the wait worthwhile!

📍Location: 777 Virgil Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90029
