Description

Dropout.tv is a subscription-based streaming service focused on comedy content. It is operated by CollegeHumor and provides access to a range of original series, sketch shows, game shows, and animated content. The platform is known for hosting exclusive content from its creators and offers an ad-free viewing experience.

How to cancel Dropout.tv subscription

Visit dropout.tv and log into your account. Navigate to 'Account Settings' or 'Subscription' section. Look for the 'Cancel Subscription' option and follow the prompts to confirm the cancellation.
