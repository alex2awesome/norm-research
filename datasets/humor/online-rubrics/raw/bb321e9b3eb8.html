    Forms and Compliance

    Forms

    The California Apartment Association offers members more than 200 rental housing forms, expertly developed by CAA’s compliance and legal team to ensure full compliance with California law.

    To fit the needs of every rental housing provider, CAA delivers forms in three flexible formats: easy printable forms, or upgrade to our time-saving online fill-in forms, or upgrade to integrated forms – files ready to upload for use in common property management solutions like Yardi, AppFolio, and many others.

    Printable Forms

    Printable forms offer CAA members simple, no-frills access to the documents you need, anytime.

    Learn more
    Online Fill-in Forms

    Online fill-in forms upgrade lets you complete and generate accurate documents quickly, right from your browser.

    Learn more
    Integrated Forms

    CAA’s integrated forms upgrade is for members with 200 or more units and is designed for smooth import into major property management systems.

    Learn more

    Online Forms Tool

    Fill out forms digitally! 

    CAA’s Online Fill-Out Forms services allows you to easily populate bundles of CAA documents on the web by simply entering the data once. Online Forms members can fill out forms online, create packages and send lease agreements to the prospective residents by email, receiving a signature using DocuSign. Learn more about online forms here.

    Sign In to Access Online Forms
    or Join CAA

    Our Online Forms Tool features include:

    • Create your own online forms library with logos and personal branding
    • Fill out applications, contracts or forms quickly and easily
    • Save time by using auto-populate fields and auto-math functions
    • E-mail, print, fax, e-sign or save your documents
    • Detailed reporting to show member or employee usage
    • Transaction management tools to monitor member/employee contracts, pending contracts and status
    • Streamline your business and reduce operating costs by eliminating the printing, storage and clutter of endless paper files
    • Reduce your overall business risk with uniform standards of practice
    • Access your document library anytime from anywhere
    • Works on all tablet platforms and smartphones

    Other ways to get CAA forms

    Incorporate into your property management software:  If you are using property management software that allows importing forms templates (i.e. Yardi) and have 200 or more units on your membership, we can provide our forms electronically to be integrated into your platform.  Just contact our office at (800) 967-4222 for more information.

    Order padded forms and brochures: CAA offers preprinted forms that members can order. They are available here.
