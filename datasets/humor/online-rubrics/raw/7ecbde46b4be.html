Applications for Fringe 2026 are now open!

For 25 years Underbelly has been at the forefront of Fringe programming as one of the largest venue operators at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe. Each year we are proud to present an eclectic and exciting mix of theatre, comedy, circus, children’s shows, cabaret, music and more. In 2025 we presented over 160 shows in 20 venues across our 4 sites; and now we are delighted to invite applicants to get in touch once more as we build our 2026 festival programme.

Please use the information on the links below as a guide to performing with us. Please note, some of the information, especially pertaining to venue specifications, is from Fringe 2025 and may be subject to change.

How to Apply
We use Eventotron.com to process all our applications for Edinburgh Festival Fringe. Eventotron is a purpose-built system that allows touring companies to log all the application, registration and pre-production information needed to produce their work in one easily accessible place. 

Click here to apply for Fringe 2026 through Eventotron.


If you have any queries about the application process, please email applications@underbelly.co.uk

For information about performing at our other festivals, please email programming@underbelly.co.uk.
