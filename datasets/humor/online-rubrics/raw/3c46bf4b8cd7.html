Welcome to Le Robert Online Dictionary!

Le Robert Online Dictionary is the best free French dictionary, and features a wealth of extra resources to help you perfect your French and explore all the subtleties of the language:

  • 145,000 regularly updated definitions,
  • 200,000 synonyms, arranged by meaning,
  • audio pronunciation of all words,
  • 6,500 conjugation tables,
  • 85,000 collocations (combinations of words frequently appearing together),
  • millions of examples in context, taken from press articles, scientific texts, works of literature or official documents,
  • all the grammar and spelling rules you need to make sustainable progress,
  • historical definitions taken from the 17th-century Dictionnaire universel de Furetière to illustrate the evolution of the language over the centuries,
  • word games,
  • and a regularly updated blog with features on words and expressions, language Top 10s, podcasts and more

The essential site for anyone seeking clear, reliable and easily accessible information about the French language: learners, students, regular or occasional writers, aspiring authors, word game enthusiasts, and those who are simply curious.

French language professionals and word lovers: discover even more content (etymology, literary quotations, more detailed definitions etc) and a range of features to enrich your research by subscribing to Petit Robert de la langue française or Grand Robert de la langue française.

Why not also explore Le Robert Correcteur, La Certification Le Robert and Grand Robert et Collins.
