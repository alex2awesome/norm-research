SmartLess is a podcast hosted by the American actors Jason Bateman, Sean Hayes, and Canadian actor Will Arnett. Its first episode was released on July 20, 2020, and new episodes are released weekly each Monday.[1][2] Every episode begins with one of the hosts revealing a mystery guest to the other two hosts. Once revealed, the three hosts begin interviewing the guest.[1]

In May 2021, the podcast signed with Creative Artists Agency.[3] In June 2021, the podcast was acquired by Amazon for around US$80 million. With the deal, the podcast was made available on Amazon Music and Wondery+ one week before other podcast and streaming platforms.[4][5] SiriusXM acquired the podcast from Amazon in 2024 for a reported US$100 million.[6]

On May 23, 2023, Max premiered a six-episode documentary-style special following the podcast's North American six-city tour, which began in February 2022, featuring behind-the-scenes footage and celebrity interviews.[7][8]

Reception

[edit]

SmartLess won "Best Comedy Podcast" at the 2022 iHeartRadio Podcast Awards.[9]

In a positive review for The Irish Times, Fiona McCann stated that "the camaraderie between hosts and guests often works to disarm the latter and allow for something fresher than your usual famous-person-talking show".[10] In November 2024, Apple announced that the podcast was the fifth most popular show on its platform in 2024.[11]
