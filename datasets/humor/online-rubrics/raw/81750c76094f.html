Hulu + Live TV is one of the best ways to get the same live TV channels as you would get with cable, but without contracts or extra fees. With Hulu, you will be able to watch Live TV on major streaming devices including Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV, Chromecast, iOS, and Android.

What Are the Plans and Pricing of Hulu + Live TV?

Hulu Live TV has two plans, starting at TBD, both which include The Disney Bundle at no extra charge.

Hulu + Live TV Plans

  • Hulu + Live TV w/ The Disney Bundle (Ads): TBD/mo.
  • Hulu + Live TV w/ The Disney Bundle (No Ads): $82.99/mo.

While both plans include the same set of Live TV channels, the cheaper Hulu + Live TV base plan ($69.99/mo.) includes The Disney Bundle with Ads, while the more expensive plan at $82.99 a month includes The Disney Bundle (No Ads).

With both plans, you will get access Hulu’s on-demand library of Hulu Originals like The Handmaid’s Tale, Only Murders in the Building, as well as Disney+ and ESPN+ included.

So what cable channels will you be able to watch when you sign-up for Hulu + Live TV?

What Channels are Included with Hulu Live TV?

Hulu + Live TV gives users cable-free access to both live and on demand content. It includes over 70 channels, 33 of which are in cable’s top 35, local channels, and regional sports networks (RSNs) in select markets.

With either plan, you will get the same line-up of live TV channels, including local channels, regional sports (select markets), and cable channels like ESPN, FS1, CNN, Fox News, MSNBC, Bravo, USA, and NFL Network.

The subscription also includes Unlimited cloud DVR storage, with the ability to skip through commercials.

If you want additional channels, they have a Sports Add-on, which includes NFL RedZone, as well as an Entertainment Add-on, and Español Add-on.

Hulu + Live TV Add-ons

  • Hulu + Live TV Sports Add-on: $10/mo.
  • Hulu + Live TV Entertainment Add-on: $8/mo.
  • Hulu + Live TV Español Add-on: $5/mo.

Local Channels on Hulu Live TV

Can You Get Local Channels on Hulu?

Hulu Live TV includes live local channels, including CBS, NBC, FOX, ABC, and Telemundo. This means that even if you can’t get a good signal with your OTA antenna, you can watch your favorite network TV shows.

Live local channels are available in most of top 200 cities. If they don’t carry your market’s local channels, you can watch the same content on-demand following day.

Local Channel Restrictions

While you’re traveling, you can watch the local channels of the market you’re visiting (if available).
