Learn Korean in Phoenix: 
King Sejong Institute & America’s #1 Korean Classes

Looking for the best way to learn Korean in Phoenix?

We offer WASC-accredited and King Sejong Institute-certified Korean language classes for learners of all ages in the Phoenix and Arizona region. From preschoolers to adults, our top-rated programs follow ACTFL standards and are trusted by families, heritage speakers, educators, and Korean culture enthusiasts across Arizona. We offer Korean online classes for beginners with little to no experience with the Korean language in the greater Phoenix and beyond. 

🇰🇷 Korean American Center – Accredited Phoenix Korean Language Institute

Learn Korean in Phoenix, AZ. The Korean American Center offers WASC-accredited and King Sejong Institute-certified Korean language classes for learners of all ages in Phoenix and Arizona. Our top-rated programs follow ACTFL standards and are designed to support a wide range of students—from preschoolers and K–12 learners to college students, working adults, and heritage speakers.

Online Korean Courses for Learners in Phoenix, AZ

All classes are held live on Zoom and led by experienced, bilingual instructors. No prior experience is necessary, and students can participate from anywhere in the Phoenix area. Join learners who are building Korean fluency, cultural understanding, and a strong sense of community—without leaving home.

Take High School level Korean in Phoenix, even if your school doesn’t offer it!

 Don’t let your school’s course list limit you. The Korean American Center (KAC) offers a nationally accessible high school Korean pathway taught by expert instructors. We’re fully accredited and issue transcripts you can submit to your school for credit or placement consideration.
