Recommend me a book on comedy writing
January 8, 2023 11:25 AM   Subscribe

What is a good book on how to write comedy?

I am a pretty funny guy. A delight at parties. I've always wanted to try my hand at comedy writing but don't know how. Sketch comedy has always interested me, though I think an ideal product from this would look like a one-person show or podcast. I want to do this purely for my own pleasure and would share a piece only if I thought it met a certain level of quality. I have no desire to go pro or become a tic-tok sensation.

Interested in
-a technical breakdown of the writing process: how to break down premises, structure scenes, develop characters, etc.
-covers scripted format, but essays or other styles are good too

Less interested in
-example of great comedy writing
-books on improve
-books on general script writing
-memoirs

From a previous related question this was recommended: Comedy Writing Secrets

Thanks everyone!
posted by munchingzombie to Media & Arts (8 answers total) 6 users marked this as a favorite
 
I like reading books about all types of writing processes and I really enjoyed Poking A Dead Frog: Conversations with Today's Top Comedy Writers by Mike Sacks.
posted by bookmammal at 1:30 PM on January 8, 2023 [2 favorites]


Not a book, but The Comedian's Comedian podcast is one comedian (Stuart Goldsmith) interviewing other comedians about their craft - mostly Brits.

It describes itself as being: "For anyone who writes comedy, makes comedy, loves comedy, or just has an interest in comedians and what makes them so annoying."
posted by penguin pie at 4:44 PM on January 8, 2023 [1 favorite]


But a book, but this podcast by Tim Ferris with Jerry Seinfeld might be of interest. A transcript is available.
posted by at at 4:49 PM on January 8, 2023 [1 favorite]


The podcast Good One: A Podcast About Jokes gets at the craft of comedy writing throughout its interviews.
posted by knile at 5:22 PM on January 8, 2023


Best answer: These two books are broader than sketch writing, which seems like they might be good for your purposes:
Creating comedy narratives for stage and screen
How to write comedy

Best answer: How To Write Funny by Scott Dikkers (The Onion) goes over a lot of basic comedy craft & structure. He has a website with additional resources, and The Second City offers a lot of remote-learning classes, including ones specifically focused on sketch comedy.

I was going to mention the UCB manual. It cannot be overstated how influential UCB's training materials and analysis of comedic scenes are. In particular the concept of "game of the scene" which roughly translates as the unusual thing that is explored and heightened.
