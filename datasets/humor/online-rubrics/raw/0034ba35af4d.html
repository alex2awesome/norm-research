Unmatched in strength, cunning, and resolve, Conan is a force to be reckoned with - and revered

Born amidst the distant crags of Cimmeria, Conan traveled far from his homeland, yearning to wring as much juice from the fruit of life as he could. From the snowy peaks of Vanaheim to the sweltering jungles of Kush, Conan feared no man or God as he wielded sword and shield to battle hordes of savage Picts, thwart the schemes of evil sorcerers, and slay otherworldly monsters. And yet, despite his growing renown, Conan’s reputation as a barbarian preceded him: a man who refused to be softened by the fickle comforts of civilization. It is this potent mixture of savagery and cunning that makes Conan so fearsome, deadly, and enigmatic.

How Conan came to life

Long has the legend of Conan the Barbarian endured, spanning the ages and capturing the imagination of countless souls. Born from the mind of the great Robert E. Howard in the 1930s, he burst forth onto the literary scene with an unmatched ferocity. From his inception, Conan was a force of nature – a wild, untamed warrior roaming the land in search of adventure and fortune. With his boundless courage and unparalleled strength, Conan faced down beasts and men alike, carving out a reputation as the fiercest warrior to have ever roamed the Earth.

As time went on and Conan’s exploits were retold in countless forms – from comic books to movies and video games – every story remained true to the roots of his character. For Conan’s identity is what makes him a true titan of fantasy fiction: his strength and determination, his strategic mind and unyielding cunning, his insatiable lust and battle prowess. Conan’ legend will only continue to grow.
