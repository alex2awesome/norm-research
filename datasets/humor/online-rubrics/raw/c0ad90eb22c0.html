    WELCOME TO IMPROV-LA!

    Be confident. Make new friends. Become fearless - both on stage... and in life.

    Improv-LA is a boutique LA-based school who has we’ve helped 100,000+ students from all over the world become funnier and more confident, while just plain have more fun in their lives- and we’re ready to help you do the same. Improv isn't just for actors!

    ​

    While other Los Angeles improv schools focus only on stage performance, we teach you the true power of improv: how to apply these skills to your real life such as communication skills, thinking faster on your feet, or just feel more confident in scary social situations, our curriculum is built for you, our student. (And yes, we'll also teach you how to be a funny, awesome improviser too!)​
     

    Why People Choose Improv-LA to Learn From?
     

    • Supportive, No-Ego Environment:
      Forget auditions, cliques, or competing for stage time. We offer a positive community focused on building you up, not tearing you down, making us the most welcoming alternative to "comedy factories."
       

    • Real-World Skills:
      We include how to use improv class to be better at public speaking, overcoming social anxiety, and talking to people with confidence. Our teachers help you think faster on your feet for work, social settings, and beyond. It's not just fun for a few weeks, but skills that will last long after class ends.
       

    • Every Improv Style:
      We’re the only school teaching every improv style using "cheat codes" that save you years of training. Be able to do improv with anyone, from anywhere.
       

    • Affordable & Student-First:
      Our curriculum puts you first, not our shows. You’ll find your voice, meet friendly people with goals just like you, and have more fun than you ever thought possible.
