40 Best Sitcoms of All Time That’ll Have You Laughing Out Loud

They're the perfect watch for a cozy night in!

By Kelly O'SullivanPublished: Sep 18, 2025

Every item on this page was chosen by an editor from The Pioneer Woman. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Don't get us wrong, we love losing ourselves in a tearjerking drama or a fast-paced action series. Sometimes, though, especially after a long and stressful day, all we really want to do is curl up on the couch and put on something mindless. Comforting, funny, and feel-good, situation comedies—or sitcoms for short—like Seinfeld, The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air, and Friends are as reliable as a childhood friend and as cozy as a warm hug from your mom. Whether it's set in a household with family, an apartment with friends, or an office with coworkers, sitcoms always feature lovable characters, hilarious lines, and ridiculous situations. Simply put, these types of TV shows are dependable and stable, and they make the outside world feel less chaotic.

Since the sitcom genre has existed for decades, there are plenty of nostalgic classics to choose from, some of which you probably used to watch when you were younger. Maybe you're a Cheers fanatic, or perhaps you can recite every major moment from I Love Lucy. If you're like Ree Drummond, you probably find yourself dreaming of Gilligan's Island reruns. Luckily, with streaming services, we can rewatch any of these old-time series any time we want. Because sitcoms are still wildly popular today, you can also try some of the many recent hits like Abbott Elementary or Schitt's Creek that have generated a lot of buzz (and some are still churning out new series!).

Some of the best sitcoms come with a little drama ("We were on a break!"), but most of the time, they're filled with humor that will have you laughing out loud. Whether it's background noise while you cook or an immersive watch that you stay up all night to finish, this roundup of the 40 best sitcoms will put a smile on your face.

1

Seinfeld

STREAM NOW

Who knew a show about nothing could be so entertaining? Follow the antics of Jerry, Elaine, George, and Kramer and be prepared to have a lot of belly laughs.

2

The Brady Bunch

STREAM NOW

"Here's the story of a lovely lady who was bringing up three very lovely girls..." The iconic theme song of The Brady Bunch tells you pretty much everything you need to know about this sitcom.

3

Friends

STREAM NOW

Whether you're a Rachel or more of a Joey, you'll relate to many of the ups and downs these friends go through as they navigate adulthood in New York City.

4

Family Matters

STREAM NOW

If the hilarious antics of Steve Urkel can't put you in a good mood, nothing can. The Winslow family’s interactions with their quirky next-door-neighbor make this show pure sitcom gold.

5

I Love Lucy

STREAM NOW

America's first smash sitcom originally premiered more than six decades ago. But trust us (and the title): You'll never stop loving Lucy.

6

The Office

STREAM NOW

The everyday lives of the Dunder-Mifflin staff are showcased in this hilarious documentary-style sitcom. It's made for binge-watching.

7

Cheers

STREAM NOW

Sam Malone (Ted Danson) hires Diane Chambers (Shelley Long) as a waitress at his cozy bar in Boston after her fiancé walks out on her. Join the regulars at the counter and watch their complicated love story unfold.

8

black-ish

STREAM NOW

Dre Johnson (Anthony Anderson) has it all: a beautiful house, a loving wife, and four great kids. But he begins to wonder if his luxurious life is taking away from his family's identity. So, he enlists the help of his father to teach them about Black culture.

9

The Simpsons

STREAM NOW

This long-running animated sitcom proves that cartoons are definitely not just for kids. When you follow the daily lives of the Simpson family in fictional Springfield, you'll realize the satirical comedy is actually commenting on key societal issues.

10

Modern Family

STREAM NOW

An eccentric group of relatives proves there's no such thing as a "typical" family anymore. There are plenty of laughs and some tears, but in the end, they're connected by one thing: love.

11

The Golden Girls

STREAM NOW

Dorothy, Rose, Blanche, and Sophia make up television's original girl gang. This unlikely circle of friends shares one roof (and a lot of cheesecake) as they live out their golden years together.

12

The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air

STREAM NOW

"In West Philadelphia born and raised..." If you don't already have the catchy lyrics to this sitcom's theme song memorized, it won't take long for them to get stuck in your head. Other than that, though, The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air is always an entertaining watch.

13

The Big Bang Theory

STREAM NOW

Leonard and Sheldon are brilliant physicists who can solve any equation, but they have no idea how to figure out women—until a beautiful aspiring actress moves in next door.

14

Parks and Recreation

STREAM NOW

If you're a fan of The Office, Parks and Rec is a must-watch. Amy Poehler leads a team of public officials in a small Indiana town on a seemingly impossible journey to make their home a better place.

15

Full House

STREAM NOW

Three dads are better than one when you're trying to raise a group of young girls. A widower enlists the help of his rockstar brother-in-law and zany best friend to give his daughters the best life they could imagine.

16

Schitt's Creek

STREAM NOW

When financial disaster strikes the Rose family, they think their lives are over—until they get to know the people of a small town called Schitt's Creek.

17

New Girl

STREAM NOW

When a quirky and awkward girl moves in with three single guys, none of them knows what to expect. But one thing's for sure: Their lives will never be the same.

18

How I Met Your Mother

STREAM NOW

Through a series of heartwarming and hilarious flashbacks, a father explains to his grown children exactly how he met their mother.

19

Arrested Development

STREAM NOW

When Michael Bluth's (Jason Bateman) father is sent to jail for white-collar crime, it's up to him to run the family real estate business that he never wanted to be a part of.

20

Boy Meets World

STREAM NOW

The love story of Cory (Ben Savage) and Topanga (Danielle Fishel) spans decades and is truly unlike any other. Nineties kids will understand!
