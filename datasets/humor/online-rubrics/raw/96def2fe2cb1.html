Contact Me

Your Comic Maven Nicole D'Andria

​I'm a freelance writer and editor with over a decade of experience. ​​
​Currently, I'm a full-time webcomic editor at Storygrounds!

Some of my current freelance clients include Tapas Media, J-Novel, and Scout Comics. I'm also the Digital Director for Scout Comics. 

One of my proudest achievements is my own comic book series, Road Trip to Hell, an ongoing series in which a man haunted by a terrible power finds out he's the son of the devil and everyone from Hell is trying to kill him. After successfully funding three issues on Kickstarter, the series was picked up by Scout Comics. Order it and related merch on their official website.

Right now, I'm editing and writing action-packed adventures of the Salty Roos. I previously adapted episodes of the television show Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug and Cat Noir and Peter David's novel Artful to comics.
