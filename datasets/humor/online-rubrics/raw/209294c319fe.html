    Yankees' power, Ryan Weathers' pitching leads to sweep over Royals

    Pete Caldera, NorthJersey.com
    Sun, Apr 19, 2026·
    6 min read

    NEW YORK - After a rain delay of nearly three hours before Sunday's first pitch, the Yankees powered toward a three-game sweep of the Kansas City Royals.

    Ryan Weathers pitched into the eighth inning, while Aaron Judge, Ben Rice and Trent Grisham slugged home runs in a 7-0 win at Yankee Stadium.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    For Rice, it was his fourth straight game with a home run. And it's the third straight sweep by the Yanks (13-9) against the Royals (7-15), having swept both home-and-away series in 2025.

    Rice was elevated to the leadoff spot by Aaron Boone, with the lefty-hitting first baseman "swinging it about as good as anyone in the league,'' said the manager.

    Boone also wanted some of his lefty hitters against Royals lefty Cole Ragans (4.1 IP, 7 ER, 8 walks, 6 strikeouts), viewed as a neutral starter.

    So, Ryan McMahon was at third base, batting ninth, and Grisham started in center field, batting fifth, and hit the third of three Yankee homers off Ragans on Sunday.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    However, Jazz Chisholm Jr. was not in the lineup (Amed Rosario got the start at second base on Sunday).

    Over his last seven games, Chisholm is 2-for-20 with two singles (one of them a pop fly that fell between two Angels infielders last Wednesday) and nine strikeouts.

    Chisholm has drawn five walks in his last four games. But he's also in a 4-for-34 (.118) slide and 6-for-49 (.122), without a home run in his first 73 at-bats.

    Weathers back out for the 8th

    Not so fast...

    Ryan Weathers will start the eighth inning, with the Royals due to send up 8-9-1 in their lineup...

    ...Weathers retires lefty-hitting No. 8 hitter Michael Massey on a pop fly and that's it. He receives a nice ovation as Angel Chivilli enters to pitch.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Seven scoreless

    Ryan Weathers gets through the seventh inning, stranding two baserunners. Guessing that's it for him, with Fernando Cruz warming and Weathers at 91 pitches, Yanks leading 7-0.

    This was a sharp comeback outing off his loss here to the Angels last Tuesday night, when he surrendered three consecutive first-inning homers (4 in all, over 5 IP) but still registered 10 strikeouts.

    Lost gamble

    With two out, Royals catcher Elias Diaz is thrown out at the plate - trying to score from first base on a Bobby Witt Jr. double to center - to end the KC sixth, Yanks leading 7-0.

    That's how it's going for the Royals these days, with Trent Grisham relaying to Jose Caballero, getting Diaz by a considerable margin.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Royal flush

    The Royals are staring at a possible seventh straight loss, as Trent Grisham cranks a three-run homer to right - his third of the year - off Cole Ragans to make it 7-0 Yanks in the fifth.

    In a rough start to 2026, KC has had a brutal weekend. On Saturday, two outfielders collided while tracking J.C. Escarra's fly ball, opening a path for a five-run inning.

    Here to start the fifth inning, Cody Bellinger's catchable fly ball to shallow left drops in as third baseman Maikel Garcia calls off left fielder Isaac Collins, only for Garcia to give up his pursuit.

    Two batters later, Grisham extends the Yanks' lead. Ragans finally departs, with one out and one on in the fifth, Yanks still up, 7-0.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Ouch

    Nice job by Jose Caballero, tagging up from first base on a Ryan McMahon fly out to deep center.

    But at second base, Caballero gets pegged in the small of the back by a Cole Ragans' pickoff throw. In obvious pain, Caballero is attended to by the Yanks' athletic training staff and stays in the game.

    Rice walks, Judge strikes out, and we go to the fifth inning, 4-0, Yanks.

    Ryan Weathers strikes out the side

    That's four straight Ks for Weathers, who has tossed four scoreless innings so far.

    Weathers strikes out Bobby Witt Jr. (sweeper), Vinnie Pasquantino (changeup) and Salvador Perez (fastball) in the fourth, with the Yanks leading 4-0.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Royals are out of ABS challenges

    And we're only in the third inning, as Maikel Garcia fails to get a called third strike overturned (with two out, none on base).

    Yanks are down to one challenge, after Paul Goldschmidt failed to get a pitch overturned in his first AB.

    Ben Rice, times 4

    For a fourth straight game, Ben Rice has hit a home run and it's 4-0, Yankees.

    It's his eighth homer of the year, one behind Judge. And the Royals' rough start to 2026 (six straight losses, 7-14 record) continues.

    Yankees lead it 3-0

    Besides yielding the Aaron Judge home run, Cole Ragans walked four Yankees in this first inning, pushing his pitch count to 34.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Austin Wells' sacrifice fly gave the Yanks a 3-0 lead after one inning. That's just the second RBI of the year for Wells.

    And just like that...

    Following a walk by Ben Rice, in the leadoff spot, Aaron Judge punishes a Cole Ragans first-pitch curveball 425 feet over the center field wall for a 2-0 Yankees lead.

    It's another first-inning homer by Judge (his fifth of 2026), who has now homered in six of his last eight games. After four starts, Ryan Weathers finally has some runs to work with.

    Trying to get on the board

    Ryan Weathers has made four starts in his very brief Yankees career, and his new team has yet to score a run while he's been on the mound.

    That's 21 total innings.

    March 30 at Seattle: Exits trailing 1-0.

    April 4 vs. Marlins: Exits trailing 3-0.

    April 9 vs. Athletics: Exits trailing 1-0.

    April 14 vs. Angels: Exits trailing 4-0.

    Yankees' minor deal

    Earlier on Sunday, the Yankees acquired minor league right-hander Wilmy Sanchez from the Astros, sending Triple-A infielder Braden Shewmake to Houston.

    A reliever for the Astros' Class AA affiliate this year, Sanchez, 22, has yielded one run in seven innings over his first five appearances with eight strikeouts and four walks allowed.

    Advertisement
    Advertisement
    Advertisement

    Over parts of five minor league seasons in the Houston organization, Sanchez has posted 10 saves and a 3.80 ERA, with 248 Ks (124 walks) in 189.1 innings (105 games, six starts).

    Shewmake, 28, was batting .250 with a .627 OPS in 10 games this year at Class AAA Scranton/Wilkes Barre.

    Claimed off waivers by the Yankees a year ago, Shewmake did not make an appearance with the big club in 2025. He has 31 combined games of MLB experience with the 2023 Atlanta Braves and 2024 Chicago White Sox.
