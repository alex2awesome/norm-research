Maria Angeles Ruiz Moneva
Maria Angeles Ruiz Moneva read her PHD in 2003 and has been a lecturer in English linguistics and translation since 1994. She is currently working at the University of
Zaragoza (Spain) as a tenured lecturer (Associate Professor). Her research interests deal with historical linguistics, translation, English for Specific Purposes (legal discourse), as well as pragmatic approaches to irony, humour and relevance theory.
