Bio

Tig (affectionately named by her brother as a child) was born in Jackson, MS and raised by her single mother in Pass Christian, MS. During hot summer days, her artistic and free-spirited mother would feed the children all three meals at once, then hose down their diapered bodies in highchairs to cut back on cooking and cleaning, leaving more time for her to paint donkeys on the outside of their house. The family eventually moved to Texas, where they gained a step-father who provided structure and alas… a house with no donkeys painted on the walls.

More

While serving time in a Texas high school, Tig failed three grades by entertaining classmates rather than entertaining the notion of a successful academic career. She decided to drop out of school in 9th grade and move to Denver, where she worked briefly in the music industry before moving to LA to lodge herself firmly into the world of comedy.

 

Tig is an Oscar-nominated producer and an Emmy- and Grammy-nominated stand-up comedian, actor, writer, podcaster, and radio contributor, as well as a favorite on numerous talk shows, including THE LATE SHOW WITH STEPHEN COLBERT, THE TONIGHT SHOW WITH JIMMY FALLON, and THE DREW BARRYMORE SHOW. Rolling Stone named her one of the “50 best stand-up comics of all time.” Her latest special, TIG NOTARO: HELLO AGAIN, premiered on Prime Video, garnering multiple Emmy nominations.

Notaro can be seen in THE MORNING SHOW on Apple TV+ and both STAR TREK: DISCOVERY and STAR TREK: STARFLEET ACADEMY on Paramount+. She also appears in the Netflix films YOUR PLACE OR MINE, WE HAVE A GHOST, and Zack Snyder’s ARMY OF THE DEAD. Notaro wrote and starred in the groundbreaking TV show ONE MISSISSIPPI and co-directed the GLAAD-nominated feature film AM I OK? with her wife, Stephanie Allynne, which debuted at Sundance and premiered on HBO Max.

Notaro hosts the critically acclaimed, GLAAD Award–winning podcast HANDSOME with comedians Fortune Feimster and Mae Martin. She is the author of the New York Times best-selling memoir I’M JUST A PERSON. Tig produced the documentary COME SEE ME IN THE GOOD LIGHT, which won a GLAAD Award, the Festival Favorite Award at Sundance, a Cinema Eye Honors Award and was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature. The film premiered on Apple TV+ and in select theaters.

In her time off, Tig enjoys bird-watching with Stephanie, their twin sons, and cats, Fluff, Linus, and Skip, at home in Los Angeles.
