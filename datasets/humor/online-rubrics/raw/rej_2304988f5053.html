Munawar Faruqui (born 28 January 1994) is an Indian comedian, rapper and singer. In 2022, he won the reality TV show, Lock Upp 1.[4] In 2023, he participated in Bigg Boss 17 and emerged as the winner.

Early life and education

[edit]

Faruqui was born in Junagadh, Gujarat,[5][6] on 28 January 1994 into a Gujarati Muslim family.[7] His father lost the money and came under a huge debt.[8] Due to financial constraints, he left school after the 5th class.[9] He started to work to make the ends meet. Still a child, he worked at a gift shop and later joined his mother & grandmother in making and selling samosas and chaklis.[8] His mother committed suicide when he was 14 years old. After his mother's death, he relocated to Mumbai at his aunt's request.[8][10] He started to do some odd jobs in Mumbai.[11] In 2020, his father died, after being paralysed.[11]

Career

[edit]

In April 2020, he uploaded a standup comedy video named "Dawood, Yamraaj & Aurat" on his YouTube channel.[12] He released his debut song "Jawab" in collaboration with Indian musician Spectra in August 2020.

On 28 February 2021, he uploaded a standup comedy video named "Ghost Story" on his YouTube Channel.[13]

In 2022, he became a contestant on Balaji Telefilms' reality television show Lock Upp, hosted by Kangana Ranaut.[14][15] On 8 May 2022, he was declared the show's winner.[16] The show was streamed on OTT platforms ALTBalaji and MX Player.[17]

In 2023, he became a contestant on the reality TV show Bigg Boss 17, hosted by Salman Khan. On 28 January 2024, he was declared the show's winner.[18] He participated in session 1 of Entertainers Cricket League.(ECL)

Personal life

[edit]

Faruqui married Jasmine[19][20] in 2017 but the marriage ended in divorce in 2022. The ex-couple has a five-year-old son.[21]

In December 2021, he started dating social media influencer Nazila Sitaishi.[6][22][23][24] In Bigg Boss 17, Ayesha Khan accused him of two-timing her and Sitaishi and being involved with multiple women.[25][26] Subsequently, on 18 December 2023, Sitaishi broke up with him on Instagram live, accusing him of cheating.[27][28] In May 2024, he married Mehzabeen Coatwala, a makeup artist.[29][30]

Controversy

[edit]

Munawar Faruqui was on his tour performing stand up comedy in different cities of India. On 1 January 2021, he performed a stand-up show at Munro Cafe in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, which was interrupted by Eklavya Singh Gaur, son of BJP MLA Malini Gaur[31] accusing him of making malicious jokes about Hindu deities and the Minister of Home Affairs of India, Amit Shah.[32] On 2 January, he was arrested by Madhya Pradesh Police in the midst of his comic act, stating that he was arrested under the Hate speech laws in India.[33]

His arrest was criticised by some people, including by a small group of global Indian diaspora on 2 February 2021, that said a group of South Asian American stand-up comedians will perform a virtual comedy show on 6 February 2021 to show their solidarity with the jailed Faruqui.[34] He was subsequently granted bail (not acquitted) by the Supreme Court of India.[35] He spent 37 days in jail.[11]

On 26 March 2024, he was detained by Mumbai Police during a raid at an illegal Hookah Bar in Mumbai. He was later released on bail as offence was bailable.[36][37]
