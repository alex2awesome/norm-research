Centimeters to feet formula

To convert centimeters (cm) to feet (ft), you can use the following conversion formula:

Feet = Centimeters ÷ 30.48

In this formula, the number of centimeters is divided by the conversion factor divided to obtain the equivalent length in feet.

How many feet in a centimeter?

There are 30.48 feet in a centimeter. In other words, 1 centimeter is equal to 30.48 feet (1 cm = 30.48 ft).
