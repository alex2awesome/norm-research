AUTHOR: Joe wrote and produced comedy/talk shows for over fifteen years and won four Emmys. He reveals his proven methods in the one-of-a-kind insider's guide "Comedy Writing for Late-Night TV." Joe takes you step-by-step through the process of writing each type of comedy piece in the late-night TV playbook.

TV WRITER/PRODUCER: Joe was a head writer for David Letterman, Caroline Rhea, and Jay Leno. His other writer/producer credits include staff positions on "In Living Color," situation comedies like "Doctor, Doctor," and "Monk." He also wrote and produced pilots for Fox, Columbia, Paramount, Comedy Central, and USA.

AI ENTREPRENEUR: Joe is Founder-President of Twenty Lane Media, LLC, a company dedicated to reimagining creativity. He invented Witscript, an AI-powered app that improvises original, contextually integrated jokes (U.S. Patent Nos. 10,642,939; 10,878,817; and 11,080,485).
