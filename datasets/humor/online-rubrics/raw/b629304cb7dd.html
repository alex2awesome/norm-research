Neighbourhoods

City Centre

  • Old Town

    • The Royal Mile
      Home to Edinburgh Castle and a UNESCO heritage site in the heart of the Old Town.
    • Grassmarket
      A historic marketplace filled with independent shops, bars & restaurants.

  • New Town

    • New Town
      Enjoy shopping & dining amongst Georgian architecture.
    • West End
      One of Edinburgh’s hidden gems, filled with colourful, stylish boutiques and much-loved pubs and bars.
    • Stockbridge
      Discover lush parks, delightful shops and family-friendly markets in this charming neighbourhood.

Beyond the City Centre

  • Southside
    Home to Edinburgh’s oldest university, fascinating museums, cosy coffee shops and leafy spaces.
  • Tollcross
    A vibrant neighbourhood that is perfect for theatre lovers and film fanatics.
  • Bruntsfield and Morningside
    Relaxing, leafy neighbourhood with specialist food & thrift shops and charming cafes.
  • Gorgie & Dalry
    Home of family fun and international food shops.
  • Leith
    A historical port that holds the title of one of the world’s coolest neighbourhoods.
  • Portobello
    Edinburgh’s year-round seaside resort.
  • Corstorphine
    A friendly suburb perfect for nature lovers.
  • South Queensferry
    UNESCO World Heritage Site with coastal views and a nautical heritage.

Day Trips outside Edinburgh

  • East Lothian
    Vast beaches, captivating wildlife and award-winning attractions.
  • West Lothian
    A region rich in heritage, expansive green spaces, and picture-worthy sights.
  • Midlothian
    An area of both natural and industrial heritage.
  • The Kingdom of Fife
    Quaint fishing villages, rich history, and an unforgettable coastline.
  • Scottish Borders
    Rolling hills, winding rivers, and farmland as far as the eye can see.
