The benchmark for creative bravery

For over 60 years, the D&AD Awards have set the standard for work that moves culture and challenges the creative industries to do better.

Judged by the best, a Pencil is a recognition, a career shifter, and a green-eyed provoking inspiration.

Whether you’re in-house, agency-side, or solo creator, it is proof you’ve made something that matters.

D&AD Awards

For professionals – the industry benchmark for commercial creativity, the D&AD Pencil stands for rigour and integrity.

The difference

Not for profit

Enter and support future generations. All our surplus is reinvested into education and development programmes such as Shift and New Blood.

Reassuringly difficult

We famously set the bar high, so that your award means something. We’re not mean, just unerringly focused on driving excellence.

Globally recognised

Entered by almost 90 countries, a win marks your position on the global stage.

Career defining

A Pencil earns you lasting respect from employers and your peers. It can help swing an interview, a pitch, and even the odd invite.

Creative connecting

Winners join the trusted D&AD community for support and advice, and to make work that pushes our craft forwards.
