Offering Comprehensive Services to Help You

WG represents family farmers growing fresh produce in Arizona, California, Colorado and New Mexico.

Western Growers and its members strive to protect and enhance the economic outlook of our association’s farmers, their farmworker partners and the many others whose livelihoods are tied to our businesses. We do this while maintaining the highest commitment to producing and supplying the freshest, healthiest and most affordable fresh produce to people here and around the world.

Learn More

Fighting to Pass Fair Laws that Help Farmers

Farming has never been more challenging, which is why Western Growers invests in fully committed advocates – your advocates – in Sacramento, Phoenix and Washington, D.C. to help pass fair laws that help farmers and support candidates who understand the rigors of working in agriculture. Only Western Growers offers members so many business services, supported by more than 400 dedicated employees.

Protecting You and Your Farmworker Partners

Western Growers Insurance Services combines deep experience with insurance carriers and a sophisticated understanding of agriculture to develop innovative risk management solutions for clients and drive value to the industry through expert consultation, training and educational services.

Learn More

Promoting a Safe and Secure Food Supply

Western Growers Science routinely coordinates with regulators, legislators, universities and scientists to provide technical expertise and resources to help both government officials and industry stakeholders develop, refine and enact world class food safety and sustainable resource management practices.
