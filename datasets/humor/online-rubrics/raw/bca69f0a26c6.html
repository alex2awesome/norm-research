    Robert

    #75 US Recent (Boy Names) #3 US All-Time 4.8M+ US births since 1880

    Meaning & Etymology

    The name "Robert" has Germanic origins, derived from the elements "hrod" meaning "fame" and "beraht" meaning "bright" or "shining." Therefore, the name carries the meaning of "bright fame" or "famous brilliance." This etymology reflects the historical significance and prominence associated with many individuals bearing the name throughout various periods.

    Linguistic Origin

    The name "Robert" has Germanic roots and is a combination of the elements "hrod" and "beraht." It has been widely used in European cultures and has variations in different languages, such as "Rupert" in German and "Roberto" in Spanish and Italian. The name's linguistic background showcases its adaptability and widespread usage across different linguistic and cultural contexts.

    Cultural Background

    In religious and cultural contexts, "Robert" does not have specific religious or spiritual significance. However, it has been widely used in Christian-majority countries and has been associated with individuals of diverse cultural backgrounds, reflecting its adaptability and secular usage.

    Pronunciation

    The name "Robert" is pronounced as ROH-bert. It is a two-syllable name with the stress on the first syllable. Regional pronunciation variants may exist, but the standard pronunciation follows the phonetic breakdown as ROH-bert.

    Gender Usage

    The name "Robert" is traditionally associated with boys and has been predominantly used as a male name. However, it has also been used as a surname and can be found in some cultures as a female given name. In recent years, there has been a trend towards unisex and gender-fluid naming, leading to increased diversity in the usage of the name.

    Mythology & Literature

    The name "Robert" has been associated with various literary and cultural figures, such as Robert Burns, the Scottish poet, and Robert Louis Stevenson, the author of "Treasure Island" and "Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde." Additionally, the name has been depicted in popular culture through characters like Robert Baratheon in "Game of Thrones."

    Historical Significance

    The name "Robert" has historical significance through notable bearers such as Robert the Bruce, King of Scotland, and various European kings and noblemen. It has been prominent in political, literary, and artistic circles, with individuals like Robert Kennedy and Robert Frost leaving a lasting legacy associated with the name.

    Additional Information

    Popularity & Demographics

    "Robert" has been a consistently popular name in the United States, ranking in the top 100 names for boys for much of the 20th century. However, its popularity has declined in recent years, and it is no longer as prevalent as it once was. The name has a rich historical association and has been widely used across different demographic groups.

    Trend Analysis

    The name "Robert" experienced a peak in popularity in the mid-20th century but has since declined in usage. While it may not be as common as it once was, the name continues to maintain a classic and timeless appeal. It is likely to endure as a traditional name choice, albeit with reduced frequency in modern times.

    Geographical Distribution

    The name "Robert" has been prevalent in various regions and cultures, particularly in English-speaking countries and European nations. It has variations in different languages, reflecting its adaptability and usage in diverse geographical locations.

    Personality Traits

    Individuals named "Robert" are often perceived as confident, intelligent, and dependable. The name is associated with qualities of leadership and resilience, reflecting the historical figures and cultural icons who have borne the name. However, as with any name, personality traits and psychological associations can vary widely among individuals.

    Compatibility & Initials

    The name "Robert" pairs well with a wide range of surnames and middle names due to its classic and versatile nature. It has a strong phonetic harmony with many surnames and can form culturally harmonious combinations with various middle names and initials.

    Sociolinguistic Usage

    The usage and perception of the name "Robert" have been influenced by social, economic, and cultural factors. Its historical prominence and widespread usage have contributed to its classic and enduring appeal, while evolving naming trends have diversified its application across different demographic groups.
