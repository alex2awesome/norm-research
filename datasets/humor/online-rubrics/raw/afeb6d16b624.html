How to Convert Centimeters to Feet

What are Centimeters and Feet?

Centimeter (cm) is a unit of length in the metric system, equal to one hundredth of a meter. Commonly used for measuring human height, object dimensions, and in construction.

Foot (ft) is a unit of length in the imperial system, equal to 12 inches or 30.48 centimeters. Widely used in the United States, United Kingdom, and other English-speaking countries.

CM to Feet Conversion Formula

To convert centimeters to feet, use this simple formula:

Feet = Centimeters ÷ 30.48

One foot equals exactly 30.48 centimeters. This is the international standard adopted in 1959.

Conversion Examples

  • 100 cm = 100 ÷ 30.48 = 3.28 feet
  • 150 cm = 150 ÷ 30.48 = 4.92 feet
  • 180 cm = 180 ÷ 30.48 = 5.91 feet
  • 200 cm = 200 ÷ 30.48 = 6.56 feet

Height Conversion

When expressing height in feet, it's common to use feet and inches combination:

  • 170 cm = 5 feet 7 inches
  • 175 cm = 5 feet 9 inches
  • 180 cm = 5 feet 11 inches
  • 185 cm = 6 feet 1 inch

When to Use CM to Feet Conversion?

  • Height specifications for documents in English-speaking countries
  • Construction and architecture when working with international projects
  • Sports — athlete heights in American leagues
  • Aviation — flight altitude often specified in feet
  • Real estate — room dimensions in US and UK

Quick Tips

  • For quick estimation: 30 cm ≈ 1 foot
  • Average adult height: 170-180 cm = 5.6-5.9 feet
  • When converting fractions, remember that 1 foot = 12 inches
