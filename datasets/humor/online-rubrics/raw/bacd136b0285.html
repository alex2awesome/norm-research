The New Yorker Live

Tig Notaro and Jason Sudeikis on Comedy Amid Chaos

As the world slowly reopens, the comedians meet for a virtual happy hour with The New Yorker’s cartoon editor, Emma Allen, to discuss humor and hope in uncertain times, and their upcoming TV projects: Notaro’s fully animated standup special, “Drawn,” on HBO, and the second season of “Ted Lasso,” starring Sudeikis, on Apple TV+. With guidance from their host, Notaro and Sudeikis will also dabble in the classic art of New Yorker cartooning.

Tig Notaro is a comedian, actor, writer, and podcast host. Her upcoming, fully animated comedy special, “Tig Notaro: Drawn,” will air on HBO on July 24th. Often praised for her unique style of deadpan confessional comedy, Notaro revealed in a critically acclaimed 2012 standup show that she had been diagnosed with cancer, not long after suffering a severe intestinal disease, losing her mother unexpectedly, and going through a breakup. Her rise in comedy and battle with cancer were chronicled in the 2015 Netflix documentary “Tig.” Notaro was nominated for a Grammy Award in 2014 for her comedy album “Tig Notaro LIVE,” and again in 2016 for “Boyish Girl Interrupted,” which also received an Emmy nomination. Her most recent special, “Tig Notaro: Happy to Be Here,” was released on Netflix, in 2018. Her numerous film and television credits include “Army of the Dead,” “Star Trek: Discovery,” “Transparent,” “One Mississippi,” “Under a Rock with Tig Notaro,” and “Instant Family.” Notaro is the host of two podcasts: “Tig and Cheryl: True Story,” on which she and her co-host, Cheryl Hines, discuss a new documentary film each week, and “Don’t Ask Tig,” on which she and notable guests offer advice on a range of topics.

Jason Sudeikis is a comedian, actor, producer, and writer. This year, he received a Golden Globe Award, a SAG Award, and a Critic’s Choice Award for Best Actor in a Comedy Series for his titular role in “Ted Lasso,” on AppleTV+. The show, which he co-created with Bill Lawrence, Brendan Hunt, and Joe Kelly, received two Writers Guild of America Awards and a Peabody Award. Sudeikis has also starred in the films “Horrible Bosses,” “We’re the Millers,” “Colossal,” and “Kodachrome,” among others, and on the TV shows “The Last Man on Earth,” “Son of Zorn,” “The Trunk,” and “Eastbound & Down.” Since 2003, he has regularly appeared on “Saturday Night Live”; he served as a writer for the show from 2003 to 2005. The second season of “Ted Lasso” will première on July 23rd.

Emma Allen is The New Yorker’s cartoon editor and edits humor pieces for newyorker.com.

The New Yorker Live events are available to New Yorker subscribers only—no registration required, though you will need to be signed in to this Web site.

At its scheduled time, the live stream will play on this page and will be followed by a Q. & A. session. Closed captioning is available.
