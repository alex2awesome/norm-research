John Locke
(1632–1704)
→
  • sister projects: Wikipedia article, Commons gallery, Commons category, quotes, Wikidata item

Influential English philosopher and social contract theorist.

410859Q9353John LockeJohnLockeJohn LockeInfluential English philosopher and social contract theorist.

[edit]
  • Fundamental Constitutions of Carolina of 1669 (1669)
  • A Letter Concerning Toleration (1689)
  • Two Treatises of Government (1689)
  • An Essay Concerning Human Understanding (1690)
  • Some Considerations of the Consequences of the Lowering of Interest and the Raising the Value of Money (1691)
  • A Second Letter concerning Toleration (1690)
  • A Third Letter for Toleration (1692)
  • Some Thoughts Concerning Education (1693) (transcription project)
  • The Reasonableness of Christianity (1695)
  • A Vindication of the Reasonableness of Christianity, etc. from Mr Edwards's Reflections (1695)
  • A Second Vindication of the Reasonableness of Christianity (1697)
  • A Letter to the Right Reverend Edward, Lord Bishop of Worcester, Concerning some Passages relating to Mr. Locke's Essay of Humane Understanding (1697)
  • Mr Locke's Reply to the Right Reverend the Lord Bishop of Worcester’s Answer to his Letter (1697)
  • Mr. Locke's Reply to the Right Reverend the Lord Bishop of Worcester’s Answer to his Second Letter (1699)
  • Of the Conduct of the Understanding (1706)
