Fantagraphics

The Comics Journal Archive Subscription

On sale date:

The TCJ Digital Archives consists of facsimile scans of the print magazine, from its origin as a 1970s fanzine until the current issue. Study career-spanning interviews with Charles M. Schulz, Alison Bechdel, Chris Ware, Jack Kirby, Moto Hagio, Frank Miller, Alan Moore, Lynda Barry, Brian Michael Bendis, and many more pioneering comics creators (many of whom were in the midst of creating ground-breaking work), consider reviews and analysis by some of the medium’s finest critics, learn about process from sketchbooks, and track industry news across decades. The Comics Journal also covers the history of the art form from 1800s newspaper strips and conversations with Mad magazine and EC cartoonists, while documenting, in real-time, the alternative, mini- and web-comics scenes springing from the underground. It's a rich treasure trove for comics fans, students, and scholars alike.

The Comics Journal Archives are available as an ongoing dubscription package. A subscription will charge you once per years for access to the digital archives until you cancel, pause, or skip your next charge. Please note that canceling, skipping, or pausing your next charge results in you losing access to the archives until you restart your subscription.

After purchasing, you will receive an order confirmation email containing a link to a Google Form, into which you'll enter your name and email. Please whitelist helpme@fantagraphics.com to ensure you receive this email. Your TCJ archive subscription will not be active until you have submitted your email to the Google Form and received an email from our customer service team confirming that your access is active.
