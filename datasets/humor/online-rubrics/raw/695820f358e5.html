Humor Styles Questionnaire

The Humor Styles Questionnaire (HSQ), developed by Rod Martin and colleagues, measures four distinct ways individuals use humor: affiliative, self-enhancing, aggressive, and self-defeating. It is widely used in research and clinical settings to assess how humor relates to personality, coping, and psychological well-being.

8 minutes to complete

Eligibility

The Humor Styles Questionnaire can be completed by anyone interested in understanding their humor style.

Interpretation

Subscales Interpretation
Affiliative High scores reflect frequent use of humor to build social bonds and ease interactions, supporting positive relationships and group cohesion.
Self-Enhancing High scores indicate a tendency to use humor to maintain a positive outlook and cope with stress, promoting personal psychological resilience and well-being.
Aggressive High scores suggest a tendency to use humor at the expense of others (e.g., sarcasm, ridicule), which may harm relationships and is linked to negative psychosocial outcomes.
Self-Defeating High scores show a pattern of using self-disparaging humor to gain approval or hide distress, often associated with lower self-esteem and poorer well-being.

Reference

  • Martin, R.A., Puhlik-Doris, P., Larsen, G., Gray, J., and Weir, K. (2003). Individual differences in uses of humor and their relation to psychological well-being: Development of the Humor Styles Questionnaire. Journal of Research in Personality, 37, 48-75.

Here are some FAQs and additional information
on Humor Styles Questionnaire

What does the HSQ measure?

It measures four humor styles: affiliative, self-enhancing, aggressive, and self-defeating, reflecting both adaptive and maladaptive uses of humor.

How is the HSQ scored?

Each item is rated on a 7-point scale; subscale scores are calculated by summing relevant items, with some items reverse scored.

Who can use the HSQ?

The HSQ is suitable for adults and older adolescents and is commonly used in psychological research, counseling, and organizational settings.
