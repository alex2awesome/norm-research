Our Hulu + Live TV review: Great for live sports

LI
Lisa Iscrupe

Apr 28, 2026 — 6 min read

Hulu + Live TV cost and features

  • $88.99/mo. for Hulu Live TV Only: Does not include the Hulu streaming library or access to Disney+ or ESPN Select
  • $89.99/mo. for Hulu (With Ads) + Live TV (95+ channels), Disney+ (With Ads) and ESPN Select (No Ads)
  • $99.99/mo. for Hulu (No Ads) + Live TV (95+ channels), Disney+ Premium (Limited Ads) and ESPN Select (No Ads)
  • Unlimited cloud DVR storage
  • Two simultaneous streams (or add unlimited screens for $9.99/mo.)

What we like about Hulu + Live TV

  • Solid channel lineup

  • Great for live sports

  • Includes Hulu on-demand

Things to consider

  • Poor DVR user experience

  • Only two simultaneous streams (unless upgraded)

  • Add-ons can raise overall price

Our expert Hulu + Live TV review: Starting at $89.99/mo., Hulu + Live TV isn’t that much cheaper than many cable plans. It also falls in the middle of the pack regarding the channels it offers, extra perks like cloud DVR storage and user experience.

What does Hulu + Live TV excel at? Live sports. It carries as many local and national games as any streaming service around.

What live TV plan is best for you? In our review of live TV streaming services, we picked YouTube TV ($82.99/mo.) as the best option for most people. But which one is right for you comes down to a few personal preferences. Would you rather get A&E or AMC? How much DVR storage do you need? For most people, Hulu + Live TV is absolutely worth a look.

Hulu + Live TV channels

You’ll get 95+ channels in total — fewer than YouTube TV’s 100+ and fuboTV’s 130+ — but Hulu is refreshingly free of the filler that pads most services’ numbers.

For a complete listing of the channels available in your area, enter your ZIP code in Hulu’s lookup tool. Here are some of the most popular channels included in Hulu + Live TV:

  • Cartoon Network
  • ESPN channels
  • Fox Sports
  • TCM
  • Disney Channel
  • Animal Planet
  • Food Network
  • Travel Channel

Hulu has one of the most well-rounded and comprehensive channel lineups of any live TV streaming service. Whether you’re looking for sports, news or TV for the whole family, chances are Hulu has everything you need.

What channels is Hulu + Live TV missing?

Even though Hulu has a decent channel lineup overall, it’s missing some big names. Here are some of the most popular channels that aren’t available in Hulu’s base plan or any of its add-ons:

  • AMC
  • BBC America
  • IFC (Independent Film Channel)
  • NBA TV
  • Sundance TV
  • YES Network (Yankees Entertainment and Sports)

If any of these are must-haves for you, YouTube TV, DIRECTV streaming TV or fuboTV might be better options, although they each have their own drawbacks, too.

Plenty of add-ons are available

Hulu also offers several add-ons to its live TV service that allows you to customize your package.

How much are Hulu + Live TV add-ons and premium channels?

  • Sports add-on package – $9.99/mo. 
  • ESPN Unlimited – $29.99/mo.
  • Español add-on package – $4.99/mo.
  • Entertainment add-on package – $7.99/mo.
  • HBO (With Ads) – $9.99/mo.; $16.99/mo. for No Ads
  • Cinemax – $9.99/mo. 
  • Paramount+ with SHOWTIME – $12.99/mo. 
  • STARZ – $10.99/mo. 

How does Hulu compare to other live TV streaming services?

Among cable TV replacements, Hulu’s closest competitors are Sling Orange + Blue, YouTube TV and fuboTV. These services share about 90% of the same channels, so the best streaming TV service for you comes down to your personal channel preferences and budget.

How does Hulu + Live TV fare for sports fans?

Hulu + Live TV is a great option for sports fans, especially college sports supporters. Most TV watchers will enjoy Hulu’s coverage of many national and local games, although diehard fans might find a couple of channels missing.

Our Hulu + Live TV review focused on two types of live games: nationally televised and local teams.

National games

Hulu has most of the channels that air nationally televised games from the major American sports, but it is missing the league-specific channels from the NFL, MLB, NBA and NHL. While these are generally second-tier marquee games, it could be a deal-breaker if your team happens to be playing in one of them.

Hulu does have excellent college football and basketball coverage — the only conference channel it’s missing is the Pac-12 Network (available on Sling TV and fuboTV).

    Navigating Hulu + Live TV: Pros and cons

    We spent about a week using the Hulu interface on an Amazon Fire TV Stick, Apple TV and Roku Streaming Stick, and it left a lot to be desired on each device.

    • Confusing navigation: It’s cumbersome to even get to the live TV part of the Hulu app. When you click the “TV” section, for example, it takes you to Hulu’s on-demand library. Fortunately, Hulu created a how-to video guide to navigate their Live TV selections
    • Limited-view channel guide: You can only see about four channels at once, and only the program that’s currently playing. To see what’s coming up next, you have to click the channel and arrow over, and even then, you can’t see anything beyond the next immediate slot

    Mobile app

    Hulu’s mobile app has received overwhelmingly positive reviews, and we enjoyed the experience of using Hulu on our phones much more than on streaming devices. While the guide still only shows one time slot at a time, it was much easier to see what was coming up next — plus, your current show continues playing while you browse. Hulu’s app has a rating of 4.6 on the iPhone App Store and 4.4 on Google Play, both with over 1.4 million reviews.

    Hulu Cloud DVR storage and streaming 

    Key features of the Hulu Cloud DVR include unlimited storage and simultaneous streaming.

    Recordings expire after nine months, or if the account is canceled or past due.
    Watch on two devices at the same time, or upgrade to unlimited devices (in the same home) for $9.99/mo.
    You can skip the ads in your Cloud DVR recordings. You cannot fast-forward when watching live TV or on-demand content.

    Expert take: Is Hulu + Live TV worth it?

    Does Hulu + Live TV actually save you money?

    At $89.99/mo., Hulu + Live TV is one of the more expensive streaming services around, although YouTube TV and fuboTV aren’t much less. Plus, when you factor in the necessary separate cost of high-speed internet for your home, you’ll likely pay more for TV and internet than for a traditional cable TV bundle.

    However, the value of the channel lineup you receive with Hulu’s Live TV service often outweighs the limited selection included with many cable providers’ most basic bundles. So the real question is: Are you willing to pay more for Hulu’s channel variety, extra streaming content and added flexibility?

    How does Hulu compare to cable bundles?

    With live streaming services, you don’t need to rent equipment and you aren’t locked into long contracts. Additionally, the American Customer Satisfaction Index (ACSI) reports that the streaming services industry received much higher satisfaction ratings than cable and satellite TV. In other words, while the savings might be negligible — or even nonexistent — you’ll probably have a better experience with Hulu than with a traditional TV company.

    Hulu + Live TV FAQs

    Which streaming devices work with Hulu + Live TV?

    Hulu + Live TV works with more devices than any other streaming service. Here’s everything you can currently stream Hulu on: Amazon Fire TV; Android, LG and Samsung smart TVs; Apple TV; Google Chromecast; Roku. You can also stream on Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, Xbox One and 360.

    What internet speed do I need for Hulu + Live TV?

    Hulu recommends at least 8 Mbps for live TV, but if you plan on watching 4K content — currently limited to Hulu’s original shows and movies — that number goes all the way up to 16 Mbps. You’ll also need to increase your bandwidth for every device that’s using the connection at the same time.

    Can you fast-forward through commercials on Hulu + Live TV?

    Unfortunately, you can’t fast-forward through commercials on Hulu + Live TV, or any live TV streaming service. You can fast-forward through ads on programs you’ve recorded in your DVR.

    Can you get local channels on Hulu?

    Yes. Hulu + Live TV is one of the only streaming services that includes local channels, including ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC, PBS and The CW.

    You can also access local channels for free using a TV antenna or the free streaming service Locast, which is currently available in 19 U.S. markets.
