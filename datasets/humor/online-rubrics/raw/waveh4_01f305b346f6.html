Arthur Schopenhauer
(1788–1860)
→
  • sister projects: Wikipedia article, Commons gallery, Commons category, quotes, Wikidata item

German philosopher; best known for his work The World as Will and Representation; attempted to make a career as an academic by responding to and expanding upon Immanuel Kant's philosophy concerning the way in which we experience the world
