How to convert cm to feet

The formula for converting centimeters to feet is:

Feet = Cm ÷ 30.48

The formula for converting centimeters to feet AND inches is:

Whole feet = floor(Cm ÷ 30.48)
+
Inches = (Cm ÷ 30.48 - Whole feet) × 12

Conversion methodology

To convert centimeters to feet, divide your cm figure by 30.48. To convert to feet and inches, divide your cm figure by 30.48 to get your feet and then multiply the remainder decimal by 12 to get your inches.

Example conversion

Example: Mabel is 171cm tall. She wants to know how tall she is in feet and inches. She can calculate as follows:

  1. Height in feet = 171 ÷ 30.48 = 5.61 feet.
  2. There are 12 inches in a foot. Her height remainder in inches = 0.61 feet * 12 = 7.32 inches.
  3. Mabel's height is 5 feet 7.32 inches (5'7").

What is 180cm in feet and inches?

180cm is equal to 5 feet and 11 inches (5'11"), rounded to the nearest inch. There are 30.48 centimeters in one foot.

How to convert feet to cm

To convert feet to centimeters, multiply your feet figure by 30.48 (cm = feet × 30.48). To convert from feet and inches, convert your feet figure to inches first (multiply it by 12), add on the inches remainder and then multiply the result by 2.54.

Example conversion

Example: James is 5ft 10 inches tall. He wants to know how tall he is in centimeters. He can calculate as follows:

  1. Height in inches only = (5 feet × 12) + 10 = 70 inches.
  2. Height in cm = 70 inches × 2.54 = 177.8cm.
  3. James is 177.8cm tall.

How many cm is 5'7"?

5 feet 7 inches is equal to 170.18 centimeters. As there are 2.54 cm in an inch, we can calculate it by converting to inches first: [(5 × 12)+7] × 2.54 = 170.18cm.
