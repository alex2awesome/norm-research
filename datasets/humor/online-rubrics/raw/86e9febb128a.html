ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

FROM THE PRODUCERS OF THE RIOT COMEDY CLUB

The Riot Comedy Festival is coming back for its fifth year in Houston, TX from April 2-10, 2027. The Riot Comedy Festival is an annual community event created by the founder of The Riot, award-winning comedian and entrepreneur Brian Gendron. Alongside his team of associate producers and dozens of volunteers The Riot Comedy Festival puts on over thirty events including famous comedians, standup, podcasts, improv, music, a national comedy contest, and more. 


The vision for the festival is to connect fans with comedians through a fun and intimate experience and to bring the comedy industry to Houston. This years festival has expanded but remains in the Montrose neighborhood! 


We will be hosting shows at Arena Theatre, The Riot Comedy Club, The Gordy Theatre (Stages.) and Riot Comedy Club at GuadalaHARRY's in downtown Conroe. 


We have a lot of fun activities planned this year throughout the weekend including exclusive parties and our third annual all-you-can-eat Riot Comedy & BBQ Cookoff on Saturday, April 10th 2-5pm on The Gordy Lawn.
