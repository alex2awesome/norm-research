    Who Is Vulture?

    Vulture is a mythic black market vendor in Gray Zone Warfare. Scavenger, trader, survivor. Every Monday he moves — relocating to two Major CoPs across Lamang at local time.

    He pulls gear from fallen operators, restores it to 95% durability, and sells it back. Players trade HVL to build rank and unlock better stock. Ex-military? Local rogue? No one's sure. But if you find him, your squad gets paid.
