CAPTION CONTESTS
A curated list of caption contests
Tracking 24 contests +2 NEW

Caption contests, much like crossword puzzles or word‑play challenges, offer a uniquely stimulating blend of creativity, humor, and quick thinking. They invite you to look at a single image and conjure a line that transforms the scene—sometimes with wit, sometimes with absurdity, and often with a surprising twist that makes the whole thing click. Rewards vary widely across contests: some offer the thrill of seeing your name printed in a prestigious magazine, while others provide generous cash prizes, artist‑signed prints, mugs, T‑shirts, conference passes, or simply the personal satisfaction of crafting the funniest, cleverest, wittiest, or most unexpected caption in the room. For many participants, the real prize is the internal spark that comes from knowing you captured the moment with just the right words.

Caption contests vary widely in tone, and the kinds of captions that win often depend on the personality of the publication hosting the contest. Some outlets, like CartoonStock, embrace more playful or edgy submissions — captions with a bit more innuendo, looseness, or creative risk. Others, such as The New Yorker Caption Contest, lean toward a more refined, understated style and avoid anything too risqué or heavy‑handed.

Quick caption-writing tip: The New Yorker follows a specific style guide, and while they may lightly edit winning entries, it’s smart to format your caption the way they prefer. For example, The New Yorker always prints “O.K.” — never OK, OKAY, or any other variation. Submitting in their house style helps your caption feel polished and contest‑ready from the start.

LEADERBOARDS
Who's winning at being funny?

There’s an old saying that you can’t improve what you don’t measure, and humor is no exception. Leaderboards offer a fun, data‑driven way to see how caption writers and cartoonists stack up across contests, revealing patterns, streaks, surprises, and the occasional underdog surge. They turn what might otherwise be scattered results into something you can explore, compare, and revisit. Use the built‑in tools — pagination, column sorting, and the search box — to dig into the rankings and discover your own insights about the data.
