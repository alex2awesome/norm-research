Description

Take the Best Stand-Up Comedy Class Around & Learn Stand-Up Comedy From the Professor of Stand-Up Comedy Himself

If you’ve ever wanted to make people laugh on stage, Greg Dean’s Level 1 stand-up comedy class is your first step. Over five weeks, you’ll learn how to write original setups, punchlines, and tags using Greg Dean’s Joke Prospector Writing System — the only method that takes you from a blank page to a complete, audience-ready joke.

Whether you join in-person in Santa Monica or live on Zoom, you’ll get the exact same training from Greg Dean or his certified instructors in a fun, supportive environment. Greg’s Stand-Up Comedy Class is one of the longest running programs too!

 Course Formats 

🎭 In-Person – Santa Monica Playhouse with Greg Dean

  • Learn in a real comedy venue.
  • Receive direct feedback from Greg Dean or certified coaches.

💻 Live Zoom – Anywhere in the World with Gayla Johnson

  • Interactive classes with live feedback and coaching
  • Same curriculum as the in-person class

Course Schedules

In Person at the Santa Monica Playhouse

  • Every Tuesday night from 7 to 10 pm for 5 consecutive weeks.

Live over Zoom

  • Every Thursday afternoon from 4 to 7 pm for 5 consecutive weeks.

Missed Classes & Homework

  • If you cannot make a class, contact your us on slack to get the recording of the class.  Make certain you go through the entire recording before the next class.
  • Homework assignments are assigned in some classes, please be certain to do all homework when assigned before the next class.

What You’ll Learn in Level 1

  • How to write setups that spark the audience’s imagination
  • How to write punchlines that surprise and make them laugh
  • How to add tags to extend the laughter
  • How to create one-liners, observational jokes, political jokes, and funny storytelling routines
  • How to perform a polished 3–5 minute stand-up routine
  • How to gain confidence in front of any audience, from open mics to comedy clubs

Why Greg Dean’s Level 1 Stand-Up Comedy Class Works

Greg Dean is the Professor of Stand-Up Comedy, author of Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy, and creator of the Joke Prospector Writing System. His methods are used by comedians all over the world to write better jokes, faster, and perform them with confidence.

This isn’t a “just get up and try” class — it’s a step-by-step system that teaches you the craft of stand-up comedy from the ground up.
