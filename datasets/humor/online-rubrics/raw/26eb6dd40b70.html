About

Produced by The Peoples Improv Theater in NYC the ANNUAL NYC Sketchfest produced by The PIT is  New York City’s premier sketch comedy festival and one of the nation’s largest.

NYC SKETCHFEST 24 aims to showcase both local and national sketch teams, spanning from established favorites to emerging groups, fostering a union of sketch brilliance. Programming will include DIGITAL SKETCH HOURS, the 24 HOUR SKETCH SHOW and more!
