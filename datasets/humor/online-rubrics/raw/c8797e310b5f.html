Exclusive Invitation for Select Authors

You have been hand-picked to join one of the most respected writing organizations — now you can activate your membership via a streamlined, invite-only pathway.



A Selective Invitation for Distinguished Authors

The Writers Guild of America (WGA) has extended an exclusive invitation to a carefully curated group of authors. This initiative acknowledges exceptional storytelling talent, strong literary merit, and the potential for works adapted to screen or digital media.

Professional Pre-Screening & Selection

All invited authors have already undergone evaluation based on:
 
• Creative excellence and originality
• Adaptability to film, TV or multimedia formats
• Publication history or professional writing credentials
• Market and adaptation potential

Simplified Onboarding & Initiation

Once invited, authors can activate membership simply by confirming their intent and paying a one-time initiation fee. No further credits or eligibility checks are required — the pre-screening is already complete.

Benefits of Joining

Creative Community & Networking
Workshops, development labs, writing programs, and connection with industry-seasoned professionals.

Professional Protections
Access to WGA contract safeguards, negotiation power, and legal support for adaptation or screenwriting projects.

Growth & Adaptation Opportunities
Visibility for book-to-film or series adaptation, and support for authors venturing into new media formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the standard WGA membership route?
No. This is a special, invitation-only pathway — different from the typical credit-based screenwriting entry.
 
Why is only the initiation fee required?
Because eligible authors have already been vetted. The initiation fee is the only remaining requirement upon acceptance.
 
Am I obligated to write a screenplay?
No. While membership offers adaptation support, there is no requirement to produce screen content immediately.
 
How common are these invitations?
This is a rare, selective outreach — only a small group of authors receive this opportunity each cycle.
 
Will WGA protect my book if adapted?
Yes. Once a project qualifies under WGA coverage, members benefit from contract protections and guild oversight.
