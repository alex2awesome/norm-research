Ofcom opens consultation on Channel 4 Corporation commissioning policy guidance

Copyright © 2026 LexisNexis
Published on: 20 June 2025
Published by LNB News

LNB News 20/06/2025

Document Information

Issue Date: 20 June 2025

Published Date: 20 June 2025

Jurisdiction(s): United Kingdom

Article summary

Ofcom has launched a consultation on proposed guidance for Channel 4 Corporation (C4C) regarding its Statement of Commissioning Policy (SoCP), as required under the Media Act. The Act removes C4C's publisher-broadcaster restriction, allowing content creation for Channel 4, while introducing new commissioning duties and requiring C4C to prepare an annual SoCP detailing duty fulfilment plans and reviewing previous year's performance. The guidance will outline how C4C should prepare this statement. The consultation closes on 1 August 2025.
