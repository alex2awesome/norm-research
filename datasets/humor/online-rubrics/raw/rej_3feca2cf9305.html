Matthew Steven Rife[1] (born September 10, 1995)[2] is an American comedian and actor. He is best known for his self-produced comedy specials Only Fans (2021), Matthew Steven Rife (2023), and Walking Red Flag (2023), his 2023 Netflix specials Natural Selection and Lucid, his Christmas special Unwrapped and his previous recurring role on the sketch improv comedy and rap show Wild 'n Out.

Early life

[edit]

Matt Rife was born in Columbus, Ohio, and raised in the village of North Lewisburg, Ohio.[3][1] He also lived in New Albany and Mount Vernon.[4] Rife first took an interest in comedy at 14 years old, when a teacher mentioned a talent show at his high school. After encouragement from a friend, he performed in the show. The next year, he began performing professionally.[2] He has four siblings: three older stepsisters and one younger half-sister.[5]

Career

[edit]

In 2015 he began working as a cast member on Wild 'n Out and in 2017, Matt Rife was a host on the short-lived reboot of TRL and appeared on MTV's The Challenge: Champs vs. Stars.[6][7] In 2018 he played a role in the B-movie slasher film Black Pumpkin.[8] Rife is also a paranormal investigator featured on the YouTube channel "Overnight".[9][10][11][12] He was further featured on the television series Bring the Funny in 2019,[13] where he advanced to the Semi-final Showcase but was eliminated in week 8, and the Netflix special Natural Selection.

Rife has self-produced the comedy specials Only Fans (2021), Matthew Steven Rife (2023), and Walking Red Flag (2023).[13] He also had guest roles on the sketch comedy and improv game show series Wild 'n Out, as Brandon Bliss on the comedy television series Brooklyn Nine-Nine, and as Logan on the sitcom television series Fresh Off the Boat.[14] The New York Times described him in 2022 as "just another struggling road comedian" who then became more popular from TikTok over the following year.[15]

In December 2022, Rife signed with Creative Artists Agency (CAA).[16] In April 2023, he released a self-titled special Matthew Steven Rife on Valentine's Day.[17] He then released another special in June 2023 titled Walking Red Flag, focusing on crowd-work. Rife states that he created an entire special surrounding the topic of audience "red flags" to stop audiences from interrupting his future sets with the bit.[18]

On November 15, 2023, Rife released his Netflix standup special Natural Selection, which Vulture reviewed as "half uninspiring dick jokes ... and entirely underwhelming."[19] Cracked stated of the special that the main take aways were "There are lots of women that Rife doesn't care for. He's got it in for waitresses with black eyes (why can't they stay hidden in the kitchen?), hippie chicks, his religious ex, flight attendants who insist on enforcing the rules and the "heavier set." The only women he claims to like are the grandmas he wants to bang."[20]

The special attracted controversy due to a domestic violence joke at the beginning. In a statement in response to the controversy, Rife linked a fake apology note to a website that offered safety helmets for people with disabilities.[21] Rife attracted further controversy in December 2023 after posting a message to a six-year-old that claimed his mother purchased gifts for him from the profits she earned on the website OnlyFans.[22][23] He deleted the message after a public outcry against his comments.[22]

In June 2025, Rife was featured on Forbes Top-Earning Creators list, ranking 7th with estimated earnings of $50 million.[24]

Influences

[edit]

Rife has said his biggest influences in comedy are Dave Chappelle, Ricky Gervais, and Dane Cook. Rife performed at Dave Chappelle's 50th birthday show at Madison Square Garden and has called Chappelle a mentor.[25][26] He has also said Adam Sandler, Jim Carrey, Jim Varney, David Spade, Pauly Shore, and Robin Williams are people he found influence from.[27]

Personal life

[edit]

Rife resides in Rhode Island near Burrillville.[28] He identifies as a Christian but is not religious,[29] stating in an Instagram post that "I wear a Jesus cross around my neck for the same reason regular dudes wear NBA player basketball jerseys — I've never met the guy, but I tell people I can dunk like him at LA Fitness."[30]

In his 2021 special Only Fans, Rife discussed his clinical depression and anxiety disorder diagnoses.[31]

Rife and Elton Castee acquired Ed and Lorraine Warren's former home with The Warrens' Occult Museum in Monroe, Connecticut, in late July 2025.[32] They will be legal guardians of the property's haunted artifacts for the next five years. Rife also mentioned that he plans to host tours as well as allow overnight stays at the property.[33]
