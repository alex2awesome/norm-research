The World-Famous Laugh Factory lands at Horseshoe Las Vegas!

December 16, 2025 by admin

From the Sunset Strip to the Vegas Strip,
The World-Famous Laugh Factory lands at Horseshoe Las Vegas!

Lineup to celebrate comedy’s evolution featuring comedians from classic standup legends to today’s viral stars

TICKETS ON SALE: Today at 10 AM including first mini-residency headliner Concrete Feb. 12-15, 2026

The renowned Laugh Factory, which has been hailed as “the #1 comedy club in the country” by USA Today, is proud to announce a partnership with Caesars Entertainment’s Horseshoe Las Vegas on the famed Las Vegas Strip. The comedy club will rebrand the 250-seat Imagine Showroom as the new Laugh Factory Las Vegas Theater. Opening is set for February 2026 with a Grand Opening celebration scheduled for President’s Day weekend.

“Laugh Factory is built for the biggest stages, from Hollywood to Chicago and now onto the Las Vegas Strip – it doesn’t get any bigger than that,” said David Fuhrer, President of Laugh Factory. “We’re ecstatic for our partnership with Horseshoe Las Vegas and to bring world-class comedians, viral stars and nonstop laughter to the heart of a city built for entertainment.”
To kick off the new comedy club, comedian Bret Ernst, known for his work on the hit Netflix show “Cobra Kai” will headline its soft opening weekend January 29-February 1 with two shows nightly in a classic comedy club three-person lineup of headliner, feature act and host. Then for its Grand Opening President’s Day weekend, viral sensation Concrete will take the stage for a mini-residency February 12-15. Additional details forthcoming.

The comedy club, with its nearly 50-year history at its flagship Hollywood location, continues to deliver laughs and feature talent from comedy’s biggest stars to new up-and-coming comedians. For more than 12 years, the Laugh Factory packed in crowds at their former location at the legendary Tropicana Hotel & Casino with two shows nightly and special event headliners primetime each weekend. Past headliners included comedic greats such as Andrew Dice Clay, Jon Lovitz, Tiffany Haddish, Roseanne, Gallagher, Louie Anderson, Jamie Lissow, Willie Barcena, Michael Blackson and legendary Impressionist Rich Little along with viral comedians from today’s digital age, including Rene Vaca, Concrete and Frankie Quinones. The new venue at Horseshoe Las Vegas will continue to build on that legacy founder Jamie Masada created and built so many years ago.

“We are thrilled to welcome Laugh Factory to Horseshoe Las Vegas,” said Amy Graca, Senior Vice President of Entertainment for Caesars Entertainment. “Bringing one of the world’s most recognized comedy brands to our resort highlights our commitment to delivering unforgettable experiences for our guests. We can’t wait for the laughter and energy Laugh Factory brings to the Horseshoe.”

“We’ve already lined up some fantastic comedians and are in talks with more. We couldn’t be more excited about the lineup taking shape. Get ready for some big laughs as we gear up for what’s going to be a great time,” said Harry Basil, General Manager and Talent Booker for Laugh Factory Las Vegas.

Laugh Factory at the Horseshoe plans to operate Monday-Sunday with two shows nightly at 8:30 and 10:30 p.m., with additional special event headliner residencies Thursday-Sunday at 6:30 p.m. Tickets for its initial soft opening are on sale Tuesday, December 16 at 10 a.m. and start at $37.95 plus tax and fees. Tickets are available for purchase at Ticketmaster.com. Guests must be 21 and older.

ABOUT LAUGH FACTORY
Since opening the doors to its original Hollywood location 50 years ago, Laugh Factory has become known for launching some of the hottest stand-up comedians in the world, including: Tim Allen, Tiffany Haddish, Dane Cook, Matt Rife, Adam Ray, Drew Carey, Sarah Silverman, Bobby Lee, Brad Williams and many more. The Laugh Factory has California locations in Hollywood, Long Beach, Covina, San Diego, plus in Chicago and Reno, Nev. The Laugh Factory is known around the world as one of the premier spots for comedy. More information at LaughFactory.com.

ABOUT THE HORSESHOE LAS VEGAS
Horseshoe Las Vegas is located at the center of the world-renowned Las Vegas Strip, across from Caesars Palace and next to Paris Las Vegas. The 44-acre resort consists of two 26-story hotel towers with more than 2,800 guestrooms, including the renovated Resort Rooms and 244 suites. Home to the World Series of Poker® (WSOP), the resort features nearly 70,000 square feet of casino space and a Hall of Fame Poker Room. The Imagine Showroom offers entertainment for all ages, including Paranormal and Potted Potter, while the X Rocks Theater features the hard-hitting, seductive topless revue, X Rocks, and Sin City Stones, a full-scale rock celebration capturing the heart and attitude of one of music’s greatest acts. Also available, BattleBots Destruct-A-Thon, from the creators of the hit TV show, offers the world’s first live robot-combat show. Dining options include Horseshoe’s signature fine dining experience, Jack Binion’s Steak; a food court; Nosh Deli and LavAzza. Guy Fieri’s newest concept, Flavortown Sports Kitchen, also joins the roster of eateries. The resort offers a fitness center, the Blu Pool and attractions such as Twilight Zone by Monster Mini Golf, REAL BODIES, The Cabinet of Curiosities Lounge and the ARCADE at Horseshoe Las Vegas. Horseshoe Las Vegas is operated by a subsidiary of Caesars Entertainment, Inc.
