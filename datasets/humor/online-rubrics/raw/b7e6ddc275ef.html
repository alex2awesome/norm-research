ASSSSCAT LA – Live Improv Comedy Show in Los Angeles

Sunday, May 10, 2026 @ 8:30 PM LA - FRANKLIN
ASSSSCAT—UCB’s signature improv juggernaut—is a live improv comedy show in Los Angeles at the Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre. An all-star comedy show featuring comedians you know from SNL, Dropout, and the world of Film & Television doing improv inspired by true stories from a surprise celebrity guest. ASSSSCAT has played to critical acclaim and sold-out audiences across the country.

What Is ASSSSCAT?

It starts with a group of improv comedians taking the stage with a surprise celebrity guest. After getting a suggestion from the audience, our “monologist “ shares off-the-cuff thoughts, feelings, and true stories inspired by your suggestions. The improvisers then spin this monologue into a series of scenes that will show you why ASSSSCAT is one of the longest running improv shows in the world.

Previous celebrity guests include: Will Ferrell, Mark Hamill, Tina Fey, Charles Barkley, Don Cheadle, Elizabeth Banks, Aubrey Plaza, Kumail Nanjiani, Amy Poehler, Robin Williams and more.

Why See ASSSSCAT?

ASSSSCAT is one of the best improv comedy shows in Los Angeles. Each show is a one-of-a-kind live comedy experience. Whether you’re a longtime fan of UCB or searching for live comedy in LA for the first time, or looking for new exciting date ideas in LA, ASSSSCAT offers a chance to see the best comedians in LA at the top of their game, and don’t forget the surprise celebrity guest at every show! And don’t worry, if you can’t see the show live, we also offer a LIVESTREAM option for all performances!

MADE IN AMERICA

Tuesday, May 12, 2026 @ 8:30 PM LA - FRANKLIN
Teruko and Titi’s Triumph over Tragedy!

MADE IN AMERICA is a funny yet shockingly raw autobiography written and performed by Japanese born artist Teruko Nakajima. She flees her homeland to escape a life filled with physical and emotional abuse. She tries surviving in America by becoming a Dominatrix, salsa dancer, and night club madame. But as the pressures in this new world overwhelm her, Teruko must learn to love herself before her own trauma destroys her.

MADE IN AMERICA is a brutally honest, surprisingly entertaining, and heartbreakingly life-affirming love letter to the human experience. It’s a story for women, Asians, first-generation immigrants, and anyone who is fighting to make their dreams come true.

Teruko’s show shines a necessary light on the tragedies of domestic violence, mental health, child abuse, sexual assault, and suicide. But ultimately provides inspiration to anyone who suffers and needs help.

Her dog Titi performs too.

MADE IN AMERICA received tremendous accolades at the Hollywood Fringe Festival 2022. Winner: Best Solo Show, Solo Splash Award, Best One Person Show (LA Theatre Bite Award), Pick of the Fringe, The Hollywood Encore Producers’ Award, and Hollywood Fringe Festival Scholarship.

MADE IN AMERICA became OFF BROADWAY show at SoHo Playhouse in New York City. MADE IN AMERICA received Critic’s Choice ENCORE at the Cincinnati Fringe Festival as well.

The Trial: An Improvised Court Experience

Tuesday, May 12, 2026 @ 9:30 PM NY - 14TH ST. Mainstage

The Trial: An Improvised Court Experience

 

Finally, an improv show that will make you want to go to jury duty! “The Trial” improvises a tense courtroom trial right before your eyes — from dramatic opening statements to thrilling verdict.

The first half of the show is a “Spokane” form in the courtroom; we flash back-and-forth in time from the courtroom to the events pertinent to the trial, all the while “seeing” testimony, witnesses, and evidence along the way.

The second half of the show is a Monoscene taking place during deliberations as a jury decides the fate of the accused.

Can you handle the truth???

Wake Up Becky

Wednesday, May 13, 2026 @ 7:00 PM LA - FRANKLIN
Wake Up Becky is fearless stand-up on race, culture, and chaos—followed by a raw comic roundtable. Big laughs, bold takes, no filters.

Wake Up Becky is a bold stand-up comedy show that tackles race, culture, and the absurdities of modern life with no filters and big laughs. The show brings together comedians from a wide range of backgrounds to share fearless material that pushes conversations forward through humor.

About the Upright Citizens Brigade

Home for alternative comedy since 1999

The Upright Citizens Brigade is home for all things comedy. With shows seven nights a week, UCB has become the home for alternative comedy — improv, sketch, stand-up, and yes, all the wonderfully weird shit in between.

UCB also runs a full Training Center with classes in Improv, Sketch Comedy, TV Writing, Characters, and more. Led by some of the best comedic talents working in entertainment today, UCB’s curriculum gives students the tools and stage experience they need to take their comedy to the next level.

Founded in 1999 in New York City by Matt Besser, Amy Poehler, Ian Roberts, and Matt Walsh, UCB has since expanded to Los Angeles and now offers live comedy every night of the week — from up-and-coming performers to established acts and everything in between.
