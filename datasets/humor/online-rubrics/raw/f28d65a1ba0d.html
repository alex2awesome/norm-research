Comics - Online Comic Repositories

  • Underground and Independent Comics     This link opens in a new window
    • This link opens in a new window
    • This link opens in a new window

    Thousands of original underground comics as well as articles, interviews and reviews from The Comics Journal.

  • Comic Books & Graphic Novels Collection at The Internet Archive This link opens in a new window
    This large database provides access to manga, comic books and books on technique. The non-profit organization Internet Archive is dedicated to preserving valuable texts, files, and websites for future research.
  • Andy's Early Comics Index This link opens in a new window
    Survey focusing on pre-20th century comics.
  • Comic Book Plus This link opens in a new window
    Extensive database of Gold and Silver Age comics. Also includes comic strips, pulp fiction, fanzines, and related works.
  • Digital Comic Museum This link opens in a new window
    Digital scans of thousands of public domain comics ranging from the birth of comics until the 70s.
  • The Pulp Magazines Project This link opens in a new window
    An open-access archive and digital research initiative for the study of pulp magazines. Contains high-quality, cover-to-cover digital facsimiles.
  • The Pulp Magazine Archive This link opens in a new window
    Large repository of pulp magazines maintained by The Internet Archive.

Comics - Web Comics

  • Library of Congress Webcomics Web Archive This link opens in a new window
    The Library of Congress maintains an extensive archive of Webcomics. The archive is searchable by contributor, subject, and location.
