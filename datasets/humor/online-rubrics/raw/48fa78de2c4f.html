Greg Dean, The Professor of Stand Up Comedy

He’s been teaching stand up comedy longer than anyone on the planet.
;

He's a Trailblazer in ComedyEducation and Performance Worldwide.

Greg Dean revolutionized how joke writing, comedic storytelling, & performance skills are taught.

Since launching his comedy career in 1973 and beginning his teaching journey in 1982, Greg has developed three proprietary joke writing systems and coined the term “Joke Structure,” now foundational in comedy writing techniques.  

His bestselling book, Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy, is considered the definitive guide to comedy fundamentals. Translated into multiple languages—including Chinese, Indonesian, and Spanish—it’s used by comedians, instructors, and universities across the globe.

A Legacy Etched in Laughter

Greg’s name is proudly displayed on the wall at The Comedy Store in Hollywood, where he was a paid regular for many years. This rare honor isn’t just a nod to his talent—it’s a testament to his enduring influence on the stand-up scene and his deep roots in the comedy community.

Greg’s years of performing alongside legends gave him firsthand insight into what works on stage—and that experience fuels his teaching. Greg’s joke writing system and performance techniques aren’t just theoretical—they’re battle-tested in one of the most iconic comedy venues in the world.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is Greg Dean?

Greg Dean, The Professor of Stand-Up Comedy, is a legendary performer and educator who created the first structured curriculum for stand-up comedy and developed groundbreaking joke writing systems, which have resulted in thousands of comedians—from beginners to stars like Whoopi Goldberg and Anthony Jeselnik—building routines that consistently get laughs. His signature methods, including the Joke Prospector System and the Two-List Technique, have transformed how humor is taught, making joke writing accessible, repeatable, and rooted in craft rather than guesswork.

 

Where can I take Greg Dean’s classes?

Classes are available live in Santa Monica at the Santa Monica Playhouse and live over Zoom, with options for comedians of all levels including beginning comedians.

What do students say about Greg Dean classes?

Reviews highlight the clarity, depth, and effectiveness of Greg’s teaching. Students appreciate the structured approach, supportive feedback, and real-world results.

Who has Greg Dean trained?

To name a few you’ll likely recognize:  Whoopi Goldberg, Anthony Jeselnik, Sherri Shepherd, Christina P. (Pazsitsky), Loni Love, and hundreds of working comedians.
