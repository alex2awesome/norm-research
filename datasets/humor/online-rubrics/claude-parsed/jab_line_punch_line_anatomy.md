# Punch Line / Jab Line Anatomy

## The Punch Line
Climactic conclusion of a story or joke. Short line that gives a humorous reveal. The pivot on which the joke text turns — signals shift between semantic scripts needed to interpret the joke.

## Joke Parts
- **Setup**: creates expectation in listener's mind.
- **Punchline**: breaks the expectation with surprise.
- **Tag**: optional follow-up that extends the punch.

## Jab Line (vs. Punch Line)
- A funny line during the body of a story.
- Functionally identical to a punch line BUT positioned anywhere in text.
- Builds humorous tension.
- Sets comic pace and rhythm.
- Multiple jabs can precede the final punch.

## Greg Dean Joke Structure (5 Mechanisms)
1. **1st Story** — the obvious / expected interpretation set up.
2. **Target Assumption** — what audience assumes about the connector.
3. **Connector** — the magical element with two interpretations.
4. **Reinterpretation** — the alternative meaning revealed by punch.
5. **2nd Story** — the new story implied by the punch.

## Mike Lukas: Premise / Setup / Punchline
- **Premise**: the broad topic / observation underlying the joke.
- **Setup**: specific stage-setting that primes expectation.
- **Punchline**: the surprise that breaks the setup.

## Why the Punch Goes at the End
- Audience laughs after the surprise.
- Talking past the laugh wastes laughs.
- The punch is a "stopping point" structurally.

## Rubric
1. Is there a clear setup (expectation building)?
2. Is there a clear punch (expectation breaking)?
3. Is the punch at the end?
4. If there are jab lines, do they pace the journey?
5. Could you state the joke as setup → connector → punch?
