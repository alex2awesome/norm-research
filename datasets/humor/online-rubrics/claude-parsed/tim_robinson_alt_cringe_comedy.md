# Tim Robinson — Alt / Cringe Comedy

## Style Identification
"Anti-comedy" / alt-comedy royalty. ITYSL has had the most profound impact on online humor in the last 5 years.

## Sketch Engine
Each sketch revolves around:
- **A character who is gloriously, spectacularly wrong**.
- **Refuses to budge**, lest they be humiliated by admitting wrong.
- **Doubles down** rather than relenting.

## Cringe Mechanics
- Mundane everyday scenarios.
- Hyper-specific writing.
- Character spirals into chaos.
- Refuses to admit they're wrong.
- Desperately maintains dignity.
- Audience cringes harder with each beat.

## Why It Works
The humor comes from:
- The character's unrelenting commitment to proving they're in control.
- It's clear they aren't.
- Audience watches the disaster compound.
- We've all been THAT person (recognition).

## Tonal Profile
- Awkwardness as engine.
- Surreal flourishes.
- "Comedy of autism" / neurodivergent feel.
- Tonal commitment without winking.

## Robinson's Methodology
- Hyper-specific scenarios.
- Maximum commitment to character.
- No exit ramp from cringe.
- Often violent / loud emotional outbursts.

## Famous Sketches
- "Driving Crooner" — escalation to the absurd.
- "Hot Dog Suit" — refuses to acknowledge.
- "Baby of the Year" — adult earnestly competing.
- "Honk if You're Horny" — bumper sticker spiral.

## Rubric (Robinson Style)
1. Is there a character who's clearly wrong?
2. Do they refuse to admit it?
3. Does the situation spiral via doubling-down?
4. Is the writing hyper-specific?
5. Does the audience cringe with recognition?
