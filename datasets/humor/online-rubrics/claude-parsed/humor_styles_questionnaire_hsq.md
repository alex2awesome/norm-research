# Humor Styles Questionnaire (HSQ)

By Martin et al. — most widely used self-report scale in humor research.

## Two Positive and Two Negative Dimensions

### Positive (Adaptive) Styles

#### 1. Affiliative Humor
- Sharing humor to amuse others.
- Strengthen relationships.
- Telling jokes / funny stories that bring people together.
- Self-tolerant.

#### 2. Self-Enhancing Humor
- Maintaining a humorous outlook on life.
- Cope with stress through perspective.
- Find humor in adversity.
- Internal coping mechanism.

### Negative (Maladaptive) Styles

#### 3. Aggressive Humor
- Sarcasm, teasing, ridicule, derision.
- Used to manipulate or put others down.
- Sexist or racist jokes fall here.
- Short-term pleasure at others' expense.

#### 4. Self-Defeating Humor
- Excessively self-disparaging.
- Trying to amuse others by putting oneself down.
- Hiding underlying negative feelings.
- May relate to depression.

## What This Means for Joke Quality
- Different jokes serve different functions.
- Same joke could be affiliative or aggressive depending on context/audience.
- Quality assessment should consider style intent.
- Aggressive humor wins laughs but harms relationships.

## Application to Reddit / Caption Contest
- r/Jokes: heavy on affiliative, some aggressive (often punching down).
- New Yorker: affiliative, self-enhancing, observational.
- Both serve in-group cohesion.

## Rubric
1. Which humor style does this joke exemplify?
2. Is the style appropriate for the audience?
3. Does it strengthen or harm relationships?
4. Is the comedian using it adaptively or maladaptively?
5. Does the joke serve a coping or attacking function?
