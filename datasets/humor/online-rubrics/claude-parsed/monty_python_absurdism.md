# Absurdist Comedy (Monty Python Lens)

## Core Principle
The main logic behind every absurdist piece is the lack of logic contained within. Funny comes from violation of conventional logic and expectation while maintaining internal consistency.

## Key Techniques

### Intellectual Juxtaposition
- High-brow references (literature, history, philosophy, art) in ridiculous contexts.
- Argument clinic about Wittgenstein with bureaucratic forms.
- Dead Parrot sketch — pet shop dispute escalates into philosophical wrangling.

### Subverting Expectations
- Establish familiar scenarios.
- Introduce completely unexpected elements.
- Derail in surprising directions.
- Spanish Inquisition appears unexpectedly.

### Avoiding Conventional Punchlines
- Sketches end by abandoning structure.
- "And now for something completely different."
- Often shifts to animation or non-sequitur.

### Meta-Awareness
- Acknowledge artificial nature of the medium.
- Break character.
- Address audience directly.
- Transition between sketches in meta-aware ways.
- Sketch about fish → animation of flying fish → fishmonger sketch.

### Logical Absurdity
- Comedy comes from a logical place WITHIN the absurd context.
- Not random nonsense.
- The Black Knight insists "tis but a scratch" while losing limbs.

## What Distinguishes Quality Absurdism
- Internal consistency despite external absurdity.
- Commitment to the bit beyond reason.
- Specificity (not generic weirdness).
- Cultural literacy as setup for parody.

## Rubric
1. Is the absurdity rule-governed (internally consistent)?
2. Is there cultural/intellectual content being parodied?
3. Are expectations clearly set then subverted?
4. Is there meta-awareness without becoming pretentious?
5. Does the absurd commit through to the end?
