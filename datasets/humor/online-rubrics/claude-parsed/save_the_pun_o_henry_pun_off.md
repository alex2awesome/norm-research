# Pun Competitions

## International Save the Pun Foundation
- Founded 1978 by John S. Crosbie.
- Aimed to preserve the medium of humor "that guarantees a reaction—be it a groan or a laugh."
- Annual "best of" pun lists.
- Monthly newsletter "The Pundit."
- Crosbie passed away 1994 at age 73.

## O. Henry Pun-Off World Championships (Austin, TX)
- Annual event hosted by P.U.N.Y. (Punsters United Nearly Yearly).
- Two main competitions:

### Punniest of Show (prepared)
- 90-second prepared performance.
- Density of puns matters.
- Puns should be clever, not strained.
- Judging: groans, laughs, audience response.

### Punslingers (head-to-head)
- Topic announced.
- Two competitors trade puns on the topic.
- 5 seconds to respond.
- Judge declares winner of each round.
- Single-elimination tournament.

## Judging Criteria (Pun-Off)
1. **Topical relevance** — does the pun relate to the assigned topic?
2. **Wordplay precision** — clean homophone/homograph?
3. **Originality** — not a hoary old chestnut?
4. **Audience reaction** — groans count!
5. **Speed of delivery** — within time limit?
6. **Cleverness vs. strain** — earned, not forced?

## Pun Quality Heuristics
- Best puns activate two valid readings simultaneously.
- Best puns illuminate something true.
- Mediocre puns merely match sounds.
- Bad puns require explanation.

## Rubric
1. Are both meanings active in context?
2. Is the substitution earned (sound + sense)?
3. Is it topical?
4. Is it original (not heard a million times)?
5. Does the audience react (groan or laugh)?
