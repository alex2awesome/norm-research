# Premise / Topic / Angle / Punch

## The Universal Joke Formula
**Premise + Point of View + Twist = Joke**

## Definitions

### Premise
The topic or what your joke is **about**. Found by asking yourself what is **weird, crazy, difficult, or stupid** about your topic.

### Topic
The subject matter — concrete object, experience, person, situation.

### Condition
A premise = topic + condition. A condition could be a setting, situation, or POV.

### Angle
The connective tissue between Topic and Punch Line. Smooth and efficient.

### Punch Line
The twist that breaks the assumption built up in setup.

## Topic Sentence Formulas

### "It was SO X that __"
- "My apartment was so quiet that I thought the flies were studying."
- Frame: superlative sets up extreme; punch lists the absurd consequence.

### "But Statements"
- Start with the normal assumption.
- Add "but."
- End with the inappropriate / weird / broken assumption.
- Example: "I love my job, but I'd love it more if I weren't there."

### "Have you ever noticed…?"
- Observational opener.
- Followed by a specific, oddly truthful detail.

### "What's the deal with…?"
- Seinfeld-style opener.
- Frames a target for examination.

## Joke Has Beginning, Middle, End
Like an essay:
- Beginning: setup / premise.
- Middle: angle / development.
- End: punch / twist.

## Rubric
1. Is the premise clear (topic + weird/crazy/etc.)?
2. Is there a specific point of view?
3. Is there a clean angle linking topic to punch?
4. Does the punch break a built assumption?
5. Could you state the joke in setup-angle-punch form?
