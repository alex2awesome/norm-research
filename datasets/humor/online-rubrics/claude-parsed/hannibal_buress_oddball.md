# Hannibal Buress — Comedy Strategy

## Style Description
- Laid-back, almost sleepy delivery disguising sharp material.
- Not quite observational, not quite absurdist — somewhere between.
- Notices things nobody else does, then describes them so audiences wonder how they missed it.
- Apple juice, dentists, escalators — masterclasses in finding comedy in the unremarkable.

## Storytelling-Witness Stance
- Primarily storytelling jokes (narrative).
- "More of a witness than a critic."
- Doesn't depict a larger social world directly.
- Fixates on how the world intersects his own life.

## Approach to Material
- Identifies incongruences in:
  - Daily life
  - American society
  - Pop culture
  - Hypothetical scenarios of his own design
- Establishes moralistic high ground in narrative.
- Dismantles potential opposition through ironic wisecracks.
- Dismisses counterarguments preemptively.

## Why It Works
- Sleepy delivery + sharp content = surprise via mismatch.
- Specificity of mundane observations.
- Story format creates investment.
- Wisecrack at the end punches above its delivery.

## Rubric
1. Is the delivery understated relative to the content?
2. Is the observation about the unremarkable?
3. Does the narrative establish a sympathetic POV?
4. Does the punchline punch harder than the delivery suggests?
5. Are counterarguments dismissed economically?
