# Stewart Lee — Meta-Comedy

## Core Approach
Deconstruction of comedy itself. Steps outside material to analyze audience reactions, undermines own punchlines, creates layered self-referential performance.

## Key Techniques

### Meta-Humor
- Openly describes structure and intent of the set while onstage.
- Abolishes illusion of routines as spontaneous.
- "I'm doing this kind of joke now" framing.

### Punchline Subversion
- Deliberately subverts conventional setup-payoff.
- Undermines own setups.
- Extends routines well beyond conventional limits.
- Forces audiences to reconsider expectations.

### Telegraph the Joke
- Makes the punchline explicit BEFORE delivery.
- Telegraphs it long ahead.
- Defeats the convention of comedy as shock/surprise.
- Refocuses attention on the journey, not the destination.

### Explain the Joke at Length
- Spends 20+ minutes explaining a joke.
- Why it is funny, how it works, what it means.
- The explanation itself becomes funnier than the joke.

## Brechtian Influence
- "Alienation effect" (Verfremdungseffekt) applied to comedy.
- Disrupts familiar rhythms of stand-up.
- Self-reflexivity, slow-paced delivery.
- Political commentary embedded.
- Tackles uncomfortable subjects without softening.

## Why It Works
- Sophisticated audience rewards layered work.
- The frustration becomes comic.
- Repetition past comfort = absurd insight.
- Meta-laughter at the form.

## Rubric (Stewart Lee Style)
1. Does the comedian comment on their own technique?
2. Are conventions deliberately subverted (telegraphed punches, repetition)?
3. Does the comedian extend bits past comfort?
4. Is there an "alienation" effect (audience pushed out of immersion)?
5. Does the meta-content itself become the source of humor?
