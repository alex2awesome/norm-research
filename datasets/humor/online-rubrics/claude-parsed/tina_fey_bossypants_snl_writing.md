# Tina Fey — Bossypants & SNL Lessons

## SNL Writers Room Culture
- Highly competitive, "rough" rewrite tables.
- Sketches torn apart sketch-by-sketch by colleagues.
- High joke quota and rapid iteration.
- Anonymous-feeling pitch process.

## Lessons from Bossypants on Comedy

### "The Rules of Improvisation Will Change Your Life"
1. **Agree** — say YES to everything.
2. **Yes, AND** — add to it.
3. **Make statements** — don't ask questions; make declarative additions.
4. **There are no mistakes, only opportunities** — turn errors into choices.

### Writing Principles
- Specificity always.
- Voice (point of view) > generic jokes.
- "If you want to make an audience laugh, you dress a man up like an old lady and push her down the stairs. If you want to make comedy writers laugh, you push an actual old lady down the stairs."
- Self-deprecating humor that punches up, not down.
- Embrace the imperfect "first draft" then ruthlessly cut.

### On Stage Presence
- Don't tell the audience to laugh.
- Commit to the choice — even if uncertain.
- The crazier the choice, the harder the commitment must be.

## Rubric (Fey-Style)
1. Does each beat agree-and-add to the previous?
2. Is the comedian making declarative statements?
3. Are mistakes converted into choices?
4. Is the joke specific (not generic)?
5. Does it have point of view?
