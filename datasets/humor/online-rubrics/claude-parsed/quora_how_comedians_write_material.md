# Quora: How Do Comedians Write Their Material

## The Working-Comic Process (synthesis of top answers from professional comics)

### Phase 1: Capture
- Notebook (or phone) ALWAYS on you.
- Capture the moment something annoys, confuses, or amuses you — without trying to make it funny yet.
- Capture the EMOTION first, not the joke.

### Phase 2: Premise
- Once a week, review captures.
- For each, write down: "What I really mean is..."
- That sentence is your premise.

### Phase 3: Drafting
- Start with the premise as the FIRST line. (You'll cut it later, but it focuses the writing.)
- Write five different punchlines for the same premise. Pick the funniest two.
- Combine the second-funniest as a tag.

### Phase 4: Stage
- Try the joke at an open mic exactly as written.
- Note what the audience laughed at — sometimes a throwaway line gets the laugh, not the punch.
- Rewrite to feature whatever actually worked.

### Phase 5: Polish
- After 5+ stage attempts, settle the wording.
- Build it into your set order — usually pair with thematically adjacent material.

## What Pros Say Distinguishes Their Process
- They edit on stage, not at the desk.
- They throw away 90% of what they write.
- They don't fall in love with a line; if it dies three times, it's dead.
- They listen to recordings of every set.
- They share material with one or two trusted comics for honest feedback.

## Quora's Rubric for "Is This Joke Worth Keeping?"
1. Does it come from a real moment you captured?
2. Does it have a clear premise underneath?
3. Have you tried at least three punchline variations?
4. Has it landed in 5+ rooms?
5. Are you still interested in saying it?
6. Does it fit your overall POV / persona?
