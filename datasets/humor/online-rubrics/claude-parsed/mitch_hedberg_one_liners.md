# Mitch Hedberg's One-Liner Technique

## Core Approach
No long narratives, no dramatic callbacks. Walked on stage, dropped perfectly engineered one-liners as if casual observations.

## Key Techniques

### Unexpected Pivots / Misdirection
"I haven't slept for ten days … because that would be too long."
- Setup makes you expect exhaustion humor.
- Instead delivers literal accuracy.
- He always zigged where other comics zagged.

### Permanent Mental Shifts (Reframings)
"An escalator can never break. It can only become stairs."
- Permanently adjusts your brain with one sentence.
- Reframing object's identity.
- Audience can't unsee.

### Absurd Reversal of Definitions
"My fake plants died because I did not pretend to water them."
- Take a category and apply rules from a different category.

### Surreal Observation
"I think Bigfoot is blurry, that's the problem. There's a large, out-of-focus monster roaming the countryside."
- Treat metaphor literally.
- Confuse representation and referent.

### Distinctive Delivery
- Deadpan expression.
- Sunglasses on stage (hide eyes).
- Random emphasis as if English is second language.
- Sentences are "adventures."

## Rubric for a Hedberg-Style One-Liner
1. Is the setup misdirecting toward an obvious read?
2. Does the punch take a surprising literal/categorical pivot?
3. Will the audience never unsee the reframing afterward?
4. Is the joke compressed to one or two sentences?
5. Could it be delivered deadpan with no setup baggage?
