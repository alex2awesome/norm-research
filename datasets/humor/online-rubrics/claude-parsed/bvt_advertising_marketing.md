# BVT in Advertising — When Humor Helps vs. Hurts

## Marketing Findings
- BVT trained students wrote funnier ad headlines than incongruity-trained or superiority-trained students.
- Brand-to-brand teasing: aggressive humor is benign when targeting competitors (most people accept mockery of rivals).

## When Humor in Ads Helps
- Familiar product, low-stakes use case.
- Audience already favorably disposed.
- Clear violation that's clearly benign.

## When Humor Hurts
Avoid:
- **Highly inappropriate humor** (aversive violations).
- Humor with a **specific target/butt** (alienates that audience).
- Humor that prompts **avoidance** (disgust + laughter).
- Humor that distracts from product info.

## Aggressive vs. Low-Aggressive Humor (Brand-to-Brand)
- **Low-aggressive humor**: promotes consumer engagement more.
- **High-aggressive humor**: backfires beyond a threshold.
- Benign appraisal mediates the relationship.

## Implications
- Funniness ≠ persuasion.
- A funny ad can fail (audience laughs, doesn't buy).
- Distance from violation matters: too aggressive = alienate target's affiliates.

## Rubric (Marketing Humor)
1. Is the violation clearly benign for the target audience?
2. Is the butt of the joke distant from the audience?
3. Does humor distract from or enhance the product message?
4. Is aggression dialed appropriately (low > high)?
5. Could a reasonable consumer be offended?
