# Cognitive Reframing in Humor

## Definition
Reframing means to change the meaning of something by putting it in a different setting, context, or frame.

## Reframing in Jokes
- "Reframing often appears in jokes, where what seems to be one thing shifts and becomes something else."
- Punchline = forced reframing of setup material.
- Audience reinterprets what they just heard.

## Why It Works
- The meaning of any event depends on how we frame it.
- Change frame → change meaning → change response.
- Funny = the new frame is unexpected and illuminating.

## Reframing Categories

### Content Reframing
- Same situation, new meaning.
- "I'm not lazy, I'm energy-efficient."

### Context Reframing
- Behavior is bad in context A, good in context B.
- "Stubborn at home; persistent at work."

### Time Reframing
- "Comedy is tragedy plus time."
- Past distress reframed as present comedy.

### Scale Reframing
- Make small things big or big things small.
- (Hyperbole and understatement as reframings.)

### Identity Reframing
- "I'm not a hoarder, I'm a curator."

## Humor as Cognitive Tool
- Humor based on positivity is variant of reframing.
- Shifts perspective of unfavorable event toward more positive outlook.
- Therapeutic function.

## Rubric (Reframing Quality)
1. Does the punch impose a new frame on setup material?
2. Is the new frame illuminating (not arbitrary)?
3. Does it change the emotional valence?
4. Does the reframing reveal a truth?
5. Is the new frame recognizably new (surprising)?
