# Comedy of Errors / Mistaken Identity

## Mechanism
Mistaken identity creates comedy by:
- Audience knows truth; characters don't.
- Dramatic irony saturates every interaction.
- Each character's actions, while internally rational, produce escalating chaos.
- Each new misunderstanding compounds the previous.

## Distinguishing Feature
Unlike comedies of disguise (where someone deliberately deceives), Comedy of Errors humor occurs when **nobody pretends to be anyone else, yet confusion still ensues**. Identity confusion is structural, not deceptive.

## Practical Engines

### Twin Confusion
- Identical twins separated and reunited (Antipholi, Dromios).
- Each interaction with a twin produces wrong outcomes.
- One twin gets beaten; other gets affection meant for him.

### Double Meanings
- Statements interpreted by hearer in wrong context.
- Speakers don't know they're being misread.

### Cascading Misunderstanding
- One mistake → more mistakes.
- Snowball effect.
- Eventually only revelation can stop.

## Audience Position
- Audience kept slightly ahead of characters.
- Knows the secret.
- Watches characters bumble.
- Anticipates collisions.

## Modern Examples
- *Frasier* (mistaken identity beats).
- *Three's Company*.
- *A Comedy of Errors*-derived films.
- *The Importance of Being Earnest* (Earnest = Ernest).

## Rubric (Mistaken Identity Comedy)
1. Is there structural identity confusion?
2. Does the audience know more than the characters?
3. Do mistakes compound through the work?
4. Is each new beat an escalation of confusion?
5. Does the eventual revelation pay off the buildup?
