# Greg Dean's Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy — Summary

## The Joke Prospector System

Greg Dean developed an original joke writing system called the **Joke Prospector**. His revolutionary approach to joke structure features his **5 Mechanisms**, which reveals exactly how jokes work and why audiences laugh.

## Core Joke Structure

Dean's joke structure diagram explains how jokes are built around two interpretations of one magical element called the **connector**:
- The **expected interpretation** of the connector (Target Assumption)
- The **unexpected reinterpretation** of the connector

The setup leads the audience to make a Target Assumption. The punch line forces a Reinterpretation that reveals a different, simultaneous meaning of the connector.

## Components
- **Setup**: leads audience to a target assumption
- **Connector**: the magical element with two valid interpretations
- **Target Assumption**: the obvious/expected interpretation
- **Reinterpretation**: the surprising/second meaning revealed by punchline
- **Punch**: the line that forces the reinterpretation

## Act Outs and POVs
Dean teaches "act outs" — quick scenes comics act out during a routine — by explaining points of view (POVs):
- The **three POVs** inside a scene are the fundamental tools for understanding why act outs are so funny.
- POV shifting via physical stage movement and facial direction changes.

## Coverage
The book also addresses joke topics, routine construction, rehearsal techniques, microphone handling, timing, heckler management, and material refinement.

## Rubric for Evaluating a Joke (Dean Method)
1. Is there a clear setup that establishes a target assumption?
2. Is there a connector — an element with two valid interpretations?
3. Does the punchline force a reinterpretation of the connector?
4. Is the reinterpretation surprising yet logical in hindsight?
5. Are POVs (act outs) used effectively if performance is involved?
