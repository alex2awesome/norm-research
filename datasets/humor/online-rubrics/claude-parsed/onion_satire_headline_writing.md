# The Onion — Satire Headline Process

## Headline-First Process
Stories begin as headlines. Once a headline is signed off, writers craft a story to match. (Opposite of real newsrooms.)

## Volume Funnel
- 500 headlines pitched every week.
- ~12 selected for publication.
- 60–70% killed in an hour during pre-meeting.
- Each writer required to bring ~15 headlines.

## Brainstorming Structure
- Everybody apart first — individual ideation in their own space.
- Short pre-meeting separates "gems from garbage."
- Main session refines surviving headlines.

## The Satire Formula

### Voice and Style
- Matter-of-fact AP-news style.
- Mimic real journalism conventions exactly.
- The earnest, deadpan tone IS the joke.

### "Saying Exactly What the Person Is Thinking"
- Write in character.
- Force them to write completely honestly.
- The unspoken truth becomes the punchline.

### Word-Substitution Strategy
- Take serious news framing.
- Change a key word or two.
- Examples: "Nation's Senators Carefully Avoid …" / "Area Man Realizes…"

## Common Onion Templates
- "Area Man / Area Woman" — universalized through specific everyman.
- "Nation Reflects on…" — collective inner monologue.
- "Sources Confirm…" — fake news convention.
- "Study Finds…" — fake research.
- "Local…" — hyperlocal absurdity.

## Rubric for an Onion-Style Headline
1. Does it sound like a real news headline (AP style)?
2. Does it reveal an uncomfortable truth nobody says aloud?
3. Is there a specific subject (Area Man, Local Mom)?
4. Is it deadpan-funny without overt jokes?
5. Could the body of the article extend the premise plausibly?
