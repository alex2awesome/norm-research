# McSweeney's Internet Tendency Guidelines

## Title Quality
Pieces should have titles that quickly lure readers in. The editor will be drawn to submissions with funny, eye-catching titles that cleverly reveal the premises of pieces.

## Length
- Pieces under 1,000 words.
- Absolute maximum 1,200 words.

## Acceptable Formats
- Lists
- Open letters to people who are unlikely to respond
- Monologues
- Food reviews
- Short satire / conceptual humor pieces

## What NOT to Submit
- Lists of goofy email names from spam or funny texts from drunk friends
- Personal essays
- Submissions formatted like news articles ("there is a better website that already does this" — i.e., The Onion)
- "Fake news" format pieces

## Voice
- McSweeney's has a specific voice different from other places.
- Read the site regularly to understand it.
- Tends toward: literary, conceptual, character-driven, often academic-tinged absurdity.

## Premise
- The title should reveal/imply the premise.
- The whole piece should sustain a single conceit.
- The conceit should be specific and tightly constructed.

## Rubric for a McSweeney's-Style Piece
1. Is there a single, clear conceit revealed by the title?
2. Is the piece short (under 1,000 words)?
3. Does it sustain the conceit without padding?
4. Is the voice literary/conceptual rather than gag-driven?
5. Does it avoid being a personal essay or fake news?
