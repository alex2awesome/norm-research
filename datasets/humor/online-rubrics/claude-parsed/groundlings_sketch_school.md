# The Groundlings

Formed 1974 by Gary Austin, Los Angeles. Spolin/Close lineage.

## Core Technique Foundations

### Story-Telling Through Connection
- Performers must connect to scene partners and audience.
- Listening to understand (not to respond).
- Authentic emotion drives connection.

### Character Point of View
- Each character has a clear POV.
- POV is consistent across scene.
- POV is the engine of choices.

### Emotional Adjustment
- Characters react with full emotional range.
- Adjustments to new info are big.
- Real feelings (heightened).

### Instant, Enthusiastic Agreement
- Yes-and with energy.
- No tepid acceptance.

### Character Escalation (Signature Technique)
- Performers build scenes through deepening emotional states and physicality.
- Heighten absurdity AND relatability.
- Bigger and bigger reactions, never breaking character.

## Curriculum Concepts
- Difference between **character sketches** vs **premise sketches**.
- Identifying beats.
- Writing for your medium (TV/film/stage).
- How to find material.

## Famous Alumni
Will Ferrell, Kristen Wiig, Maya Rudolph, Phil Hartman, Lisa Kudrow, Melissa McCarthy, Jimmy Fallon's pre-SNL training, etc.

## Rubric
1. Is there a clear character POV?
2. Are emotional adjustments full and big?
3. Is the connection to partners/audience genuine?
4. Does the scene escalate (heightening)?
5. Is it character-driven OR premise-driven (clearly one)?
