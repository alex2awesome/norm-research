# Clean Comedy

## Definition
A comedy genre generally free of:
- Explicitly sexual content
- Profanity
- Politically or racially controversial jokes
- Toilet humor
- Similarly objectionable material

## Levels of "Clean"

### Club Clean (PG-13)
- General clean comedy for adult audiences.
- Some adult themes acceptable.
- No graphic sexual references.

### TV Clean (Edited PG-13)
- Sex jokes possible: "we were having sex" OK.
- But no specific imagery (genitalia, fluids, positions).
- No profanity.

### Church Clean (PG)
- Stricter — not all PG-13 topics acceptable.
- Religious sensitivities.

### Squeaky Clean (G)
- Disney / Nickelodeon level.
- No edge whatsoever.
- Family jokes, animal jokes, pun comedy.

## How Clean Comedy Works
- Wit, clever observations.
- Universal themes.
- Shared experiences (parenting, work, relationships).
- Wordplay.
- Self-deprecation.
- Visual / situational comedy.

## Why It's Hard
- Many comedy "shortcuts" off-limits.
- Forces deeper craft.
- Must rely on insight, structure.
- Requires more material refinement.

## Rubric (Clean Comedy)
1. Is profanity absent?
2. Is the topic family-appropriate at the target level?
3. Does the joke rely on wit/structure rather than shock?
4. Could the joke land at the target audience's "clean" level?
5. Is the comedian's persona consistent with clean register?
