# Weird Al Yankovic — Parody Song Writing

## Core Rule
**Parody must be funny whether or not the listener is familiar with the source material.**

## Tone
- Less biting and derogatory.
- "More of a poke in the ribs than a punch in the face."
- Affectionate, not contemptuous.

## Method

### 1. Song Selection
- Study the charts.
- Focus on songs with the biggest hits / most mainstream appeal.
- Cultural saturation = parody-ready.

### 2. Source Study
- In-depth study of the original.
- Meticulously analyze structure, rhythms, nuances.

### 3. Musical Recreation
- Keep backing music identical to original.
- Transcribe by ear.
- Re-record from scratch (band re-performs).

### 4. Concept Selection
- Pick a concept that **maintains humor through the entire song**.
- Don't rely on a single joke.
- "It has to feel like it could exist in that world."

### 5. Lyric Construction
- Match syllable counts and stresses precisely.
- Sound substitutions (phonological play).
- Each verse develops the comic concept.
- Chorus repays the joke.

## Two Parody Types
- **Direct parody**: same melody, new lyrics about new subject.
- **Style parody**: original song in the style of another artist.

## Why Weird Al Lasts
- Sustained concept, not single joke.
- Faithful musical reproduction.
- Affectionate tone.
- Specificity — pop culture details.
- Re-listenable songs.

## Rubric
1. Is the source song instantly recognizable?
2. Does the parody work even without source familiarity?
3. Does the concept sustain the full song length?
4. Are syllables matched precisely?
5. Is the tone affectionate (not contemptuous)?
