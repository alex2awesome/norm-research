# Brian Regan — Clean Anecdotal Comedy

## Style Identifier
School of "befuddled anecdotal narrators."

## Material Topics
- Everyday events:
  - Shipping a package with UPS
  - Mortgages
  - Optometrist visits
  - Doughnut decisions
  - Trampoline
- Mundane recognition humor.

## Three Stylistic Pillars
1. **Observational** — sees the absurd in the ordinary.
2. **Sarcastic** — but gentle, not biting.
3. **Self-deprecating** — he's the bumbling narrator.

## Performance Style
- Body language as engine.
- Facial expressions as punctuation.
- Physical mimicry of his own embarrassment.
- Voice modulation (whining, exasperation).
- Vignette format (matches sketch translation).

## Why It Works for Mass Audience
- Universal topics.
- No politics.
- No profanity.
- Recognition pleasure.
- Physicality crosses cultural lines.

## Material Construction
- Story-shaped jokes.
- Setup: he attempts ordinary task.
- Complication: ordinary task confounds him.
- Punchline: his bewilderment voiced.
- Tag: another absurd realization.

## Rubric (Brian Regan / Clean Anecdotal)
1. Is the topic universal and mundane?
2. Is the comedian the befuddled narrator (self-deprecating)?
3. Does physicality augment the verbal?
4. Is the language clean (no profanity)?
5. Does it land via recognition (not provocation)?
