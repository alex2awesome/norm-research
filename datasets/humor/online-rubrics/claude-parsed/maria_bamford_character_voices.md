# Maria Bamford — Character Voices

## Style
Surreal comedy with voice impressions of various character types. Technically skilled — instant transformation between characters.

## Voice Repertoire
Her recurring characters include:
- Passive-aggressive Midwestern matriarch with backhanded compliments.
- High-powered, over-caffeinated businesswoman with no self-awareness.
- Impossibly high, anxious voice during stress.
- The voice of her darkest thoughts.

## Technique
- Effortless shifts between voices.
- Never gives audience time to anticipate punchline.
- The shifts ARE the comedy.
- Each character is a complete persona, not a caricature.

## Mental Health Integration
- Central focus of her work.
- Open about OCD, bipolar II, suicidal ideation.
- Ground-breaking comedy + destigmatization.
- Transforms experiences with psychiatric hospitals, medication, therapy into hilarious-and-profound material.

## Vignette Form
- Often vignettes rather than setup-punchline.
- Each vignette is a complete world.
- Surreal narrative beats.

## Why It Works
- Vulnerability earns audience trust.
- Voice work shows craft / dedication.
- Specificity of mental illness experience.
- Cultural taboo handled with grace.

## Rubric (Bamford-Style Character Comedy)
1. Are character voices distinct and committed?
2. Do voice shifts function as comic beats?
3. Is vulnerability genuinely present?
4. Do taboo topics treated with grace?
5. Does the form (vignette vs. joke) suit the content?
