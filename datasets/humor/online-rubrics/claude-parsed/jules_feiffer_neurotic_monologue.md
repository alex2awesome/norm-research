# Jules Feiffer — Neurotic Monologue Cartoons

## Signature Form
- Satirical comic strip with very literate captions.
- Verbal elements take form of monologues.
- Speaker (sometimes pathetic, sometimes pompous) exposes their own insecurities.
- Often a single character speaking across panels with shifting emotion.

## Therapeutic Culture in Comics
- Brought awareness of therapy-talk into comics.
- Eschewed gag format of vaudeville tradition.
- Urbane, inward-looking, language-based humor.
- Captured how therapy-speak was remaking everyday speech.

## Visual Approach
- Borderless frames.
- Scratchy lines.
- Stripped comic strips to essence.
- "Jangly looseness of a Rorschach test or psychiatrist's notepad."
- Allowed characters to free-associate to self-discovery.

## Themes
- Relationships
- Sex
- Depression
- Family troubles
- Current events
- Existential angst

## Why It Works
- Audience recognizes their own neurotic monologues.
- Speaker doesn't realize what they're revealing.
- The character is sympathetic AND pathetic.
- Each panel is a beat in their unwinding self-deception.

## Influence
- Foreshadowed therapy-culture comedy on TV.
- Laid groundwork for self-aware sitcom characters.
- Bridge between editorial cartoon and personal comic.

## Rubric (Feiffer Style)
1. Is there a single speaker across panels?
2. Does the monologue reveal more than the speaker realizes?
3. Is the language literary / therapeutic?
4. Are panels minimal (line-art focus on speech)?
5. Does the audience laugh AND identify with the speaker's blindness?
