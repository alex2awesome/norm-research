# XKCD — Randall Munroe's Approach

## Tagline
"A webcomic of romance, sarcasm, math, and language."

## Core Appeal
Focus on science, mathematics, technology, and general geekiness with light, quirky humor and occasional profound philosophizing.

## What Makes It Funny

### Intellectual + Emotional
- Humor both intellectually stimulating and emotionally resonant.
- Doesn't rely on slapstick or topical gags.
- Delves into fundamental principles of science, logic, behavior.

### Precise Language for Absurdity
- Uses scientific language to dissect everyday absurdities.
- Mathematical / programming framings of relationships.
- Unit-conversion jokes, edge cases, hypothetical extremes.

### Stick-Figure Minimalism
- Stick figures = readers project; specific look would distract.
- Clean visuals foreground the joke.

### Alt Text Reward
- Hovering reveals secondary joke / commentary.
- Rewards engagement.
- Layered humor structure.

### Reductio ad Absurdum
- Take a premise to its logical extreme.
- "What If?" answers serious-seeming questions absurdly.

## Recurring Engines
- Misuse of statistics ("Correlation doesn't imply causation, but…").
- Standards proliferation comic ("Now there are 15 standards").
- Map projections.
- Programming jokes (compile errors, pointer humor).
- Romance via spreadsheets.

## Rubric
1. Is there a precise (scientific/logical) framing?
2. Does the precision intersect everyday human experience?
3. Is the visual minimal, foregrounding the joke?
4. Is there a layered punch (alt text, secondary)?
5. Does it reward expertise without excluding novices?
