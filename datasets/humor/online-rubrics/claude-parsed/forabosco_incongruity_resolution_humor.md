# Giovannantonio Forabosco — Incongruity & Resolution

## Core Position
- Perception of incongruity is **necessary but not sufficient** for humor.
- Many incongruities are not funny.
- Resolution (or attempted resolution) of incongruity matters.

## Definition of Incongruity
A stimulus is perceived as incongruous when it diverts from the cognitive model of reference.

## Domains-Interaction Approach
- Two cognitive domains interact in the joke.
- The interaction creates incongruity.
- Resolution attempts a partial reconciliation.

## Key Findings
- Jokes with both incongruity AND resolution rated most humorous.
- Pure incongruity (without resolution) → confusion, not laughter.
- Pure resolution (no incongruity) → no humor.

## Beyond Single Theory
> "It is presumptuous to think about cracking the secret of humor with a unified theory."
- Many aspects, no single explanation.
- Neuroscience now contributes.
- Multiple complementary perspectives needed.

## What Makes a Joke "More Funny" (per Forabosco)
1. Stronger incongruity.
2. Cleverer resolution.
3. Resolution that opens new insight.
4. Resolution that requires modest cognitive work.
5. Resolution that audience can perform quickly.

## Rubric
1. Is there clear incongruity (a divergence from expectation)?
2. Is there a resolution path the audience can find?
3. Is the resolution clever (not obvious)?
4. Does it require modest, satisfying cognitive work?
5. Does the resolution illuminate something new?
