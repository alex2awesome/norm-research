# Mike Birbiglia: Story-Driven Stand-Up (Sleepwalk With Me era)

## The Personal-Story Doctrine
Birbiglia's signature is comedic story (memoir as material). His craft talks emphasize narrative structure as the spine on which jokes hang.

## The Birbiglia Three-Act for a Story Bit
1. **Act 1**: A normal, recognizable setup. We are in your world. (~25%)
2. **Act 2**: A complication that escalates. We are following a trajectory. (~50%)
3. **Act 3**: A resolution that surprises us emotionally AND comically. (~25%)

The jokes inside each act must serve the act's purpose — laughs that break the story are CUT.

## The "What I Should Have Done" Reveal
Birbiglia frequently structures the punchline as: "And I should have realized X. But I was 22, so instead I..." This signals to the audience that the comic understands the absurdity NOW, which gives them permission to laugh AT past-self.

## The Cold Open
- Open with a small, specific image (a brown paper bag, a hospital bracelet).
- The image reappears in act 3 as a callback that closes the story.
- Audiences love the closing of the loop.

## On Honesty as Engine
- "If it's not embarrassing, it's not interesting."
- The story must reveal something the comic would rather hide.
- The audience laughs because they recognize the same shame in themselves.

## Birbiglia Working Rubric
1. Is there a clear three-act spine?
2. Is there an opening image that closes in act 3?
3. Does the comic-now make peace with comic-then?
4. Are all jokes in service of the story (none that break it)?
5. Is the comic willing to be embarrassed?
6. Does the audience leave having laughed AND felt something?
