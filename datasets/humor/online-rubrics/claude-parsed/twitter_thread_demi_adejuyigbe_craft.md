# Demi Adejuyigbe: Craft Threads on Comedy Production Value

Demi Adejuyigbe (Gilmore Guys, The Late Late Show, viral September 21st videos) is known for high-production-value joke videos. His threads on craft emphasize the marginal returns of POLISH.

## On Production Value as Joke Multiplier
- A joke video gets funnier as production gets more inappropriate.
- A musical number with one absurd lyric is funny.
- A musical number with full choreography, costume changes, and orchestration for ONE absurd lyric is much funnier.
- The disproportion between effort and silliness IS the joke.

## On Single-Idea Maximalism
- Pick ONE silly idea.
- Execute it with absolute commitment, technical excellence, and disproportionate resources.
- The joke is the commitment.

## On the Annual Bit
- A repeated annual bit (September 21st) builds expectation.
- Each iteration must escalate the production AND vary the angle.
- The audience is partly laughing at the FACT that you're still doing it.

## On Twitter Bits vs. Video Bits
- Twitter rewards SPEED of joke.
- Video rewards COMMITMENT of joke.
- Don't confuse the two — a Twitter joke that demanded a video probably wasn't strong enough.

## On Knowing When to Stop
- A bit is over when the audience has stopped being surprised by the commitment.
- One good annual bit beats five mediocre ones.
- Know when to retire it.

## Adejuyigbe-Inflected Working Rubric
1. Is the disproportion between effort and silliness maximal?
2. Is there ONE single silly idea, executed completely?
3. Does the platform suit the joke (text vs. video vs. live)?
4. If recurring, is each iteration meaningfully escalated?
5. Are you committing more than the audience expects you to?
6. Do you know when to retire it?
