# Andy Goldberg — Improv Comedy

Founder of OFF THE WALL improv troupe; worked with Robin Williams, John Ritter, Corbin Bernsen.

## Five Core Teaching Principles

1. **Don't Deny** — accept what your scene partner offers (yes-and).
2. **Show, Don't Tell** — take the active choice; embody, don't narrate.
3. **Play the Moment** — don't plan ahead; LISTEN.
4. **Play the Scene Legitimately** — humor comes out of the situation; be real.
5. **Don't Try to Be Funny** — funniness emerges from authentic play.

## Book Structure
- 83 improv games / scene setups.
- Toolbox of advice for characters, plots, situations.
- Used by students, teachers, professional actors, corporate trainers.

## Why "Don't Try to Be Funny" Matters
- Reaching for jokes kills the scene.
- The scene generates its own humor when played truthfully.
- The audience laughs at recognition, not at gags.

## Key Concept: Building Engaging Characters
- Specific physicality.
- Clear point of view.
- Stakes the audience can read.

## Plot from Improv
- Don't plan; let actions reveal motivation.
- Consequences accumulate.
- Resolution emerges.

## Rubric (Goldberg Method)
1. Are players listening (not pre-planning)?
2. Are offers accepted, not denied?
3. Is the choice shown (embodied), not told?
4. Is the scene played for truth, not jokes?
5. Are characters and situation specific?
