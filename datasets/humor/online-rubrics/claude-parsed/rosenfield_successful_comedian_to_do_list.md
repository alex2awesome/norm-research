# Stephen Rosenfield — Mastering Stand-Up

Founder of the American Comedy Institute (1989). NYT: "probably the best known comedy teacher in the country." Alumni include Lena Dunham, Jim Gaffigan, Eric Slovin, Jessica Kirson.

## The 12-Item Successful Comedian's To-Do List

(Selected items from the list as available)

### 1. Master the Techniques of Stand-Up Comedy Performing
- Ability to produce onstage emotions that give life to performance.
- Create this emotional life night after night.
- In a way that seems spontaneous and unrehearsed.

### 2. Create Your Comic Persona
- Vivid, distinctive onstage personality.
- As individual as your real-life personality, only more so.
- The persona is the lens through which all material works.

### 3. Master the Techniques of Stand-Up Comedy Writing
- Joke craft.
- Premise development.
- Editing.

### 4. Develop a Strong Comic Voice
- Recognizable point of view.
- Worldview audience can identify quickly.

### 5. Develop Original Material
- Avoid obvious topics handled by others.
- Find the angle no one else has.

### 6. Understand the Business
- Need to be knowledgeable about the comedy business.
- Clubs, bookings, agents, contracts.

### 7. Have Fun
- Stand-up is the one endeavor where having fun is actually a requirement.
- The audience reads enjoyment.

(Other items implied but not fully recovered: handle hecklers, refine material through performance, network, develop multiple sets, build a brand, manage stage fright.)

## Forty Sharply Written Chapters
Cover every angle of the joke and how to tell it.

## Rubric (Rosenfield)
1. Is there a clear comic persona?
2. Does the material feel original to this comedian?
3. Are the techniques (writing AND performing) on display?
4. Does the comedian seem to enjoy themselves?
5. Could the audience describe the comedian's worldview?
