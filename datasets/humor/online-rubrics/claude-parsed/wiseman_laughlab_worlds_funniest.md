# LaughLab — Wiseman's Search for the World's Funniest Joke

2001 project with British Science Association. Largest internet joke vote (Guinness record).

## Methodology
- 40,000+ jokes submitted globally.
- 350,000 raters.
- 5-point scale: "not very funny" to "very funny."
- Demographics tracked (sex, age, nationality).

## The Winning Joke (Gurpal Gosall)
Two hunters joke involving 911 operator and accidental confirmation of death.

## Three Elements Common in Funniest Jokes
1. **Sense of superiority** — feeling smarter than someone in the joke.
2. **Unexpected response** — incongruity / surprise.
3. **Sad feeling of mortality** — touch of darkness / death.

## Cultural Findings

### British / Irish / NZ / Australian
- Prefer wordplay / puns.

### French / Danish / Belgian
- Prefer surreal humor.

### American / Canadian
- Prefer one group's superiority over another.

### Germans
- Found the widest range of jokes funny.

## Length Findings
- Jokes containing 103 words rated funniest.
- Winning joke was 102 words.
- Longer than soundbites; shorter than essays.

## Other Findings
- Wednesday afternoon at 6:03pm = peak funniness time.
- Animal jokes: ducks were funniest animal.
- Scientific reasoning: hard consonants, especially "K" sounds.

## Rubric (LaughLab Empirical)
1. Does the joke make someone feel superior to a target?
2. Does it have an unexpected twist?
3. Does it touch on darkness / mortality?
4. Is it ~100 words long?
5. Does it match the cultural humor preference of the audience?
