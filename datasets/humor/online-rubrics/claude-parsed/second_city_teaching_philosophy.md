# The Second City — Teaching Philosophy

Founded December 1959 by Bernie Sahlins, Howard Alk, and Paul Sills (son of Viola Spolin).

## Methodology Roots
Draws directly from Spolin's theater games:
- Spontaneous creation
- Ensemble work
- Embracing uncertainty

## Core Principles

### 1. "Yes, And"
- Foundational concept introduced in Improv 1.
- Acceptance + collaborative idea-building.
- Always agree, then add.

### 2. Spolin Fundamentals
- Ensemble (group over individual)
- Object work (mime objects with conviction)
- Environment (specific where)
- Sidecoaching (gentle guidance)

### 3. Being in the Moment
- Don't pre-plan.
- React to what just happened.
- Trust scene partners.

### 4. Character POV
- Each player has a clear point of view.
- POV drives consistent choices.
- POV makes scenes fertile for comedy.

### 5. Creating from Abundance
- Don't withhold; offer many ideas.
- Specific is better than safe.
- Play full out.

### 6. Joke Structure (in Sketch)
- Setup → twist → punch.
- Sketch comedy applies the same principles.
- Game of the scene (UCB-influenced).

## Sketch Creation Principles
- Collaboration > solo writing.
- Character development drives comedy.
- Joke structure layered on character truth.

## Rubric (Second City Style)
1. Are players "yes-and-ing"?
2. Is the ensemble prioritized over individuals?
3. Is each character given a specific POV?
4. Are players in the moment (not planning)?
5. Are choices specific and abundant?
