# Twitter / X Joke Writing

## Structural Principles

### Compression Above All
- The shorter the distance between setup and punchline, the funnier.
- Punchline always at the end.
- "Digital lightning bolt — short, sharp, unexpected."

### 280 Characters as Constraint
- Brevity forces precision.
- Every word counts (echoes Seinfeld).
- Often single-sentence.

## Common Twitter Joke Forms

### Observational One-Liner
"Did you ever notice…?" type, compressed.

### Misheard / Reframed
Take a familiar phrase and twist a word.

### Self-Deprecating Confession
"I just spent 45 minutes pretending to be busy so my dog wouldn't think I'm boring."

### Domain Mash-Up
Apply rules from one domain to another absurdly.

### Reply Format
Quote-tweet + sharp comment.

### Visual Pun
Image + text combo (when text alone won't carry).

## Why Twitter Humor Spreads
- Quotability (RT culture).
- Identifiable POV.
- Surprise endings.
- Specific to current events / culture (kairos).

## Audience-Specific
- Each Twitter community has its register.
- Stay current on trending topics and memes.
- Fresh, relevant, engaging.

## Comedy Bot vs. Human
- Bots predictable; humans surprise.
- Algorithmic patterns get stale.
- Voice matters.

## Rubric
1. Is the tweet under ~280 characters?
2. Is the punch at the very end?
3. Is it specific (not generic)?
4. Could it be quote-tweeted with "this"?
5. Is the voice recognizable?
