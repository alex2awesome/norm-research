# Buster Keaton — The Master of Physical Comedy

## Style Identifier
"The Great Stone Face." Lucidity and precision + acrobatics of ballistic precision and kineticism.

## Distinguishing from Contemporaries
- Unlike Chaplin (sentimental) and Lloyd (frantic).
- Keaton: deadpan + precise physical comedy.
- "Slow burn" mastery — emotion withheld, surfaces in micro-expressions.

## Precision and Timing
- Used gravity, timing, physical props in innovative ways.
- Stunts looked effortless and seamless.
- Meticulously choreographed sequences.
- Each movement precisely timed.
- Falls, leaps, stumbles each contribute to the climax.

## Falling Technique
> "The secret is in landing limp and breaking the fall with a foot or a hand."
- Stunts meticulously rehearsed.
- Often years building up to the climactic gag.

## Dangerous Precision
- Stood in specific locations relying on precise timing.
- Famous house-front gag: window dropped around him with inches of clearance.
- Train stunts performed without doubles.
- Did his own stunts at significant injury risk.

## Camera as Comic Tool
- Single takes preferred.
- Audience must see the full action.
- No cuts to fake the stunt.
- Wide shots showing scale.

## Comedic Beats
- Setup: establish ordinary scene.
- Disruption: physical force.
- Survival: comic absence of expected harm.
- Continuation: scene proceeds.

## Why It Lasts
- Visual humor crosses language.
- Body-as-machine vs. body-as-grace tension.
- Death-defying precision implies real stakes.
- Deadpan amplifies absurdity.

## Rubric (Keaton-Style Physical)
1. Is the timing precise (microseconds)?
2. Is the physical commitment real (no fake)?
3. Is the face deadpan / unreactive?
4. Does the gag combine physical risk with comic absence of harm?
5. Does the camera show the full action (no edit cheating)?
