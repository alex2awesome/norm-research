# Nathan Fielder — Awkward Comedy

## Core Persona
- Awkward loner.
- Blank stare.
- Seeming inability to respond to social cues.
- Generates one deeply uncomfortable moment after another.

## Format (*Nathan For You*, *The Rehearsal*, *The Curse*)
- Plays a slightly more awkward fictional version of himself.
- Offers absurd/expensive business advice to real businesses.
- Real subjects, real reactions.
- Fielder maintains the persona without breaking.

## Signature Tactical Elements

### The Excruciating Silence
Seth Rogen on Fielder:
> "Nathan is willing to let things get more excruciatingly awkward than most people have a tolerance for."

> "He is willing to sit silently for long periods to the point that the people he's interacting with are forced to reveal themselves, because the silence is excruciating."

### Disrupting Small Talk
- Bizarre comments without rudeness.
- Destabilizes interviewees.
- Gets authentic reactions in age of careful "reality" TV.

### Sincerity Mode
- Never winks at the camera.
- Always "earnest."
- The persona is the joke, but persona never breaks.

## Subject Ethics
- Real people involved.
- Some debate over consent / manipulation.
- Fielder generally edits to make subjects sympathetic.

## Why It Works
- Real reactions = authentic comedy.
- The audience watches Fielder break others.
- Or, more often, watches the others reveal genuine warmth/weirdness.
- The comedy comes from the gap between Fielder's stilted demeanor and his outlandish suggestions.

## Notable Bits
- "Dumb Starbucks" — replicating Starbucks with "Dumb" prefix.
- The Holocaust survivor / petting zoo bit.
- The pig saving a goat in the lake (manufactured).

## Rubric (Fielder Style)
1. Is there a committed awkward persona?
2. Are real people / situations involved?
3. Is silence used as a tool?
4. Is the sincerity uncracked?
5. Does the comedy come from authentic reaction?
