# Joe Toplyn — Witscript & Late-Night Joke Formulas

Four-time Emmy winner, former head writer for David Letterman and Jay Leno.

## Core Premise (from Comedy Writing for Late-Night TV)
The jokes that got the biggest laughs follow **identifiable formulas** populated with "handles" — people, places, things — each with a variety of related associations that combine to form a punch line.

## Witscript System (computational humor)

### Process
1. **Keyword Identification**: NLP and word embeddings find the keyword pair most likely to seed a joke.
2. **Association Linking**: Word associations with the best wordplay link to create a punch line.
3. **Bridge Text Generation**: Fine-tuned language model fills the gap between input and punch line in a natural-sounding way.
4. **Quality Filtering**: Only outputs jokes that exceed a preset score.

## Late-Night Joke Anatomy
- **Topic** → familiar news handle
- **Angle** → secondary aspect
- **Punch line** → connects topic and angle via wordplay or unexpected association
- **Tag** → optional second punchline that escalates

## Joke Formulas (selected)
- The Comparison (X is like Y because Z)
- The Reverse (turn the obvious meaning around)
- The Pun
- The Misdirection
- The Personification
- The Fake-Out Quote
- The Topical Mash-Up

## Rubric
1. Is there a recognizable handle from the news/audience knowledge?
2. Is the angle on the handle specific?
3. Does the punch line link via wordplay or shared association?
4. Is the bridge between topic and punch natural?
5. Is the joke specifically about the handle, not generic?
