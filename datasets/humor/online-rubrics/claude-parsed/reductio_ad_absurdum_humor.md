# Reductio ad Absurdum in Comedy

## Definition
Latin for "reduction to absurdity." A method of proving a statement by showing that assuming the opposite leads to a ridiculous or contradictory outcome. Take a claim, follow it to its furthest conclusion, demonstrate that conclusion is illogical/undesirable.

## Comedy Application
Pushes arguments to ridiculous extremes for comic effect.

### Process
1. Start with a premise or claim (often the audience's tacit belief).
2. Apply logic faithfully and consistently.
3. Follow the chain to a wildly absurd conclusion.
4. Implication: the original premise must be wrong (or society's logic is broken).

## Examples / Practitioners
- **Fawlty Towers**: structured humor follows premises to absurd ends.
- **Dave Chappelle**: signature use in specials. Daniel Dennett: "general purpose crowbar of rational argument."
- **Swift's *Modest Proposal***: classic reductio satire.
- **Monty Python**: sketches that follow rules to absurd conclusions.

## Why It's Funny
- Audience follows logic willingly.
- Sees own commitments turn into absurdities.
- Cognitive surprise: "I agreed step by step, but I don't agree with this conclusion."
- Reveals hidden assumptions.

## Comedy Pitfalls
- Easily becomes a straw man (distorting the premise).
- Loses force if the chain is unfair.
- Can come across as preachy if the target is too obvious.

## Subforms
- **Modest Proposal**: deadpan policy proposal.
- **Faux-Earnest Argument**: comedian argues for an absurd position with full conviction.
- **Logical Extension**: take a small annoyance to apocalyptic conclusion.

## Rubric
1. Is there a clear starting premise?
2. Are the logical steps fair (not straw men)?
3. Does the absurd endpoint follow rigorously?
4. Does the audience follow the chain willingly?
5. Does the conclusion expose a hidden assumption or absurdity?
