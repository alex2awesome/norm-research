# Magnet Theater: Improv Pedagogy

Magnet Theater is the third major NYC improv institution (alongside UCB and the Annoyance), founded by Armando Diaz. Its house philosophy emphasizes RELATIONSHIP-FIRST improv.

## The Magnet Approach
- Begin EVERY scene by establishing a relationship (lovers, siblings, coworkers, longtime friends).
- The relationship is the engine; the game is the fuel.
- Without relationship, comedy is mechanical.

## Why Relationship Matters
- The audience invests in PEOPLE more than in patterns.
- A funny game between strangers is a sketch; a funny game between people who clearly know each other is a scene.
- Stakes require relationship.

## The Honest-Reaction Rule
- React the way your character honestly would.
- Honest reactions to absurd offers create the comedy.
- Performative reactions create distance.

## On Discovery vs. Initiation
- The first lines of a scene should DISCOVER the relationship, not declare it.
- "Mom, you forgot my birthday" is a declaration; the audience now knows the relationship as data.
- "Mom, do you remember when you...?" begins to discover the relationship as story.

## On Form (the Armando)
- Start with a true monologue from a guest monologist.
- Improvise scenes inspired by the monologue.
- Each scene returns thematically to the monologue's truth.
- The form keeps improvisers anchored in real human experience.

## Magnet Working Rubric
1. Is the relationship established within the first three lines?
2. Are characters reacting honestly to offers?
3. Is the comedy emerging from the relationship's stress, not random escalation?
4. Have you DISCOVERED rather than declared the relationship?
5. If using a form, is the form anchoring or constraining?
6. Could the scene make sense even if you removed the punchlines?
