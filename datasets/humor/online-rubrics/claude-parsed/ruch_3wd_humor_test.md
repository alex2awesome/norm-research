# Ruch 3-WD Humor Test

Authored by Willibald Ruch (1983). Foundational humor appreciation measure.

## Method
Subjects presented with many jokes and cartoons; rate each on:
- **Funniness** (how funny it is)
- **Aversiveness** (how aversive it is)

## Three Factors (factor-analyzed)

### 1. Incongruity-Resolution Humor (INC-RES)
- Jokes with clear setup, incongruous twist, and resolvable punch.
- Audience finds the resolution; satisfaction.
- "Classical" joke structure.

### 2. Nonsense Humor (NON)
- Jokes where the incongruity is NOT fully resolved.
- Absurd, surreal.
- Resolution is partial or playful.
- Examples: surreal one-liners, anti-jokes.

### 3. Sexual Humor (SEX)
- Jokes whose content involves sex/eroticism.
- Independent dimension from incongruity-vs-nonsense.

## Six Scores
- INC-RESf, NONf, SEXf (funniness)
- INC-RESa, NONa, SEXa (aversiveness)

## Personality Correlates
- Conservative personalities prefer Incongruity-Resolution.
- Open personalities prefer Nonsense.
- Sensation-seekers prefer Sexual.
- Each style has its audience.

## Implications for Joke Quality
- "Funny" depends on audience type.
- Same joke may rate high for one personality, low for another.
- Score on multiple dimensions, not single funniness.

## Rubric (3-WD Style Tagging)
1. Is the joke incongruity-resolution (clear punch)?
2. Is it nonsense (absurd without resolution)?
3. Is it sexual content?
4. What is the funniness score (1–7)?
5. What is the aversiveness score (1–7)?
