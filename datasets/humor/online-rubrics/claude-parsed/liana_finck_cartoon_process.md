# Liana Finck — Cartoonist Process

## Style
- Specific, awkward sense of humor; cult following.
- Pokes at contradictions and absurdities of daily life and modern parenting.
- Dry humor, philosophical reflections.
- Themes: class, equality, access.

## Single-Panel Cartoon = Joke in Drawing Form
- **Setup**: something most readers will recognize.
- **Punchline**: the visual or caption that subverts.

## Two Production Modes

### New Yorker Cartoons (Sonnets)
- Spend a day per week thinking.
- Calm state of mind, leave herself behind.
- Doing scales — practice and discipline.
- Slow-cooked.

### Instagram Cartoons (Haikus)
- One take, photographed.
- ~2 minutes from thought to publishing.
- Single punch (not setup-punchline).
- Drawing as way to process real-life bothers.

## What Makes a Cartoon Land
- Recognition: "I've felt that."
- Compression: visual + caption together carry meaning.
- Drawing imperfection signals authenticity.
- Specificity of small social moment.

## Modern Cartoon Strategies
- The thought bubble made literal.
- The internal monologue externalized.
- Parenthood scenes magnified to absurdity.
- Anxiety personified.

## Rubric (Finck-Style Cartoon)
1. Is there a recognizable everyday setup?
2. Does the visual + caption combine to make the punch?
3. Is the punch single (haiku) or compound (sonnet)?
4. Does it process a real anxiety / contradiction?
5. Could a viewer say "that's so me"?
