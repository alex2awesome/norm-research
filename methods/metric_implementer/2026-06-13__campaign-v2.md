## The estimand (one row per trial)

The deliverable is the **long table** (`batch_scoring.LONGTABLE_COLUMNS`): one row per
**(judge_model × metric × prompt-version × item × pass)** with `operator` + `round` (the GEPA
iteration that minted the prompt), `token_cap`, `score`, `applicable`, `prompt_hash`. From it we
derive: the per-tier articulability curve `F_E(L)`, the gap `G = F_C − F_E(L)` and `τ(E)` (§1, fidelity
units), reliability (CV vs ρ — Choi), the additive-vs-multiplicative fit (T7a/U2), the V-calibration
leg (§8), and `words_share` (triad).

## What each trial tests, and where the theory is predicted to break

| Prediction | The test on this data | Predicted break / kill condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Capability axis (U1)** `F_E(L)` rises with judge capability | per-tier seed fidelity across the model ladder | non-monotone across tiers ⇒ capability axis ill-defined for judges (Ruan family-heterogeneity) |
| **T7a additive vs multiplicative (U2)** thin=separable, thick=complementary | fit both forms to the L×C grid; `I_m` vs `words_share` | `I_m` uncorrelated with words_share ⇒ demote to curve-fit (not thickness) |
| **Reliability ≠ validity (Choi/Baumann)** instructions buy stability not discrimination | CV (cross-pass/phrasing) vs ρ (signal) per metric | if CV dominates ρ everywhere ⇒ articulability unmeasurable above noise (report null) |
| **V ⊆ A calibration (§8)** verifier items recover d→1, θ-independent | V-amenable items (code/math w/ verifiers) as known-answer anchors | instrument fails to recover the known ceiling ⇒ instrument broken (E0-real fail) |
| **Anchor ≠ ground truth (§1)** anchor is a fallible ceiling | anchor vs outcome `y` divergence (E6, held out) | — (descriptive) |
| **Recover vs exploit (prompt-opt)** GEPA gain must survive controls | after SEARCH: cross-family transfer + counterfactual edits + length-cap arm | gain collapses under length-cap ⇒ frontier was optimizer slack, not articulability |

## Confound controls baked in (mandatory before any length claim)

- **Pipal (arXiv:2604.03684):** vary L *within a fixed criterion set* (more words for the SAME
  construct), never by adding criteria — else we measure scheme-count, not budget. Length-matched
  filler control.
- **Liu lost-in-the-middle (2307.03172):** randomize clause order across passes; report
  position-averaged fidelity.
- **Tokenizer-invariant budget (U6):** measure L in clauses/quanta (or bytes), not raw judge tokens.
- **Phrasing distribution (Baumann):** report articulability as a distribution over phrasings, not one
  prompt; correct downstream `y` estimates with PPI/DSL.
- **Cross-family judge≠anchor:** avoid self-preference leakage (Panickssery).
