ELEGANCE AND INSIGHT RUBRIC — Math.SE Answers

An answer is ELEGANT and INSIGHTFUL when it reveals WHY a result is true through a minimal, well-chosen idea — NOT merely when it is short, polite, or uses correct mathematical vocabulary.

Score on a 0.0–1.0 scale using the sub-criteria and anchors below. Average the sub-scores.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SUB-CRITERION 1 — CONCEPTUAL CORE
Does the answer identify a single unifying idea or structural observation that makes the result feel inevitable — an "aha" moment?
  HIGH: Points to a structural reason (e.g. "lower-triangular matrices expose eigenvalues on the diagonal; no calculation needed"), a non-obvious reframing, or a connecting principle.
  LOW: The answer works through cases, applies a standard algorithm, or lists steps without naming why the approach works.
  ZERO: No explanation at all, or just a link/reference.

SUB-CRITERION 2 — ECONOMY OF MEANS
Does the answer reach its conclusion with minimal machinery? Could the key point be stated in 1–3 sentences even if the answer is longer?
  HIGH: The core idea is compact; any additional steps are clearly subordinate. The answer could not be meaningfully shortened without losing correctness.
  LOW: The answer uses a long sequence of symbol manipulations, case splits, or algorithmic steps where a slicker path exists.
  ZERO: The answer is a wall of computation or case-bash with no identification of a shorter path.

SUB-CRITERION 3 — EXPLANATORY FORCE
Does the answer explain WHY, not just WHAT or HOW? After reading, would a reader understand the mechanism, not just verify the result?
  HIGH: The reader learns something transferable — a principle, a pattern, or a perspective shift.
  LOW: The answer demonstrates correctness (HOW to compute) without illuminating the underlying reason.
  ZERO: The answer is incorrect or purely procedural with no explanatory content.

SUB-CRITERION 4 — AVOIDANCE OF BRUTE FORCE
Does the answer deliberately avoid or criticize mechanically exhaustive approaches even when they are available?
  HIGH: The answer explicitly chooses a principled path over a mechanical one, or notes why the elegant approach is preferred.
  MID: The answer is mechanical but brief; no obviously cleaner path exists for this problem type.
  LOW: The answer relies on case enumeration, computer assistance, or direct symbol-grinding when a conceptual shortcut is available.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORE ANCHORS

1.0 — ALL four sub-criteria are HIGH. The answer is a model of mathematical insight: compact, explanatory, avoids brute force, and delivers a genuine "aha."

0.75 — THREE sub-criteria are HIGH or the answer is strong on Conceptual Core + Explanatory Force but slightly mechanical in Economy. Clear insight present but not perfectly distilled.

0.5 — TWO sub-criteria are HIGH. Partial insight: the answer identifies the right idea but buries it in computation, OR is economical but shallow (short without depth).

0.25 — ONE sub-criterion is HIGH (typically Explanatory Force only — answer is correct and clear but step-by-step). Competent but not elegant.

0.0 — NONE or only procedural: the answer is a brute-force computation, a bare link/reference, a one-liner that only states the answer without any reasoning, or is non-mathematical.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS (common judge errors)
• A SHORT answer is NOT automatically elegant — brevity without insight scores LOW on Conceptual Core.
• A CORRECT, PEDAGOGICALLY ORGANIZED answer that walks through inclusion-exclusion steps (e.g. A∪B counting) is MECHANICAL even if well-written — score it 0.25.
• A PROOF that begins "Note that [key structural observation], therefore..." scores HIGH on Conceptual Core even if some algebra follows.
• Computer-assisted answers (Wolfram Alpha, GAP code outputs) score LOW on Economy and Avoidance of Brute Force unless they pivot to explain the mathematical WHY behind the output.
• A ONE-SENTENCE answer that merely states the correct result without any explanation scores 0.0 or 0.25 at best.
