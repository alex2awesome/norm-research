CRITERIA:

[X01] Validation-seeking and uncertain posture
Score 0-10 the extent to which the post is framed as a question, request for checking, or expression of doubt rather than a confident solution. Look for phrases like 'Does this work?', 'Can someone please explain why', 'Wouldn't that be overcounting?', or 'I'm getting X, is this right?'. Do not judge whether the uncertainty is warranted; score only how much the text seeks validation.

[X02] Algebraic step transparency
Score 10 if all key algebraic manipulations are shown step by step with no major leaps. Score 0 if the post jumps directly to results with significant intermediate algebra omitted.

[X03] Explicit claim-proof structuring
Score 10 if the solution contains multiple explicitly labeled claims or lemmas (using 'Claim:', 'Lemma:', or equivalent markers), each with its own proof block. Score 0 if the solution is unstructured flowing prose or raw calculations with no organizational markers.

[X04] Strategic use of symmetry
Score 10 when symmetry, invariance, or an interchangeable reformulation materially reduces the argument and is used correctly; score 0 when symmetry is absent, accidental, or merely mentioned without consequence.

[X05] Argues the step a competent reader would stop at
Score 0-10 for whether the one move a competent reader would question -- an unjustified case reduction, an equality claimed without proof, a divisibility or convergence step waved through, an appeal to a theorem whose hypotheses are not obviously met -- is actually argued rather than asserted. 10 = the load-bearing step is supported; 0 = the argument's weight rests on an unsupported assertion.

[X06] Legacy autocorrupted LaTeX and era-marked forum markup
Score 0-10 the extent to which the post carries typographic markers of an older era of this forum's software, judged purely by their presence. Count: math macros of the form backslash-equal-brace-brace and backslash-minus-brace-brace appearing where a plain equals sign or minus sign belongs; a stray space immediately after an opening dollar sign (for example a dollar sign followed by space then a numeral); bracketed url tags with a full http address inside the prose; double-square-bracket wiki-style links or File inclusions; hide tags whose argument is wrapped in double quotes rather than bare; and other markup that is not the modern plain form. 0 = none of these appear; 10 = they appear throughout. Do NOT judge whether old or new markup is better, cleaner or more readable, and do NOT judge the mathematics. Score only how much era-marked markup is visibly present.

[X07] Absence of unconventional-approach markers
Score 10 if the post contains NO language self-labeling the approach as unusual, unconventional, or excessive (e.g., 'troll,' 'scuffed,' 'overkill,' 'different,' 'cheap,' 'ridiculous'). Score 0 if multiple such markers appear, signaling the author themselves recognizes the approach deviates from standard.

[X08] Contest-conditions self-report and score talk
Score 0-10 for the EXTENT to which the post narrates the circumstances of its own production under exam conditions: phrases such as 'my solution during the contest', 'in-contest', 'I wrote this on the test', reported grades or points ('somehow got a 7/7', 'hoping it's not more than 1 point off', 'I sure do hope it is valid'), reported elapsed time ('it took ~1.3 hours'), or comparisons to other problems on the same paper ('meanwhile I thought #4 was far harder'). 0 = the post says nothing about when or under what conditions it was written; 10 = several such self-reports, and the framing of the whole post is 'here is what I did on the day'. Score only how much such self-report is present. You must NOT judge whether the claim is credible or whether writing under contest conditions makes the post better or worse.

[X09] Colloquial and emotional register
Score 0-10 the EXTENT to which the text uses informal colloquial language, slang, self-deprecation, emoticons, or emotional interjections (e.g., 'rip', 'cuz', ':(', 'nooooo', 'Bro'), as opposed to standard mathematical prose. 0 = entirely formal; 10 = heavily colloquial/emotional. Do not judge whether informality is good or bad.

[X10] Global closure of local alternatives
Score 10 when pointwise possibilities are shown to combine into one globally consistent form, with mixed assignments explicitly ruled out; score 0 when the post stops after listing possibilities for each individual input.

[X11] Answer-choice anchoring
Score 0-10 for the extent to which the post foregrounds answer choices, option letters, boxed numerical outputs, or answer-checking language. Judge only this assessment-format trace; do not judge whether it is good.

[X12] Single external theorem does all the work
Score how much of the argument is discharged by invoking one heavy named or external result whose statement is not proved or unpacked in the post. 10 = no such invocation; every step is carried out on the objects of the problem. 0 = essentially the whole problem is dispatched by one citation - the hook-length formula for Young tableaux (with a link), Minkowski's inequality applied once to get the answer, a scaling trick that manufactures the answer in two lines - so that the post contains almost no reasoning specific to this problem. Mid scores when a named result is invoked but substantial problem-specific work surrounds it.

[X13] Exploits the multiple-choice option list
For posts on problems that display answer choices (A)-(E). 10 = the post determines the answer as a value, never using the option list except to report the letter at the end. 0 = the option list is load-bearing: the post computes a residue or parity and picks the unique surviving choice, or argues that the choices are far enough apart that a coarse computation distinguishes them, or works in a substitute setting on the grounds that the listed answers are distinguishable there. Score 5 if the option list is used only as a final sanity check on an independently obtained value. Score 5 for problems with no answer choices.

[X14] Completeness of candidate classification
Score 10 when the post both derives all possible candidates and directly verifies that each candidate satisfies the original conditions; score 0 when it guesses candidates, verifies only some, or never excludes alternatives.

[X15] Explicit self-assessment labels
Score 0-10 the extent to which the post labels its own solution with qualitative descriptors such as 'cheap', 'fast', 'overkill', 'scuffed', 'troll', 'cursed', 'different', 'alternative', or 'simple'. Do not judge whether the label is accurate; score only how many such self-assessment phrases appear.

[X16] Inter-post referencing and sequence markers
Score 0-10 the extent to which the text explicitly references other posts, posters, or its place in the thread sequence. Look for phrases such as '@above', 'as above', 'previous post', 'Solution 2', 'different from the others', 'similar to what was posted', or direct replies to a named user. Do not judge whether referencing is good or bad; score only how much such referencing is present.

[X17] Isolable pivotal-fact statement
Score whether the post states a single crisp pivotal identity, lemma, or structural fact as a self-contained claim that could stand alone as the crux of a solution, independent of whether surrounding derivation or final computation is completed. A 10: a specific formula/identity/lemma is explicitly isolated and named as the key fact (e.g., stated on its own line or flagged as 'the key point is...'), even if little else is derived. A 0: no such isolable pivotal fact ever appears; the post is either a diffuse mass of computation with no single fact singled out, or contains no mathematical content at all.

[X18] Names and separately addresses a trap or edge case
Score whether the post explicitly names a specific trap, degenerate configuration, or edge case (rather than only implicitly handling it inside general casework) and pauses to address it as its own labeled step. A 10: a specific trap/edge case is explicitly named (e.g., 'the pointwise trap', 'if a=b this fails', 'the degenerate case where...') and separately resolved. A 0: no edge case or trap is ever explicitly named as such, even if some casework is present.

[X19] Confident, unhedged register
Score 0-10 for how unhedged the prose is: absence of 'I think', 'probably', 'it seems', 'I might be wrong', 'can someone check', question marks addressed to the reader, and apologetic asides. 10 = flatly declarative throughout; 0 = hedged in almost every sentence. Score the register only; do not judge whether the confidence is warranted.

[X20] Collaboration attribution
Score 0–10 for the extent of explicit references to collaborators, helpers, prior assistance, or thanking another participant; do not judge the value of that assistance.

[X21] Exact modular periodicity
Score 10 when the post proves a valid period or residue restriction and then uses it exhaustively to constrain the target; score 0 when it merely reports a pattern or invokes a modulus without justifying all relevant cases.

[X22] Use of AoPS forum markup furniture
Score 0-10 for how much AoPS forum furniture the post uses -- hide or spoiler blocks, boxed final answers, quote blocks, bold run-in labels, numbered or bulleted step lists -- rather than continuous prose. 10 = the post is built out of such furniture; 0 = unbroken prose with none of it. Score the layout only; do not judge whether the steps are right or whether the structure helps.

[X23] Method sophistication proportionality to problem
Score 10 if the mathematical tools used are proportionate in sophistication to the apparent difficulty of the problem (e.g., elementary number theory for a modular arithmetic problem). Score 0 if the method is wildly over-powered (e.g., finite field theory for a root-of-unity computation) or under-powered for the problem.

[X24] Density of display mathematics and align environments
Score 0-10 how much of this post's mathematics is set as centred display or in multi-line aligned environments rather than run inline inside sentences. Consider the proportion of equations that are broken out on their own lines, the use of aligned/align-star or eqnarray blocks, chains of implication set on separate display lines, and tabular blocks. 0 = all mathematics is inline within running prose; 10 = the post is essentially a stack of display blocks with connective text between them. Do NOT judge whether the typesetting is attractive, readable or appropriate, and do NOT judge whether the mathematics is right. Score only the extent of display-versus-inline setting.

[X25] Post questions or disowns the argument it posts
Score the post's stance toward the reasoning it displays. 10: the post asserts its argument as correct and finished. 5: the post asserts the argument but hedges about a specific step. 0: the post displays reasoning it does not stand behind -- asks why its answer is wrong, asks whether the approach works, or reproduces someone else's argument in order to object to it.

[X26] Objective-preserving optimization
Score 10 when the varying target is rigorously reduced to a simpler quantity using a fixed or monotone factor, and the optimization direction is preserved; score 0 when calculations are performed without establishing that they optimize the requested quantity.

[X27] Delivers the object the problem asked for
Score 0-10 for whether the post delivers the thing the problem requested -- a proof where a proof was demanded, all solutions where all were demanded, a construction where one was demanded, the requested closed form -- rather than a related result, a special case, or a different question the poster found more interesting. 10 = exactly the requested deliverable; 0 = a substitute the problem did not ask for.

[X28] Markup scaffolding
Score 0-10 for how much the post uses visual organization such as hides, colored headings, bullets, boxed answers, quote blocks, or elaborate sectioning; do not judge whether this feature is good.

[X29] Generalizes beyond the minimum needed
Score whether the post goes beyond exactly what is needed to answer the stated problem, e.g. by extending the argument to a broader family (different n, different configuration), stating additional properties of the configuration, or adding a labeled 'further observations' section. A 10: the post clearly extends its reasoning or findings beyond the minimal answer, with the extension clearly marked as bonus/general material. A 0: the post stops the instant the requested quantity is obtained, with no exploration of the broader structure.
