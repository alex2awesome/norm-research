For each criterion, decide:

  "incidental" -- the criterion measures something that may well PREDICT how the item

The corpus is: short jokes posted to a large public joke-sharing forum, where readers vote on what they find funny. Each item is a joke.

SECOND JUDGEMENT, only for criteria you label "incidental". Some incidental channels have

Judge each criterion ON ITS OWN TEXT. Do not try to guess which pool it came from, and

CRITERIA:

[X01] Self-undermining irony
Score 10 when the speaker’s stated attitude is contradicted by their own behavior or wording in a precise, revealing way; score 0 when no meaningful contradiction exists.

[X02] Source-transfer residue
On a 0-10 scale, how much does the wording suggest transcription, translation, quotation, or transfer from a pre-existing joke source through canonical phrasing, awkward lexical choices, or excess explanation? Judge only the visible residue, not its quality.

[X03] Taboo and shock-content reliance
Score 0-10 for the extent to which the item's comic engine is carried by taboo material: sexual or scatological content, degradation of a person or group, atrocity or tragedy (terror attacks, the Holocaust, a parent's death), or slur-adjacent stereotype. 0 = no taboo content; 10 = the item is wholly built on shock. Extent of presence only; do not judge appropriateness, bravery, or funniness.

[X04] Overall length of the post
Score 0-10 for how long the text is. 10 = many sentences or paragraphs; 5 = a few sentences; 0 = a single short line. Judge extent only -- do not consider whether the length is warranted, whether the build is worth it, or whether anything in it lands.

[X05] Orthographic irregularity
On a 0-10 scale, how much does the text contain spelling errors, grammatical fragments, unusual capitalization, punctuation runs, repeated glyphs, or malformed layout? Judge only the extent of irregularity, never whether it improves the joke.

[X06] Taboo-register intensity
On a 0-10 scale, how much does the text rely on sexual, scatological, violent, tragic, religiously inflammatory, or slur-adjacent material? Judge only the amount and explicitness of taboo content, never whether it is acceptable or funny.

[X07] Community self-satire with crafted payoff
Judge whether the comic target is the joke-sharing community itself (reposting, lurking, commenting, hate-following, addiction) AND whether that self-reference carries a real constructed payoff rather than a bare mention. 10 = the community is unmistakably the butt and a crafted vehicle delivers the turn (a scripture passage repurposed as repost-lore; a book-title recommendation that lands the insult). 5 = community referenced but the turn is generic. 0 = no community self-reference, or a mention with nothing built on it.

[X08] Length and elaboration
Score 0-10 for how much text bulk, preamble, lineation, or explanatory padding is present; judge extent only, never whether the joke is good or funny.

[X09] Recognizable internet format, twisted
Score whether the joke's engine is faithful mimicry of a recognizable internet format (song-lyric meme block, app-launch pitch, clickbait headline, listicle, how-to) followed by a twist that exploits a convention of that format. 10 = the format replication is precise AND the twist depends on knowing the format. 5 = format present, twist generic to it. 0 = no recognizable format play.

[X10] Absurd concrete specificity
Score 10 when unusual concrete details make the comic situation vivid and heighten its incongruity; score 0 when the joke remains generic or detail-free.

[X11] Freshness within a familiar form
Score 10 when a recognizable joke template receives a specific, non-obvious twist; score 0 when it is a stock pun, direct repetition, or minimally altered repost.

[X12] Premise-punchline fit
Score 10 when the punchline directly answers, extends, or resolves the setup; score 0 when it could be attached to almost any setup.

[X13] Reveal forces a rereading of the setup
Score 0-10 for how far the ending makes the reader go back and reinterpret what the setup meant -- the setup supported one reading, and the last move shows a second reading was there all along. 10 = the whole setup flips meaning on the final move; 0 = the ending adds information but nothing earlier is re-read. Judge the mechanism, not whether the joke amused you.

[X14] Ends on a short final line
Score 0-10 for how short the last line is relative to the rest of the text -- a clipped closing line set off on its own scores high, a final line as long as the body scores low. 10 = the final line is a few words on its own; 0 = there is no distinct short closing line. Count the layout only; do not judge whether the ending works.

[X15] Headline-dependence of the comic engine
Delete the news-specific proper nouns and current-event hooks from the item and judge what comic mechanism survives. 10 = nothing survives — the item is a date-stamped gesture at a headline, and recognition of the news item is the entire joke. 5 = a topical hook plus some independent turn. 0 = evergreen; no news dependency. Reverse-scored: 10 means the joke is rented, not owned.

[X16] Phonetic phrase transformation
Score 10 when a crisp sound-alike or spoken resegmentation makes an ordinary phrase suddenly fit; score 0 when no such transformation exists or it is unclear.

[X17] Repost-chain disclosure
Score 0–10 for how much the text explicitly signals reposting, prior circulation, being a variant, or familiarity with an earlier joke. Judge only the textual signal; do not judge whether it is good, funny, effective, or desirable.

[X18] Delayed frame reversal
Score 10 when the setup establishes one plausible interpretation and the final line sharply reinterprets it; score 0 when the ending adds no meaningful reversal.

[X19] Stock-punchline position in the retelling chain
Score how recognisably the punchline is a pre-existing, widely circulated classic payload the audience is assumed to already know -- famous set-piece puns and riddle answers that have circulated for years (e.g., the 'super-calloused fragile mystic hexed by halitosis' chain-joke, 'a play on word(s)', 'it's snot', 'she loves me... not', the antennas/reception wedding joke). 0 = punchline appears newly minted for this post; 10 = verbatim or near-verbatim retelling of a canonical joke. Score recognisable retelling status only; do not judge how well it is told or whether it is funny.

[X20] Deadpan literal answer where wit expected
Judge whether an expectation of cleverness is set up and then answered with a flat, literal, or obvious reply played perfectly straight. 10 = the refusal to be clever is total and the mundanity is the laugh (the view was blocked by the mountains; the oil used is olive oil; the described person is simply the cashier). 5 = literal answer with a visible wink. 0 = figurative or witty punchline, or no expectation established.

[X21] Delivery-template commitment
Score 0-10 for commitment to a stock delivery shell such as a question-answer riddle, difference joke, dialogue, narrative, or list; judge the format's presence only, never whether the format is effective.

[X22] Reception-management furniture: vote appeals, repost apologies
Score how much audience-management furniture surrounds the joke: apologies, disclaimers, predictions or pleas about the post's own fate that appear as quoted text in the item ('this joke won't get any upvotes', 'technically a repost', 'sorry if repost', 'first post', 'all I need is a good one-liner', self-flagellation like 'I'm a terrible person'). 0 = none; 10 = the framing is dominated by talk about the post's own fate rather than the joke. Score presence and share of the text only; this is not a quality judgement.

[X23] Reference transformed into mechanism
Score 10 when a recognizable cultural, literary, or fictional reference is actively reshaped into the punchline; score 0 when it is only named or quoted without transformation.

[X24] Compression and payoff immediacy
Score 10 when every word contributes and the punchline is immediately legible without an explanatory tail; score 0 when the joke is padded, delayed, or requires extended unpacking.

[X25] Constructed shock handling
Score 10 when taboo, hostile, sexual, or tragic material is paired with a precise reversal, absurdity, or wordplay mechanism; score 0 when provocation, cruelty, or shock is the main substance.

[X26] Conversational voice authenticity
Score 10 when the wording sounds like a spontaneous speaker with a distinct attitude, rhythm, and social perspective; score 0 when it sounds mechanically templated, padded, or disclaimer-heavy.

[X27] Surface readability
Score 10 when wording, spelling, and formatting make the comic mechanism immediately easy to follow; score 0 when errors or awkward construction substantially obscure it.

[X28] Era-coded forum idiom
Score 0-10 for vocabulary, current events, platform references, political slogans, or calendar markers that locate the text in a recognizable forum era; judge extent only, never whether the joke is good.

[X29] Nothing in the text is removable
Score 0-10 for how much of the text is load-bearing: every clause either plants something the ending uses or is required to make the situation legible. 10 = removing any clause breaks the joke; 0 = whole sentences could be deleted without the joke changing. Judge necessity, not brevity -- a long text in which everything is used scores high.
