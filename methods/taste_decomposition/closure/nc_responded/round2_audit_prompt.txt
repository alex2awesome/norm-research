You are an independent auditor. Below are candidate criteria for scoring
PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each
would be scored 0-10 by a judge reading only one comment's text.

Classify EACH criterion as exactly one of:

  "quality_relevant" -- it measures something a careful regulatory analyst would call
      substantive merit of the comment as regulatory input: evidence, analysis,
      specificity about the rule, feasibility, actionable alternatives, first-hand
      knowledge, engagement with the agency's reasoning.

  "incidental" -- it is predictive-but-not-merit: a surface or shape feature, a
      formatting or length proxy, boilerplate or template similarity, a topic or
      agency-style marker, a submitter-identity or professional-drafting fingerprint,
      a tone or register marker, or the bare presence of a word or phrase.

DECISION AIDS.
  * If the criterion could be scored high by a comment that says nothing substantive,
    it is incidental.
  * If the instruction tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document rather than
    what the document establishes, it is incidental -- even when the shape usually
    co-occurs with merit. Say so in your justification if you see this.
  * "Related to quality" is not enough. The question is what the JUDGE would be
    scoring when following this exact instruction.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"id": "P01", "label": "quality_relevant" or "incidental",
   "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "justification": "<one or two sentences>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion ...
]}


--- CRITERION P01 ---
NAME: Supplies data the agency does not already have in the record
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment supplies information the agency would not otherwise possess. Score 10 when it provides measurements, operational records, survey results, or field observations and states where they come from. Score 0 when it supplies nothing beyond opinion. Judge whether new information enters the record, not whether it favors any position.

--- CRITERION P02 ---
NAME: Flags an ambiguous term and shows the interpretive consequence
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment isolates a specific word, phrase, or definition in the regulatory text and shows it admits two or more readings with different practical results. Score 10 when the term is quoted or cited to its section, the competing readings are laid out, and the divergent consequence of each is stated. Score 5 when it only calls a term unclear or asks for a definition without showing what turns on it. Score 0 when the operative text's wording is never engaged.

--- CRITERION P03 ---
NAME: Explains why the proposed approach will not achieve its stated aim
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment gives a reasoned account of a mechanism by which the rule as proposed would fail to produce the effect the agency states it intends. Score 10 when that failure mechanism is spelled out and reasoned through. Score 0 when no such account is given. Judge the reasoning offered, not the writer's confidence or tone.

--- CRITERION P04 ---
NAME: Personal anecdote as the sole evidentiary support
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment's entire argument rests on first-person anecdote. Score 10 when every supporting claim comes from the writer's own experience (I noticed…, in my case…), with no external citation, data, or source referenced anywhere in the text. Score 0 when the argument is built on external sources, data, regulatory text, or reasoning that does not depend on the writer's personal experience.

--- CRITERION P05 ---
NAME: Agency-relationship disclosure
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the writer discloses a prior or ongoing relationship with the agency. Score 10 when the comment explicitly describes previous permits, recurring meetings, ongoing communications, or past dealings with the agency. Score 0 when no prior agency relationship is mentioned. This is a disclosure feature of the text: score its presence, not whether the relationship lends authority.

--- CRITERION P06 ---
NAME: Coalition consensus packaging
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment presents itself as speaking for many parties. Score 10 when it claims to represent multiple organizations, a membership, or a consensus process and names those parties. Score 0 when it is clearly an individual submission made on the writer's own behalf. Score the claim as presented in the text; no outside verification is expected.

--- CRITERION P07 ---
NAME: Monitoring, verification, and feedback design
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment proposes a way to check how the approach performs over time. Score 10 when it specifies measurable indicators, data collection, verification methods, reporting, or triggers for revisiting the approach. Score 0 when it offers no way to test or track performance at all.

--- CRITERION P08 ---
NAME: Distributional incidence and equity
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment differentiates who bears the costs and benefits. Score 10 when it names particular populations, firms, places, or users that face distinct costs or benefits and explains why exposure or access is unequal among them. Score 0 when effects are described only for an undifferentiated public.

--- CRITERION P09 ---
NAME: Regulatory-lawyer fluency
INSTRUCTION: Score how precisely the comment maps proposed text onto the regulatory apparatus. Score 10 when it ties the proposal to specific docket provisions, CFR sections, statutory authority, or requested edits in the register a regulatory practitioner uses. Score 0 when it offers only broad support or opposition with no such mapping.

--- CRITERION P10 ---
NAME: Generalized firsthand evidence
INSTRUCTION: Score whether firsthand experience is reported concretely and carried up to a general pattern. Score 10 when the writer gives specific observations from personal experience and translates them into a rule-relevant pattern that could hold beyond the writer's own case. Score 0 when the personal material is only anecdote, praise, or emotion, with no analyzable detail.

--- CRITERION P11 ---
NAME: Submission-wrapper residue
INSTRUCTION: Score how much submission-wrapper residue the text carries. Score 10 when portal or email headers, attachment placeholders, filenames, OCR errors, duplicated cover text, or page artifacts appear abundantly throughout. Score 0 when the text is clean, direct prose containing none of this material. This is a surface feature of the document: score how much residue is present, nothing else.

--- CRITERION P12 ---
NAME: Implementation-workflow specificity
INSTRUCTION: Score how specifically the comment describes the mechanics of putting the rule into effect. Score 10 when it names concrete forms, responsible offices, transition steps, testing scenarios, timelines, or documentation requirements. Score 0 when it states only a general preference, with no workflow detail of any kind.

--- CRITERION P13 ---
NAME: Formal salutation and signature block present
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment carries formal letter furniture. Score 10 when it opens with a formal salutation (Dear Administrator, To Whom It May Concern) and closes with a signature block naming a person or organisation. Score 0 when neither is present, and an intermediate score when only one is. This is a surface feature of format: score only the salutation and signature block.

--- CRITERION P14 ---
NAME: Institutional credential display
INSTRUCTION: Score how prominently the comment advertises institutional credentials. Score 10 when it foregrounds organizational size, market share, longevity, assets, employees, facilities, or senior titles. Score 0 when no institutional credentials are given. This is a self-presentation feature of the text: score how prominent the display is, not whether the credentials are impressive.

--- CRITERION P15 ---
NAME: Actionable procedural request with a reason tied to the record
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment requests a procedural step within the agency's power and gives a reason. Score 10 when a specific procedural action is requested — extend or reopen the comment period, defer action pending analysis, phase the effective date, re-notice, publish a tracking or timeline commitment — with a reason grounded in the record or the requester's circumstances. Score 5 when process is criticized but nothing is requested, or a request carries no reason. Score 0 when there is no procedural dimension.

--- CRITERION P16 ---
NAME: Legal authority and interpretive fit
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment connects the proposal to legal authority accurately. Score 10 when it ties the proposal to specific statutory language, delegated authority, precedent, or applicable legal limits, and states that connection correctly as far as the text shows. Score 0 when legal conclusions are asserted with no textual or doctrinal support.

--- CRITERION P17 ---
NAME: Rule-text pinpointing
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment targets identifiable rule text with a concrete revision. Score 10 when it names an exact proposed provision — by section, paragraph, or quoted language — and states a specific revision to that provision. Score 0 when the comment expresses only support or opposition with no identifiable rule-text target.

--- CRITERION P18 ---
NAME: Actionable amendment precision
INSTRUCTION: Score how precisely the comment specifies the change it asks for. Score 10 when it supplies exact replacement language, numeric thresholds, definitions, procedures, or parameters, stated concretely enough to be used as written. Score 0 when the requested action is only to do more, to act quickly, or a bare statement of support or opposition.

--- CRITERION P19 ---
NAME: Names a specific omission or missing commitment in the proposal
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies something the proposal fails to say, define, decide, or commit to, specifically enough for an agency to act on. Score 10 when it points to a particular gap — an unaddressed scenario, an undelivered commitment, an unassigned responsibility, an unanswered question the rule creates — and says what filling it would take. Score 5 when it calls the proposal incomplete without locating the gap. Score 0 when it engages only what the proposal does say.

--- CRITERION P20 ---
NAME: Bare presence of a numeric figure anywhere in the text
INSTRUCTION: Score whether any digit-bearing quantity appears anywhere in the comment. Score 10 when at least one number, percentage, dollar amount, or date written with digits is present. Score 0 when the comment contains no digits at all. This is a surface feature: do not consider what the number is used for or whether it is correct.

--- CRITERION P21 ---
NAME: Campaign template reuse
INSTRUCTION: Score how strongly the text reads as reproduced from a shared campaign template. Score 10 when the wording, headings, demands, or paragraph order appear duplicated from a circulated form submission, with little individualized content. Score 0 when the wording is clearly original and specific to this writer. This is a surface feature of the wording, scored as such and not as a judgment of quality.

--- CRITERION P22 ---
NAME: Evidence critique and uncertainty calibration
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment weighs the strength of the evidence it relies on. Score 10 when it distinguishes strong evidence from weak, names assumptions or limitations, and states confidence in proportion to the support offered. Score 0 when every claim is treated as certain or contrary evidence is passed over in silence.

--- CRITERION P23 ---
NAME: Feasible alternative design
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment offers an implementable alternative to the proposal. Score 10 when it proposes a concrete alternative, exception, threshold, or safeguard and explains how that variant still serves the proposal's stated legitimate objective. Score 0 when it offers only support, opposition, or an unelaborated demand. Score in between when an alternative is named but its fit to the objective is left unexplained.

--- CRITERION P24 ---
NAME: Explicit causal-mechanism / behavioral-response reasoning
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment traces a step-by-step causal chain from the rule to the effect it claims. Score 10 when each link in that chain is stated, including, where relevant, how affected parties would change their behavior in response. Score 0 when a harm or benefit is asserted with no account of the mechanism producing it. Score in between when a chain is offered but skips steps.

--- CRITERION P25 ---
NAME: Operational implementability
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment addresses what carrying the rule out would actually require. Score 10 when it identifies responsible actors, workflows, training needs, system dependencies, or failure modes that determine whether the rule can work in practice. Score 0 when implementation is treated as automatic, with no attention to who would do what or what could break.
