You are an independent auditor. Below are candidate criteria for scoring
PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each
would be scored 0-10 by a judge reading only one comment's text.

Classify EACH criterion as exactly one of:

  "quality_relevant" -- it measures something a careful regulatory analyst would call
      substantive merit of the comment as regulatory input: evidence, analysis,
      specificity about the rule, feasibility, actionable alternatives, first-hand
      knowledge, engagement with the agency's reasoning.

  "incidental" -- it is predictive-but-not-merit: a surface or shape feature, a
      formatting or length proxy, boilerplate or template similarity, a topic or
      agency-style marker, a submitter-identity or professional-drafting fingerprint,
      a tone or register marker, or the bare presence of a word or phrase.

DECISION AIDS.
  * If the criterion could be scored high by a comment that says nothing substantive,
    it is incidental.
  * If the instruction tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document rather than
    what the document establishes, it is incidental -- even when the shape usually
    co-occurs with merit. Say so in your justification if you see this.
  * "Related to quality" is not enough. The question is what the JUDGE would be
    scoring when following this exact instruction.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"id": "P01", "label": "quality_relevant" or "incidental",
   "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "justification": "<one or two sentences>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion ...
]}


--- CRITERION P01 ---
NAME: Professional regulatory drafting
INSTRUCTION: Score how closely the text resembles polished counsel-style regulatory drafting. A 10 has professional organization, precise docket and provision references, issue-by-issue proposed edits, and administrability framing. A 0 is informal, unsystematic, and nontechnical. This scores the drafting register and apparatus as a surface feature, not whether the positions taken are sound.

--- CRITERION P02 ---
NAME: Reads as a graded course assignment rather than a stakeholder filing
INSTRUCTION: Score how closely the comment reads as a graded student writing exercise rather than a stakeholder filing. Marks of a 10: explicit assignment or student framing ('Final Assignment', 'we are masters students at X'); an opening that paraphrases the notice's own background back to the agency before opining; a five-paragraph thesis-and-restatement shape; hedged conviction verbs ('I believe') standing in for evidence; general-audience or commercial pages (law-firm blogs, marketing sites) cited as authority; statistics adjacent to but not about the rule; and no operational detail knowable only from inside the sector. A 5 is essayistic and general but carries one concrete sector-specific fact. A 0 supplies operational, regulatory, or technical detail from direct working knowledge and never gestures at the act of commenting itself.

--- CRITERION P03 ---
NAME: Implementation burden realism
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment grounds any claim of implementation burden in concrete constraints. A 10 explains specific staffing, timing, equipment, workflow, or cost constraints and relates them to what implementation would require. A 0 asserts burden only as inconvenience, with no constraint described. Score the concreteness of the constraints described, not how heavy the burden is said to be.

--- CRITERION P04 ---
NAME: Identifies a specific analytical error in the agency's own analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies a specific defect in the agency's own analysis. A 10 points to a particular step, assumption, figure, or table in that analysis and explains concretely why it is wrong or incomplete. A 0 makes no reference to the agency's analysis at all. Score whether a specific analytical defect is identified, not how forcefully it is argued.

--- CRITERION P05 ---
NAME: Comparative tradeoff analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment weighs plausible alternatives against one another. A 10 compares alternatives, states the advantages and disadvantages of each, and explains why the preferred option performs better. A 0 presents only a categorical conclusion with no alternative considered. Score the presence and completeness of the comparison, not which option the comment favors.

--- CRITERION P06 ---
NAME: Differentiated identification of affected stakeholder groups
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment distinguishes how the rule's impact differs across identified affected groups. A 10 names at least two distinct affected populations or entities and explains how the effect on them differs. A 0 refers only to a single undifferentiated group such as 'the public' or 'everyone', or does not identify who is affected at all. Score the differentiation actually stated in the text.

--- CRITERION P07 ---
NAME: Compliance feasibility grounded in how the work is done
INSTRUCTION: Score how concretely the comment shows what complying would actually require and uses that knowledge to evaluate the proposal. A 10 walks through the real sequence of work, access constraints, staffing, or timing and shows why the requirement is or is not achievable as written, ideally offering the achievable version. A 5 asserts that something is burdensome or unrealistic without describing the work; a 0 makes a demand with no account of how anyone would carry it out. Score detailed knowledge of the operation, not the force of the complaint.

--- CRITERION P08 ---
NAME: Scanned/faxed physical-document artifacts (OCR noise, stamps)
INSTRUCTION: Score the visible marks that the text originated from a scanned, faxed, or mailed physical document rather than a native digital web-form entry. A 10 shows clear, heavy artifacts of this kind: garbled OCR character strings, broken word-spacing from multi-column layout, date-stamped footers, or 'RECEIVED' or 'POSTMARKED' stamps. A 0 is clean native digital text with no scan or transmission artifacts. This is a surface-format feature; score the artifacts only.

--- CRITERION P09 ---
NAME: Substance survives deletion of the position statement
INSTRUCTION: Delete every sentence that states support, opposition, gratitude, feeling, or a purely procedural request, then score what remains. A 10 leaves a body of reasons, facts, or requested changes that stands on its own as regulatory input. A 5 leaves a thin residue: one reason, or a general consideration. A 0 leaves nothing, because the comment consisted entirely of a position, an attitude, an endorsement of someone else's filing, or a scheduling request.

--- CRITERION P10 ---
NAME: Monitoring and verification design
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment proposes a concrete way to tell whether the regulatory approach is functioning. A 10 specifies measurable monitoring, testing, reporting, feedback, or verification that would reveal how the approach is performing. A 0 asks for protection or compliance with no means of assessing it. Score the presence and specificity of the proposed check, not how important the underlying request seems.

--- CRITERION P11 ---
NAME: Bare mention of the phrase 'cost-benefit analysis'
INSTRUCTION: Score only whether the phrase 'cost-benefit analysis', 'regulatory impact analysis', or 'RIA' appears anywhere in the comment. A 10 means at least one of those phrases appears. A 0 means none of them appears. This is a surface word-presence check: do not consider whether the comment engages with any analysis.

--- CRITERION P12 ---
NAME: Detail-level concreteness of any proposed alternative
INSTRUCTION: Score how fully any alternative approach the comment proposes is specified in the text itself. A 10 lays out the mechanism -- steps, components, numbers, procedure -- in enough detail that another reader could implement or evaluate it. A 0 names an alternative without describing it, or points to a solution held outside the comment ('I already have one, contact me'). A comment proposing no alternative at all also scores 0.

--- CRITERION P13 ---
NAME: Causal mechanism linking action to stated goal
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment explains a specific causal chain connecting the proposed rule or action to the effect it claims will follow. A 10 traces an explicit mechanism ('X causes Y because Z') with enough specificity that a reader could evaluate whether the logic holds. A 0 states support, opposition, or a general value ('this is important', 'this is bad') with no explanation of how the action produces the claimed effect.

--- CRITERION P14 ---
NAME: States who would be affected and how, with concrete detail
INSTRUCTION: Score how concretely the comment states who would be affected and what would change for them. A 10 names the specific population, firms, or operations affected and describes in concrete terms what would be different for them under the rule. A 0 describes no affected party and no effect. Score the concreteness of the described effect, not how severe it is.

--- CRITERION P15 ---
NAME: Engages a named provision, alternative, or solicited question
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment both pinpoints something specific in the proposal -- a section, a defined alternative, a numeric threshold, a phrase, or a question the agency expressly asked -- and says what is wrong with it or answers it. A 10 does both, naming the exact target and giving the reason or answer, so the target is locatable without guesswork. A 5 pinpoints a target with only a bare preference attached, or reasons without landing on a locatable part. Pinpointing alone scores no higher than 4; a 0 addresses only the general subject matter of the rulemaking.

--- CRITERION P16 ---
NAME: Temporal and cumulative effects
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment's reasoning extends past immediate impacts to effects over time. A 10 analyzes delayed, repeated, cumulative, or long-term effects and says what unfolds over that horizon. A 0 confines its reasoning to a short-term assertion about immediate impact. Score the time horizon actually reasoned about in the text, not whether the analysis is correct.

--- CRITERION P17 ---
NAME: Quantified scale of real-world impact
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment supplies specific, position-relevant numbers establishing the scale of the problem or benefit the rule addresses. A 10 gives concrete, comment-specific figures -- dollar amounts, percentages, counts, timeframes -- that make the magnitude of the issue legible. A 0 gives no numbers at all, or only a generic or borrowed statistic not tied to the specific matter at hand. Score the specificity and relevance of the figures, not whether they favor any side.

--- CRITERION P18 ---
NAME: Concrete distributional effects
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies specifically affected or vulnerable groups and traces how they are exposed. A 10 names the group, describes its exposure or access pathway, and connects that pathway to a particular regulatory choice. A 0 refers only generically to the public, to fairness, or to unspecified harm. Score the concreteness of the group and the pathway described.

--- CRITERION P19 ---
NAME: Verifiable, attributable evidentiary sourcing
INSTRUCTION: Score the extent to which the comment's factual claims are attached to identifiable, checkable sources. A 10 gives specific citations to named studies, agency data, statutes, or official reports that actually support the claim made. A 0 states factual claims confidently with no source, or sources them to non-authoritative or unrelated material such as unnamed blogs, hearsay, or 'as I understand it'. Score the sourcing, not whether you agree with the claim.

--- CRITERION P20 ---
NAME: Numbered or bulleted list formatting
INSTRUCTION: Score only whether the comment is organised into numbered or bulleted items. A 10 uses numbered or bulleted list markers. A 0 is continuous prose with no list markers. This is a surface-formatting check: never consider the content of the items.

--- CRITERION P21 ---
NAME: First-hand local exposure
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the commenter describes a first-hand personal or local stake in the proposal. A 10 describes personal residence, occupation, direct exposure, or concrete local consequences tied to the proposal. A 0 contains no personal or geographically specific stake. Score the presence and concreteness of the stated first-hand connection, not how sympathetic it is.

--- CRITERION P22 ---
NAME: OCR and attachment provenance
INSTRUCTION: Score the presence of filing and document-wrapper artifacts in the text. A 10 shows document-wrapper language, duplicated covers, slide or page extraction, OCR corruption, attachment references, or similar filing residue. A 0 is clean native prose without such artifacts. This is a surface-format feature; score the artifacts only, not the substance around them.

--- CRITERION P23 ---
NAME: Pseudo-quantified authority
INSTRUCTION: Score how much quantified or attributed authority the comment accumulates as rhetorical support. A 10 piles up precise statistics, dollar amounts, percentages, named reports, studies, media links, or numerical forecasts. A 0 contains no quantified or attributed authority at all. This scores the presence and density of such material as it appears in the text, not whether it is accurate or well used.

--- CRITERION P24 ---
NAME: Credentialed insider self-positioning
INSTRUCTION: Score how prominently the author asserts credentialed standing in the regulated field. A 10 prominently states a professional license, a senior role, years of experience, or a close occupational relationship to that field. A 0 asserts no expertise or role. Score the self-positioning as stated in the text, not whether the credential is verified or relevant.

--- CRITERION P25 ---
NAME: Coordinated campaign residue
INSTRUCTION: Score the presence of mass-campaign language in the comment's text. A 10 contains recognizable repeated campaign phrasing, mass-appeal slogans, campaign metadata, or standardized calls to action. A 0 is clearly bespoke prose written for this specific docket. This is a surface textual marker: score the wording only, never the merit or sincerity of the comment.
