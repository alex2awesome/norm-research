You are auditing a pool of candidate scoring criteria written by several
independent analysts for judging PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal
agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores because
they name one concept in different words. Answer DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly
score high on one and low on the other -- including when the two are closely related,
overlapping, or members of the same family. Related is not the same.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "FP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR FP001 ---
X NAME: Continuity references to prior filings, meetings, or pending petitions
X INSTRUCTION: Score how strongly the comment presents itself as one turn in an ongoing exchange rather than a first and only contact. 10 = it explicitly cites its own earlier comment letters on related dockets, a pending petition it is supplementing, a dated meeting with agency staff, or an emailed agency request it is answering, with dates. 5 = a vague gesture at past involvement ('we have long supported', 'we continue to be on the record'). 0 = nothing in the text indicates the writer has ever contacted this agency before. Score the continuity references only; do not reward the substance being continued.
Y NAME: Coordination fingerprints: naming other commenters and incorporating their filings
Y INSTRUCTION: Score how much the text reveals that the writer has read the rest of the docket and is acting in concert with others. 10 = the comment names other submitters and answers them directly, adopts or incorporates another organization's comments by reference, is labeled a joint or 'et al.' submission, or is one numbered piece of a multi-part batch with a master index of exhibits. 5 = it points readers to allied organizations without engaging them. 0 = the comment shows no awareness that anyone else is commenting. Judge coordination evidence only, not the quality of the coordinated position.
