You are an editor for a bank of scoring criteria used to judge PUBLIC
COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each criterion
is scored 0-10 by an independent judge who sees ONLY one comment's text and ONE
criterion.

Rewrite EVERY criterion below into the bank's house style. House style is:

  * ONE scorable property per criterion, stated in the first sentence;
  * an explicit description of what a 10 looks like and what a 0 looks like;
  * judgeable from the comment text alone, by a reader with no outside information;
  * plain declarative sentences, 2-4 sentences, no more than ~80 words;
  * NO reference to any outcome (agency response, rule change, acceptance, approval);
  * NO instruction that tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document in place of
    its substance (e.g. "score the question-and-answer structure, not the importance of
    the question"). If the underlying property is substantive, the instruction must
    score the substance; if the underlying property is a surface feature, say so
    plainly and score the surface feature, without dressing it as merit.

HARD CONSTRAINT: preserve the CONCEPT exactly. You may sharpen, disambiguate and
anchor the wording. You may NOT broaden it, narrow it, or replace it with a nearby
idea. An independent judge must agree that the rewritten criterion measures the same
thing as the original.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"rewrites": [
  {"id": "<the id given below, unchanged>",
   "name": "<short name, <= 12 words; keep the original unless it is unclear>",
   "instruction": "<the rewritten 0-10 scoring instruction>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion below ...
]}


--- CRITERION A01 ---
NAME: Explicit causal-mechanism / behavioral-response reasoning
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 if the comment lays out a specific step-by-step causal chain (including, where relevant, how affected parties will change their behavior) connecting the rule to its claimed consequence, rather than merely asserting the consequence. Score 0 if the comment asserts a harm or benefit with no explanation of the mechanism producing it.

--- CRITERION A02 ---
NAME: Feasible alternative design
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment offers an implementable alternative, exception, threshold, or safeguard and explains how it preserves the proposal’s legitimate objective; score 0 when it offers only approval, rejection, or an unelaborated demand.

--- CRITERION A03 ---
NAME: Actionable amendment precision
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment supplies precise replacement language, thresholds, definitions, procedures, or parameters; score 0 when the requested action is only “do more,” “act quickly,” or approval/rejection.

--- CRITERION A04 ---
NAME: Operational implementability
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies responsible actors, workflows, training, system dependencies, or failure modes that determine whether the rule can work in practice; score 0 when implementation is treated as automatic.

--- CRITERION A05 ---
NAME: Legal authority and interpretive fit
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment accurately connects the proposal to statutory language, delegated authority, precedent, or applicable legal limits; score 0 when legal conclusions are asserted without textual or doctrinal support.

--- CRITERION A06 ---
NAME: Monitoring, verification, and feedback design
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment proposes measurable indicators, data collection, verification methods, reporting, or triggers for revisiting the approach; score 0 when it offers no way to test or track performance.

--- CRITERION A07 ---
NAME: Generalized firsthand evidence
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when personal experience supplies concrete observations and is translated into a rule-relevant, potentially generalizable pattern; score 0 when it is only anecdote, praise, or emotion without analyzable detail.

--- CRITERION A08 ---
NAME: Distributional incidence and equity
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies which populations, firms, places, or users bear distinct costs and benefits and explains unequal exposure or access; score 0 when effects are described only for an undifferentiated public.

--- CRITERION A09 ---
NAME: Rule-text pinpointing
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies an exact proposed provision and specifies a concrete revision; score 0 when it only expresses support or opposition without an identifiable rule-text target.

--- CRITERION A10 ---
NAME: Actionable procedural request with a reason tied to the record
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment asks the agency to do something procedural that is within its power, with a stated reason. 10 = a specific procedural action is requested — extend or reopen the comment period, defer action pending analysis, phase the effective date, re-notice, publish a tracking or timeline commitment — with a stated reason grounded in the record or in the requester's circumstances. 5 = process is criticized but no request is made, or a request is made with no reason. 0 = no procedural dimension.

--- CRITERION A11 ---
NAME: Evidence critique and uncertainty calibration
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment distinguishes strong evidence from weak evidence, identifies assumptions or limitations, and calibrates confidence; score 0 when it treats all claims as certain or ignores contrary evidence.

--- CRITERION A12 ---
NAME: Flags an ambiguous term and shows the interpretive consequence
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment isolates a specific word, phrase, or definition in the regulatory text and demonstrates that it admits more than one reading with different practical results. 10 = the term is quoted or cited to its section, at least two readings are laid out, and the divergent consequence of each is stated. 5 = the comment says a term is unclear or asks for a definition without showing what turns on it. 0 = no engagement with the operative text's wording.

--- CRITERION A13 ---
NAME: Names a specific omission or missing commitment in the proposal
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies something the proposal fails to say, define, decide, or commit to, at a level of specificity the agency could act on. 10 = the comment points to a particular gap — an unaddressed scenario, an undelivered commitment, an unassigned responsibility, an unanswered question the rule creates — and says what filling it would require. 5 = the comment says the proposal is incomplete or 'falls short' without locating the gap. 0 = the comment engages only what the proposal does say, or offers no gap analysis at all.

--- CRITERION A14 ---
NAME: Supplies data the agency does not already have in the record
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment supplies information -- measurements, operational records, survey results, field observations -- that the agency would not otherwise possess, and says where it comes from. Score 0 when the comment supplies no information beyond opinion. Judge whether new information enters the record.

--- CRITERION A15 ---
NAME: Explains why the proposed approach will not achieve its stated aim
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment gives a reasoned account of a mechanism by which the rule as proposed would fail to produce the effect the agency intends. Score 0 when no such account is given. Judge the reasoning, not the confidence.

--- CRITERION B01 ---
NAME: Agency-relationship disclosure
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the writer explicitly describes prior permits, recurring meetings, ongoing communications, or previous dealings with the agency; score 0 when no prior agency relationship is mentioned.

--- CRITERION B02 ---
NAME: Campaign template reuse
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when wording, headings, demands, or paragraph order closely duplicates another apparent submission; score 0 when the wording is clearly original and individualized.

--- CRITERION B03 ---
NAME: Submission-wrapper residue
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment contains abundant portal/email headers, attachment placeholders, filenames, OCR errors, duplicated cover text, or page artifacts; score 0 when it is clean, direct prose.

--- CRITERION B04 ---
NAME: Coalition consensus packaging
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment claims to represent many organizations, members, or a consensus process and names them; score 0 when it is clearly an individual submission.

--- CRITERION B05 ---
NAME: Personal anecdote as the sole evidentiary support
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 if the comment's entire argument rests on a first-person anecdote or personal experience ('I noticed...', 'in my case...') with no external citation, data, or source referenced anywhere in the text. Score 0 if the argument is built on external sources, data, regulatory text, or reasoning that does not depend on the writer's personal experience.

--- CRITERION B06 ---
NAME: Implementation-workflow specificity
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies concrete forms, responsible offices, transition steps, testing scenarios, timelines, or documentation requirements; score 0 when it states only a general preference.

--- CRITERION B07 ---
NAME: Institutional credential display
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment prominently advertises organizational size, market share, longevity, assets, employees, facilities, or senior titles; score 0 when it gives no institutional credentials.

--- CRITERION B08 ---
NAME: Regulatory-lawyer fluency
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment precisely maps proposed text to docket provisions, CFR sections, statutory authority, or requested edits; score 0 when it offers only broad approval or opposition.

--- CRITERION B09 ---
NAME: Bare presence of a numeric figure anywhere in the text
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when any digit-bearing quantity (a number, a percentage, a dollar amount, a date) appears anywhere in the comment. Score 0 when the comment contains no digits at all. Do not consider what the number is used for or whether it is correct.

--- CRITERION B10 ---
NAME: Formal salutation and signature block present
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment opens with a formal salutation ("Dear Administrator", "To Whom It May Concern") and closes with a signature block naming a person or organisation. Score 0 when neither is present. Score only the letter furniture.
