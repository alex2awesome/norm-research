You are an independent auditor. Below are candidate criteria for scoring
PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each
would be scored 0-10 by a judge reading only one comment's text.

Classify EACH criterion as exactly one of:

  "quality_relevant" -- it measures something a careful regulatory analyst would call
      substantive merit of the comment as regulatory input: evidence, analysis,
      specificity about the rule, feasibility, actionable alternatives, first-hand
      knowledge, engagement with the agency's reasoning.

  "incidental" -- it is predictive-but-not-merit: a surface or shape feature, a
      formatting or length proxy, boilerplate or template similarity, a topic or
      agency-style marker, a submitter-identity or professional-drafting fingerprint,
      a tone or register marker, or the bare presence of a word or phrase.

DECISION AIDS.
  * If the criterion could be scored high by a comment that says nothing substantive,
    it is incidental.
  * If the instruction tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document rather than
    what the document establishes, it is incidental -- even when the shape usually
    co-occurs with merit. Say so in your justification if you see this.
  * "Related to quality" is not enough. The question is what the JUDGE would be
    scoring when following this exact instruction.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"id": "P01", "label": "quality_relevant" or "incidental",
   "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "justification": "<one or two sentences>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion ...
]}


--- CRITERION P01 ---
NAME: Ask serves more than the commenter's own burden or exposure
INSTRUCTION: Score who benefits from the change the comment requests, as the comment itself frames it. 10 = the change would improve the rule's functioning for third parties, the protected resource, or the public, including where it plainly costs the commenter something. 5 = the ask helps the commenter, but the stated reasons are about workability for everyone. 0 = the ask is entirely relief for the submitter (delay, exemption, a higher threshold, more time), and its public-interest framing adds no independent content.

--- CRITERION P02 ---
NAME: Implementation realism
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment addresses what carrying out the proposal would practically require. 10 = it maps specific steps, resources, timing, personnel, or supply-chain conditions needed to implement the proposal. 0 = it ignores implementation realities and treats the proposal as self-executing.

--- CRITERION P03 ---
NAME: Cross-rule and system interaction
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment relates the proposal to another rule, program, document, or process. 10 = it identifies a specific overlap, contradiction, gap, or sequencing problem with that other instrument and specifies how to resolve it. 0 = it analyzes the proposal in isolation and refers to no other rule, program, document, or process.

--- CRITERION P04 ---
NAME: Tradeoff and side-effect analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment weighs the proposal's benefits against what it costs. 10 = it sets benefits against costs, risks, displaced protections, or unintended effects and states what the resulting tradeoff is. 0 = it presents one-sided approval or one-sided opposition, with nothing on the other side of the ledger.

--- CRITERION P05 ---
NAME: Embedded formal citation within personal-voice prose
INSTRUCTION: Score whether a formal citation sits embedded inside otherwise personal, informal, first-person prose. 10 = the narrating voice is personal and informal yet carries one or more formal legal or scholarly citations (a full case citation, a parenthetical citation to a named organization, a footnoted source) that read as inserted by another hand, creating a clear register shift. 0 = no such embedded citation appears, or the comment is uniformly formal or uniformly informal with no citations at all.

--- CRITERION P06 ---
NAME: Quantified supporting evidence
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment supports its position with numbers. 10 = it uses relevant numerical data, calculations, comparisons, or rates that bear on its position and are stated concretely enough to be checked. 0 = it offers no verifiable quantitative support at all.

--- CRITERION P07 ---
NAME: Credential and status parade
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment's opening prominently parades the writer's status markers. 10 = the opening prominently lists multiple degrees, certifications, fellowships, offices, titles, or years of experience. 0 = no status or expertise signals appear. This is a surface feature: score the display of the markers, not the writer's actual expertise.

--- CRITERION P08 ---
NAME: Distributional consequence analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies who bears distinct effects from the proposal. 10 = it names particular vulnerable, dependent, underserved, or differently situated groups and explains the mechanism by which those groups are affected differently. 0 = it invokes the broad public interest without identifying any affected group.

--- CRITERION P09 ---
NAME: Emotional advocacy intensity
INSTRUCTION: Score how intense the comment's emotional advocacy register is. 10 = repeated alarm, outrage, moral condemnation, emphatic praise, all-caps, exclamation marks, or vivid metaphors run through the text. 0 = the prose is consistently restrained and neutral. This is a surface feature: score the register, not whether the emotion is warranted.

--- CRITERION P10 ---
NAME: NGO action-alert templated phrasing
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment uses stock activist phrasing typical of NGO mass-comment action-alert tools. 10 = it strongly exhibits that templated register: formulaic gratitude closings ('THANK YOU for your commitment to...'), superlative-modified positions ('I HIGHLY support...', 'I strongly urge...'), and emphatic capitalized keywords, especially in a short, largely interchangeable one-fact-plus-a-plea structure. 0 = no such stock phrasing appears. This is a surface feature: score the phrasing, not the sincerity behind it.

--- CRITERION P11 ---
NAME: Credible alternative meeting the regulatory objective
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment offers a worked alternative to the proposal. 10 = it proposes a specific alternative, exception, phase-in, or substitute and explains why that option can achieve the same regulatory objective, naming the relevant tradeoffs. 0 = it only registers support for or opposition to the proposal as written.

--- CRITERION P12 ---
NAME: Formal itemized technical/regulatory rebuttal structure
INSTRUCTION: Score how far the comment is laid out as a formal point-by-point regulatory rebuttal. 10 = the whole comment is addressed to a named official or office and broken into discrete numbered or lettered items, each citing a specific rule paragraph, CFR subsection, AD or docket number, or service-bulletin reference, with little connective persuasive narrative between points. 0 = it is continuous persuasive prose with no addressee-directed itemized rule-section corrections.

--- CRITERION P13 ---
NAME: Text is entirely upper case
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment's text is written in capital letters. 10 = it is wholly or almost wholly in capitals. 0 = it uses ordinary sentence casing. Score only the letter casing.

--- CRITERION P14 ---
NAME: Causal mechanism from provision to consequence
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment traces a causal chain from the proposed provision to a consequence. 10 = it follows the provision through specific behavior or implementation steps to a safety, health, environmental, or administrative consequence, with each link stated. 0 = it asserts that harm or benefit will occur without naming any intervening step.

--- CRITERION P15 ---
NAME: Applies a controlling standard to the facts rather than citing it
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment works the proposal's facts through a governing legal, scientific, or professional standard. 10 = the standard is named, its elements are identified, and the proposal's facts are matched against those elements to show fit or misfit, with that result driving the recommendation. 5 = a standard is invoked and a conclusion asserted, with the application left implicit. 0 = authority or the notice is quoted or summarized at length in place of analysis, or no standard is engaged.

--- CRITERION P16 ---
NAME: States the conditions under which its own recommendation would fail
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment states limits on its own recommendation. 10 = it names circumstances in which its own proposed course of action would not work, or the evidence that would change its recommendation. 0 = it offers no such limits on its own claim.

--- CRITERION P17 ---
NAME: Audits the agency's own figures, record, or arithmetic
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment tests the agency's stated justification against numbers or statements the agency itself placed in the record. 10 = it reworks the agency's arithmetic, or sets the proposal against the agency's own admission or its own prior rule, and shows the figures so the check can be reproduced. 5 = it disputes a number but supplies no computation or source. 0 = the agency's figures are accepted or ignored, and the comment argues only from preference.

--- CRITERION P18 ---
NAME: Professional document apparatus
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the submission carries professional document-production apparatus. 10 = it contains elaborate exhibits, master indexes, slide decks, service-bulletin reproductions, revision histories, or attachment-heavy packaging. 0 = it is a plain standalone comment with no document-production signals. This is a surface feature: score the packaging, not the quality of what is packaged.

--- CRITERION P19 ---
NAME: Marks the limits of its evidence and reasons from them
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment separates what it knows from what it does not, and whether its recommendation follows from that separation. 10 = it states where the evidence runs out, names the specific unknowns, and gives a recommendation responsive to that uncertainty, such as precaution, a specified disclosure, or a question it says must be resolved first. 5 = uncertainty is acknowledged in passing and changes nothing. 0 = uniform certainty regardless of basis, or hedging with no consequence attached.

--- CRITERION P20 ---
NAME: Explicit evidence-to-requested-remedy linkage
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment draws an explicit, traceable line from the evidence or reasoning it presents to the exact provision, number, or action it asks be changed. 10 = the requested remedy is derived step by step from the stated evidence, so the derivation can be followed. 0 = evidence or complaint and a specific ask sit as disconnected pieces, with no traceable link between them.

--- CRITERION P21 ---
NAME: Docket number or RIN quoted verbatim
INSTRUCTION: Score whether a docket identifier or RIN appears verbatim in the comment. 10 = such an identifier string is present in the text. 0 = neither a docket number nor a RIN appears. Score only whether the identifier string is present, never whether the comment addresses the right rule.

--- CRITERION P22 ---
NAME: Identifies an internal inconsistency between parts of the proposal
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment shows a specific conflict inside the proposal. 10 = it points to two parts of the proposed rule or its preamble that cannot both hold and says why they cannot. 0 = no inconsistency is identified, or a conflict is alleged without naming the two parts that clash.

--- CRITERION P23 ---
NAME: Surfaces an affected class or case the proposal overlooked
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies a specific category of person, entity, or operating situation that the proposal's text does not appear to contemplate, or contemplates wrongly. 10 = a concrete class or edge case is named, its exclusion or misclassification is tied to specific proposal language, and the resulting harm or absurdity is stated. 5 = a gap is gestured at, but neither the class nor the consequence is made concrete. 0 = the comment discusses only the population the notice already addresses.

--- CRITERION P24 ---
NAME: Agency-relationship cues
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment refers to a prior or ongoing relationship with the agency. 10 = it mentions prior meetings, ongoing collaboration, earlier submissions, established partnerships, or direct exchanges with agency personnel. 0 = it carries only a generic salutation and no relationship history. This is a surface feature: score the presence of the cues, not what they imply about the comment's worth.

--- CRITERION P25 ---
NAME: First-person credential or stake self-disclosure
INSTRUCTION: Score how explicitly and prominently the comment establishes the writer's own identity, credentials, professional role, or stake, as in 'I am a Professor...', 'I have X years of experience...', 'I represent...', or naming a degree, certification, or affiliation. 10 = the comment opens with or centers on an extended personal or professional credential or identity statement. 0 = no self-identifying credential or stake language appears at all. Score the disclosure itself, whether or not that identity is load-bearing for the argument.
