You are auditing edits to a bank of scoring criteria for PUBLIC COMMENTS
submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores. Answer
DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly score high on one and low on the other --
including when one is a broadened, narrowed, or shifted version of the other. A
sharper wording of the same property is SAME; a different property is DIFFERENT.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "GP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}

Score 5 for an oblique or single-clause reference to earlier contact. Score 0 when no prior agency relationship is mentioned anywhere in the text. Whether the disclosed relationship lends authority, and whether anything follows from it, are outside this criterion.

Markers: template or boilerplate phrasing with a small personalised insert; crowd framing ('I join the thousands of Americans', 'add my name'); calls for the reader or others to act, sign, or share; head-count or vote-count framing; slogan or campaign-title language; a single short paragraph of sentiment with no rule-specific detail, citation, or factual claim; a salutation addressed to a cause or the public rather than the agency. Score 10 for several such markers, unmistakably a campaign artifact; 5 for individually written but clearly campaign-prompted text; 0 for rule-specific, individualised text addressed to the agency. Do not score the position taken.
Y NAME: Mass-campaign petition register
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on how strongly the text reads as one voice in an organized mass-participation drive rather than an individually composed submission to a decisionmaker. Markers: template or form-letter phrasing and boilerplate blocks with a small personalized insert; explicit crowd framing ('I join the thousands of Americans', 'add my name'); direct calls for the reader or others to act, sign, or share; head-count or vote-count framing ('count me among those opposed', 'the public has spoken'); slogan or campaign-title language; a single short paragraph of personal sentiment with no rule-specific detail, section citation, or factual claim; salutation addressed to a cause or to the public rather than to the agency. 10 = several of these, unmistakably a campaign artifact. 5 = individually written but clearly campaign-prompted (e.g., recites a campaign talking point and urges others to comment). 0 = no campaign markers at all: rule-specific, individualized, addressed to the agency as a decisionmaker. Do not reward or penalize the position taken.

X NAME: Reconciles conflicting evidence rather than citing one side
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment acknowledges evidence pointing the other way and explains how to reconcile it with its own position. Score 0 when only supporting evidence is presented. Judge whether conflicting evidence is engaged.
Y NAME: Reconciles conflicting evidence rather than citing one side
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment engages evidence pointing against its own position. Score 0 when only supporting evidence is presented. Judge the engagement with conflicting evidence, not which side of the question is right.
