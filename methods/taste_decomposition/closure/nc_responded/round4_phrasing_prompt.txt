You are an editor for a bank of scoring criteria used to judge PUBLIC
COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each criterion
is scored 0-10 by an independent judge who sees ONLY one comment's text and ONE
criterion.

Rewrite EVERY criterion below into the bank's house style. House style is:

  * ONE scorable property per criterion, stated in the first sentence;
  * an explicit description of what a 10 looks like and what a 0 looks like;
  * judgeable from the comment text alone, by a reader with no outside information;
  * plain declarative sentences, 2-4 sentences, no more than ~80 words;
  * NO reference to any outcome (agency response, rule change, acceptance, approval);
  * NO instruction that tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document in place of
    its substance (e.g. "score the question-and-answer structure, not the importance of
    the question"). If the underlying property is substantive, the instruction must
    score the substance; if the underlying property is a surface feature, say so
    plainly and score the surface feature, without dressing it as merit.

HARD CONSTRAINT: preserve the CONCEPT exactly. You may sharpen, disambiguate and
anchor the wording. You may NOT broaden it, narrow it, or replace it with a nearby
idea. An independent judge must agree that the rewritten criterion measures the same
thing as the original.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"rewrites": [
  {"id": "<the id given below, unchanged>",
   "name": "<short name, <= 12 words; keep the original unless it is unclear>",
   "instruction": "<the rewritten 0-10 scoring instruction>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion below ...
]}


--- CRITERION A01 ---
NAME: Specific, precise proposed regulatory fix
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 how concretely the comment specifies what should change: exact replacement text, a specific numeric threshold, or a precise procedural modification, versus a general expression of support or opposition. A 10: the comment proposes exact substitute wording, a specific number, or an unambiguous procedural change the agency could adopt directly. A 0: the comment states only a general 'for' or 'against' position with no specific alternative.

--- CRITERION A02 ---
NAME: Traceable causal chain from the rule to the claimed effect
INSTRUCTION: Score whether consequences are connected to the rule by a mechanism a reader can follow and check. 10 = intermediate steps are named, so the claim is falsifiable (the rule permits A, which changes B, which produces C, which matters because D). 5 = one link is stated and the rest assumed. 0 = consequences are asserted as bare outcomes with no pathway ('this will destroy the program', 'this will endanger everyone'). Judge the presence and checkability of the chain, not whether you agree with its conclusion.

--- CRITERION A03 ---
NAME: Viable alternative design
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment offers a practicable alternative regulatory design that advances the stated objective while addressing the identified defect; score 0 when it offers no alternative beyond withdrawing or enforcing the proposal.

--- CRITERION A04 ---
NAME: Traceable Evidence Use
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when claims are supported by identifiable studies, datasets, statutes, official analyses, or clearly described observations whose relevance and limitations are explained; score 0 when claims are unsupported or rely on vague references.

--- CRITERION A05 ---
NAME: Acknowledgment of tradeoffs and counterarguments
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 whether the comment engages with costs, competing interests, or plausible reasons the agency might favor the opposite position, versus being purely one-sided. A 10: the comment explicitly identifies and weighs a genuine tradeoff or counter-consideration even while disagreeing. A 0: the comment shows no awareness that any opposing consideration exists.

--- CRITERION A06 ---
NAME: Names a specific omission in the agency's supporting analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment engages the agency's own record rather than only its own position. 10 = it points to a particular thing the notice, preamble, or impact analysis failed to address - an unexamined failure mode, an uncosted burden, a population or entity class not analyzed, a required analysis not performed, a study that does not exist - and says why that omission bears on the decision. 5 = it gestures at inadequate analysis without locating the gap. 0 = the agency's record never enters; the comment argues only for its preferred outcome.

--- CRITERION A07 ---
NAME: Operational Feasibility
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment explains how affected parties would actually implement, administer, document, or comply with the proposed approach and identifies concrete operational obstacles; score 0 when implementation is discussed only abstractly.

--- CRITERION A08 ---
NAME: Unintended-consequence safeguards
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies a plausible secondary failure or harm and proposes a concrete safeguard, monitoring step, exception, or contingency; score 0 when it predicts harm without explaining the pathway or mitigation.

--- CRITERION A09 ---
NAME: Field observation that disconfirms a factual premise of the rule
INSTRUCTION: Score whether firsthand experience is aimed at a premise rather than at a preference. 10 = the commenter reports something they have directly observed, operated, or measured that contradicts a specific factual assumption the agency is relying on (a stock estimate, an occupancy map, an achievability assumption, an exposure estimate), and identifies which assumption it contradicts. 5 = genuinely relevant firsthand experience offered as grounds for a preference, without confronting any stated premise. 0 = no firsthand observation, or observation with no bearing on any premise.

--- CRITERION A10 ---
NAME: Challenge to agency assumptions
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies a specific premise, dataset, model, or inference in the proposal and presents concrete evidence or reasoning that could disprove or materially revise it; score 0 when it merely disagrees with the conclusion.

--- CRITERION A11 ---
NAME: Identifies a conflict with operative statutory text
INSTRUCTION: Score legal grounding by whether a conflict is demonstrated. 10 = the comment quotes or precisely cites the controlling provision and shows the proposal cannot be squared with it, or that it triggers a procedure the statute requires and the notice omitted. 5 = a relevant statute or standard is cited but the tension is asserted rather than shown. 0 = no legal grounding, or citations used as decoration. Score the demonstrated conflict, not the number or formatting of citations.

--- CRITERION A12 ---
NAME: Tests whether the chosen instrument will actually bind
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment interrogates the mechanism, not just the policy direction. 10 = it asks or shows whether the instrument will change behavior at all - whether a numeric limit sits above observed practice, whether a threshold will ever be triggered, whether the voluntary approach has already been tried and failed, whether the model or measurement underneath the standard can resolve the effect being regulated - and supports the answer. 5 = it questions stringency without examining whether the instrument operates. 0 = it argues only that the policy should be stronger or weaker.

--- CRITERION A13 ---
NAME: Reasoning-based persuasion vs rhetorical amplification
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 whether the comment's persuasive force comes from developed reasoning and evidence, versus relying on repetition, capitalization, insults, or hyperbole in place of an argument. A 10: persuasive force rests entirely on developed reasoning/evidence delivered in a measured tone, even when the position is strongly held. A 0: the comment relies on capitalized/repeated demands, insults, or hyperbolic rhetoric as a substitute for developing any argument.

--- CRITERION A14 ---
NAME: Ties a requested change to a specific paragraph of regulatory text
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment states what it wants changed AND anchors that request to an identified paragraph, section or table of the proposed text. Score 0 when no change is requested or the request floats free of any provision. Judge the anchoring of the request.

--- CRITERION A15 ---
NAME: Distinguishes what the rule requires from what it merely permits
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment correctly separates mandatory from discretionary elements of the proposal and reasons about the difference. Score 0 when it treats the proposal undifferentiated. Judge whether the distinction is drawn and used.

--- CRITERION B01 ---
NAME: Organizational scale self-credentialing
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment foregrounds quantified membership, customers, assets, market share, national reach, or institutional prestige; score 0 when it makes no organizational-scale claims.

--- CRITERION B02 ---
NAME: Docket-lineage familiarity
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment narrates prior filings, earlier amendments, pilot participation, waivers, agency precedents, or repeated involvement in the same regulatory process; score 0 when it treats the docket as entirely new.

--- CRITERION B03 ---
NAME: Litigation-brief posture
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment adopts an adversarial legal-brief structure with claims of statutory conflict, plain-text mandates, legal jeopardy, formal violations, and requested legal remedies; score 0 when it lacks that posture.

--- CRITERION B04 ---
NAME: Unsolicited commendation of agency staff and process
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on how much the comment praises the agency itself - its staff, its effort, its priorities, or the rulemaking process - beyond the pro-forma 'we appreciate the opportunity to comment' line, which does not count. Score 10 for explicit commendation of the work or the workers ('kudos to the staff who undertook such a massive job', 'Service staff are to be commended', 'thanks to the Department for balancing the mandate with industry concerns', 'thank you for your dedication and drive', 'Great initiative'). Score 5 for a single warm sentence about the proposal being a step in the right direction. Score 0 for no praise of the agency anywhere. Do not count praise of the underlying policy goal by third parties, and do not count the ritual opening thanks.

--- CRITERION B05 ---
NAME: Submission-production residue
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when visible text contains OCR corruption, duplicated headers, raw email routing, attachment placeholders, odd spacing, extraction errors, or other ingest artifacts; score 0 when the prose is cleanly presented.

--- CRITERION B06 ---
NAME: Operational implementation microdetail
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment supplies granular workflow, equipment, filing, validation, staffing, transition, or compliance-process details from inside an affected operation; score 0 when it offers only broad policy preferences or values.

--- CRITERION B07 ---
NAME: Emotional-hook opening sentence before substantive content
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment opens with a short, punchy, emotionally-loaded sentence or fragment functioning as a headline/attention-grabber (a declarative claim of personal harm, moral outrage, or a rhetorical jab) before any substantive policy content appears. 10 = a clear stand-alone emotional hook line opens the comment, disconnected from any immediately following factual support. 0 = the comment opens directly with a substantive claim, fact, or procedural statement, with no rhetorical hook.

--- CRITERION B08 ---
NAME: Formal institutional filing apparatus / letterhead conventions
INSTRUCTION: Score the density of formal legal/regulatory filing conventions: an addressee block, a 'Re:' case caption with docket/RIN number, numbered footnotes describing the association's membership or assets, a formal salutation ('Dear Sir or Madam'), and a closing with a professional title. 10 = full law-firm/trade-association filing apparatus present (address block + Re: caption + footnoted membership stats + titled signature block). 0 = plain conversational text with no address block, no formal salutation, and no filing conventions.

--- CRITERION B09 ---
NAME: Mentions the number of members or constituents represented
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment states how many members, employees, customers or constituents the submitter speaks for. Score 0 when no such count appears. Score only the presence of the constituency claim, never whether it makes the argument stronger.

--- CRITERION B10 ---
NAME: Repeats the same sentence or phrase multiple times
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when a sentence or phrase is repeated verbatim three or more times in the comment. Score 0 when there is no verbatim repetition. Score only the repetition.
