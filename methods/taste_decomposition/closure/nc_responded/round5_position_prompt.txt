You are proposing SUSPECTED-SPURIOUS channels for scoring PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to
United States federal agencies on proposed rules (the notice-and-comment process).

This brief is narrow and deliberate. An audit across several corpora in this research
program found that criterion proposers systematically NEVER name one family of
predictive-but-not-merit features unprompted: POSITION IN CONTAINER — where an item
sits in the ordered collection it belongs to. In a patent, that was the ordinal
position of a claim. In a code corpus, it was recency. Nobody proposed either; both
were found only by manual audit, and both turned out to be strongly predictive.

For public comments the container is the DOCKET: the agency's numbered collection of
all comments filed on one proposed rule, opened when the rule is published and closed
at the end of the comment period. A comment therefore has a position in it — early or
late in the comment period, early or late in the sequence of filings, and it sits in a
docket that is either tiny or enormous.

YOUR TASK. Propose exactly 5 channels that are TEXTUAL FINGERPRINTS of a comment's
position in its docket. You may NOT ask the judge to know the position — the judge
sees only the comment's text. You must propose what the text would LOOK LIKE if the
comment were early or late, or if its docket were large or small.

Think about, and go beyond, these starting points:
  * deadline and timing language ("before the close of the comment period", "we
    request an extension", "filed timely", "supplemental comments");
  * awareness of the record so far (references to other commenters, to a public
    hearing already held, to the agency's responses to earlier submissions, to
    testimony, to a coalition letter already filed);
  * first-mover vs follower framing (introducing the issue from scratch versus
    endorsing, echoing, or incorporating by reference something already said);
  * signals that the docket is a mass-participation one (campaign framing, "join the
    thousands", petition language) versus a specialist one (assumption of a small
    expert readership, undefined technical shorthand);
  * re-opened or extended comment periods, corrections, and re-submissions.

HARD CONSTRAINTS.
1. LABEL-BLIND. No channel may reference agency responses, rule changes, or any other
   outcome variable.
2. Each channel must be SCORABLE 0-10 by an independent judge reading ONLY the comment
   text, with a clear 10 and a clear 0.
3. TAG each channel with `upstream_parent` (the position/timing fact you conjecture
   produces the fingerprint) and `mixed` (true if that fact plausibly also causes real
   comment quality).
4. Aim for 5 DISTINCT channels. Do not restate one idea five ways.

OUTPUT FORMAT. Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"criteria": [
  {"id": "Q01",
   "name": "<short channel name, <= 12 words>",
   "instruction": "<the 0-10 scoring instruction an independent judge would follow; say what a 10 looks like and what a 0 looks like>",
   "upstream_parent": "<the position/timing fact you conjecture causes this fingerprint>",
   "mixed": true or false,
   "rationale": "<why you expect this to be predictive without being merit>"}
  ... exactly 5 entries ...
]}
