You are an editor for a bank of scoring criteria used to judge PUBLIC
COMMENTS submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules. Each criterion
is scored 0-10 by an independent judge who sees ONLY one comment's text and ONE
criterion.

Rewrite EVERY criterion below into the bank's house style. House style is:

  * ONE scorable property per criterion, stated in the first sentence;
  * an explicit description of what a 10 looks like and what a 0 looks like;
  * judgeable from the comment text alone, by a reader with no outside information;
  * plain declarative sentences, 2-4 sentences, no more than ~80 words;
  * NO reference to any outcome (agency response, rule change, acceptance, approval);
  * NO instruction that tells the judge to score the SHAPE of the document in place of
    its substance (e.g. "score the question-and-answer structure, not the importance of
    the question"). If the underlying property is substantive, the instruction must
    score the substance; if the underlying property is a surface feature, say so
    plainly and score the surface feature, without dressing it as merit.

HARD CONSTRAINT: preserve the CONCEPT exactly. You may sharpen, disambiguate and
anchor the wording. You may NOT broaden it, narrow it, or replace it with a nearby
idea. An independent judge must agree that the rewritten criterion measures the same
thing as the original.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"rewrites": [
  {"id": "<the id given below, unchanged>",
   "name": "<short name, <= 12 words; keep the original unless it is unclear>",
   "instruction": "<the rewritten 0-10 scoring instruction>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY criterion below ...
]}


--- CRITERION A01 ---
NAME: Verifiable, attributable evidentiary sourcing
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether factual claims used to support the comment's position are attached to identifiable, checkable sources (named studies, agency data, statutes, official reports) as opposed to vague, unattributed, or dubious sourcing (unnamed blogs, hearsay, 'as I understand it'). A 10: specific citations to checkable sources that actually support the claim made. A 0: confident factual claims with no source, or sourced to non-authoritative/unrelated material.

--- CRITERION A02 ---
NAME: Detail-level concreteness of any proposed alternative
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether, when the comment proposes an alternative approach, the mechanism is specified in enough detail (steps, components, numbers, procedure) that someone else could implement or evaluate it -- versus a vague gesture toward an unseen or undescribed solution. A 10: a fully specified alternative mechanism laid out in the text itself. A 0: an alternative is mentioned in name only, or the commenter points to a solution that exists outside the comment ('I already have one, contact me') without describing it.

--- CRITERION A03 ---
NAME: Causal mechanism linking action to stated goal
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether the comment explains a specific causal chain connecting the proposed rule/action to the outcome it claims will follow, rather than merely asserting a stance. A 10: the comment traces an explicit mechanism (e.g., 'X causes Y because Z') with enough specificity that a reader could evaluate whether the logic holds. A 0: the comment states support/opposition or a general value ('this is important', 'this is bad') with no explanation of how the action produces the claimed effect.

--- CRITERION A04 ---
NAME: Engages a named provision, alternative, or solicited question
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment fastens onto something specific in the proposal (a section, a defined alternative, a numeric threshold, a phrase, a question the agency expressly asked) AND says what is wrong with it or answers it. Both halves are required: a 10 both identifies the exact target and gives a reason or an answer, so the agency could act on the comment without guessing which provision is at issue. A 5 identifies a target with only a bare preference attached, or gives reasoning that never lands on a locatable part of the proposal. A 0 addresses only the general subject matter of the rulemaking. Pinpointing alone, with no stated reason, does not earn above a 4.

--- CRITERION A05 ---
NAME: Comparative tradeoff analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment compares plausible alternatives, states their advantages and disadvantages, and explains why the preferred option performs better; score 0 when it presents only a categorical conclusion.

--- CRITERION A06 ---
NAME: Temporal and cumulative effects
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment analyzes delayed, repeated, cumulative, or long-term effects rather than only immediate impacts; score 0 when its reasoning is confined to a short-term assertion.

--- CRITERION A07 ---
NAME: Concrete Distributional Effects
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies specifically affected or vulnerable groups, describes their exposure or access pathway, and connects that pathway to a regulatory choice; score 0 for generic references to the public or fairness.

--- CRITERION A08 ---
NAME: Monitoring and Verification Design
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment proposes measurable monitoring, testing, reporting, feedback, or verification that would reveal whether the regulatory approach is functioning; score 0 when it asks for protection or compliance without a way to assess it.

--- CRITERION A09 ---
NAME: Quantified scale of real-world impact
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether the comment supplies specific, position-relevant numbers (dollar figures, percentages, counts, timeframes) that establish the scale of the problem or benefit the rule addresses. A 10: concrete, comment-specific figures that make the magnitude of the issue legible. A 0: no numbers at all, or only a generic/borrowed statistic not tied to the specific matter at hand.

--- CRITERION A10 ---
NAME: Implementation burden realism
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment explains concrete staffing, timing, equipment, workflow, or cost constraints and relates them to implementation; score 0 when burden is asserted only as inconvenience.

--- CRITERION A11 ---
NAME: Differentiated identification of affected stakeholder groups
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether the comment identifies which specific populations or entities are affected by the rule and distinguishes how the impact differs across them, rather than referring only to an undifferentiated 'the public' or 'everyone'. A 10: names at least two distinct affected groups and explains how the rule's effect on them differs. A 0: refers only to a single undifferentiated affected group or does not identify who is affected at all.

--- CRITERION A12 ---
NAME: Substance survives deletion of the position statement
INSTRUCTION: Apply this test: delete every sentence that states support, opposition, gratitude, feeling, or a purely procedural request, and score what remains. A 10 leaves behind a body of reasons, facts, or requested changes that stands on its own as regulatory input. A 5 leaves a thin residue: one reason, or a general consideration. A 0 leaves nothing, because the comment consisted entirely of a position, an attitude, an endorsement of someone else's filing, or a scheduling request. This targets comments that carry every outward marker of seriousness while contributing no content.

--- CRITERION A13 ---
NAME: Compliance feasibility grounded in how the work is done
INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment demonstrates concrete knowledge of what complying would actually require, and uses it to evaluate the proposal. A 10 walks through the real sequence of work, access constraints, staffing, or timing and shows why the proposed requirement is or is not achievable as written, ideally proposing the achievable version. A 5 asserts that something is burdensome or unrealistic without describing the work. A 0 makes a demand with no account of how anyone would carry it out. Detailed knowledge of the operation, not the strength of the complaint, is what is being scored.

--- CRITERION A14 ---
NAME: Identifies a specific analytical error in the agency's own analysis
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment points to a specific step, assumption, figure or table in the agency's analysis and explains concretely why it is wrong or incomplete. Score 0 when the comment makes no reference to the agency's analysis at all. Judge whether a specific analytical defect is identified, not how forcefully.

--- CRITERION A15 ---
NAME: States who would be affected and how, with concrete detail
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment names the specific population, firms or operations affected and describes concretely what would change for them under the rule. Score 0 when no affected party or effect is described. Judge the concreteness of the described effect.

--- CRITERION B01 ---
NAME: Coordinated Campaign Residue
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment contains recognizable repeated campaign language, mass-appeal slogans, campaign metadata, or standardized calls to action; score 0 when the prose is clearly bespoke and docket-specific.

--- CRITERION B02 ---
NAME: Scanned/faxed physical-document artifacts (OCR noise, stamps)
INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on visible marks that the text originated from a scanned, faxed, or mailed physical document rather than a native digital web-form entry: garbled OCR character strings, broken word-spacing from multi-column layout, date-stamped footers, or 'RECEIVED'/'POSTMARKED' stamps. A 10 shows clear, heavy artifacts of this kind. A 0 is clean native digital text with no scan or transmission artifacts.

--- CRITERION B03 ---
NAME: OCR and Attachment Provenance
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the text shows document-wrapper language, duplicated covers, slide or page extraction, OCR corruption, attachment references, or other filing artifacts; score 0 when it is clean native prose without such artifacts.

--- CRITERION B04 ---
NAME: Reads as a graded course assignment rather than a stakeholder filing
INSTRUCTION: Score how much the comment resembles a student writing exercise. Marks of a 10: explicit assignment or student framing (Final Assignment, we are masters students at X); an opening that paraphrases the notice's own background section back to the agency before opining; a five-paragraph shape with a thesis and a restating close; hedged conviction verbs (I believe, I strongly believe) standing in for evidence; general-audience or commercial web pages cited as authority (law-firm blogs, marketing sites); statistics quoted that are adjacent to but not about the rule; and no operational detail the writer could only know from inside the sector. 5 = essayistic and general but with one concrete sector-specific fact. 0 = the writer supplies operational, regulatory or technical detail from direct working knowledge and never gestures at the act of commenting itself.

--- CRITERION B05 ---
NAME: Pseudo-Quantified Authority
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment accumulates precise statistics, dollar amounts, percentages, named reports, studies, media links, or numerical forecasts as rhetorical support; score 0 when it contains no quantified or attributed authority.

--- CRITERION B06 ---
NAME: Professional regulatory drafting
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the text has polished counsel-style organization, precise docket and provision references, issue-by-issue proposed edits, and administrability framing; score 0 when it is informal, unsystematic, and nontechnical.

--- CRITERION B07 ---
NAME: Credentialed Insider Self-Positioning
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the author prominently states a professional license, senior role, years of experience, or close occupational relationship to the regulated field; score 0 when no expertise or role is asserted.

--- CRITERION B08 ---
NAME: First-hand local exposure
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the commenter describes personal residence, occupation, direct exposure, or concrete local consequences tied to the proposal; score 0 when the comment contains no personal or geographically specific stake.

--- CRITERION B09 ---
NAME: Bare mention of the phrase 'cost-benefit analysis'
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the phrase 'cost-benefit analysis', 'regulatory impact analysis' or 'RIA' appears anywhere in the comment. Score 0 when none of those phrases appears. Do not consider whether the comment engages with any analysis -- score only the presence of the phrase.

--- CRITERION B10 ---
NAME: Numbered or bulleted list formatting
INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment is organised into numbered or bulleted items. Score 0 when it is continuous prose with no list markers. Score only the visual organisation, never the content of the items.
