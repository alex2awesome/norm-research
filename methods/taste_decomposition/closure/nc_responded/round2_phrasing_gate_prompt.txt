You are auditing edits to a bank of scoring criteria for PUBLIC COMMENTS
submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores. Answer
DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly score high on one and low on the other --
including when one is a broadened, narrowed, or shifted version of the other. A
sharper wording of the same property is SAME; a different property is DIFFERENT.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "GP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR GP001 ---
X NAME: Feasible alternative design
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment offers an implementable alternative to the proposal. Score 10 when it proposes a concrete alternative, exception, threshold, or safeguard and explains how that variant still serves the proposal's stated legitimate objective. Score 0 when it offers only support, opposition, or an unelaborated demand. Score in between when an alternative is named but its fit to the objective is left unexplained.
Y NAME: Feasible alternative design
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment offers an implementable alternative, exception, threshold, or safeguard and explains how it preserves the proposal’s legitimate objective; score 0 when it offers only approval, rejection, or an unelaborated demand.

--- PAIR GP002 ---
X NAME: Monitoring, verification, and feedback design
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment proposes measurable indicators, data collection, verification methods, reporting, or triggers for revisiting the approach; score 0 when it offers no way to test or track performance.
Y NAME: Monitoring, verification, and feedback design
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment proposes a way to check how the approach performs over time. Score 10 when it specifies measurable indicators, data collection, verification methods, reporting, or triggers for revisiting the approach. Score 0 when it offers no way to test or track performance at all.

--- PAIR GP003 ---
X NAME: Offers an actionable substitute regulatory design
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when an alternative approach is described concretely enough to be implemented; 0 when the comment proposes nothing.
Y NAME: Names a specific implementable alternative
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment sets out a concrete different design the agency could adopt, specific enough to write into the rule; 0 when it only objects.

--- PAIR GP004 ---
X NAME: Personal anecdote as the sole evidentiary support
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment's entire argument rests on first-person anecdote. Score 10 when every supporting claim comes from the writer's own experience (I noticed…, in my case…), with no external citation, data, or source referenced anywhere in the text. Score 0 when the argument is built on external sources, data, regulatory text, or reasoning that does not depend on the writer's personal experience.
Y NAME: Personal anecdote as the sole evidentiary support
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 if the comment's entire argument rests on a first-person anecdote or personal experience ('I noticed...', 'in my case...') with no external citation, data, or source referenced anywhere in the text. Score 0 if the argument is built on external sources, data, regulatory text, or reasoning that does not depend on the writer's personal experience.

Y NAME: Agency-relationship disclosure

Y NAME: Supplies data the agency does not already have in the record

--- PAIR GP010 ---

Y NAME: Quantifies the rule's cost using the commenter's own data

Y NAME: Legal authority and interpretive fit

--- PAIR GP012 ---
X NAME: Generalized firsthand evidence
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when personal experience supplies concrete observations and is translated into a rule-relevant, potentially generalizable pattern; score 0 when it is only anecdote, praise, or emotion without analyzable detail.
Y NAME: Generalized firsthand evidence
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether firsthand experience is reported concretely and carried up to a general pattern. Score 10 when the writer gives specific observations from personal experience and translates them into a rule-relevant pattern that could hold beyond the writer's own case. Score 0 when the personal material is only anecdote, praise, or emotion, with no analyzable detail.

Score 0 when effects are described only for an undifferentiated public.
Y NAME: Distributional incidence and equity

--- PAIR GP014 ---
X NAME: Campaign template reuse
X INSTRUCTION: Score how strongly the text reads as reproduced from a shared campaign template. Score 10 when the wording, headings, demands, or paragraph order appear duplicated from a circulated form submission, with little individualized content. Score 0 when the wording is clearly original and specific to this writer. This is a surface feature of the wording, scored as such and not as a judgment of quality.
Y NAME: Campaign template reuse
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when wording, headings, demands, or paragraph order closely duplicates another apparent submission; score 0 when the wording is clearly original and individualized.

--- PAIR GP015 ---
X NAME: Submission-wrapper residue
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment contains abundant portal/email headers, attachment placeholders, filenames, OCR errors, duplicated cover text, or page artifacts; score 0 when it is clean, direct prose.
Y NAME: Submission-wrapper residue
Y INSTRUCTION: Score how much submission-wrapper residue the text carries. Score 10 when portal or email headers, attachment placeholders, filenames, OCR errors, duplicated cover text, or page artifacts appear abundantly throughout. Score 0 when the text is clean, direct prose containing none of this material. This is a surface feature of the document: score how much residue is present, nothing else.

--- PAIR GP016 ---

Y NAME: Rule-text pinpointing

--- PAIR GP018 ---
X NAME: Formal salutation and signature block present

--- PAIR GP020 ---
X NAME: Actionable amendment precision

Y NAME: Actionable amendment precision

--- PAIR GP021 ---
X NAME: Operational implementability
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies responsible actors, workflows, training, system dependencies, or failure modes that determine whether the rule can work in practice; score 0 when implementation is treated as automatic.
Y NAME: Operational implementability

Score 0 when implementation is treated as automatic, with no attention to who would do what or what could break.

--- PAIR GP023 ---
X NAME: Names a specific omission or missing commitment in the proposal
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies something the proposal fails to say, define, decide, or commit to, at a level of specificity an agency could act on. Score 10 when it points to a particular gap — an unaddressed scenario, an undelivered commitment, an unassigned responsibility, an unanswered question the rule creates — and says what filling it would require. Score 5 when it calls the proposal incomplete without locating the gap. Score 0 when it engages only what the proposal does say.
Y NAME: Names a specific omission or missing commitment in the proposal

--- PAIR GP026 ---
X NAME: Actionable procedural request with a reason tied to the record
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment asks the agency to take a procedural step within its power and states a reason. Y NAME: Actionable procedural request with a reason tied to the record
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment asks the agency to do something procedural that is within its power, with a stated reason. 10 = a specific procedural action is requested — extend or reopen the comment period, defer action pending analysis, phase the effective date, re-notice, publish a tracking or timeline commitment — with a stated reason grounded in the record or in the requester's circumstances. 5 = process is criticized but no request is made, or a request is made with no reason. 0 = no procedural dimension.

--- PAIR GP027 ---

X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment isolates a specific word, phrase, or definition in the regulatory text and shows it admits more than one reading with different practical results. Score 5 when the comment only calls a term unclear or asks for a definition without showing what turns on it. Score 0 when the operative text's wording is never engaged.
Y NAME: Flags an ambiguous term and shows the interpretive consequence
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment isolates a specific word, phrase, or definition in the regulatory text and demonstrates that it admits more than one reading with different practical results. 10 = the term is quoted or cited to its section, at least two readings are laid out, and the divergent consequence of each is stated. 5 = the comment says a term is unclear or asks for a definition without showing what turns on it. 0 = no engagement with the operative text's wording.

--- PAIR GP028 ---

--- PAIR GP029 ---
X NAME: Explicit causal-mechanism / behavioral-response reasoning
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 if the comment lays out a specific step-by-step causal chain (including, where relevant, how affected parties will change their behavior) connecting the rule to its claimed consequence, rather than merely asserting the consequence. Score 0 if the comment asserts a harm or benefit with no explanation of the mechanism producing it.
Y NAME: Explicit causal-mechanism / behavioral-response reasoning
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment traces a step-by-step causal chain from the rule to the effect it claims. Score 10 when each link in that chain is stated, including, where relevant, how affected parties would change their behavior in response. Score 0 when a harm or benefit is asserted with no account of the mechanism producing it. Score in between when a chain is offered but skips steps.
