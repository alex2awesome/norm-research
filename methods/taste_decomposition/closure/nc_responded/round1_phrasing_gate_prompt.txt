You are auditing edits to a bank of scoring criteria for PUBLIC COMMENTS
submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores. Answer
DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly score high on one and low on the other --
including when one is a broadened, narrowed, or shifted version of the other. A
sharper wording of the same property is SAME; a different property is DIFFERENT.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "GP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR GP001 ---
X NAME: Scanned/faxed physical-document artifacts (OCR noise, stamps)
X INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on visible marks that the text originated from a scanned, faxed, or mailed physical document rather than a native digital web-form entry: garbled OCR character strings, broken word-spacing from multi-column layout, date-stamped footers, or 'RECEIVED'/'POSTMARKED' stamps. A 10 shows clear, heavy artifacts of this kind. A 0 is clean native digital text with no scan or transmission artifacts.
Y NAME: Scanned/faxed physical-document artifacts (OCR noise, stamps)
Y INSTRUCTION: Score the visible marks that the text originated from a scanned, faxed, or mailed physical document rather than a native digital web-form entry. A 10 shows clear, heavy artifacts of this kind: garbled OCR character strings, broken word-spacing from multi-column layout, date-stamped footers, or 'RECEIVED' or 'POSTMARKED' stamps. A 0 is clean native digital text with no scan or transmission artifacts. This is a surface-format feature; score the artifacts only.
