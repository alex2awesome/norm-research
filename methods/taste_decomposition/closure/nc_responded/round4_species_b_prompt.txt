You are auditing a pool of candidate scoring criteria written by several
independent analysts for judging PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal
agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores because
they name one concept in different words. Answer DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly
score high on one and low on the other -- including when the two are closely related,
overlapping, or members of the same family. Related is not the same.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "FP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR FP001 ---
X NAME: Access-and-continuity choreography
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment signals an ongoing agency relationship through prior meetings, pilot participation, named officials, offers to discuss issues, or collaborative language; score 0 when it is a one-off submission with no such cues.
Y NAME: Agency-native consultation mapping
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment mirrors the notice’s numbered topics or questions, uses agency-specific section labels, limits itself to requested issues, and offers procedural follow-up; score 0 when it is not mapped to the agency’s consultation structure.

--- PAIR FP002 ---
X NAME: Cites CFR provisions by part and section
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 for explicit part/section CFR citations identifying the provisions addressed; 0 for none.
Y NAME: Argues the rule exceeds statutory authority
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 for a developed legal argument that the statute does not authorise the proposal; 0 when no authority argument is made.

--- PAIR FP003 ---
X NAME: Mail-merge campaign residue: trailing docket line, name-city-ZIP signoff
X INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 for evidence that the text was emitted by a mass-campaign submission platform rather than composed and filed by the signer. Award points for: a signature block of bare name, city, two-letter state and a nine-digit ZIP appended after the argument; the docket or 'Re:' line appearing at the END of the comment, after the argument, rather than at the top; residual mail headers from a campaign vendor (From:/To:/Date:/Subject: lines, or an address at a bulk-mail domain); bulleted asks rendered with an odd platform delimiter (e.g. '::' or '*' repeated as list markers); an argument body that reads as a fixed template with one or two slots filled. A 10 shows the trailing docket block or vendor headers plus the name-city-ZIP tail. A 0 is a comment whose docket reference (if any) is at the top and which carries no appended address, no vendor headers, and no template slotting.
Y NAME: Campaign mail-merge signature block (name + city, state)
Y INSTRUCTION: Score how strongly the comment's closing matches a mass-campaign mail-merge format: a plain personal name immediately followed by 'City, ST ZIP' with no title, organization, or contact info, appended to a short, generically-applicable body. 10 = textbook mail-merge signature (bare name + city/state/zip closing a generic, could-apply-anywhere body). 5 = a personal name+location sign-off but the body contains some docket-specific content. 0 = no personal name+city signature block at all (unsigned, or institutional letterhead signature with title/organization instead).

--- PAIR FP004 ---
X NAME: Expresses intense personal feeling about the proposal
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 for strongly-worded approval or outrage; 0 for flat neutral register.
Y NAME: Reports first-hand operational experience
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the commenter describes what they themselves do in the regulated activity and how the rule would change it; 0 when no first-hand practice is described.

--- PAIR FP005 ---
X NAME: Unsolicited commendation of agency staff and process
X INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on how much the comment praises the agency itself - its staff, its effort, its priorities, or the rulemaking process - beyond the pro-forma 'we appreciate the opportunity to comment' line, which does not count. Score 10 for explicit commendation of the work or the workers ('kudos to the staff who undertook such a massive job', 'Service staff are to be commended', 'thanks to the Department for balancing the mandate with industry concerns', 'thank you for your dedication and drive', 'Great initiative'). Score 5 for a single warm sentence about the proposal being a step in the right direction. Score 0 for no praise of the agency anywhere. Do not count praise of the underlying policy goal by third parties, and do not count the ritual opening thanks.
Y NAME: Deferential praise-laden tone toward the agency
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment includes explicit praise, gratitude, or expressions of trust directed at the agency or its staff (e.g., thanking them for their dedication, calling the process something that makes the submitter proud, opening with unprompted thanks). 10 = comment contains clear unprompted praise or gratitude toward the agency's competence, effort, or process. 0 = comment is neutral or openly adversarial/accusatory toward the agency, with no praise language.

--- PAIR FP006 ---
X NAME: Organizational scale self-credentialing
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment foregrounds quantified membership, customers, assets, market share, national reach, or institutional prestige; score 0 when it makes no organizational-scale claims.
Y NAME: Quantified membership and economic-footprint self-credentialing
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on how much of the comment is spent quantifying the submitting organisation's own size, reach or economic weight - member counts, asset totals, employee counts, market share, covered-lives figures, miles or shipments - as distinct from evidence about the rule. A 10 devotes a full paragraph or a long footnote to figures like 'over 1,600 banks', 'more than 2,300 companies', 'US banking assets over 3.7 trillion dollars', 'coverage for 105 million Americans', '44 million autonomous miles', 'more than 65,000 pilots'. A 5 gives a one-line institutional identification with a single number. A 0 makes no quantified claim about the submitter at all, either because the submitter is an individual or because the letter goes straight to the merits.

--- PAIR FP007 ---
X NAME: Deferential praise-laden tone toward the agency
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment includes explicit praise, gratitude, or expressions of trust directed at the agency or its staff (e.g., thanking them for their dedication, calling the process something that makes the submitter proud, opening with unprompted thanks). 10 = comment contains clear unprompted praise or gratitude toward the agency's competence, effort, or process. 0 = comment is neutral or openly adversarial/accusatory toward the agency, with no praise language.
Y NAME: Deference sandwich
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment opens with thanks or praise, acknowledges the agency’s goals, and then advances a narrowly framed exception or revision; score 0 when it is uniformly supportive, hostile, or uncalibrated.

--- PAIR FP008 ---
X NAME: Litigation-ready statutory attack
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment frames the proposal as legally vulnerable, identifies precise statutory commands, argues that the agency lacks discretion, or references litigation; score 0 when it contains no legal-threat or statutory-validity framing.
Y NAME: Litigation-brief posture
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment adopts an adversarial legal-brief structure with claims of statutory conflict, plain-text mandates, legal jeopardy, formal violations, and requested legal remedies; score 0 when it lacks that posture.

--- PAIR FP009 ---
X NAME: Coalition amplification signals
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment speaks for multiple associations or members, endorses allied submissions, names coalition partners, or describes broad sector coverage; score 0 when it presents only an individual, unaffiliated position.
Y NAME: Coalition / membership-breadth enumeration as legitimacy signal
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment explicitly enumerates or quantifies the breadth of the submitter's coalition or membership (naming member companies, partner states/agencies, or stating a specific membership/assets figure) as a way of establishing standing, independent of the substantive argument. 10 = comment prominently lists member organizations/partner states or cites a specific large membership or assets number as a legitimacy marker. 0 = comment speaks only in the voice of the individual submitter with no membership/coalition breadth claims.

--- PAIR FP010 ---
X NAME: OCR/scan artifact noise in submitted text
X INSTRUCTION: Score visible evidence that the text passed through OCR or was retyped from a scanned/faxed document: broken word spacing (e.g., 'Postpouemeut', 'Compliauce'), header/footer fragments interleaved mid-paragraph, stray page numbers, or garbled characters. 10 = heavy, frequent OCR-type garbling running throughout the text. 0 = clean, apparently born-digital text with no such artifacts.
Y NAME: OCR and portal residue
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the text contains conspicuous extraction or ingestion artifacts—stray page numbers, broken words, malformed spacing, email-routing debris, or corrupted headers—and 0 when it is cleanly rendered prose.

--- PAIR FP011 ---
X NAME: Organizational scale self-credentialing
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment foregrounds quantified membership, customers, assets, market share, national reach, or institutional prestige; score 0 when it makes no organizational-scale claims.
Y NAME: Quantified institutional scale
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the commenter foregrounds multiple figures proving organizational scale—members, employees, assets, customers, market share, or operational miles—and 0 when no institutional scale is asserted.

--- PAIR FP012 ---
X NAME: Accusatory stance toward the agency or a rival interest
X INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on how adversarial the comment's posture is, independent of whether the accusation is supported. Score 10 for explicit imputation of bad faith, illegality, capture or contempt - accusing the agency of violating its own statute or an oath, calling the rule or its authors names, or alleging that a competitor or advisory committee is bending the regulatory process to raise barriers against the commenter. Score 5 for firm disagreement framed as error or oversight ('the agency erred', 'the analysis fails to account for'). Score 0 for a uniformly cooperative posture that frames every disagreement as a request for clarification or a suggested revision. Judge posture only, not evidence.
Y NAME: Litigation-brief posture
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment adopts an adversarial legal-brief structure with claims of statutory conflict, plain-text mandates, legal jeopardy, formal violations, and requested legal remedies; score 0 when it lacks that posture.

--- PAIR FP013 ---
X NAME: Quantifies the rule's cost using the commenter's own data
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment supplies its own numeric estimate of compliance cost with the basis stated; 0 when no numbers appear.
Y NAME: Supplies own dollar estimates of compliance burden
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the submission puts forward concrete monetary burden figures it derived itself and says where they come from; 0 when it offers no figures.

--- PAIR FP014 ---
X NAME: Offers an actionable substitute regulatory design
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when an alternative approach is described concretely enough to be implemented; 0 when the comment proposes nothing.
Y NAME: Names a specific implementable alternative
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment sets out a concrete different design the agency could adopt, specific enough to write into the rule; 0 when it only objects.

--- PAIR FP015 ---
X NAME: Precedent-chain anchoring
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment densely anchors its position in prior rules, earlier docket filings, agency statements, pilots, amendments, or historical practice; score 0 when it addresses only the current proposal without a regulatory history.
Y NAME: Docket-lineage familiarity
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment narrates prior filings, earlier amendments, pilot participation, waivers, agency precedents, or repeated involvement in the same regulatory process; score 0 when it treats the docket as entirely new.

--- PAIR FP016 ---

Y NAME: OCR and portal residue
