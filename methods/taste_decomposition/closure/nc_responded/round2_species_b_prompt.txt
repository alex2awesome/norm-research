You are auditing a pool of candidate scoring criteria written by several
independent analysts for judging PUBLIC COMMENTS submitted to United States federal
agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores because
they name one concept in different words. Answer DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly
score high on one and low on the other -- including when the two are closely related,
overlapping, or members of the same family. Related is not the same.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "FP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR FP001 ---
X NAME: Tenure/place-based persona anchoring
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 if the comment establishes the writer's persona primarily through stated personal tenure, residency length, or generational/occupational identity tied to a place or trade ('26 year resident of Kodiak', 'lifelong Canton, NY family', 'watermen and women... June through October') used as a credibility device, independent of any data or citation. Score 0 if the comment contains no such tenure or place/occupation-identity framing.
Y NAME: Place-anchored lived-tenure standing claim
Y INSTRUCTION: Score how strongly the writer stakes a claim to firsthand standing by anchoring themselves to a NAMED place plus a COUNTED span of time or a concrete owned asset. 10 = an explicit locality plus a number of years plus a tangible stake, e.g. 'I am a 26 year resident of Kodiak, have fished out of Kodiak for 30 years and own the 85 foot Caravelle', 'wholesaling fresh fruits and vegetables in Philadelphia for over 100 years', 'over 20 years of experience studying the Argentine ant in the field in California'. 5 = a generic affiliation with no place, no tenure, and no asset ('as a farmer', 'as a concerned citizen'). 0 = no first-person standing claim at all; the writer never says who they are relative to the regulated activity. Score only the presence and specificity of the standing claim, not whether the argument that follows is good.
