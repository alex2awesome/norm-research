You are auditing edits to a bank of scoring criteria for PUBLIC COMMENTS
submitted to United States federal agencies on proposed rules.

For each PAIR below, decide ONE question:

  Would an independent judge, scoring public comments against criterion X and against
  criterion Y, be measuring THE SAME UNDERLYING CONCEPT?

Answer SAME only if the two would produce essentially interchangeable scores. Answer
DIFFERENT if a comment could plausibly score high on one and low on the other --
including when one is a broadened, narrowed, or shifted version of the other. A
sharper wording of the same property is SAME; a different property is DIFFERENT.

Emit exactly one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdicts": [
  {"pair_id": "GP001", "verdict": "SAME" or "DIFFERENT", "confidence": "high"/"medium"/"low",
   "reason": "<one sentence>"},
  ... one entry for EVERY pair ...
]}


--- PAIR GP001 ---
X NAME: Surfaces an affected class or case the proposal overlooked
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies a specific category of person, entity, or operating situation that the proposal's text does not appear to contemplate, or contemplates wrongly. 10 = a concrete class or edge case is named, its exclusion or misclassification is tied to specific proposal language, and the resulting harm or absurdity is stated. 5 = a gap is gestured at, but neither the class nor the consequence is made concrete. 0 = the comment discusses only the population the notice already addresses.
Y NAME: Surfaces an affected class or case the proposal overlooked
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment identifies a specific category of person, entity, or operating situation that the proposal's text does not appear to contemplate, or contemplates wrongly, and shows the consequence of that gap. 10 = a concrete class or edge case is named, its exclusion or misclassification is tied to specific proposal language, and the resulting harm or absurdity is stated. 5 = a gap is gestured at but neither the class nor the consequence is made concrete. 0 = the comment discusses only the population the notice already addresses.

--- PAIR GP002 ---
X NAME: Cross-rule and system interaction
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies overlap, contradiction, gap, or sequencing with another rule, program, document, or process and specifies how to resolve it; score 0 when it analyzes the proposal in isolation.
Y NAME: Cross-rule and system interaction
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment relates the proposal to another rule, program, document, or process. 10 = it identifies a specific overlap, contradiction, gap, or sequencing problem with that other instrument and specifies how to resolve it. 0 = it analyzes the proposal in isolation and refers to no other rule, program, document, or process.

Y NAME: Implementation realism
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment maps the practical steps, resources, timing, personnel, or supply-chain conditions needed to implement the proposal; score 0 when it ignores implementation realities.

--- PAIR GP004 ---

Y NAME: Names a specific implementable alternative
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment sets out a concrete different design the agency could adopt, specific enough to write into the rule; 0 when it only objects.

--- PAIR GP005 ---
X NAME: Emotional advocacy intensity

This is a surface feature: score the register, not whether the emotion is warranted.
Y NAME: Emotional advocacy intensity

--- PAIR GP006 ---
X NAME: Explicit evidence-to-requested-remedy linkage
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment draws an explicit, traceable line from the evidence or reasoning it presents to the exact provision, number, or action it asks be changed. 10 = the requested remedy is derived step by step from the stated evidence, so the derivation can be followed. 0 = evidence or complaint and a specific ask sit as disconnected pieces, with no traceable link between them.
Y NAME: Explicit evidence-to-requested-remedy linkage
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 0-10 on whether the comment draws an explicit, traceable line from the evidence or reasoning it presents to the exact provision, number, or action it asks the agency to change. A 10 shows the requested remedy derived transparently, step by step, from the stated evidence. A 0 presents evidence/complaint and a specific ask as disconnected pieces, with no traceable link between them.

--- PAIR GP013 ---

Y NAME: Tradeoff and side-effect analysis
Y INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment weighs the proposal’s benefits against costs, risks, displaced protections, or unintended effects and explains the relevant tradeoff; score 0 when it presents only one-sided approval or opposition.

Y NAME: NGO action-alert templated phrasing

--- PAIR GP015 ---

Y NAME: Formal itemized technical/regulatory rebuttal structure

--- PAIR GP016 ---
X NAME: Audits the agency's own figures, record, or arithmetic
X INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment tests the agency's stated justification against numbers or statements the agency itself placed in the record. 10 = it reworks the agency's arithmetic, or sets the proposal against the agency's own admission or its own prior rule, and shows the figures so the check can be reproduced. 5 = it disputes a number but supplies no computation or source. 0 = the agency's figures are accepted or ignored, and the comment argues only from preference.
Y NAME: Audits the agency's own figures, record, or arithmetic
Y INSTRUCTION: Score whether the comment tests the agency's stated justification against numbers or statements the agency itself placed in the record. 10 = the comment reworks the agency's arithmetic, or juxtaposes the proposal with the agency's own admission or its own prior rule, and shows the figures so the check is reproducible. 5 = the comment disputes a number but supplies no computation or source. 0 = the agency's figures are accepted or ignored entirely; the comment engages only at the level of preference.

--- PAIR GP017 ---
X NAME: Distributional consequence analysis
X INSTRUCTION: Score 10 when the comment identifies which vulnerable, dependent, underserved, or differently situated groups bear distinct effects and explains the mechanism; score 0 when it invokes broad public interest without identifying affected groups.
Y NAME: Distributional consequence analysis

--- PAIR GP018 ---
X NAME: Ask serves more than the commenter's own burden or exposure
X INSTRUCTION: Score the beneficiary of the requested change as the comment itself frames it. 10 = the change would improve the rule's functioning for third parties, the protected resource, or the public, including where it plainly costs the commenter something. 5 = mixed: the ask helps the commenter but the stated reasons are about workability for everyone. 0 = the entire ask is relief for the submitter -- delay, exemption, a higher threshold, more time -- with the public-interest framing supplying no independent content.
Y NAME: Ask serves more than the commenter's own burden or exposure
Y INSTRUCTION: Score who benefits from the change the comment requests, as the comment itself frames it. 10 = the change would improve the rule's functioning for third parties, the protected resource, or the public, including where it plainly costs the commenter something. 5 = the ask helps the commenter, but the stated reasons are about workability for everyone. 0 = the ask is entirely relief for the submitter (delay, exemption, a higher threshold, more time), and its public-interest framing adds no independent content.
