# N&C VAT run (v42, 2026-07-15)

One-day VAT row for notice-and-comment on the v4.2 corpus, plus the per-agency ladder for
the organizational-size / time comparison.

## Design

- **Corpus**: `datasets/notice-and-comment/v4/nc_v42_training.jsonl.gz` (176K labeled rows,
  38 agencies, docket-level stable-hash split).
- **Sample**: `v4/nc_vat_sample.jsonl` — 7,482 comments, 22 agencies (16 at 400 balanced
  rows, 6 smaller balanced), built by `v4/build_vat_sample.py`. Exact-text dedup + per-docket
  cap 10/class.
- **Primary y**: MAJORITY outcome across a comment's MADE/NONE labels, ties dropped
  (n=7,084). The any-MADE union y is confounded by n_labels (# matched responses;
  n_labels-alone AUC .695 any-MADE vs .589 majority) — any-MADE demoted to sensitivity.
- **A**: 198 merged rubrics (`rtc_extracted/rubrics.jsonl`) scored 1.0/0.5/0.0/NA by
  Gemma-4-31B, offline batch vLLM, 5 shards on sk2 H200s (~45 min each), scorer
  `v4/score_va_gemma_nc.py`, raw scores `v4/nc_scores_shard{0..4}.npz` (also on
  sk2:/lfs/skampere2/0/alexspan/nc_vat/). Anchor separation strong .872 / mid .523 /
  weak .032 identical on all shards; NA rate .64 (peer-review comparator .65).
- **V**: 27 regex features (length family, CFR/USC cites, docket refs, econ, data,
  alternatives, caps-ratio, first-person, ...) in `v4/aggregate_vat_nc.py`.
- **Dense**: TF-IDF word(1-2)+char_wb(3-5) LR, vectorizer fit per fold, matched rows.
- **CV**: StratifiedGroupKFold grouped by docket everywhere (form-letter near-dups are
  within-docket: only 14/685 prefix-dup groups span dockets).
- Results JSON: `notebooks/data/nc_vat.json`.

## Headline VAT row (pooled, outcome-majority, docket-grouped, n=7,084)

| | V (27 regex) | A (198 rubrics) | V+A | dense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUC | .595 [.582,.609] | .592 [.579,.607] | .593 | .588 [.576,.601] |

- Nuisance baselines: char_len alone .595; n_labels (metadata, not text) .590.
- **All four arms ≈ length-alone.** A adds nothing over V; dense adds nothing over either.
  At the outcome-y, comment text beyond length/style carries little transportable signal
  (T̂ ≈ 0, and even the A-bank ≈ V-regex ≈ length).
- Sensitivity any-MADE (n=7,439): V .628 / A .607 / VA .603 / dense .623 — inflated via
  n_labels (.693); do not quote.
- Secondary engagement-y (substantive response vs not, unbalanced 85/15): V .500 /
  A .559 / VA .557 / dense .585 — the only y where A beats V outright (regex sees nothing,
  judge + dense see a little).

## Per-agency ladder

Reliability rule: ≥20 dockets and ≥8 dockets per class in-sample, else the docket-grouped
readout is dominated by rule-level outcome clustering (outcomes are ~63% docket-pure; tiny
docket counts give wild anti-signal AUCs, e.g. USCBP VA .167 on 14 dockets, ICEB .359 on 4,
MSHA .225 on 7 — these are docket-confound artifacts, NOT negative text signal).

(CDC VA .739/dense .732 excluded by the rule at 20 dockets/7-per-class — flagged, not hidden.)

- **Size vs signal: no correlation.** Spearman agency-size vs VA ρ=.05 (p=.87); vs dense
  ρ=-.27 (p=.37), n=13. Small/specialist agencies (FAA, NHTSA, AMS) sit at the top;
  the giants (CMS, ED, FWS) are at chance-to-weak.
- Time (pooled year buckets): 2014-17 VA .531 / 2018-20 .467 / 2021-26 .591 — no
  monotone trend; bucket composition shifts agencies, so treat as descriptive only.

## Caveats / landmines

1. Topical rubrics act as docket detectors (e.g. "Tobacco advertising" n=68 AUC .282 = one
   docket's outcome, not comment quality). Univariate A tables must be read with this in mind;
   the multivariate readouts are protected by docket-grouped CV.
2. Outcome-y is heavily rule-level: whether the agency changes anything is substantially
   decided per docket, not per comment. Within-docket comparative designs (which comment
   within a rule won changes) are the natural next step.
3. Majority-y leaves agencies mildly unbalanced (pos-rate .27-.48); pooled row may carry a
   small agency-style lift, per-agency rows immune.
4. flashinfer sampler JIT fails on sk2 under nohup (AFS cache) — launch with
   `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0` + `HOME=/lfs/skampere2/0/alexspan`.
