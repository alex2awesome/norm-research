## 0. Terms, spelled out (standing rule)

**V** = the block of programmatic surface features (HashtagWars 20, Style Invitational
19: character/token counts, capitalisation ratio, punctuation density, rhyme and
alliteration rates, prompt-overlap measures);
**A** = the articulated-criterion bank, Gemma-4-31B-judged rubric scores (HashtagWars 30
rubrics, Style Invitational 32);
**VA_nl** = the HistGradientBoosting aggregation of the V+A matrix, mean over seeds
{0,1,2}; **VA_lin** = its logistic counterpart;
**T** = the dense readout (Llama-3.1-8B LoRA reward model on raw text), here the
**dense-standard 3-seed chain** with select-on-eval;
**Δ_beyond** = T − VA_nl, the unarticulated residual; **Δ_r** = Δ_beyond after r rounds
of mining; **ε** = .005, the frozen per-round saturation threshold;
**AUC** = area under the ROC curve; **OOF** = out-of-fold; **GKF** = GroupKFold;
**FIT+MINE / MONITOR** = the closure splits (§1); **M** = the mining slice, the
dense-held-out rows inside FIT+MINE; **HONEST population** = every dense-held-out row
(M ∪ MONITOR), the population on which T and VA are both out-of-sample;
**MONITOR_FULL** = a VA-honest-only monitor over all groups, used for the Track-A gain
statistic where the T-honest MONITOR is too coarse (§1.3);
**Track A** = the quality-criterion miner (k_A = 15 scored criteria/round);
**Track B** = the suspected-spurious-channel miner (k_B = 10 scored channels/round);
**alone-AUC** = the AUC of a single judged channel's raw 0–10 score, no fitting;
**joint B model** = HistGB on the B channels only;
**stacked increment** = AUC(logistic stack of joint-B + X) − AUC(joint-B), the
stratification-free version of a discount;
**mixed channel** = a Track-B channel whose conjectured upstream parent plausibly causes
real quality as well (ADDENDUM 2), reported in both the discounted and undiscounted
readouts as a sensitivity band;
**MIXED PARENT** (ADDENDUM 3, this batch) = an existing Layer-1 **bank criterion** whose
SHAP contribution is entangled with a programmatic length/format feature — the form the
"MIXED channel" problem actually takes on these two cells;
**candidate-real / surface component** = the two replacement criteria a parent is
decomposed into;
**Good-Turing missing mass M̂** = f1/N over proposal *species*, the estimated probability
that the next independent proposal names a species not yet seen;
**LOPO** = leave-one-proposer-out jackknife; **P** = fleet size;
**GEPA** = the prompt-iteration pass required before any final quoted phrasing —
**not applied here**, so everything below carries a pre-GEPA flag.

### 1.2 Dense predictions: two recorded decisions

**DECISION 1 — where T exists.** Unlike every batch-1 cell there is no same-rows dense
rescore for these two: the dense-standard chain wrote per-row probabilities only on its
own eval/test rows (3 seeds × {eval, test}). The HONEST population is exactly those
rows, which is the only population the closure protocol reads T on, so nothing is lost —
but `dense` is NaN on the ~80% dense-train rows and the code never assumes otherwise.

**DECISION 2 — which seed is T.** The registry T is the *mean over seeds of the per-seed
eval AUC*. A row-level readout needs one score per row, so every fit, stratification and
stack here uses the **seed ensemble** (mean of the three seeds' probabilities). Both are
reported everywhere and no claim rests on the difference.

**A consequence worth stating up front: the registry T for these cells is the
SELECTED-ON half.** The chain selects checkpoints on eval, so *eval* is the contaminated
half and *test* is the selection-free one — the mirror image of the N&C responded cell,
whose chain selected on test. On HashtagWars the two halves differ enormously
(eval .6641 vs **test .7638** mean-of-seed AUC, on 4 contests each), so the registry
number is not just imprecise, it is systematically the low one.

### 1.3 Splits

MONITOR is defined INSIDE the dense-held-out rows, as the freeze requires and as
maps_batch1 operationalised it. One **coarse-group adaptation, recorded**: HashtagWars
has 8 dense-held-out contests, and a raw .50 hash cut on 8 units landed 6 MONITOR / 2
mining — a disagreement slice drawn from two contests. The cut is therefore taken at the
**median of the held-out groups' hashes** (still a stable sha256 order, no seeded
shuffle, identical in expectation to .50), applied uniformly to both cells.

| cell | FIT+MINE | MONITOR (groups) | mining slice M (groups) | HONEST | MONITOR_FULL (groups) |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| hashtagwars_verdict | 3,712 | 516 (4) | 408 (4) | 924 | 694 (7) |
| style_inv_toptier | 8,722 | 915 (37) | 1,005 (36) | 1,920 | 1,533 (54) |

Group-purity of the dense split verified in code for both cells (no contest/week

contests; no Δ is ever read on it. Precedent: DECISION 1 of the N&C responded campaign.

### 1.4a Ordering deviation, recorded

The brief asks for the decomposition pass **before** round 1. Its criteria were authored,
audited and queued for scoring first, and its A-routed components enter the bank for the
round-1 readout and every later round. But the **round-1 disagreement slice was mined
against the round-0 bank (V + A only)**, in parallel with decomposition scoring, rather
than against the post-decomposition bank — a wall-clock decision, recorded here rather
than taken silently. Bank states are therefore: `state0` = V + A; `state_d` = state0 +
A-routed decomposition components; `state1` = state_d + A-routed round-1 criteria;
`state2` = state1 + round-2. Round 2's slice **is** mined against `state1`.

Mid-run I misread a `pgrep` (an escaped alternation that matches nothing under pgrep's

round-d HashtagWars job was ~48% through generation; vLLM returned the remaining requests

`screen` supervisor that clears orphaned EngineCores and resumes at tag granularity

| cell | n | HONEST n | MONITOR n | T (HONEST, mean-of-seed) | T (HONEST, ensemble) | VA_nl (HONEST) | **Δ_beyond (HONEST)** | 95% CI | P(>0) |

Two things that change the incoming picture, both instances of the pilot's own
"closure-split Δ levels are protocol-specific" amendment:

1. **HashtagWars' residual is at least as large as advertised and probably larger.** The

Also on the record: Style Invitational's bank leans very hard on nonlinearity and on V

criterion). HashtagWars is the opposite: VA_lin .685 > VA_nl .674 on HONEST — the frozen
nonlinear aggregation is actively *worse* than the linear one, reproducing Layer-1's
negative Δ_interact.

## 4. The judge-free surface audit — and the decisive answer on Style Invitational

Before any mined channel is scored there is a nuisance model that costs zero judge calls:
the cell's own **programmatic V block**. On Style Invitational this is not a footnote —
the FIT+MINE SHAP screen ranks `v_char_count` (mean|SHAP| .442) and `v_word_count` (.213)
three to four times above the strongest *judged* criterion ("Self-contained clarity",
.125). Driver: `vblock_audit.py`, `<cell>_vblock_audit.json`.

| cell | joint V AUC (HONEST) | strongest V features (alone AUC, HONEST) |
|---|---:|---|
| hashtagwars_verdict | .5796 | v_char_count .586, v_token_count .578, v_hashtag_count .473, v_mention_count .464 |
| style_inv_toptier | **.6315** | v_word_count .611, v_char_count .609, v_sentence_count .587, v_uppercase_ratio .416, v_punctuation_density .419 |

| cell | AUC(joint V) | dense increment over V | p | bank increment over V | p |

| cell | T (HONEST) | V_nl | A_nl | VA_nl | **A's increment over V (HONEST)** | P(>0) |

On HashtagWars the judged bank is genuinely informative on its own (A_nl .670 against
V_nl .580). On Style Invitational the judged bank **alone scores below the length block**
(A_nl .613 vs V_nl .632) and adds .006 on top of it.

Read together these say two different things about the two cells.

**HashtagWars: the dense model's signal is not length-borne, the bank's partly is.**
Holding length fixed, T does not move at all (.7315 → .7334) while VA_nl falls .674 →
.635, so the residual nearly doubles under the discount (+.057 → +.098). The dense model
adds +.127 over everything the V block knows, at P(>0) = 1.00.

**Style Invitational: the articulated bank adds essentially nothing over length.** Its
increment over the joint V model is **+.0061 with P(>0) = .80** — not distinguishable
from zero — while the dense model's is +.0227 at P = .97. Inside length strata the bank
falls to **.5409** while T holds .5889. This is the exact mirror image of the
cap_finalist finding in maps_batch1 (§4.2), where stratifying on the nuisance score drove
*T* to chance while the bank held: here it is the **bank** that collapses. Combined with
§3, the conclusion for this cell is already firm before a single mined channel is scored:
**Style Invitational's "bank > dense" is (i) absent on the honest population and (ii)
carried by character and word count rather than by any judged criterion.**

### 14.5 The caveat that must travel with every HashtagWars number

saturation, on a corpus whose positive class was assembled by a different retrieval pass
than its negative class.** This is a property of the SemEval-2017 Task 6 release, not of
the instruments, and any future use of this cell should carry it.
