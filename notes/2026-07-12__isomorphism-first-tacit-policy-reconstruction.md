## Scientific object

For the original G0–G9 3B→8B experiment, the target is exactly the item-level soft policy invoked when Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct receives only a
construct name. The executor is Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct supplied with explicit text. No corpus label,
human rating, archival outcome, independent evaluator, or third model defines success. Those targets
belong to the separate capability/practice experiments; this experiment is direct policy
transplantation.

The intervention is **tacit target knowledge made explicit**: definitions, explanations, recognition
rules, boundary logic, contrastive examples, holistic/gestalt specifications, and residual-driven
textual revisions. Boilerplate, length, and post-hoc numerical calibration are not counted as
knowledge. Policy strictness may be stated inside the prompt because it is itself part of the target
policy, but it is isolated as a calibration arm and never applied as a batch quota.

This broadens the lexical experiment without changing its target. The construct name is one
condition. The stronger conditions ask the target model to articulate non-name gestalt and to revise
that full text from its own held-out behavioral residuals.

## Exact estimand

For item `i`, let `q_i^phi` be the 8B name-only YES probability under target form `phi`, and

```
q_i = mean_phi q_i^phi.
```

For a 3B articulation `a`, let `p_i^{a,phi}` be its corresponding form-orbit score. The primary
distance is adverse-form Bernoulli TVD,

```
d(a,q) = max_phi mean_i |p_i^{a,phi} - q_i|.
```

The target supplies its own empirical identity radius,

```
r(q) = max_phi mean_i |q_i^phi - q_i|.
```

An articulation passes the **near-identity policy certificate** only when paired bootstrap intervals
put it inside the target's own form-identity band on all of:

- adverse-form MAE/TVD: `d(a,q) - r(q) <= .02`;
- rank fidelity: worst-form Spearman no more than `.05` below target self-form rank;
- binary flips: excess no more than `.02`;
- absolute calibration bias: excess no more than `.02`;
- positive target polarity.

This is deliberately harder than equal aggregate recovery against a third target. Prompt length and
unit count do not enter the objective. A shorter prompt never displaces a more isomorphic prompt.

### Nested functional and near-identity standards

The target-self band above is a **near-identity transplantation** standard. It should not erase the
broader, scientifically useful notion of approximate ordinal isomorphism. We therefore also report
a nested functional tier:

```
epsilon_rank = 0.30  <=>  adverse-form and form-quotient Spearman rho >= 0.70.
```

Observed functional ordinal isomorphism requires both point estimates to clear `.70`, target health,
and positive polarity. Certified functional ordinal isomorphism requires both bootstrap lower
bounds to clear `.70`. Functional **policy substitution** additionally requires direct MAE
improvement and a smaller name-only adverse rank below the floor, at the point or interval level
respectively. Flips and bias remain mandatory reported coordinates, but do not redefine the `.70`
ordinal tier.

This absolute floor is a new reporting standard adopted after the G6 residual lockbox was opened.
It is retrospective on existing artifacts and can become confirmatory only on a future frozen
panel. The original near-identity gates and G6 conclusions are not rewritten.

### Four-policy scale-step relations (canonical v4 semantics)

The fixed-target ladder introduces two more policies: the smaller sparse policy `(e,n)` and the
larger sparse endpoint `(E,n)`, both scored against a fixed top target `Q`. The articulated policy
is `(e,h)`. Version 4 keeps the following relations separate:

- **One-sided target-relative noninferiority recovery:** `(e,h)` is no worse than `(E,n)` against
  `Q` by more than the adverse-rank/MAE margins, after demonstrating a native rank/MAE scale gap and
  an articulation rank/MAE gain. It may pass by overshooting the larger endpoint and is not policy
  isomorphism.
- **Two-sided target-loss equivalence recovery:** the adverse rank and MAE losses of `(e,h)` and
  `(E,n)` against `Q` lie within both margin edges, with the same native-gap and articulation-gain
  prerequisites. This is equality of the robust target-loss envelope, not matched-form equality
  and not equality of the two item policies. A vector sensitivity grade also requires flip and bias
  equivalence.
- **Direct endpoint functional fidelity:** both adverse-form and quotient Spearman between `(e,h)`
  and the larger sparse endpoint clear `.70`, with endpoint health and positive polarity. Direct
  endpoint **substitution** additionally requires direct MAE improvement over `(e,n)` and uses the
  predeclared adverse-rank coordinate to show that `(e,n)` is below `.70`; quotient and
  any-coordinate baseline exclusions are descriptive only. The scale-step grade also retains the
  native-gap and articulation-gain prerequisites.
- **Fixed-target functional substitution:** adverse-form and quotient Spearman to `Q` both clear
  `.70`, direct MAE improves over the smaller sparse baseline, and that baseline's predeclared
  adverse-rank coordinate is below `.70`, again with the native-gap and articulation-gain gates.
- **Joint grades:** the functional-isomorphism conjunction requires both the fixed-target and direct
  endpoint substitutions. Its stricter equal-performance version additionally requires the
  two-sided target-loss relation. Matched inert and wrong-construct controls remain a separate
  content-specificity test.
- **Direct endpoint near-identity:** the candidate enters the endpoint's own MAE, rank, flip, and
  bias self band and also clears the functional rank gates. Substitution adds direct MAE gain and a
  predeclared adverse-rank baseline exclusion from the self band.

Observed grades use point estimates. Certified grades require the corresponding bootstrap interval
edges, and simultaneous grades additionally carry the arm-family correction. One-sided recovery,
two-sided equal target loss, and direct endpoint isomorphism are different relations rather than a
single nested sequence. Only near-identity is nested under direct functional fidelity; the joint
grades are explicit conjunctions.

The intact-text incumbent is the best stable source arm in each construct: humor #23 definition,
humor #49 explanation, creative-writing #27 dossier, and press-release #8 definition. A new arm moves
the public frontier only if adverse-form MAE improves on both folds and the secondary identity axes
are non-inferior under the same already-frozen margins. Strict Pareto improvement is retained
separately. Paired intervals after public selection are search diagnostics, not confirmatory p-values.

## Data isolation

The public development panel is two disjoint 200-item folds:

- `residual_prompt_selection`;
- `residual_unit_certification`.

Behavior-indexed text generated from one fold is executed only on the other. Construct-only
self-explications are independent of both folds and are evaluated on both. The two folds jointly
supply 400 public development items without consuming the 400-item `residual_lockbox`. Final refits
may use all 400 public items only after a recipe has cross-fitted; their exact text must then be frozen
before the lockbox is scored.

The objective remains oracle-free throughout. Using the fixed target policy as a teaching signal is
policy compression/distillation, not a switch to an external supervised ground truth.

## Search generations

### G0 — intact source text

Definitions, explanations, rubrics, exemplars, and dossiers frequently improve direct MAE over 3B
name-only, with the same winning source arm on both public folds for all four constructs. None enters
the full identity band. This is the incumbent frontier, not a failed experiment.

### G1 — intact composition and ordering

Name-anchored intact/composed stacks tested source order, explanation/rubric composition, and
examples-first presentation. Three domains improved over 3B name-only, but no composed arm beat its
stable intact-source incumbent. These arms were correctly not promoted.

### G2 — cross-fitted ostension

Residual 1x1, 2x2, and 4x4 positive/negative contrasts and prototype pairs were selected from one
public fold and evaluated on the other. No recipe improved the intact incumbent on both folds.
Examples often corrected mean calibration while degrading item order. This rules out the simple
"add more demonstrations" strategy, not ostensive/formative articulation in general.

### G3 — target self-explication

The 8B target produced 72 independent explicit specifications across three views:

- holistic/gestalt interactions and vetoes;
- contrastive positive/negative distinctions and mechanisms;
- ordered procedures, threshold, and boundary resolution.

Six construct-only specifications per metric were generated from the name alone. Six additional
specifications per metric and teaching fold compressed ten target-policy examples spanning poles,
boundaries, and 8B-versus-3B residuals. Rule-only, source-first, rule-first, and explicit strictness
variants were frozen before 3B execution.

No G3 prompt reaches the complete identity band. Five recipes lower adverse-form MAE on both folds.
Two pass the public pointwise margin-frontier rule, both for **Wordplay quality and clarity**. The
cleanest is a construct-only contrastive self-explication:

The two-fold mean paired MAE gain is `.01334`, with an unadjusted public-development 95% interval
`[.00004, .02904]`. Rank, flip, and bias are jointly non-inferior under the pre-existing margins in

content replaces part of the 3B-to-8B policy distance. It is not full substitution because the
remaining worst-fold rank deficit from target self-identity is about `.313`.

### G4 — cross-fitted full-text residual revision

The target wrote 114 standalone minimal-repair, rank-repair, and gestalt-repair specifications from
cross-fitted residual packets; the compiled bank contained 232 arms. No revision reached exact
isomorphism. Two creative-writing recipes improved point MAE on both folds (`.1473 -> .1325` and
`.1408 -> .1360` for the best recipe), while improving rank relative to the intact dossier. Their
paired public intervals were not decisive. More importantly, semantic review found drift from
"pitch/query and synopsis effectiveness" toward general story quality, so these arms are retained
as behavioral-only exploration and excluded from the tacit-content claim. Press-release target
identity was invalid on one fold and therefore ineligible. No humor revision stably displaced G3.

### G5 — all-item hierarchical full-text articulation

Each 200-item teaching fold was compressed through eight batch memos and then three synthesis modes
with two variants apiece. This directly tested whether the earlier failures were caused by too few
teaching examples or too-short text. No hierarchical recipe improved the intact incumbent on both
held-out folds; none reached exact isomorphism. The longest creative-writing textbooks were actively
harmful (adverse MAE about `.29`, versus about `.14` for the incumbent). Thus articulation amount is
not monotone in reconstruction fidelity. Compact target self-explication currently beats exhaustive
all-item summary because the latter introduces instruction/interference load.

### G7 — rank-contrast articulation (post-lockbox public development only)

The lockbox diagnosis motivated a new public-only generation; the opened lockbox was not used for
generation, selection, or scoring. On each public teaching fold, 10–14 disjoint severe target/
executor rank reversals were selected (47 total across name and behavior parents). The 8B target
wrote one criterion-specific micro-rule per pair. These were compiled into 24 rank lexicons,
conditional decision lists, minimal patches, and fixed `k=2/4` ostensive curricula, then executed by
3B only on the opposite fold.

No common recipe improved the incumbent's MAE on both folds, no recipe entered the identity band,
and the fixed curricula were often harmful. The best points were fold-specific: one held-out fold had
MAE/rho `.1808/.6777` versus incumbent `.1843/.6516`; the other had `.1751/.6754` versus
`.1838/.6436`, but these came from different recipes selected after seeing their evaluation folds and
therefore are not promotable. A training-fold minimum-identity-loss selector improved one
direction (`.1751/.6754`) and failed the other (`.1958/.6229`); each evaluation direction is clean
conditional on the selector, but the selector menu itself is post-hoc and exploratory. Thus pairwise explanation sometimes
contains the missing ordering distinction, but the current global compression does not transport it
stably. Of four exploratory training-fold selectors, none improved MAE in both directions; pure
maximum-rank selection improved rho in both but drove one direction's MAE to `.2991`, an explicit
coordinate tradeoff rather than scale-like substitution. Another round of longer global prose is not justified; any next channel should isolate
compact rank-bearing units or use explicitly item-adaptive ostension, with a new untouched panel.

### G8 — comparative elicitation and marginal-preserving rank transplant

The next public-only channel asked the 3B executor to compare items directly rather than assign each
an independent YES probability. Seven articulation conditions were crossed with three comparison
forms on deterministic degree-12 regular graphs. Every edge was scored in both A/B orders and
symmetrized before Borda and regularized Bradley--Terry recovery. This was necessary: mean
probability disagreement between presentation orders was `.290` and `.263` on the two folds, even
though all 100,800 A/B readouts were finite.

Comparative elicitation alone did not reveal the missing target order. The best raw condition was
name-only Bradley--Terry, with adverse form rho `.527/.520` and form-consensus rho `.572/.569`; the
explicit conditions were lower. The direct 3B policies still carried more target rank information.
However, the comparison signal contained a small complementary component. A frozen-marginal
transplant retained the exact multiset of each direct articulation's YES probabilities and changed
only item order. Across 540 public candidates, 49 normalized recipes improved both MAE and rho on
both folds and 35 point-improved all four identity coordinates on both folds. The strongest stable
family was a 20--25% comparison-rank correction to the construct-only self-explication. One
representative Borda/median recipe moved MAE/rho from `.1643/.691` to `.1635/.696` on one fold and
from `.1771/.639` to `.1641/.672` on the other.

This is a reproducible frontier movement, not exact substitution. No recipe entered the point
identity band and `0/16` leading recipes passed the full bootstrap certificate on both folds. The
remaining adverse rho was about `.67--.70`, versus target self-rho near `.95`. Thus task form can
extract a useful residual component, but pairwise judgment plus global content does not span the
target policy's fine order. The opened residual lockbox was not read.

### G9 — item-adaptive ostension (public development)

G8 motivates a stronger channel that does not compress all target behavior into one global prompt.
For each evaluation item, word-, character-, or hybrid-TF-IDF retrieval selects balanced positive
and negative demonstrations from the opposite 200-item public fold. Policy-neighbor variants expose
the larger Llama's soft score; residual variants additionally expose the smaller name-only score,
making the direction and size of the missing distinction explicit. Demonstrations are crossed with
the intact explanation, construct-only self-rule, fold-derived behavioral rule, or no global parent.
No evaluation-fold target score enters retrieval, prompt construction, or 3B scoring. The frozen
bank has SHA-256 `bc7fdb97581ae3f6056f6a7a957034e4653f6c8aef239e00ce5a1eca06db3b4d` and 32 strategies per
direction. It is evaluated under the same three-form YES/NO quotient; constrained decoding merely
computes the declared conditional YES probability without missing-label imputation. An initial
constrained-top-k score artifact was withdrawn before target analysis because vLLM sometimes
returned only the generated allowed label, producing artificial exact zeros/ones. The valid `v2`
readout teacher-forces the YES and NO continuations separately and normalizes their actual token
log-likelihoods; it is the same conditional binary estimand as the target readout.

The result is a clear rejection of this raw retrieval operator. None of 32 normalized recipes
improved MAE and rho over its direct parent on both folds; none point-Pareto-improved all four
coordinates; none was near-identical under the full policy certificate. Every recipe worsened its
parent in both directions. The
least harmful, behavior-rule plus one positive and one negative hybrid policy neighbor, moved
MAE/rho from `.1830/.637` to `.2794/.472` and from `.1729/.669` to `.2407/.481`. It also induced
large negative calibration shifts (absolute bias `.248/.198`). More demonstrations did not repair
this. Cross-fitted examples are therefore not automatically usable articulation: the executor
treated them as a stricter local classification regime and lost both level and order. This channel
is not eligible for promotion or untouched-panel scoring.

### G10 — adjacent within-family scale step: Llama-3.2 1B to 3B

To test whether exact substitution was hidden by an overly large executor gap, the same frozen
16-arm intact/composed articulation bank was evaluated with Llama-3.2-1B against the
Llama-3.2-3B name-only policy. The target is healthy on both public folds: target self-form rho is
`.971/.963` and self-form MAE is `.061/.057`. No candidate is near-identical. The authored dossier
is a stable component-wise improvement: it reduces adverse MAE from `.2180` to `.1717` and from
`.2206` to `.1648`, while reducing flips and bias. But rank remains `.353/.329`, versus target self-rank
above `.96`. Most other compositions are harmful. Thus a smaller parameter interval does not by
itself make tacit target order executable; at 1B the limiting factor is more clearly executor
capacity, not absence of enough source text.

The disjoint-fold and 400-item pooled audits rule out mere sample imprecision as the explanation.
Among arms that also improve MAE, the largest stable crossfold rank capacity is only `.329`; pooled
authored-dossier rho is `.339` with interval `[.252,.424]` despite a clear `.051` MAE gain. The
highest pooled raw rank is definition at `.437`, but it *worsens* MAE by `.037`, so it is not a
functional substitution at any rank threshold. Increasing the evaluated item count therefore does
not move the 1B executor toward the `.70` region.

### G11 — upper within-family step: Llama 8B to 70B

The same frozen 15-arm intact/composed bank was evaluated with Llama-3.1-8B against the available
Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-FP8 name-only target. This is within family but **not** a pure same-version
parameter contrast, so it cannot identify a scalar size law. The target is healthy: adverse
self-form rho is `.974/.977` and MAE is `.093/.080` across the two public folds.

All 15 explicit-text arms improve adverse MAE over 8B name-only on both folds. The best stable arm,
definition plus explanation, moves MAE/rho from `.422/.625` to `.232/.714` and from `.424/.578` to
`.227/.741`; paired MAE-gain intervals are `[.143,.236]` and `[.147,.241]`. No arm is near-identical:
target rank remains near `.975`, and threshold/form deficits remain large. Together with G10 and
the G6 lockbox, this replicates **probability-level partial substitution** at three Llama scale
steps while also replicating the failure of available text to transport target-fine item order.
Only the 1B→3B pair is same-version; construct breadth remains one cell.

Under the new retrospective functional tier, four single-text arms clear adverse rho `.70` and
improve MAE on both public folds: definition, rubric, definition+explanation, and
definition+rubric. Each also clears quotient rho `.70`; because the fixed 70B target is the larger
sparse endpoint in this comparison, all four pass both direct endpoint substitution and the joint
fixed-target/direct-endpoint functional grade. Definition reaches `.749/.771` adverse rho with
lower bounds `.671/.692`; therefore these are **observed**, not bootstrap-certified,
substitutions. None is two-sided target-loss-equivalent or near-identical. No 1B→3B arm reaches
`.70`.

A threshold-free crossfold audit records the largest rank floor each fixed articulation can sustain
on both folds. The four-member fiber is not a rounding artifact: all four survive through at least
`.714`, two survive through `.723`, and definition survives through `.749`. It is nevertheless an
upper-edge result rather than a broad plateau: no arm reaches `.75` on both folds. At `.65`, eight
arms are observed and definition is lower-bound certified on each fold.

Pooling the two disjoint public folds gives 400 items and a fold-stratified bootstrap. This is a
precision diagnostic, not a new holdout. Definition reaches pooled rho `.760` with nominal 95%
interval `[.707,.804]`, so a frozen single candidate with 400 comparable new items appears capable
of certifying the `.70` floor. After Bonferroni correction over all 15 already-inspected arms, its
interval is `[.679,.823]` and no arm certifies. Thus the present gap is partly finite-sample
precision and partly search multiplicity; pooling does not upgrade the claim grade.

### G12 — multi-articulation prompt portfolios

Because distinct prompts make different item-level errors, a supplementary system-level test fits
one nonnegative, sum-one mixture of 3B prompt policies, shared across all three forms. This is not a
single larger prompt: it averages multiple small-model executions. It can establish complementary
articulation information, but cannot by itself establish single-text scale substitution or an
equal-but-different fiber.

The first diagnostic portfolio is withdrawn from any tacit-content claim: an unconstrained feature
bank assigned substantial weight to inert and wrong-construct controls and contained six duplicate
copies of the name policy. The corrected implementation excludes all controls and retains exactly
one name baseline. A 126-policy reciprocal-development atlas still improves over 3B name-only on
MAE in both directions for every fitted objective, but no objective beats the best cross-fitted
single articulation on the complete paired frontier in both directions.

The stricter analysis uses only 24 fold-independent policies: five intact source tellings, eighteen
construct-only target self-explications, and one name baseline. Weights learned on one public fold
are transferred unchanged to the other. The best MSE portfolio improves the selected single on one
held-out fold from MAE/rho `.177/.639` to `.161/.691` (paired MAE gain `[.008,.025]` and rho gain
`[.017,.089]`), but on the reverse transfer it moves `.164/.691` to `.173/.748`: better order, worse
MAE, flips, and bias. Rank-aware and rank-plus-MSE objectives do not stabilize the tradeoff; their
best held-out ranks remain about `.72–.78` against target self-rank `.95`. No portfolio is exact,
and no objective passes the full paired frontier in both directions.

Under the `.70` functional tier, the rank and rank+MSE portfolios have observed **direct endpoint
functional fidelity** in both transfer directions: adverse rho is `.719/.779` and `.705/.764`, with
uncertainty-clearing MAE gains over 3B name-only. They are not functional endpoint substitutions
under the canonical v4 semantics, because the 3B name baseline is already inside the direct `.70`
region (adverse rho `.760/.780`). The rank portfolio's candidate-rho lower bounds are `.641/.715`;
rank+MSE gives `.624/.698`, so neither is fidelity-certified on both folds. This is a positive
system-level approximate-isomorphism result, not a missing-scale-capability replacement or a

## Equal-but-different articulations

A near-identity fiber is the set of substantively distinct prompts whose adverse-form policies all
enter the same target identity band. Diversity is evaluated only after membership:

1. certify each articulation independently;
2. retain only identity-band members;
3. require pairwise behavioral equality of their 3B policies;
4. audit lexical/channel/provenance distinctness and semantic fidelity;
5. report the number and topology of distinct fiber members.

The near-identity public and lockbox results contain zero certified fiber members. The three G6
confirmatory texts are equal-purpose and surface-distinct, but their policies cannot be called equal
or near-identical merely because their aggregate MAEs are in the same region.

The retrospective functional tier does reveal an **observed ordinal multi-realization set** at the
8B→70B upper pair. Definition, rubric, definition+explanation, and definition+rubric all clear
adverse and quotient rho `.70` and improve MAE on both public folds. All six pairings clear a stricter mutual
behavior floor of rho `.90`; their actual mutual rhos are `.963–.989` on both folds, with declared
text/channel surface distances `.353–.732`. This spans declarative, procedural, and composed
realizations. The mutual gate is point-only, so this is not numerical policy identity. It is not
interval-certified, not untouched-panel confirmation, and inherits the
Llama-3.1→3.3/FP8 version caveat, but it is positive evidence that functionally equivalent tacit
content has multiple textual realizations.

The four surfaces should not be counted as four independent semantic generators. Component-set
minimality leaves two cores: the declarative definition and the procedural recognition rubric.
Their direct pair has surface distance `.630` and policy rho `.978/.974` across folds. The other two
members are component supersets. More articulation is not monotonically better: all ten strict
supersets of definition reduce adverse rho on both folds (deltas `-.136` to `-.028`), and all ten
strict supersets of rubric do likewise (`-.140` to `-.007`). The defensible topology is therefore
two minimal high-mutual-rank routes plus redundant/interfering composed realizations, not four
independent knowledge decompositions.

The `.70/.80/.85/.90` sensitivity profile is now emitted directly. Laugh density supplies a second
construct-distinct case: definition and explanation are both stable target members and have mutual
quotient rho `.857/.871`, so they form a distinct two-route set at `.85` but not at the frozen `.90`
primary mutual gate. This is reported as threshold sensitivity, not as a post-hoc threshold change.

If multiple members appear, they support a stronger result than one lucky prompt: membership in the
accepted ordinal target region has multiple textual realizations rather than a unique wording.
Only the stricter mutual vector and matched-form refinements can support stronger policy-equality
language. Channel-spanning members (for example, a rule and an ostensive curriculum) would support
an even broader representational claim.

## Claim map

The evidence is a lattice rather than one cumulative scalar ladder:

1. **Directional articulation:** explicit target-relevant content improves direct fidelity.
2. **Frontier advance:** a prompt improves the intact-text incumbent on both public folds under the
   identity margins.
3. **One-sided target-relative recovery:** the smaller articulated executor is noninferior to the
   larger sparse endpoint against `Q`; overshoot is permitted.
4. **Two-sided target-loss equivalence:** the two robust rank/MAE losses to `Q` are within both
   margin edges; item-policy equality is not implied.
5. **Functional fidelity:** adverse-form and quotient rho both clear `.70` to the declared fixed
   target or direct endpoint. Functional **substitution** additionally needs direct MAE gain and the
   predeclared adverse-rank baseline exclusion, plus native-gap and articulation-gain gates for a
   scale-step claim.
6. **Joint functional scale substitution:** the same articulation passes both fixed-target and
   direct-endpoint substitution; the stronger joint grade also passes two-sided target-loss
   equivalence.
7. **Near-identity policy transplantation:** a frozen articulation enters the endpoint's complete
   target-self band on an untouched lockbox, nested under the functional floor.
8. **Fiber multiplicity and channel breadth:** substantively distinct frozen articulations
   independently pass the declared membership grade and their mutual ordinal-policy gate; full
   probability/threshold/calibration equality requires a separate vector-equivalence refinement.
9. **Construct/domain prevalence:** simultaneous inference over a preregistered construct bank.
10. **Scale law:** horizontal articulation shifts align a genuinely same-version/training-regime
    model ladder and predict held-out constructs.
11. **Family breadth:** the law transfers to a second scale-matched model family.

G6 confirms directional articulation and a frontier advance for one construct on an untouched
lockbox: explicit target-relevant content improves the 3B name-only policy, and one frozen
behavior-indexed specification improves the intact full-text incumbent under family-wise inference.
Under the retrospectively chosen `.70` floor, its best confirmatory articulation has adverse rho
`.6986` and therefore does not pass by rounding; its quotient rho `.710` is retained as a boundary
diagnostic. It provides no confirmatory functional, joint, near-identity, prevalence, or scaling-law
claim.

The strongest confirmatory statement is therefore a **component-wise scale-articulation
substitution**: tacit target knowledge made explicit replaces a confirmed portion of the scale gap
on probability-level fidelity, robustly across prompt forms. Here “scale gap” names the observed
3B-to-8B model contrast; their Llama-version difference prevents a pure parameter-size causal
interpretation. The broader retrospective evidence also supports four stable observed joint
fixed-target/direct-endpoint functional substitutions at
the upper pair and four stable observed one-sided target-relative noninferiority recoveries at
3B-to-8B for Wordplay. The latter
are not direct policy-isomorphism results: no member is stably two-sided-equivalent or directly
endpoint-isomorphic after imposing the baseline gap. At the upper pair, the same four texts do pass
the direct `.70` endpoint-substitution gate, yielding six stable mutually high-rank pairs, but none
passes the stricter joint two-sided-equal-loss grade or near-identity. No candidate is
bootstrap-certified for any v4 functional, direct, joint, equal-loss, or near-identity grade; no 3B
candidate joins local recovery to `.70` fixed-target fidelity across folds, and no near-identity
member exists. The 400-item pooled upper definition
interval clears `.70` only at the nominal per-arm level. This is stronger than "names come online
with scale" and weaker than near-identity policy transplantation. It also refutes a scalar "more
text = more scale" story: articulation is a structured operator with dimension-specific transport,
construct dependence, and interference. Because the positive upper pair is Llama-3.1-8B versus
Llama-3.3-70B-FP8, it cannot identify a pure parameter-size effect.

Historically, the post-G6 3B→8B recommendation was to keep the same unsupervised 8B name-only
target while making candidate text explicitly rank-bearing. Such text must encode conditional boundary
distinctions, interactions, vetoes, and ordered comparisons that change which items outrank which,
while a separate compact calibration clause preserves G6's level gain. Development must remain
cross-fitted, and any revised candidate requires a new untouched item panel before another exact
claim. Merely increasing the current lockbox sample cannot repair a `.249` point rank deficit.
