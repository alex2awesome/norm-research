## Bottom line

The existing results support scale-dependent lexical access and enculturation effects. They do not
currently support a general claim that explicit articulation replaces model scale. The previous
horizontal-shift statistic measures displacement only over the performance range both readers
already reach. It neither requires a sparse-prompt scale gap nor asks whether the smaller reader
actually reaches the larger reader's target. It must not be described as an exchange rate.

The defensible stronger target is a **partial substitution law with an enculturation ceiling**:
within the subset of construct/model pairs with a genuine sparse-prompt scale gap, estimate (a) the
probability that tacit knowledge about that same construct, made explicit as definitions,
explanations, rules, mechanisms, or ostensive contrasts, closes that gap; (b) the minimum held-out
articulation budget when it does; and (c) the right-censored mass for which no tested articulation
budget substitutes for scale. The current artifacts falsify **finite-bank universal reachability at
the tested budget**. They cannot falsify reachability under every possible articulation channel.

The treatment is therefore **articulated construct knowledge**, not generic prompt content, token
count, verbosity, or an arbitrary optimized instruction. Filler is a control, not a weak dose of the
treatment. A performance-selected prompt trick is not evidence that tacit knowledge was made
explicit unless every included element was independently certified as a faithful externalization of
the target construct before test outcomes were inspected.

### Functional ordinal addendum

For approximate policy reconstruction, freeze `epsilon_rank=.30`, equivalently adverse-form and
form-quotient Spearman floors of `.70`. Report a point-estimate **observed** grade and a distinct
**certified** grade whose bootstrap lower bounds both clear `.70`. This functional tier does not replace the stricter
target-self identity region; it prevents a near-cloning standard from erasing substantively strong
ordinal reconstruction. Call it functional **substitution** only when the smaller name baseline is
also below the floor (point-below for observed; upper confidence bound below for certified) and the
explicit arm improves MAE. Otherwise it is functional reconstruction without a demonstrated scale
gap. Existing lockbox re-reads are retrospective under this addendum.

## Correct estimand

There are two related but non-interchangeable model-to-model estimands in this stream:

1. **Policy transplantation:** set `T_i` to the larger executor's own sparse/name-only policy and
   require direct fidelity to it. Two-sided identity and the `.70` functional tier are meaningful
   here because cloning the target policy is constitutive.
2. **Capability substitution under fixed top target:** freeze one higher model-internal `T_i` for
   the whole executor ladder, then ask whether `(s,ak)` reaches or exceeds `(S,a0)` against that
   same target. Overshooting the larger sparse comparator is success, not failure; target-self
   identity remains a separately reported reconstruction tier.

The stronger joint claim evaluates both views at once: the articulation must enter the fixed-top
target region and directly reproduce the larger sparse endpoint. One-sided capability
noninferiority remains useful, but it is not called isomorphism merely because overshoot is allowed.

Neither estimand introduces human labels, compiler output, or an external supervised anchor. Moving
native targets may be reported as local transplantation experiments, but their debts cannot be
added to estimate one executor curve.

For construct `i`, smaller reader `s`, larger same-family reader `S`, sparse prompt `a0`, articulation
arm `ak`, fixed target `T_i`, and held-out performance `Q`:

1. **Eligibility / baseline gap:** `Q(S,a0;T_i) - Q(s,a0;T_i) > delta`, with the paired-bootstrap
   lower bound also above `delta`. No-gap cases are parity, not substitutions.
2. **Selection without test leakage:** choose among frozen, independently validated articulations,
   or choose their minimum-cost `k`, on a stratified development split only. Development outcomes
   may order or subset a fixed knowledge inventory; they may not generate new prompt tricks.
3. **Small-reader gain:** the held-out lower bound for `Q(s,ak)-Q(s,a0)` is above zero.
4. **Noninferior rescue:** the held-out lower bound for `Q(s,ak)-Q(S,a0)` is at least `-delta`.
5. **Direct endpoint isomorphism:** now treat `(S,a0)` itself as the target policy. Functional
   endpoint isomorphism requires adverse-form and quotient rank lower bounds at `.70`, direct MAE
   improvement over `(s,a0)`, and a predeclared rank coordinate proving that `(s,a0)` was outside
   the endpoint region. Near-identity instead requires entry into `(S,a0)`'s own form-self band.
6. **Two-sided equal performance:** for the anti-overshoot tier, the entire paired interval for each
   target-relative loss difference between `(s,ak)` and `(S,a0)` lies in its frozen symmetric
   margin. Equal loss alone is not policy identity, so this augments rather than replaces step 5.
7. **Articulation specificity:** `ak` beats a word-, form-, and budget-matched inert control `fk`
   on the same held-out probes. The confirmatory design should also include an equally articulate
   but wrong-construct control, showing that the gain comes from externalizing the target knowledge
   rather than generic semantic scaffolding. For segment grids, `uk` must be compared with `fk`, not
   the best filler at some other `k`.

For direct policy transplantation, unqualified `certified_functional_endpoint_isomorphic_scale_substitution`
means conditions 1, 3, 5, and 7. Its anti-overshoot refinement also requires condition 6; its
near-identity refinement replaces the functional part of condition 5 with the endpoint self-band.
For fixed-top-target capability substitution, one-sided `certified_scale_step_noninferiority`
uses conditions 1--4 and 7. The strongest joint fixed-target/direct-endpoint claim requires both
fixed-target functional fidelity and direct endpoint isomorphism; condition 6 is a stricter nested
grade, not a substitute for direct behavioral fidelity. The fixed `T_i` is shared by all arms;
arm-specific consensus is a convergence diagnostic, not a valid substitution target.

The implemented condition-6 comparison is two-sided equivalence of the **robust/adverse-form
fixed-target loss envelope**: candidate-minus-larger rank and MAE differences must both lie inside
their symmetric margins. It is not equality on every matched prompt form. The prospective design
therefore treats matched-form rank/MAE equivalence as a separately reported secondary robustness
analysis; failure there localizes a form-specific residual without retroactively changing the
frozen primary estimand.

### Frozen prospective hypotheses and decision rule

Let `F_Q(a)` mean that articulated small-model arm `a` clears the fixed-top-target adverse-form and
form-quotient Spearman floor of `.70`. Let `F_E(a)` be the analogous direct comparison with the
larger sparse endpoint, including direct MAE improvement and the predeclared proof that the small
sparse baseline lies outside the endpoint region. Let `N(a)`, `G(a)`, and `C(a)` denote,
respectively, a genuine native scale gap, articulation gain over the small sparse arm, and
simultaneous superiority to the exact-count inert and wrong-construct controls. All confirmatory
symbols below refer to confidence-bound, not point-only, gates on the untouched final panel.

1. **Functional joint prerequisite (`H_J`).** `H_J(a) = N(a) and G(a) and C(a) and F_Q(a) and
   F_E(a)`. This is the minimum confirmatory claim that explicit construct knowledge functionally
   replaces a native scale step while reconstructing both the common target channel and the actual
   larger sparse policy. A point-only or public-fold pass is labelled observed/retrospective, never
   confirmed.
2. **Anti-overshoot joint refinement (`H_J^eq`).** `H_J^eq(a) = H_J(a) and E_Q(a)`, where `E_Q`
   requires the complete paired intervals for robust fixed-target rank and MAE loss differences
   between `(s,a)` and `(S,a0)` to lie within the frozen symmetric margins. This stricter grade
   combines direct item-policy fidelity with equal target-relative performance, so neither
   undershoot nor overshoot can create the result. It is not the primary functional endpoint:
   when `(S,a0)` is also the fixed target, the current `.05` rank-loss margin demands roughly the
   larger model's `.95`-level self agreement and would silently undo the declared `.70` epsilon.
3. **Near-identity secondary (`H_NI`).** `H_NI(a)` replaces the direct functional region in `H_J`
   with entry into the larger endpoint's full target-self band, while retaining native-gap, gain,
   and control gates. It is a stricter secondary endpoint, not the definition of all useful
   isomorphism.
4. **Convergent ordinal articulation fiber (`H_fiber`).** Freeze substantively distinct routes
   before outcomes. Both must pass `H_J`; the multiplicity-adjusted lower confidence bound on their
   mutual held-out form-quotient Spearman must be at least `.90`, and at least 99% of bootstrap rank
   draws must be finite. Report `.85` as a frozen sensitivity analysis and the subset in which both
   arms also pass `H_J^eq`. This establishes distinct textual realizations with highly concordant
   item ordering; it does **not** establish equality of probability levels, threshold decisions,
   calibration, individual forms, or semantic contents. When a bank declares component
   inventories, minimality and incomparability are additional structural evidence. The present v1
   sentinel does not declare those inventories and therefore uses two preselected atomic
   declarative/procedural routes plus a surface-distance gate. No post-test recombination enters
   the confirmatory family. Both the six-member content/control family and the one-pair mutual
   family use central two-sided intervals. Ordinal `H_fiber` consumes the rank lower edge;
   `H_fiber^vec` is an intersection-union test over that edge and the mutual form-quotient MAE,
   threshold-flip, and absolute-bias upper edges at `.02` margins. Co-primary coordinates need no
   within-pair alpha split. The two family bounds sum to a composite FWER no larger than `.05`;
   matched-form and semantic equality remain outside the claim.
5. **Breadth and censored debt (`H_prev`).** On the fresh, performance-unselected construct cohort,
   the denominator is every construct/model contrast with a certified native gap. Estimate the
   cluster-bootstrap lower bound on the fraction with at least one `H_J` arm, and report the
   complementary right-censored mass at the frozen maximum full-text budget. Report `H_J^eq`
   prevalence as the strict calibration-preserving tier. No-gap constructs, prior-selected
   sentinels, and failed target-health cells remain visible but do not enter that denominator.
   Freeze a substantive prevalence threshold before final scoring; without that threshold, the
   panel supports an existence estimate rather than a claim that substitution is prevalent.

`H_J` is the primary functional endpoint and deliberately a prerequisite rather than a fallback:
if it fails, neither equal target loss nor prompt-specific parity can be promoted to policy
substitution. Conversely, `H_J` can pass while `H_J^eq` fails; that outcome means useful ordinal
replication with a remaining target-relative level/calibration mismatch, which is scientifically
sharper than collapsing all outcomes into success/failure.

### Articulation arms

- Every arm must be traceable to a frozen knowledge inventory for the same construct: its
  definition, constitutive conditions, explanatory mechanism, recognition rule, boundary cases, or
  contrastive/ostensive examples. Exclude probe-specific answer hints, generic reasoning advice,
  role instructions, and post-outcome prompt optimization.
- Audit fidelity independently: an articulation must preserve the construct under paraphrase and
  must not silently redefine it. Record author/source provenance and human or independent-model
  review before reader outcomes are opened.
- Call the current fragments **address segments**, not Omega units. Promote a segment to a certified
  unit only after its identity, necessity/sufficiency, and form-stability tests pass.
- Construct nested articulation arms and exactly word/form-matched filler arms. Apply the same
  canonical, question, boilerplate, and suffix operators to both.
- Because a single rubric-first prefix confounds amount with segment order, use either several
  frozen random orders or development-only selection over the frozen, certified knowledge atoms.
  Selection may not alter or invent atoms. Confirm only the selected arm on test. For a quantitative
  exchange rate, use `minimal_cost_noninferior`, which selects the first development arm reaching
  the larger reader's sparse target and tests that arm untouched.
- Include a full-specification endpoint and contrastive positive/negative examples. A failure even
  at that endpoint is right-censored evidence for an enculturation ceiling, not an arbitrarily large
  segment cost.

Keep the confirmatory arm family small. The two primary full-text routes are (a) a compact
declarative definition/explanation containing interactions, vetoes, boundary distinctions, and a
calibration clause, and (b) a procedural recognition/ordered-comparison rule. In later breadth
panels these should ideally be independently authored; the current frozen sentinel does not claim
that provenance. Full text is primary because gestalt interactions may be irreducible. CUF-certified atoms and
unit count are secondary mechanism/dose analyses: they estimate minimal debt only after the full
endpoint is viable, and remain right-censored otherwise. A contrastive or exhaustive route may be a
frozen diagnostic, but a new 15-arm confirmatory search would spend power on multiplicity rather
than the scientific claim.

### Primary population analysis

For each eligible construct and coordinate, estimate `k*(s,S)` as the minimum test-confirmed
articulation budget; otherwise record right censoring. Do not regress this only on the log-parameter
gap. Debt can depend on recipient absolute capability, the confirmed native scale-advantage vector,
baseline target debt, construct difficulty, content channel, family, and executor-by-articulation
interaction. A suitable preregistered censored multilevel model includes those terms and treats raw
parameter gap as one predictor only after same-version scale-matched checkpoints exist.

The structural prediction is curve collapse under
`u_(c,j)(e,h)=f_(c,j)(kappa_(e,j)+a_(c,h,j)+gamma_(e,h,j))`, with small interaction `gamma` in the
domains where a horizontal substitution approximation is claimed. A scalar `beta_scale` is a
strong nested special case, not the default law.
Report the certified rescue probability among genuine gaps and the censored ceiling mass alongside
the slope. A strong *prevalent* substitution claim should additionally freeze a substantively
meaningful lower-bound threshold for the rescue probability; a merely nonzero rescued subset is an
existence claim. Require the direction to replicate by family and require articulation to beat
matched filler and wrong-construct controls. Do not pool no-gap cases into the rescue numerator.
